======================================================================================

TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Every id below was read from a source this week;
anything without a readable source says "unverified" instead of a guess.

4.3 Creature side (mob mining / ore skinning)
  Rule: mining skill must be at least 5 x the creature's level (a level 70
  mob needs 350, level 80 needs 450) and this gives NO skill-ups -
  "Unfortunately, mining cannot be leveled in this fashion"
  (warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mob_mining, read 2026-10-25). A mining pick is
  required for corpses too. Eligible creatures: giants and elementals -
  "Low-level elementals and giants have been added, as well as all level 60+
  Giants and elementals". Drops: uncommon/rare gems, ore, elemental
  ingredients, vendor trash; drops are locked to the mob's own expansion
  (TBC mobs do not drop WotLK ore and vice versa).
  Wowhead's current "Minable (mining)" NPC filter returns 491 creatures
  (page read 2026-10-25; sample rows: Morcrush 18690 Blade's Edge
  Mountains, Enraged Crusher 18062 Nagrand, Apex 19940 Netherstorm,
  Ozruk 42188 The Stonecore, Rockweed Shambler 150583 Nazjatar,
  Avarius 150191 Nazjatar). The 2018-09-28 capture of spell 2575 shows
  "Mined from NPC (622)" against today's 954 - which creatures carried the
  flag inside 8.3.7 exactly is unverified; read your own creature data.
  TrinityCore master's spell effects contain a skinning path but no
  mining-on-corpse path in the effects read 2026-10-25, so whether your fork
  can mine corpses at all is unverified.

5. HOW THE SKILL RISES (SERVER RULES)
--------------------------------------
  - The required skill of a node comes from the lock of its gameobject
    (client Lock.db2 via Spell::CanOpenLock, TrinityCore master). Skill below
    that value returns SPELL_FAILED_LOW_CASTLEVEL; every gather spell in
    section 2 adds its effect value (+1275) to the check, which is why a
    skill-1 character can pick any node (matches the wiki's post-6.0.2 rule).
  - Bands around the required value: yellow = red + 30, green = yellow + 30,
    grey = green + 30; a gameobject_template can override yellow and grey via
    its trivial-skill low/high fields, and then green is the midpoint
    (Player::UpdateGatherSkill, TrinityCore master).
  - Chance per colour from worldserver.conf: SkillChance.Orange = 100,
    SkillChance.Yellow = 75, SkillChance.Green = 25, SkillChance.Grey = 0;
    SkillGain.Gathering = 1 point per success (worldserver.conf.dist read
    2026-10-25).
  - Mining DOES have chance decay, unlike herbalism: SkillChance.MiningSteps
    right-shifts the chance by skill/steps, halving it once per steps skill
    points; setting it to 0 disables the decay (BfA_8.3.7_party_features.txt
    4.6). The TrinityCore master conf read has SkillChance.MiningSteps = 0
    (decay off by default there) - your fork's conf decides, unverified.
  - One skill-up per character per node GUID until the node respawns
    (EffectOpenLock per-GUID skill-up list) - a party cannot chain skill-ups
    off one vein.
  - Smelting also gives Mining skill points (wiki Mining: possible since
    patch 3.0.8; BfA_8.3.7_party_features.txt 4.6), so a companion cannot
    infer profession skill from gathered node count alone.
  - The cap belongs to the rank learned (section 1 caveat 150 vs 175); the
    routine refuses to raise a skill at its maximum.

6. HOW NODES RESPAWN
--------------------
  - Same gameobject machinery as herbs (TrinityCore master, read
    2026-10-25): a mined node runs GO_ACTIVATED (lootable) then
    GO_JUST_DEACTIVATED, schedules m_respawnTime = now + delay where the
    delay is gameobject.spawntimesecs of its spawn row (code constructor
    default before load is 300 s), and at expiry a non-pooled node is put
    back on the map while a pooled node calls PoolMgr::UpdatePool and the
    pool re-rolls which member spawns (chances in pool_members, limits in
    pool_template). Respawn checks run at Respawn.MinCheckIntervalMS = 5000;
    Respawn.DynamicMode = 0 by default.
  - Wiki node-variant rules (warcraft.wiki.gg Mining, "Respawns"): rare
    versions can spawn IN PLACE of common metal when the node respawns -
    Tin to Silver, Iron to Gold, Mithril to Truesilver, Fel Iron and
    Adamantite to Khorium, Saronite to Titanium, Elementium to Pyrite, Ghost
    Iron to Trillium. Rich nodes give more output; Seams (introduced in
    Legion) are longer nodes with more ore, usually in caves or
    underground; the guide adds that a cave's seam can respawn as a
    Felslate Seam next visit.
  - No source read gives any numeric respawn time: BfA guide route notes
    only say "the respawn rate should be fast enough on the yellow path",
    Legion guide says watch caves respawn - every actual interval lives in
    your world database (gameobject.spawntimesecs, pools), and the client
    build 35662 ships no spawn rows, coordinates or respawn times
    (BfA_8.3.7_party_features.txt 4.7) - all values unverified from client
    sources.

9. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------
  1. Cannot see nodes: spawn coordinates, per-zone spawn lists, respawn
     times and pool layout are world-database rows; build 35662 carries none
     of it (BfA_8.3.7_party_features.txt 4.7). The companion must read them
     from your DB.
  2. Cannot use Find Minerals 2580 for anything: the spell's whole effect is
     the client mini-map overlay. Route from DB positions instead.
  3. Cannot receive input-driven casts: no CMSG_CAST_SPELL arrives from a
     player. The bot must start the 3.2-second Mining cast itself with the
     node guid as target, inside the 5-yard interact range.
  4. Cannot watch the cast bar or the loot window: mining opens the node via
     GameObject::Use and the wiki's "loot window appears" step is client UI -
     the companion must move ore out of the node's loot storage itself.
  5. Cannot re-farm one vein for skill-ups: the per-GUID skill-up list
     grants at most one point per character until the node respawns.
  6. Cannot decide the pick question from the sources: spells say a pick is
     required, the wiki says it is optional for nodes, mob mining says
     required - the bot's pick-item rule for your fork is unverified; test
     before enforcing (item "Mining Pick" id not read here - unverified).
  7. Cannot mine corpses unless the fork implements it: no mining-on-corpse
     path was found in the TrinityCore master effects read for this file;
     the wiki's 5 x level rule (no skill-ups) describes retail behaviour.
     unverified on your core.
  8. Cannot trust mounted mining on 8.3.7: the 2018 capture lacks the
     mounted flag, current data has it, and the guide sells stirrups - test
     mounted casting before relying on it (section 3).
  9. Cannot infer skill from node counts: smelting raises Mining too, and
     your fork may enable SkillChance.MiningSteps decay - companion skill
     accounting must read the skill line directly.
 10. Cannot exceed the cap: whichever cap applies (150 or 175, section 1),
     the skill routines refuse raises at the maximum.
