=== 2. HEALTH PERCENTAGES / PHASE TRIGGERS ===

Heartsbane Triad (DATA 0):
Three sisters share health pool (single ENCOUNTER_ID 2116 not used; each has
own HP).
  Iris passes at fixed intervals (FOCUSING_IRIS every 30s script, 30s LW).
Stage 1: Solena has iris (start). Stage 2: Briar gets iris at 30s. Stage 3:
Malady at 60s.
  Each stage: 2s delay -> aura (Apathy/Thorns/Dread) + Dire Ritual 15s later.
  Reset iris at 18s after Malady gets it (EVENT_RESET_IRIS).
  No health-based phase transitions in script.

Soulbound Goliath (DATA 1):
No health-based phases. Burning Brush every 30s (script) / 30s (LW) / not in
DBM.
On Heroic/Mythic: Burning Brush spawns 6 Burning Souls at fixed position
(-409,-261,234).

Raal the Gluttonous (DATA 2):
  No health-based phases. Root + stationary (SetCombatMovement false).
Consume All when player >35yd (evade). Call Servant every 30s spawns 3 Wasting
Servants.
  Servants walk to (-494,-345,237) then wait to be consumed (heal Raal).

Lord & Lady Waycrest (DATA 3):
  Lord: Immune until player within 35yd. Lady: Rooted until Lord 31% HP.
At Lord 31% (HealthBelowPctDamaged): Vitality Transfer -> Lady heals Lord to
full,
  Lady loses 30% max HP per transfer (up to 3 transfers = 90% HP loss).
After 3rd transfer: Lady teleports to (-550,-249,185), becomes unrooted,
engages.
  Lady HP: 15,369,900 (MDT) - extremely high; Lord HP: 1,383,291.
  No further health thresholds.

Gorak Tul (DATA 4):
No health-based phases. At 40% HP (SmartAI event 2): Summon Deathtouched
Slaver.
  Dread Essence (266181) every 25s script / 25.5s LW / 25s DBM.
Deathtouched Slavers have own timers: Dark Leap 15s, Dread Bolt 2s, Death Lens
20s (H/M).

=== 4. ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS & EXACT TRIGGERS ===

HEARTSBANE TRIAD:
  - Unstable Runic Mark (260703): Random players (Malady stage).
    On expire -> Unstable Runic Mark Explosion (260702) on same player.
    Soak: spread 8yd. No assigned soak list in script.
  - Soul Manipulation (260907): Random target (Solena stage).
    Target gets SOUL_MANIPULATION_AURA (260926) + damage reduction.
Kick/interrupt: Solena casts SOUL_MANIPULATION_REDUCE_DAMAGE (260923) on self.
  - Dire Ritual (260773): AOE from sister holding iris. Dodge.
  - Auras (H/M only): Apathy/Thorns/Dread apply to all players in 100yd.
    No assigned dispel; heal through.

SOULBOUND GOLIATH:
- Soul Thorns (260551): Random player. Summons Soul Thorns at target location.
    Player must move out of 3yd radius before Thorns explodes (on death).
    No assigned player; personal responsibility.
  - Burning Brush (260541): Goliath gains aura, removes Soul Harvest.
    H/M: spawns 6 Burning Souls at fixed pos (-409,-261,234, o=3).
    Burning Souls chase random players (walk speed). Kill after 10s.

RAAL THE GLUTTONOUS:
  - Wasting Servants (133361): 3 spawn at fixed positions (L/M/R).
    Walk to (-494,-345,237). Raal consumes on melee -> CONSUME_SERVANT_BUFF.
    Assigned: DPS must kill servants before Raal reaches them.
  - Rotten Expulsion (264694): Targets all players with missile (264697).
    Spawns 5 Bile Oozelings at (-493,-328,236). Spread / dodge.
  - Consume All (264734): If any player >35yd, Raal casts -> evade wipe.
    Assigned: stay within 35yd of Raal at all times.

LORD & LADY WAYCREST:
  - Virulent Pathogen (261440): Random player. On expire ->
    Virulent Pathogen Exp (261441) + Contagious Remnants Missile (268385).
    Spread 8yd. No assigned soak.
  - Discordant Cadenza (268306): Lady channels, all players hit.
    Dodge: move to edge of room. No assigned positions.
  - Wracking Chord (268278): Random target. Tank swap not required.
  - Vitality Transfer: At Lord 31% HP (HealthBelowPctDamaged).
    Lady casts 261446 -> Lord full heal, Lady -30% max HP. Repeat x3.
    After 3rd: Lady teleports to (-550,-249,185, o=3.19), engages.

GORAK TUL:
  - Darkened Lightning (266225): Random target. Spread.
  - Dread Essence (266181): AOE. At 40% HP (SmartAI) also summons Slaver.
- Deathtouched Slavers: Must be killed. On Dread Essence, dead slavers
respawn.
    Assigned: DPS focus slavers. Dark Leap (farthest), Dread Bolt (random),
    Death Lens (H/M, farthest, 268202 + 266724 periodic).
  - Dread Essence Dispell (272829): Exists in enum, not used in script.
