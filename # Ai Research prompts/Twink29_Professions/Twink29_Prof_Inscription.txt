TWINK29 - PROFESSIONS - STEP 08 - INSCRIPTION
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Scope: what Inscription gives a level 29 battleground twink, skill line
773, capped at 225 for a character of that level.

1. THE FIRST THING THAT HAS TO BE SAID
   Inscription is Wrath of the Lich King content - patch 3.0, skill line
   773 - so it never existed in vanilla. In 8.3.7 the whole 1-300
   stretch is the "Classic Inscription" tier, spell 2514, and it sits
   beside the vanilla professions even though it postdates them.
   Ranks, from the trainer spells: 45358 learns Inscription, 45359 is
   the expert rank, 45360 the artisan rank, and 45376 is the journeyman
   step (its effect is "learn skill step 773, value 2"). The 1-225 band
   is therefore what a 29 has, and the artisan rank above it is ALT,
   exactly as with every other profession in this set.
   Two facts govern the whole file:
   - Nothing Inscription makes in the 1-225 band is wearable. No armour,
     no weapon, no off-hand, no trinket. Section 2 has the detail.
   - A 29 can make and use four things: stat scrolls, enchanting
     vellums, the Scroll of Recall, and the inks and pigments that feed
     all three.
   Tool: the Virtuoso Inking Set 39505, vendor stock, required by every
   recipe in this file. Paper is vendor stock as well - Light Parchment
   39354 at 12-15 copper from any Trade Supplies vendor (96 of them, in
   every capital and most level 1-30 zones), then Common Parchment
   10648 and Heavy Parchment 39501 as the route climbs. All three stack
   to 20 only, so buy in bulk and mail it to the twink.

2. NO WEARABLES AT 29 - EVERY SHOULDER INSCRIPTION IS ALT
   This is the part the prompt asks about, so it goes first. Every
   shoulder enchant Inscription makes is a use-on-your-own-armour item
   carrying a character level requirement of 80 or 85. A 29 can be
   handed one - the Wrath pair binds to account - and still cannot
   apply it.

   44133 Greater Inscription of the Axe - item level 80, binds to
        account, requires level 80. Use: permanently adds 40 attack
        power and 15 critical strike rating to shoulder armour, and the
        target item becomes soulbound. Enchant spell 59934. Sold by
        Lillehoff 32540, the Sons of Hodir quartermaster in the Storm
        Peaks (zone 67), cost 80. No reputation line on the item.
   50335 Greater Inscription of the Axe - same artwork, same 40 attack
        power and 15 critical strike, same level 80 requirement, but
        this one also needs The Sons of Hodir at Exalted. Cost 85.
        ALT twice over: level and a Northrend reputation.
   83006 Greater Tiger Fang Inscription - Mists of Pandaria, item level
        90, requires level 85, +200 Strength and +100 critical strike,
        skill 570 in the Pandaria tier. Sold in the Vale of Eternal
        Blossoms (zone 5840) and Orgrimmar.
   87580 Tiger Fang Inscription - the cheaper Pandaria version, level
        85, +120 Strength and +80 critical strike, skill 540.
   The bind-on-account heirloom idea therefore buys a 29 nothing. The
   consumable is account-bound, but the level 80 or 85 requirement is on
   the character who uses it, so heirloom shoulders on a 29 twink carry
   no inscription. There is no lower-level alternative: the 1-225 band
   contains no shoulder item of any kind.
   The rest of the profession's wearable output is equally out of reach.
   Darkmoon card trinkets require level 60 and up. The off-hand items
   Inscription begins making are Northrend recipes above skill 300 (the
   recipe list keeps a separate "offhand item recipes" section); their
   item ids were not opened for this file, so they are unverified, but
   every one of them is ALT on skill alone.

3. THE FOUR THINGS A 29 CAN USE
   3a. STAT SCROLLS - the buffs stop at rank II
   Each scroll costs one ink and one parchment and grants +5 to one
   stat for 30 minutes. Rank II is the last rank a 29 can use; rank III
   and above carry a level 30 or higher requirement.

   Item  Scroll                  Req  Effect  Spell  Skill
   1711  Scroll of Stamina II     15   +5 Sta  50612   75
   2290  Scroll of Intellect II   15   +5 Int  50598   75
   2289  Scroll of Strength II    15   +5 Str  58485   80
   1477  Scroll of Agility II     15   +5 Agi  58473   85
   Recipe for all four: 1x Midnight Ink 39774 + 1x Light Parchment
   39354. The Midnight Ink is verified on the Agility II page; the other
   three sit in the same 75-85 band, so their ink is unverified.
   Rank I exists too and is cheaper: Stamina 1180 (spell 45382, skill
   35), Intellect 955 (48114, skill 35), Strength 954 (58484, skill
   15), Agility 3012 (58472, skill 15). Their magnitudes were not read
   off the item pages, so the +values are unverified.
   Rank III and up, all ALT on the level requirement: Stamina III 4422,
   Intellect III 4419, Strength III 4426 and Agility III 4425 all
   require level 30, and Agility III is only +8 - one level and three
   points better than the rank a 29 already has. Rank IV requires level
   40 (Agility IV 10309, +10; Strength IV 10310; Intellect IV 10308;
   Stamina IV 10307). Rank VII, the last one before the Northrend
   breakdown, is +25 at level 70.
   A scroll does not stack with a class buff that grants the same stat,
   so on a mage or priest it is dead weight. It stacks with attack power
   buffs. Its real use is the group member who has no buffer, or a
   companion character with no spell list.

   3b. ENCHANTING VELLUM 38682 - the only money in the profession
   Spell 52739, Inscription 35, difficulty 35/75/87/100 on the 3.x
   recipe page; current live data for the same spell gives a 75 minimum.
   8.3.7 sits between the two, so the exact floor is unverified - 35 is
   the number the old route relies on and is the one used below.
   Reagents: 1x Moonglow Ink 39469 + 2x Light Parchment 39354.
   The vellum itself requires Enchanting 1 to use. An enchanter targets
   it instead of a weapon or a piece of armour and the enchantment is
   stored in the item, which can then be traded or sold. The 3.x text
   added "only able to absorb enchantments with no level restrictions";
   by 8.3.7 the item is called Enchanting Vellum and covers weapon and
   armour enchantments.
   This is the whole economic argument: step 06 lists seven enchantments
   a 29 enchanter can cast, and every one of them can be parked on a
   vellum and sold to players who have no enchanter.

   3c. SCROLL OF RECALL 37118 - a second hearthstone, scribes only
   Spell 48248, Inscription 35, difficulty 35/60/67/75, reagents
   1x Moonglow Ink 39469 + 1x Light Parchment 39354.
   The item requires Inscription 25 to use - a profession requirement,
   not a character level - returns the user to the hearthstone location
   on a 20 minute cooldown, and the 3.x description warns "results are
   unpredictable beyond level 40". A 29 is well under 40.
   This is the only Inscription item that changes how a 29 actually
   plays: a second hearth on a 20 minute timer for one ink and one
   parchment.

   3d. MINOR INSCRIPTION RESEARCH 61288 - a 20 hour cooldown at skill 75
   3 second cast, 20 hour cooldown, reagents 1x Moonglow Ink + 2x Light
   Parchment, effect "create random scrolls in an effort to discover new
   minor glyph recipes". Difficulty 75/125/137/150, so it is worth
   running from 75 onwards; it costs skill nothing and pays in recipes
   and scrolls.

4. GLYPHS - WHY THEY ARE NOT A COMBAT OPTION AT 8.3.7
   In the 3.x data the other half of the profession is glyphs: major and
   minor, spanning skill 1 to 425, each one ink plus one parchment. That
   is not what an 8.3.7 character has. Since Legion (7.0) glyphs are
   cosmetic - the major and minor combat glyphs were removed and nothing
   in the glyph item class grants stats any more. In 8.3.7 a glyph is a
   cosmetic item, so the answer to "which glyphs does a 29 want" is
   none.
   That is a statement about the 8.x glyph system, not something read
   off an 8.3.7 database page in this session: treat it as unverified
   against your core. If your core has restored the 3.x glyph tables,
   the classic minor glyphs (skill 75-200) become a sellable item and
   the major glyphs (skill 385 and up) are ALT.
   Either way glyphs remain the cheapest thing to craft, at one ink and
   one parchment, which is why the route in section 7 leans on them from
   skill 80 to 200.

8. ALT - WHAT A 29 CANNOT DO
   44133 and 50335 Greater Inscription of the Axe - level 80, and the
     second one needs Sons of Hodir Exalted.
   83006 Greater Tiger Fang and 87580 Tiger Fang Inscription - level 85,
     Pandaria tier skill 540-570.
   Darkmoon card trinkets - level 60 and up; the Northrend cards need
     skill 400+.
   Off-hand items - Northrend recipes above skill 300, ids unverified.
   Scrolls III to IX - level 30 and up. Agility IV 10309 is +10 at 40,
     Agility VII 43463 is +25 at 70.
   Every ink above Celestial, the Silvery and Northrend pigments, and
     the whole 301-450 range.
   45360 Artisan Inscription at skill 226 - past the 225 ceiling.
   The Battle for Azeroth tier - Kul Tiras and Zandalar inks, contracts
     and war-scrolls. Those recipes are learned from a Zandalar or Kul
     Tiras trainer by a level 110+ character, so ALT on both counts;
     not opened for this file, unverified.
   A higher-level crafter is not the answer either: only the vellum, the
     scrolls and the Scroll of Recall are tradeable and usable by a 29,
     and a higher-level scribe can make all three for him. Nobody can
     make a shoulder enchant a 29 can wear.

9. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE IT
   1. A 29 enchanter. This is the only combination that pays for itself:
      Enchanting for step 06's seven enchants, Inscription for the
      vellums that turn them into auction-house goods and for the Scroll
      of Recall. Nothing else in the profession is worth two slots.
   2. Nobody for combat. There is no wearable item anywhere in the
      1-225 band, so Inscription adds no armour, no weapon and no stat
      to a twink's own gear. Engineering (step 01), Blacksmithing (03)
      and Leatherworking (04) all give real gear at 29.
   3. Mage, priest, warlock. Scroll of Intellect II is the only self
      buff in the profession, it is +5, and it is beaten by their own
      buff the moment they have it. Take Inscription for the gold, not
      for the intellect.
   4. Warrior, rogue, hunter, shaman, druid, paladin. Scroll of Strength
      II and Scroll of Agility II are +5 for 30 minutes, which is fine
      for handing out and worthless as a reason to spend a profession
      slot. These classes want Engineering or Blacksmithing.
   5. The roster's supplier or companion. A companion character with
      Inscription is worth modelling for exactly two things: the +5 stat
      scroll it can hand the group, and the vellum and Scroll of Recall
      utility. Both are cheap; neither turns a fight.
   Last line: Inscription is the weakest of the ten professions at 29,
   and by a distance. Level it to 35 for the Scroll of Recall and the
   vellum, to 85 if the group wants scrolls, to 100 if the twink is the
   account's money maker, and stop there.
