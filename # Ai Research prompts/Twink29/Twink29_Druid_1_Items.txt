HOW TO READ THIS
   A row is SLOT | item | id | item level | required level | source | tag.
   V marks a row whose numbers were read on the item page. Rows without V
   come from a level 29 guide and were not opened, so ilvl and req are
   unverified there.
   One caveat covers every V row: the only pages with full classic stat
   blocks are WotLK-era (3.3.5). Where 8.3.7 re-itemised an item its item
   level can differ. No required level moved on any item that was checked.

1. TRUE FOR ALL FOUR SPECIFICATIONS
   Leather, so every slot is open from level 1.
   Random-enchant greens of the right suffix ("of the Bear", "of the
   Eagle", "of the Owl", "of the Monkey", "of Intellect", "of Stamina")
   are the cheap filler in almost every slot, and the suffix decides the
   spec. Re-read the level 29 row in the client; the pattern is stable
   even where the numbers are not.
   Boots: the Arathi Basin leather boots come in one version per level
   bracket. 20052 Highlander's Leather Boots and 20186 Defiler's Leather
   Boots are the level 58 versions and a 29 cannot wear either (V). The
   29-bracket ids are unverified - check the supply officer in Arathi.
   Heirlooms scale with the character, so they never go stale. At level 29
   Stained Shadowcraft Spaulders 42952 read: 82 armour, +7 Stamina, +6 hit
   rating, +20 attack power, item level shown as 1 (V).

6. THE THREE THINGS THAT DECIDE A 29 DRUID SET
   1. The weapon is a stat stick. The usual explanation is that cat and
      bear damage does not scale with weapon damage (not verified in the
      8.3.7 tables), so Crowd Pummeler 9449 is worn for +16 Strength and
      its 30 second haste button, not for the 29.0 dps. Caster specs take
      a staff for Intellect instead.
   2. Three world drops carry every list: Pathfinder Hat 15339, Cutthroat's
      Mantle 15140 and River Pride Choker 13087, all with a required level
      of 29 or lower (V). All three drop at under one percent, which is
      why the second option in each slot is a random-enchant green.
   3. Heirlooms fill shoulders and chest without a farm, and the shoulder
      alone is 82 armour, +7 Stamina, +6 hit, +20 attack power at 29 (V).
   2. Correction from the audit of this file: Raptor Hunter Tunic 4119
      was the strongest chest all along - item level 43, +16 Stamina, and
      no required level - and the two Arathi Basin boot ids the first
      draft gave are the level 58 versions.
