BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - HERBALISM GATHERING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
==========================================================================================

Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client) gathers with Herbalism on a
TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Every id below was read from a source this week; where
no readable source exists the text says "unverified" instead of guessing.

3. HOW LONG A GATHER TAKES
--------------------------
  Base cast time: 2 seconds (wowhead spell 2366, 2020-10-25 capture AND live
  page 2026-10-25; every 2658xx family page read also says "2 sec cast").
  Range: 5 yards, interact range. Global cooldown: 0. Mana/resource cost: none.
  Disruption: the cast can be interrupted by damage while it runs - the
  wow-professions BfA and Legion guides both warn "mobs can still interrupt your
  casting" and recommend Monel-Hardened Stirrups spell 253112, Coarse Leather
  Barding item 154166, or a tank spec to avoid it.
  Glove enchants: Enchant Gloves - Kul Tiran Herbalism item 153430, Enchant
  Gloves - Zandalari Herbalism item 159464 (and Legion's 128558). The
  wow-professions guides say these bring gathering time "down from 1.5 seconds
  to 0.5 seconds"; that 1.5 conflicts with the 2-second spell data above, so the
  guide's numbers are recorded but marked unverified - the spell pages are the
  primary source and they say 2 seconds for 8.3.7-era data.
  Mounted gathering: the 2020-10-25 capture of spell 2366 shows the flag "Can be
  used while mounted", while the wow-professions BfA guide sells stirrups and the
  Sky Golem item 95416 as things that "let you gather herbs while mounted".
  The two sources disagree; treat baseline mounted gathering on 8.3.7 as
  unverified and let the server test decide.

5. HOW THE SKILL RISES (SERVER RULES)
--------------------------------------
  - The required skill of a node comes from the lock of its gameobject
    (client Lock.db2 via Spell::CanOpenLock, TrinityCore master, read
    2026-10-25). Skill too low below that value returns
    SPELL_FAILED_LOW_CASTLEVEL; every Herb Gathering spell adds its effect
    value (+1275) to the check, which is why a skill-1 character can pick any
    herb (matches the wiki's post-6.0.2 statement).
  - Bands around the required value: yellow = red + 30, green = yellow + 30,
    grey = green + 30; a gameobject_template can override yellow and grey via
    its trivial-skill low/high fields, and then green is the midpoint
    (Player::UpdateGatherSkill, TrinityCore master).
  - Chance per colour comes from worldserver.conf: SkillChance.Orange = 100,
    SkillChance.Yellow = 75, SkillChance.Green = 25, SkillChance.Grey = 0;
    SkillGain.Gathering = 1 point per success (worldserver.conf.dist read
    2026-10-25). Herbalism has NO chance decay with skill - only mining and
    skinning have SkillChance.MiningSteps/SkinningSteps (both = 0 in the conf
    read, i.e. decay off by default there; your fork's conf may differ,
    unverified).
  - One skill-up per character per node GUID until the node respawns
    (EffectOpenLock "Allow one skill-up until respawned", per-GUID skill-up
    list on the gameobject) - a party cannot chain skill-ups off one node.
  - The cap belongs to the child line learned (section 1); the routine refuses
    to raise a skill at its maximum.

6. HOW NODES RESPAWN
--------------------
  - A harvested node runs the gameobject loot-state machine: use with the
    gather spell -> GO_ACTIVATED (lootable) -> after the loot is taken
    GO_JUST_DEACTIVATED (GameObject.cpp, TrinityCore master, read 2026-10-25).
  - On deactivation the node schedules m_respawnTime = now + delay, where the
    delay is gameobject.spawntimesecs of its spawn row (the code's constructor
    default before load is 300 s). Respawn checks are not free-running:
    Respawn.MinCheckIntervalMS = 5000 in worldserver.conf.dist;
    Respawn.DynamicMode = 0 by default (a non-zero mode scales the delay by
    map activity).
  - At timer expiry: a non-pooled node is simply put back on the map; a pooled
    node instead calls PoolMgr::UpdatePool, and the pool re-rolls which of its
    members spawns next - explicit chance rows from pool_members (chance,
    pool id) under pool_template limits (maxobjects). That is why picking a
    node can make a different spot of the same zone light up instead of the
    same bush.
  - Every number here (each node's spawntimesecs, every pool's chance and
    limit, linked respawns) lives only in your world database; the client
    build 35662 ships no spawn rows, coordinates or respawn times, so all
    actual values are unverified from client sources.

9. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------
  1. Cannot see nodes: gameobject spawn coordinates, per-zone spawn lists,
     respawn times and pool layouts are world-database rows; build 35662
     carries none of it. The companion must read them from your DB - there is
     no client-side source to fall back on.
  2. Cannot use Find Herbs 2383 for anything: the spell's whole effect is the
     client minimap overlay. With no client the tracking spell buys the
     companion nothing; route from DB positions instead.
  3. Cannot receive input-driven casts: no CMSG_CAST_SPELL arrives from a
     player. The bot must start the 2-second Herb Gathering cast itself
     (server-side spell invocation) with the node guid as target, within the
     5-yard interact range, out of combat restrictions it has to model alone.
  4. Cannot watch the cast bar or react to interrupts through UI: damage can
     interrupt the 2-second cast (guide warning above); the bot needs its own
     threat/interrupt handling or the cast fails silently into a recast loop.
  5. Cannot loot through a window: the OPEN_LOCK effect runs GameObject::Use
     and opens loot server-side; the companion must move items out of the
     node's loot storage itself.
  6. Cannot re-farm one node for skill-ups: the per-GUID skill-up list grants
     at most one skill point per character until the node respawns - a bot
     that loops the same bush gains nothing.
  7. Cannot skin herb corpses unless the fork implements it: no
     herbalism-on-corpse path was found in TrinityCore master's spell effects
     read for this file; the wiki's 5 x level rule describes retail behavior.
     unverified on your core.
  8. Cannot trust mounted-gather flags blindly: sources disagree (section 3);
     a mounted companion should be tested before being relied on.
  9. Cannot exceed the cap or beat the grey band: child-line maximums and
     SkillChance.Grey = 0 are enforced in the skill routines, so no amount of
     node cycling pushes a bar past its cap.
