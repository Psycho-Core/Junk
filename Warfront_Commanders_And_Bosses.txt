CHOICE RULE
Stromgarde Horde attacker: one of three ALLIANCE final commanders — Muradin, High Exarch Turalyon, Danath. Stromgarde Alliance attacker: one of three HORDE final commanders — Lady Liadrin, Rokhan, Eitrigg. Icy Veins' January 2020 achievement overview explicitly says only ONE enemy commander appears per battle. Whether choice is random per instance, fixed by a weekly/warfront cycle, linked to allied leader, weighted, independently chosen on Heroic, and server seed/ScriptEvent: UNVERIFIED. Do not say 'equal 1/3 odds' or spawn all three. Darkshore Horde final enemy is Maiev; Darkshore Alliance final enemy is Sira, based on contemporary normal play accounts, NOT proven to rotate among the six Stromgarde commanders. The opposite faction's named Darkshore commander can be an allied escort/speaker. Specific selection mechanism and heroic variants unverified.
