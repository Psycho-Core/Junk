CORRECTED (applied with this audit):
 1. Beth'tilac does NOT descend at "~20% health". She
    descends after her THIRD Smoldering Devastation
    (page). Spiderling handling also sharpened: they
    are Drone food (100634 Consume = +20% heal), and
    exploding broodlings are heroic-only Engorged
    Broodlings, not the normal adds.
 2. Alysrazor "miss 3 rings and you fall" was invented.
    98619 Wings of Flame last 30 sec; each ring passed
    REFRESHES them and stacks 99461 Blazing Power (to
    25). You fall when wings expire, not on a count.
    Hatchlings also corrected: they imprint on the
    nearest player (who tanks with +1000% damage -
    page), not generic "two tanks".
 3. Baleroc's spark economy was attributed backwards:
    soakers gain Torment/Tormented, never Vital Spark.
    HEALERS gain Vital Spark by healing the Tormented
    (1 per 3 stacks) and convert it via a direct heal
    on the Blaze of Glory tank into 99263 Vital Flame.
    Decimation Blade now exact (99353: 90% of max, min
    250000, unmitigable but dodgeable). Countdown is
    heroic-only - the file implied it was general.
 4. Staghelm swap logic was inverted: swapping does
    NOT dodge his 100-energy special and does not
    "reset energy as a favour" - he casts the special
    AT 100 in-form; you endure as many as healing
    allows (era 5-7) and swap late, because each swap
    permanently stacks Fury (+8% specials) while only
    resetting Adrenaline. Burning Orbs/Searing Seeds
    (every third change) are normal mode, not heroic.
