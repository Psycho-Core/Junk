WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 571 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: all 52 resolved. 43 come from the research pass whose Wowhead lookup returned the exact master name for
  8.3.7, and 9 were matched on the exact NPC name against the creature_template exports (the 4.3.4 TDB update pack and the 8.x
  creature_template dump from the same server repository). Every entry here is a digit string. Two of the 52 names carry a
  second creature_template row: Penumbrius (31069 printed, 30227 also present) and Nethestrasz (26851 printed, 57287 also
  present). Nethestrasz needed a real decision because both ids are live Wowhead NPCs with that name: 26851 is the
  Dragonblight <Flight Master> and 57287 is a Dragon Soul encounter added in 4.3.0, so the block prints 26851 and says so.
  Penumbrius 31069 is the one Wowhead shows as the Icecrown <Flight Master>.
  One of the 43 Wowhead-verified entries, Adeline Chambers at Vengeance Landing, is not in either creature_template export under
  that name at all; 27344 is what the 8.3.7 lookup returns, and Wowhead files that NPC as <Bat Handler>, Howling Fjord, faction
  The Hand of Vengeance. The block prints the entry and says all of this under the master note.
  Gate evidence: one of the 52 nodes is gated and 51 are not. ConditionID 7376 sits on 309 Nesingwary Base Camp and decodes as
  "after quest 12523", which Wowhead names "Have a Part, Give a Part", a Sholazar Basin quest that starts and ends with Weslex
  Quickwrench at that same camp. No Northrend taxi node is gated on an achievement or on reputation, and no other node
  carries any ConditionID at all.
  Faction: TaxiNodes.Flags gives 13 Alliance-only roosts, 16 Horde-only roosts and 23 with both bits set; each block prints the
  bits it actually found rather than inferring a side from the name.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Every one of the 304 directed rows in section 3 targets another
  node of this same set, so no leg in this file carries an off-map tag. Distances are per direction: the row 308 -> 309 reads
  1277 yd while the row 309 -> 308 reads 1519 yd, and each block prints its own direction rather than reusing the other one.
  Sources: TaxiNodes and TaxiPath exports for build 8.3.7.35662, the PlayerCondition decode of ConditionID 7376, the Warcraft Wiki
  subzone page that places the River's Heart master at Lakeside Landing, Wowhead pages for Adeline Chambers (27344), Aludane
  Whitecloud (28674), Helidan Lightwing (33849), Nethestrasz (26851 and 57287), Penumbrius (31069), Derek Rammel (26877), The
  Spirit of Gnomeregan (28037) and quest 12523, and the creature_template exports named above. Everything read from a source is
  printed; nothing was estimated.
