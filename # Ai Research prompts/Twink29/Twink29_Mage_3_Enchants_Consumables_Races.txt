TWINK29 - MAGE - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29). ALT = needs a higher-level crafter or content.

1. THREE THINGS THAT SHAPE A MAGE'S SHOPPING LIST
   Mana is real. A mage is the one class in this set where mana potions,
   mana food and mana regeneration actually matter, and it is the class
   that can conjure its own water, so half the supply problem solves
   itself out of combat.
   A wand is a stat stick. Wands have no auto-shoot role worth planning
   around in BfA; the wand slot exists to carry Intellect, and 13063
   Starfaller is the only rare one in the bracket.
   The verified enchant list in this set is melee-shaped. That is the
   single most useful thing this file can tell a mage, and it gets its own
   paragraph below.

2. THE CLASSIC ENCHANTS, AND WHY MOST OF THEM ARE WRONG FOR A MAGE
   The seven enchants the professions set verified at 225 or below are the
   same seven for every class. Six of them were chosen for a melee build:
     CLOAK   13882 Enchant Cloak - Lesser Agility, +3 Agility, skill 225,
             formula 11206, 2x Lesser Nether Essence 11174.
     CLOAK   13746 Enchant Cloak - Greater Defense, +50 armor, skill 205,
             3x Vision Dust 11137.
     CHEST   13640 Enchant Chest - Greater Health, +35 health, skill 160,
             3x Soul Dust 11083.
     BRACERS 13661 Enchant Bracer - Strength, +5 Strength, skill 180. A
             mage gets nothing from Strength. Do not buy it.
     GLOVES  13815 Enchant Gloves - Agility, +5 Agility, skill 210.
             Agility is dodge and armor for a cloth wearer - not nothing,
             but not the point.
     BOOTS   13890 Enchant Boots - Minor Speed, skill 225, 1x Small
             Radiant Shard 11177, 1x Aquamarine 7909, 1x Lesser Nether
             Essence 11174. The one enchant on the list that is right for
             every class, and for a caster it is the best of the seven:
             distance is a mage's armor.
     WEAPON  13693 Enchant Weapon - Striking, +3 weapon damage, skill 195.
             Useless to a mage - a mage's weapon damage is not the
             rotation.
   So the honest answer for the classic tier is two enchants: boots with
   Minor Speed, chest with Greater Health. The Intellect and mana variants
   of the bracer, chest and weapon enchants exist somewhere in the classic
   book, but none was verified for this set, so no id is quoted. Read the
   enchanting trainer's window in game and take an Intellect bracer and a
   spell power weapon enchant if the server has them at 225.
   Weapon oil is verified and is the caster's weapon enchant at this tier:
     20744 Minor Wizard Oil, requires level 5, +8 spell power for 1 hour,
     5 charges, Enchanting 45, spell 25124, cannot be applied to an item
     above item level 165. Every staff and sword a 29 can hold qualifies.
     20750 Wizard Oil needs level 40. ALT
   Heads and shoulders take nothing: no head arcanum inside the 225 cap,
   and the Mists shoulder inscriptions 83006, 87580, 44133 and 50335 need
   level 80 or 85 on the character who applies them.

3. NECK AND RING - TWO SLOTS, BOTH ALT, BOTH LEGION
   Each carries an item level cap and a level 29 item at item level 33 or
   less passes it.
   NECK   190894 Mark of the Hidden Satyr, enchant id 5439. Sometimes
          summons a satyr that fires a nightmare bolt. Cap: "cannot be
          applied to items above level 120". 15x Chaos Crystal 124442,
          12x Leylight Shard 124441, Runed Enchanting Rod. The pick for
          Fire and Arcane, where the fight is a race.
         190892 Mark of the Claw. Sometimes +critical strike and +haste for
          6 sec. The safer pick for Frost, because a Frost mage's damage
          is spread over more casts and a crit window feeds Shatter.
         228405 Mark of the Trained Soldier. Mastery, which a level 29
          does not have. Do not buy it.
   RING   190871 Binding of Haste rank 1, 191014 rank 3, item form 128542.
          Flat Haste on a ring, capped at "items above level 60". Two
          rings, two enchants, and this is the largest stat gain available
          to a mage at 29 - casters scale hardest off haste at low level.
         The Critical Strike and Versatility versions of the same family
         exist; their ids were not read. There is no Intellect ring enchant
         in that family as far as these pages show, so a caster's ring
         enchant is a secondary stat and nothing else.

4. ARMOR KITS, AND THE SLOT THEY COMPETE WITH
   4265 Heavy Armor Kit, +24 armor, item level 15 and up, no character
   level gate, crafted at Leatherworking 150 (spell 3780, 5x Heavy Leather
   4234). 8173 Thick (+32, level 30) and 15564 Rugged (+40, level 40) are
   ALT.
   A kit and an enchant occupy the same enhancement slot on the same item,
   so one piece takes one or the other. For a mage the split should be
   kits on the LEGS, where no cloth leg item or leg enchant exists anyway,
   and enchants everywhere else. Whether this server enforces the conflict
   is unverified; test it on one piece first.

5. CONSUMABLES A 29 MAGE CAN USE
   POTIONS - one shared cooldown, one per fight.
     2459  Swiftness Potion        req 5   +50% speed 15 sec. A mage's
           escape, and the reason a mage carries more of these than of
           anything else.
     1710  Greater Healing Potion  req 21  455-585 health
     5634  Free Action Potion      req 20  stun and root immunity 30 sec
     3385  Lesser Mana Potion      req 14  the only mana potion at 29.
           Greater Mana Potion 6149 needs level 31. ALT
   ELIXIRS - one hour, lost on death.
     6373  Elixir of Firepower       req 18  +10 fire spell damage, 30 min.
           Fire only, and it is the only spell-school elixir verified at
           this tier.
     8951  Elixir of Greater Defense req 29  +250 armor
     3825  Elixir of Fortitude       req 25  +120 health
     8949  Elixir of Agility         req 27  dodge and armor for a cloth
           wearer - marginal.
   PROTECTION POTIONS - one hour, one school each.
     6051 Holy req 10, 6048 Shadow req 17, 6049 Fire req 23, 6050 Frost
     req 28, 6052 Nature req 28.
   SCROLLS - 30 minutes, usable on another character. Only one scroll
   effect can be active at a time - the item page says so in as many words -
   and a scroll is not an elixir, so the two may stack.
     955  Scroll of Intellect, +3 Intellect, no required level, spell 8096.
          The caster scroll at this tier. Verified.
     1478 Scroll of Protection II, +105 armor, requires level 15, spell
          8094. Verified, and the only other scroll a cloth wearer wants.
     A Scroll of Intellect II exists - the next rank up - but its item id
     was not found and is unverified. 1477 Scroll of Agility II and 2289
     Scroll of Strength II are verified and both wrong for a mage.
   FOOD - well fed, 15 minutes, ten seconds seated.
     12210 Roast Raptor req 25, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit. The Stamina is
     real; the Spirit half is very likely wasted, because Spirit does not
     drive mana regeneration in Battle for Azeroth as far as these pages
     show - unverified.
     A mage's own conjured food and water are the practical answer, and
     conjuring costs nothing.
   BANDAGES - no profession, no level, 60 second cooldown, usable in
   arenas. 8544 Mageweave Bandage heals 1600 over 8 sec; 6451 Heavy Silk
   heals 1280. A mage who has spent mana is a mage who needs one.

6. THE TWO PROFESSIONS
   Engineering first, and unlike the hunter this one gives a mage a real
   armour slot as well as the toys.
     4385 Green Tinted Goggles, cloth head, item level 30, 35 armor, +8
          Stamina and +7 Spirit, skill 150, spell 3956. Rare quality and
          inside the item rule, so this is a legal blue head for a mage
          and it beats a green. Make 4368 Flying Tiger Goggles first (skill
          100, spell 3934) because they are the reagent.
     4390 Iron Grenade, skill 175, 132-218 Fire and a 3 sec stun in 3
          yards. A mage adds a stun to a class that has none.
     4394 Big Iron Bomb, skill 190, the 5 yard version.
     4388 Discombobulator Ray, skill 160, spell 3959. Turns the target
          into a leper gnome for 12 sec: -40 melee and spell damage, -20%
          movement, forces a dismount. The strongest item debuff a 29 can
          apply, and it answers the melee that closes on a mage.
     4397 Gnomish Cloaking Device, skill 200, spell 3971. 10 sec of
          invisibility on a 1 hour cooldown, and it needs no
          specialisation. A second escape on top of Blink.
     10518 Parachute Cloak, skill 225. Item level 45, so outside the item
          rule and worth nothing to a caster's stats, but the slow fall
          crosses battleground gaps. A toy, not gear.
     10720 Gnomish Net-o-Matic Projector, skill 210 and Gnomish
          Engineering, so a 29 cannot make one. ALT - buy it from a
          higher-level engineer. It roots a target, which is the one thing
          a mage's kit lacks against a runner.
     109076 Goblin Glider Kit is auction house only; the recipes behind it
          are Draconic-to-Legion engineering. ALT
   Second slot:
     Tailoring - bags, and the bandages a mana class actually uses. The
       cloth crafts inside the classic tier are not gear a 29 wants, so
       this is a utility slot.
     Alchemy - 3385 and 2459 for a whole bot roster, and the elixirs.
     Enchanting - disenchanting and the service; no self-only perk.
     Not worth it: Jewelcrafting has nothing to socket at 29, Inscription
     has nothing wearable inside the cap, Blacksmithing is stones for
     weapons a mage should not be swinging, and the three gathering trades
     are for supplier characters.

7. RACES THAT CAN BE A MAGE
   Alliance: human, dwarf, gnome, night elf, draenei, worgen, void elf,
   dark iron dwarf, kul tiran, mechagnome, lightforged draenei. Horde:
   orc, troll, undead, blood elf, tauren, goblin, nightborne, zandalari
   troll, mag'har orc, highmountain tauren, vulpera. Pandaren on either
   side. Every allied race starts at level 20, so all of them are legal 29
   twinks. The Horde and Alliance full lists beyond the classic races are
   from memory of the race roster, not from a page opened this turn -
   unverified.
   GNOME - the Alliance pick for a mage.
     20589 Escape Artist, removes movement-impairing effects. A root break
           that is not the PvP trinket, which matters because the one PvP
           slot a 29 has is spent on a trinket talent anyway.
     20591 Expansive Mind, maximum mana increased. The only racial in the
           game that gives a caster a bigger mana pool.
     20593 Engineering Specialization, 20592 Arcane Resistance, 92680
           Nimble Fingers. 154744, 154746, 154747 and 227057 are other
           versions of Expansive Mind - read which one the spellbook has.
   HUMAN - the other Alliance pick, and the better one in a stun-heavy
     bracket.
     59752 Will to Survive, the human crowd-control break. It does not
           share a cooldown with the PvP trinket talent, so a human mage
           effectively carries two breaks. The exact 8.3.7 effect text was
           not read - unverified whether it clears stuns only or more.
     20598 The Human Spirit, a Spirit passive. Worth little if Spirit does
           nothing for mana, which is the open question above.
   BLOOD ELF - the Horde pick.
     50613 Arcane Torrent, the mage version: silence nearby enemies and
           restore mana. A silence and a mana refill on one button, on the
           class that wants both.
           The other ids in that family - 25046, 28730, 69179, 80483,
           129597, 155145, 202719, 232633 - are one per class version;
           which one a hunter or a mage receives is unverified.
     154742 Arcane Acuity, 28877 Arcane Affinity, 822 Arcane Resistance.
   UNDEAD - 7744 Will of the Forsaken breaks fear, charm and sleep, and a
     feared mage is a dead mage. 5227 Touch of the Grave, 20577
     Cannibalize, 20579 Shadow Resistance.
   TROLL - 26297 Berserking, a haste cooldown that stacks with everything
     a caster does. 20557 Beast Slaying, 20555 Regeneration, 58943 Da
     Voodoo Shuffle. Ids for the last three unverified.
   NIGHT ELF - 58984 Shadowmeld drops a mage out of combat standing still,
     which cancels a cast and can reset a fight. 20582 Quickness, 21009
     Elusiveness, 20583 Nature Resistance, 154748 Touch of Elune (+1%
     haste at night or +1% crit by day).
   WORGEN - 68992 Darkflight, +40% movement for 10 sec; 68975 Viciousness,
     +1% crit; 68976 Aberration. A second escape next to Blink.
   DWARF - 20594 Stoneform removes bleed, poison and disease and reduces
     damage taken. 59224, 20596, 92682. Nothing caster-specific.
   DRAENEI - 28880 Gift of the Naaru heals over time, the only racial heal
     a mage can get. 6562 Heroic Presence, 28875 Gemcutting, 59221 Shadow
     Resistance - ids unverified.
   TAUREN - 20549 War Stomp, 20550 Endurance, 154743 Brawn, 20552
     Cultivation, 20551 Nature Resistance. The stun has a 0.5 sec cast and
     a mage under pressure cannot afford it.
   ORC - 21563 Command, 20572 Blood Fury, 20573 Hardiness: stun duration
     reduced. The one orc racial a cloth wearer keeps, and it is a decent
     one.
   Everything else - void elf, dark iron dwarf, kul tiran, mechagnome,
     lightforged draenei, nightborne, zandalari troll, mag'har orc,
     highmountain tauren, vulpera, goblin, pandaren - can be a mage and
     none of their racial ids was opened for this file. Unverified.

8. SPECS AT LEVEL 29
   All three are open and one is chosen at level 10.
     Arcane    the burst spec, and the one whose level 15 row is passive.
     Fire      the crit spec. Firestarter covers the opening of every
               engagement, which is most of what a battleground bot sees.
     Frost     the control spec, and the spec that best uses Ice Nova from
               the level 15 row.
   No forms, stances or pets except the Frost mage's Water Elemental,
   whose level requirement at 29 is unverified.

9. THE SHORT VERSION
   Gnome or human on the Alliance, blood elf or undead on the Horde. Put
   Minor Speed on the boots, Greater Health on the chest, Minor Wizard Oil
   on the staff, Hidden Satyr on the neck and Haste on both rings. Carry
   Swiftness Potions, Lesser Mana Potions, Greater Healing Potions and
   Mageweave Bandages, and drink your own conjured water between fights.
   Take Engineering for the goggles, the grenades and the ray.
