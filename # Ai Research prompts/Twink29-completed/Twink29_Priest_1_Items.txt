TWINK29 - PRIEST - STEP 1 OF 3 - ITEMS FOR EVERY SLOT
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
RULE FOR THIS FILE: cloth armour, rare (blue) or bind-on-account, item
level 35 or less, required level 26 to 29. Weapons, wands, jewellery,
off-hand frills and trinkets follow the same quality and level window.
SLOT|item|id|ilvl|req|source|tag    tag: SOLO ALT RNG
V = name, item level, required level, stats and source read off an item
tooltip or a search table. Anything not read says unverified.
Searches run for this file, rare quality, required level 26-29:
cloth 19 rows, cloaks 11, amulets 9, rings 18 (opened to level 24),
off-hand frills 7 (opened to 20), wands 2, staves 5, daggers 5,
one-hand maces 3. Values read on the pre-squish item database, which is
the item level and required level 8.3.7 still uses.
One caveat that covers four rows: 7712, 7708, 65966 and 55928 carry hit
rating. Hit was taken off gear in a later patch and what 8.3.7 turned it
into is unverified. Every other stat line below is Int, Spirit, Stamina
or crit and needs no conversion.

0. THE POOL IS BIGGER THAN THE MAGE FILE ASSUMED
   A priest wears cloth and cloth is the best served armour type at 29:
   nineteen rare rows, and two of them fill slots the mage file wrote off
   as empty (V).
     LEGS  7709 Blighted Leggings, 31/26, +8 Stamina +11 Intellect,
           +8 crit. Azshir the Sleepless 6490 in the Scarlet Monastery.
     BACK  19533 Caretaker's Cape for the Alliance and 19529 Battle
           Healer's Cloak for the Horde, both 33/28, +6 Stamina +6
           Intellect +4 Spirit, both from the Warsong Gulch vendor.
   What is genuinely missing is a second blue leg and a neck with a real
   caster line on it. The heirloom leg does exist - 62029 Tattered
   Dreadmist Leggings - and it is the only alternative to 7709 (V).
   The other gap is the mace. A priest can hold one, and the bracket has
   three blue one-hand maces, but none of them carries Intellect:
   7752 Dreamslayer is +7 Strength, 7687 Ironspine's Fist is +3 Agility
   +5 Stamina and 13048 Looming Gavel is +3 Agility +6 Stamina (V). A
   priest's weapon is a staff, a dagger or a wand, not a mace.

4. THE THREE SPECS
   DISCIPLINE  Section 1 as written, staff 19573 or 19569 in hand, orb
     7685 as the swap if a dagger is used. Disc heals off damage, so the
     Intellect stack matters more than Spirit: 7713 Illusionary Rod and
     the 7714 plus 7685 pair are both defensible here.
   HOLY  The Spirit build: 19573 or 19569, 19525 ring, 19533 cloak,
     65914 hood and 7711 robe. That is the highest Spirit total a 29
     priest can assemble, and it is the only spec that wants all of it.
   SHADOW  Same cloth. Shadow wants Intellect and crit, so 7713 plus
     7712 Mantle of Doan and 7709 legs. The one Shadow-specific item in
     the window is not gear and is not here: it is in step 3.

6. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   Every slot is covered by at least one blue except the second ring,
   which is covered by the same three rings the first ring uses. That is
   a much better position than the paladin, the hunter or the monk is in,
   and it is entirely because the priest wears cloth.
   The chain that decides a priest twink is the Spirit set: 19573 staff,
   19525 ring, 19533 cloak, 65914 hood and 7711 robe, all five farmable
   by a 29 alone except the cloak, which needs Warsong Gulch marks.
   The two things a 29 priest cannot get and would want are a neck with
   Intellect and Spirit on it (55928 has Intellect only) and a second
   rare leg. Neither is in the window, so neither is invented here; the
   heirloom leg 62029 is the substitute, not a second blue.
