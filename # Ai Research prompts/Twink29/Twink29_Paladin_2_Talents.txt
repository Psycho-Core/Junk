TWINK29 - PALADIN - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
V = read off a wiki page in this turn. Unverified means unverified.

0. THE SHORT ANSWER
   A level 29 paladin has ONE normal talent and ONE PvP talent.
   One normal talent because the second row opens at 30, and the second row
   is where Crusader's Judgment, Hammer of Wrath and Cavalier sit.
   One PvP talent because the PvP pane gives its first slot at level 20 and
   its second at 40.
   Which PvP talent that slot can hold depends on the spec, and for Holy the
   answer is "not verified" rather than "none" - see section 6.

1. WHICH SPECIALIZATIONS ARE OPEN AT 29
   All three. Holy, Protection and Retribution are chosen at level 10 and
   every one of them is legal at 29. Each has its own talent tree and its
   own PvP talent list, so there are three different answers to every
   question below.

2. THE NORMAL TREE AT 29
   Rows open at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and 100, one point per row, and the
   row opens on level alone - nothing has to be spent in the row above.
   Two patch notes pin the shape of the tree in this era:
     Zeal, 8.0.1: "Redesigned as a Passive level 15 talent (was level 30)".
     Bestow Faith, 10.0.0: "Now a row 3 Holy talent (was a level 15 Holy
     talent)", which is the note that tells us row 1 was still level 15 at
     the end of BfA.
   So at 29: ROW 1 OPEN, one pick. ROW 2 at 30 CLOSED, one level away.
   ROWS 3 to 7 at 45 to 100 CLOSED.
   ROW 2, for reference, is the row a 29 misses:
     Holy  Cavalier, Unbreakable Spirit, Blessing of Spellwarding
     Prot  First Avenger, Crusader's Judgment, Bastion of Light
     Ret   Fires of Justice, Blade of Wrath, Hammer of Wrath

3. ROW 1 - HOLY (three options)
   CRUSADER'S MIGHT, spell 196926 (V)
     Crusader Strike reduces the cooldown of Holy Shock and Judgment.
   BESTOW FAITH, spell 223306 (V)
     12 sec cooldown, instant, heals a friendly target for a large amount
     after 5 seconds. In BfA it did not generate Holy Power - that was added
     on 9.0.1 (V).
   LIGHT'S HAMMER, spell id unverified
     A hammer on the ground that pulses damage and healing in an area.
   PICK: CRUSADER'S MIGHT.
     A Holy paladin's throughput at 29 is Holy Shock, and this is the only
     talent in the row that shortens its cooldown. It is passive, so a bot
     cannot get it wrong.
   SECOND CHOICE: BESTOW FAITH, for a bot that is expected to keep one
     tank alive rather than heal a group, because it adds an instant heal to
     a spec that otherwise has none at 29.

4. ROW 1 - PROTECTION (three options)
   HOLY SHIELD, spell 152261 (id from a Legion-era talent list, unverified
     for 8.3.7)
     Increases block chance, lets the paladin block spells, and deals Holy
     damage to anything it blocks.
   REDOUBT, spell id unverified
     The Avenger's Shield / block-and-stamina option.
   BLESSED HAMMER, spell 204019 (id from the same Legion-era list,
     unverified for 8.3.7)
     Replaces Hammer of the Righteous with a thrown hammer that hits in a
     spiral and reduces the damage of the next attack from everything it
     hits.
   PICK: BLESSED HAMMER.
     A Protection twink at 29 tanks other twinks, and Blessed Hammer is the
     one option that adds area damage and area damage reduction at the same
     time - it is the only pick that helps a bot holding three attackers.
   SECOND CHOICE: HOLY SHIELD, when the enemy team is casters, because
     blocking spells is the one thing no other row-1 talent gives.

5. ROW 1 - RETRIBUTION (three options)
   ZEAL, spell 269569 (V)
     Passive: Judgment empowers the next 2 auto attacks to swing 30% faster
     and hit for extra Holy damage.
   RIGHTEOUS VERDICT, spell id unverified
     Templar's Verdict increases the damage of the next Templar's Verdict.
   EXECUTION SENTENCE, spell 213757 (id from a Legion-era list, unverified
     for 8.3.7)
     1 min cooldown, 30 yd: a hammer falls and the target takes a large
     amount of Holy damage after the delay.
   PICK: ZEAL.
     It is passive, it needs no target selection, and a 29 Retribution
     paladin lands more auto attacks than anything else. For a bot this is
     the zero-management option that also happens to be the BfA pick for
     sustained damage.
   SECOND CHOICE: EXECUTION SENTENCE, for a bot built to burst one target
     in a team fight or a duel.

6. PVP TALENTS - THE SLOT, AND WHAT FITS IN IT
   The PvP pane is SEPARATE from the normal tree - the normal row and the
   PvP slot are two independent picks.
   Four slots in BfA, opening at 20, 40, 70 and 110 (V, from the PvP talent
   page read earlier in this set). At 29 that is ONE slot, opened at 20.
   Individual PvP talents carry their own level requirement, and for this
   class that gate is the whole story:
   PROTECTION
     STEED OF GLORY, spell 199542 (V)
       Level 20. Divine Steed lasts 2 sec longer, makes the paladin immune
       to crowd control while it is up, and knocks back anything it rides
       through. 8.0.1 converted it "from a rank 20 honor talent to a PvP
       talent" and no later patch changed its level, so 20 is the BfA
       number (V, and the "rank 20" in that note is the corroboration).
     PICK: 199542. It is the only paladin PvP talent verified available at
       29, and it is the best of them for a battleground anyway - a
       crowd-control-immune rush that scatters a group.
   RETRIBUTION
     BLESSING OF SANCTUARY, spell 210256 (V)
       Level 20. Removes stun, silence, fear and horror from a friendly
       target and cuts the duration of those effects by 60% for 5 sec on a
       45 sec cooldown in BfA (it was raised to 1 min in 2025). 8.0.1
       converted it from a rank 25 honor talent (V).
     PICK: 210256. On a 29 this is a second trinket on a friendly target,
       which is worth more than any damage talent in the bracket.
   HOLY
     NOTHING VERIFIED. Cleanse the Weak is the obvious candidate and it is
     the wrong answer: 9.0.1 changed it to "available at level 27 (was 60)",
     so on 8.3.7 it is a level 60 talent and a 29 cannot take it (V). The
     rest of the Holy list - Avenging Light, Divine Plea, Luminescence,
     Shining Revelation, Searing Glare - was not opened for this file, so no
     level and no id is quoted. A 29 Holy paladin should be assumed to have
     the slot and nothing legal to put in it until a page says otherwise.

7. TALENTS THAT LOOK AVAILABLE AND ARE NOT
   DENOUNCE. It is in the paladin PvP talent category, so it looks legal.
   Its own patch notes say it was removed on 7.0.3 and only re-added on
   10.1.5 as a level 30 talent (V). It does not exist on 8.3.7 at all.
   CLEANSE THE WEAK. Level 60 in BfA, level 27 only from Shadowlands (V).
   THE ROW 2 TALENTS. Hammer of Wrath 30, Crusader's Judgment 30, Cavalier
   30 - all one level out of reach. A 29 paladin that wants Hammer of Wrath
   has to level once.
   THE SPEC LOCK. Steed of Glory is a Protection talent and Blessing of
   Sanctuary a Retribution one on the current pages. Whether those spec
   locks were the same in 8.3.7 was not verified, and if they were not, a
   Holy paladin has one more option than this file gives it.

8. WHAT THE BOTS SHOULD TAKE
   HOLY        196926 Crusader's Might + a PvP slot with nothing verified
   PROTECTION  Blessed Hammer (204019, id unverified) + 199542 Steed of
               Glory
   RETRIBUTION 269569 Zeal + 210256 Blessing of Sanctuary
   One talent each, one PvP talent each, and the spec choice decides both.
   UNVERIFIED, and worth a look on the server before the roster is written:
   the Light's Hammer, Redoubt and Righteous Verdict ids; the BfA ids of
   Holy Shield and Blessed Hammer; the whole Holy PvP list; and whether the
   spec locks on Steed of Glory and Blessing of Sanctuary existed on 8.3.7.
