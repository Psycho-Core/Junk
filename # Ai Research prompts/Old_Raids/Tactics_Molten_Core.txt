TACTICS - MOLTEN CORE (OLD RAIDS STEP 1 OF 16)
Server: private BfA 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Context: level 60 raid entered by a level 120 character with
computer-controlled companions. Damage numbers below are the
original raid values read on warcraft.wiki.gg; at 120 they are
mostly trivial, so the AI RULE per boss is the mechanic that can
still kill, move, or stall a companion.
Zone: Molten Core (Wowhead zone 2717). Instance map id and all
coordinates (map, x, y, z): unverified - not read for this file.
Boss order and creature entries verified on Wowhead NPC pages:
Lucifron 12118, Magmadar 11982, Gehennas 12259, Garr 12057,
Baron Geddon 12056, Shazzrah 12264, Sulfuron Harbinger 12098,
Golemagg the Incinerator 11988, Majordomo Executus 12018,
Ragnaros 11502. Add entries unverified unless stated.
Dispel tags: D-Curse, D-Magic. I = interruptible (only where a
cast bar was read; otherwise unverified).

1. LUCIFRON (12118) + 2x Flamewaker Protector (12119, verified)
PHASES: single phase.
TANK: hold Lucifron away from the group, facing away; a second
  tank holds both Protectors away from him (mind control range).
GROUP: ranged spread; nobody stands near the tank group or the
  west tunnel trash (chain-aggro risk read on the boss page).
GROUND: none read. Impending Doom 19702 is a delayed blast, not
  a ground effect.
ADD ORDER: P1/P2 Protectors (12119), then Lucifron - or boss
  first at 120 damage; never leave a Protector free to cast.
ABILITY LIST:
  19702 Impending Doom - 2000 Shadow after 10 sec, nearby.
  19703 Lucifron's Curse - D-Curse; +100% ability costs, 5 min.
  19460 Shadow Shock - instant Shadow on target.
  20605 Cleave (Protector) - frontal, up to 3 targets.
  20604 Dominate Mind (Protector) - mind control 15 sec,
    humanoid up to level 100; I unverified.
AI RULE: dispel Lucifron's Curse (D-Curse) on cooldown, tank
  first; kill Protectors before the boss.

2. MAGMADAR (11982)
PHASES: single phase; Frenzy 19451 is a timed buff, not a phase.
TANK: hold him facing the group (strategy page: tank faces him
  toward the healers), back to a wall where possible; tank must
  not be feared into lava - fear break/ward is the tank's job.
GROUP: healers at max range (outside Panic range, read on page);
  melee at his sides/behind, never in front (Lava Breath).
GROUND: 19411 Lava Bomb leaves a burning target (4800 over
  12 sec), not a placed ground zone.
ADD ORDER: none (no adds in the base encounter).
ABILITY LIST:
  19450 Magma Spit - Fire + DoT on target.
  19451 Frenzy - +150% attack speed; remove with Tranquilizing
    Shot (hunter; tome drops from Lucifron, 100% per page).
  19272 Lava Breath - frontal cone, 1157-1343 Fire.
  19408 Panic - AoE fear 8 sec, nearby.
  19411 Lava Bomb - see GROUND.
AI RULE: dispel/remove Frenzy immediately; if Panic lands,
  regroup behind the boss, never in his frontal cone.

3. GEHENNAS (12259) + 2x Flamewaker (add entry unverified)
PHASES: single phase.
TANK: Gehennas in the centre of his area; adds tanked far from
  him and apart from each other (their Fist of Ragnaros stuns
  would chain-lock stacked melee, read on page).
GROUP: only tank/healers/decursers stay near the boss; everyone
  else kills adds first, out of Rain of Fire.
GROUND: 19717 Rain of Fire - placed area, 368 Fire/2 sec, 6 sec.
  Move out on cast.
ADD ORDER: P1/P2 Flamewakers, then Gehennas.
ABILITY LIST:
  19728 Shadow Bolt - 2250-2750 Shadow.
  19717 Rain of Fire - see GROUND.
  19716 Gehennas' Curse - D-Curse; -75% healing taken, 5 min.
  20277 Fist of Ragnaros (add) - AoE stun 4 sec.
  15502 Sunder Armor (add) - stacking armour reduction.
  19730 Strike (add) - melee +200.
AI RULE: decurse the tank instantly (heals land at 25% while
  cursed); never stack the two adds together.

4. GARR (12057) + 8x Firesworn (add entry unverified)
PHASES: single phase; the fight changes only as Firesworn die.
TANK: Garr held apart from the Firesworn group; adds spread out
  singly (tanked or banished), never clumped.
GROUP: melee leave a dying Firesworn - Eruption 19497 on death
  is 22194 Fire to nearby with knockback. At 120, kill Garr
  first and the adds after is the page's second strategy.
GROUND: 19496 Magma Shackles is a slow aura, not a zone;
  Eruption point is the dying add's corpse position.
ADD ORDER: Garr P1 at 120 (adds cannot out-damage the group);
  at-level order is banished adds LAST, one at a time.
  NEVER stack-kill Firesworn: each death erupts.
ABILITY LIST:
  19492 Antimagic Pulse - dispels 1 beneficial magic effect
    from nearby enemies (offensive dispel; nothing to cure).
  19496 Magma Shackles - -60% move speed 15 sec, nearby.
  20294 Immolate (Firesworn) - D-Magic (dispel type read as a
    standard DoT; tag unverified in source, treat as Magic).
  19497 Eruption (Firesworn, on death) - see ADD ORDER.
AI RULE: when a Firesworn is about to die, all companions move
  away from it; never kill two Firesworn at the same moment.

5. BARON GEDDON (12056)
PHASES: single phase; at 5% approx. he casts Armageddon-type
  finisher in some versions - id 20478 read on the page as
  self-destruct 8000 Fire to all (trigger point unverified).
TANK: hold him in the open area (Garr's room if pulled there);
  during Inferno 19695 he is rooted and pulses - tank steps out
  too, nobody melees through it.
GROUP: spread in a wide arc; the Living Bomb target runs AWAY
  from everyone alone and explodes clear (8 sec fuse read).
  Low-ceiling spots remove the fall damage (page strategy 2).
GROUND: 19695 Inferno - 8 sec pulsing Fire around the boss;
  20475 Living Bomb explosion point = the target's position.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  19695 Inferno - see GROUND; he cannot move while channelling.
  19659 Ignite Mana - D-Magic; burns 400 mana/3 sec, damage per
    mana burned; dispel from mana users, casters first.
  20475 Living Bomb - 3200 Fire to target and nearby after 8s.
  20478 Armageddon - see PHASES.
AI RULE: if you are the Living Bomb, run away from the group
  immediately; dispel Ignite Mana from healers/casters first.

6. SHAZZRAH (12264)
PHASES: single phase; Blink resets threat (approx. every 45
  sec read), which is a reposition event, not a phase.
TANK: hold him centre-room; after every Blink the nearest tank
  picks him up where he lands - he teleports to a random
  target and then Arcane Explosions.
GROUP: groups spread at range around him (35 yd spread read);
  melee stay out until late - Arcane Explosion is 20 yd.
GROUND: 19712 Arcane Explosion - 925-1075 Arcane, 20 yd around
  the boss after Blink; the danger zone is wherever he lands.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  19712 Arcane Explosion - see GROUND.
  19713 Shazzrah's Curse - D-Curse; +100% magic damage taken,
    5 min. Decurse tank first, then the Blink landing group.
  19714 Magic Grounding - self-buff, -50% magic damage taken
    30 sec; purge/dispel off him (offensive dispel).
  19715 Counterspell - AoE school lockout 10 sec, ~35 yd.
  21655 Blink - teleport + threat wipe (see PHASES).
AI RULE: after Blink, stop, let a tank re-tag him, and move
  away from his landing point before Arcane Explosion.

7. SULFURON HARBINGER (12098) + 4x Flamewaker Priest
   (add entry unverified)
PHASES: single phase.
TANK: boss and each Priest tanked apart (5 tanks at level);
  Priests must be separated so Dark Mending cannot reach the
  boss or another Priest's target.
GROUP: interrupt rotation on every Priest; kill one Priest at
  a time, boss after the Priests (heals make boss-first stall).
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: P1-P4 Flamewaker Priests (one at a time), P5 boss.
ABILITY LIST:
  19778 Demoralizing Shout (boss) - -300 attack power 30 sec.
  19779 Inspire (boss) - ally +25% physical, +100% attack
    speed 10 sec; offensive dispel unverified - kill/priority
    target is the inspired add.
  19780 Hand of Ragnaros (boss) - 300-400 Fire, knockback,
    stun 2 sec, nearby.
  19781 Flame Spear (boss) - 850-1150 Fire + splash.
  19775 Dark Mending (Priest) - heal 27750-32250; INTERRUPT -
    the highest-priority interrupt in this raid.
  19776 Shadow Word: Pain (Priest) - DoT 18 sec; D-Magic
    (unverified tag, standard for the spell family).
  20294 Immolate (Priest) - see Garr.
AI RULE: interrupt Dark Mending every cast; never fight the
  boss in range of a living Priest.

8. GOLEMAGG THE INCINERATOR (11988) + 2x Core Rager
   (add entry unverified)
PHASES: single phase; Earthquake 19798 begins below 10%
  health (page: ranged finish from there).
TANK: boss held in place; each Core Rager off-tanked FAR from
  him - out of Golemagg's Trust range (horns stop glowing red
  when far enough, read on page).
GROUP: ranged finish below 10%; melee leave before Earthquake.
GROUND: 19798 Earthquake - 1388-1612 to nearby, end of fight.
ADD ORDER: NEVER kill the Core Ragers - they cannot be killed
  while Golemagg lives (do-not-kill adds; tank-and-ignore).
  P1 Golemagg only.
ABILITY LIST:
  20553 Golemagg's Trust - buff on Ragers in range: +500
    physical damage, +50% attack speed.
  13880 Magma Splash - stacking Fire/armour-melt on tank.
  20228 Pyroblast - 1009 Fire + DoT; I unverified.
  19798 Earthquake - see GROUND.
  19820 Mangle (Rager) - bleed + 50% slow 20 sec.
AI RULE: never target the Core Ragers; keep them away from
  the boss and kill Golemagg, ranged only below 10%.

9. MAJORDOMO EXECUTUS (12018) + 4x Flamewaker Elite +
   4x Flamewaker Healer (add entries unverified)
PHASES: single phase; encounter ends when all 8 adds die -
  Executus himself is NOT killed (he submits and summons
  Ragnaros). Crowd control stops working after 4 adds die.
TANK: one tank holds Executus away from the raid (he teleports
  the nearest player into the burning coals, 20534); one tank
  per Elite; Healers polymorphed/CC'd or tanked.
GROUP: stand off during Magic Reflection 20619 (spell reflect);
  melee pause during Damage Shield 21075 (damage returned).
GROUND: the burning coals at the room's centre - the Teleport
  destination; move out immediately if teleported.
ADD ORDER: P1 one Elite, P2-P5 the four Healers, P6-P8 the
  remaining Elites (page's "follow the leader" order).
  NEVER kill Executus - he is a do-not-kill target; damage on
  him is wasted (Aegis of Ragnaros 20620 absorbs 30000).
ABILITY LIST:
  20620 Aegis of Ragnaros (Executus) - absorb shield.
  21075 Damage Shield - nearby minions reflect 100 Arcane.
  20619 Magic Reflection - nearby allies reflect 50% spells
    10 sec.
  20534 Teleport - nearest enemy into the coals.
  20623 Fire Blast (Elite) - 841 Fire.
  20229 Blast Wave (Elite) - 694-806 Fire + 50% slow 8 sec.
  20603 Shadow Shock (Healer) - instant Shadow.
AI RULE: kill adds in order, never the boss; when a shield is
  up (white/purple read on page) stop the reflected damage
  type until it drops.

10. RAGNAROS (11502)
PHASES: Phase 1 surface; at 3 minutes he submerges and summons
  8x Son of Flame (add entry unverified) for up to 90 sec;
  repeats every 3 min. At level 120 he should die in Phase 1 -
  the submerge is written here because a stalled group hits it.
TANK: hold him at his pool edge; melee eat Wrath of Ragnaros
  knockback 20566 into lava - tank positions so knockback
  lands on rock, and the tank stays in melee range at all
  times (if nobody is in melee he Magma Blasts the raid).
GROUP: ranged spread around the pool; mana users spread from
  each other (second Wrath 20566 targets a random mana user
  and knocks back everyone around the target, 20-30 sec CD).
GROUND: lava itself and Lava Splash near flows (spell id
  unverified in source) - never stand at a flow edge; know the
  exit ramps before the pull.
ADD ORDER (Phase 2 only): P1-P8 Sons of Flame, all of them;
  banished Sons count as defeated and bring him back early.
ABILITY LIST:
  20566 Wrath of Ragnaros - 1000 Fire + 25 yd knockback (two
    targeting modes, read on page; ids same).
  20564 Elemental Fire - main-target 2160-2640 + 600/sec 8s.
  20565 Magma Blast - 6000 Fire, only when nobody is in melee.
  21388 Melt Weapon - melee hits cost weapon durability.
AI RULE: someone must always be in melee range of Ragnaros;
  if knocked into lava, get out by the nearest ramp and run
  back - do not heal in the lava.

TRASH (pack breakdown; creature entries unverified - not read
for this file, so no entry numbers are given)
  Molten Giant / Molten Destroyer packs at the entrance:
    frontal melee; tank facing away from the group, kill one
    at a time. Spells/interrupts: none verified - unverified.
  Core Hound packs (before Magmadar): the pack must die
    together - a hound revives if its packmates are not killed
    within seconds of it (mechanic read in the Magmadar page's
    Season of Mastery note for the same hound type; base-pack
    behaviour stated from the Lucifron strategy page - treat
    the exact revive timer as unverified). Kill order: all
    together, AoE, lowest-health first to synchronise deaths.
  Flame Imp packs: many small casters; interrupt priority and
    spell ids unverified - group them and AoE, do not pull
    two packs.
  Lava elemental / surger packs (Golemagg bridge area):
    chargers and AoE; spread, kill order unverified.
  Flamewaker patrols (Sulfuron area): same ability family as
    the Sulfuron/Gehennas adds (19775-type heals if a Priest
    is present - interrupt first); exact pack lists unverified.
  General trash AI RULE: never pull a second pack while a
  healer-type (any Flamewaker that casts a heal) is alive;
  interrupt cast heals before all other interrupts.
