1. RAZORGORE THE UNTAMED (12435)
P1 add group: Grethok the Controller (12557) + 2x Blackwing
  Guardsman (14456). P2 spawn groups: Blackwing Mage
  (12420), Blackwing Legionnaire (12416), Death Talon
  Dragonspawn (12422). Egg: Black Dragon Egg (object;
  entry unverified).
PHASES: P1 kill Grethok and his Guardsmen fast (a spawn
  timer runs from the pull, page). P2: a companion uses
  the Orb of Domination to mind-control Razorgore and
  destroy every egg (control is channeled, ~90 sec per
  control period, page) while adds spawn in the corners
  (caps: 40 orcs - Legionnaires/Mages - and 12 Dragonspawn
  total; killed adds respawn as replacements until the
  caps are met, page). P3: last egg destroyed - adds
  flee, Razorgore heals to full (23040), kill him.
  NEVER let Razorgore die before the eggs are gone: he
  instantly kills every unit in the room and resets the
  encounter (page, explicit). The controller companion
  takes Razorgore's full aggro in P3 (page) - use a hard
  class (warrior/paladin/feral) on the orb, pets dismissed.
TANK: P1 tank the Guardsmen (frontal 15284). P3 tank
  Razorgore facing away from the group (19632 Cleave,
  24375 War Stomp).
GROUP: one corner group per spawn corner (page: rogues/
  mages/warlocks + an assist target); every add's first
  aggro is the orb controller - adds must be peeled off
  the controller, Mages before they reach mid-room and
  start casting (page). Dragonspawn/Legionnaires are
  kited/CC'd, not killed (Dragonspawn: hard to kill, high
  damage; page).
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER (P2): eggs are the real objective (controlled
  19873 Destroy Egg). Kills: P1 Blackwing Mage (12420).
  Legionnaire (12416) and Dragonspawn (12422): kite/CC
  only - killing them only spawns replacements and their
  23967 Dragonbane (400% weapon damage) is aimed at
  Razorgore (mob pages: "only used against Razorgore"),
  i.e. a loose add can kill the boss and reset the room.
ABILITY LIST (Razorgore, uncontrolled):
  23023 Conflagration - 3000 Fire/10 sec + panic; the
    burning target periodically scorches nearby allies
    for 150. Spread from the burning companion.
  24375 War Stomp - normal +500 nearby, knockback, 5 sec
    stun, 15 yd.
  19632 Cleave - frontal, up to 10 targets.
  23040 Warming Flames - heals to full, starts P3.
ABILITY LIST (while controlled - the controller's bar):
  19873 Destroy Egg - 10 yd; 3 sec cast, 7 sec cooldown
    (8.3.7 tooltip, audit 2 - the page's ability list
    said instant and its strategy text said 1.5 sec CD;
    both wrong for this build).
  19872 Calm Dragonkin - sleep a young dragon 30 sec.
  22425 Fireball Volley - 2 sec cast; 133-154 Fire to
    hostile units within 45 yd (page).
ABILITY LIST (Grethok 12557 - kill him first, page):
  14515 Dominate Mind - I (2 sec cast read); humanoid up
    to level 25 (page) for 15 sec.
  22274 Greater Polymorph - instant; up to 20 sec, one
    target. Dispel: D-Magic unverified (standard for
    polymorph; not printed).
  13747 Slow - +50% attack/cast time, -60% move, 6 sec,
    nearby. 22273 Arcane Missiles - 66-77 Arcane.
ABILITY LIST (adds): Guardsman/Legionnaire/Dragonspawn
  share 15284 Cleave (up to 3 frontal) + 15580 Strike;
  Legionnaire/Dragonspawn also 23967 Dragonbane (above).
  Blackwing Mage: 17290 Fireball (3 sec cast - interrupt
  or kill first), 22271 Arcane Explosion (instant).
AI RULE: control Razorgore, destroy eggs, kill only the
  Mages; never let Razorgore die before the last egg.

2. VAELASTRASZ THE CORRUPT (13020)
PHASES: single phase, but a 3-minute race: 23513 ends
  after 3 minutes and with it the raid's resources (page:
  fights are "won at 2:45"). He starts the fight already
  corrupted and low (exact starting % unverified in the
  text read). At 120 this is a burn; the mechanics below
  are what still kill companions.
TANK: tanking LINE, not one tank: 18173 Burning
  Adrenaline hits a mana user every 15 sec, and every
  THIRD cast hits the current tank instead (page) - that
  tank dies when it expires. Next tank moves into the
  tank spot and takes over immediately (page transitions
  section); healers shift to the new tank. Hold him with
  his head to a wall/alcove: 23461 cone in front, 15847
  Tail Sweep behind (150 yd knockback, page).
GROUP: casters clumped on one flank against the wall,
  hunters/tank line opposite (page setups); melee at his
  broadside between front and hind legs. Never in front
  (23461 + 19983 chain), never behind (15847). A companion
  with 18173 moves clear of everyone and keeps DPSing:
  on death it deals 4376-5624 to surrounding allies.
GROUND: none placed; the death-blast point is the dying
  18173 target's position.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  23513 Essence of the Red - raid buff at pull: 500
    mana / 50 energy / 20 rage / 20 runic power per sec,
    3 min (page).
  18173 Burning Adrenaline - +100% damage done, all
    spells instant, max health -5% per sec, explodes on
    death (see GROUP); 20 sec duration (8.3.7 tooltip,
    audit 2 - the target is dead when it expires).
  23461 Flame Breath - 2 sec cast; 3500-4500 Fire cone
    + 938-1062/3 sec for 15 sec.
  23642 Fire Nova - instant; 555-645 Fire, 100 yd radius.
  19983 Cleave - up to 10 targets, chains within 10 yd.
  15847 Tail Sweep - 925-1075 behind him, huge knockback.
AI RULE: kill him before 23513 ends; if you get 18173,
  run clear of the group and keep casting until you die.

3. BROODLORD LASHLAYER (12017)
The two Suppression Rooms before him are the real first
  phase - see TRASH T1.
PHASES: single phase.
TANK: tank him by his gate/doorway away from suppression
  devices (solo-strategy text). Tanks ROTATE: Knock Away
  on the highest-threat target cuts that target's aggro
  by 50% every time (page), so the next tank taunts and
  the knocked tank rebuilds. DPS must be modulated - the
  fight is lost when cloth aggro passes the tanks (page).
GROUP: ranged at max range; nobody but the tank in front
  (Cleave). Shield the tank through the Mortal Strike
  debuff window (page: Power Word: Shield timing).
GROUND: none in the boss room. Suppression devices (see
  T1) radiate the slow aura - fight him away from them.
ADD ORDER: none in the boss fight.
ABILITY LIST (spell ids unverified - the page names the
  abilities without wowhead id links):
  Blast Wave - fire AoE knockback, "hefty" damage.
  Cleave - high-damage frontal.
  Mortal Strike - crits to 8000 on plate, 15000+ on
    cloth (page); leaves the healing-reduction debuff.
  Knock Away - single-target knockback, -50% threat on
    the target (see TANK).
AI RULE: when your tank is knocked away, the next tank
  taunts at once; non-tanks never stand in front of him.

  all times (halves that healing; page). Tanks at
  12/3/9 o'clock, DPS at 6 (page).
GROUP: DPS at his rear (6 o'clock); only tanks in front.
GROUND: none.

7. CHROMAGGUS (14020)
PHASES: single phase; at 20% a hard Enrage for the rest
  of the fight (page; id cited there only via an old
  external link - spell id unverified). Frenzy every
  10-15 sec throughout (Tranquilizing Shot; its spell
  id unverified on this page). His TWO breaths are
  chosen from the five below per instance lockout and
  alternate every 30 sec (page). He also shimmers:
  vulnerability to one magic school changes over the
  fight (page; school/timing unverified per shimmer).
TANK: tank where the raid can break line of sight (door
  of his room / ramp top / cubbies; page list) - breaths
  hit everything in LINE OF SIGHT, not just frontal.
  Everyone except the tank hides on each breath warning.
  Time Lapse exception: the MT takes every Time Lapse;
  an off-tank who hid from it holds him, with healers
  who also hid, until the MT's stun ends (page).
GROUP: dispel teams by type (below); keep every
  companion at 3 or FEWER Brood Afflictions - at all
  five, the companion mutates into an elite drakonid
  (cannot be dispelled; polymorph or kill the mutant -
  its death heals Chromaggus significantly, page).
GROUND: none placed in the base encounter (the ground
  patches on cleanse are a Season of Mastery variant;
  not used - unverified). Breaths are LoS, see TANK.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST (breath and affliction spell ids
  unverified - the page names them without id links):
  Breaths (2 of 5 per lockout, alternate every 30 sec):
   Incinerate (Red) - 3675-4275 Fire (~9000 if the
    target has Brood Affliction: Black).
   Corrosive Acid (Green) - 875-1125 Nature/3 sec for
    15 sec; armour reduced 3938-5062.
   Frost Burn (Blue) - ~1400 Frost; attack speed -80%.
   Ignite Flesh (Black) - 657-843 Fire/3 sec for 60
    sec; stacks on recast.
   Time Lapse (Bronze) - 6 sec stun; max health halved
    while stunned (returned when it ends, page).
  Brood Afflictions (radiated zone-wide, all five cycle):
   Red D-Disease: 50 Fire/3 sec; if the holder dies it
    heals Chromaggus.
   Green D-Poison: -50% healing received; 250 Nature
    /5 sec.
   Blue D-Magic: move -70%, cast speed -50%; drains
    50 mana + 1 health per sec.
   Black D-Curse: +100% Fire damage taken.
   Bronze (no dispel type): random 4-sec stuns for
    10 min; removed ONLY by Hourglass Sand (item;
    drops from the trash packs before Chromaggus) or
    Ice Block (page). Prioritise sand for healers,
    tanks, hunters (page).
  Frenzy (10-15 sec) - Tranquilizing Shot; id unverified.
  Enrage at 20% - id unverified (see PHASES).
AI RULE: dispel every Brood Affliction fast (Disease,
  Poison, Magic, Curse - never let one companion reach
  five), and break line of sight on every breath unless
  you are the tank.

8. NEFARIAN (11583)
P1 add groups: Chromatic Drakonid (14302) - always - plus
  two random colours (one per door, fixed per lockout;
  page): Red/Blue/Green/Black/Bronze Drakonid (their
  individual entries unverified - mob pages not read;
  abilities/resists from the tactics page below). P3:
  Bone Construct (14605). Lord Victor Nefarius (his
  human form on the throne): creature entry unverified.
PHASES: P1: talk to Nefarius on his throne; he is
  invulnerable, wanders shadow-bolting, mind-controlling
  and fearing random targets (page; his P1 spell ids
  unverified) while drakonids flood from both doors.
  After 42 drakonids are killed no more spawn; kill the
  rest (page). He then casts a raid-wide Shadow Flame
  (ignores LoS, ~1000 Shadow, page; behind the throne is
  the one safe spot) and lands: P2 class calls every
  ~25-35 sec (page). P3 at 20%: he resurrects every
  drakonid killed in P1 as Bone Constructs where they
  died (page); constructs do not despawn on his death
  (high-level strategy text) - re-kill them after.
TANK: P2 tank him near his throne facing away from the
  raid (22539 cone front, 23364 behind). AoE fear about
  every 30 sec (ground shakes first, page): Fear Ward /
  Berserker Rage on the tank; ranged over 35 yd avoid it.
GROUP: P1 split evenly to both doors (2-3 tanks per side
  for Chromatics, page doorway method); ranged/healers
  at max range mid-room to switch sides. P2 spread on
  his flank; obey class calls (below); pre-position AoE
  where drakonids died before he hits 20% (page).
GROUND: none placed read.
ADD ORDER: P1 leaking drakonids first, then the assist
  target (page); colour handling (page): AoE Red groups,
  assist-train Blue, prefer DPS on Black, AoE Bronze,
  Green is governed by its partner colour. Chromatics
  are "sunder" tanked (most health, page). P3: Bone
  Constructs (14605) are tanks' P1 - pull them to the
  AoE spot; companions keep DPS on Nefarian unless
  constructs reach the healers.
ABILITY LIST (Nefarian):
  22686 Bellowing Roar - fear 4 sec, all within 35 yd.
  19983 Cleave - normal +100%, up to 10 targets.
  22539 Shadow Flame - frontal cone (ability list value
    2210; tactics text prints 3938-5062 for the drake
    version - value for Nefarian unverified).
  23364 Tail Lash - behind him: 614 damage, drops the
    target's weapon (disarm), 2 sec stun.
  22687 Veil of Shadow - healing -75% for 6 sec, on the
    target nearest the centre of his hitbox (page);
    decurse the tank first. D-Curse VERIFIED (8.3.7
    tooltip buff type is Curse, audit 2).
  Class calls (every ~25-35 sec; call spell ids
  unverified - companion responses from the page):
   Death knight: Death Grip pulls the whole raid into
    his hitbox - run back out (no root, page).
   Demon hunter: controlling player's screen blacks out
    (player-only effect; companion rule: keep attacking
    the current target - page solution is tab-target).
   Druid: stuck in Cat Form - stay back, wait it out.
   Hunter: ranged weapon durability destroyed - unequip
    the ranged weapon before every call (page macro
    exists; companions: swap to a spare/cheap weapon).
   Mage: Wild Polymorph on random raid members (no

   Monk: uncontrolled Roll for ~5 sec - wait, run back.
   Paladin: Blessing of Protection on Nefarian - STOP
    all damage until it drops (tank cannot gain threat).
   Priest: direct heals add a stacking DoT (Corrupted
    Healing) - priests use Renew/Power Word: Shield
    only (page: both still work normally).
   Rogue: teleported and rooted beside him - tank turns

   Shaman: corrupted totems spawn (incl. windfury/fire

   Warlock: 2 Infernals per warlock land on/near them
    (stun + minor damage) - warlocks stand isolated at
    the back; mage AoE kills the Infernals (fire immune).
   Warrior: stuck in Berserker Stance, +30% damage
    taken - extra heals on the tank; threat is lower,
    watch DPS.
   (Evoker call exists in current retail text - Pitiful
    Whelp 389632 - but Evokers do not exist in 8.3.7;
    not used.)
AI RULE: obey your class call the second it lands;
  otherwise never stand in front of or behind him,
  and re-kill the Bone Constructs after he dies.

TRASH (entries page-read where stated)
T1. Suppression Rooms (between Vaelastrasz and
  Broodlord): Suppression Devices (object; entry
  unverified) radiate an aura cutting move/attack/cast
  speed to 20% (Broodlord page). Non-elite whelps
  (entry unverified) swarm and respawn 30 sec - AoE
  constantly, keep moving; patrolling elite humanoids
  + Death Talon Hatchers (entry unverified) respawn
  20 min. At-level rule: a rogue does nothing but
  disarm traps ahead of the group (page). Retail note
  (page solo strategy): devices can now be clicked off
  by any class but reactivate - companions should
  click devices on their path and use instants inside
  the aura. Kill order: elites first (hunters primary
  - caster mana is saved for whelps, page), whelps by
  AoE in passing; move in the respawn gaps.
T2. Death Talon packs (room above Vaelastrasz; also the
  first Broodlord room): 6 mobs per pull, at least one
  of each type, max one Captain (pack page): Captain,
  Seether, Flamescale, Wyrmkin types (individual
  entries unverified - their pages not read). Pack
  page mechanics: Wyrmkin Fireball Volley is the big
  threat (druids keep Wyrmkins asleep); Captain has
  Aura of Flames (fire thorns to all Death Talon
  within ~30-40 yd - tank Seethers/Flamescales AWAY
  from him) and Mark of Detonation (random target
  explodes on nearby allies - DISPEL it; dispel type
  unverified on the page, or kill the Captain before
  the Flamescales if the group cannot dispel, page).
  Seethers enrage (attack speed doubles; hunters
  assigned - Tranquilizing Shot by analogy, unverified
  on this page) and brand their tank +1000 Fire damage
  taken from fire attacks (FR on seether tanks).
  Kill order (page): Seether P1, Flamescale P2,
  Captain P3, sleeping Wyrmkin last.
T3. Crimson Laboratories packs (Broodlord to Firemaw,
  and between the drakes): Blackwing Technician
  (13996) verified: 22334 Bomb (Fire to a selected
  area), 22335 Bottle of Poison (179 Nature/3 sec,
  30 sec; D-Poison unverified). They are the only
  source of Elementium Ingot (page). Mixed with
  whelp/dragonkin packs (composition per pack
  unverified). Hourglass Sand for Chromaggus Bronze
  drops from the packs BEFORE Chromaggus (Chromaggus
  page) - loot it, never vendor it.
General trash AI RULE: in suppression rooms keep moving
  and click devices off; in Death Talon packs keep the
  Captain's aura away from the other tanks' targets
  and dispel Mark of Detonation; loot Hourglass Sand.
