WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 530 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: all 38 resolved. 23 come from the research pass whose Wowhead lookup returned the exact master name for
  8.3.7, and 15 were matched on the exact NPC name against the creature_template exports (the 4.3.4 TDB update pack and the 8.x
  creature_template dump from the same server repository). No Outland master name in this file has more than one creature row, so
  no entry here is written unverified.
  Two of the 23 Wowhead-verified entries are 8.3.7 versions that the 4.3.4 dump does not carry under that name: Skymaster Sunwing
  is 238480 (the dump has the older 16189) and Skymaster Skyles is 239639 (the dump has 44244). Both are printed as the 8.3.7
  lookup returned them, because the row that spawns in 8.3.7 is the one a server needs.
  Gate evidence: four of the 38 nodes are gated and 34 are not. 6211 on 140 Altar of Sha'tar (after quest 10551, and the wiki
  marks that master for Aldor); 6212 on 159 Sanctum of the Stars (after quest 10552, wiki: Scryers); 5953 on 213 Shattered Sun
  Staging Area (MinLevel 65, wiki: learned automatically at 65); 924 on 624 Azure Watch (Alliance race mask). No Outland taxi
  node is gated on an achievement and none is gated on reputation in PlayerCondition.
  Node 149 Shatter Point is the one faction oddity: the export sets neither faction bit, while the wiki lists Runetog Wildhammer
  with the Alliance masters. Both facts are printed as they stand rather than reconciled.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Four nodes have links that leave the map - 83 and 205 to the
  Light's Hope nodes 67/68 on map 0, 94 to Lor'danel 26 and Rut'theran 27 on map 1, and 213 to node 67 on map 0. Those legs are
  tagged "[node on map n]" and are long taxi connections across continents, not local hops.
  Sources: TaxiNodes and TaxiPath exports for build 8.3.7.35662, the PlayerCondition decode of every ConditionID listed above, the
  Warcraft Wiki flight-master tables by location and zone, and the creature_template exports named above. Everything read from a
  source is printed; nothing was estimated.
