TWINK29 - MONK - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
V = read off a wiki or database page this turn. Everything marked unverified
was not opened.

0. THE SHORT ANSWER
   A level 29 monk has ONE normal talent and ONE PvP talent.
   One normal talent because the second row opens at 30: Tiger's Lust's own
   patch notes say 9.0.1 "now available at level 25 (was 30)", so in BfA
   that row sat at 30 and a 29 is one level short of it.
   One PvP talent because the PvP pane gives its first slot at 20 and its
   second at 40.
   So the whole file comes down to one row, and that row is per spec.

1. WHICH SPECIALIZATIONS ARE OPEN AT 29
   All three. Brewmaster, Mistweaver and Windwalker are chosen at level 10
   and every one of them is legal at 29. A monk has no forms or stances to
   worry about, unlike the druid, and no spec is locked out by armour or
   weapon type, because all three wear leather.
   Each spec has its own talent row, so "the level 15 row" is three
   different rows, not one shared row.

2. THE NORMAL TALENT TREE AT 29
   BfA rows open at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and 100 - one point per row, and
   you must finish the row above before the next one opens. At 29 that is
   row 1 only.
   ROW 1, level 15 - OPEN, one pick, three options for Brewmaster and
   Windwalker, two for Mistweaver.
   ROW 2, level 30 - CLOSED, one level away, and it is the movement row:
   Chi Torpedo, Tiger's Lust 116841 (V), Celerity.
   ROWS 3 to 7, levels 45 to 100 - CLOSED, nine or more levels away.
   Nothing in rows 2 to 7 can be bought, enchanted or cheated into a 29.
   The only thing a twink gains from them is knowing what it does not have.

3. ROW 1 - BREWMASTER (three options)
   EYE OF THE TIGER, spell 196607 (V)
     Tiger Palm also applies a bleed that deals Nature damage and heals the
     monk over 8 sec, one target at a time.
   CHI WAVE, spell 115098 (V)
     15 sec cooldown, sends a wave that bounces up to 7 times, damaging
     enemies and healing whoever it lands on next.
   CHI BURST, spell 123986 (V)
     30 sec cooldown, 40 yd line, Nature damage to everything in its path
     and healing to the monk and allies in the same line.
   PICK: EYE OF THE TIGER.
     Brewmaster is the spec that holds aggro, so it is the one taking hits;
     a free bleed that heals on every Tiger Palm is worth more to a tanking
     bot than a 15-second wave. The wiki confirms it was a level 15
     "Brewmaster and Windwalker" talent in this era (V), so it is legal
     here.

4. ROW 1 - WINDWALKER (three options)
   The same three: 196607, 115098, 123986.
   PICK: CHI BURST.
     In BfA Chi Burst generated one Chi for each enemy it hit, which made it
     the strongest talent in the row for a damage spec (peakofserenity's
     2018 Windwalker write-up calls it the undisputed king of the row for
     exactly that reason). For a bot that fights packs, more Chi means more
     Blackout Kicks.
   SECOND CHOICE: CHI WAVE, when the bot is expected to be alone, because a
     wave that heals the monk for 150% of attack power on a 15 sec cooldown
     is the only self-healing this row offers a Windwalker.

5. ROW 1 - MISTWEAVER (two options, and that is not a typo)
   CHI WAVE, spell 115098 (V)
   CHI BURST, spell 123986 (V)
   PICK: CHI WAVE.
     A Mistweaver bot heals parties, not packs, and Chi Wave bounces to
     allies on its own without a positional read. Chi Burst needs the group
     to be lined up in front of the caster, which is the one thing a bot
     cannot be trusted to arrange.
   WHY ONLY TWO: Zen Pulse used to be the third option in this row. It was
     removed on 8.0.1, the patch that opened BfA (V, from the spell's own
     patch notes), four patches before 8.3.7. If a private server has not
     taken that removal, Zen Pulse would be the third option; on a stock
     8.3.7 core the row is two.

6. PVP TALENTS - HOW MANY SLOTS AND WHEN
   The PvP pane is SEPARATE from the normal talent tree. It is not one slot
   shared with the normal tree - that belief is wrong, and it is worth
   stating plainly because it changes what a 29 can do: the normal row and
   the PvP slot are two independent picks.
   A character has four PvP talent slots in BfA, opening at levels
   20, 40, 70 and 110 (V, from the PvP talent page, which describes the
   pre-Shadowlands four-slot system).
   At 29 that means ONE PvP talent slot, opened at level 20.
   Individual PvP talents also carry their own level requirement inside the
   pane, and this is where a 29 gets caught out - see section 8.

7. PVP TALENTS A LEVEL 29 CAN ACTUALLY TAKE
   Only talents whose own level requirement is 29 or lower count. Three are
   verified, one is likely, and the rest of the monk list sits above 29.
   WINDWALKER
     TURBO FISTS, spell 287681 (V)
       Fists of Fury slows everything it hits by 90% and the monk parries
       all attacks while channelling it. Level 20, and it was added in
       8.1.0 (V), so it exists in 8.3.7.
     RIDE THE WIND, spell 201372 (V)
       Flying Serpent Kick clears all snares and leaves a wind path that
       gives allies 30% movement speed and snare immunity. Level 21 on the
       page; the BfA requirement is unverified but cannot be far off.
   BREWMASTER
     DOUBLE BARREL, spell 202335 (V)
       Next Keg Smash deals 50% more damage and stuns everything it hits
       for 3 sec, on a 45 sec cooldown. Level 21 on the page; a rank 28
       honour talent converted to a PvP talent in 8.0.1 (V).
   MISTWEAVER
     ZEN FOCUS TEA (V)
       Thunder Focus Tea grants 5 sec of immunity to silence and interrupt.
       Level 20 on the page. The spell id is unverified for 8.3.7 - the
       current page links 468430, which is a modern id.
   PICK PER SPEC, since there is only one slot:
     BREWMASTER  202335 Double Barrel - the only stun in reach.
     MISTWEAVER  Zen Focus Tea - the only thing that keeps a heal landing.
     WINDWALKER  287681 Turbo Fists - a 90% slow on the button a Windwalker
                 presses most, plus parry while it channels.

8. PVP TALENTS THAT LOOK AVAILABLE AND ARE NOT
   GRAPPLE WEAPON, spell 233759 (V)
     A 45 sec disarm. The live page says level 20, and that is a trap: the
     same page's patch notes say 8.0.1 converted it "from a rank 28 honor
     talent to a level 40 PvP talent", and 9.0.1 was the patch that lowered
     it to 20 for Windwalker. On 8.3.7 it is a level 40 talent and a 29
     cannot take it. It also belonged to Windwalker only in this era.
   COUNTERACT MAGIC, spell 353502 (V)
     Currently level 30, so it misses a 29 by one level. Its BfA
     requirement is unverified, but nothing on the page suggests it was
     ever lower.
   Everything else in the monk PvP category - Avert Harm, Microbrew, Hot
   Trub, Niuzao's Essence, Mighty Ox Kick, Disabling Reach, Eminence,
   Refreshing Breeze, Wind Waker, Peaceweaver, Nimble Brew, Perpetual
   Paralysis, Predestination - has no level requirement read for this file,
   and none of their ids are quoted here. Assume they are gated until the
   server says otherwise.

9. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE BOTS
   One normal talent, one PvP talent, chosen per spec:
     Brewmaster  196607 Eye of the Tiger + 202335 Double Barrel
     Mistweaver  115098 Chi Wave        + Zen Focus Tea
     Windwalker  123986 Chi Burst       + 287681 Turbo Fists
   There is no second decision to make and no alternate build at 29. The
   spec choice is the build.
   UNVERIFIED, and worth checking on the server itself before the roster is
   written: whether the core gives a 29 one PvP slot or none, whether
   Zen Pulse is still in the Mistweaver row, and whether the BfA-era level
   requirements on the PvP talents match the current pages.
