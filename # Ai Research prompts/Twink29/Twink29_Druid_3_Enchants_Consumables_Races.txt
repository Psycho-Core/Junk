TWINK29 - DRUID - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, PROFESSIONS, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29). ALT = needs a higher-level crafter or content.

1. ENCHANTS - ONE PER SLOT, ALL INSIDE THE CLASSIC CAP
   The wearer needs no profession. Skill numbers are the enchanter's; every
   one is 225 or below, so no higher-level crafter is needed. No classic
   enchant carries a character level requirement, and none carries an item
   level limit except the weapon oils in section 3.
   CLOAK    Enchant Cloak - Lesser Agility, spell/enchant 13882, +3 Agility
            Skill 225. Formula 11206 (drops, the enchanter must own it).
            2x Lesser Nether Essence 11174. Rod: Runed Truesilver 11145.
   CLOAK    Enchant Cloak - Greater Defense, spell/enchant 13746, +50 armor
            Skill 205, trainer-taught. 3x Vision Dust 11137. Same rod.
            The choice: this on a bear, the agility one on everything else,
            or carry both cloaks and swap.
   CHEST    Enchant Chest - Greater Health, spell/enchant 13640, +35 health
            Skill 160, trainer-taught. 3x Soul Dust 11083. Rod: Runed
            Golden 11130. The only chest enchant inside the cap.
   BRACERS  Enchant Bracer - Strength, spell/enchant 13661, +5 Strength
            Skill 180, trainer-taught. 1x Vision Dust 11137. Melee only.
            The caster bracer enchant inside the cap: unverified.
   GLOVES   Enchant Gloves - Agility, spell/enchant 13815, +5 Agility
            Skill 210, trainer-taught. 1x Lesser Nether Essence 11174 +
            1x Vision Dust 11137. Rod: Runed Truesilver 11145.
   BOOTS    Enchant Boots - Minor Speed, spell/enchant 13890, "a slight
            movement speed increase" (tooltip's own words, the percentage is
            unverified). Skill 225, trainer-taught. 1x Small Radiant Shard
            11177, 1x Aquamarine 7909, 1x Lesser Nether Essence 11174.
            The most valuable enchant in the bracket.
   WEAPON   Enchant Weapon - Striking, spell/enchant 13693, +3 weapon damage
            Skill 195, trainer-taught. 2x Greater Mystic Essence 11135 +
            1x Large Glowing Shard 11139. Rod: Runed Golden 11130.
            Flat damage on every swing.
   HEAD     Nothing. No head enchant inside the 225 cap was found; every
            classic arcanum needs a higher skill. Treat the slot as empty.
   Scroll versions of these enchants: not verified. Assume a crafter.

2. SHOULDERS AND LEGS - THE TWO SLOTS THAT NEED A WARNING
   SHOULDERS - the bind-on-account heirloom idea does not work. The Mists
   of Pandaria shoulder inscriptions are account-bound, but the level
   requirement is on the character who applies them:
     83006 Greater Tiger Fang Inscription - item level 90, requires 85. ALT
     87580 Tiger Fang Inscription - requires 85. ALT
     44133 Greater Inscription of the Axe - requires 80. ALT
     50335 Greater Inscription of the Axe - requires 80, needs Sons of
           Hodir Exalted. ALT twice over.
   So an heirloom shoulder on a 29 carries no inscription. Nothing in the
   1-225 Inscription band is a shoulder item.
   LEGS - no separate leg armor exists in the classic tier. The slot takes
   the same armor kit as the chest.
   ARMOR KITS - permanent, one per item, chest, legs, hands or feet. The
   item level limit is the kit's, not the wearer's:
     2304 Light Armor Kit    +8 armor,  no item level limit
     2313 Medium Armor Kit   +16 armor, no item level limit stated
     4265 Heavy Armor Kit    +24 armor, item level 15 and up. The best a 29
            can apply; four of them are +96 armor. Skill 150, 5x Heavy
            Leather 4234 per kit, crafted by spell 3780.
     8173 Thick Armor Kit    +32 armor, item level 25 and up, but usable
            only at level 30. ALT
     15564 Rugged Armor Kit  +40 armor, item level 35 and up, level 40. ALT
   Arms and shoulders take nothing at this level.

4. THE TWO PROFESSIONS TO TAKE
   Engineering first, unconditionally. It is the only profession whose
   output a 29 uses every fight, and nothing on the list needs the Gnomish
   or Goblin specialisation, which needs level 30 and is ALT.
     4390  Iron Grenade        skill 175, spell 3962. 132-218 Fire and a
           3 sec stun in 3 yards, 1 min cooldown.
     4394  Big Iron Bomb       skill 190, spell 3967. 149-201 Fire and a
           3 sec stun in 5 yards.
     10507 Solid Dynamite      skill 200.
     4384  Explosive Sheep      skill 150, a pet that charges and explodes.
     4381  Minor Recombobulator trinket, 10 charges, no level requirement.
     4388  Discombobulator Ray  5 charges, disorients and slows.
     10518 Parachute Cloak     back slot, +8 Agility, binds on equip.
     4385  Green Tinted Goggles ilvl 30, cloth head, 35 armor, +8 Stamina,
           +7 Spirit. Head armor type does not gate a 29.
     4368  Flying Tiger Goggles ilvl 20, 27 armor, +4 Stamina, +4 Spirit.
     109076 Goblin Glider Kit   bought, not crafted; a slow fall on demand.
   Second slot, in order of value to a druid:
     Leatherworking - the Heavy Armor Kits 4265 above, four of them, plus
       the leather pieces in the items file.
     Alchemy - it supplies the twink's own potions and elixirs, and it is
       the only way to keep a roster stocked with 2459 and 5634.
     Tailoring - Heavy Silk Bandage 6451 at 210 and bags.
     Enchanting - no self-only perk at classic tier; it is a service
       profession and a disenchanting mule.
     Blacksmithing - Solid Sharpening Stones 7964 and Solid Weightstones
       7965 at skill 200, if the twink swings a staff or mace and the stone
       is confirmed to work.
     Jewelcrafting - nothing. No item at or below item level 29 has a gem
       socket, and the gem cuts start at 300.
     Inscription - nothing wearable inside the cap. Herbalism, Mining and
       Skinning - supplier characters only.
   Caveat carried from the professions set: the 225 ceiling comes from 3.x
   pages. Since 8.0.1 the four ranks were rolled into one Classic bar with
   a 300 cap, so a 29 may be able to go higher. Read the trainer window.

5. RACES - SIX LEGAL DRUIDS, RACIAL SPELL IDS
   Alliance: night elf, worgen, Kul Tiran. Horde: tauren, Highmountain
   tauren, Zandalari troll. The three allied races start at level 20, which
   makes them legal 29 twinks.
   TAUREN - the best pick for anything that expects to be hit.
     20549 War Stomp, 0.5 sec cast, stuns up to 5 enemies within 8 yards
           for 2 sec. The only hard crowd-control a 29 druid has. Cooldown
           is 1.5 min on the current page and 2 min in the classic entry;
           the 8.3.7 value is the one to read in the spellbook. The classic
           entry also carries "cannot be used while shapeshifted" - check
           that in bear form before planning around it, because it decides
           the whole racial.
     20550 Endurance, passive Stamina scaling with level.
     154743 Brawn, +2% critical strike bonus damage and healing.
     20552 Cultivation, Herbalism bonus and faster gathering. The 8.3.7
           magnitude is unverified.
     20551 Nature Resistance, Nature damage taken -1%.
   NIGHT ELF - the base sitter and flag watcher.
     58984 Shadowmeld, drop into stealth while standing still.
     20582 Quickness, avoidance. 21009 Elusiveness, stealth detection.
     20583 Nature Resistance, -1% Nature damage.
     20585 Wisp Spirit, faster movement as a ghost.
     154748 Touch of Elune, +1% Haste at night or +1% Critical Strike by
           day, added patch 6.0.2, so it is live in 8.3.7.
   WORGEN - the flag runner.
     68992 Darkflight, +40% movement for 10 sec.
     68975 Viciousness, +1% critical strike.
     68976 Aberration, -1% Shadow and Nature damage taken.
     68978 Flayer, Skinning only. 68996 Two Forms, 87840 Running Wild.
   KUL TIRAN (Alliance allied) - 287712 Haymaker, 291628 Brush It Off,
     291622 Child of the Sea, 291442 Jack of All Trades, 291417 Rime of
     the Ancient Mariner. Numbers and whether Haymaker's knockback stun
     works at 29: unverified.
   HIGHMOUNTAIN TAUREN (Horde allied) - 255654 Bull Rush (charge and
     knockback), 255659 Rugged Tenacity (Stamina), 255658 Mountaineer (fall
     damage), 255656 Waste Not Want Not, 255655 Pride of Ironhorn. Has no
     stun to replace War Stomp; the tauren War Stomp is not inherited.
   ZANDALARI TROLL (Horde allied) - 291944 Regeneratin' (a channelled heal
     that breaks when the caster takes damage), 292752 Embrace of the Loa
     (passive 292751:
     one of four loa blessings), 291619 City of Gold, 281954 Pterrordax
     Swoop (slow fall). If Regeneratin' is unbroken for its full channel it
     is the strongest heal in the bracket; the 29 numbers are unverified.
   RACES TO AVOID - none are illegal, but do not pick a race whose racial
   you cannot test at 29. That rules out Kul Tiran and Highmountain tauren
   for a first roster, and it rules out Zandalari unless Regeneratin' is
   confirmed, because their value is a single unverified ability each.
   Everything else is a neutral stat package.

6. SPECS AND FORMS AT LEVEL 29
   Specs: all four are open - Balance, Feral, Guardian, Restoration. The
   spec is chosen at level 10 and can be changed at the class trainer in a
   capital. Only one spec is active; the other three are inaccessible
   abilities, not a second spec.
   Forms in the 8.3.7 spellbook at 29 - the learned levels are not verified
   here, the spellbook decides:
     5487  Bear Form      tank form, armor and health multiplier, rage
     768   Cat Form       melee form, energy, combo points
     5215  Prowl          stealth, from Cat Form
     1850  Dash           sprint, from Cat Form
     783   Travel Form    ground speed outdoors, and a shapeshift that
                          breaks roots
     1066  Aquatic Form   swim speed
     24858 Moonkin Form   Balance only; caster armor and spell damage
   Not available: 33943 Flight Form and the flight it needs, and every
   form-granting talent past the level 15 row.
   Guardian and Feral cannot be played simultaneously: a bear that wants to
   damage is in the wrong spec, not the wrong form.
   One PvP talent slot exists at 29, opened at level 20, and it holds a
   trinket-type talent - see the talents file.
