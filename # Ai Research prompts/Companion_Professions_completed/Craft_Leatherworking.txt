BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - LEATHERWORKING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
=================================================================================================
Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client, no human at the keyboard) levels
Leatherworking on a TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Everything below was read from a
source fetched this week (2026-10-26); anything without a readable source says
"unverified" instead of being guessed. Craft counts are exactly the counts the linked
guides print (they warn that yellow/green recipes need more crafts - those warnings are
kept where printed).

need Leatherworking 25.
Base-rank note: the wiki training table still shows the classic four titles
(Apprentice/Journeyman/Expert/Artisan at 1-75/75-150/150-225/225-300) and the BfA
row shows "1 - 150" while every 8.3.7 source says the BfA cap is 175 (section 9
item 1).

     (NPCHandler 97/130), vendor purchases (Amber Tanning Oil from Aman 141609,
     Arctic Fur from Braeg 32515, patterns from Almaador 21432, Alurmi 21643,
     Braeg 32515, Krogo 64054, Tanner Pang 64094, traders 87057/87549) need the

     incl. dungeon leads, Suramar to Masquerade 42079 + Demon Flesh 40211, Uniting
     the Isles 43341 (friendly with every Legion faction), the WoD chain (Dirty
     Note 114877 from Karnoth 75043 in quest 34019 / Tattered Frostwolf Shroud
     116173 from Dorogg 74254 in quest 33410, then the Tannery work-order quest),

     ... even on the day it is supposed to spawn"), and the guide's EU/NA date

     of Time Honored (Drums of Panic), Aldor/Scryers exclusivity (wiki: Daenril
     not for Aldor, Korim not for Scryers), and BfA rank-2/3 vendors only sell to

     Isles world drop, and the Withered Army Training 43943 chest; also Legion
