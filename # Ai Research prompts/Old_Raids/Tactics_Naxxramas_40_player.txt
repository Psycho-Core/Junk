Server: private BfA 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.

in patch 3.0.2 (wiki pages say so on every boss) and replaced

that this server restores the original; whether it does, and
with what scripting, is unverified. Values are the original
40-player values read on warcraft.wiki.gg (Classic) pages.
All creature entries: unverified (not read on a list page
for this step). Map id and coordinates: unverified.
Spell ids are given only where a fetched page links them;
everything else is named with id unverified.

SPIDER WING
1. ANUB'REKHAN (+2 Crypt Guards at pull, +1 per Locust Swarm)
PHASES: Locust Swarm cycles - he slows 40% for 20 sec and
  the tank kites him around the room's edge while ranged
  kill him at range; a new Crypt Guard joins per swarm.
TANK: kite during Swarm (tank stays ahead of him, never in
  the 30-yd swarm); offtank holds Crypt Guards away.
GROUP: nobody within 30 yd during Swarm (28786 stacks:
  875-1125/2 sec + pacify/silence effect); Impale 28783 is
  a line at a random player - spread so the line is empty.
GROUND: 28785 Locust Swarm aura around the boss (30 yd).
ADD ORDER: P1 Crypt Guards, boss between swarms.
ABILITY: 28783 Impale, 28785/28786 Locust Swarm, Crypt
  Guard adds (abilities unverified).
AI RULE: when Locust Swarm starts, run - the whole group
  stays 30+ yards from him until it ends.

2. GRAND WIDOW FAERLINA (+ Worshippers + Followers)
PHASES: Enrage 28798 cycles; a mind-controlled Worshipper
  sacrificed on her removes/prevents it (page: sacrifice
  AFTER she enrages for a full 60-sec stop; before, only
  a 30-sec delay). Keep at least one Worshipper alive.
TANK: hold her centre; adds tanked at the sides.
GROUP: decurse/poison cleanse (Poison Bolt Volley 28796);
  move out of Rain of Fire 28794 (1850-2150/2 sec).
GROUND: 28794 Rain of Fire patches.
ADD ORDER: NEVER kill all Worshippers - one is needed per
  Enrage. P1 Followers, Worshippers CC'd (priest MC) and
  sacrificed on Enrage. Add entries unverified.
ABILITY: 28796 Poison Bolt Volley (D-Poison by family,
  unverified), 28794 Rain of Fire, 28798 Enrage (+75%
  speed, +150 dmg, 30 min if unsacrificed).
AI RULE: sacrifice a Worshipper the moment she enrages;
  never let the last Worshipper die to damage.

3. MAEXXNA
PHASES: Web Spray every 40 sec incapacitates the raid 8
  sec; Frenzy at 30% (attack/damage up, tranq-able by
  family - unverified here).
TANK: tank her in place, facing away (Poison Shock frontal
  15 yd). Pre-heal/pre-HoT the tank before every Web Spray.
GROUP: Web Wrap sends 3 players to the wall cocooned
  (650-850/2 sec) - ranged kill cocoons immediately
  (cocoon ~6000 health, page). Timers read: Wrap at 20 sec
  then every 40 sec; Spray every 40 sec.
GROUND: none; the web wall is the wrap destination.
ADD ORDER: cocoons are targets, not adds. Spiderling spawns
  exist in the encounter family - unverified here.
ABILITY (ids from old external links in source; treat as
  read-but-legacy): Web Wrap, Web Spray (1750-2250, cannot
  be resisted - page), Poison Shock (1750-2250 frontal),
  Necrotic Poison (healing -90%).
AI RULE: free cocooned companions before the next Web
  Spray, and have heals already rolling before each Spray.

PLAGUE WING
4. NOTH THE PLAGUEBRINGER
PHASES: ground phase (curses + skeleton waves) alternating
  with balcony phase: he teleports away ~every 90 sec
  (unverified timing) and skeleton waves attack; he returns
  with more health on the wave each cycle in the original.
TANK: hold him centre; skeleton waves tanked at their
  spawn edges.
GROUP: decurse Curse of the Plaguebringer INSTANTLY
  (29213, D-Curse): if it expires undispelled it infects
  the 20 nearest with Wrath of the Plaguebringer (page).
  Spread in small clusters so one miss is not a raid bomb.
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: P1 skeleton waves during balcony, boss on ground.
ABILITY: 29213 Curse of the Plaguebringer (D-Curse),
  Wrath of the Plaguebringer (id unverified), Blink and
  skeleton summons (ids unverified).
AI RULE: decurse before anything else - a single expired
  curse damages twenty companions.

5. HEIGAN THE UNCLEAN (the dance)
PHASES: 135-sec cycle (page): P1 90 sec he is on the floor,
  slow Eruptions, tank moves him between marked points;
  P2 45 sec he is on his platform (untargetable by melee),
  Eruptions speed up and EVERYONE dances. Platform must be
  empty in P2 (platform disease DoT read).
TANK: in P1 move him point to point between eruption waves.
GROUP: safe zones run 1-2-3-4-3-2-1 (page) and RESET to
  zone 1 at every phase change; move at your own pace -
  do not follow another player's position (latency note).
  Teleported companions run back from the tunnel mouth;
  kill Eye Stalks if they are up on the way (500 health,
  Mind Flay - tunnel section read).
GROUND: lava Eruptions - the entire mechanic; only one of
  the four floor sections is safe at a time (spell id
  unverified).
ADD ORDER: Eye Stalks (in the teleport tunnel) when present.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Eruption, Teleport (players to
  the tunnel), Mana Burn, Decrepit Fever (D-Disease by
  family - unverified).
AI RULE: always know which floor quarter is safe and be
  moving to the next one early - never stand still in P2.

6. LOATHEB
PHASES: single phase with a healing lockout: Corrupted Mind
  lets each healer cast a healing spell only once per
  minute (aura mechanic; exact form unverified here).
  Inevitable Doom starts at 2 min, every 30 sec, then
  every 15 sec from 5 min (page consumable table).
TANK: standard hold; Spore groups rotate.
GROUP: kill Spores in an assigned GROUP ORDER - Fungal
  Bloom (spore death buff: +50% crit, no threat) must go
  to one group at a time or it is wasted (spores every
  ~13 sec, page); survive Doom with own consumables,
  bandages, healthstones (heals are rationed).
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: Spores in assigned order (kill), boss otherwise.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Corrupted Mind, Inevitable Doom,
  Fungal Bloom, poison aura (cleanse totem/judgement note
  on page).
AI RULE: never cast a heal outside your assigned window,
  and only your group kills its spore.

ABOMINATION WING
7. PATCHWERK
PHASES: single phase; 7-minute enrage (page: ~9500 raid DPS
  needed at level). The purest gear/heal check here.
TANK: main tank holds him; 2-3 OFFTANKS stand in melee as
  Hateful Strike targets - Hateful Strike hits the
  highest-current-health melee target after the MT
  (22100-29900 pre-mitigation, every ~1.2 sec, never crits
  or crushes - page), so offtanks must be kept topped and
  melee DPS must NEVER be at full health near him.
GROUP: ranged max distance; healers split per offtank with
  proactive pre-heals (page's healing allocations).
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: none (all trash is linked and cleared first).
ABILITY: Hateful Strike (id unverified in chunk; mechanic
  as above), enrage at 7 min.
AI RULE: if you are not a designated Hateful tank, keep
  your health below the offtanks' - full-health melee
  eats a Hateful Strike and dies.

8. GROBBULUS
PHASES: single phase; the tank kites him continuously
  around the room because of the clouds (below).
TANK: kite in a slow circuit, never double back through
  the poison trail; ranged stay on the upper ramp side.
GROUP: the Mutating Injection target runs AWAY from the
  raid before it expires (it becomes a cloud and damages/
  poisons nearby); Slime Spray is frontal - only the tank
  in front.
GROUND: Poison Clouds left behind him as he moves and
  dropped by expired injections (ids unverified) - never
  stand in the trail; the kite path is the mechanic.
ADD ORDER: Fallout Slimes from spray victims (unverified
  detail) - kill them, boss returns.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Poison Cloud, Slime Spray
  (frontal cone), Mutating Injection (D-Disease/Magic
  unverified).
AI RULE: keep moving behind the tank's path and never
  cross the poison trail; injected = run from the group.

9. GLUTH
PHASES: Decimate every ~90 sec (unverified timing) drops
  EVERYTHING (raid + zombies) to 5% health and Gluth then
  tries to eat the zombies to heal.
TANK: hold Gluth; a kiter (hunter/mage) rounds up Zombie
  Chow across the room, slowed/rooted in a pile.
GROUP: before Decimate, zombies are CC'd in place; after
  Decimate, AoE the whole zombie pile instantly before
  Gluth reaches one (each eaten zombie heals him ~5% -
  unverified %). Mortal Wound on the tank: rotate.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: P1 Zombie Chow after each Decimate (they are
  the boss's health pool), boss otherwise. Boss's own
  Frenzy is tranq-able (family, unverified).
ABILITY (ids unverified): Decimate, Devour (eat zombie),
  Mortal Wound, Frenzy, Enrage timer (10 min, unverified).
AI RULE: when Decimate hits, AoE the zombies first - every
  zombie Gluth eats is boss health back.

10. THADDIUS (Feugen + Stalagg first)
PHASES: P1 kill Feugen and Stalagg on their two platforms
  SIMULTANEOUSLY (within 5 sec of each other or the dead
  one resurrects at full - page). Both throw/swap players
  between platforms (mechanic read). Then 15 sec to jump
  to the centre platform before Thaddius activates.
  P2 Polarity Shift 28089 every 30 sec (3-sec cast, page).
TANK: one tank per wight; Thaddius tanked centre, tank
  moves with his own charge group (page: threat is hard).
GROUP: charges: stand ONLY with your own polarity (left =
  negative, right = positive by the common assignment,
  page); on a shift you have 5 sec to cross - run straight
  through/near him by the shortest path. NO combat rez in
  P2 (a rezzed player has no charge and kills neighbours,
  page). Missed the jump = stand alone until next shift.
GROUND: none; the two charge zones are positions, not floor.
ADD ORDER: Feugen/Stalagg together (P1 both), then boss.
ABILITY: 28135 Static Field (Feugen: mana burn aura 30 yd),
  28134 Power Surge (Stalagg +200% speed 10 sec), 28169
  Chain Lightning (up to 15, growing), 28089 Polarity Shift,
  28084/28059 Negative/Positive Charge (opposite = 2000/5
  sec to neighbours), 28299 Ball Lightning (only if nobody
  in melee), 27680 Berserk (5 min into P2).
AI RULE (the rule of this raid): after every Polarity
  Shift, check your own charge and stand only with the
  same sign; someone must always be in his melee range.

DEATHKNIGHT WING
11. INSTRUCTOR RAZUVIOUS
PHASES: single phase; he is tanked BY THE ADDS: two Death
  Knight Understudies (4 in the original 40 - count
  unverified for this step) are priest mind-controlled;
  only their Shield Wall rotation survives his hits
  (Disrupting Shout raid-wide, Jagged Knife - names read
  in the encounter family; specifics unverified here).
TANK: the MC'd Understudy tanks; controlling priest rotates
  Shield Wall/bone-barrier equivalent before each big hit;
  a second priest holds the spare Understudy.
GROUP: nobody else tanks him, ever - his melee one-shots
  non-understudies (era design; unverified numbers). Kill
  order at the end: he dies first, adds after (in the
  original, killing him ends it; understudy fate after -
  unverified).
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: NEVER kill the Understudies before the boss -
  they are the tanks (do-not-kill until he dies).
ABILITY (ids unverified): Disrupting Shout, Jagged Knife,
  Understudy taunt/shield abilities (on the possessed bar).
AI RULE: if you are the priest, your job is the Understudy's
  defensive buttons, not your own healing.

12. GOTHIK THE HARVESTER
PHASES: P1 two rooms split by a gate: undead side kills
  spawn waves that become spectral versions on the living
  side when killed there (trainees/knights/riders + horses
  in escalating waves - compositions unverified here).
  P2 at a fixed timer (or when waves end) he descends and
  is fought in the middle; Shadow Bolt volleys + Harvest
  Soul stacking debuff (unverified detail).
TANK: one tank per side in P1; boss tank centre in P2.
GROUP: balance DPS between sides - killing too fast on one
  side floods the other side with spectral adds. Never open
  the gate early (the sides must stay separate in P1).
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER (per side): riders/knights before trainees by
  era practice - unverified; spectral versions mirror it.
ABILITY (ids unverified): summon waves, teleport (P2),
  Shadow Bolt, Harvest Soul.
AI RULE: kill at the pace of the other room - your speed
  is their add spawn rate.

13. THE FOUR HORSEMEN
PHASES: single fight, four bosses in four corners, each
  with a stacking Mark (Mark of Blaumeux/Korth'azz/
  Mograine/Zeliek): marks stack on anyone near that
  horseman and damage grows per stack - tanks and groups
  ROTATE corners before stacks get lethal (original era:
  ~5-6 stacks rotation, unverified number here).
TANK: at least two tanks per corner in rotation; the back
  two (Blaumeux/Zeliek) are often ranged/warlock-tanked
  (Zeliek is a caster who stays put; Blaumeux summons
  void zones - unverified detail).
GROUP: nobody stands near a horseman without a job; swap
  corners on the rotation call, passing through the centre.
GROUND: Blaumeux Void Zone (black circles - move out; id
  unverified). Korth'azz Meteor (soak by stacking briefly
  to split damage - era mechanic, unverified here).
ADD ORDER: kill order era-standard is Mograine, Korth'azz,
  Blaumeux, Zeliek - UNVERIFIED for this file; each death
  removes one mark source but the others enrage in the
  WotLK version - 40-player behaviour unverified.
ABILITY (ids unverified): four Marks, Void Zone, Meteor,
  Zeliek's Holy Wrath chain, Mograine's Righteous Fire.
AI RULE: count your mark stacks; on the call, leave the
  corner - stacks, not the horsemen, kill the raid.

FROSTWYRM LAIR
14. SAPPHIRON
PHASES: ground/air cycles: air phase he launches Frost
  Breath over the room - everyone hides BEHIND the ice
  blocks made from icebolted players (those players must
  be freed after). Chill aura ticks the whole fight.
TANK: ground phase centre, facing away; frost-resist
  framing is era; at 120 the blocks mechanic is the point.
GROUP: Ice Bolt freezes a companion in a block (they live;
  do not break early if the Breath is coming); everyone
  else lines up behind a block for Frost Breath, then
  frees them. Curse of Life (D-Curse by family) decurse
  rotation - unverified detail.
GROUND: Blizzard patches (move out), Frost Breath sweep
  (air phase - hide behind blocks only).
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Ice Bolt, Frost Breath, Chill
  (aura), Blizzard, Tail Sweep, Cleave by family.
AI RULE: when he takes off, get behind an ice block - not
  near it, directly behind it - then free the frozen.

15. KEL'THUZAD
PHASES: P1 waves in a circle room: kill timed waves of
  Soldiers/Shadows/Abominations for ~5 min while he is
  untargetable (wave list unverified here). P2 fight him;
  at 45% P3: Guardians of Icecrown join (big adds) while
  he continues everything.
TANK: P2 centre; Guardians off-tanked at the edges in P3.
GROUP: spread for Frost Blast (freezes a player and
  neighbours in ice - spread so it takes one); the mind-
  controlled companion is CC'd instantly (Chains of Kel'-
  Thuzad, up to several targets); mana users watch Detonate
  Mana (a companion with it must run from casters - burns
  their mana with damage). Interrupt Frostbolt casts where
  possible; Frostbolt Volley is raid-wide (unavoidable).
GROUND: Shadow Fissure - a red circle under a player that
  kills anyone still in it when it erupts (era: ~3 sec -
  unverified numbers; MOVE is the rule).
ADD ORDER: P1 waves as they come (abominations before
  casters by era practice - unverified); P3 Guardians
  P1 for offtanks, boss for everyone else.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Chains of Kel'Thuzad (MC),
  Detonate Mana, Frost Blast, Shadow Fissure, Frostbolt
  (I - kick it), Frostbolt Volley, Summon Guardians.
AI RULE: if a red fissure opens under you, move NOW; CC
  mind-controlled companions before they kill a healer.
