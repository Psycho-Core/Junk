3. POISONS - THE SECTION THAT MATTERS MOST HERE
   12 PASS  Poisons are spellbook abilities, not crafted items, since
      5.0.4: "Poisons are now Spellbook abilities that affect both weapons
      at once instead of crafted items" (V). So the prompt's "where to buy
      the ingredients" has no answer in this build, and the file says so
      instead of inventing a vendor.
   13 PASS  Deadly Poison is an Assassination ability in BfA, lasts 1 hour,
      30% application chance, and the 2018-08-10 hotfix (inside the 8.3.7
      window) makes it deal 20% less damage against players (V). That
      hotfix is dated after 8.0.1 and before the end of BfA, so it applies
      to build 35662 - the file's reasoning is sound.
   14 PASS  Wound Poison: Assassination only since 7.0.3 ("Now available
      only to Assassination"), learned at 25, Lethal so it excludes Deadly
      Poison, healing reduction 25% and 20% in PvP per the 2017-02-21
      hotfix, 24% with Master Poisoner (V). The non-stacking claim is
      supported by 9.0.1 describing stacking as the new behaviour
      ("previously 25% and did not stack").
   15 PASS  Crippling Poison at 29 is unavailable, and this is the audit's
      most important confirmation: 7.0.3 made it Assassination only,
      7.1.0 moved it to 24, and 7.1.5 moved it to 38 ("Now learned at
      level 38 (was 24)"), after which no BfA patch touches it. It only
      came back down in 9.0.1 (all rogues, 33) and 10.0.0 (10). A 29 rogue
      of any spec cannot have it, and the file's warning about guides is
      justified.
   16 PASS  The 50% slow with 30% in PvP: 2017-01-31 hotfix, "now reduce
      movement by 50% (was 30%). The reduction remains at 30% in PvP" (V).
   17 PASS  Outlaw and Subtlety have no poisons in 8.3.7. Nothing in the
      three poison pages gives either spec a poison before 9.0.1 returned
      them to the class, so the disadvantage the file reports is real.
