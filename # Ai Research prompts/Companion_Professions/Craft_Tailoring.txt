BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - TAILORING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
=========================================================================================
Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client, no human at the keyboard) levels
Tailoring on a TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Everything below was read from a source
fetched this week (2026-10-27); anything without a readable source says "unverified"
instead of being guessed. Craft counts are exactly the counts the linked guides print
(they warn that yellow/green recipes need more crafts - those warnings are kept where
printed).

264629 as both "Reward from" and "Triggered by" that quest (section 9 item 16). Spell

Effect #2 Learn Skill Step (197) Value 10 - the same double grant the Leatherworking
file records for spell 201705 (Learn 195119 + Step 165 value 10).
The guide then gates recipes behind 38 Tailoring profession quests (the guide's own
count, not independently verified): "you should get to level 45 and finish every
Tailoring quest before you continue"; the key quest is The Queen's Grace Loom 38970
(around the 30th in the chain) rewarding Imbued Silkweave Bracers spell 185926 and
Imbued Silkweave spell 185962 (which unlock the Imbued Silkweave vendors/quests used
at 90-100); Uniting the Isles 43341 (Archmage Khadgar npc 90417) unlocks World
Quests - the guide prints "(the reputation requirement was removed)" for this quest
(section 9 item 17); profession World Quests need Legion Tailoring 25; most rank-3
Silkweave recipes come from World Quests.
Base-rank note: the wiki training table shows the classic four titles
(Apprentice/Journeyman/Expert/Artisan at 1-75/75-150/150-225/225-300), Master 1-75
(Outland), Grand Master 1-75 (Northrend), Illustrious Grand Master 1-75 (Cataclysm),
Zen Master 1-75 (Pandaria), Draenor Master 1-100, Legion Master 1-100, and the BfA
row reads "Kul Tiran Master / Zandalari Master" with Skill Range "1 - 150" while
every 8.3.7 source says the BfA cap is 175 (section 9 item 1). The wiki article page
carries an "This article is outdated" banner.
