Purpose  : the positions, the phase numbers, the ability timers and the

================================================================================
0. HOW TO READ THIS FILE
================================================================================
Every number carries a tag saying where it came from. Nothing is estimated.
Where no source I could read resolves a value, the line says "unverified" and
section 13 says what would resolve it.

  [SRV]    server boss scripts for map 2070, 8.3.x-era TrinityCore fork
           (HavenWoW/BFA-HavenCore src/server/scripts/Zandalar/
           BattleOfDazarAlor/). These are the ONLY coordinates in this file.
           They are script-supplied spawn positions, not client spawn rows.
  [DBM]    Deadly Boss Mods, tag 8.3.34, DBM-ZuldazarRaid modules
           (ChampionofLight, Grong, JadefireMasters, Opulence,
           ConclaveoftheChosen, Rastakhan, Mekkatorque, StormwallBlockade,
           Jaina) - first-cast times, cooldowns, phase triggers
  [GUIDE]  MMO-Champion / Icy Veins / Warcraft Wiki / Wowpedia 2019
           write-ups - phase health percentages and geometry in words
  [TAC]    the client-derived ids already present in your own tactics
           document, BfA_8.3.7_companion_tactics_v2.txt section 15
  [CALC]   arithmetic done on two [SRV] coordinates in this file
  unverified  not in any source I could read

COORDINATE ORDER. Every coordinate is (x, y, z) in yards on map 2070, exactly
as written in the script, with orientation in radians (0 = +x axis) where the
script supplies one.

THE BIG DIFFERENCE FROM ULDIR. For Uldir a world DB export gave 632 spawn rows
and every boss position was verified. For Dazar'alor no world DB spawn export
could be found at all: the instance is spawned entirely from the world
database, and the database dumps that are publicly reachable do not carry map
2070's creature table. What IS reachable are the server boss scripts, and they
hard-code 23 useful coordinates. Those are used and tagged [SRV]. Every boss
without a script coordinate says so - none of them is guessed.

================================================================================
1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS
================================================================================
1.1 [SRV] is a TrinityCore-family BfA core. The scripts are partial: they
    implement Champion of the Light (both faction versions), Grong, Jadefire
    Masters, Conclave of the Chosen, King Rastakhan, High Tinker Mekkatorque
    and Opulence. The Opulence file is 219 lines but contains no spawn or
    movement constants. There is NO Jaina script and NO Stormwall Blockade
    script in the tree, which is why those three encounters carry no
    coordinates at all in this file.
1.2 The script coordinates are hard-coded constants and movement points. A
    movement point is a scripted destination, not necessarily a spawn: it is
    tagged as such in the text.
1.3 [DBM] 8.3.34 is the last BfA build of the DBM-ZuldazarRaid modules, so its
    timers are the 8.3.x timers.
1.4 Battle of Dazar'alor is faction-flavoured but mechanically identical
    (Icy Veins notes every encounter is mechanically identical and that both
    factions face all 9). Faction-specific names and
    ids are given side by side; the timers are the same.

================================================================================
4.1 IDENTITY [TAC]/[DBM]
  Horde:   journal 2333 | Frida Ironbellows, creature 144680
  Alliance: journal 2344 | Ra'wani Kanae,   creature 144683
  DungeonEncounter 2265. The tactics document lists both creatures as

4.2 SPAWN [SRV]
  No boss spawn constant exists. The arena anchors are the add spawns:
      Horde arena:    (-631.000, 1089.000, 321.000) o 0.77
      Alliance arena: (-2023.000, 833.000, 6.000) o 4.73
  Those are the add spawn points (see 4.6), and they are the best verified

  TANK POSITION: no coordinate in any readable source -> unverified. What is

  and the boss's Sanitizing Strike is a frontal the tank must point away from

4.3 PHASES [GUIDE]
  There is no health-gated phase change. The fight is a seal cycle:
      Crusader's Seals (Seal of Retribution, then the next seal, and so on)
      Forces of the Crusade (the add waves that come with each seal)
  Seals advance on ENERGY, not health: at 100 energy the seal is released and
  its extra effect fires (Wowhead Encounter Journal outline). Call to Arms
  (283662) summons 1 Darkforged Crusader and 2 Anointed Disciples each cycle.
  The only health trigger is Avenging Wrath: cast at 30% health on every
  difficulty, +30% damage (Wowhead and Wowpedia both state 30%).

4.4 MAIN ABILITY TIMERS, seconds from pull [DBM]
  Wave of Light 283598            first 13.1, cooldown 10.5
  Judgment: Righteousness 283933  first 50.7 (52.3 on a variant), cooldown 50.3
  Judgment: Reckoning 284474      first 52.3, cooldown 50.3
  Prayer for the Fallen 287469    MYTHIC ONLY: first 25.5, cooldown 50.2
  Blinding Faith add line         first 13.4, cooldown 13.4 (caveat below)
  Call to Arms 283662             cooldown 104.6, but the timer is commented
                                  out in the module: "Adds come with phase
                                  change, redundant"
  Sacred Blade 283573             no timer at all, stack-driven only
  CAVEAT: DBM registers its 13.4 s timer against spell 284474, which is
  Judgment: Reckoning, while the Blinding Faith warning it drives is built on
  283650. One of those two is a copy-paste slip in the 8.3.34 module. The
  13.4 s number is real; which spell it measures is unverified.

4.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Sacred Blade 283573 - assigned to the TANK.
     Trigger: stack count (module warns on stack, then asks for a taunt).
     Action: tank swap.
  b) Wave of Light 283598 - assigned to a DISPELLER or PURGER.
     Trigger: the cast. Action: dispel the healing buff from the adds, or move
     the adds out of the wave; DBM raises both a dispel warning and a dodge
     warning for the same id.
  c) Call to Arms 283662 - assigned to the OFF TANK.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START. Action: pick up the 1 crusader + 2 disciples.
  d) Divine Burst 283626, Penance 284578, Angelic Renewal 283628 - assigned to
     the KICK ROTATION. Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of each id.
     Angelic Renewal heals the boss for its maximum health [GUIDE] - it must
     never get through.
  e) Blinding Faith 283650 - assigned to EVERYBODY (look away).
  f) Prayer for the Fallen 287469 (Mythic) - assigned to the KICK ROTATION.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START; all living and dead crusaders cast Divine
     Mallet and all disciples cast Angelic Renewal at once.

4.6 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified anchors: the two add spawn points in 3.1 / 3.2. The arena floor
  radius around them is not published -> unverified.
  Consecration (283579) leaves ground that the tank must leave; Wave of Light

  No coordinate for either is in any readable source -> unverified.

5.3 PHASES [GUIDE]
  Single phase. The only thresholds are resource-rate thresholds at 70% and
  40% health: Icy Veins puts the gain at roughly 0.75 per second from the
  pull, rising at 70% and again at 40% to about 1 per second. gamingcy calls
  the fight single-phase and rage-managed.
  The wipe condition is reaching 100 energy: Tantrum (Horde, 281936) /
  Death Knell (Alliance, 282399) hits once per 25 resources spent and applies
  Shattered, +30% damage taken per stack for 40 s (MMO-Champion).

5.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Apetagonizer Core / Necrotic Core - assigned to a CORE CARRIER.

     Tantrum immediately, clearing Shattered stacks (Icy Veins).
  b) Add interrupt - assigned to the KICK ROTATION.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of Horde 282243 / Alliance 282533. Every cast

  c) Bestial Throw 289292 / 289307 - assigned to the TANK and the MARKED.
     Trigger: 289292 on the tank, 289307 on the marked ranged player.
     Action: the marked player runs away; the tank keeps the boss still.
  d) Ferocious Roar 285994 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START. Action: be within 5 yards of another player or
     be feared 5 s (Icy Veins).
  e) Crushed 285671 / Rending Bite 285875 - assigned to the TANK.
     Trigger: aura stacks; taunt warning.

      (MMO-Champion), so the rooms map to the bands 100-90, 90-80, 80-70,
      70-60, 60-50, 50-40 and then the jewel room at 40-30, which Icy Veins
      places in the seventh room with the Constructs between 40% and 30%.
      Room contents, in order (Icy Veins): 1 no mechanics, 2 Flame Jet,
      3 Ruby Beam, 4 Pulse-quickening Toxin, 5 Ruby Beam + Hex of Lethargy,
      6 Flame Jet + Ruby Beam, 7 jewels.
  Phase Two: Treasure / Opulence  from about 30% to 0
      Begins when both guardians die. Thief's Bane 287424 is applied to every
      player who took a jewel; DBM times it as a 30 s fade, and that fade is
      the practical clock on the phase. How it resolves at the end of those
      30 s is not stated in any source read here -> unverified.
  Mythic adds Chaotic Displacement 289383 and Surging Gold 289155.
