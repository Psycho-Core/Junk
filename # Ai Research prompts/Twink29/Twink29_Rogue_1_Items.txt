0. THE TWO THINGS THAT MAKE ROGUE GEARING DIFFERENT
   The battleground set is open. 20117 Highlander's Leather Girdle is
   +12 Agility +4 Stamina and 20114 Highlander's Leather Boots are
   +7 Agility +8 Stamina with a run speed bonus, both 33/28, both locked
   to Rogue and Druid (V). The monk file has no belt or boots at all; a
   rogue buys both from Samuel Hawke 15127 (Alliance) or Rutherford
   Twing 15126 (Horde) in Arathi Highlands. The Lizardhide pair
   (20105/20172 girdle, 20102/20169 boots) is the Intellect line and is
   not for a rogue.
   The head and shoulder the older files lead with are not blue in this
   build. 15339 Pathfinder Hat and 15140 Cutthroat's Mantle both read as
   quality 2 with a random enchantment on the 8.3.7 tooltip (V), so under
   this file's rule they are out, and the rogue head is 3020.

2. WEAPONS - WHERE THE SPECS SPLIT
   Rogue weapon skills: daggers, fist weapons, one-hand axes, one-hand
   maces, one-hand swords. The rare one-hand axe search at required 26-29
   returns nothing at all, so the axe skill is dead at this tier (V).
   Assassination and Subtlety are dagger specs: their core abilities in
   8.3.7 require a dagger, so sections 3 and 4 are daggers only. Outlaw
   takes any one-hand weapon and wants the slowest main hand it can get.

3. ASSASSINATION
   Armour: section 1.
   Weapons: 7682 Torturing Poker in both hands if two drop, otherwise
   7682 main hand and 19545 [19549] off hand. Assassination's damage is
   poison-driven, and the off-hand speed is what applies it, so the
   1.70-speed Warsong dagger beats the slower fist weapon here.
   Trinkets: 19024 and the Insignia; 4381 third.

4. SUBTLETY
   Armour: section 1, with 7690 Ebon Vise preferred over 7756 for its
   critical strike.
   Weapons: 7682 main hand, 19545 [19549] off hand. Subtlety is the one
   spec that might take 2912 for the proc, but the proc is 35 shadow
   damage against +7 Agility, and the Agility wins.
   Trinkets: the same three.

5. OUTLAW
   Armour: section 1.
   Weapons: 8226 The Butcher main hand (2.80 speed is the slowest in the
   pool), 7683 Bloody Brass Knuckles or 19545 [19549] off hand. Outlaw is
   the only spec that can use the sword and fist pools at all, which
   makes it the cheapest rogue to gear: two of its three weapons are
   world drops and dungeon drops rather than a single boss's dagger.
   Trinkets: the same three.

7. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   A rogue is the best-served leather class in this set, because it is
   the only one that can buy the Arathi Basin belt and boots and because
   Interrogator Vishas drops both its best dagger and its only fist
   weapon. Its weak slots are the same as everyone's: neck, rings, back
   and chest have no blue Agility item in the window.
   Three conflicts between the search pool and the 8.3.7 tooltip are
   printed in place rather than smoothed over: 6688 and 6690 read as
   required 30, 9455 reads as required 24, and 15339 and 15140 read as
   green. Where the two disagree the search value is the one in the row,
   because that is the convention the other seven files in this set use,
   and the disagreement is stated so it can be settled on the server.
