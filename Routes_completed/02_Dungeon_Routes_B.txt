World coordinates (map id, x, y, z, orientation) exist in three kinds of source. Use them, in this order:
1. Open-source world database dumps on GitHub: the `creature` and `gameobject` tables of any TrinityCore-based Battle for Azeroth 8.x world database (search GitHub code search for the entry id together with the word creature or gameobject in .sql files, and for repositories named like BFA, 8.3, TDB 83x).
