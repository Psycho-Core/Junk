0. THE PLATE PROBLEM, AND IT IS THE WHOLE FILE
   THERE IS NO RARE PLATE IN THE BRACKET. The plate search - quality rare,
   required level 26 to 29, item level 35 or less - returns zero rows, and
   the same search with the required level filter removed and only an item
   level cap of 35 returns zero rows too (V). The reason is old itemisation:
   in the Classic data a plate wearer could not put plate on until level 40,
   so every plate item in the game starts around there. The four plate items
   a 29 can actually wear are the bind-on-account ones, because those scale
   down (V).
   WHAT A PALADIN'S BATTLEGROUND SET ACTUALLY IS. The Arathi Basin paladin
   set is not plate either - it is filed under MAIL, and the item names say
   so: 20108 / 20111 Highlander's Lamellar Girdle and Greaves on the
   Alliance side, 20178 / 20182 Defiler's Lamellar Girdle and Greaves on the
   Horde side, each 33 and required 28, each carrying a PALADIN class label
   in the search result (V). A paladin and a warrior share a second pair:
   20126 / 20129 Highlander's Mail Girdle and Greaves, and 20207 / 20210
   Defiler's Mail Girdle and Greaves, labelled Paladin, Warrior (V).
   Those four Alliance and four Horde rows are the only class-locked
   battleground armour a 29 paladin has, and they only fill waist and feet.
   The rest of the body is mail, leather or the two plate heirlooms.
   This is not the monk's problem in reverse. A paladin can wear cloth,
   leather, mail and plate - it always could - so the cross-type rows in
   section 3 are legal, they just carry less armour than plate would.

7. SUMMARY OF WHAT THE POOL GIVES AND DOES NOT
   Plate: four bind-on-account pieces and nothing else. No rare plate item
   with an item level of 35 or less exists in the database at all, which is
   the single fact that shapes this file.
   Mail: fifteen rares, of which eight are the paladin's battleground
   girdle-and-greaves pairs and the rest cover head, hands, chest and feet.
   Nothing for wrists, legs, shoulders or back.
   Leather and cloth: the only source of wrists and legs, and the only
   source of Intellect for a Holy build.
   Weapons: three one-hand maces, four one-hand swords, two two-hand maces,
   two two-hand swords, three two-hand axes and ONE shield.
   A 29 paladin is therefore the most cross-type class in this set - it wears
   its own battleground set on two slots, mail on five, leather on two and
   plate only where an heirloom covers it.
