==============================================================================
TEMPLE OF SETHRALISS - POSITIONS AND NUMBERS, BFA 8.3.7 BUILD 35662
==============================================================================
 Map id 1877. Four encounters, five boss creatures: Adderis and
 Aspix, Merektha, Galvazzt, Avatar of Sethraliss.
 Companion file to the tactics document. Every number is tagged with
 the source it came from. Anything I could not read is written
 "unverified".

0. READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS FILE CAN AND CANNOT GIVE

  Tags:
    [DB]   a row in a world SQL: a smart_scripts row, a
           creature_template row, or a game object id.
    [SRV]  a line in the 8.3.7 script: temple_of_sethraliss.h,
           instance_temple_of_sethraliss.cpp, temple_of_sethraliss.cpp,
           or one of the four boss files.
    [MDT]  Method Dungeon Tools BattleForAzeroth/TempleofSethraliss.lua:
           name, per-kill force, health, level, clone count.
    [DBM]  DBM-Party-BfA/TempleofSethraliss/*.lua: a stopwatch value.
    [LW]   LittleWigs BfA/TempleOfSethraliss/*.lua: a second stopwatch
           value.
    [CALC] arithmetic on the numbers above, inputs always given.
    unverified   no readable source carries the value. Never a guess.

  THE HEADLINE, because it shapes the whole file: there is NO public
  creature spawn table for map 1877. The TrinityCore 11.x 2024 dungeon
  file series and the 9.x world dump were both checked; the only place
  this dungeon's creature ids appear is creature_template_difficulty
  and creature_template_scaling, never in a creature row. So in this
  file:
    - boss and NPC WORLD POSITIONS are unverified, except the seven
      script coordinates in section 3, which are exact;
    - pull positions with z are unverified; MDT's layout is reproduced
      in 8.3 as RELATIVE map-space geometry only, because MDT stores
      positions in its own map space and no conversion to world
      coordinates was readable;
    - line of sight is unverified. The script does carry three door
      object ids (2.3) and three area-trigger ids (3.3), which is more
      than most instances in this set give;
    - timers, percentages and assignments are fully sourced, because
      they live in the scripts, the DB and the two addons.

4. BOSS 1 - ADDERIS 133379 AND ASPIX 133944
==============================================================================

  4.1 ONE ENCOUNTER, TWO CREATURES. Both register BossAI on
      DATA_ADDERIS_AND_ASPIX = 0, they share a health-agnostic fight,
      and the encounter only ends when BOTH are dead: each JustDied
      counts the survivors and, at zero, opens door 292551 [SRV].
      MDT hp is 3,073,980 each, level 120 [MDT].
  4.2 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION. Spawn unverified for both. The arena
      centre is exact: (3344.53, 3148.79, 88.43), commented //45y
      [SRV], so the room is about 90 yd across. TANK: unverified as a
      coordinate. The tank-shaped mechanic is that Adderis casts Gust
      and Arcing Blade at his current victim (4.4).
  4.3 PHASES: NONE BY HEALTH. There is no HealthBelowPct in either
      file. The fight is driven by the energy bar and by which of the
      two is currently shielded:
      - both have POWER_ENERGY, max 100, starting at 0, and both
        remove UNIT_FLAG2_REGENERATE_POWER, so energy comes only from
        the 3 s tick;
      - ASPIX STARTS SHIELDED: his EnterCombat casts 263246 on
        himself;
      - the shielded one is rooted and takes zero damage
        (DamageTaken sets damage = 0 while 263246 is up);
      - while shielded, the 3 s tick adds urand(1, 5) energy;
      - at 50 energy the shielded one arms its special: Adderis arms
        263365 A Pearl of Thunder (10 s), Aspix arms 263371
        Conduction (15 s);
      - at 100 energy the shield is handed to the other boss, the
        shielded one's shield is removed and it fires its finisher:
        Adderis does three Arc Dashes in a row, Aspix casts 263257
        Static Shock on himself;
      - the count then resets to 0 and the other one is now the one
        that cannot be damaged.
      So the practical phase list is two states, not three phases,
      and a group that does not swap targets loses half its damage.

  4.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
      a) ARC DASH 263425 - THE ONLY REAL ASSIGNMENT. Trigger: when
         Adderis reaches 100 energy, three times in a row. Each dash
         takes GetPlayerListInGrid(100.0f), truncates the list to one
         player, charges at 40.0f speed to that player's position and
         casts 263425 on them. It is a random player, not the tank,
         and it lands on a body, so it is the one mechanic a bot can
         eat for the group. There is no soak and no kick.
      b) A PEARL OF THUNDER 263365 - Adderis teleports onto a random
         player inside 100 yd (NearTeleportTo) and casts on them. Not
         targeted at a role.
      c) GUST 263775 and ARCING BLADE 263234 - victim-targeted, i.e.
         the tank by definition. Arcing Blade's damage is divided by
         the number of players it hits (spell script bfa_spell_arcing_
         blade), so it is a stack-or-spread decision, not an assignee.
      d) JOLT 263318 and CONDUCTION 263371 - random player inside
         100 yd, only while Aspix is shielded. Nobody is assigned; the
         ability picks.
      e) CYCLONE STRIKE 261773 - self-cast, only while ASPIX IS
         UNSHIELDED, i.e. only in the window when he can be damaged.
         It spawns area trigger 263325 and damage 263573.
      f) TANK SWAPS: none are written. Because the shield alternates,
         the tank's target changes every 100 energy whichever way the
         group plays it.

  4.6 SAFE SPOTS, WITH z. Unverified. The script fixes no point in the
      room, and the only two positional facts are the 45 yd centre
      comment and the fact that the shielded boss is rooted wherever
      it happens to stand. Arc Dash and Pearl of Thunder both resolve
      onto a player's position, so no fixed safe spot exists for them;
      spreading is the only counter the script leaves available.

5. BOSS 2 - MEREKTHA 133384
==============================================================================

  5.1 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION. Spawn unverified. Her burrow point is
      exact: (3557.00, 3422.00, 40.00) facing 3.30 [SRV]. TANK:
      unverified. Nothing in the file is cast at the top-aggression
      target by name; Noxious Breath picks a random player inside
      5 yd, so the tank is hit by geometry, not by design.
  5.2 PHASES - ONE HEALTH GATE, AT 61 PERCENT, AND A SECOND BURROW ON
      A TIMER.
      Phase 1, from pull: Noxious Breath 3.0 s then every 15.0 s;
      Blinding Sand 8.0 s then every 20.0 s.
      At 61% (HealthBelowPctDamaged(61)) and only once, because the
      event phase is then set to PHASE_2: burrow in 1.0 s and wave 1
      in 3.0 s.
      Burrow: CastStop, REACT_PASSIVE, UNIT_STAND_STATE_SUBMERGED,
      NearTeleportTo(3557, 3422, 40, 3.3), Noxious Breath and Blinding
      Sand cancelled, Unburrow scheduled 30.0 s later. burrow_count is
      incremented, and if it is 2 the script also schedules wave 2 in
      3.0 s, cancels the burrow event and resets the counter.
      Unburrow, 30.0 s later: NearTeleportTo(home), submerged cleared,
      REACT_AGGRESSIVE, then Noxious Breath 3.0 s, Blinding Sand 8.0 s
      and Burrow 30.0 s. So the second burrow starts 30 s after the
      first one ends, and it is timer-driven, not health-driven.
      Wave 1 is six Sand-Crushed Striker 134390 at
      GetRandomPoint(wave_1_pos, 10.0f); wave 2 is six Sand-Crusted
      Striker 135846 at the same point and the same radius. Every
      summon gets 256359 and 256336 on spawn (JustSummoned).
      On death she teleports home, despawns both striker types inside
      125 yd and opens door 290906.

6. BOSS 3 - GALVAZZT 133389
==============================================================================

  6.1 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION. Spawn unverified: boss_galvazzt.cpp has
      no position at all and `const Position pos = { };` is an unused
      empty placeholder. MDT hp 5,379,459, level 120 [MDT].
      TANK: effectively none. He has no cast that selects the tank and
      no melee-relevant mechanic in the file; the whole fight is the
      energy bar, and the tank's job is the same as everyone else's.
      That is a sourced statement about the script, not about retail.
  6.2 PHASES: NONE. No HealthBelowPct, no intermission. POWER_ENERGY
      max 100, starts 0, REGENERATE_POWER removed in the constructor,
      so the bar only moves when a core feeds it.
  6.3 THE LOOP, EXACTLY AS WRITTEN [SRV]
      a) EnterCombat schedules EVENT_ENERGY_CORE at 15.0 s. It repeats
         with events.Repeat(15s, 30s), i.e. a random 15.0 to 30.0 s
         interval.
      b) Each time it fires, three 135445 Energy Cores are summoned,
         each at GetRandomNearPosition(20.0f) from Galvazzt, so all
         three land within 20 yd of him.
      c) A core is passive, NON_ATTACKABLE and IMMUNE_TO_NPC, and
         casts 265977 and 265986 on itself. Its UpdateAI runs on a
         1000 ms timer and does exactly two things, in this order:
           - if a player is within 5.0 yd (SelectNearestPlayer(5.0f)),
             it casts 266923 Galvanize on that player and returns;
           - otherwise, if Galvazzt is within 100 yd, it grants him
             +1 energy.
      d) At the top of ExecuteEvent, before the switch, if his energy
         is 100 he sets it to 0 and casts 266512 Consume Charge.
      So: a core that is body-blocked by a player gives the boss
      nothing; a core left alone gives him 1 energy per second; three
      cores left alone give 3 per second; and the cap arrives at 100.
      The core geometry is why this file can say something exact about
      positioning in a fight with no fixed safe spot: the counter-play
      is proximity, and the number is 5.0 yd.

  6.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
      a) CORE SOAK - THE ASSIGNMENT, and it is a real one: three
         bodies have to stand inside 5.0 yd of three separate cores,
         every wave, or the boss gains energy at 1 per second per
         unblocked core. The trigger for the block is the core's own
         1 s tick, so a body only has to be inside 5 yd at that
         instant; there is no channel and no cast to interrupt.
      b) GALVANIZE 266923 - a stacking debuff applied to whoever
         blocks. It is the cost of the assignment, not a separate
         mechanic: the same player should not hold two cores in a row.
         DBM warns at 5 stacks (specWarnGalvanize(266923, 5)), which is
         the only number the addons add to this fight.
      c) CONSUME CHARGE 266512 - unavoidable tank-damage style hit at
         100 energy. Nobody is assigned; the group controls the rate.
      d) SOAK LISTS, KICK ORDER, TANK SWAPS: none.

  6.6 SAFE SPOTS, WITH z
      - the only exact geometry is relative: cores within 20 yd of
        the boss, and a 5.0 yd block radius on each. Where the boss
        stands is unverified, so the absolute positions are too.
      - because the block radius is smaller than the scatter radius,
        the correct spot is ON a core, not near it: 5.0 yd is the
        difference between denying 1 energy per second and not.
      - no ground effect, no ring and no edge exists in this fight.

7. BOSS 4 - AVATAR OF SETHRALISS 133392
==============================================================================

  7.1 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION. Spawn unverified. Her adds have exact
      spawn points: Heart Guardian and Plague Doctor at
      (4140.00, 3659.00, -43.00) o 0.59, six Plague Toads at
      GetRandomPoint((4161.00, 3673.00, -34.00), 30.0f) [SRV].
      TANK: none. She is rooted for the whole encounter and never
      melees the group; there is no tank mechanic in this file.
  7.2 HOW THE FIGHT STARTS - GOSSIP, NOT PROXIMITY [SRV].
      The Avatar carries a gossip option. On gossip select, if
      GetBossState() says NOT_STARTED: the boss state goes
      IN_PROGRESS, she is rooted, the gossip flag is removed, HER
      HEALTH IS SET TO 10 PERCENT OF MAX, her faction is set to
      FACTION_MASK_PLAYER, EVENT_JOLT is scheduled at 100 ms, every
      Hoodoo Hexer inside 150 yd is made visible, put on
      REACT_DEFENSIVE and engaged, and the intro lines play at 0, 6.0
      and 8.0 s.
      So the encounter is a heal, not a kill: the group starts with
      her at 10% and has to keep her alive while the temple fights
      back.

  7.3 PHASES: NONE BY HEALTH, BUT THE WIN CONDITION IS A HEALTH
      THRESHOLD.
      DamageTaken: if she is ABOVE 99 percent when she takes damage,
      the encounter is declared done - CastStop, boss state DONE,
      kill credit 2127, every player inside 150 yd gets the credit
      and is dropped from combat, and the treasure is summoned at
      (4149.73, 3665.59, -43.0365) o 3.68391. On mythic with a
      challenge-mode run the Challenger's Cache 290758 also appears at
      (4185.00, 3688.00, -43.00) o 3.84.
      Read that together with 7.2 and the objective is explicit: heal
      her from 10% to above 99%, then hit her once. The last hit is
      the win, not a phase.
      Three more health checks exist in the same function, at 80, 70
      and 40 percent, each intended to respawn the Hoodoo Hexers as a
      wave. They are written as `(this->wavecount = 0)`, `(= 1)` and
      `(= 2)` - assignments, not comparisons (10.3).

10. DISCREPANCIES AND TRAPS
==============================================================================

  10.1 THERE IS NO SPAWN TABLE FOR MAP 1877. Every boss spawn, tank
       position and safe spot in this file is unverified for that
       reason, and every pull position with z is unverified with it.
  10.2 GALE FORCE 263776 IS SCHEDULED AND NEVER CAST. Aspix's
       EnterCombat schedules EVENT_GALE_FORCE at 22.0 s on heroic and
       mythic, and the case body does nothing but re-schedule it. Both
       addons still bar it, which is why the timers in 4.4 disagree.
  10.3 THE AVATAR'S THREE WAVE CHECKS USE ASSIGNMENT AS A CONDITION.
       They read `(this->wavecount = 0)`, `(= 1)` and `(= 2)` instead
       of ==. The first assigns 0, which is false, so the 80 percent
       respawn never runs; the other two assign non-zero, which is
       true, so the 70 and 40 percent branches run on every qualifying
       damage event.
  10.4 THE AVATAR'S GOSSIP CHECK COMPARES AN ENUM TO A BOSS STATE.
       instance->GetBossState(DATA_AVATAR_OF_SETHRALISS == NOT_STARTED)
       evaluates (3 == 0), which is false, then asks for boss state 0 -
       Merektha's. The encounter therefore starts on a state that does
       not belong to it.
  10.5 `encouter_done` IS DECLARED AND NEVER USED, and like
       Merektha's burrow_count it is a uint8 with no initialiser.
  10.6 MEREKTHA'S burrow_count IS NEVER INITIALISED. Reset does not set
       it, and the wave-2 branch tests it against 2, so the first
       burrow's second-wave decision reads whatever the object was
       constructed with.
  10.7 GALVAZZT'S ENERGY ONLY MOVES INSIDE ExecuteEvent. The 100-point
       check sits at the top of the function, so a core can push the
       bar to 100 and the Consume Charge cast waits for the next
       Energy Core event - up to 30.0 s later.
  10.8 THE CORE'S OWNER CHAIN IS UNVERIFIED. npc_energy_core gates
       every action on me->GetOwner(); the summon is a CastSpell from
       Galvazzt onto a position, and whether that sets the owner in
       this core is not written anywhere. If it does not, a core never
       grants energy and the fight cannot end. The line
       me->GetOwnerGUID(); in Reset has no effect at all.
  10.9 THE ENCOUNTER DATA IS ZERO-BASED (2.2). A DB row that opens
       encounter 1 opens Merektha, not the first boss.
 10.10 THE ADDONS USE SPELL IDS THIS CORE NEVER CASTS. Merektha is
       barred as 263912, Galvazzt as 1291618/1309525, and Pulse 268024
       is barred for the Avatar without any cast of it in the script.
       The two addon ids that do exist here are 263958 and 268007, and
       they belong to trash, not to the boss the bar is on. Read every
       addon number in this file as a retail cadence, not as this
       core's.
 10.11 THE LOOSE SPARK HAS TWO IDENTITIES. The geometry is created
       with misc id 13248, the template comment says 17715, and the AI
       registers under the string at_loose_sparks. Three names for one
       object, and only the geometry numbers are exact.
 10.12 NONE OF THE FOUR BOSSES HAS A SMART_SCRIPTS BLOCK. The 30
       entries in 8.2 are trash only, so there is no C++-versus-DB
       conflict on this map the way there is on Siege.

11. WHAT IS NOT IN THIS FILE, AND WHY
==============================================================================

 11.1 Boss spawn coordinates, tank coordinates and safe-spot
      coordinates are unverified for all five boss creatures. No
      spawn table for map 1877 is public, and the scripts carry seven
      coordinates, none of which is a spawn.
 11.2 Pull positions with z are unverified. MDT's clone layout is in
      map space and has no z; 8.3 says what it can and cannot be used
      for.
 11.3 Line of sight is unverified. The door ids in 2.3 and the area
      triggers in 3.3 are the only object data the script carries,
      and neither gives a mesh.
 11.4 No radius, duration or damage figure came from a client table.
      Radii are unverified unless a script literal states them, which
      is why 45 yd (centerPos), 5.0 yd (Noxious Breath and the core
      block) and the 10/8/6/4/2 yd spark rings are the only precise
      distances in the file.
 11.5 Item ids are not quoted. No loot SQL for this instance was
      readable.
 11.6 Nothing here came from a video, a guide site or a forum post.
