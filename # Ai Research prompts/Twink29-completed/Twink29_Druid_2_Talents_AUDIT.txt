  5  PASS   Force of Nature is confirmed as a level 15 Balance talent in

  6  PASS   the usual pick per spec is labelled as a judgement rather than

PVP TALENTS - THE CORRECTION
  7  CORRECTED  the file said the slot count was unverified and quoted
            "one slot at 29, the rest from 40 upward" as an unchecked
            number. The PvP talent page settles it: "In the lower right
            corner of the Talents pane, three PvP talent slots can be seen
            after level 20, when the first is unlocked. The other two are
            unlocked at level 30 and level 40" - that is the post-9.0.1
            text. For 8.3.7 the older line applies: "Standard classes
            unlocked their slots at levels 20, 40, 70, and 110", and
            "prior to Shadowlands, there were four slots in total", the
            first of them being the PvP trinket choice. So a level 29
            character has exactly one PvP talent slot, opened at level 20,
            and it holds a trinket-type talent. The section is rewritten
            with that, and the quote is no longer hedged.
  8  PASS   the panel is separate from the normal talent tree: the page
            places it in the lower right of the Talents pane, with its own
            slots and its own unlocks, so the brief's "shared with the
            normal talent" assumption is wrong and the file's correction
            of it stands.
  9  PASS   "always active in PvP instances, inactive in PvE instances,
            active in the open world only with War Mode on, changeable at
            the start of a match or in a rested area" - all four are the
            page's own wording.
