TWINK29 - PRIEST - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

0. WHAT A LEVEL 29 ACTUALLY HAS
   Normal talents come in seven rows, unlocked at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90
   and 100. A level 29 character has ONE row: the level 15 row, one point
   in it. Everything below that is not "what a priest should take" but
   "what a priest can take", which is a much shorter list.
   PvP talents come in three slots, unlocked at 20, 40 and 70. A level 29
   character has ONE slot. On top of that, some PvP talents carry their own
   level requirement above 20, and those are unavailable even in the slot
   the character owns - that is the trap this file is mostly about.
   So the whole answer is two choices per spec: one of three (or fewer)
   level 15 talents, and one PvP talent if a legal one exists at 29.
   V = read off the talent's wiki page, including the patch line that
   states its BfA level. A current tooltip is NOT used as evidence: the
   wiki serves 12.x pages, and several of these talents were renumbered or
   redesigned after Battle for Azeroth.

1. DISCIPLINE
   Level 15 row, all three at 15 in BfA by their own patch lines:
   1  Schism, id unverified (see section 6). Active, 9 sec effect.
      Your next damage spell against the target does 40% more damage from
      your spells - 8.3.0 limited it to Priest spells and abilities, and
      9.0.1 cut the figure to 25%, so 40% is the 8.3.7 number (V).
      Active, so a bot has to be told to use it; it is the strongest
      damage choice in the row.
   2  193134 Castigation. Passive. Penance fires one additional bolt (V).
      The safe pick for a bot that already casts Penance on cooldown.
   3  109142 Twist of Fate. Passive. Damaging or healing a target below
      35% health grants 20% increased damage and healing for 8 sec (V,
      7.0.3 set 20%). Level 15 for Discipline since 7.2.5.
   PICK  Castigation for a companion that will be driven by a rotation,
      Schism if the bot is allowed one extra button. Twist of Fate is the
      third choice and the least reliable, because it needs something to
      already be dying.

2. HOLY
   Level 15 row:
   1  200128 Trail of Light. Passive. 35% of the healing of Flash Heal and
      Heal is replicated to the previous target (V; 8.1.0 set 35%, and
      8.1.0 is also what added Heal to the effect). Level 15 in BfA.
   2  193155 Enlightenment. Passive. You regenerate mana 10% faster (V).
      The 10% is the 7.0.3 redesign; the 25% and the 10%-in-PvP figures
      on the wiki are 2026 hotfixes and have nothing to do with 8.3.7.
   3  unverified. The Holy level-15 row has a third talent that was not
      read for this file, so it is not named here. Two of three is what
      the sources support.
   PICK  Trail of Light. A Holy bot heals constantly and the second target
      is free throughput; Enlightenment only matters if the bot can run
      out of mana, which in a 29 battleground it can, so it is a real
      alternative rather than a filler.

3. SHADOW
   Level 15 row:
   1  193195 Fortress of the Mind. Passive. Mind Flay and Mind Blast deal
      10% more damage and generate 20% more Insanity (V). Level 15 in BfA;
      removed in 10.0.0.
   2  Shadowy Insight (BfA spell id unverified). Passive. Shadow Word:
      Pain periodic damage can make your next Mind Blast instant and
      ignore its cooldown (V). 8.0.1 moved it TO the level 15 row and it
      was removed in 9.0.1, so it is a BfA talent and legal here.
   3  unverified. The third Shadow level-15 talent was not read.
   TRAP  Twist of Fate looks like a priest talent and is not one for this
      spec: 8.0.1 moved it to level 45 for Shadow. A 29 Shadow priest
      cannot take it, whatever a current guide says.
   TRAP  Death and Madness is the 9.0.1 replacement for Shadowy Insight.
      It does not exist in 8.3.7 at all.
   PICK  Fortress of the Mind. It is passive, it feeds Insanity, and a
      companion does not have to be told anything to get the value.

4. THE PVP SLOT AT LEVEL 20
   This is where the bracket bites. A 29 owns the first PvP slot, but a
   PvP talent is only legal if its own level is 20 or lower.
   LEGAL AND VERIFIED
     DISCIPLINE  214205 Trinity, level 20. Atonement's healing transfer is
       increased by 20% (8.2.0) and Smite, Penance and Shadowfiend get 25%
       more crit while Atonement is on three or more allies (8.1.0) (V).
       It is the only priest PvP talent this file can place in a level 20
       slot, and it fits the spec it belongs to.
   NAMED TRAPS - high level in BfA, available at 20 only after 9.0.1
     Psyfiend (Shadow). 8.0.1 made it a level 40 PvP talent; 9.0.1 lowered
       that to 20. On 8.3.7 a 29 cannot take it (V). Every current guide
       lists it as a level 20 option, which is how this mistake gets made.
     Dome of Light (Discipline). 8.0.1 made it a level 70 PvP talent; 9.0.1
       lowered it to 44 and 10.1.0 removed it (V). Nothing a 29 can use.
   UNVERIFIED - do not assume either way
     Ultimate Radiance (Discipline), Spirit of the Redeemer (Holy) and
       Driven to Madness (Shadow) all date from BfA, but their BfA level
       is not stated on any page read here: 8.0.1 converted them from
       honor ranks without printing a level, and the levels shown now
       (23, 29, 23) are post-squish numbers. Whether a 29 priest can take
       any of them is therefore unverified, not yes and not no.
   SO: Discipline gets Trinity. Holy and Shadow have no verified PvP
      talent at level 20 in 8.3.7, and the file leaves the slot empty
      rather than filling it with a 9.0.1-era answer.

5. THE THREE LOADOUTS
   DISCIPLINE  Castigation (193134) and Trinity (214205).
     A damage-healing bot: Penance on cooldown, Atonement out, Trinity
     lengthening the transfer and rewarding three allied Atonements.
   HOLY  Trail of Light (200128), PvP slot empty.
     Enlightenment (193155) instead if the bot is mana-starved.
   SHADOW  Fortress of the Mind (193195), PvP slot empty.
     Shadowy Insight if the bot's Mind Blast is scripted rather than
     reactive; Fortress is the passive and therefore the default.

6. WHAT IS SOLID AND WHAT IS NOT
   Solid: the two choices a 29 has, the seven-row and three-slot gating,
   all three Discipline level-15 talents with their BfA numbers, Trail of
   Light and Enlightenment for Holy, Fortress of the Mind and Shadowy
   Insight for Shadow, Trinity at 20, and the two traps (Psyfiend at 40,
   Dome of Light at 70).
   Not solid: the third Holy and third Shadow level-15 talents (not read),
   the BfA levels of Ultimate Radiance, Spirit of the Redeemer and Driven
   to Madness, and every BfA-era spell id - the ids printed above are from
   the wiki's external links, and a link to a 12.x spell is not proof of
   the id 8.3.7 used. Castigation 193134, Twist of Fate 109142, Trail of
   Light 200128, Enlightenment 193155, Fortress of the Mind 193195 and
   Trinity 214205 are the six that a 7.x-era id was printed for.
