STROMGARDE BUILDINGS — both attacker factions; published 2018/2020 guide costs
The Horde's main structure is Great Hall at Ar'gorok; Alliance's is Town Hall at Stromgarde. Start ruined/contested, capture and rebuild first. Starting rebuild Iron/Wood cost: unverified. Then Stronghold (Horde)/Keep (Alliance), published cost 380 Iron + 180 Wood, requires Altar of Storms and Armory; boosts resource collection and unit production, unlocks commander-specific troop. Fortress/Castle upgrade 620 Iron + 260 Wood, after Workshop; further resource/troop production and siege damage. For EACH main building/upgrade: GO/creature entry, exact interaction/build spell, phased object, cost validation on this core, x/y/z unverified. Do not apply Darkshore 360/380 to Stromgarde.
Barracks — 140 Iron + 140 Wood in a Dec 2018 Wowhead guide and Jan 2020 strategy; requires captured main hall, produces four troop families. Earlier alpha/later third-party reports disagree on its Wood cost, so a single exact 8.3.7 cost is unverified without this core's purchase data. The barracks production UI/summon spell and GO/creature entry unverified.
War Mill (Horde) / Armory (Alliance) — 260 Iron + 140 Wood as the War Mill figure in the 2018 guide; Barracks prerequisite; upgrades troop weapons/armor (Steel Weapons 252214, Steel Armor 256454 in that guide). Alliance Armory's distinct cost, exact tier research costs, effect magnitudes and faction-specific build/GO spell entries unverified. Do not transfer Steel spell IDs to an unverified Alliance variant.
Altar of Storms (Horde) / Altar of Kings (Alliance; some guides use Altar of Storms generically) — 260 Iron + 140 Wood, Barracks prerequisite in a contemporary Horde guide. Grants purchasable personal Bristling Power and Essence of Storms exchange for Call of the Storm. Exact Alliance altar name, its build cost/GO ID, building spell and server interaction unverified; 260/140 is a GUIDE figure not proved separately for each faction.
Workshop — 500 Iron + 220 Wood, requires level-2 Stronghold/Keep in 2020 guide; produces Demolishers (Horde) or Siege Engines (Alliance), published normal cost 80 Iron each and up to three active. Heroic 160 Iron in the same 2020 guide: do NOT apply that to normal. Building GO entry/build spell, 8.3.7 core costs/vehicle cap and site coordinates unverified.
Other outposts: Drywhisker Mine/Hatchet Ridge (Horde) and Galson's Lode/Highlands Mill (Alliance) provide iron/wood; Circle of Elements unlocks healer troop, Newstead mounted troop, High Perch transit/tower, and Valorcall Pass (Horde) or Northfold Crossing (Alliance) the siege approach. Capturable flags, local troop-building structures (Spirit Lodge etc.), optional towers, resource chests and production objects: GO entries, construction costs/spells and world positions unverified. Do not treat an optional outpost as a purchased main-base building.

DARKSHORE BUILDINGS — both attacker factions, January 2019 Darkshore guide
Alliance Bashal'Aran: Tree of Life / Tree of Ages / Tree of Eternity. Horde Bashal'Aran: Great Hall / Stronghold / Fortress. Level 1 rebuilt after base capture; restoration cost unverified. Level 2 upgrade: published 360 Iron + 380 Wood, after Altar of Elders (Alliance) or Altar of Storms (Horde), and Hunter's Hall (Alliance) or Armory (Horde); raises resource/troop production and unlocks special troops. Level 3: published 520 Iron + 620 Wood, requires Glaiveworks/Plagueworks plus Cinderfall Grove and Lornesta Mine, boosts vehicle health and production. These are collaborative contribution totals, not a single player's inventory (guide cites personal caps 200 Iron/100 Wood). Actual 35662/core costs, GO IDs, spell interactions, phase variants, and coordinates unverified.
Ancient of War (Alliance) / Barracks (Horde) — initial required troop production building, outputs personal bodyguards; 2019 guide does not establish the pair's build cost: unverified. Published two bodyguards maximum. Horde recruits Deathguard/Deadshot/Alchemist/Lancer; Alliance Sentinel/Archer/Druid/Huntress. Exact recruitment building entry and build spell unverified. Allied zone-10084 Horde candidates 144795 Forsaken Deathguard, 144744 Forsaken Deadshot, 144802 Forsaken Alchemist, 144800 Forsaken Lancer; zone has other same-named NPCs. Alliance candidates 144973/145126 Kaldorei Sentinel, 144970 Kaldorei Archer, 144971/148610 Druid of the Branch, 144972 Kaldorei Hunter; these may be defenders or other phases, NOT confirmed summoned bodyguards.
Altar of Elders (Alliance) / Altar of Storms (Horde) — required build for level-2 main base; published guide does not give a construction price or GO/build spell: unverified. Buys Bristling Power and permits Essence of Storms embodiment in three role forms per faction (below). Do not use Warcraft III Altar of Elders gold/lumber costs.
Hunter's Hall (Alliance) / Armory (Horde) — 260 Iron + 280 Wood published for the Darkshore pair; improves FRIENDLY NPCs, not direct hero form stats. Three research tiers, each claimed as 10% health/damage by 2019 guide: Monelite Armor 264430 / Weapons 264431; Storm Silver Armor 264451 / Weapons 264513 (requires level-2 base); Platinum Armor 264452 / Weapons 264453 (requires level-3 base). Those are listed spell IDs, not verified core-specific research transaction IDs or historical per-tier coefficients; precise 35662 effects/GO IDs/build interactions unverified.
Glaiveworks (Alliance) / Plagueworks (Horde) — required level-2 base; the paired siege-production buildings (not Stromgarde Workshop); January 2019 guide does not specify construction Iron/Wood cost: unverified. Produces Glaive Thrower / Forsaken Blight Thrower at published 80 Iron each with maximum THREE active NPC-controlled siege vehicles; exact per-core cost/cap unverified. Build Glaive Thrower 282838 and Build Forsaken Blight Thrower 284819 are BfA quest-choice-launch spells, NOT evidence that directly casting either summons a vehicle or completes a criterion; separate spawn/script is required and its production mapping unverified. Building GO entries/build spells/coordinates unverified.
Darkshore other structures and locations: Lornesta Mine supplies passively generated Iron to Bashal'Aran War Chest and Ore Crates; Cinderfall Grove supplies Wood, including passive generation. Gloomtide Strand unlocks healer bodyguards; Ashwood Depot mounted bodyguards/transport; Forlorn Crossing controls roads; ship ballistae and repair-tool barrels at Lor'danel are battle equipment, not player-built base structures. Outpost flag, War Chest, Ore Crate, wood pile, altar, structure, ballista and barrel GO IDs, object states, construction costs, spell interactions, xyz unverified. 2018 Horde account cautions Lornesta Mine has no ordinary mining nodes; Ore Crates/chests and kill loot are not ordinary mineable nodes.

ALTAR BUFFS — the two distinct Essence names matter
Bristling Power 266163 (BfA 8.0.1 spell) and 282474 (added 8.1): CURRENT tooltips give +10% damage, +10% healing, +10% maximum health per stack for ten minutes, FOUR stacks maximum; altar purchase cited as 10 Iron OR 10 Wood per stack. December 2018 Wowhead Stromgarde guide claims 'up to 10 stacks' while its linked spell page currently says FOUR; earlier alpha guides also give 2% damage/healing and 1% health, and 2018 guides differ. The precise historical 8.3.7 cap, actual faction-specific cast ID and whether changes were retroactive to Stromgarde: UNVERIFIED; do not silently use the contradictory ten-stack claim or add percentages beyond the current tooltip.
Essence of Storms is CURRENCY 1559 (rare warfront pickup, cap one in 2018/19 guides); Bristling with the Essence AURA 267784 is a distinct screen-effect proc/marker, NOT the +150% Call buff and NOT an item; Essence of Storms spell 287111 is a quest-start effect on a current listing, NOT proof of a currency item. Call of the Storm buff 266977 (BfA 8.0.1) or 278582 (8.1.0) lists FIVE minutes: +150% damage dealt, +150% healing, +150% maximum health and +50% movement, plus unspecified allied nearby damage/health benefits. Pet damage effect listed +100%, size +30%; exact allied coefficient and how either ID is selected per faction/patch unverified. The two Call spell pages show different Area Trigger IDs, so do not merge their script records. Contemporary exchange is ONE Essence at Altar; Darkshore guide describes embodiment choices as an alternative essence spend. The +150% is additive percentage increase (2.5 times the base stat), NOT '150% of original'.

LIMIT OF VERIFICATION
The requested exhaustive every-building GO/creature entries, building interaction spells, every troop summon/ability/level-120 HP/damage, all vehicle seats/action bars and all world (map,x,y,z) positions cannot be truthfully reconstructed from these public guides and current spell pages. An implementation must query build-35662 client spell/vehicle/quest-choice tables plus each of the four instance maps' spawn/phase/GO records, and inspect/test the private core's transaction, recruitment and aura scripts. Do not treat the published guide cost as a proven database row or call a candidate entry verified recruitment output.
