SIEGE OF BORALUS — map 1822; Wowhead zone page 9354
Boss order: Alliance Chopper Redhook (creature 144160) OR Horde Sergeant Bainbridge (144158); Dread Captain Lockwood (129208); Hadal Darkfathom (128651); Viq'Goth (128652; 129415 is an outdoor quest NPC and must NOT be used as the dungeon boss). Positions: first boss (1822, unverified, unverified, unverified); Lockwood (1822, unverified, unverified, unverified); Hadal (1822, unverified, unverified, unverified); Viq'Goth (1822, unverified, unverified, unverified).
Alliance enters from Boralus northeastern waterfront below the Tradewinds district; Horde starts at Plunder Harbor, Tiragarde Sound. These are DIFFERENT starting approaches/faction first bosses, not two bosses in a run. Advance through the faction-specific guards/streets to the first boss; kill the guards you actually pull. Heavy Ordnance falls during the fight: lead the fixating boss across it before expiry (otherwise dangerous detonation); it is NOT a door key. Continue along the waterfront and through the hostile Ashvane/Irontide streets toward Lockwood's ship-side arena. Spotter/artillery patrols and side streets can change pulls: clear exposed chokepoints; skip separated groups only with a safe verified path. At Lockwood's 66% and 33% Withdraw phases, kill her Ashvane Cannoneer, PICK UP the dropped Unstable Ordnance and FIRE it at her ship (extra-action targeting) to force her back. Deckhands are add-wave enemies; avoid Dread Volley. Ordnance item/spell/GO IDs: unverified. After Lockwood, follow the shoreline/harbor passage to Hadal's flooded arena, defeat the sea giant, and proceed to the ruined three-platform harbor where Viq'Goth waits. The final boss is not killed by normal direct DPS: on EACH platform tank/kill the Demolishing Terror(s), kill the Gripping Terror holding the Kul Tiran Engineer, wait for engineer to repair the cannon, CLICK/USE that platform's repaired cannon and fire at Viq'Goth; leave platform before Eradication. Move to next platform and repeat for all THREE cannons. Gripping Terror kills and cannons are mandatory; do not treat their combat add waves as optional trash. Exact world coordinates, door GO IDs and optional street-pack list: unverified. Heroic access exists in 8.3 (added 8.1); do not import a later opening-layout change without checking faction phase.

TEMPLE OF SETHRALISS — map 1877; Wowhead zone page 9527
Boss order: Adderis (133379) and Aspix (133944) together, Merektha (133384), Galvazzt (133389), Avatar of Sethraliss (133392). Positions: Adderis/Aspix (1877, unverified, unverified, unverified); Merektha (1877, unverified, unverified, unverified); Galvazzt (1877, unverified, unverified, unverified); Avatar (1877, unverified, unverified, unverified).
Enter the vestibule and take the guarded passage to the two sethrak entrance bosses; kill them to pass onward. Cross the serpent/egg-filled corridor and hazardous bridge to Merektha's clutch, avoiding the ground hazards and clearing the enemies physically controlling the chosen corridor; defeat Merektha, including her snake-hatch/burrow waves. Then descend into Galvazzt's lightning grotto: avoid moving electric orbs and eliminate the Spark Channeler on the crossing route if its orb hazard blocks passage (exact 8.3 spawn behavior unverified); kill Galvazzt. His Lightning Spires are boss mechanics and must be blocked by players, not clicked. Proceed to the Bazaar of Watchful Eyes. This is the crucial 8.3 ROUTE GATE: recover the EYE(S) OF SETHRALISS from the side pedestals, carry or THROW each eye using the provided extra-action control to the Skull of Sethraliss at the end of the bazaar, and put each required eye in its socket to open the skull-mouth passage into the Atrium. Normal historically requires ONE eye; Heroic/Mythic require BOTH. Eye carriers are slowed; Orb Guardians (135007) attempt to steal/return an eye to its pedestal and can inflict Numb Hands, so protect the carrier, kill/CC the guardians and reacquire a dropped eye if needed. Each side of bazaar is a route branch to a separate eye; no key from inventory is needed. Eye GO/item/use-spell IDs and positions: unverified. In BfA this is an eye CARRY/THROW puzzle, NOT the later 'click and channel energy to fill eye progress' mechanic. Enter the atrium and HEAL the Avatar: in original BfA waves, kill Hoodoo Hexers (137204) to remove Taint (reduced healing), kill Heart Guardians (136391) before their tank damage stacks, then USE the Energy Fragment dropped by a dying Heart Guardian to grant the Avatar Life Force and heal her in the Taint-free window. Handle Plague Doctors/Plague Toads (137233), particularly toads chasing the healer; further add waves arrive as the Avatar is restored. Healing her to full completes the encounter (do not attack her as if hostile). Do NOT substitute the 2026 Essence Defiler/Corrupted Lifeforce/Faithless Tormentor redesign. Mandatory trash: guardian interference on eye-carry paths if it blocks transfer, and BfA Hoodoo Hexer/Heart Guardian encounter waves; other off-route packs may be skipped if the carrier remains safe and enemy forces are budgeted. Door animation from inserting eye(s) is automatic; exact gate GO entry unverified.

THE MOTHERLODE!! — map 1594; Wowhead zone page 8064
Boss order: Coin-Operated Crowd Pummeler (dungeon NPC 129214; modern Wowhead also lists NPC 139904, variant/spawn used on this core unverified), Azerokk (129227), Rixxa Fluxflame (139273), Mogul Razdunk (131227). Positions: Pummeler (1594, unverified, unverified, unverified); Azerokk (1594, unverified, unverified, unverified); Rixxa (1594, unverified, unverified, unverified); Razdunk (1594, unverified, unverified, unverified).
Horde reaches the instance via boat at Dazar'alor harbor; Alliance uses the Dark Iron mole machine near Xibala; the same interior route follows. From the mine's populated entry follow either the LEFT or RIGHT path around Venture Co. buildings to the Pummeler arena. Clear packs that aggro; control Mech Jockeys before they enter vehicles, interrupt vendors, and avoid unrelated side packs. In the fight, get near an Azerite Footbomb and use the Punt interaction to kick it AT the Pummeler before Timed Detonation; this is a combat object, not an exit lever. Move out by the arena's far side toward the mining/earth-elemental area. Both left and right approaches to Azerokk are documented; interrupt Venture Co. Earthshapers, clear the final engaged pack, use Fracking Totems or CC on Earthragers and defeat Azerokk. Fracking Totems are combat helpers, not gate keys. Continue toward R&D/chemical processing via the FAR LEFT route behind the buildings, or another path if safe; interrupt Alchemist Transmute: Enemy to Goo and Assistant Transfiguration Serum, and clear only the blocking packs needed to reach Rixxa. Fight her on the platform, directing Propellant Blast away from players and using it to clear catalyst patches. Follow the left-side industrial corridor or other safe passage toward the final flight/mechanical arena, interrupt Venture Co. Technicians and defeat Razdunk. In his airborne drill phase lure Drill Smash into Big Red Rocket shells to force him down; this is an encounter interaction, not a clickable door. A Mine Cart NPC/ride is recorded for the instance, but its use, route and whether mandatory on 8.3.7 are unverified: do NOT script a required minecart ride without in-core confirmation. No ordinary route lever, key or mandatory trash-on-death gate is verified here. Exact enemy-forces pack count and skips require inspecting this core's spawn layout.

THE UNDERROT — map 1841; Wowhead zone page 9391
Boss order: Elder Leaxa (131318), Cragmaw the Infested (131817), Sporecaller Zancha (131383), Unbound Abomination (133007). Positions: Leaxa (1841, unverified, unverified, unverified); Cragmaw (1841, unverified, unverified, unverified); Zancha (1841, unverified, unverified, unverified); Abomination (1841, unverified, unverified, unverified).
Enter the corrupted Nazmir cave and choose the bridge/path with fewer hostile packs on the way to Leaxa's blood-ritual area (documented left/right BRIDGE split); clear only the packs on chosen bridge that would otherwise aggro. Kill Leaxa and her Blood Effigy clones. Follow the descending bridge/rotting cave toward Cragmaw's large pit: bloodswarmers, diseased lashers, living rots and fetid maggots on the approach can overwhelm the group; clear the arena's dangerous nearby packs to leave room for Tantrum/larva stomping, then kill Cragmaw. These are tactical clear recommendations, not a verified door-kill count. Pass through the ruined fungus-filled passages to Sporecaller Zancha, interrupt dangerous Bloodsworn Defiler casts and avoid or clear off-route packs depending on proximity. Kill Zancha: position his Shockwave and Upheaval to remove spore pods before Festering Harvest (combat requirement, not a clickable spore switch). Descend into the corrupted last chamber; hug a wall to bypass some of the packs before the Unbound Abomination if safe (beware Faceless Corruptors/Grotesque Horrors), then engage the final boss. Hezrel's Cleansing Light removes Putrid Blood/nearby corruption; direct damage charges Abomination's Blood Barrier until Blood Visages spawn; KILL those visages to damage the boss through Fatal Link. This mandatory add cycle is the final encounter's progress mechanism. No confirmed door, gong, player-operated lever or inventory key on this normal four-boss route; exact trash packs required by any scripted gate: unverified. Optional trash skips must still satisfy Mythic+ enemy forces; do not use a later max-level mount-farm 'skip every boss' route as a BfA completion path.
