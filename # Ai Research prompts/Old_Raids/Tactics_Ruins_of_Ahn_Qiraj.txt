1. KURINNAXX (15348)
PHASES: single phase; at 30% health he enrages (26527
  Frenzy, patch 1.10.1 note and strategy text): healers
  focus the tank, DPS blow cooldowns to finish (page).
TANK: two-tank swap: 25646 Mortal Wound stacks from his
  frontal attack (-10% healing per stack); the off-tank
  stays BEHIND him and taunts when the MT's stacks get
  too high (page). Wide aggro radius; sees stealth (page).
GROUP: melee stack on ONE side of him, ranged spread
  (page) - keeps 25656 Sand Trap victims grouped for
  healing and keeps Mortal Wound off non-tanks.
GROUND: 25656 Sand Trap - placed under a player: -75%
  chance to hit and silence (page spell text). The
  trapped companion cannot cast - bandage/potion/wand
  until it wears off (page strategy). Move out is not
  possible while trapped (unverified); treat as a
  timed disable, not a zone to stand in.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  25646 Mortal Wound - healing received -10% per stack
    (from his frontal hits; see TANK).
  25814 Wide Slash - normal +314, frontal cone.
  25656 Sand Trap - see GROUND.
  26527 Frenzy - +75% attack speed, +87 physical damage,
    2 min (at 30%, see PHASES). Dispel: enrage-type;
    Tranquilizing Shot removal unverified on this page.
  3391 Thrash - 2 extra attacks. 26446 Summon Player.
AI RULE: off-tank stays behind him and taunts on Mortal
  Wound stacks; if Sand Trapped, stop casting, bandage.

2. GENERAL RAJAXX (15341)
Event adds (all 7 waves): 1 officer (entries unverified)
  + Swarmguard Needler (15344) x2-5 + Qiraji Warrior
  (entry unverified) x1-4. Friendly NPCs: Lieutenant
  General Andorov + 4 Kaldorei Elite (entries unverified)
  spawn after Kurinnaxx and FIGHT WITH YOU (page) -
  NEVER let Andorov die if you can heal him: his Aura
  of Command heals the raid every 3 sec (page).
PHASES: 7 waves (below), then Rajaxx himself. Talk to
  Andorov to start. Waves come from alternating sides.
  Wave officers and their one mechanic (boss page):
   W1 Captain Qeez - Intimidating Shout (AoE fear;
     fear-break the tank).
   W2 Captain Tuubid - Attack Order: marks ONE target,
     ALL adds aggro it - kill Tuubid FIRST (page,
     "especially crucial"), the marked companion runs
     to the tanks.
   W3 Captain Drenn - Hurricane, channeled AoE around
     himself: tank him AWAY from Andorov (page).
   W4 Captain Xurrem - Shockwave: heavy AoE + move
     speed to 1% for 2 sec - spread from him.
   W5 Major Yeggeth - Blessing of Protection (physical
     immunity): casters kill him / purge (page lists
     the ability; removal method unverified).
   W6 Major Pakkon - Sweeping Slam, frontal cone +
     knockback: tank him facing the wall, away (page).
   W7 Colonel Zerran - Enlarge (+physical damage, him
     and allies): DISPEL it (page: "must be dispelled").
  Wave spell ids: unverified (page names them without
  id links). Adds can be frozen/feared/rooted/stunned
  (page Weaknesses) - CC Warriors, kill Needlers first
  (deadly but fragile, page) once the officer is down.
TANK (Rajaxx): position the raid near a wall (page):
  Thundercrash knocks everyone back. After each
  Thundercrash and after his aggro-drop, re-taunt
  instantly - he resets/drops aggro (page).
GROUP: stay near the wall; keep Andorov healed (above);
  if Andorov is dead, healers keep everyone topped -
  Thundercrash halves health (minimum 100) and a
  follow-up hit kills the low (page).
GROUND: none placed. Drenn's Hurricane is a channeled
  AoE centred on Drenn (see waves).
ADD ORDER: per wave: P1 the officer, P2 Swarmguard
  Needler (15344; 40504 Cleave up to 3 frontal), P3
  Qiraji Warriors. NEVER priority: do not pull the next
  wave early - waves are sequenced by the event.
ABILITY LIST (Rajaxx; ids unverified - no id links):
  Thundercrash - halves ALL players' health (minimum
    100 damage), knockback everyone, resets aggro.
  Aggro drop - drops his current target periodically.
AI RULE: kill each officer first; after Thundercrash,
  tank re-taunts at once and everyone stays by the wall;
  heal Andorov before raid members who are safe.

3. MOAM (entry unverified)
Add group: 3x Mana Fiend (15527) at 90 seconds.
PHASES: P1 mana race: he drains mana (Drain Mana: 500
  from 6 random players, +1000 to himself per player,
  page) - at 100% mana he casts Arcane Explosion (3000
  to the whole raid + knock-up, page) and his mana
  resets. At 90 sec he Turns to Stone: immune to damage,
  cannot attack or drain, summons 3 Mana Fiends; reverts
  after 90 sec or when all Fiends die (page). Kill goal:
  below 45% by the 90-sec stone form (page), or kill him
  outright in under 90 sec at high DPS (~340000 health,
  page numbers). At 120: burn him; the Fiend rules below
  are the stall case.
TANK: standard tank in P1; no aggro management needed
  while he is stone (he cannot move/attack, page).
GROUP: mana-drain team is the fight: warlocks + priests
  mana-burn him continuously, hunter keeps Viper Sting
  up (page) - pet classes and non-mana users are free
  DPS (cat-form druids unaffected by the drain, page).
  If the group CANNOT out-drain him: everyone but tank
  + one healer runs out before the 100%-mana explosion
  (page "fewer drainers" strategy).
GROUND: none. His Arcane Explosion is raid-wide, not a
  zone; the Mana Fiends' Arcane Explosion is a local AoE
  without knockback (page).
ADD ORDER: P1-P3 Mana Fiends (15527) - banish (warlock)
  or freeze-trap and kill one at a time AFTER Moam dies
  (they do not despawn on his death, page); while he is
  stone, banished Fiends are NEVER priority - DPS Moam.
  Fiend abilities (page): Arcane Explosion (AoE),
  Counterspell (school lockout 12 sec - their cast on
  YOU; interrupt priority: kill/banish before casts).
ABILITY LIST (Moam; ids unverified - page lists no ids):
  Trample - physical AoE around him.
  Drain Mana - see PHASES.
  Arcane Explosion - at 100% mana, 3000 raid-wide +
    knock-up.
  Turn to Stone - see PHASES.
AI RULE: if you can drain mana, drain Moam, never DPS;
  keep him below 100% mana or be out of the room-side
  group when he fills.

4. BURU THE GORGER (entry unverified)
Arena objects: 6 Buru Eggs (object entry unverified).
  Spawned add: Hive'Zara Hatchling (entry unverified).
PHASES: P1 (100-20%): he CANNOT be tanked or DPSed
  down normally - he fixates a random player and chases,
  continually speeding up; he catches a player after
  ~60 sec without an egg hit and Dismembers them
  (stacking bleed 1248/2 sec, page). Kite him onto an
  egg, destroy the egg UNDER him: 45000 damage to Buru
  (page), 100-500 to nearby players by proximity, resets
  his speed, he picks a new target, a Hatchling spawns.
  Pre-damage eggs to ~5% and finish them as he crosses
  (page). P2 at 20%: shell breaks, normal tank-and-burn,
  but Creeping Plague starts (below) - a DPS race.
TANK: P1: the fixated player IS the tank - run him over
  the marked egg, never through the deep water (slows
  you, he catches up; page). P2: standard tank.
GROUP: two groups (page): egg group (wands/light DPS to
  hold eggs at 5%, burst on the call) and Hatchling
  group (kill each spawned Hatchling before they build
  up). Healers watch the kite target after ramp jumps.
GROUND: the eggs themselves (explosion radius around
  the destroyed egg); deep water = slow zone, avoid.
ADD ORDER: P1 Buru Egg (only when Buru is on top of it;
  NEVER destroy an egg he is not standing on - it wastes
  one of six and spawns a Hatchling for nothing), P2
  Hive'Zara Hatchlings as they spawn.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified - page lists no ids):
  Dismember - stacking bleed 1248/2 sec, if he catches
    his target. Thorns - 200 damage thorns aura.
  Creeping Plague (below 20%) - raid-wide: 80 per tick,
    stacking +80 every 3 ticks, UNRESISTABLE and
    UNDISPELLABLE, even Ice Block (page, explicit).
  Immune to taunt (page).
AI RULE: if Buru is chasing you, run him onto the marked
  egg and keep running; never DPS Buru directly in P1.

5. AYAMISS THE HUNTER (15369)
Add groups: Hive'Zara Swarmer swarm (~20; entry
  unverified), Hive'Zara Larva (entry unverified), and
  the elite Hive'Zara Buzzer (entry unverified) that a
  successful Larva spawns.
PHASES: P1 airborne until 70%: ranged only (melee cannot
  hit him, page). P2 at 70%: he lands - COMPLETE AGGRO
  WIPE (page) and he is untauntable: let the tank build
  aggro before DPS (page). Tank him on the altar, back
  to the mountain border (page).
TANK: P1: the highest-aggro ranged player tanks him -
  Poison Stinger stacks on the top-aggro target only
  (25 Nature/sec per stack, to 100 stacks, page): swap
  the ranged tank at ~20 stacks (page strategy). P2:
  see PHASES; offtank ready if the MT is sacrificed.
GROUP: P1 ranged together at the back; melee wait at
  the altar base on Larva duty (below). Throughout:
  Stinger Spray ~1000 Nature to the whole raid every
  ~30 sec (page); swarms of ~20 Swarmers descend - AoE
  them (very low health, ~60 a hit on cloth, page).
GROUND: the altar top = the Paralyze sacrifice spot: a
  random player is teleported/shackled there and a
  Larva runs at them from the altar base; if it arrives
  it KILLS the player and spawns an elite Buzzer (page).
  The Larva is fast and cannot be slowed or stunned
  (page) - melee must already be in position.
ADD ORDER: P1 Hive'Zara Larva (the instant it spawns -
  nothing else matters while it lives), P2 Hive'Zara
  Swarmers (AoE in passing), P3 Hive'Zara Buzzer (only
  exists if a Larva got through - kill it, then boss).
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified - page lists no ids):
  Stinger Spray - ~1000 Nature, raid-wide, ~30 sec.
  Poison Stinger - stacking DoT on top-aggro, P1 only.
  Paralyze - see GROUND. Immune to Taunt (page).
AI RULE: melee live at the altar base and kill the
  Larva the second it appears; ranged swap the sting
  tank at 20 stacks.

6. OSSIRIAN THE UNSCARRED (15339)
Arena objects: Ossirian Crystals (object entry
  unverified) spawn around the courtyard. Hazard: Sand
  Vortex tornadoes (entry unverified).
PHASES: single phase, one mechanic: he STARTS in
  Supreme Mode (huge melee, very fast, page). Clicking
  a crystal puts a 45-sec debuff on him: much less
  damage, slowed, and vulnerable to one magic school
  (can stack several; Holy is exempt from the
  resistance/weakness system, page). The whole fight
  is: kite him crystal to crystal, click the next at
  30-35 sec elapsed on the debuff (crystals have a
  slight cast time - never at the last second, page),
  DPS only in the weakened windows, and NO raid DPS
  until after the 2nd crystal (tanks have almost no
  threat built, page). Alternative (page): two fast
  clickers patrol the edges clicking every crystal so
  they respawn in the centre; raid fights him in the
  centre - faster, but casters easily over-aggro.
TANK: MT stands BEHIND the first crystal, gains initial
  aggro from range, stays out of his reach until the
  first debuff lands (Supreme hits kill, page). Tank
  him just BEFORE each crystal, never on top of it -
  his knockback can throw the clicker (page). OT
  covers the Enveloping Winds stun (below): he will
  not target the stunned player but threat is preserved
  (page) - OT holds him until it breaks.
GROUP: stay between the tanks and the next crystal;
  healers stay close to the MT (knockback LoS, page).
  Decurse Curse of Tongues instantly, healers first
  (page). Watch the kite path for tornadoes.
GROUND: Sand Vortexes/tornadoes roam the zone once he
  is engaged (very high damage inside; hunters Track
  Elementals, page) and a sandstorm runs instance-wide
  from the pull (visibility only, page). The crystals
  are the safe-tempo points, not ground effects.
ADD ORDER: none. NEVER ignore a crystal timer for DPS:
  Supreme Mode returning is the wipe.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified - page lists no ids):
  Supreme Mode - see PHASES.
  Enveloping Winds - 10 sec stun on the aggro holder;
    cannot be dispelled; breaks on damage; Nature
    resist can resist it (page).
  Curse of Tongues - AoE curse around him: D-Curse -
    decurse instantly, healers first (page).
  Warstomp - ~1500 around him + knockback.
  Crystal Weakness debuff - 45 sec per crystal (above).
AI RULE: the crystal timer is the fight - move to the
  next crystal at 30 seconds, click by 35, and never
  DPS a Supreme Ossirian in melee range.

TRASH (Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj)
T1. The Rajaxx waves ARE the trash for the first half
  (boss 2 above): CC-able adds (frozen/feared/rooted/
  stunned, page); kill order officer > Needler >
  Warrior per page strategy.
T2. Reservoir/Canal trash before Moam (Obsidian
  Destroyer / Canal Frenzy types are named in zone
  listings; entries and spells unverified - pages not
  read). Kill order unverified; do not pull them into
  Moam's area.
T3. Hatchling/Hive trash around Buru and Ayamiss
  (Hive'Zara types; entries/spells unverified beyond
  the boss-spawned adds above). Rule: clear the egg
  room edges before pulling Buru; never start Buru
  with Hatchlings up.
General trash AI RULE: in this raid the "trash" that
  kills groups is event trash - keep Andorov alive,
  kill officers first, and never pull a boss with his
  room's adds still up.
