=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

Three pillars in a triangular layout, on a circular floor.

  "Ashamane's Fall is a circular map that features two square
   pillars and a rectangular pillar set out in a triangular
   formation."   (Liquipedia)

  "Three Large Pillars located around the outside of the room,
   circular arena, Pillars are closer than the Tol'viron Pillars"
   (Ministry of Defense arena guide, 2020-01-10)

  "a relatively small map with 3 small pillars ... It is
   circular and has thick bushes on the outside"
   (NamuWiki arena page)

The sources agree on three pillars and a circular arena. They do
not fully agree on placement: Liquipedia says a triangular
formation, the other two describe the pillars as around the
outside of the room. Both can be true at once - a triangle set
toward the rim - and that is the reading used here. The map is
smaller than Tol'viron, so the three pillars sit closer together
than Tol'viron's four.

Pillar count: 3. Verified by three independent sources
Pillar positions, size and shape: unverified. Nothing in server
  data places them, and no readable source gives x, y, z
Only one of the three is rectangular; two are square
No other LoS object is spawned: the map spawns exactly four
  objects, two gates and two orbs

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Three pillars and a small circular floor. The walk from each
spawn to the centre is 64.68 units, and the gate line is
narrower than the spawn line, so both teams funnel inward.

Melee DPS:
  Take the nearest pillar and use its face, not the centre. With
  only three pillars the cover is scarce, so the fight is decided
  by who owns the pillar nearest their own spawn
  The map is small; there is no long rotation to reset, so do not
  chase across the open middle

Ranged DPS:
  Three pillars on a small circle means constant repositioning
  rather than one safe spot. Hug the pillar that keeps the other
  two between you and the enemy melee
  Because the pillars sit close to the rim, the centre is open -
  avoid holding it against a melee cleave

Healers:
  Circle the pillars rather than standing still. The map is
  small enough that a healer can keep the whole arena in range
  from just outside the centre
  Do not park on the side of a pillar that faces the enemy spawn

General opener notes:
  Both teams are 64.68 from the centre, so first contact is
  simultaneous
  The gate line is 31.36 units narrower than the spawn line, so
  the two teams are pushed toward each other on the way in
  Both Shadow Sight orbs sit off the start axis, so taking one
  costs a detour

=== 7. MATCH FLOW NOTES ===

Gates close before the start and open together.
Worldstate 0xE1A is set to 1 on this map.
Dampening applies like every other arena.
Arena Preparation is applied on zone-in.
No out-of-bounds or under-map return points are wired in the BfA
core for this map.

Zone-file defect worth knowing, because it changes orb behaviour:
BattlegroundAF.cpp passes only three rotation floats to AddObject
and then the respawn value, so the arguments are misaligned by
one. The core signature wants four rotation floats followed by a
respawn time. In this core the respawn time parameter is unused
and the four floats become the game object's quaternion, so:
  - the two gates pass 0, 0, 0 then RESPAWN_IMMEDIATELY (0),
    giving four zeros, which trips the core's zeroed-rotation
    fallback and builds the quaternion from the orientation
    field, so the gates end up correct
  - the two orbs pass 0, 0, 0 then 120, giving the quaternion
    0, 0, 0, 120, which is not a unit quaternion, so the fallback
    does not trigger and the orbs keep a bad rotation
The practical effect is cosmetic on the orbs and harmless on the
gates, and the 120 was never a respawn time here anyway.
