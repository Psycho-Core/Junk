NAXXRAMAS (40-PLAYER) - TACTICS (BFA 8.3.7 build 35662)
Raid: original 40-man Naxxramas (patch 1.11 design,
as restored on this core). 15 bosses, 4 wings plus
Frostwyrm Lair.
Zone id / map id: unverified in this pass (the 3.0
Naxxramas reuses the same instance shell; do not
assume its ids without checking this core).
Coordinates (x, y, z): unverified for every spawn.
Sources: the "(Classic)" boss pages on
warcraft.wiki.gg, read for this file. Spell ids are
the Classic Wowhead links on those pages; they have
NOT been re-tooltipped against 8.3.7, so treat every
id as page-verified only. Any spell the page does
not link is printed "id unverified". NOTE: the
Kel'Thuzad page mixes later-expansion spell links
(55802/55807); those two are flagged in place.
Creature entries: NONE were verified this pass (no
NPC pages read) - all entries below are unverified.
That is deliberate; fill from this core's DB.

1. ANUB'REKHAN (entry unverified) - 1,665,500 HP
Starts with 2 Crypt Guards (204,380 HP each); one
more Crypt Guard joins at every Locust Swarm.
PHASES: Tank phase alternating with Locust Swarm
  kite (Swarm lasts 20 sec, boss slowed 40%).
TANK: Tank him at the far end; on Locust Swarm kite
  him slowly along the wall - he must not catch the
  raid, and nobody may be inside 30 yd of him while
  the swarm is up (pacify + stacking DoT).
GROUP: Ranged spread (Impale is a line at a random
  player). Kill Corpse Scarabs from player corpses
  fast (Summon Corpse Scarabs off Crypt Guard
  bodies too).
GROUND: Locust Swarm aura (30 yd) is the hazard.
ADD ORDER: P1 Crypt Guards (new one each swarm) >
  P2 Corpse Scarabs > boss.
AI RULE: when Locust Swarm starts, stop everything,
  stay 30+ yd away, and let the tank kite; resume
  only when it ends.
ABILITY LIST:
  28783 Impale - line, up to 3938 Physical + knockup
  28785 Locust Swarm - 20 sec aura, boss -40% speed
  28786 Locust Swarm (DoT) - 875-1125 Nature/2 sec,
    6 sec, pacified, stacks indefinitely
  Summon Corpse Scarabs - id unverified
  Crypt Guard abilities: unverified this pass

ADD ORDER: P1 Followers (kill both in about 30 sec

  kill by damage - they are the Enrage mechanic;

AI RULE: never damage a Worshipper; when she
  Enrages, the controlled Worshipper's sacrifice is
  the only answer - everything else is survival.
ABILITY LIST:
  28796 Poison Bolt Volley - Nature + DoT. D-Poison
