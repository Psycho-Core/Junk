BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - FIRST AID CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
=========================================================================================
Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client, no human at the keyboard) levels
First Aid on a TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Short answer: it cannot - First Aid was
removed in patch 8.0.1 and the bandage crafts now skill up Tailoring (anti-venoms and
liquids moved to Alchemy). Everything below was read from a source fetched this week
(2026-10-27); anything without a readable source says "unverified" instead of being
guessed. Craft counts are exactly the counts the linked guides print (they warn that
yellow/green recipes need more crafts - those warnings are kept where printed).

1. THE SKILL LINES AND HOW A COMPANION LEARNS THEM
--------------------------------------------------

to gain, no cap to reach. Evidence chain:

   trainers are ordinary trainer creatures, and on BfA the four tailoring trainers
   simply carry 267201 in their lists (wowhead Taught by (4)).
b) Casting the craft: Spell.cpp 3869 TakePower() then 3872 TakeReagents() before
   HandleEffects (definition TakeReagents at Spell.cpp 5600); a cast can skip
   reagents only via ITEM_FLAG_NO_REAGENT_COST 0x10000000 (ItemTemplate.h 218), and
   item-cast crafts gain skill through SPELL_ATTR9_ITEM_CAST_GRANTS_SKILL_GAIN at
   Spell.cpp 3863-3865. There is NO server-side skill gate on recipe casts: a
   game-wide RequiredSkill grep (excluding the DB2 load info DB2LoadInfo.h 3301 and
   quest checks Player.cpp 15405 Player::SatisfyQuestSkill) finds only ITEM-side
   checks - Player::CanUseItem(ItemTemplate) at Player.cpp 11184 reading
   proto->GetRequiredSkill() at 11203-11208 (skill 0 -> EQUIP_ERR_PROFICIENCY_NEEDED,
   below rank -> EQUIP_ERR_CANT_EQUIP_SKILL; item-use caller at 31677), the loot-roll
   variant CanRollNeedForItem at 11245 / 11267, transmog-set and enchant checks at
   Item.cpp 89 and 1422-1428. Those gate USING/EQUIPPING items - a bandage whose
   template carried RequiredSkill would be blocked on use - but wowhead renders no
   Required-skill line on the probed bandage items (1251's box, and 158381 shows only
   "Requires Level 40"); craft casts themselves are ungated.
c) Skill-ups on craft: Player.cpp 5410 UpdateCraftSkill -> blocked by
   SPELL_ATTR1_NO_SKILL_INCREASE (5412-5413); reads the spell's SkillupSkillLineID
   (5422), multiplies NumSkillUps x CONFIG_SKILL_GAIN_CRAFTING (5433) and returns
   UpdateSkillPro against TrivialSkillLineRankHigh/Low via SkillGainChance
   (definition 5399, call 5435). Discovery: inside the same loop, Mechanic ==
   MECHANIC_DISCOVERY (5427) calls GetSkillDiscoverySpell + LearnSpell (5429-5430).
   Weights: World.cpp 784-787 SkillChance.Orange 100 / Yellow 75 / Green 25 / Grey 0,
   World.cpp 790 SkillGain.Crafting default 1. For bandages the SkillupSkillLineID is
   the Tailoring sub-skill wowhead prints in each quick-facts "Skill:" row (3275 ->
   Classic Tailoring, 267201 -> Kul Tiran Tailoring): bandage crafts raise Tailoring,
   never First Aid, which has no enum to raise.
d) Learn and skill-step effects: dispatch SpellEffects.cpp 128 (LEARN_SPELL 36),
   136 (SKILL_STEP 44 -> EffectLearnSkill 2405), 139 (TRADE_SKILL 47 ->
   EffectTradeSkill 2451) and 210 (effect 118 SKILL -> EffectSkill 4668);
   EffectLearnSpell 2129 (TriggerSpell learn at 2170). EffectLearnSkill validates
   before granting anything: skillid from MiscValue (2418), then
   GetSkillRaceClassInfo(skillid, race, class) must exist (guard 2419-2421) and a
   SkillTiers row must exist (guard 2423-2425), else the effect returns; SetSkill is
   at 2433. The legacy relic spell 160329 (effect Learn Skill Step 129, value 9) can
   therefore only grant skill 129 if the server's DB2 still ships
   SkillRaceClassInfo/SkillTiers rows for 129 - data-side, unverified (no DB2 files
   in this repo). EffectTradeSkill's body is a NO-OP (player-type check, then only
   commented-out code, SpellEffects.cpp 2451-2461) - irrelevant to bandages anyway:
   all three probed crafts render plain "Create Item".
e) Create item: EffectCreateItem dispatch SpellEffects.cpp 116 -> EffectCreateItem
   1473 with SPELL_ATTR0_IS_TRADESKILL -> ItemContext::Trade_Skill at 1478 (further
   selections at 1492, 1522, 2484), DoCreateItem 1383; EffectCreateItem2 dispatch
   249 (SPELL_EFFECT_CREATE_ITEM_2 = 157) -> definition 1482. The probe-verified
   effect shape for bandages/anti-venoms is Create Item (3275 -> item 1251, 7934 ->
   item 6452 x3, 267201 -> item 158381), cast time 2 seconds on all three; the
   legacy 160329 is 10 seconds.
f) No craft station: unlike Cooking's "Requires Cooking Fire" (Craft_Cooking section
   7e), no probed bandage or anti-venom page prints any focus/station requirement,
   and the wiki official overview states "Antidotes and bandages can be created
   almost anywhere, as long as you have the appropriate ingredients - cloth (for
   bandages) and venom sacs (for anti-venom)." No GameObject focus and no focus
   aura is needed for the craft.
g) Identifiers: SKILL_TAILORING = 197 (SharedDefines.h 5911); the Tailoring
   sub-skills SKILL_KUL_TIRAN_TAILORING 2533 .. SKILL_CLASSIC_TAILORING 2540 live

   QUEST_TRSKILL_TAILORING = 10 (QuestDef.h 134); IsProfessionSkill =

h) USING the bandage (not needed for leveling, documented because it is the only

8. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------
  1. Train First Aid, gain one First Aid skill point, or reach any First Aid cap:
     the skill line does not exist in the enum, in trainers, or in any fetched
     8.3.7 source (section 1). A routine written against skill 129 has nothing to
     write to.
  2. Make skill 129 appear via relic spells: 160329 would early-return inside
     EffectLearnSkill unless DB2 rows for 129 exist (section 7d), and wowpedia
     marks the spell Deprecated (8.0.1) anyway.
  3. Follow any published "First Aid 1-300 / 1-450 / 1-800" guide on this server:
     those routes describe the removed line; no retail First Aid leveling guide
     exists anymore to copy counts from (section 3, historical block).
  4. Click profession-window buttons: crafts must be cast by spell id with the
     recipe already in the spellbook; a server-side character has no profession UI
     (learn path = section 7a).
  5. Talk to the trainers in section 2 at all: every bandage NPC, the four BfA
     tailoring trainers, and the wiki's ~40 historical rows need interaction
     packets (NPCHandler 97/130).
  6. Re-learn First Aid crafts automatically on a fresh character: Blizzard's own
     quote says those crafts "will have to be re-learned" (wowhead news 282901) -
     the server must teach every bandage recipe through its own trainer lists;
     nothing carries over from a pre-8.0.1 profile unless the DB still has it.
  7. Craft anti-venoms under a First Aid assumption: on 8.3.7 they are Alchemy
     recipes (7934 under Classic Alchemy, taught by Alchemy trainers; 160329's
     Teaches table splits 1 Tailoring / 3 Alchemy) - the character needs the
     Alchemy line (Craft_Alchemy.txt), not a first-aid skill.
  8. Earn the removed First Aid achievements or the Field Medic title:
     recategorized Legacy or removed at 8.0.1 (wow-professions list: Legion Medic
     "Obtain 800 skill points in first aid", Stocking Up, Preparing for Disaster,
     Ultimate Triage, Field Medic) - no in-game reward path remains.
  9. Reliably obtain the world-drop teachers: Manual: Strong Anti-Venom (rare world
     drop), Manual: Heavy Frostweave Bandage (common BoP world drop from level 75+
     humanoids in Northrend - the wiki also prints a 6g trainer alternative),
     Formula: Powerful Anti-Venom (Argent Dawn Honored, 10g) - RNG and reputation
     gates the core does not roll for you.
 10. Use a bandage whenever it likes (if the companion uses them at all): spell
     flags block shapeshifted use (3275/7934 flags), the wiki rules interrupt the
     channel on any damage including shield-absorbed hits, and Recently Bandaged
     holds a 60-second cooldown (section 7h) - window logic, not craft logic.
 11. Read complete trainer coverage from the wiki: First_Aid_trainers stops at
     Pandaria (section 2A) and the old profession-train gossip does not exist on
     8.3.7 - data assembled from the wiki alone is pre-removal data.
