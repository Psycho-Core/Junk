Method notes / fixes
--------------------
- Preflight: 533/560 with 27 FAILs - every failure was a script-side needle
  problem (a needle spanning a line wrap, or wording drifted from the payload);
  each was located with a +80-char context dump around the needle and shortened
  to a wrap-safe contiguous fragment (e.g. the Angela Leifeld / McQueen / Clegg /
  Applewood / Tompkins trainer rows, "Loremaster Skosiris npc 33642", the
  wrap-split "Spell\n201708 (added 7.0.1)" line, the lowercase "granting wrapper
  264629", the "Draenor Tailoring\"; 267201" join, the sec.9 fact "->" vs "->" arrow
  drift). No payload line was changed for the audit.
- grep across a wrap-spanning needle with `set -e` aborts silently (exit 1 on the
  first miss); the python context dump replaced it.
- Mutation design: literals chosen wrap-free and mostly single-occurrence;
  replace-first is safe here because a miss in the *mutated* text only matters if
  the checks still pass - the self-test fails loudly if a mutation literal
  disappears from the payload or mutates a no-op.
- Hygiene: applied to the deliverable (760 lines, ASCII, 0 >100 cols,
  0 trailing-ws) and to this report; the audit script itself is exempt per
  established practice.
- Containment checks cannot prove a number never appears elsewhere; the
  structural families (section headings, LEARN/ENTRY pairing, totals captions,
  cost partitions) make a wrong number almost impossible to hide.
