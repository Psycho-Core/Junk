1. ATTUMEN THE HUNTSMAN + MIDNIGHT (opt.)
PHASES: P1 Midnight alone; at 95% Attumen spawns at
  her position. P2 both active. At 25% of EITHER he
  mounts: one boss at the HIGHER of the two health
  percentages, gaining Berserker Charge.
TANK: Two tanks P2 (Attumen taunt-immune). Face his
  Shadow Cleave (about 4000) away. Disarm him
  (about -65% damage while disarmed).
GROUP: Decurse Intangible Presence (about every 30
  sec, -50% hit, centred on his target). D-Curse.
  P3: spread 8-40 yd is his charge band - expect
  random charges with knockdown.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: P1 (P2) damage both evenly to the 25%
  merge - never burn one far ahead.
AI RULE: even damage before the mount, decurse
  always; the merged boss keeps the higher health.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Shadow Cleave;
  Intangible Presence (D-Curse); Berserker Charge;
  Midnight kick/charge set unverified.

2. Moroes + 4 DINNER GUESTS (of 6 possible, 47-58k)
GUESTS (page kill discussion, all class-based):
  Baroness Dorothea Millstipe (shadow priest):
    Mana Burn (THE danger spell - interrupt/stun/
    silence every cast; no tank needed), Mind Flay
  Lady Catriona Von'Indi (holy priest): Greater Heal
    (interrupt), Power Word: Shield, Dispel Magic,
    Holy Fire
  Lady Keira Berrybuck (holy paladin): Holy Light,
    Cleanse (frees CC'd guests - keep her shackled
    all fight or kill early), Blessing of Might
    (+550 AP - dispel/purge it), Divine Shield
  Baron Rafe Dreuger (ret paladin): Hammer of
    Justice (stuns his tank then runs at healers -
    tank required to kill), Cleanse
  Lord Robin Daris (arms warrior): Mortal Strike,
    Whirlwind (keep away from raid); trap/shackle
  Lord Crispin Ference (prot warrior): Disarm,
    Shield Bash, Shield Wall; slowest kill - leave
    him offtanked (even as a rage battery) and kill
    after Moroes
PHASES: One phase; Moroes Enrages at 30% (stops
  casting); berserk dagger-throwing after a long
  timer (page gives no exact time - unverified).
TANK: Moroes taunt-immune. Gouge stuns his current
  tank and he goes to 2nd threat - BOTH tanks stay
  top-2 threat and nobody else may pass them.
GROUP: CC on pull (Shackle Undead, Turn Evil,
  Freezing Trap) and hold it; Garrote after every
  Vanish: 1000/3 sec for FIVE MINUTES, undispellable
  (D: none - Stoneshield/immunities only in era;
  it is a healing-budget race). Blind (poison)
  disorients the closest non-tank - tank swaps and
  spacing matter. D-Poison for Blind.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: by table discussion: healers and the
  mana-burner first as the raid's control allows;
  NEVER break CC without a kill call - a free guest
  kills cloth players. Moroes second-to-last,
  Crispin Ference last if present.
AI RULE: control first, kill in the called order,
  and track every Garrote - the fight is a healing
  budget, not a DPS check on the boss.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Vanish; Garrote;
  Blind (D-Poison); Gouge; Enrage 30%.

3. MAIDEN OF VIRTUE (optional)
PHASES: None - same behaviour throughout; Berserk
  at exactly 10 min.
TANK: Centre of the room where she stands
  (taunt-immune). Load HoTs before Repentance; tank
  cooldowns (Shield Wall etc.) are for Repentance
  windows.
GROUP: 8 pillars: one ranged/healer per pillar gap,
  spread so Holy Wrath cannot chain (it chains -
  never stand within jump range of another player).
  Holy Fire MUST be dispelled instantly - cancel a
  cast to dispel. D-Magic. Repentance: raid-wide
  12-sec incapacitate except her current target,
  30-sec CD, not cast during... it breaks on ANY
  damage: era answers are a paladin taking damage
  via Blessing of Sacrifice, a healer stepping into
  Holy Ground, immunities (Divine Shield/Ice Block)
  or pre-loaded HoTs. She does not Holy Fire during
  Repentance.
GROUND: Holy Ground - permanent 12-yd aura around
  her: 240-360 Holy/3 sec + 1-sec silence. Melee
  and tank only inside.
ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: dispel Holy Fire before healing anything;
  spread, and have a Repentance wake-up plan before
  the pull.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Holy Ground; Holy
  Fire (1-sec cast, 3238-3762 + 1750/2 sec 12 sec,
  D-Magic; reflectable/Grounding Totem); Holy Wrath
  (chained, 20-sec CD); Repentance (1750-2250 +
  12-sec incapacitate).

5. THE Curator
PHASES: Flare phase (90 sec: an Astral Flare every
  10 sec, 10 total, draining his mana 10% each) >
  Evocation (20 sec: inactive, takes +200% damage)
  > repeat. At 15%: soft enrage - no more Flares or
  Evocations, faster Hateful Bolts. Hard enrage 10
  min. Immune to Arcane, poison, taunt, mana drain.
TANK: Boss tank (about 3000 melee). Flares: an
  assigned holder with high threat-on-spawn keeps
  them still (hunter pet ideal era answer).
GROUP: EVERY damage dealer kills the Flare within
  its 10-sec window: Astral Flare (17,000 HP) chains
  Arcing Sear to 3 targets within 10 yd (675-825).
  Two Flares alive is the wipe path. Hateful Bolt
  (4435-5999 Arcane) hits the highest-threat
  non-tank since last Evocation - an assigned
  arcane-geared soaker is optional. All cooldowns
  into Evocation - but kill the 10th Flare first.
GROUND: Flares ARE the ground problem (moving).
ADD ORDER: P1 Astral Flare (always) > boss only
  when no Flare lives; in Evocation, Flare then
  boss.
AI RULE: a living Astral Flare outranks the boss at
  every moment of the fight except inside your own
  assigned Evocation burst after the 10th is dead.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Summon Astral
  Flare; Hateful Bolt; Evocation (+200% taken).

AI RULE: chains first, instantly, every time -

ABILITY LIST: Sacrifice/Demon Chains (id
  unverified); Kil'rek 30053, 30065 (page-linked);

7. SHADE OF ARAN (no tank; threat mostly ignored)
Specials every 30-35 sec, never the same twice:
  Flame Wreath (5-sec cast): rings on 3 players for
  20 sec - DO NOT MOVE AT ALL (moving in/out deals
  3000-4000 Fire to the WHOLE room; rings persist
  even after he dies).
  Circular Blizzard: slow clockwise blizzard, 1313-
  1687 Frost/2 sec + 65% slow; centre is safe.
  Magnetic Pull + Slow, then 10-sec cast Super
  Arcane Explosion (9000-11000, 20 yd): run to the
  outer wall.
At 40% HP: 4 Conjured Water Elementals (13,000 HP,
Waterbolts 1000-2000, despawn 90 sec) - kill them.
At 20% MANA: mass Polymorph, he drinks 10 sec,
then raid-wide Pyroblast 7000-7500. Do NOT
interrupt the drink (interrupting brings the
Pyroblast early onto a polymorphed raid; era raids
let him finish). Page strategy goal: keep the 40%
HP and 20% mana events FAR apart via interrupt
management of his basic spells (Fireball/Frostbolt/
Arcane Missiles, all interruptible. I). AoE
Counterspell (10 yd, 10-sec lockout) fires often -
casters stay out of 10 yd.
AI RULE: movement discipline is the encounter:
  Wreath = freeze, Blizzard = centre/edge as called,
  Pull = run to the wall. When in doubt, do not
  move during Flame Wreath.
Berserk at 12 min (Shadows of Aran).
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Fireball, Frostbolt
  (chance to root - dispellable, D-Magic), Arcane
  Missiles, Flame Wreath, Blizzard, Magnetic Pull/
  Super Arcane Explosion, Polymorph/Pyroblast.

8. NETHERSPITE
PHASES: Portal phase 60 sec > Banish phase 30 sec,
  alternating. Enrage at 9 min (+500%).
Portal phase: 3 fixed portals beam at him; players
  BLOCK beams by standing in them (a beam reaching
  him buffs/heals him; buffs on him last 5 sec).
  RED (Perseverance, tank): blocker gains aggro,
  +defense/health, full heal per tick; boss gains
  -1% damage taken per tick if unblocked.
  GREEN (Serenity, healer/manaless): boss HEALS
  4000/tick stacking if unblocked - never allow;
  player: +5% healing/tick, mana pool shrinks.
  BLUE (Dominance, DPS): player +5% damage/tick,
  +8% spell damage taken; boss +1% spell damage.
  After leaving a beam: Nether Exhaustion - cannot
  block THAT colour for 90 sec, so two blockers
  rotate per beam (era: about 20 ticks each).
Banish phase: he is banished (cannot be damaged -
  verify on this core; page strategy treats DPS as
  paused), casts Nether Burn (shadow aura), Void
  Zones appear - move out. 2.5-sec casts (page:
  Netherbreath-type volleys) unverified.
TANK: the red-beam blocker IS the tank; no one
  else needs threat skills in portal phase.
GROUP: assigned beam teams; everyone else DPS/heals
  from max range; spread for Void Zones.
GROUND: Void Zones (Banish phase, id unverified).
ADD ORDER: none. Blocking is the add mechanic.
AI RULE: your beam is your job for the whole phase:
  be in it before it ticks on him, swap at your
  tick count, and never take a colour you are
  Exhausted for.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Empowerment (+200%
  damage, portal phase, undispellable); Nether
  Burn; Void Zone; Enrage 9 min.

13. Gruul THE DRAGONKILLER (about 3.4M HP)
PHASES: One phase against the clock: Growth every
  30 sec (+15% damage, +10% size, stacks to 30) -
  roughly a 10-minute practical kill window; at
  high Growth Hurtful Strike one-shots the OT.
TANK: MT centre; OT is the designated Hurtful
  Strike soak: it always hits the 2nd-highest
  threat IN MELEE RANGE (12,350-13,650 base, about
  every 20 sec). Melee DPS must stay below the OT
  on threat - or they take it and die.
GROUP: SPREAD always at range: Ground Slam throws
  everyone randomly, then Gronn Lord's Grasp slows
  -20% x5 over 5 sec, then Stoned, then Shatter:
  damage scales with proximity (about 1100 at 15+
  yd to 8500+ point blank, goes through immunities;
  pets don't count). After the Slam, run APART
  before you freeze. Reverberation: zone-wide
  4-sec silence (pre-cast HoTs; not dispellable).
GROUND: Cave In - shaking ground/ceiling warning,
  about 3000/3 sec in the patch, more frequent and
  harder with Growth. Move on the shake.
ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: after Ground Slam, distance from OTHER
  PLAYERS is your only defence - run apart first,
  DPS second.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Growth; Hurtful
  Strike; Reverberation; Cave In; Ground Slam /
  Gronn Lord's Grasp / Stoned / Shatter chain.

14. MAGTHERIDON (3,372,866 HP)
PHASES: P1 (120 sec): 5 Hellfire Channelers (240k)
  around 5 Manticron Cubes; Magtheridon inactive.
  Kill at least 3 Channelers before he frees (era
  target: all 5). Each Channeler death buffs the
  survivors (Soul Transfer: +30% damage/cast speed
  per stack - the last channels at 220%).
  P2: boss active. Blast Nova every 60 sec: all 5
  cubes clicked (channelled) SIMULTANEOUSLY
  interrupts it and gives him +300% damage taken
  while all 5 channel; channel ticks 800 on the
  clicker, and ending gives 30-sec Mind Exhaustion
  (rotate 2 clicker teams of 5). At 30% (P3 in era
  terms): roof collapses debris (page detail not
  read this pass - unverified beyond this line).
TANK: MT on boss (frontal Cleave about 8000 -
  face away); one tank per Channeler (or two share).
GROUP P1: interrupt Dark Mending (2 sec cast, heals
  32k) ABOVE ALL, then Shadow Volley (1 sec); Fear
  on tanks called. Burning Abyssals (max 5):
  banish/fear/trap/nova or tank; landing hit
  2625-3375, Fire Blast 3300 (20 yd).
GROUP P2: spread for Conflagration patches (6000
  over 8 sec); Quake (every 50 sec): 7 sec of
  1-sec knockbacks - stop casting, it interrupts.
  Cube teams: click on the Nova cast, all 5 or
  nothing; a partial click is worse than none
  (Nova continues AND clickers are exhausted).
GROUND: Conflagration patches; debris P3
  (unverified).
ADD ORDER P1: Channelers in rotation order >
  Abyssals (CC, do not chase) > boss (inactive).
  NEVER leave a Channeler free-casting Mending.
AI RULE: cubes are a five-player chord: click
  together on Blast Nova, or do not click at all.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Channeler: Shadow
  Volley I, Dark Mending I (top), Fear, Summon
  Abyssal, Soul Transfer. Magtheridon: Cleave,
  Conflagration, Quake, Blast Nova (cube-answer
  only), Enrage 22 min.
