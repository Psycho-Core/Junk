AUDIT OF Travel_Zandalar.txt - ONE CLAIM PER LINE
==============================================================================

Every block was re-derived from the source document with a second, independent
parser (not the one that wrote the file) and compared field by field. One entry
inherited from the source document was replaced, and four inherited entries were
withdrawn as unverified, because the ids section 3.1 prints belong to NPCs on
the other continent.

RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
30 claims: 27 PASS, 1 FIXED, 2 UNVERIFIED. One edit to the file: the Port of
Zandalar entry changed from 146507 to 121252, with a note explaining why, and
the footer sentence that counts document-sourced entries was changed from 33+4
to 32+5 to match. No coordinate, link, travel time, faction or gate value
changed, and the block count is unchanged at 44.

What was re-read rather than trusted:
  - all 44 positions, bit numbers, link sets and travel times were re-parsed
    from the section-1642 text and compared with the file;
  - the four nodes that appear in no TaxiPath row were confirmed to be isolated
    in both directions, which is why the file prints no reverse-only legs;
  - the five entries that are not straight section-3.1 copies were re-opened on
    Wowhead, and the Scroll of Flight family was re-read after the four Zuldazar
    rows turned out to point at a Stormsong Valley NPC;
  - the one gate, the faction tally, the 190-row link total and the four
    duplicate-master cases were recounted.
Not re-read: the 8.3.7 TaxiNodes export itself (section 1642 of the research
document is the transcription used here, as in steps 01 to 07).
