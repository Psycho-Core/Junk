BEGUILING — historical Battle for Azeroth Mythic+ season 3; client 8.3.7 (build 35662)

SCOPE: Beguiling (affix 119) was the season-3 +10 seasonal affix. Retail season 4, including 8.3.7, used a different seasonal affix: the historical Beguiling placements and mechanics below do NOT establish that this private core enables it, or that its database/script matches retail. All health, spawn, spell and forces values on this particular core: unverified without its data and an in-game test. No Beguiling-specific item ID was established (unverified).

EMISSARY OF THE TIDES — creature 155434: Passive Queen's Decree: Unstoppable (emitter spell 302417) grants nearby allied enemies recipient aura 302418, protecting them from interrupts, silence, disorient, stun, daze, incapacitate, sleep, snare, slow and root. This protects nearby enemies, not the party. Exact area radius, aura linger after moving out and server-side refresh interval: unverified; the spell's range is not proof of the emitter radius. An affected enemy can be controlled after separation from the emissary/aura; the Tides emissary itself can be controlled when susceptible. Later 2019 hotfix stopped the decree applying across large vertical separations. At one health it teleports away rather than dying normally (Teleport: The Eternal Palace, 302415); exact starting health/attack damage by level: unverified. 302417 has no fixed buff duration in the spell listing; 302418 listed duration is n/a: do not assign a timed buff after leaving its area.

VOID-TOUCHED EMISSARY — creature 155433: Queen's Decree: Hide (302420) is an uninterruptible cast that damages players within its approximately 60-yard line-of-sight area; spell listing gives a nine-second cast and a hit equal to 50% of the target's maximum health as Shadow damage. July 12, 2019 LIVE hotfix: the emissary no longer stacks a Shadow-damage-dealt buff on itself; a player struck instead gets a stacking Shadow-damage-TAKEN debuff lasting 12 seconds. Do not implement the early PTR self-buff. The 302420 listing itself includes a damage-taken aura effect; a separate post-hotfix debuff spell ID is unverified. Related spell 302421 is a 15-second SELF Shadow-damage-dealt aura in the current listing, but the July live hotfix explicitly removed that self-stacking behavior. Whether the present 302420 listing's differing 13-second aura duration represents the July live script or later data: unverified; prefer the dated live hotfix for historical effect. It does not give a beneficial buff to nearby enemy trash. Line of sight or moving beyond its range avoids a hit; Void was made immune to Distract in a July 2019 hotfix. At one health it teleports (302415); exact starting health/attack damage by level: unverified.

ENCHANTED EMISSARY — creature 155432: Queen's Decree: Blowback emitter 290027 grants nearby allied enemies recipient reflect aura 290026; the spell listing says 150% of damage dealt reflected back at the attacker. Earlier 2019 PTR previews say 200%; the exact coefficient in the historical season-3 live build 35662 server implementation is unverified; do not silently copy the early PTR value. Direct damage to the emissary knocks it back (Enchanted, 303632), allowing positioning away from affected trash. Its combat energy runs down and it teleports away (302415) when exhausted rather than being killed like ordinary trash; published descriptions disagree on exact elapsed time, so precise energy drain, combat-reset behavior, health/attack damage by level: unverified. Emitter 290027 has no listed fixed duration; recipient 290026 lists one minute in current spell data, but whether that is an effective lingering retail aura or a refreshed/script-controlled area aura is unverified. Do NOT promise a full minute of reflection after the emissary leaves. The emissary itself is not a normal reflect-buffed trash target.

BUFF OVERLAP / REMOVAL: Tides' 302418 and Enchanted's 290026 are different effects from different emitters. If an enemy is in both areas, independently check both protections; exact overlap, stacking rules, refresh timing, and actual coexistence of these two emissaries in a specific historical weekly pack: unverified. Void's 302420 harms players, not a third ally buff. Remove nearby-enemy protection by separating trash from Tides or Enchanted, moving/knocking the emitter away, or causing that emitter to teleport; precise lingering duration is unverified. Crowd control on Tides can isolate it but is not evidence that its area aura is dispelled while it remains in range. No verified player dispel or mass-dispel shortcut for either aura: unverified. The Shadow vulnerability on players expires after 12 seconds according to the July live hotfix; other methods of clearing it: unverified. Emissaries were made unaffected by Sanguine in the July 2019 hotfix.

ENEMY FORCES: Contemporary PTR observation: Tides (155434) and Void (155433) grant count when they cast teleport 302415, not when simply pulled; Enchanted (155432) grants ZERO. PTR measured raw counts of 4 each in King's Rest and MOTHERLODE!!, 9 in Shrine, and 0 in then-bugged Tol Dagor. Other dungeon counts in that article were explicitly SPECULATION, not measurements. Blizzard's July 26 live hotfix fixed missing credit in Tol Dagor and Siege of Boralus. Later community reports describe approximately 4 raw count in most dungeons for Tides/Void but exceptions in Shrine and Tol Dagor; final per-dungeon live raw count, percentage of bar and exact 8.3.7 private-core values: unverified. Never equate raw count 4 with 4% of a dungeon bar; never give count just because an Enchanted emissary was knocked away. Ordinary trash still grants its ordinary dungeon-specific count when killed.
