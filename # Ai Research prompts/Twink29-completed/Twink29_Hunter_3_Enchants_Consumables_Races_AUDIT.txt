1. THE ENGINEERING SECTION IS WHERE THE FILE FAILS
   The file told the reader to take Engineering "for the one item that does
   what a hunter cannot" - the Gnomish Net-o-Matic Projector - and then
   gave that item the wrong skill gate and an invented material list.
   1  CORRECTED, AND THE WORST ERROR IN THE HUNTER SET  10720 Gnomish
      Net-o-Matic Projector. The recipes file is explicit: spell 12902,
      Gnomish Engineering, Engineering 210. Gnomish and Goblin
      specialisation need level 30 and 200 skill, so a 29 cannot make one
      at all. What a 29 can do is wear one made by somebody else: the item
      carries RequiredAbility 0, so it binds to whoever equips it and needs
      no specialisation to use. The file now says ALT and buy.
   2  CORRECTED  The materials printed in the file - "5x Mithril Bar 3860,
      1x Jade 1529, 1x Gyrochronatom 4389" - are wrong, and the numbers
      were reconstructed rather than read, which is exactly what this set
      forbids. The real list from the recipes file: 1x Mithril Tube 10559,
      2x Shadow Silk 10285, 4x Thick Spider's Silk 4337, 2x Solid Blasting
      Powder 10505, 4x Mithril Bar 3860. Corrected.
   3  CORRECTED  The effect line was thin. It nets the target in place for
      a root on a 10 minute cooldown, and it can backfire and net the user.
      Both halves now in the file, because a bot that roots itself is a
      bot that has lost.
   4  CORRECTED  10716 Gnomish Shrink Ray was listed as an ordinary 205
      craft. It is spell 12899, Gnomish Engineering, 205 - another ALT
      item. Its effect is -250 attack power for a window on a 5 minute
      cooldown, not the vaguer "reduces damage and health" the file gave.
   5  PASS  4390 Iron Grenade, skill 175, spell 3962, 132-218 Fire and a
      3 sec stun in 3 yards. 4394 Big Iron Bomb, skill 190, spell 3967,
      149-201 Fire and a 3 sec stun in 5 yards. Both craftable at 29.
   6  PASS  4388 Discombobulator Ray, skill 160, spell 3959, craftable at
      29. The file called it a disorient and a slow; the recipes file is
      more useful than that - it turns the target into a leper gnome for
      12 sec at -40 melee and spell damage and -20% movement, and it
      forces a dismount. Rewritten, because that is the strongest item
      debuff in the bracket and the file buried it.
   7  PASS  10518 Parachute Cloak, skill 225, spell 12616, +8 Agility on
      the back, 10 sec of slow fall on a 30 minute cooldown. Craftable at
      29 and the best back item on the list.
   8  CORRECTED  109076 Goblin Glider Kit is not "bought from an
      engineer": it is auction house only, because spell 162204 and spell
      221234 are Draenor and Legion recipes a 29 cannot learn.
   9  MISSED  4397 Gnomish Cloaking Device, skill 200, spell 3971, and
      the recipes file is emphatic that this one needs no specialisation
      to craft or to use - the wiki names it as its own counter-example.
      10 sec of invisibility on a 1 hour cooldown. Added to the file: for
      a hunter bot it is the reset button, and it was absent.
   10 PASS  4381 Minor Recombobulator, skill 140, 10 charges, dispels a
      polymorph and heals. 4385 Green Tinted Goggles, cloth head, +8
      Stamina and +7 Spirit - correctly called the one engineering item to
      skip on a mail build, because Spirit does nothing for a hunter.
   11 PASS  "Gnomish or Goblin specialisation needs level 30 and 200
      skill, so every specialised recipe is ALT." True, and it is the
      sentence the Net-o-Matic paragraph should have obeyed.
