How the pool was built: an item search filtered on rare quality, required
level 26 to 29 and item level 35 or less, run per armour and weapon type,
then each row read off the search page. Where a slot has no rare item in
that window the row says so; nothing was invented to fill a slot.

THREE THINGS THAT APPLY TO EVERY SPEC
 1. A hunter wears mail from level 1 in 8.3.7 (established), but leather
    is legal too and several of the best 29 pieces are leather. Mixing
    costs the all-one-armour-type bonus (unverified at this level).
 2. The 20-29 Arathi Basin reward set is two pieces, a belt and boots,
    class-locked: 20090 and 20093 on Alliance, 20152 and 20157 on Horde,
    all item level 33, required 28, tagged Hunter and Shaman.
 3. Only two jewellery pieces in the whole 26-29 rare pool came back
    verified: 13087 and 13097. The ring and neck rows are therefore thin,
    and that is a gap in the data, not a claim that nothing else exists.

4. WHAT IS NOT IN THIS FILE, AND WHY
   Rings, neck and trinkets are the thin slots. The rare search returned
   no jewellery category at 29 at all, so 13087 and 13097 are the only two
   verified pieces and both are world-drop rares with random availability.
   A second ring, a better neck and any trinket beyond 19024 and the
   battleground Insignia are unverified.
   Heirlooms: only 42952 Stained Shadowcraft Spaulders was verified before
   (82 armor, +7 Stamina, +6 hit, +20 attack power at 29, item level shown
   as 1). The leather tunic, the bow and the two-hand axe heirlooms were
   not opened for this file, so no ids are quoted for them.
   Polearms were not returned by the weapon search in this window; if a
   Survival hunter wants one, it is unverified rather than absent.
   All armour numbers are the WotLK-era item pages. Where 8.3.7 re-itemised
   an item its item level can differ, and no required level moved on
   anything checked.
