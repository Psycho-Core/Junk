1. THE TWO SLOTS THAT WERE MISSING - CORRECTED
   The file listed eight enchant slots and said nothing about neck or ring.
   Both slots take an enchant at 29, and both need a higher-level enchanter,
   because they are Legion recipes, not classic ones. This is the biggest
   error in the file and it changes the shopping list.

   Neck - the Mark of the ... family, Legion Enchanting.
     190894 Mark of the Hidden Satyr. Enchant id 5439. Permanently enchants
        a necklace to sometimes summon a satyr that fires a nightmare bolt
        at the enemy, dealing damage. Tooltip: "Cannot be applied to items
        above level 120", so a level 29 neck of item level 33 is inside the
        cap. Reagents 15x Chaos Crystal 124442 and 12x Leylight Shard
        124441, tool Runed Enchanting Rod. The damage enchant: the pick for
        Balance and Feral.
     190892 Mark of the Claw. Sometimes increases critical strike and haste
        for 6 seconds. The sustained-stat pick, and the one to put on a
        Restoration or Balance healer who is not chasing a damage proc.
     228405 Mark of the Trained Soldier. Mastery. Worthless at 29: mastery
        is not a stat a level 29 character has, so the proc does nothing.
        Do not buy it.
     Mark of the Heavy Hide (armor proc, the Guardian choice) and Mark of
     the Ancient Priestess (heal proc): ids unverified.
     All of them: Legion Enchanting, so ALT - a level 98+ enchanter applies
     it, the 29 only wears it.

   Ring - the Binding of the ... family, Legion Enchanting, one ring each.
     190871 Binding of Haste rank 1, spell; 191014 rank 3.
        Item form: Enchant Ring - Binding of Haste 128542.
        The item page caps it at "items above level 60" in the 8.x data and
        at a higher figure in current data; either way a level 29 ring of
        item level 33 or less is inside the cap. The 8.x value is a small
        flat Haste figure, 3 on the page that resolves the placeholder.
        Unverified: whether TrinityCore's 8.3.7 applies the full Legion-era
        value instead of the scaled one.
     The same family covers Critical Strike, Mastery and Versatility rings;
     their spell ids were not opened, so they stay unverified. Mastery is
     dead at 29 for the same reason as above.
     Two rings means two enchants - that is the largest single stat gain the
     audit found, and it was entirely absent from the file.
