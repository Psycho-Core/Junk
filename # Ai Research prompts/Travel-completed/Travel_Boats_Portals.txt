ZEPPELIN TOWERS STANDING IN 8.3.7
  Eight towers at six sites in 8.3.7, from the wiki zeppelin-tower page. A tower is a route end point, not a route.
    two stone towers between Brill and the Undercity, Tirisfal Glades (map 0)
    docking platform on Spirit Rise, Thunder Bluff (map 1)
    zeppelin landing at Grom'gol Base Camp, Stranglethorn Vale (map 0)
    Forsaken tower at Vengeance Landing, Howling Fjord (map 571)
    docking platform inside Warsong Hold, Borean Tundra (map 571)
    two zeppelin towers inside Orgrimmar, Durotar (map 1)
  Two plus one plus one plus one plus one plus two is eight towers at six sites. The page says "seven" because it counts
  each site once - the Brill pair and the Orgrimmar pair are one entry each - and then adds the tower at the Dranosh'ar
  Blockade, which came with Dragonflight and is not in 8.3.7.
  The Brill pair and the Vengeance Landing tower still stand, but the three Undercity routes were removed in 8.0.1 and are
  not in 8.3.7: The Thundercaller (Orgrimmar <-> Undercity), The Purple Princess (Undercity <-> Grom'gol) and The Cloudkisser
  (Undercity <-> Vengeance Landing). This is why three zeppelins sail here and not five. No zeppelin serves the Alliance.

WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Routes and dock pairs: the wiki transportation page's active boat list has eight rows - six named ships, the Kul Tiras ferry
  masters and the Zuldazar wavesingers, printed here as B1 to B8 - plus two Northrend turtle boats on a separate list (B9, B10),
  and three active zeppelins (Z1 to Z3). The research document lists the same eight boat rows but marks B4 The Northspear's
  route unverified; the wiki page supplies it as Menethil Harbor <-> Valgarde, and that is what is printed.
  Zeppelin routes: the wiki's active list has three - The Iron Eagle, The Zephyr and The Mighty Wind - and its defunct list has
  three more removed in 8.0.1: The Thundercaller, The Purple Princess and The Cloudkisser. The research document inferred five
  routes from the seven standing towers; the tower list is still right, but only three of those routes sail in 8.3.7, and this
  file prints the three.
  Dock coordinates: the route blocks print six coordinate triples, and all six come from four of the twelve "Transport"
  boarding nodes - 34, 35, 47 and 175. The eight other nodes in the table are dead routes (46, 54, 88, 206), lifts (218, 219)
  or the two Icecrown gunship platforms (328, 329), none of which belongs to a boat in this file. Every other dock says
  unverified rather than carrying a guessed coordinate, and no coordinate here is a player standing spot, so every
  "wait position" says unverified too.
  Transport and portal gameobject entries: 2 of the portal objects were pinned down (323845 and 284431); every transport object
  and every other portal object is unverified, because the 8.3.7 export has no transport or portal object table and Wowhead
  carries several objects under each name.
  Schedules: the only schedule fact in any source read here is the wiki statement that a transport stays docked for exactly 60
  seconds before departing. Crossing times and the interval between visits are not in the client tables - TaxiPath.Cost is
  copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a clock - and the wiki gives no interval, so both
  are unverified on every route.
  Portal rooms: four main rooms (Wizard's Sanctum, Pathfinder's Den, Sanctum of the Sages, Hall of Ancient Paths) and four minor
  ones, from the wiki portal-room page. The portal lists for the two capital rooms come from their own wiki pages with the patch
  each portal was added in, so the post-8.3.7 portals (Oribos 9.0.2, Bel'ameth 10.2.5, Valdrakken 10.0.2, Dornogal 11.0.2,
  Founder's Point 11.2.7, the new Silvermoon portal 12.0.1) are left out. The Boralus and Dazar'alor rooms have portal lists but
  no coordinates at all, so their positions are unverified.
  Mage spells: the ten teleport/portal pairs, the eight capital and expansion portal spells and the four hearthstones are the
  ids the research document verified against SpellName and Wowhead tooltips. The two spell rows the document marks unverified are
  still unverified here: Shattrath has no portal id, and the Vale of Eternal Blossoms row has no level.
  Sources: warcraft.wiki.gg Transportation, Zeppelin tower, Portal room, Wizard's Sanctum, Pathfinder's Den, Sanctum of the
  Sages, Hall of Ancient Paths, Eastern Earthshrine; Wowhead object pages 323845 and 284431 and the object searches for "Portal
  to Boralus" and "Portal to Zuldazar"; and sections 4 and 5 of BfA_8.3.7_flight_points_and_travel.txt for the transport nodes
  and spell ids. Everything read from a source is printed; nothing was estimated.
