POSITIONS AND NUMBERS - ULDIR
================================================================================
Instance : Uldir (raid, 8 encounters)
Map      : 1861   Area: 9389   Client: 8.3.7 build 35662
Written  : 2026-10-23
Purpose  : the positions, the phase numbers, the ability timers and the
           assignment triggers that the tactics document could not give.
           Companion bots read this to path, to stack and to spread.

================================================================================
0. HOW TO READ THIS FILE
================================================================================
Every line that carries a number carries a tag saying where the number came
from. Nothing is estimated. Where no source I could read resolves a value the
line says "unverified" and section 12 says what would resolve it.

  [SQL]    TrinityCore-format 8.3.x world DB export
           (sql/LatincoreBfa/world/2020_08_13_00_uldir.sql, VerifiedBuild 28153)
           - all creature, gameobject and areatrigger spawn coordinates below
  [DBM]    Deadly Boss Mods, tag 8.3.34, DBM-Uldir modules (Taloc, Mother,
           Zekvhozj, Vectis, Zul, Mythrax, Ghuun, FetidDevourer, UldirTrash)
           - first-cast times, cooldowns, phase-change triggers
  [GUIDE]  MMO-Champion / Icy Veins / Warcraft Wiki (warcraft.wiki.gg) /
           Wowpedia 2018-2020 Uldir write-ups
           - phase health percentages and mechanic geometry in words
  [CALC]   arithmetic done on two [SQL] coordinates in this file
           (distance = 3D euclidean, yards)
  unverified  not in any source I could read

COORDINATE ORDER. Every coordinate in this file is (x, y, z) in yards on map
1861, exactly as stored in the world DB. Orientation is radians, 0 = +x axis.

THE ONE LIMIT THAT MATTERS. In 8.3.7 the Encounter Journal gives no
coordinates. Blizzard never published a safe-spot coordinate for any Uldir
mechanic. What IS resolvable is (a) where the boss and the room landmarks are
spawned, (b) the phase percentages and the timer of every main ability, and
(c) the geometry in words from the guides. So this file gives verified anchors
plus, for each mechanic, the verified anchor you move away from or toward.
Lines that would need an invented number are marked unverified, not guessed.

================================================================================
1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS
================================================================================
1.1 [SQL] is a BfA-era world DB snapshot stamped VerifiedBuild 28153
    (8.2.x), not 35662. Uldir was not re-terraced between 8.2 and 8.3.7 and
    the boss entries match the 8.3.7 keep list (entry ids 137119, 135452,
    133298, 134445, 134442, 138967, 134546, 132998), so the coordinates are
    used as-is. Treat them as correct to the yard, not to the centimetre.

1.2 [DBM] tag 8.3.34 is the last BfA build of the DBM-Uldir module, so its
    timers are the 8.3.x timers, not current-expansion retunings. Timer values
    in DBM are encounter-time seconds measured from pull unless a line says
    otherwise.

1.3 No client DB2 export was readable in this step (no SpellEffect,
    SpellMisc or SpellCooldowns table). Ability ids below are therefore the
    ids DBM registers for 8.3.x plus the ids already in the tactics document;
    radii, cast times and damage numbers are not given because they could not
    be read. Section 12 lists the DB2 tables that would fill them.

4.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Cudgel of Gore soak-aim - assigned to the ACTIVE TANK.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of 271296 (or the boss hitting 100 energy).
     Action: the tank moves so the slam lands on a Blood Storm 270290 pool;
     the pool is cleared within 15 yards of the impact [GUIDE].
     Everyone else: 30 yards from the impact point [GUIDE]. The module
     raises specWarnCudgelofGoreEveryone ("run") for exactly this.
  b) Retrieve Cudgel 271728 - assigned to NOBODY, avoided by everybody.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of 271728. Action: clear the line between the
     boss and the cudgel; anyone on it is charged and knocked back [GUIDE].
  c) Sanguine Static - assigned to the TARGET.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of 271895, then the module scans the boss's
     current target. Action: the target walks to the SIDE of the raid, the
     raid does not stand on the boss-to-target line (that line is the
     knockback lane) [GUIDE].
  d) Plasma Discharge - assigned to the MARKED PLAYERS.
     Trigger: aura 271224 / 271225 applied. Action: walk to the outer edge
     and drop the pools there [GUIDE]; do not overlap the other marked
     players.
  e) Hardened Arteries 275189 / Enlarged Heart 275205 (Mythic) - assigned
     to the MARKED PLAYER and, for Enlarged Heart, to the OFF TANK.
     Trigger: aura applied. Action: marked players move 8+ yards apart (the
     module's CheckNearby(8) threshold); the off tank taunts and moves into
     the marked player to split the hit.
  f) Volatile Droplet fixate 275270 - assigned to the OFF TANK.
     Trigger: aura 275270. Action: the off tank intercepts, the fixated
     player takes the add to the platform edge [GUIDE].

4.6 SAFE SPOTS PER MECHANIC
  Blood Storm 270290 pools: dropped where the tank aims the cudgel. No
      published coordinate; the mechanic is "clear the pool you are standing
      on". unverified as coordinates.
  Plasma Discharge pools: outer edge of whatever platform is current. The
      edge radius is not published -> unverified.
  Uldir Defensive Beam 275445 (stage two): the raid stands BETWEEN two beams
      [GUIDE]. Beam origins/orientation are in the client spell data, which
      was not readable -> unverified as coordinates.
  Intermission stack point: "on the edge of the elevator, stacked on one
      marked player" [GUIDE]; the module has no coordinate for it ->
      unverified. Use the platform's live edge: max radius from the current
      boss position, less 3 yards.

  That is chamber 02's centre line, 18.1 yards east of the Chamber 01 marker

5.3 ROOM
  Three chambers in a row along y = -255.7, x = -135.43 / -81.60 / -27.60,
  separated by two Defense Grids at x = -108.61 and x = -54.67 (3.1).
  Each chamber is 53.9 x 59.5 yards. The grid wall blocks line of sight, so
  healers cannot heal through it [GUIDE].

5.4 PHASES [GUIDE]
  No health percentage gates this fight: MOTHER changes phase when she is
  moved through a Defense Grid into the next chamber. Three chambers, so
  three stages. The chamber she is in builds energy; at 100 energy the
  chamber is purged by Cleansing Purge 268095 and everything inside dies.
  [GUIDE] "each chamber has an energy bar that starts at 0 and ticks up 1
  energy every 2 seconds", i.e. 200 s to purge. [DBM] starts the
  Cleansing Flame timer line at 180 s on non-Mythic and 123 s on Mythic,
  which is the same 200 s bar seen through DBM's safety margin - use the
  180 / 123 values as the practical move-out deadlines.
  Chamber 3 buffs the raid: MOTHER takes 100% increased damage there
  (Depleted Energy 274205 is applied when the raid is cleansed) [GUIDE].

5.5 MAIN ABILITY TIMERS, seconds from pull [DBM]
  Sanitizing Strike 267787        first 5.9, then 23.1
  Purifying Flame 267795           first 10.8, then 20.1
  Wind Tunnel 267878 / 267885      first 20.6, then 39.5
                                   (cast 267878, timer registered on 267945)
  Surgical Beam 269827 (Mythic)    chamber 1: 50 (often delayed)
                                   chamber 2: 30, then 11
                                   chamber 3: 15-29 (top/side split)
  Cleansing Purge 268095           chamber timer 180 s normal/heroic,
                                   123 s Mythic

5.6 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Defense Grid crossing - assigned in GROUPS OF 3-5 (4-5 Normal, 3-4
     Heroic, 2-3 Mythic) [GUIDE].
     Trigger: the grid debuff from the previous crossing has fallen off
     (6 s) [GUIDE]. Action: that group walks through the wall; each player
     through the wall spawns one Remnant of Corruption 136315 in the new
     chamber.
  b) Remnant of Corruption 136315 - assigned to an INTERRUPT ROTATION.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of Clinging Corruption 268198. Pull it away
     from the raid, kick it. Un-interrupted it applies the debuff to
     everyone.
  c) Wind Tunnel 267878 / 267885 - assigned to NOBODY, movement only.
     Trigger: aura applied (SPELL_AURA_APPLIED 267878 / 267885). Action:
     the player is pushed across the chamber; the bot must path out of the
     areatrigger (77.5 x 50 yards centred on (-81.61, -255.66) when the
     tunnel is in chamber 02).
  d) Surgical Beam 269827 - assigned to NOBODY, dodged by everybody.
     Trigger: beam cast start. Action: move into the open gap in the wall of
     beams. Beam geometry is not in any readable source -> unverified.
  e) Sanitizing Strike 267787 - assigned to the TANK.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START. Action: sidestep so the frontal does not
     point at the raid.

5.7 SAFE SPOTS
  Chamber centres (verified, 3.1): (-135.43,-255.88), (-81.60,-255.65),
      (-27.60,-255.88), z 695.37. A stacked raid belongs on a centre line,
      not on a wall, because the flame pads (136325, z 695.30) sit on both
      walls at x = -135.6/-81.6/-27.5, y = -285.40 and -225.92.
  Purifying Flame drop zone: the flame pads are the spawn anchors; the
      "stand between them" spot is the chamber centre above -> verified
      anchor exists, exact safe radius unverified.
  Wind tunnel: exit the trigger box. The box is 77.5 x 50 yards, so from the
      chamber centre the safe distance is at least half the box on both axes
      (38.75 and 25 yards) - [CALC], not published.
  Beam gaps: unverified (see 12).

12. WHAT IS STILL UNVERIFIED, AND WHAT WOULD RESOLVE IT
================================================================================
12.1 Taloc's world position. Resolve with the instance script
     (instance_uldir.cpp, SpellScript for the elevator transport) or by
     reading the live boss position in game. The world DB row is local
     space and cannot be used.

12.2 Every "safe spot" that is defined by a random angle: Surging Darkness
     gaps, Uldir Defensive Beam gaps, Blood Geyser lanes, Liquefy puddles,
     Malignant Growth slices. These are random per cast by design. What a
     bot needs is the radius and the cast window, and both live in
     SpellEffect / SpellMisc:
        radius            SpellEffect.EffectRadiusIndex -> SpellRadius
        cast time         SpellMisc.CastingTimeIndex
        cooldown          SpellCooldowns
        aura duration     SpellMisc.DurationIndex
     None of those tables were readable in this step. If you want them, a
     DB2 dump for build 35662 of SpellRadius, SpellEffect, SpellMisc and
     SpellCooldowns fills every "unverified" in sections 4-11 that is a
     number rather than a coordinate.

12.3 Ability radii quoted in a few guide lines (Cudgel clearing 15 yards,
     Imminent Ruin 12 yards, Plague Bomb 8 yards, Malignant Growth 8 yards)
     come from guide text, not from client data. They are marked as guide
     values where used and must be re-checked against SpellRadius before
     the bots trust them.

12.4 Berserk timers. None of the eight DBM modules keeps an active berserk
     timer in 8.3.34 (all commented out). Unverified for every boss.

12.5 Trash pull positions. Uldir is a raid, so this step did not deliver
     "every pull position with z" - that clause of the task applies to
     dungeons. The trash creatures with verified spawn coordinates are
     listed under each boss (3.x) and are enough to place a pull, but there
     is no published pull-by-pull route for the raid.
