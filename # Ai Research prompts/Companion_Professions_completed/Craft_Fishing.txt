BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - FISHING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
============================================================================
Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client, no human at the keyboard)
levels Fishing on a TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Short answer: it can - Fishing
exists on 8.3.7 (unlike First Aid), every step is server-resolved, and since
patch 8.0.1 the profession is nine independent regional lines with their own
caps. The limits are client-side splash timing (section 8) and world-DB loot
tables that are not in the code repo. Everything below was read from a source
fetched this week (2026-10-27); anything without a readable source says
"unverified" instead of being guessed. Printed counts are exactly what the
linked guides print; the hook-count model in section 3 is arithmetic over
TrinityCore's own formula, which the code itself labels "guessed".

1. THE SKILL LINES AND HOW A COMPANION LEARNS THEM
--------------------------------------------------

Miscellaneous/SharedDefines.h line 5939). The region sub-skills
(SharedDefines.h lines 6155-6162): KUL_TIRAN_FISHING 2585, LEGION 2586,
DRAENOR 2587, PANDARIA 2588, CATACLYSM 2589, NORTHREND 2590, OUTLAND 2591,

regional fishing trainer, which varies by faction and location." Patch 8.0.1

profession skill bar, with smaller caps for each expansion, instead of one

3. CHEAPEST ROUTE 1 TO THE CAP OF EACH LINE
-------------------------------------------
The rate mechanics first, because they make the route short:
- Skill gains only in the matching region: "Skill is increased by fishing in
  that region" (wiki), and wow-professions' Legion guide states it flatly:
  "You can only level your Legion fishing at Broken Isle zones, you won't gain
  Legion Fishing skill if you fish elsewhere." TrinityCore implements this as:
  the hooked bobber reads the area's ContentTuning expansion
  (GameObject.cpp line 2993 UpdateFishingSkill(areaContentTuning->ExpansionID))
  and the area's difficulty from skill_fishing_base_level (line 2996).
- The rate is hook-count-driven, identical in every zone of that region, and
  identical for fish and junk: UpdateFishingSkill runs BEFORE the loot roll
  (GameObject.cpp line 2993, chance computed afterwards at 3001-3009), and
  when its internal step counter fills, UpdateSkillPro is called with chance
  100*10 = always-take (Player.cpp lines 5519-5524). Consequence: choosing a
  zone changes WHAT you catch, never how fast the bar moves; and low-skill
  junk catches still advance the bar (the wiki's 3.1.0 row: "Every catch has
  the potential for fishing skill gains, but you are likely to catch worthless
  junk in areas that are too difficult for your skill").
- Hook-count model straight from GetFishingStepsNeededToLevelUp (Player.cpp
  5491-5501: <75 = 1 step, <=300 = skill/44, else skill/31 - the code comment
  says "These formulas are guessed to be as close as possible to how the skill
  difficulty curve for fishing was on Retail"). Summing that formula from
  skill 1 gives TC-model hooks per cap: 1->75 = 74 hooks; 1->100 = 111 hooks;
  1->175 = 304 hooks; 1->300 = 919 hooks. These are TC-model numbers only,
  retail-actual counts are unverified (section 9 item 6).
- Printed counts that exist in readable guides: wow-professions Legion guide
  - "If you start from Legion Fishing skill 1, you need to catch around 180
  Fish to reach 100, which takes around 45 minutes" and, with auction baits,
  "you only have to do around 5 minutes of Fishing"; its school-fishing loop -
  "You can usually get 17-22 [Oodelfjisk] in 5 minutes, which means you will
  probably reach Legion Fishing 100 by using only one Ancient Vrykul Ring".
  No other region's guide prints counts in any page read this week - all other
  per-region counts: unverified.
- Historic table the wiki itself prints (labeled "Before the vast changes to
  professions... El even calculated", i.e. the pre-rework single-bar system):
  1-100 = 100 fish / 34 min at 3 fish/min, 100-200 = 160 / 54, 200-300 = 190 /
  64, 300-450 = 540 / 180, 450-600 = 825 / 260, totals ~1800 fish / ~600 min.
  Not a 8.3.7 regional cap guide - kept as history only.
The route itself (cheapest = no auction house needed):
  1) Learn Classic Fishing (271616) at any trainer - it is the only main with
     no "Requires Fishing (1)" line, so it is the entry point; equip or don't
     (section 9 item 4), cast, hook until 300 in Eastern Kingdoms/Kalimdor
     water (wiki caps row "World of Warcraft 1-300").
  2) For each further region: pay 10 at any trainer in section 2 for that
     line, then fish that expansion's zones to its cap (75/75/75/75/100/100/
     175 from the table in section 1). Order is free - the lines are
     independent; a level-1 character can train them all.
  3) Loot quality while below an area's skill level follows TC's chance =
     (player/area)^2 * 100 (minimum 1 percent) at GameObject.cpp 3001-3009;
     vendor lures (section 4) raise the effective skill and therefore catch
     quality, they do not speed up skill gain (the step counter is
     independent of skill value).
  4) Legion shortcut as printed by wow-professions: train at Marcia Chase in
     Dalaran, fish Azsuna or Stormheim, use Rusty Queenfish Brooch /
     Ancient Vrykul Ring (5-minute school windows, ~17-22 catches each) or buy
     them on the auction house.
  5) Pools are friendlier than open water by loot, not by rate: "Fishing pools
     have no skill requirement as of patch 3.3.0 (undocumented change)" -
     TrinityCore's own comment at GameObject.cpp line 3016 - and a pool pass
     skips the chance roll entirely (line 3017 condition). Wiki extras read:
     schools hold about five fish; Find Fish comes from the Weather-Beaten
     Journal; a nocturnal window 18:00-00:00 is printed for some catches.
  6) Expect junk early in each new region - the wiki's skills section says
     "early attempts often yielding junk items until skill improves", and TC
     answers failed rolls with the junk table (section 7e).

8. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
----------------------------------------------------
- See or hear the splash. The wiki tips (read this step): "As of patch 2.2,
  each cast of fishing will result in a bobber splash; the splash can occur
  when there are approximately 17, 13, 7, or 3 seconds left of the Fishing
  channeled spell", plus its gallery caption "Wait patiently for up to 19
  seconds before clicking the bobber". TC server side randomizes its own
  window (3/7/13 s lead, 5 s ready time - section 7b). A headless companion
  must synthesize the GameObject Use call inside that window; there is no
  client UI to watch, and the exact 8.3.7 live window versus TC's
  approximation is unverified (section 9 item 7).
- Aim the cast. The wiki's school technique is pure client play: "always cast
  into the circle of the school or at its edge... re-cast your line at any
  time... about 90% of the casts when the bobber lands in or at the edge of
  the school" - the server only checks whether a pool GO exists within
  20yd + CONTACT (GameObject.cpp 3013). Pool positions live on the map/minimap
  client-side; the companion can only iterate coordinates it already knows.
- Client conveniences the wiki lists: right-click/auto-loot behavior, first-
  person camera to keep the bobber in view, gear-manager/macro weapon swaps
  while fishing, sound cues. None exist without a client; the companion's
  equivalent is scripted packets only.
- World-DB contents: which fish/items each area's LootTemplates_Fishing row
  can drop, where lures/poles are sold, vendor prices beyond the printed ones
  in section 4, and the numeric skill_fishing_base_level per area - none of
  these tables ship in the code repo, so their 8.3.7 values are unverified
  here.
- Event and reputation content behind materials: Stranglethorn Fishing
  Extravaganza and the Darkmoon Faire schedules (hats, Arcanite pole,
  doughnut), daily-quest lures (Glass Bobber/Sharpened Hook/Glow Worm),
  Legion token vendors, Anglers reputation (Sharpened Tuskarr Spear), Nat
  Pagle exalted gear. A server-side character can hold the items and use
  them, but ACQUIRING them depends on event schedules, dailies and faction
  states that are account/character data, not fishing mechanics.
- Time-gated loops are borderline: Legion's 5-minute school buffs and the
  wiki's 18:00-00:00 nocturnal window are computable from the in-game clock,
  but school respawn positions and the 17-22 catches per window figures are
  guide observations (printed values kept as printed).
