TWINK29 - HUNTER - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

1. HOW MUCH OF THE TREE IS OPEN AT 29
   Battle for Azeroth talent rows unlock at levels 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90
   and 100, one row at a time. A level 29 hunter has exactly one row open -
   the level 15 row - and one choice to make per character.
   Everything from row 2 up is ALT: it needs level 30 or higher.
   This is the usual BfA rule and the 8.3.7 client's own row gates were not
   opened, so treat the exact numbers as unverified. The pattern matches the
   druid file, which is the one checked against a page.
   Talent ids were not read for any column. Every id below says unverified
   rather than a guess, because no reachable source carries BfA talent ids.

2. THE THREE SPECIALISATIONS ARE ALL OPEN
   A hunter picks a specialisation at level 10, so Beast Mastery,
   Marksmanship and Survival are all legal at 29. The choice decides the
   level 15 row, the PvP row, and the whole weapon answer:
     Beast Mastery  ranged, pet does part of the damage
     Marksmanship  ranged, the pet is optional and the spec works without it
     Survival      melee, two-hand weapon, no ranged slot
   The spec cannot be changed inside a battleground, so this is a build
   decision made once per bot.

3. ROW 1 - LEVEL 15 - ONE CHOICE PER SPEC
   BEAST MASTERY
     col 1  Killer Instinct   - Kill Command damage, cooldown reset on kill.
     col 2  Animal Companion  - a second pet fights beside the first.
     col 3  Dire Beast        - summons a beast for a short window.
     The 9.0.2 tree lists these same three, and the 9.0.2 notes only say
     that Dire Beast no longer costs focus, which points to the row being
     unchanged from BfA. Animal Companion is the strongest of the three for
     a bot: passive extra damage that cannot be mis-timed. Killer Instinct
     is the burst pick. Ids unverified.
   MARKSMANSHIP
     col 1  Master Marksman  - critical shots leave a bleed.
     col 2  Serpent Sting     - a poison damage-over-time shot.
     col 3  Careful Aim       - bonus damage against high-health targets.
     This row is inferred, not read: in 9.0.2 Careful Aim sits in the level
     25 row, so it was the level 15 row in 8.3.7 that moved. Two of the
     three are therefore solid and the third is the one that changed.
     For a twink, Serpent Sting is the pick a bot uses well - it is a
     fire-and-forget damage source that survives being kited. Ids
     unverified.
   SURVIVAL
     col 1  Viper's Venom        - poison damage on the melee rotation.
     col 2  Terms of Engagement  - mobility and a damage rider.
     col 3  unverified           - the BfA third column was Guerrilla
            Tactics as far as any list shows; 9.0.2 has Alpha Predator in
            that slot and Guerrilla Tactics one row down, so the swap may
            have happened in Shadowlands, not in BfA.
     Both named columns are old BfA talents and either is usable. Viper's
     Venom is the damage pick, Terms of Engagement the pick that survives
     being kited. Ids unverified.

4. ROWS THAT ARE NOT OPEN AT 29 - ALL ALT
   Beast Mastery  row 2 to 7: level 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 100. The pet and
                  Kill Command rows, all of the passive pet damage.
   Marksmanship   row 2 to 7: the same levels. Includes the aimed-shot and
                  Trueshot support rows.
   Survival       row 2 to 7: the same levels. Includes the trap and
                  Mongoose Bite rows.
   No column of any of these rows can be taken by a 29, so the file does
   not list them: a bot that cannot spend the point does not need the name.
   The row levels themselves are the unverified item in this section.

5. PVP TALENTS AT 29
   The PvP panel is not the specialisation tree. It has its own slots and
   its own unlocks, and taking a level 15 talent neither spends nor blocks
   one of them.
   How many slots a 29 has: one. warcraft.wiki.gg, PvP talent, on the
   system this build uses: the slots sat "in the lower right corner of the
   Talents pane", and before Shadowlands "there were four slots in total.
   The first offered a choice of PvP trinket-type talents". Standard
   classes unlocked them at levels 20, 40, 70 and 110. So a 29 has the
   level 20 slot - the trinket - and the other three are ALT.
   What that means for a hunter bot: the one PvP slot is spent on the
   crowd-control breaker, not on a hunter ability. Hunter PvP talents
   (Roar of Sacrifice, Master's Call, Scatter Shot, Survival Tactics,
   Viper Sting, Hunting Pack - names only, ids and which spec each belongs
   to unverified) are ALT at this bracket because no second slot exists.
   Where it applies: always on in battlegrounds and arenas, always off in
   dungeons and raids, and in the open world only with War Mode on. It can
   be changed at the start of a match or in a rested area.

6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT COVER
   Talent ids: unverified, all of them.
   The row gates 30 to 100: the standard BfA rule, not read off a client.
   The Survival third column: named as the likely BfA talent, not as fact.
   Spell ids for the abilities these talents modify belong to step 3.
   Anyone building the bots should open the talent pane once at 29 and
   correct this file from what the client shows. The two facts worth
   carrying into the code are that there is one row and one PvP slot, and
   that the PvP slot holds the trinket.
