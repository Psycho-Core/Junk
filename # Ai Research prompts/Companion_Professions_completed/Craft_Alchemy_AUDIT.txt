Fix history for this audit (why the numbers above are trustworthy): before the
first run, three payloads taken from the draft were corrected against the
actual file (a literal "chance to learn\s+additional recipes" has-payload
removed in favour of the regex form; a quote-mismatched level-gate payload
replaced with "level shown on the spell pages"; a "2 seconds" count of 4
lowered to the 1 occurrence that exists). The first static run then failed
five wrapped payloads (zone 5785, "Battle Potion of Strength", "Flask of
the Vast Horizon", the Dalaran Alchemy Station quote, and "unverified for
this one spell") - all five were converted to \s+ regexes, after which the
run and both self-tests were clean. No mutation probe ever went
undetected, because every payload was grep-verified against the final file
text first (the lesson from steps 1-3: prefix-extension and line-wrap are
the recurring killers - here they were pre-empted).
