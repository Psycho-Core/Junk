TWINK29 - DRUID - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

1. HOW MUCH OF THE TALENT TREE IS OPEN AT 29
   Battle for Azeroth talent rows unlock at levels 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90
   and 100, one row at a time. A level 29 character therefore has exactly
   one talent row open - the level 15 row - and one choice to make.
   Everything from row 2 up is ALT: it needs level 30 or higher.
   This is the usual BfA rule; the 8.3.7 client's own row gates were not
   opened for this file, so treat the exact numbers as unverified.
   Talent ids were not read either - every id column below says unverified
   rather than a guess. The names and the row are from the archived druid
   talent table.

2. THE FOUR SPECIALISATIONS ARE ALL OPEN
   A Druid picks a specialisation at level 10, so all four are available
   to a level 29 twink. Which one is picked decides the level 15 row, the
   PvP row, and every ability below level 29 that a bot can use.
   Balance, Feral, Guardian, Restoration - all four legal at 29.
   Once picked, the spec cannot be changed inside a battleground without
   a trip to a class trainer, so the choice is a build decision, not a
   per-fight switch.

3. ROW 1 - LEVEL 15 - ONE CHOICE PER SPEC
   BALANCE
     col 1  Force of Nature        - 3 treants, 10 sec, 1 min cd. unv. id
     col 2  Warrior of Elune       - next 3 Starfires instant.  unv. id
     col 3  Starlord               - Astral Power generation.    unv. id
     Usual twink pick: Warrior of Elune. A 29 Balance druid lives on
     burst windows, and three instant Starfires is the only column that
     changes a fight inside a battleground minute. Depends on Starfire
     being known at 29 - unverified.
   FERAL
     col 1  Predator               - reset Tiger's Fury on kill.  unv. id
     col 2  Blood Scent            - faster stealth, heal on kill. unv. id
     col 3  Lunar Inspiration      - Moonfire from cat form.      unv. id
     Usual twink pick: Lunar Inspiration. It gives a cat a ranged
     finisher and a second damage school, which is the only one of the
     three that adds something a 29 does not otherwise have.
   GUARDIAN
     col 1  Brambles               - damage back while barkskin-ed. unv.
     col 2  Bristling Fur          - rage on being hit, short cd.   unv.
     col 3  Blood Frenzy           - Mangle bleed on kill.          unv.
     Usual twink pick: Brambles. A flag-carrying bear is hit constantly,
     so a reflect-style column does more work than a rage trick.
   RESTORATION

     col 2  Cenarion Ward          - absorbs the next hit, 30 sec cd. unv.

4. ROWS THAT ARE NOT OPEN AT 29 - ALL ALT
   Balance    row 2 to 7: level 30, 45, 60, 75, 90. Includes the eclipse
              and Starfall support talents.
   Feral      row 2 to 7: level 30 upward. Includes the combo-point and
              Berserk support talents.
   Guardian   row 2 to 7: level 30 upward. Includes the active-mitigation
              talents a tank normally builds around.
   Restoration row 2 to 7: level 30 upward. Includes the throughput rows.
   No column of any of these rows can be taken by a 29, so the file does
   not list them: a bot that cannot spend the point does not need the
   name.

5. PVP TALENTS AT 29
   Correction to the assumption in the brief: the PvP talent row is not
   shared with the normal talent row. They are two separate panels - the
   specialisation tree takes one point at 15, 30 and so on, and the PvP
   panel has its own slots and its own unlocks. Taking a level 15 talent
   does not cost a PvP slot and does not block one.
   How many slots a 29 has: one. warcraft.wiki.gg, PvP talent, on the
   pre-Shadowlands system this build uses: "Standard classes unlocked
   their slots at levels 20, 40, 70, and 110", and "prior to
   Shadowlands, there were four slots in total", the first being a choice
   of PvP trinket talents. So a 29 has the slot that opened at 20 - the
   trinket - and nothing else. The other three are level 40, 70 and 110
   and are ALT at this bracket.
   Where it lives: the lower right corner of the Talents pane, not inside
   the specialisation tree, which is why taking a level 15 talent neither
   spends nor blocks a PvP slot.
   When it applies: always on in battlegrounds and arenas, always off in
   dungeons and raids, and in the open world only with War Mode on. It can
   be changed at the start of a match or in a rested area.
   The Druid PvP talents that exist for these four specs (names and ids
   both unverified here): Thorns, Deep Roots, Owlkin Frenzy, Tiger's
   Lust, Freedom of the Herd, Dazed and Confused, Twin Moons, Cyclone.
   Usual twink pick, by what a 29 fight looks like rather than by table:
   a defensive or a mobility row before a damage row, because at 29 the
   character dies to being caught, not to being out-damaged. Thorns on a
   Guardian or a healer-facing Restoration build, a mobility row on a
   flag-running Feral.

6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT COVER
   Talent ids: unverified, all of them. Spell ids for the abilities the
   talents modify: not read, and they belong to step 3.
   The row gates 30 to 100: standard BfA rule, not read from the 8.3.7
   client.
   Anyone building the bots should read the level 15 row and the PvP
   panel once in game and correct this file from what the client shows.
