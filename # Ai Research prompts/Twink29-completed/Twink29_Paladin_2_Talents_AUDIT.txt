      third class in the set where the answer is "one talent", and by now
      the pattern is solid: every class uses the same row levels in BfA.
   3  PASS  Row 2's contents per spec, from a BfA-era tier table: Protection
      gets First Avenger / Crusader's Judgment / Bastion of Light,
      Retribution gets Fires of Justice / Blade of Wrath / Hammer of Wrath,
      Holy gets Cavalier / Unbreakable Spirit / Blessing of Spellwarding.
      The table is a guide, not a database, and the file only uses it to say
      what a 29 cannot have.

2. THE THREE ROW-1 LISTS
   4  PASS  Holy: Crusader's Might 196926, Bestow Faith 223306, Light's
      Hammer. The two ids are verified and each one's page says the talent
      was level 15 in this era. Bestow Faith's "did not generate Holy Power
      until 9.0.1" is right, and it is the sort of detail that decides
      whether a bot healer can use the talent at all.
   5  CORRECTED  "Crusader Strike reduces the cooldown of Holy Shock and
      Judgment." On 8.3.7 it reduces Holy Shock only. The spell's own patch
      notes add the Judgment half on 11.0.0, and 9.0.1's nerf to one second
      sits between them. The file now says 1.5 sec on Holy Shock and names
      the patch that added the rest. This is exactly the class of error the
      wiki's current text invites - the live tooltip describes a 2024 spell,
      not an 8.3.7 one.
   6  CORRECTED  Zeal: the 8.0.1 note says "next 2 auto attacks", later BfA
      and Shadowlands descriptions say 3. The file now says both and marks
      the 8.3.7 number unverified instead of picking one silently.
   7  UNVERIFIED, AND NOW LABELLED  Light's Hammer as BfA's third Holy
      option is an inference from a Legion row table plus a partial BfA
      table. The file now says so in the row itself.
   8  PASS  Protection: Holy Shield, Redoubt, Blessed Hammer, all three
      confirmed as a row by the BfA tier table, with Holy Shield's own note
      ("7.0.3 now available at level 15") as the check on the row's level.
   9  UNVERIFIED  The ids quoted for Holy Shield (152261) and Blessed Hammer

   10 PASS  Retribution: Zeal 269569 verified, Righteous Verdict given no

3. PVP TALENTS
   11 PASS  Steed of Glory 199542, Protection, level 20. The page's

   12 PASS  Blessing of Sanctuary 210256, Retribution, level 20, 45 sec

   13 PASS  Cleanse the Weak is a level 60 talent on 8.3.7. The page's own

   14 PASS  Denounce does not exist on 8.3.7: removed in 7.0.3, re-added in

   15 UNVERIFIED  That the Holy paladin has "the slot and nothing legal to
