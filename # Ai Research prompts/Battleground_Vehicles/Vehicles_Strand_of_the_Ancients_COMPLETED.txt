=== VEHICLES - STRAND OF THE ANCIENTS - BFA 8.3.7 (TRINITYCORE) - COMPLETED ===

Prompt 2 of the Battleground_Vehicles set (Strand of the Ancients, map 607).
All coordinates are map id, x, y, z (map 607 unless noted). Facing is
"o <value>". Every entry, coordinate, spell id and number below is taken
from a cited source; anything without a readable source is marked
unverified. Plain text, no code.

1. MATCH CONTEXT
   - 15 v 15, two rounds. Round length 600 s (BG_SA_ROUNDLENGTH =
     60 * IN_MILLISECONDS, LC BattlegroundStrandOfTheAncients.h).
     Round 2 is given the round-1 clear time (capped at 600 s); if the
     second round runs past its budget both scores sit at 600 ->
     EndBattleground(0) tie (cpp PostUpdate/EventPlayerClickedOnFlag
     logic). Neither side reaching the relic = tie (wiki).
   - Warmup 120 s (BG_SA_WARMUPLENGTH = 120 * 1000); the attackers'
     boats start moving at 60 s (BG_SA_BOAT_START = 60 * 1000);
     second-round warmup 60 s (Status BG_SA_SECOND_WARMUP, TotalTime
     >= 60000) with broadcast texts 29448 (one minute) and 29449
     (half minute). Round start announced by NPC Kanrethad (29).
   - Attacking side is re-rolled at every Reset (Attackers =
     urand(0,1) ? ALLIANCE : HORDE) = the patch 3.2.0 coin toss
     (wiki); before 3.2.0 Alliance always attacked (wiki).
   - Attacker start: two ships off the north end; on-deck start
     positions (607, 2682.936, -830.368, 30.0) o 2.895 and
     (607, 2577.003, 980.261, 30.0) o 0.807 (GetTeamStartPosition
     fallbacks while the ship is not parented); after the ships have
     started, attackers spawn at the landing beach
     (607, 1600.381, -106.263, 8.8745) o 3.78.
   - Defender start (round start and pre-round free movement):
     (607, 1209.7, -65.16, 70.1) o 0.0 (GetTeamStartPosition).
   - Goal: attackers click the Titan Relic gameobject 192834 at
     (607, 836.5, -108.8, 120.219) o 0.0 (BG_SA_ObjSpawnlocs).
     EventPlayerUsedGO only accepts the click when ALL of these are
     DESTROYED: Ancient Gate (192549), Yellow Moon (190727), Red Sun
     OR Purple Amethyst (190726/190723), Green Emerald OR Blue
     Sapphire (190722/190724), and the clicker is on the attacking
     team. The relic becomes selectable only when the Ancient Gate is
     destroyed (DestroyGate removes GO_FLAG_NOT_SELECTABLE from it).
     Clicking ends the round (or the match in round 2) instantly
     (wiki: "Clicking the orb at the end will instantly win the
     round"; cpp round-score logic).
   - Sources: TrinityCore-derived Titans-Project/LegionCore-Reforged
     (LC) BattlegroundStrandOfTheAncients.h/.cpp, GameObject.cpp /
     GameObjectData.h, scripts/Spells/spell_generic.cpp,
     scripts/World/go_scripts.cpp; AzerothCore (AC) db_world base SQL
     rows (VerifiedBuild 12340 = WotLK-era content, same caveat as
     step 1) and AC BattlegroundSA.cpp / GameObject code;
     warcraft.wiki.gg "Strand of the Ancients"; wowhead spell pages
     for 52338, 60206, 49872, 52408, 52410, 52415; wowpedia mirror;
     player guides (wow-pro, mmorpg.com, almarsguides) for tactics.
   - Retail note: the battleground was removed in patch 8.0.1 (wiki);
     the template rows quoted here are the WotLK-era rows a 8.3.7
     server inherits.

       Strategies: "The gates can only be brought down through

4. SEQUENCE A GOOD TEAM FOLLOWS
   (Code gates the order; the tactical wording comes from the wiki
   and the cited guides. The same map plays out twice with sides
   swapped - ResetObjs rebuilds gates, piles, ships, factions.)
   4a. Attackers - phase 0 (warmup, 0-120 s; ships sail from 60 s):
       board at the two north ship spawns (3d), beach demos are
       invisible until the round whistle (3a); pre-round the wiki
       says defenders may already be in position anywhere.
   4b. Attackers - phase 1, outer wall (Blue 190724 south and Green
       190722 north): land at (607, 1600.381, -106.263, 8.8745);
       grab a charge from pile A (north dock) or pile B (south dock);
       drive the two beach demos - from (607, 1611.597656,
       -117.270073, 8.719355) toward Blue gate front
       (607, 1431.3413, -219.437, 30.893), and from (607, 1618.047729,
       61.424641, 7.248210) toward Green gate front (607, 1411.57,
       108.163, 28.692). Fire-from: Hurl Boulder has unlimited range
       but slow travel - wiki/guides say drive up and release near
       the gate, keep moving while defenders aim (projectile travel
       1-2 s, wiki); Ram works from 15 yd in front. Plant 1-2
       seaforium at the gate base (2 s plant, retreat or bodyguard
       for the 10 s fuse - guides). First of each gate pair pays the
       bonus honor (2d).
   4c. Attackers - workshop unlock: with Blue OR Green down, channel
       West flag (607, 1309.192017, 9.416233, 30.893402) and/or East
       flag (607, 1338.859253, -153.327316, 30.895077) - each flag
       immediately spawns its workshop demolisher (rows in 3a), its
       salesman (3e), and opens pile C/D or E/F seaforium (3c).
       Wiki tactics: capture BOTH first-2 graveyards ASAP for tank
       supply; optional South yard later (wiki notes holding it
       back can be smarter early because it moves your respawn
       away from the tanks - contest of opinions, unverified as
       canonical).
   4d. Attackers - phase 2, inner wall (Red 190726 south, Purple
       190723 north): drive the four demolishers to Red front
       (607, 1227.667, -212.555, 55.372) and Purple front (607,
       1214.681, 81.21, 53.413) (guides: all tanks on one gate also
       works - which gate order is "best" unverified); fire-from
       range as in 4b; charges from piles C/D/E/F. Defenders cover
       with the red/purple turret pairs (3b fire-from rows).
   4e. Attackers - phase 3, south yard: outer pair AND inner pair
       down unlocks the South/Central flag (607, 1215.108032,
       -65.715767, 70.084267); taking it pushes defender respawns
       back to their last-resort yard (wiki: "captures the last
       graveyard will push the defensive side back to spawn in
       their own courtyard") and opens pile G seaforium (z 68-73
       condition also satisfied by red/purple falling).
   4f. Attackers - phase 4, Yellow Moon 190727 (front (607, 1055.452,
       -108.1, 82.134), HP 14000 - the highest of the six): it is
       attacked with demolisher fire and with charges carried from
       pile G (pile H does not open until Yellow itself is down).
       Defenders get the yellow turret pair (3b).
   4f2. Attackers - chamber approach, Chamber of Ancient Relics

5. WHAT ONE CHARACTER CAN DO ALONE / WHAT NEEDS TWO OR MORE
   Solo-capable (mechanic needs only 1 player):
   - Drive a demolisher: driver alone has both weapons (wiki
     Demolisher page); passengers optional extras.
   - Man an Antipersonnel Cannon alone (single operator per wiki
     turret section; seat count unverified).
   - Pick up, carry and plant a seaforium charge alone (pickup 5 yd
     interact, plant is a 2 s self-cast, wiki) and sneak it in
     mounted/stealthed (wiki) - guarding it is a different matter.
   - Disarm a planted charge alone (2 s channel, wiki - interruptible,
     so "can be done" not "always succeeds").
   - Capture a graveyard flag alone (one AB-style channel, wiki;
     interruptible).
   - Click the relic alone (ends the round for everyone, section 1).
   - Hold a gate vs. attackers alone only via the paired turrets
     (one turret per player); wiki notes a single turret operator
     can defend a GY when the wall turret has line onto it.
   Needs two or more (mechanic or hard tactics):
   - Keep a planted charge alive: the plant takes 10 s while any
     defender can start a disarm channel (wiki) - someone must hold
     the spot off the disarmer; whether one attacker can always do
     that is tactics, unverified.
   - Break a defended gate: guides require bodyguarding the
     demolisher against footmen (one player cannot both drive and
     clear attackers) and syncing several tanks (mmorpg/wow-pro) -
     tactic, but treated as required by every readable guide.
   - Escort multi-role push: driver + passengers for CC (guides
     list passenger crowd control as the way to keep tanks alive);
     passenger count itself unverified (3a).
   - Simultaneous two-gate play (wiki Defensive: watch both Red and
     Purple) splits turret crews - each turret is 1 player, two
     active gates need 2+ turret players plus beach defenders.
   - The "right" split for bots beyond the above: unverified (no
     canonical strategy source readable; guides disagree on South
     yard timing and one-gate vs two-gate pushes).
