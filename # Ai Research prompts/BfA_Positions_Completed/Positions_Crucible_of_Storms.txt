COORDINATE ORDER. (x, y, z) in yards on map 2096, exactly as written in the

THE BIG DIFFERENCE FROM DAZAR'ALOR. Here the boss scripts DO carry hard
coordinates, and they are consistent across three separate repositories: two
arena centres, six relic positions, the four Piercing Gaze emitters and the
lines they sweep, the add spawn points and the boss's stage-transition walk.
What the scripts do NOT carry is the bosses' own spawn rows - those live in
the world DB, which is not reachable - so both boss spawns are unverified and
the arena centres are used as the anchor instead.

MAP ID 2096 is confirmed three times over: every copy of
instance_crucible_of_storms.cpp registers

1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS
================================================================================
1.1 The instance script itself is a stub: it registers the map, then does
    nothing ("later to do, PH atm"). So it provides the map id and no
    positions.
1.2 The two boss scripts are not stubs. They implement the relic system, the
    stage transitions, the gaze beams and the add waves, and they hard-code
    the coordinates in section 3.
1.3 One add-spawn line in the Uu'nat script is malformed in all three copies:
    "{ 3507.61f, -1067.70f - 382.98f }" has a minus where the second comma
    belongs. The intended z is almost certainly -382.98, but that is an
    inference, so the row is marked unverified.
1.4 One Piercing Gaze emitter is malformed in the same way: its y is -124.17
    where every other value in the room is around -1100. The other three
    emitters and four sweep endpoints are self-consistent, so the bad one is
    reported as-is and flagged.
1.5 DBM 8.3.34 is the last BfA build of these modules, so the timers are the
    8.3.x timers. Where DBM's spell id is not in the script's spell enum, the
    line says so.

3.3 THE ARENA EDGE [SRV], and this one matters for every safe spot
    The script enforces the platform boundary with the Gift of N'Zoth:
    Obscurity damage check, and the distance table is:
        Obscurity stack 1 -> players beyond 50.0 yards from the centre take
        Obscurity stack 2 -> 40.0 yards
        Obscurity stack 3 -> 30.0 yards
        Obscurity stack 4 -> 20.0 yards
    CAVEAT: the script writes that table as an if / else-if chain that tests
    ">= 1" first, which makes every later branch unreachable. So as shipped
    the enforced rim is 50 yards and the shrink never happens. The intended
    shrink is 50 -> 40 -> 30 -> 20. Treat 50 as the enforced boundary and the
    shrink as unverified.

4. BOSS 1 - THE RESTLESS CABAL
================================================================================
4.1 IDENTITY [TAC]/[DBM]/[SRV]
  journal 2328 | DungeonEncounter 2269 | creatures 144755 Zaxasj the Speaker
  and 144754 Fa'thuul the Feared. The two bosses do NOT share health, which is
  what makes the 75/50/25 relic schedule a per-boss schedule.

4.2 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION
  Boss spawn: unverified (world DB, no export reachable).
  Anchor: the platform is built around (3631.75, -1729.25, 85.65), and the
  three relic discs are 32.9-36.1 yards from that centre.
  TANK POSITION [GUIDE]: Fa'thuul is tanked by two real tanks and Zaxasj by
  one ranged player, because Aphotic Blast 283524 applies 150% increased
  threat to whoever it hits (worldofmoudi, MMO-Champion). The tanks stand at
  the relic they are about to use: the three verified relic coordinates in
  3.1 ARE the tank positions, and the whole relic schedule is driven by
  dragging a boss within reach of a chosen disc.
  Exact tank coordinates for the pulls that are not next to a relic:
  unverified.

4.3 PHASES - gated by each boss's own health, not by a shared bar [GUIDE]
  Relic activation at 75%, 50% and 25% of that boss's health (Icy Veins,
  Wowpedia "whenever one of the members loses 25% of their maximum health",
  tanknotes, worldofmoudi). Each boss activates the nearest relic, gains 10%
  damage, and if it ever activates the same relic a second time it gains
  Power Overwhelming 282914 (+300% or 1000% depending on the source: Icy Veins
  says 300%, Warcraft Wiki says 1000% - the exact multiplier is unverified,
  the wipe is not).
  The fight is therefore six relic activations, two bosses x three
  thresholds. Recommended order per Icy Veins: Tempest Caller, Void Stone,
  Trident, Tempest Caller, Void Stone, Trident - alternating bosses so no
  boss repeats a relic.
  END: Pact of the Restless 282675. Both bosses must be brought to 1 health
  within a short window or they heal back to 20%. The window is given as 12 s
  by Icy Veins, ~20 s by MMO-Champion and gamingcy -> unverified.

4.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Shear Mind 282384 - assigned to the OFF TANK.
     Trigger: aura stack (SPELL_AURA_APPLIED_DOSE / REFRESH on 282384).
     Action: taunt swap; Icy Veins says swap at 4 stacks, tanknotes says at
     2-3 stacks and "swap when your stack drops" - the stack number is
     unverified, the swap is not.
  b) Dark Herald 282561 - assigned to the MARKED PLAYER.
     Trigger: aura applied. Action: the marked player separates from the
     raid; anyone within 5 yards gains Promises of Power 282562 (a +10% damage,
     -10% max health stacking buff), and on Heroic+ dispelling it hits the
     dispeller with Wrack 292880.
  c) Promises of Power 282562 - assigned to the DISPELLER.
     Trigger: dispel decision, not a cast. Action: strip stacks before heavy
     raid damage, and never dispel it onto a low-health player.
  d) Aphotic Blast 283524 / 282386 - assigned to the RANGED TANK.
     Trigger: Zaxasj targets the ranged threat holder. Action: a single ranged
     DPS holds Zaxasj away from melee; nobody stacks within 5 yards of them.
  e) Cerebral Assault 282589 - assigned to NOBODY, dodged.
     Trigger: the cone cast. Anyone hit is charmed 4 s (dvorakgaming).
  f) Crushing Doubt 282432 - assigned to the MARKED PLAYER.
     Trigger: aura. Action: run out - the burst at expiry falls off with
     distance, and it slows by 25%.
  g) Void Crash 285416 - assigned to the SOAK GROUP.
     Trigger: the circle spawns. Action: soak it once it is small; on Mythic
     the healers soak, because a low-health DPS with Promises stacks dies.
  h) Witness the End 282621 / 283540 - assigned to the KICK ROTATION.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START. Action: interrupt or kill the Eldritch
     Abomination 145053. A landed cast is lethal-scale raid damage and leaves
     a 12 s +100% damage taken debuff.
  i) Terrifying Echo 282517 - assigned to the VISAGE HANDLER.
     Trigger: 15 s cast on the Visage from Beyond 144996.
     Action: the only interrupt is bringing the Visage to 1 health; it
     regenerates through Coalesce Void 282524.
  j) Relic use itself - assigned to the RAID LEAD, one relic at a time.
     Trigger: a boss crossing 75/50/25. Action: that boss must be nearest a
     relic it has not used.

4.6 SAFE SPOTS
  The only hard boundary that can be verified is Enveloping Darkness 287876:
  leaving the platform applies it, 30 s (MMO-Champion), so the arena edge is
  the safe-spot limit in every direction.
  Verified drop points: the three relic discs (3.1) are safe ground - the
  designers put them there to be stood next to.
  Crushing Doubt 282432 must be run out of the raid; Void Crash 285416 is a
  soak, so its "safe spot" is inside the circle once it is small.
  No coordinates exist for the platform rim, for the ranged-tank spot or for
  the Visage tank spot -> unverified.

5.2 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION
  Boss spawn: unverified. Anchor: arena centre (3487.13, -1077.65, -381.98).
  TANK POSITION [SRC]: Uu'nat is walked by the script, not by a tank, during
  the transitions - the boss moves to (3486.29, -1041.76, -383.98) at both
  stage changes (POINT_VOID_SHIELD), which is 36.0 yards due +y of the centre.
  For the rest of the fight the tank holds him on the platform; the exact
  holding spot is unverified, but the constraint is verifiable:
  Sightless Bond gives Uu'nat 99% damage reduction while any Undying Guardian
  is within 10 yards of him, so the tank position must keep the boss at least
  10 yards from every guardian (guiaswow: "within 10 meters"). The id is split
  across sources: the server script's spell enum has 287693 and the client
  journal data in your tactics document has 287692 -> the aura is 287693 [SRV]
  and 287692 is the journal id [TAC]. The add spawns are 20.3 and 22.5 yards
  from the centre (3.2), so tanking him at the centre satisfies that by
  construction.

5.3 PHASES [GUIDE] - health-gated, with a 20 s invulnerable channel
  Stage One: His All-Seeing Eyes   100% - 70%
  transition                       70%: 20 s channel, 99% damage reduction
  Stage Two: His Dutiful Servants  70% - 40%
  transition                       40%: the same 20 s channel
  Stage Three: His Unwavering Gaze 40% - 0%
  Sources: MMO-Champion ("transitions at 70% and 40% HP ... channel a spell
  for 20 seconds, while being immune to damage"), dvorakgaming ("ends when
  Uu'nat reaches 70% health"), tanknotes ("20 seconds taking 99% less
  damage"), guiaswow ("at 70% of life we will go to Phase 2 ... at 40% we
  will go to Phase 3").
  DBM does not detect a health percentage at all: it advances its own stage
  counter when Void Shield 286310 lands, and it re-arms every timer from that
  event. The numbers in 5.5 marked "re-arm" are those start values.
  Verified in-script consequence: the same movement point is used at both
  transitions, so both channels happen at (3486.29, -1041.76, -383.98).
  DISCREPANCY: the script implements the two gates as HealthBelowPct(70) and
  HealthBelowPct(45), so on this core stage three begins at 45%, not the 40%
  every 2019 guide prints. Both are given above; the operative one on your
  server is the script's 45%.

5.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Touch of the End 284851 - assigned to the TANK.
     Trigger: the frontal cone. Action: it marks the tank for 30 s and every
     Undying Guardian is drawn to that mark; the tanks swap on the mark.
     Because it is a cone, nobody stands in front of Uu'nat.
  b) Undying Guardian / Sightless Bond 287693 - assigned to the ADD GROUP.
     Trigger: Call Undying Guardian 285820. Action: never let a guardian sit
     within 10 yards of Uu'nat (99% damage reduction), kill them with the
     Tempest Caller relic below 25% so their death effects do not fire.
     Unnatural Regeneration 285333 heals them from 1 health back up.
  c) Tempest Caller (relic, creature 144808, button 286750) - assigned to a
     DPS. Trigger: the relic is charged and used. Effect: Storm of
     Annihilation 286755, 15 s, and Annihilate 286753 kills any target below
     25% health. Feedback: Storm 286458 locks the user out.
  d) Void Stone (relic, creature 144804, button 284684) - assigned to a
     HEALER. Effect: Embrace of the Void 284722 stops all healing until the
     Umbral Shell 284722 shield on the boss is broken; the wielder takes
     damage to break their own shell.
  e) Trident of Deep Ocean (relic, creature 147574, button 284768) - assigned
     to the TANK. Effect: Custody of the Deep 284772 dome absorbs 75% of
     damage into itself and explodes for the remainder after 20 s
     (Abyssal Collapse 282886, the 20 s cast timer DBM keeps).
  f) Piercing Gaze of N'Zoth 285367 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: the two beams in 3.4 appear. Action: get off the swept line,
     not merely away from the boss; the aura is applied by a beam test in the
     script (IsOnBeam, 5.0 yard width, 3.0 yard offset).
  g) Maddening Eyes of N'Zoth 285345 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: the cast; the aura makes hit players hostile to allies for 8 s
     (guiaswow).
  h) Unknowable Terror 285562 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START; the cast is an area cast placed at the point
     3506.11, -1167.28, -375.83 hard-coded in the script. Action: leave that
     area before the cast ends, or flee for 6 s.
  i) Consume Essence 285427 - assigned to the KICK ROTATION.
     Trigger: Primordial Mindbender 146940 cast. Action: interrupt; a landed
     cast heals the mindbender to full and hurts the raid.
  j) Insatiable Torment 285652 - assigned to the AFFECTED PLAYER, and to
     everybody else by proximity. Trigger: aura. Action: the victim cannot be
     healed and instead leeches health from players within 5 yards every 2 s
     (Feed on the Unworthy 285653 / 285676), so the raid stacks around them.
     It never expires - this is the soft enrage.
  k) Gift of N'Zoth (Obscurity 285453 / Hysteria 285638 / Lunacy 285685) -
     assigned to the HEALERS and to the RAID LEAD.
     Obscurity shrinks the arena (3.3) and its damage check is the only
     enforced boundary; Hysteria heals and hastes every living add; Lunacy
     turns the raid hostile to itself for 4-5 s, so no AoE during it.
  l) Unstable Resonance 293653 (Mythic) - assigned to every player.
     Trigger: the cast, which picks per-player variants 293661 storm,
     293662 ocean, 293663 void. Action: stand on the matching relic; DBM
     starts a 10 yard range check and a 15 s fades timer.

5.6 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified geometry: the arena is a 50 yard circle around
  (3487.13, -1077.65, -381.98) while Obscurity stack 1 is up (3.3). Anything
  inside that radius is legal ground; anything outside it takes Obscurity
  damage. Exact rim coordinates are derivable from that radius and centre but
  are not themselves published -> treat any specific rim point as [CALC], not
  as a source value.
  Dropping ground effects: Oblivion Tear 285185 must be dropped at the rim
  (Icy Veins: "bait Oblivion Tears on the outside of the room"), because a
  tear that reaches the boss heals him for 1% of maximum health (guiaswow).
  Therefore the safe spot for the raid is the inner disc, and the tear baiters
  are the ones standing at radius ~50.
  The relic cluster at 34.8-36.2 yards from the centre is the only verified
  piece of interior furniture, and it is on the south side (y -1111 to -1112).
  The stage-transition channel point is 36.0 yards due +y of the centre, so
  during a transition the boss is north of the middle and the raid should
  clear the south.
  Piercing Gaze: the emitters are at radius 66-88 (3.4), i.e. outside the
  arena, so the beams sweep the whole circle - the safe spot changes with the
  sweep and has no fixed coordinate -> unverified as a point.
