Map IDs below are instance Map.db2 IDs, NOT Wowhead /zone IDs, UI map IDs or map-percentage coordinates. No verified boss world-space (map,x,y,z) spawn coordinates for this private core were found; each x/y/z is therefore unverified. Door/gameobject IDs, spell IDs and item IDs not individually verified are explicitly unverified. TOL DAGOR — instance map 1771 (Wowhead dungeon zone 9327, not 10272, which is a Tol Dagor scenario).
Boss order/positions: The Sand Queen, NPC 127479 (1771, unverified, unverified, unverified) -> Jes Howlis, 127484 (1771, unverified, unverified, unverified) -> Knight Captain Valyri, 127490 (1771, unverified, unverified, unverified) -> Overseer Korgus, 127503 (1771, unverified, unverified, unverified).
Start on the sandy prison-island shore, pass the crabs and kill Sand Queen at the entrance to the prison drain. Go through the SEWER GRATE/Drain into the cellblock; kill only guards in the chosen passage. A locked gate in the Drain can be opened with a found prison key or appropriate lockpicking (rogue, Monelite Skeleton Key or Scroll of Unlocking if available), providing a shortcut; without one, take the longer open passage. Key spawn positions, item entries and gate GO IDs: unverified. On the Brig level, cell doors along the right side contain friendly prisoners that award optional lasting buffs when freed. The far gate leads to the Detention Block and Jes's room. Opening optional side cell doors BEFORE engaging Jes can remove some prisoner adds from his later release; even if not pre-opened, at 70% he flees behind Smoke Powder and releases cell prisoners: defeat the encounter add wave (including Bobby Howlis) to finish him. No optional cell/buff is required to open the main route. Continue UP the stairs through the Officer Quarters; at the far end on the right open the gate to the exposed armory approach. Avoid Flamecasters/Spotters or clear those in the chosen lane; use a mounted prison CANNON on the exterior to fire into hostile guards and clear a safe path. The cannon is a helpful vehicle, NOT a mandatory door lever; its vehicle/spell IDs: unverified. Enter the Ashvane Armory and kill Valyri, physically moving unstable munitions BARRELS to avoid simultaneous detonation. The Ashvane Skeleton Key on the armory wall by the exit is for an optional dungeon QUEST, not a required drop to unlock Korgus (key item entry: unverified). After Valyri, continue UP to the Overseer's Summit; another cannon can clear outdoor trash. Defeat Korgus on the roof. Route splits: locked-gate shortcut versus normal stairway; optional prisoner cells versus direct corridor; fight versus bypass exterior packs by safe stealth/line of sight. Mandatory: four bosses, Jes encounter adds, any enemies actually blocking the chosen route; no independently verified trash-kill-count gate. Never tell companions Korgus's post-kill quest key is required BEFORE Korgus.

WAYCREST MANOR — instance map 1862 (Wowhead dungeon zone 9424).
Bosses/positions: first three are Heartsbane Triad [Sister Solena 135359, Sister Malady 135358, Sister Briar 135360] (1862, unverified, unverified, unverified), Soulbound Goliath 131667 (1862, unverified, unverified, unverified), and Raal the Gluttonous 131863 (1862, unverified, unverified, unverified), IN THE ORDER ALLOWED BY THAT RUN'S DOORS; then Lord Waycrest 131527 and Lady Waycrest 136918 (1862, unverified, unverified, unverified), then Gorak Tul 131864 (1862, unverified, unverified, unverified).
From the entry foyer inspect which of the starting doors is OPEN. In original BfA some doors are randomly locked; unlike later seasons, do not assume every doorway is freely traversable or script a fixed first boss. Starting LEFT/ballroom branch: cross the manor halls to the Heartsbane sisters; then go into the courtyard for Soulbound Goliath and through the dining room/kitchen for Raal. Starting RIGHT/dining branch: take the available hall/kitchen to Raal, go around through the courtyard for Goliath, then reach the sisters by the open upstairs OR downstairs door. An upstairs starting door can take you through the upper gallery before those rooms; follow whichever connected passage is actually open. The first three bosses may be tackled in the opposite order or via the gallery, but ALL THREE are required before the basement route unlocks. Kill only packs in the chosen hallway/courtyard that block travel; no verified trash kills automatically unlock a randomly closed starting door. At the Triad, target the sister currently holding the Focusing Iris (combat mechanic, NOT a loose key/GO to click); their defeat opens the relevant exit. At Goliath, pull him into Wildfire to shed Soul Harvest stacks; at Raal, eliminate Devouring Maggot/add waves. After all three, go through the newly available cellar/catacombs passage reached from the kitchen/dining side or near the witch hall as available; descend to the couple's chamber. Kill Lord Waycrest until Lady Waycrest spends her health healing him via Vitality Transfer, then defeat the two when Lady joins. Continue deeper into the basement/ritual chamber and fight Gorak Tul. Kill his Deathtouched Slaver adds and USE Alchemical Fire on their corpses to stop Dread Essence resurrecting them. Alchemical Fire source/GO/item/spell ID: unverified; do not confuse with any ordinary entry door. The cellar is boss-gated; courtyard/hall trash is skippable if not aggroed and the required boss route is accessible. This explicitly describes BfA randomized door logic, NOT the later always-open Manor.
Sources (BfA): https://www.mistyteahouse.com/battle-for-azeroth/dungeons/waycrest-manor/ ; https://www.tentonhammer.com/articles/world-of-warcraft-waycrest-manor-guide ; https://www.reddit.com/r/CompetitiveWoW/comments/f86m69/witch_skipping_waycrest_mannor/ ; https://www.wowhead.com/npc=131864/gorak-tul

OPERATION: MECHAGON — JUNKYARD (8.3 wing) — instance map 2097 (Wowhead zone 10225).
Order/positions: first THREE may be taken in any order: King Gobbamak 150159 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified), Gunker 150222 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified), Trixie Tazer 155407 + Naeno Megacrash 155406 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified). FOURTH: HK-8 Aerial Oppression Unit 155157 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified); the ordinary Tank Buster MK1 add is 150295.
From the Rustbolt-side starting camp the outdoor junkyard SPLITS: take the scrap/trogg route to Gobbamak, the southern OUTflow toxic-water route to Gunker, or the roads to Trixie and Naeno's motorbike yard; return along connected scrap roads to the next objective. Exact directional bearings on this core: unverified; rely on named landmarks and boss/area markers, not a guessed world-coordinate path. GUNKER IS GATED: kill all THREE Toxic Monstrosities (NPC 150168) in the Outflow to free Squirt Bots and spawn him. These three are mandatory, not skippable trash. Free Gooped Squirt Bots during the fight so their Sanitizing Spray clears slime; no separate item click is required. During Gobbamak's Charged Smash, players with Electrical Charge move near the arena's stolen-tech devices to power them and deal with trogg add waves (combat interaction, not the way to a door). At Trixie/Naeno, avoid Mechacycle charges, interrupt Taze and hide in the bike's Smoke Cloud when targeted by Mega Taze. HK-8 patrols OVER one of these encounters. For an ordinary route fight a boss WITHOUT HK-8 overhead, letting it change patrol targets; to deliberately activate the optional baseline-Mythic HARD MODE, kill all three encounters while HK-8 is above each in turn. Hard mode is not required for 8.3 Junkyard completion. After THREE boss kills HK-8 shifts to Junkwatt Depot; go there and kill the Tank Buster MK1. During HK-8's Annihilation Ray, follow the marked NORTH or SOUTH Overcharge Station, cross the active vent grates and dodge detection bots/beams (they return you to the starting area); CHANNEL/HACK the station until its energy reaches zero, dropping HK-8 in Haywire, then damage it. Repeat if it survives one down phase. This player-operated hack is mandatory; station GO/spell ID: unverified. Outdoor side packs and optional buff robots may be bypassed where safe; no other trash-kill gate is verified. A full Mythic 0 continues through the newly accessible mountain-side city/workshop entrance after HK-8; an 8.3 Mythic+ Junkyard ends here.

OPERATION: MECHAGON — WORKSHOP (8.3 wing) — instance map 2097 (separate confirmed 8.3 wing MAP ID if any: unverified; 2097 is the verified Operation: Mechagon base instance map).
Boss order/positions: Tussle Tonks [The Platinum Pummeler 144244 + Gnomercy 4.U. 145185] (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified) -> K.U.-J.0. 144246 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified) -> Machinist's Garden/Head Machinist Sparkflux 144248 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified) -> King Mechagon 150397 (2097, unverified, unverified, unverified; boss phase/vehicle NPC variants unverified).
Enter the underground WORKSHOP either from the 8.3 wing's finder/keystone entry, or after completing Junkyard's HK-8 in full Mythic 0. Follow the mechanical passage to the Robodrome. Defeat the preliminary trash Tonks in the arena to SPAWN the paired Tussle Tonks bosses (mandatory event waves, not optional trash); lure the Platinum Pummeler beneath three active Piston Smashers to remove its starting Platinum Plating while dealing with Gnomercy, saws and mines. Smashers are environmental combat hazards triggered by proximity/positioning, not inventory keys. Exit toward the UNDER JUNK, clear only blocking waste-handling robots, and fight K.U.-J.0. while Wendy Cogsworth drops Junk Cubes: hide BEHIND unheated cubes to break line of sight of Venting Flames. Heats/bombs are combat phases, not a door interaction. After K.U.-J.0. climb the backwards-moving CONVEYOR BELT while avoiding wall flame jets; a player reaching its far end can CLICK the small red control button to turn the belt/flames off for followers (whether this button spawns on every core/affix: unverified; if absent, cross normally). Continue through the Mechagon City/town-square DEFENSE EVENT: defeat its spawned robot waves, including Blastatron X-80 (151476) where spawned, to access the next passage. Whether the precise wave count on this core is fixed: unverified; do not try to walk through a closed event exit. Cross the Ampvolt Arcade DETECT-O-BOT gauntlet: stay outside blue detection circles or stand inside STEALTHY STEAM VENTS until patrol gaps open, then move from vent to vent. Detection returns the player to the start of this segment; attacking bots or treating them as trash is not the intended gate. Proceed through the garden entrance and kill Sparkflux, avoiding rotating Hidden Flame Cannons, bouncing hedge blades and spawned Inconspicuous Plants. Take the open route onward to King Mechagon's final chamber. Defeat his Aerial Unit R-21/X phase, then his ground/waste-processing machine phase; avoid Plasma Orbs and drill hazards. A separate small RED BUTTON near the final arena activates OPTIONAL baseline-Mythic HARD MODE and its timed panel sequence; do NOT click it during a standard completion. Its GO ID: unverified. Other packs can be skipped only when not needed to finish city defense waves or Mythic+ forces. The belt button and the optional hard-mode button are different controls.
