TWINK29 - MONK - STEP 1 OF 3 - ITEMS FOR EVERY SLOT
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
RULE FOR THIS FILE: leather armour, rare (blue) or bind-on-account, item
level 35 or less, required level 26 to 29. Weapons and jewellery follow
the same quality and level window. Same rule the druid, hunter and mage
files use.
SLOT|item|id|ilvl|req|source|tag    tag: SOLO ALT RNG
V = name, item level, required level and source read off a search or item
page.
Pool: rare leather, required 26-29, item level 35 or less returns 25 rows,
and most of them are class-locked to other classes. What is left for a
monk is nine pieces. The leather heirloom list has six items, the fist
weapon list has one, and the general weapon list has 37.

0. THE TWO THINGS THAT MAKE MONK GEARING DIFFERENT
   No battleground set. All ten Arathi Basin leather pieces in the pool -
   20090, 20093, 20102, 20105, 20114, 20117, 20169, 20172, 20188, 20191 -
   carry a Druid or Rogue class lock, and the search shows no monk-tagged
   battleground piece at this tier at all. A monk's belt and boots are
   therefore heirloom, green or nothing, while every other class in this
   set has a battleground pair to buy.
   No chest either. The 25-row leather pool has no rare chest piece, so
   the chest slot is one of the two leather heirlooms or a green.
   With belt, boots, chest and back all unsupported, a 29 monk is wearing
   greens in four slots no matter how the roster is funded.

3. BREWMASTER
   Armour: section 1, with the Agility heirlooms 42952 and 48689.
   Weapon: 7683 Bloody Brass Knuckles, or a staff, or one-hand sword and
   shield - a monk cannot use a shield, so the off-hand is a weapon.
   Trinkets: 19024, the Insignia, 4381.
   A Brewmaster tanks with a two-hander in some expansions; at 29 the
   staff 19569 or 19573 is the only two-hand option in the pool, and no
   two-hand monk weapon other than a staff exists at this tier.

4. MISTWEAVER
   Armour: section 1, with 42984 and 48687, the Intellect leather pair.
   That is the only spec-specific gearing decision in the whole file:
   two heirlooms, and they are the two best items in their slots anyway.
   Weapon: staff 19569 or 19573, or 7683 if the build wants dual wield.
   Trinkets: the same three.
   The neck, ring and wrist slots carry no Intellect item in the window,
   so a Mistweaver's spell power comes from the heirlooms and the weapon
   alone at this tier.

5. WINDWALKER
   Armour: section 1, with 42952 and 48689.
   Weapon: 7683 Bloody Brass Knuckles is the pick; 13033 Zealot Blade is
   the cheaper one-hand sword. Dual wield needs a second one-hand weapon
   and the Dual Wield question answered first.
   Trinkets: the same three. 4381 Minor Recombobulator is worth more to a
   melee spec than to a caster, because it also clears a polymorph.

7. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   Nine rare leather pieces in the window, of which two are hands and legs
   and the rest are head, wrist, shoulder and feet. Four slots - back,
   chest, waist and the rings - have no blue item a monk can wear, and the
   chest is the one that matters most because it is where the heirlooms
   live. A 29 monk is therefore the most heirloom-dependent class in this
   set: two heirlooms are the best item in two slots and the only item in
   the chest, while the belt, boots and cloak stay green.
   Every number above is the WotLK-era item page; where 8.3.7 re-itemised
   an item, its item level can differ. No required level moved on anything
   checked.
