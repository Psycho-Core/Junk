AWAKENED — Battle for Azeroth season 4, client 8.3.7 build 35662

Awakened (affix 120) is the +10 seasonal affix. Four obelisks per eligible Mythic+ dungeon: Brutal Spire of Ny'alotha (NPC entries 160984 and 161143, Urg'roth 161124), Cursed Spire (161163 and 161164, Voidweaver Mal'thir 161241), Defiled Spire (161166 and 161167, Blood of the Corruptor 161244), Entropic Spire (161168 and 161169, Samh'rek 161243). Listings confirm both IDs in each named spire pair; WHICH of any pair is the clickable world-side NPC rather than a phased/destroyed counterpart is UNVERIFIED. Do not assume the first ID is clickable. Verify the server's phase masks, gossip/spell-click flags and spawn records for each entry. Each spire's map ID and precise world x/y/z, as well as the particular dungeon/week permutation of servant-to-location, are unverified here. A UI-map pin or a dungeon's instance map ID does not supply a world position.

USING A SPIRE: Only interact out of combat. The activated spire admits players to N'Zoth's alternate phase and spawns THAT spire's lieutenant. Ny'alotha Incursion (313445) is the player's phase/screen-effect aura (listed as a debuff, indefinite duration): the normal dungeon's creatures and bosses are blurred/inaccessible while the player is in the incursion, rather than being killed or skipped for Enemy Forces. Players in the normal world do not attack the phased lieutenant. Drag a lieutenant through the alternate version of the dungeon if desired; on its death a rift appears where it died and the original obelisk becomes a rift too. These rifts can be used out of combat to travel both directions, placing an exit after a trash pack. Unactivated spires are one-way entrances. Exact phase ID and all transition/portal spell and creature IDs: unverified. Do not implement this as a raid-zone teleport or grant credit for trash bypassed in either phase.

COMMON SERVANT RULE: When fought after activating a spire, each lieutenant summons its own adds via Armies of N'Zoth (314397; spell listing: 2-second cast). The exact first-cast time and repeat cooldown of 314397 are unverified. Each defeated lieutenant gives approximately 4 RAW Enemy Forces according to a February 2020 contemporary guide; per-dungeon/keystone live and private-core count, percent of total, event timing and summoned-add counts: unverified. The 4 is NOT 4% and is NOT loot. No verified lieutenant-specific item drop or direct chest loot: unverified; normal completion rewards come from completing the keystone, not clicking an obelisk. Their HP and ordinary melee swing damage scale with dungeon/keystone and are unverified as fixed numbers on this core. Spell database basepoint damage is NOT a measured +10 hit.

BRUTAL — Urg'roth, Breaker of Heroes (161124): Spirit Breaker 314308, 2-second cast, 20-yard range, current target: 200% weapon damage Physical and +100% ALL damage taken for 8 seconds in spell listing. Contemporary PTR testing estimates roughly 10 seconds between successful casts, but exact live cooldown and time to FIRST cast are unverified; tooltip cooldown n/a is not an AI cooldown. Dark Fury 314309: 3-second cast, Shadow blast in 4-yard circle; dodge the circle. First cast and repeat cooldown: unverified. Armies of N'Zoth 314397 summons Malicious Growth (161408); Growth's Vile Corruption 314392 slows and damages nearby players, remove growths to clear the affected space. Tank should plan mitigation/positioning and avoid chaining Spirit Breaker into the next hit. HP, autoattack damage and exact growth count: unverified.

CURSED — Voidweaver Mal'thir (161241): Crippling Pestilence 314406, 3-second cast on a player, Nature hit plus Disease: -70% movement for 8 seconds (spell listing). Lingering Doubt 314411, 3-second cast on a player, Shadow hit plus Curse: -70% casting speed for 8 seconds. The caster alternates these; dispel the appropriate Disease/Curse and handle player damage. First cast and repeat cooldown of EACH: unverified. Armies of N'Zoth 314397 summons Explosive Scarabs (161437); Volatile Rupture 314467 is a 4-second explosion cast, with a harmful poison on hit: control/kill or avoid scarabs. HP, autoattack damage, exact add count on this core: unverified (contemporary guide reports five).

DEFILED — Blood of the Corruptor (161244): Defiled Ground 314565 (damage aura is listed as instant, every 1 second); ground is placed under the creature and at locations of two players in alternating attacks. Move away from the patches; pools go away when Blood dies. The ground-effect trigger/cast spell distinct from 314565: unverified; 314565's instant tooltip must not be interpreted as proof of the NPC's first cast or AI cooldown. First cast and repeat cooldown: unverified. Armies of N'Zoth 314397 summons Mindrend Tentacles (161510); Mind Flay 314592 is an interruptible damaging/slow channel, so interrupt or control tentacles. HP, melee damage, exact live add count: unverified (contemporary guide reports five). Blizzard's 2020 patch notes describe 60,000 damage per second for the ground at their reference tuning, NOT a universal 8.3.7 or keystone-level value.

ENTROPIC — Samh'rek, Beckoner of Chaos (161243): Cascading Terror selector 314477 is instant and targets up to three players; applied Magic debuff 314478 lasts 8 seconds in the current spell listing; on expiration it triggers 314483, Shadow damage and a 2-second fear to targets within 5 yards. A February 2020 guide says three seconds of fear, reflecting an earlier state: spell listing and later tuning differ; exact historical build-35662 fear duration should be checked in this server, not hard-coded from the early guide. Dispel the Magic debuffs where safe and spread debuff holders away from allies before expiry. First cast and AI repeat cooldown: unverified; spell's n/a cooldown does not imply no delay. Armies of N'Zoth 314397 summons Ravenous Fleshfiends (161502); Tear Flesh 314531 is a stacking bleed on their tank: manage/add-control and heal. HP, melee damage, exact add count: unverified (contemporary guide reports five).

LAST BOSS IF ANY SERVANT WAS NOT DEFEATED: On engagement of the dungeon's FINAL boss, EACH still-alive unhandled lieutenant teleports into that encounter and attacks alongside it; all four can therefore be faced together if none was killed beforehand. These arrivals still use their own lieutenant abilities; unlike activated-spire fights they do NOT cast Armies of N'Zoth (314397), so their special extra adds do not appear. If the party wipes, the lieutenants despawn from the final-boss encounter and respawn on the next pull; they have not thereby been permanently cleared. Killing them during the final encounter grants their ordinary lieutenant credit (exact trigger/count on the private core: unverified). Defeated servants do not reappear merely because the final boss is pulled. Whether special boss reset/door boundaries affect a particular core's phasing: unverified. Do not substitute a generic all-at-once spawn at dungeon start.
