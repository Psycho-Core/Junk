Patch 8.3.7 (build 35662), level 29 battleground twink
================================================================================

All ids checked against 8.3.7 client tables (Item, ItemSparse, ItemSearchName,
SpellEffect, SpellReagents, SpellCooldowns, SkillLine, SkillLineAbility) and the

================================================================================
1. WHAT A LEVEL 29 CAN ACTUALLY REACH
================================================================================

Classic Engineering is skill line 202 in 8.3.7, with the classic tier as skill
line 2506 (ParentSkillLineID 202, ParentTierIndex 4). Both are live in 8.3.7.

Rank gates (Classic table, re-stated by every 1-300 guide; costs undiscounted):
  Apprentice  1-75    level 5   10 copper
  Journeyman  1-150   level 10  5 silver      (needs 50 skill)
  Expert      1-225   level 20  50 silver     (needs 125 skill)
  Artisan     1-300   level 35  5 gold        (needs 200 skill)

=> At level 29 the ceiling is 225. Artisan (300) is 6 levels out of reach.

Caveat: the level gate on the ranks is trainer/world data, not something the
8.3.7 client DB2 tables expose, so it could not be re-verified inside 8.3.7
itself. Classic guides plus the 20-29 twink guide on xpoff all give 225 as the
level-29 cap and level 35 for Artisan. Treat 225 as the working cap; confirm on
the trainer before spending gold.

Specialisation is worse: Goblin and Gnomish Engineering both need level 30 and
200 skill (quest chain: "Gnomish Engineering" / "Goblin Engineering" -> "The
Pledge of Secrecy" -> "Show Your Work"; Show Your Work is quest 3639 for Goblin
at Nixx Sprocketspring, creature 8126, Gadgetzan, Tanaris, required level 30).
A level 29 cannot take it.  ALL specialisation recipes are therefore ALT.

Consequences that shape the whole shopping list:
  - Anything with RequiredSkillRank 226+ on skill line 2506 is unusable: Artisan
    only.
  - The specialisation gate is ItemSparse.RequiredAbility: 20219 = Gnomish
    Engineering, 20222 = Goblin Engineering. An item carrying either value is
    unusable at 29 however it was obtained. Items carrying RequiredAbility 0 need
    only the Engineering rank (7189, 10716, 10720, 10721, 10726 all carry 0).
  - Items a 29 cannot craft but can wear are still worth buying from an alt or a
    level 60 engineer, as long as RequiredAbility is 0.
  - Every engineering explosive sits on one shared-cooldown group, so grenades,
    dynamite and bombs cannot be chained: Wowhead Classic files Iron Grenade
    4068, Big Iron Bomb 4069, Solid Dynamite 12419 and Explosive Sheep 4074 all
    under shared cooldown 65. The 8.3.7 SpellCooldowns table was not queried,
    so treat the grouping as unverified in-client.
  - Recipes are learned from the trainer unless noted; no reputation gate applies
    to anything in this file.


================================================================================
2. CRAFTABLE AT LEVEL 29 (skill 225 or less, no specialisation)
================================================================================

--- Iron Grenade -------------------------------------------------------------
Item 4390. Item level 35, stack 20, not bound, no level requirement.
Use (spell 4068): 132-218 Fire damage and 3 sec stun in a 3 yard radius, any
damage breaks the stun. 1 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 3962. Engineering 175.
Materials: 1x Iron Bar 3575, 1x Heavy Blasting Powder 4377, 1x Silk Cloth 4306.
  Iron Bar: Mining, smelted from Iron Ore 2772.
  Heavy Blasting Powder (4377): made from Heavy Stone 2838, 1:1 (trainer
  recipe, Engineering 135).
  Silk Cloth 4306: humanoid drops, roughly level 25-40 mobs.
This is the workhorse: cheapest stun in the game and the stun is what wins
mid-field fights, not the damage.

--- Big Iron Bomb ------------------------------------------------------------
Item 4394. Item level 43, stack 10, not bound.
Use (spell 4069): 149-201 Fire damage and 3 sec stun in a 5 yard radius, any
damage breaks the stun. 1 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 3967. Engineering 190.
Materials: 3x Iron Bar 3575, 3x Heavy Blasting Powder 4377, 1x Silver Contact
4404.
  Silver Contact 4404: 1x Silver Bar 2842 + 1x Copper Bar 2840 (Classic
  recipe; craft spell id unverified).
Bigger radius than the Iron Grenade, slightly less damage. Position it on a
flag/node, not on a single target.

--- Solid Dynamite -----------------------------------------------------------
Item 10507. Item level 35, stack 20, not bound.
Use (spell 12419): 213-287 Fire damage in a 5 yard radius, no stun. 1 minute
cooldown.
Recipe: spell 12586. Engineering 175.
Materials: 1x Solid Blasting Powder 10505, 1x Silk Cloth 4306.
  Solid Blasting Powder 10505: made from Solid Stone 7912, 1:1.
Highest raw damage a 29 can craft, but it is on the same shared cooldown as the
stuns, so pick one per fight.

--- Explosive Sheep ----------------------------------------------------------
Item 4384. Item level 30, stack 5, not bound.
Use (spell 4074): summons a sheep that charges a nearby enemy and explodes for
135-165 damage. 1 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 3955. Engineering 150.
Materials: 1x Bronze Framework 4382, 1x Whirring Bronze Gizmo 4375, 2x Heavy
Blasting Powder 4377, 2x Wool Cloth 2592.
  Bronze Framework 4382: Classic recipe 2x Bronze Bar 2841 + 1x Medium Leather
  2319 + 1x Wool Cloth 2592 (not re-checked in 8.3.7 SpellReagents).
  Whirring Bronze Gizmo 4375: Classic recipe 2x Bronze Bar 2841 + 1x Wool Cloth
  2592.
Only use is interrupting a drink or a resurrect; the damage is small at 29.

--- Minor Recombobulator -----------------------------------------------------
Item 4381. Item level 28, trinket, 10 charges, not bound, no level requirement.
Use (spell 18805): dispels Polymorph on a friendly target and restores 150-250
health and mana. 5 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 3952. Engineering 140.
Materials: 1x Bronze Tube 4371, 2x Whirring Bronze Gizmo 4375, 2x Medium Leather
2319, 1x Moss Agate 1206.
  Bronze Tube 4371: 2x Bronze Bar 2841 + 1x Weak Flux 2880 (vendor).
  Moss Agate 1206: Mining, low-level veins.
Charge-limited but keeps working after a mage sheeps your healer or your FC.

--- Discombobulator Ray ------------------------------------------------------
Item 4388. Item level 32, 5 charges, not bound.
Use (spell 4060): turns the target into a Leper Gnome: -40 melee and spell
damage, -20% movement speed, 12 sec. 1 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 3959. Engineering 160.
Materials: 3x Whirring Bronze Gizmo 4375, 2x Silk Cloth 4306, 1x Jade 1529,
1x Bronze Tube 4371.
Strongest debuff a 29 can apply with an item, and it also forces a dismount.
Twelve seconds of a rogue doing 40 less damage per hit is usually the fight.

--- Gnomish Cloaking Device --------------------------------------------------
Item 4397. Item level 40, trinket, binds when equipped.
Use (spell 4079): invisibility for 10 sec. 1 hour cooldown.
Recipe: spell 3971. Engineering 200.  No Gnomish specialisation needed to craft
or to use (the wiki names this item as its counter-example).
Materials: 4x Gyrochronatom 4389, 2x Jade 1529, 2x Lesser Moonstone 1705,
2x Citrine 3864, 1x Fused Wiring 7191.
  Gyrochronatom 4389: crafted part, Engineering 175; made from Iron Bar 3575
  plus a Gold Power Core. Exact sub-recipe not verified here.
  Fused Wiring 7191: crafted part, Engineering 185; recipe not verified here.
One use per battleground at most, but 10 sec of invisibility resets a fight or
walks a flag out of a stacked defence.

--- Parachute Cloak ----------------------------------------------------------
Item 10518. Item level 45, back slot, +8 Agility, binds when equipped.
Use (spell 12438): reduces fall speed for 10 sec. 30 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 12616. Engineering 225.
Materials: 4x Bolt of Mageweave 4339, 2x Shadow Silk 10285, 1x Unstable Trigger
10560, 4x Solid Blasting Powder 10505.
  Bolt of Mageweave 4339: 2x Mageweave Cloth 4338 (tailoring) or bought.
  Shadow Silk 10285: drop from higher-level spiders, auction house.
  Unstable Trigger 10560: Classic recipe 1x Mithril Bar 3860 + 1x Mageweave
  Cloth 4338.
The single best engineering item for a 29 flag carrier: 10 sec of slow fall
crosses the Warsong Gulch roof gaps and the Arathi Basin lumber-mill drop
without taking the fall damage that normally kills a 29.

--- Green Tinted Goggles -----------------------------------------------------
Item 4385. Item level 30, cloth head, 35 armour, +8 Stamina, +7 Spirit, binds
when equipped, no level requirement.
Recipe: spell 3956. Engineering 150.
Materials: 4x Medium Leather 2319, 2x Moss Agate 1206, 1x Flying Tiger Goggles
4368.
Best head slot a 29 engineer can make for a caster or a spirit-stacking class,
and it upgrades the level-20 goggles instead of replacing them.

--- Flying Tiger Goggles -----------------------------------------------------
Item 4368. Item level 20, cloth head, 27 armour, +4 Stamina, +4 Spirit, binds
when equipped, no level requirement.
Recipe: spell 3934. Engineering 100.
Materials: 8x Bronze Bar 2841, 2x Tigerseye 818.
Cheap filler head slot while levelling; it is also the reagent for the Green
Tinted Goggles, so make two.

--- Goblin Glider Kit (buy, do not craft) ------------------------------------
Item 109076. Item level 1, stack 20, not bound, RequiredLevel 1, no Engineering
skill required to use.
Created by spell 162204 (Draenor Engineering) and spell 221234 (Legion
Engineering). A 29 cannot learn either tier, so this is auction house only.
Puts a glider on your back: forward movement with slow fall, the same role as
the Parachute Cloak but usable by any class and without spending Engineering.
Whether it is still permitted inside battlegrounds in 8.3.7: unverified.

--- Tools you must carry -----------------------------------------------------
Arclight Spanner 6219. Craft spell 7430, Engineering 50, 6x Copper Bar 2840.
  Required in the bag for most early recipes; every engineer ends up with one.
Gyromatic Micro-Adjustor 10498. Craft spell 12590, Engineering 194, 4x Steel Bar
3859.
  Required for the scope and later recipes.
Blacksmith Hammer 5956: vendor trash item, needed for the anvil recipes.
An anvil is required for the metal-part recipes; every capital city has one.

3. USABLE AT 29, CRAFTED BY SOMEBODY ELSE (Bind on Equip, specialisation recipes)
================================================================================
A 29 cannot learn Goblin or Gnomish Engineering, but these items only require
Engineering skill to USE. Have an alt or a level 60 engineer craft them and mail
them over. Bind on Equip and RequiredAbility 0 confirmed in 8.3.7 ItemSparse
for 7189, 10716, 10720, 10721 and 10726; 10646 is unbound and needs no
specialisation.

--- Goblin Rocket Boots ------------------------------------------------------
Item 7189. Item level 45, cloth feet, binds when equipped.
Use (spell 8892): +run speed for 20 sec, can backfire and explode. 5 minute
cooldown.
Recipe: spell 8895, Goblin Engineering, Engineering 225 (SkillLineAbility 4927,
TrivialSkillLineID 2506).
Materials: 1x Black Mageweave Boots 10026, 2x Mithril Tube 10559, 4x Heavy
Leather 4234, 2x Goblin Rocket Fuel 9061, 1x Unstable Trigger 10560.
  Black Mageweave Boots 10026: tailoring, cloth feet, item level 46, requires
  level 41 to equip. The crafter supplies them; the twink never wears them.

--- Gnomish Net-o-Matic Projector --------------------------------------------
Item 10720. Item level 42, trinket, binds when equipped.
Use: nets the target in place (root), 10 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 12902, Gnomish Engineering, Engineering 210.
Materials: 1x Mithril Tube 10559, 2x Shadow Silk 10285, 4x Thick Spider's Silk
4337, 2x Solid Blasting Powder 10505, 4x Mithril Bar 3860.
The only ranged root a 29 can get from a profession. It can also backfire and
net the user.

--- Gnomish Shrink Ray -------------------------------------------------------
Item 10716. Item level 41, trinket, binds when equipped.
Use (spell 13006): shrinks the target, -250 attack power. 5 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 12899, Gnomish Engineering, Engineering 205.

--- Gnomish Mind Control Cap -------------------------------------------------
Item 10726. Item level 47, head, +Versatility, binds when equipped.
Use: mind controls a humanoid target for a short duration, 30 minute cooldown.
Recipe: spell 12907, Gnomish Engineering, Engineering 215.
Materials: 10x Mithril Bar 3860, 4x Truesilver Bar 6037, 1x 10558 (Essence of
Fire per Classic), 2x Star Ruby 7910, 4x Mageweave Cloth 4338.

--- Gnomish Harm Prevention Belt --------------------------------------------
Item 10721. Item level 43, waist, +Stamina, binds when equipped.
Use: absorbs incoming damage, 1 hour cooldown.
Recipe: spell 12903, Gnomish Engineering, Engineering 215.
Materials: 1x Dusky Belt 7387 (leather waist, item level 39, requires level 34
to equip), 4x Mithril Bar 3860, 2x Truesilver Bar
6037, 1x Unstable Trigger 10560, 2x Aquamarine 7909.

--- Goblin Sapper Charge -----------------------------------------------------
Item 10646. Item level 41, stack 20, not bound.
Use: large Fire damage in a radius, also hits the user. 5 minute cooldown.
Recipe: Goblin Engineering, Engineering 205.
The classic "one-shot the group on the flag" bomb; a 29 can use it at 205 but
must buy it.

================================================================================
7. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE ENGINEERING AT 29
================================================================================
Take it on every class that can afford it - it is the only profession that adds
new buttons rather than stats. Ranked by how much it changes the class:

  Druid (flag carrier)  Parachute Cloak 10518 plus Rocket Boots 7189 is the
                        best escape kit in Warsong Gulch at 29.
  Warrior               Iron Grenade 4390 / Big Iron Bomb 4394 give the stun
                        the class otherwise lacks at 29; boots close gaps.
  Rogue                 Grenade stun into an opener, Discombobulator Ray 4388
                        to cut a target's damage and speed while you reposition,
                        Gnomish Cloaking Device 4397 for a second vanish.
  Hunter                Grenade into Freezing Trap is the strongest 29
                        lockdown; scopes are dead for a 29 (Sniper Scope is
                        level 40 / skill 240), so the value is the stun only.
  Mage / Warlock / Priest  Peel tools: Explosive Sheep 4384 to interrupt,
                        Shrink Ray 10716 and Mind Control Cap 10726 to remove a
                        melee, Minor Recombobulator 4381 to undo a polymorph on
                        yourself or a healer.
  Shaman / Paladin      Least dependent on it; still worth it for the stun bomb
                        and the cloak, but Alchemy or Blacksmithing compete.

Second profession: Mining on the twink itself (halves the 1-225 bill) or
Herbalism/Alchemy on an alt to fund the auction-house parts.
