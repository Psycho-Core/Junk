THE REMOVAL
  2  PASS   First Aid was removed in patch 8.0.1 (2018-07-17), the Battle
            for Azeroth pre-patch. Wowpedia and warcraft.wiki.gg both say
            so, and add that the trainers were renamed "Bandage Trainer".
  3  PASS   bandages became Classic Tailoring recipes and the items no
            longer require First Aid skill. Confirmed on the live pages:
            8544 by spell 10840 at Classic Tailoring 240, 14529 by 18629 at
            290, 14530 by 18630 at 300 - all in the 2540 tier.
  4  CORRECTED  the id in the file was wrong and so was its status. 6450 is
            the plain Silk Bandage, 800 healing, spell 7928 at Tailoring
            180. The heavy version is 6451 Heavy Silk Bandage, 1280
            healing, spell 7929 at Tailoring 210 - which is inside a 29's
            225 Tailoring ceiling, so the file's "a 29 tailor makes at most
            the silk bandages and should not bother" was too dismissive.
            Both rows are now in the table and the consequence paragraph
            says the heavy silk bandage is a 29 tailor's best.
  5  CORRECTED  the file claimed the 3.x tooltips "read 640, 800, 1200 and
            2000", which reads as if every row changed. Spell 7929 shows
            the 3.x heavy silk craft as First Aid 180, difficulty
            180/180/210/240, 2x Silk Cloth 4306, item healing 640, while
            spell 7928 shows plain Silk at First Aid 150 and item healing
            400. The 3.x Mageweave and Runecloth items heal 1600 and 1200,
            the same as live, so the rework is uneven and only some rows
            moved. Both craft tiers are now stated in the paragraph.
  6  PASS   bandages are flagged "usable in arenas" on every bandage page
            opened, share a cooldown, and apply the 60 second Recently
            Bandaged debuff, which is the wowpedia description.
  7  UNVERIFIED  "no character level requirement any more". The live
            database shows no requirement line on any bandage, but that is
            current retail data, not a build 35662 readout. The file says
            so and tells the reader to re-check in game.
  8  PASS   anti-venoms moved to Alchemy in the same patch, per both wikis.

COOKING AS A PROFESSION
  9  PASS   skill line 185, and the 225 ceiling is documented in the
            strongest way of anything in this set: artisan Cooking is not
            trainer-taught at all, it is the "Clamlette Surprise" quest
            6610 from Dirge Quikcleave in Gadgetzan, which needs level 35
            and 225 skill, 12x Giant Egg 12207, 10x Zesty Clam Meat 7974
            and 20x Alterac Swiss 8932.
 10  PASS   the rank gates and the Expert Cookbook: apprentice at level 5,
            journeyman at level 10 and skill 50, expert at level 20 and
            skill 125 via Expert Cookbook 16072 sold by Wulan in Desolace
            and Shandrina in Ashenvale.
