GEAR RULES AUDIT 2 - ALL CLASSES, STEP 1 ITEM FILES
Rule checked (as instructed): blue (rare) rarity or bind-on-account
only, item level 35 or less, required level 26 to 29. Legal sources
only (vendor, quest, dungeon drop, BG/dungeon crate, crafted, heirloom
vendor, Darkmoon, world rares; no auction house).
Scope: every id-bearing row in all ten Twink29_*_1_Items.txt files
(320 rows parsed) plus every named item in the what-to-wear sections.
Heirloom rows printed "scales/1" are BoA and are the stated exception
to the 26-29 equip window; they are not counted as breaches.

RESULT: 5 files corrected, 3 breach classes, 2 unverified classes.

BREACH 1 - GREEN (QUALITY 2) ITEMS LISTED AS GEAR. CORRECTED.
  15339 Pathfinder Hat and 15140 Cutthroat's Mantle read quality 2
  with a random enchantment on the 8.3.7 tooltip (verified in the
  rogue file itself). They were still listed as gear in:
   - Druid: both rows, and both in the what-to-wear list. REMOVED.
     Head now 6688 then 3020; shoulder now 42952 then 7727.
   - Monk: both rows. REMOVED (3020 and 7727 etc. remain).
   - Paladin: 15140 named as the leather shoulder alternative.
     REMOVED; 7727 Watchman Pauldrons 32/27 remains.
  Rogue, Shaman and Warrior already excluded them. Not re-added.

BREACH 2 - REQUIRED LEVEL BELOW THE 26 FLOOR LISTED AS A ROW.
CORRECTED where it was a row; already only a warning elsewhere.
  13097 Thunderbrow Ring is 29/24 (all files agree).
   - Hunter RING 1 row and Mage RING 1 row: REMOVED as rows; the
     slot now reads "none legal" with the reason.
   - Druid RING row: REMOVED as a row (it had been kept "because
     nothing else is verified"; that is not a legal reason).
   - Monk, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior already
     carried it as a note/"do not use", not a row. PASS.
  Druid also recommended "break the rule for 4119" (Raptor Hunter
  Tunic, ilvl 43): that line is corrected to OUT. 4119 was never a
  row; the recommendation is withdrawn.

BREACH 3 - GREEN PLACEHOLDER ROWS ("a green, of the Monkey" etc.).
These appear in every class file in slots where no blue exists
(backs, some rings/legs/waists, Mage legs, Monk waist/rings).
Under the rule as now stated they are NOT gear and must not be
read as recommendations: the legal state of those slots is
"heirloom if one exists for the slot, otherwise empty".
This audit does not delete the slot lines, because they also carry
the true information that no blue exists in the window - but any
companion build generated from these files must treat every
"green ..." row as EMPTY, never as an item to equip. Files most
affected: Hunter, Mage, Monk, Paladin, Rogue (rings/back).

NOT BREACHES (checked and clear):
  - No id-bearing row in any file prints ilvl above 35.
  - No id-bearing row prints required level 30+ or 25- in its own
    ilvl/req columns (parser check, all 320 rows), apart from the
    13097 rows corrected above and trinket rows below.
  - Class armour-type rules hold: Hunter/Shaman/Warrior/Paladin
    mail-or-BoA (no leather gear rows for Hunter), casters cloth,
    Druid/Monk/Rogue leather. Cross-type leather rows in mail
    files (e.g. 18948 Barbaric Bracers) are stated as cross-type
    in place and are blue, 32/27: legal, if suboptimal.

UNVERIFIED 1 - TOOLTIP-VS-SEARCH REQUIRED-30 CONFLICTS.
  A group of shared items prints req 26-29 from search but reads
  required 30 on the BfA tooltip: 6686, 6688, 1488, 9445, 9446,
  9455 (reads 29/24 the other way), 6690, 2816, 6687.
  If this server uses the tooltip values, those items are OUT at
  29 and several files lose their best row in a slot (Warrior
  loses 6687 Corpsemaker). If it uses the search values they are
  legal. This cannot be settled from web sources; it needs one
  on-server check. Until then every such row stays flagged in
  place and must not be treated as confirmed-legal. This is the
  single largest open risk in the whole gear set.

UNVERIFIED 2 - TRINKET ROWS WITH NO LEVEL DATA.
  19024 Arena Grand Master (ilvl "unv.", req "none") and 4381
  Minor Recombobulator (req "none", Engineering 140) appear in
  most files. Their rarity and 8.3.7 item level were never read,
  so they cannot be passed against ilvl <=35. Do not treat them
  as confirmed-legal until checked on-server. Insignia trinkets
  are honour-vendor BoP blues in the source files that were read;
  their at-29 item level is likewise unverified.
