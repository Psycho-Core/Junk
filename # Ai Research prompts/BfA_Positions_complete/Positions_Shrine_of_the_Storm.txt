================================================================================
 SHRINE OF THE STORM - POSITIONS AND NUMBERS, BFA 8.3.7 BUILD 35662
 Map id 1864. Four encounters: Aqu'sirr, the Tidesage Council (Brother
 Ironhull and Galecaller Faye), Lord Stormsong, Vol'zith the Whisperer.
 Companion file to the tactics document. Every number is tagged with the
 source it came from. Anything I could not read is written "unverified".

0. HOW TO READ THIS FILE

  Tags, used on every claim:
    [DB]    a row in a world SQL for this instance: creature spawn, game
            object, areatrigger, instance_template, or a smart_scripts row.
    [SRV]   a line in the 8.3.7 script: shrine_of_the_storm.h,
            instance_shrine_of_the_storm.cpp, shrine_of_the_storm.cpp, or
            one of the four boss files.
    [MDT]   Method Dungeon Tools, BattleForAzeroth/ShrineoftheStorm.lua:
            the name, the per-kill force value, the health pool, the level.
    [DBM]   DBM-Party-BfA/ShrineoftheStorm/*.lua: a stopwatch value.
    [LW]    LittleWigs BfA/ShrineOfTheStorm/*.lua: a second stopwatch value.
    [CALC]  arithmetic I did on the numbers above. The inputs are always
            given so you can redo it.
    unverified   no readable source carries the value. It is never a guess.

  Two of those tags need a qualifier, stated here so that no number in this
  file is mistaken for something it is not.
    [DB] on a creature spawn is the TrinityCore master world DB for map
    1864: sql/old/11.x/world/24051_2024_09_03/2024_05_27_00_world.sql, 398
    rows, plus one row for Aqu'sirr in the 24091_2024_09_23 file. It is NOT
    the 8.3.7 database; it is used because no 8.3.7 spawn dump for this map
    is public. The map is the same map, and where the 8.3.7 script carries
    its own constant the two agree to the last digit - Aqu'sirr's row lands
    exactly on the script's centerPlatform (4.3) and Vol'zith's floor z
    agrees with the script's add spawn points to 0.5 yd (7.3). Read every
    trash coordinate here as "this spot on this map", not "your DB row".
    [DB] on smart_scripts is the Shrine block of the LatincoreBfa line:
    vSLDB sql/updates/world/Shrine of the Storm.sql, 52 rows over 31
    entries. It is the same lineage as the King's Rest SQL used in step 08.

  Coordinates are (x, y, z) in world yards on map 1864. Orientation is
  radians and is quoted only where the row carries one.

  The structural fact that shapes this whole file: every trash mob that
  spawns from the world table is SmartAI, and its timers are readable out
  of the SQL, which is why section 8 can give them at all. The four
  bosses are hand-written. The five summoned helpers - Aqualing, Grasping
  Tentacle, Forgotten Denizen, Sunken Denizen and Manifestation of the
  Deep - are also hand-written, in the boss files, and they also have
  SmartAI rows in the DB that the script AI outranks. A different world DB
  will therefore run different trash, and the helpers are the part to
  check first.

1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS

  1.1 The scripts are Psycho-Core-8.3.7_Scripts, scripts/KulTiras/
      ShrineOfTheStorm: four boss files, instance_shrine_of_the_storm.cpp,
      shrine_of_the_storm.cpp and shrine_of_the_storm.h, 89 KB in total.
      The four bosses run 22 scheduled events between them. Two mechanics
      live outside the boss files: the Stormsong intro and the Slicing
      Hurricane area trigger are both in shrine_of_the_storm.cpp (8.3).
  1.2 No client table was read. Radii, durations and damage below are
      unverified unless the script, DBM or LittleWigs states them.
  1.3 MDT clone positions were not used. They are stored in MDT's own map
      space, not in world coordinates, so only MDT's names, forces, health
      and levels are cited here.
  1.4 The 8.3 Awakened lieutenants (161124, 161241, 161243, 161244) and the
      season-4 emissaries (155432, 155433, 155434) are in MDT with force
      values and have no spawn row for this map, so they are not counted.

2.4 THE ROUTE: 49 LEGS, EACH WITH ITS DISTANCE AND ITS dz

  This is a distance bookkeeping walk, not a walked route. It was built in
  two passes - every pack whose centroid z is 90 or above, then every pack
  below that - and each pass is a nearest-neighbour tour from the previous
  end point, improved by 2-opt and single-node relocation until neither
  helped. That split is the flood line: the upper temple is one walk and
  the sunken city is entered once. It is the shortest order I could find,
  not the order a group walks, and the map has no stairs data, no mesh and
  no door list to check it against.

2.5 WHAT THE GEOMETRY DOES NOT GIVE
  There is no mesh and no collision extent in any source I could read.
  The only blockers the data names are the 39 gameobject positions in
  3.4 - collision walls, arcane barriers, invisible doors - and those
  rows carry a point, not a width. Apart from them the physical facts are
  the rows themselves: the upper temple is flat to 0.28 yd across the 92
  rows above z 150, and the Council tier is flat to 1.7 yd across the 80
  rows between z 100 and z 120.
  Water is the one terrain feature the data proves: z 61.4 to 86.1 is
  below every other level, 53 rows stand in it, and the route crosses
  that line once. Leg 42 is a 135.6 yd walk that drops 76.7 yd onto
  P43 at z 77.59. Leg 43 then loses another 10.6 yd to P42 at z 66.95,
  the lowest ground in the instance.

8.3 THE TRASH THAT IS NOT SMART SCRIPTED
  Five summoned types run hand-written AI in the boss files. Their DB
  blocks are printed in 8.2 for completeness, but on TrinityCore a
  creature_template ScriptName outranks AIName='SmartAI', so the C++ is
  what runs. That precedence is taken from TrinityCore's own AI init;
  whether the 8.3.7 core orders it the same way is unverified.
  Aqualing 134828 (Aqu'sirr's intermission adds) - bfa_npc_aqualing:
    rooted on creation, applies 268899 Diminish, and casts Sea Blast
    265001 every 2.0 s on its victim if the victim is out of melee range.
    The DB block for it (8.2) reads Sea Blast 0 s then every 3.0-3.5 s,
    Undertow 264144 every 12-15 s, Surging Rush 264101 at 10 s then every
    12-15 s and Choking Brine 264560 every 5-15 s - so if the precedence
    is the other way round on your core, the intermission adds do a very
    different fight.
  Grasping Tentacle 134612 - rooted; its only code is that on death it
    strips 264526 from every player.
  Forgotten Denizen 136297 and Sunken Denizen 136100/136295:
    display 68244, damage gating per 7.4b. Forgotten Denizens are rooted,
    hit for melee and cast Consume Essence 267809 on the victim at 3.0 s
    then every 11.0 s. Both denizen ids carry one on-spawn SmartAI row
    that applies Leviathan's Wake 267548 to themselves; 136295 also gets
    it from its creature_template_addon, and the C++ sunken denizen AI
    applies it again in its constructor.
  Manifestation of the Deep 135903 - chases Vol'zith and erupts at 1 yd;
    its DB row is Abyssal Eruption 267310 on death.
  Awoken Void 137036 - see 6.4c.

10. DISCREPANCIES AND TRAPS
================================================================================
10.1 ALL FOUR ARENA EDGE CHECKS TEST THE BOSS, NOT THE PLAYER. Aqu'sirr
     checks both (4.1) and is the only one that works. Stormsong (6.1),
     both council bosses (5.1) and Vol'zith (7.1) compare the BOSS's
     distance to the anchor point and then kill the player. A group can
     stand outside the intended arena forever unless the boss is kited out.
10.2 AQU'SIRR IS SET TO 15% HEALTH WHEN THE INTERMISSION ENDS. ReturnCombat
     calls SetHealth(CountPctFromMaxHealth(15)), so the last 15% is all
     that is left after the adds die regardless of what the boss was on at
     50%. A bot that has a burn phase planned at 20% should know the
     floor is 15%.
10.3 GRASPING TENTACLES IS DEAD CODE. EVENT_GRASPING_TENTACLES is gated on
     IsHeroic() && IsMythic(), which is impossible; 264477 and 264526 are
     never cast by Aqu'sirr. LittleWigs still bars 264526 at 24.0 s.
10.4 CHOKING BRINE IS A ONE-SHOT. It is scheduled at 11.0 s and the case
     does not re-arm itself; only the post-intermission ReturnCombat does.
     DBM and LittleWigs both treat it as a repeating cooldown.
10.5 SURGING RUSH DELAYS EVERY OTHER EVENT BY 5.0 s each cast. A companion
     that models Undertow as a fixed 30.0 s clock will be 5 s late per
     rush after the second one.
10.6 FIVE SMART_SCRIPTS BLOCKS ARE NOT THIS DUNGEON'S. 137805 Black Blood,
     140149 Released Crawg, 139842 Risen Ravasaur and 143520 Vanquished
     Tendril of G'huun have rows for creatures that do not spawn on map
     1864. The fifth is labelled Lord Stormsong and sets @ENTRY := 268347,
     the spell id of Void Bolt, so it scripts no creature at all - the
     rows a reader would want for the 139737 intro actor (Void Bolt,
     Ancient Mindbender, Waken the Void) are sitting under a spell id.
10.7 VOL'ZITH'S CALL THE ABYSS PICKS urand(0, 15) FROM A 15-ELEMENT ARRAY.
     One draw in sixteen reads past the end of manifestSpawnPoints.
10.8 VOL'ZITH'S PHASE RESET CHECK IS BROKEN. checkSunkenPhase overwrites its
     own alive flag for every player and reacts to the last one, so a
     group can be evaded out of a live phase.
10.9 STORMSONG HAS NO HEALTH PHASES AND NO INTERMISSION despite DBM's
     commented-out "could be health based" notes; on this core Waken the
     Void is a flat 30.0 s timer.
10.10 THE COUNCIL'S WARDS ARE MANA-DRIVEN, NOT TIMED. Two bosses at +1000
     power per 2.0 s means one ward each, first at 21.0 s and every 20.0 s
     after, but any change to the regen rate moves every ward, while DBM's
     21.3, 10.9, 30.2 and 36.4 come from the live client.
10.11 THE INSTANCE BINDS BY MAP ID, NOT BY NAME. instance_template carries
     'instance_shrine_of_the_storm' for map 1864, but the C++ binds with
     RegisterInstanceScript(instance_shrine_of_the_storm, 1864), so the
     name string only has to exist for a human reader.
10.12 THIS IS NOT THE 8.3.7 SPAWN DB. See the note under section 0: the
     creature rows are TrinityCore master for map 1864, and the force
     column does not sum to MDT's 695.
10.13 THE SCRIPT NAMES IN THE 8.x SQL DO NOT CARRY THE bfa_ PREFIX. The
     C++ registers 'bfa_boss_aqusirr', 'bfa_boss_volzith_the_whisperer',
     'bfa_npc_aqualing' and the rest. The 8.x world file I could read sets
     'boss_aquasirr' (note also the misspelling), 'boss_volzith',
     'npc_aqualing'. If that is what your creature_template holds, no boss
     AI in this file runs at all. Which spelling the 8.3.7 database uses
     is unverified and it is the first thing to check before trusting any
     timer here.
10.14 VOL'ZITH HAS SIX DENIZENS, NOT THREE. The three phase positions are
     also standing spawn rows: 136297 twice and 136295 once. The script
     then summons 136297 twice and 136100 once at the same three points,
     so a Grasp produces two mobs on the first two points and a third plus
     a static on the third. The phase counter needs three deaths.
10.15 THE DB AND THE C++ DISAGREE ON CONSUME ESSENCE. The SmartAI row for
     136297 casts 267459 on itself every 12-15 s; the C++ casts 267809 on
     the victim at 3.0 s then every 11.0 s. Only one of the two runs,
     depending on the ScriptName precedence in 10.13.
10.16 THE THIRD POINT'S STATIC MOB IS NOT THE SCRIPTED ONE. The 8.x world
     file gives ScriptName 'npc_sunken_denizen' to 136100 only; 136295
     gets no ScriptName, so a static row there is an ordinary SmartAI mob
     with Leviathan's Wake, while the summoned 136100 is the damage-gated
     tank-phase add.

11. WHAT IS NOT IN THIS FILE, AND WHY
================================================================================
11.1 No radius, duration, cooldown or damage figure is quoted from a client
     table. Every number is a script literal, a DB value, an addon timer
     or arithmetic on those.
11.2 Tank positions are unverified for all four encounters. No script in
     the instance carries a tank constant, a facing requirement or a taunt
     swap.
11.3 Line of sight is unverified everywhere. The 39 gameobject rows in
     3.4 give collision walls, barriers and doors as POINTS, with no
     extent, so they narrow the question without answering it, and 2.4 is
     distance bookkeeping and not a walk.
11.4 The route order is a shortest-path heuristic, not the path a group
     walks; the five legs over 100 yd are floor changes, not corridors.
11.5 Whether the 8.3.7 world DB spawns the same 295 combat rows as the
     TrinityCore master DB is unverified. Re-run the clustering against
     your own creature table before trusting a pull centroid.
11.6 The Tidesage Council's tank-swap point is unverified: Hindering Cleave
     has no stack limit in the script.
11.7 The Awoken Void's and the Aqualing's movement speeds are script
     literals (0.8 and rooted) but whether the client honours them is
     unverified.
11.8 No item id is quoted. The only loot SQL I found for this instance
     (2020_08_24_ShrineoftheStormCreatureLoot.sql) covers four entries
     and no boss.
11.9 Nothing here was taken from a video, a guide site or a forum post.
