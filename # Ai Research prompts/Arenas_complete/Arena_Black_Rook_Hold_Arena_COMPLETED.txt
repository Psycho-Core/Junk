Radius is measured from the midpoint of the two starts; it runs
from 32.3 to 56.4 units, so these are not two symmetric gates.

Open behaviour, verified in the zone file:
  The close event spawns every object from door 1 to the end of
  the array, which includes the buffs and the anvil.
  The open event runs a loop that stops before door 7, so only
  doors 1 to 6 are ever opened. Doors 7, 8 and 9 - entries
  245762, 245765 and 245769 - are spawned shut and are never
  opened by the core.
  Whether those three are meant to stay shut, or are a loop
  off-by-three, is unverified. They are close to the centre
  (33.7, 39.9 and 53.2 units out) so it matters for pathing.

=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

One central pillar: the statue of Black Rook Hold. Layout:

  "The map features a statue in the centre of the map that
   functions as a pillar. At the base of the statue is a slightly
   raised, circular platform. Stairs surround most of the
   platform, with a higher platform in front of the statue
   completing the circle. Another feature of this map is that the
   rooms that each team enters from are quite large. These rooms
   remain accessible after the gates have opened."
   (Liquipedia)

  Notable features listed by the same page: "One Pillar", "Two
  Large Rooms", "Platform".

Pillar count: 1, plus the raised platform that shares its base
Pillar position: unverified. The statue itself is not a server
  object; the server spawns only doors, the two orbs and the
  anvil
Platform position: unverified, but the arena graveyard at
  1419.42, 1255.49, 34.1073 sits 6.43 units from the midpoint of
  the starts and 0.84 above the door band, which matches the
  "slightly raised, circular platform" at the statue's base
The two entry rooms are the only large LoS structures behind
  each spawn and they stay open after the gates open

The distinctive feature against the other arenas: the cover here
is in the middle, not at the rim. The two entry rooms are the
only deep LoS behind the spawns.

=== 4. HEIGHT LEVELS AND RAMPS ===

Two levels plus the entry rooms.

  Arena floor (door band):      33.21 to 33.27
  Team 2 start:                 32.1104
  Team 1 start:                 33.0047
  Central platform (graveyard): 34.1073

So the floor the doors sit on is 33.21 to 33.27, the spawn
shelves are slightly below it (32.1104 and 33.0047), and the
central platform is 0.84 to 1.10 above the floor. That is the
"slightly raised" platform; the step is under 1.2 units.

Ramps and stairs: the platform is reached by stairs that surround
most of it, with a higher platform in front of the statue
completing the circle. That much is documented. Stair count,
width, direction and the height of the higher platform are
unverified; no readable source and no server data give them.

No second floor. The two entry rooms are on the same level as
the floor they lead from.

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

The walk from spawn to the centre is 52.19 units, and the cover
is in the middle, so both teams arrive at the statue at the same
time. The two entry rooms stay open, which makes them a retreat
rather than a place to hold at the start.

Melee DPS:
  Go over the central platform edge rather than around it. The
  statue itself is the only real pillar, so whoever holds the
  statue side controls the first exchange
  Do not get pushed into the open ring between the statue and
  the doors; there is no cover there

Ranged DPS:
  Take the raised platform and cast over the stairs. The step is
  small but it is the only elevation on the map
  Because the rooms behind each spawn stay open, an escape into
  your own room is always available

Healers:
  The centre platform is the safest ground, and the graveyard is
  on it, so a death near the middle returns you close to the
  fight
  The two large rooms behind the spawns give a deep LoS reset if
  the platform is lost

General opener notes:
  Both teams are 52.19 from the midpoint and the axis is 15.54 deg
  off X, so the opening paths are close to head-on
  The gate ring is not symmetric (radii 32.3 to 56.4), so the
  distance from each spawn to its first door differs

=== 7. MATCH FLOW NOTES ===

Gates close before the start and the first six open together.
This zone does not override the world state list, so unlike
Tol'viron, Tiger's Peak and Ashamane's Fall it does not send
worldstate 0xE1A. It only sends the base arena alive-player
world states from Arena::FillInitialWorldStates.
Dampening applies like every other arena.
Arena Preparation is applied on zone-in.
No out-of-bounds or under-map return points are wired in the BfA
core for this map, and there are no area-trigger overrides.

Known consequence of the door loop, from section 2: three door
objects stay shut for the whole match. If a private server finds
players stuck against an invisible or closed gate near the
centre, that loop is the first thing to check.
