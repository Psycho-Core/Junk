RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
29 claims: 26 PASS, 1 FIXED, 2 UNVERIFIED. One edit: the Kun-Lai Serpent's
Spine entry changed from 63497 to 65863 with an explanatory master note. No
coordinate, link, travel time, faction or gate value changed, and the block
count is unchanged at 51.

What was re-read rather than trusted:
  - all 51 positions, bit numbers, link sets and travel times were re-parsed
    from the section-870 text and compared with the file;
  - the eight wiki-resolved masters were re-checked against their NPC pages,
    and two of them (Tiper Windman, Morla Skyblade) were cross-checked against
    the export's link sets, which is what proved the 1117 error;
  - the two gates, the quest behind each gate, the faction counts and the
    258-row link total were recounted.
Not re-read: the 8.3.7 TaxiNodes export itself (section 870 of the research
document is the transcription used here, as in steps 01 to 04).
