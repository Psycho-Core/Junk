=== 4. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Melee DPS:
  Start: On ground level at team start position
  Common opening: Rush up nearest ramp to bridge for height advantage
  Or: Stay ground level to kite under bridge pillars

Ranged DPS:
  Start: At team start position (ground level)
  Common opening: Move up ramp to bridge for LoS advantage and safety
  Bridge center provides best LoS coverage

Healers:
  Start: At team start position (ground level)
  Common opening: Follow melee up ramp, position on bridge near pillar for LoS
  Alternative: Stay ground level behind SE/NW pillars for maximum LoS breaking

General strategy (from guides):
  "Control of the bridge creates height advantage for
   ranged damage and healer positioning"
  "Falling off the bridge does not kill you but does
   create gaps in your positioning"
  "Caster-heavy teams with crowd control chains use
   the bridge for safe freecast windows"
  "Melee teams often fight on ground level using
   pillars for LoS"

=== 5. BUFF / HEALTH PICKUPS ===

Note: Unlike some modern arena maps (e.g., Glade Arena
has mirrored heal/haste pickups), Blade's Edge Arena in
8.3.7 does NOT have arena power-up pickups (healing/
haste orbs) in the instanced arena. The "Arena
Preparation" buff is applied on zone-in (restores
health/mana, removes short buffs).

Outdoor world version (Circle of Blood in Blade's Edge Mountains):
  Has an Arena Vendor (Meminnie, Goblin female) at [52.7, 41.4] zone coords
  No health/haste pickups in instanced arena

The Crowd Chose You (dampening mechanic after 15 min):
  Applied automatically based on team alive count / lowest enemy health
  Not a physical pickup - server-awarded buff

=== 6. ADDITIONAL NOTES ===

Arena Layout Summary:
- Rectangular arena, bridge spans E-W across center
- Two pillars under bridge (SE and NW) with rope walkways to bridge
- 6 ramps: 4 corners + 2 center
- Center pit under bridge with 4 small pillars
- Starting areas on opposite corners (ground level)

Stealth Detection Crystals (historical, TBC):
  Located on top of the two main pillars
  Removed in later patches

Outdoor Access:
  Circle of Blood accessible in open world (Blade's Edge Mountains)
  Coordinates: [69, 35] zone coords from Zangarmarsh cave
  Serves as PvA (PvP Arena) in outdoor world

Performance Issues:

(players falling through floor)
