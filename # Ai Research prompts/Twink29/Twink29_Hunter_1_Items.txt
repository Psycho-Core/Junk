0. THE POOL IS SEVEN PIECES, AND THREE SLOTS ARE EMPTY
   The filtered mail search at rare quality, required level 26-29 and item
   level 35 or less returns 15 rows. Eight of them are class-locked to
   Paladin or Warrior (20108, 20111, 20126, 20129, 20178, 20182, 20207,
   20210), the rest are the Hunter/Shaman battleground belt and boots and
   one more, and what is left for a hunter is seven items.
   There is no rare mail shoulder, wrist or leg in that window at all.
   Those three slots take an heirloom, a green, or nothing. That is the
   single most important fact about gearing a 29 hunter in this rule set.
   The Arathi Basin belt and boots are the one exception, and they are
   flagged as such below.

4. WHAT THE RULE COSTS, IN ONE BREATH
   Three head pieces and the weapon slots are healthy; the armour is not.
   Seven wearable mail pieces at this tier means the average bot hunter is
   an heirloom shoulder, an heirloom chest or Avenger's Armor, one of two
   helms, Grubbis Paws, one of two belts, one of two boots, and greens in
   the wrist and leg slots. Weights, necks and rings are the same problem
   the druid set hit: 13087 and 13097 are the only two verified pieces in
   the whole window.
   Not fixed by any of this: heirloom legs were not verified (no id), the
   neck and ring pool beyond those two pieces is unverified, and the
   trinket list stops at the arena chest and the battleground Insignia.
