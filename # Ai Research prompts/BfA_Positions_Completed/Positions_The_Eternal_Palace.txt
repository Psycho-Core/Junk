4. BOSS 1 - ABYSSAL COMMANDER SIVARA
================================================================================
4.1 IDENTITY [TAC]/[DBM]
  journal 2352 | DungeonEncounter 2298 | creature 151881

4.2 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION
  Boss spawn: unverified (world DB).
  Arena: centred on (298.54, 153.91, 1449.97); the script sizes its checks to
  40 yards, so treat the fight area as a 40-yard disc on that centre.
  TANK POSITION: hold her on that centre facing away from the raid. The
  script's own logic guarantees the tank cannot simply stand still: the two
  ground effects are isareatrigger entries 16563 (frost) and 16564 (toxic)
  and the script checks which one a unit is standing in. Exact tank offsets:
  unverified.

4.3 PHASES [TAC]/[GUIDE]
  HEALTH PERCENTAGE: none. The client journal carries a single stage, and no
  guide read here puts a percentage gate on the fight. The fight is a mark and
  ground rotation: Chimeric Marks 294726 assigns Frost Mark 294711 or Toxic
  Brand 294715, Inversion 295791 swaps every mark, and the raid must be
  standing on ground matching its current mark.
  Berserk 360 s [DBM] (the module's own default is 600 s; its combat-start
  value is 360-delay).

4.4 MAIN ABILITY TIMERS, seconds from pull [DBM]
  Crushing Reverberation 295332   first 10.6, cooldown 22.3
  Overwhelming Barrage 296551     first 40, cooldown 40
                                  (journal id 295138 [TAC])
  Overflow 295346                 first 15.7, cooldown 40.1
                                  (Mythic 40, other difficulties 34.4 inside
                                  the module)
  Inversion 295791                first 70 or 90, cooldown 72.9
  Frostshock Bolts 295601         first 50.8, cooldown 60.8
  Chimeric Marks 294726           first 23, cooldown 22.8

4.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Chimeric Marks 294726 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: the cast. Action: read the new mark and move to matching ground
     (Frost 294711 -> frost ground, Toxic 294715 -> toxic ground).
  b) Inversion 295791 - assigned to EVERYBODY, and it is the reset.
     Trigger: the cast. Action: every mark flips, so every unit re-reads its
     colour and swaps sides; a unit caught in the wrong ground takes Frozen
     295807 or Venomous Blood 295796.
  c) Overwhelming Barrage 295138 - assigned to the TANK.
     Trigger: the tank-targeted cast. Action: keep her turned away, use a
     cooldown, and do not let it point into the raid.
  d) Frostshock Bolts 295601 - assigned to the KICK ROTATION.
     Trigger: SPELL_CAST_START of 295601, plus Frost Bolt 295704 and Toxic
     Bolt 295705.
  e) Crushing Reverberation 295332 - assigned to NOBODY, dodged.
  f) Overflow 295346 / Overflowing Chill 295348 / Overflowing Venom 295421 -
     assigned to the HEALERS; the stacks are the real damage of the fight.
  g) Unstable Mixture 294847 and Delirious 295850 - the failure states of
     standing in the wrong ground; no assignment, just avoidance.

4.6 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified: the 40-yard disc around (298.54, 153.91, 1449.97) is the arena the
  script checks against.
  The frost and toxic grounds are cast by areatrigger entries 16563 and 16564,
  so the safe spot is defined by which trigger a unit is standing in, not by a
  coordinate this file can give -> unverified as points.
  Practical rule from the mechanics: half the raid must be standing in frost
  ground and half in toxic ground after every Inversion, and no unit may stand
  in the ground of the other colour.

5. BOSS 2 - RADIANCE OF AZSHARA
================================================================================
5.1 IDENTITY [TAC]/[DBM]
  journal 2353 | DungeonEncounter 2305 | creature 152364

5.2 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION
  Boss spawn: unverified. Arena centre (772.46, 638.60, 1290.00), script
  radius note "//100y" - the platform is 100 yards across in that note.
  TANK POSITION: she is stationary (the script disables combat movement with
  SetCombatMovement(false) and drives her by power, not by positioning), so
  the tank holds her at the centre and the raid moves around her. Tide Fist
  296566 is the tank hit and Unshackled Power 296894 is the soft enrage.

5.3 PHASES [GUIDE] - energy, not health
  HEALTH PERCENTAGE: none.
  Phase One lasts 100 seconds, i.e. until her energy reaches 100%, then
  Ancient Tempest 295916 is cast and Phase Two begins; Phase Two lasts until
  the Stormwraith is killed, and then Phase One restarts. The cycle repeats
  until she dies (Icy Veins, both the fight-overview and the ability text).
  In Phase Two she takes 99% reduced damage and only keeps casting Arcane Bomb
  296746 (Icy Veins).
  Berserk 540 s Mythic / 720 s otherwise [DBM].

5.4 MAIN ABILITY TIMERS, seconds from pull [DBM]
  Tide Fist 296546               first 15 or 18, cooldown 58.2
                                 (journal id 296566 [TAC])
  Arcanado Burst 296430          first 6 or 9, cooldown 58.2
                                 (journal id 296449)
  Arcane Bomb 296737             first 7 or 10, cooldown 58.2
                                 (journal id 296746)
  Unshackled Power 296894        first 10 or 13, cooldown 58.2 same id
  Ancient Tempest 295916         first 95.6 or 98.8, cooldown 95.9
  Gale Buffet 304098             first 11, cooldown 22.6
                                 (journal ids 296701 / 304098)

5.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Arcane Bomb 296746 - assigned to the DISPELLER and to the marked player.
     Trigger: aura; the module warns on the player. Action: run to the edge of
     the room and dispel there, never in the stack (burstjames: "drop at edge
     of room and call for dispel"; Icy Veins treats it as the only dispel).
  b) Tide Fist 296566 - assigned to the TANK (taunt swap on stacks).
  c) Focus Power 304951 - assigned to the KICK ROTATION.
     Trigger: the Stormwraith's cast. Action: interrupt it, then kill the
     Stormwraith; Phase Two ends when it dies.
  d) Chain Lightning 296673 - assigned to the KICK ROTATION (Stormlings).
  e) Squall Trap 296462 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: a line of traps forms across the platform (the three lines in
     3.2). Action: cross through a gap; touching one detonates it and spawns
     an Arcanado 296421.
  f) Gale Buffet 296701 - assigned to EVERYBODY; it pushes units, so the raid
     keeps its back to a wall and never to the open platform edge.
  g) Unshackled Power 296894 - assigned to the HEALERS; it is the soft enrage.
  h) Swirling Winds 296389 / Radiant Fury 299093 - assigned to NOBODY, dodged.

5.6 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified: the platform centre (772.46, 638.60, 1290.00) and the three Squall
  Trap lines in 3.2. The safe spot during Phase Two is the Eye of the Storm
  where the Stormwraith stands; the script does not hard-code it, so its
  coordinate is unverified.
  Because the Squall Traps are the only fixed furniture on the platform, a bot
  can pre-plan the three crossings: pattern 1 runs west-to-east along y ~622,
  pattern 2 runs north-to-south at x ~768, pattern 3 runs north-to-south at
  x ~727. Cross perpendicular to the line, not along it.

  the guides describe as one continuous fight.
  Berserk 545 s on Heroic/Mythic ("IsHard") and 720 s otherwise [DBM].

6.4 MAIN ABILITY TIMERS, seconds from pull [DBM]
  Toxic Spine 292167             first 5.4 (11 or 20 later), cooldown 20

  Next-phase timer               100 s (the module's own countdown to the

6.5 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Cavitation 292084 - assigned to the WHOLE RAID as an interrupt.
     Trigger: the 32 s channel at the start of a new platform. Action: swim
     down the Slipstream 301180 / 305085 currents and interrupt before it
     finishes, or it pulses heavy raid damage every 2 s.
  b) Bioelectric Feelers 292247 - assigned to EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: a scan every 4 s; any two units within 5 yards are hit.
     Action: spread, and spread vertically: the fight is in open water, so
     depth counts (tanknotes).
  c) Radiant Biomass 292138 / Feeding Frenzy 298424 - assigned to the ADD

     Action: never let a Shimmerskin Pufferfish 150773 be eaten; kill it away.
  d) Bioluminescence 292133 - assigned to the RAID (buff pickup).
     Trigger: killing a pufferfish leaves a Bioluminescent Cloud 292205.
     Action: swim through it for the buff; tanks get the same effect from
     Radiant Biomass on every swing.
  e) Glowing Stinger 298595 / 305404 - assigned to NOBODY, dodged (jellyfish).
  f) Slipstream 301180 / 305085 - assigned to EVERYBODY during a transition;
     it is the only way to cross the 287.8-481.0 yard gaps in time.

6.6 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified: the three platforms (3.3) and the two jellyfish spawn points.
  Platform 1 is a single disc; platforms 2 and 3 have a visible gap between the
  melee and ranged parts (tanknotes), and the script's arrival checks
  (3.3) encode roughly where the raid is expected to surface.
  There is no ground to stand on between platforms: the transition is swum
  through Slipstreams, and any coordinate between the platform constants is
  open water -> not a safe spot.

7.3 PHASES [GUIDE] - shield phasing, not health percentage
  HEALTH PERCENTAGE: none, and this is worth stating plainly: Ashvane's two
  stages cycle on her damage-absorption shield, not on her health.
  Stage One: Hardened Carapace 296650 absorbs a share of her maximum health -
  30% in the Wowhead journal text, 40% in tanknotes and mmotip - and each time
  it regrows it is 150% stronger. Stage Two: Exposed Azerite 297206 begins
  when the shield breaks and lasts until her energy reaches 100, about 60
  seconds (Icy Veins), then the shield returns.
  The DBM module carries a 70.5 s shield timer [DBM].
  Because of that cycle there is no single figure like "phase two at 70%" that
  is true for the whole fight; the one hard number that recurs in guides is
  that the first shield break lands around 70-75% of her health (mmotip).
  Berserk 505 s Mythic / 559 s Heroic [DBM].
