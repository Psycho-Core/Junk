TWINK29 - WARLOCK - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

0. WHAT A LEVEL 29 HAS
   Normal talents: seven rows, unlocked at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and 100.
   A level 29 character owns the first row only, one point.
   PvP talents: three slots at 20, 40 and 70. A 29 owns the first slot
   only, and even then a PvP talent is legal only if its own level is 20
   or lower.
   So the answer for each spec is small: one level 15 talent, and one PvP
   talent if a legal one exists.
   V = read off the talent's wiki page including the patch line that states
   its BfA level. A current tooltip is not evidence: several of these
   talents moved rows after 8.3.7, and three of the six named below changed
   level between Battle for Azeroth and 9.0.1, which is the whole trap.

1. AFFLICTION
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Nightfall, spell id unverified. Passive. Corruption's damage has a
     chance to make your next Shadow Bolt instant and hit harder. 8.0.1
     re-added it as a level 15 Affliction talent, and 8.3.0 raised the
     damage bonus from 25% to 50% and the chance to trigger by 33% (V), so
     50% is the 8.3.7 number. The current tooltip also says Drain Soul
     channels 50% faster while affected; whether 8.3.7 had that half is
     unverified.
   TRAP  Writhe in Agony is not available to a 29. 8.0.1 moved it to a
     level 30 talent, and it only came back down to 15 in 9.0.1 (V). This
     is the Affliction talent every current guide lists first, and it is
     one level out of reach here.
   THE THIRD TALENT of the row was not read for this file, so it is not
     named. One legal option of the row is what the sources support.
   PICK  Nightfall. It is passive, it fires off Corruption, and a bot needs
     no instruction to get value from it.
   PVP  see section 4.

2. DEMONOLOGY
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Dreadlash, spell 264078. Passive. When your Dreadstalkers charge in,
     their Dreadbite hits all targets within 8 yards. It was added in 8.0.1
     as a level 15 Demonology talent (V); the 10% damage bonus on the same
     page is a 9.0.5 change and was not in 8.3.7.
   THE OTHER TALENTS of the row were not read, and the file does not name
     them. Demonology's row is the one most likely to have moved level
     between 8.0 and 8.3, which is why the patch line matters more here
     than anywhere else in the file.
   PICK  Dreadlash. It upgrades a spell a Demonology bot is casting anyway,
     and the cleave is worth more in a battleground than a single target
     line.
   PVP  see section 4.

3. DESTRUCTION
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Flashover, spell 267115. Passive. Conflagrate deals 25% more damage
     and grants an additional charge of Backdraft (V). Added in 8.0.1 as a
     level 15 Destruction talent and removed in 10.0.0; no patch in between
     changed the 25%, so 25% is the 8.3.7 number.
   TRAP  Eradication is not available to a 29. 9.0.1 says "Now available at
     level 15 (was 30)" (V), so in 8.3.7 it was a level 30 talent. Its BfA
     damage bonus was 10%, cut from 15% in 8.0.1, which is the number a
     current page will not show.
   THE THIRD TALENT of the row was not read.
   PICK  Flashover. An extra Backdraft charge is extra free cast speed, and
     a Destruction bot spends Conflagrate on cooldown.
   PVP  see section 4.

4. THE PVP SLOT AT LEVEL 20
   LEGAL AND VERIFIED
     Nether Ward, spell 212295, level 20, 45 sec cooldown. Surrounds the
     caster with a shield lasting 3 sec that reflects all harmful spells
     cast on you. 8.0.1 converted it from a rank 40 honor talent and no
     later patch changed its level (V), so 20 is right for 8.3.7 too.
     Note the page contradicts itself on the duration: the ability line says
     3 sec and the buff it applies says 5 sec. Both are printed here
     because neither was resolved.
   WHY IT IS THE PICK  A 29 warlock's problem is the same as a 29 priest's:
     a fear or a stun lands and the character stops playing. Nether Ward is
     not a break, it is a reflect, so it has to be up before the cast
     rather than after - which for a computer-controlled companion means a
     scripted pre-cast, not a reaction.
   NOT VERIFIED
     Rapid Contagion, Call Fel Lord, Bane of Havoc, Jinx, Impish Instincts,
     Gateway Mastery, Soul Swap and Shadow Rift are all warlock PvP talents
     that existed in some part of BfA, but none of their BfA levels was read
     for this file. Any of them may be legal at 20; the file does not claim
     it either way, and Nether Ward is the only one it can stand behind.
   THE PATTERN TO REMEMBER  Psyfiend for priests was level 40 in BfA and
     became 20 only in 9.0.1. Warlock PvP talents went through the same
     renumbering, so a current list of "level 20 options" is a 9.0 or later
     list, not a BfA one.

5. THE THREE LOADOUTS
   AFFLICTION  Nightfall. PvP Nether Ward.
   DEMONOLOGY  Dreadlash. PvP Nether Ward.
   DESTRUCTION  Flashover. PvP Nether Ward.
   The three damage specs have one verified level 15 talent each and they
   share the one verified PvP talent, so in practice the choice at 29 is
   made by the spec, not by the player.

6. WHAT IS SOLID AND WHAT IS NOT
   Solid: the row and slot gating, one level 15 talent per spec with its
   8.3.7 numbers (Nightfall 50%, Dreadlash cleave with no damage bonus,
   Flashover 25%), Nether Ward at 20, and the two traps (Writhe in Agony
   level 30, Eradication level 30).
   Not solid: the third talent of every row, the eight other PvP talents'
   BfA levels, the Nightfall Drain Soul half, and every spell id in the
   file except 264078 (Dreadlash), 267115 (Flashover) and 212295 (Nether
   Ward), which are the three links a 8.x-era id was printed for. Nightfall
   and all eight unread PvP talents carry no id, which is deliberate.
