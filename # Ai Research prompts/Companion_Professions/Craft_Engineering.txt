BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - ENGINEERING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
=============================================================================================

Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client) levels Engineering on a
TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Every id below was read from a source this
week; anything without a readable source says "unverified" instead of a guess.

1. THE SKILL LINES AND HOW A COMPANION LEARNS THEM
---------------------------------------------------

Learn Skill (Engineering, skill=202, value 1); wowhead infobox "Training
cost: 10"; text "Allows an engineer to make basic contraptions. Requires
stone and metal found with the Mining skill". The page shows "Taught by
(49)" (list not rendered) and "Other ranks (9)": 158739 (1), 195112 (1),
4037 (50), 4038 (125), 12656 (200), 30350 (275), 51306 (350), 82774 (425),

Learn Spell 4036, Effect2 Learn Skill Step (202) value 1. The Classic main
page's See-also list adds 49383 to the same family.

7. WHAT THE CORE MUST DO FOR A CRAFT TO SUCCEED
-----------------------------------------------
Verified against TrinityCore master fetched this week (blobless sparse
clone; paths under src/server/game/). Line numbers are from this fetch;
upstream moves - section 9 item 15 records the drift against the
Enchanting file.
a) Learn press: NPCHandler.cpp 97 HandleTrainerListOpcode -> 112
   SendTrainerList -> 130 HandleTrainerBuySpellOpcode -> 145
   interaction guard -> 155 trainer->TeachSpell; Trainer.cpp 76
   TeachSpell -> 79 CanTeachSpell -> 149 CanTeachSpell body: state must
   be Available (172 GetSpellState: known check, race/class, skill gate
   181-183, prerequisite spells 185-187, level 189-191, rank-chain
   logic), free primary profession point (156/165), money at 100-106

   LearnSpell. This is how 4036, the apprentices wrapper 4039, the eight

   (wowhead samples 3918 and 255392 both print Effect: Create Item;
   Stealthman 54 prints Create Item x5). TC path: Spell::cast takes
   reagents at Spell.cpp 3872/3877 TakeReagents() (definition 5600);
   DoCreateItem SpellEffects.cpp 1383 handles the item grant, reports
   inventory full at 1440 (EQUIP_ERR_INV_FULL || EQUIP_ERR_ITEM_MAX_COUNT
   -> SendEquipError), and grants skill at 1469 (`if (!m_CastItem)
   player->UpdateCraftSkill(m_spellInfo);`).

   Gearspring Parts yields 8 (guide prints), Stealthman yields 5

   (EffectCreateItem2 SpellEffects.cpp 1482, 1495-1497 AutoStoreLoot,
   second AutoStoreLoot at 1522) - that is the server mechanism for
   random amounts; item-cast crafts also grant skill through Spell.cpp
   3864 (SPELL_ATTR9_ITEM_CAST_GRANTS_SKILL_GAIN).
d) Tinker recipes are ENCHANT-ITEM crafts, not Create Item: sample
   Nitro Boosts 55016 prints "Requires Belt", Tools: Arclight Spanner,

   your belt", Effect Enchant Item: Nitro Boosts (4223), flag "Target

   UpdateCraftSkill(m_spellInfo);` - direct casts grant skill, item-cast

   (no engineering vellum in the guide).
