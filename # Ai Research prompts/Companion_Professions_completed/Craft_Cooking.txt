BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - COOKING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
=========================================================================================
Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client, no human at the keyboard) levels
Cooking on a TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Everything below was read from a source
fetched this week (2026-10-27); anything without a readable source says "unverified"
instead of being guessed. Craft counts are exactly the counts the linked guides print
(they warn that yellow/green recipes and lucky discoveries change the real number -
those warnings are kept where printed).

1. THE SKILL LINES AND HOW A COMPANION LEARNS THEM
--------------------------------------------------
Base spell: Cooking 2550 - Training cost: 10, Difficulty: 111, Skill: Cooking
(skill=185), Effect #1 Trade Skill, Effect #2 Learn Skill (Cooking, skill=185)
Value 1; description "Allows a cook to make basic recipes. Requires meat from
beasts and fire to make food." Related lists Other ranks (9) and Taught by (66).
The nine other ranks read:
  - 158765 (skill 1), 3102 (50), 3413 (125), 18260 (200), 33359 (275), 51296
    (350), 88053 (425), 104381 (500), 195128 (700).

where the Tailoring block ended (264631 = Zandalari Tailoring wrapper) and ending
before a verified non-Cooking spell (264648 = "Order Blast", an 8.0.1 server-side
Dummy for the A.F.M.O.D.), plus the Horde pair 265817/265818. Every id inside was
probed: there is NO internal gap. Eight main/wrapper pairs:

unverified), quest 31302 "Ready for Greatness" (make 5 Rice Pudding - spell id

Sungshin: 31479 Way of the Brew (deliver 5 Ginseng to Bobo Ironpaw 58717),
31311 Way of the Grill (Kol Ironpaw), 31478 Way of the Oven (Jian Ironpaw),

Ironpaw), 31470 Way of the Wok (Anthea Ironpaw). "You gain cooking skill in
each Way separately. Your cooking skill in general is equal to your highest
skill in any Way" - one Way to 75 is enough (section 3.5 uses the cheapest).
