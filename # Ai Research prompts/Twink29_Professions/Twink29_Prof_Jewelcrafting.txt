TWINK29 - PROFESSIONS - STEP 07 - JEWELCRAFTING
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Scope: what Jewelcrafting gives a level 29 battleground twink, skill line
755, capped at 225 for a character of that level.

1. THE FIRST THING THAT HAS TO BE SAID
   Jewelcrafting did not exist in vanilla. It was added in patch 2.0, so
   there is no Classic-tier jewelcrafting at all - the skill line every
   other file in this set uses (164, 165, 197, 333...) has no jewelcrafting
   equivalent. In 8.3.7 the profession is skill line 755, and what this file
   calls the low band is the Outland line's own 1-300 stretch, which still
   crafts from old-world ore and gems.
   The rank shape is different from the other professions. From the rank
   spells themselves: apprentice 25229 (cap 75), journeyman 25230 at skill
   50, expert 28894 at skill 125, artisan 28895 at skill 200, master 28897
   at skill 275. Journeyman arrives at 50 here, not 75.
   For a 29 that means: artisan is reachable, master is not, so 225 is the
   ceiling exactly as with the other professions - by a different gate.
   The level requirement to learn apprentice jewelcrafting is not on the
   spell page, so whether a level 5 can train it is unverified; the level 29
   in question can.
   Tools: a Jeweler's Kit 20815 is required by every recipe in this file.

2. WHAT A 29 CAN ACTUALLY WEAR
   Jewelcrafting is the only profession in the set that makes wearable
   stat gear in the 1-225 band, and the gear is good for its level. Five
   pieces fit a 29; one of them is the best item any profession in this set
   produces for a twink.

   20950 Pendant of the Agate Shield - neck, item level 31, requires level
        26, binds when equipped.
        +4 Strength, +4 Stamina, Equip: +6 defense rating.
        Use: gives 10 additional Stamina to nearby party members for 30
        minutes. 10 charges.
        Spell 25610, Jewelcrafting 120, difficulty 120/150/165/180.
        Reagents per craft: 1x Moss Agate 1206 + 1x Bronze Setting.
        Design 20970 is NOT trainer-taught: Horde buy it from Jandia 4877 in
        Thousand Needles (zone 400) at about 46.0, 51.6; Alliance from Neal
        Allen 1448 in the Wetlands (zone 11) at about 10.5, 56.8. Both are
        limited supply, so it may need several visits or the auction house.
        This is the one jewelcrafting item worth levelling the profession
        for: an equippable neck with strength, stamina and defense, plus a
        ten-charge group stamina buff that works on companion characters.

   20828 Ring of Twilight Shadows - finger, item level 28, requires level
        23, binds when equipped, unique-equipped.
        +4 Stamina, +4 Intellect.
        Spell 25318, Jewelcrafting 100, difficulty 100/130/145/160.
        2x Shadowgem 1210 + 2x Bronze Bar 2841.
        The cheapest +8 stat points a 29 will ever slot, and it needs only
        skill 100.

   20820 Simple Pearl Ring - finger, item level 22, requires level 17,
        binds when equipped.
        Spell 25284, Jewelcrafting 80. One Small Lustrous Pearl 5498,
        one Bronze Setting, two Copper Bar 2840.

   21931 Woven Copper Ring - finger, item level 15, requires level 10,
        binds when equipped, unique-equipped. +1 Intellect.
        Spell 26925, Jewelcrafting 30.

   21932 Heavy Copper Ring - finger, item level 17, requires level 12,
        binds when equipped, unique-equipped. +2 Stamina.
        Spell 26926, Jewelcrafting 5.
        Reagent names were not read for these last two - the pages give the
        counts only - so their material lists are unverified.

   Out of reach, all of them ALT:
   20960 Engraved Truesilver Ring - item level 37, requires level 32.
        Spell 25620, skill 185.
   20961 Citrine Ring of Rapid Healing - item level 38, requires level 33.
        Spell 25621, skill 200.
   21756 Figurine - Golden Hare - trinket, item level 40, requires level 35.
        Spell 26873. The first wearable figurine is already past 29.
   Anything else the profession makes at this tier is a setting, a wire or
   a statue - trade goods, not gear.

3. GEMS - THE THING THE PROMPT ASKS ABOUT, AND THE ANSWER IS NOT YET
   A 29 in this set does not gem anything, for two independent reasons.
   The cuts are too high. The first Outland cuts start above the cap:
   Bold Blood Garnet 23095, +6 Strength, red socket, is spell 28905 at
   Jewelcrafting 305, difficulty 305/305/325/345, one Blood Garnet 23077,
   and its design 23131 is vendor stock in Hellfire Peninsula - a level 60
   zone. Everything below 300 in this profession is settings, wire, rings
   and statues; the gem-cutting half of jewelcrafting begins at 300.
   And there is nothing to put a gem in. No item at or below level 29 was
   found with a socket. Socketed gear in the game begins with Outland items
   for level 60 and above; that statement is from general knowledge, not
   from a page opened for this file, so the "no 29 item has a socket" line
   is unverified for this build.
   So the answer to "jewelcrafting gems" at 29 is: none, and it will stay
   none until the character is 60.

4. PROSPECTING - THE ONLY ECONOMY ANGLE
   Spell 31252, Jewelcrafting 20, trainer-taught for 1 copper. Destroys five
   ore of a base metal for a chance at precious gems.
   A 29 can prospect copper, tin, iron, mithril and thorium at 20 skill.
   That is a way to turn cheap ore into Tigerseye 818, Shadowgem 1210,
   Moss Agate 1206 and the rest of the low gems, which is exactly what the
   route below consumes. The per-ore gem tables were not opened, so the
   yields are unverified.

5. TRAINERS
   Jewelcrafting trainers exist in the capital cities and in Outland. The
   creature entries, the cities they stand in and their coordinates were not
   checked for this file, so all three are unverified.
   What is verified is that the profession is taught by trainer spells, not
   by patterns: 25229 apprentice, 25230 journeyman at skill 50, 28894 expert
   at 125, 28895 artisan at 200, 28897 master at 275. A 29 buys the first
   four and never the fifth.
   The single non-trainer recipe the route touches is Design: Pendant of the
   Agate Shield 20970, and its two vendors are in section 2.

7. ALT - WHAT A 29 CANNOT DO
   23095 and every other cut gem - skill 300 and up, needs the master rank
     at 275 first, which needs level 40 or better; ALT on every count.
   20960 Engraved Truesilver Ring - wearable only at level 32.
   20961 Citrine Ring of Rapid Healing - wearable only at level 33.
   21756 Figurine - Golden Hare - wearable only at level 35.
   28897 Master Jewelcrafting at skill 275 - past the 225 ceiling.
   Outland, Northrend and later jewelcrafting - prospecting tables for
     Outland ore, the epic gem cuts, the BoP jewelcrafter-only gems and the
     Panther statuettes - all of it needs a level 60+ character. ALT.
   A higher-level crafter is also the answer for the Pendant if the 29 does
     not want to level the profession at all: it binds when equipped, so
     anyone can make it and hand it over.

8. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE IT
   Jewelcrafting's 1-120 band produces one neck and four rings, so the
   question is who wants +4 Strength, +4 Stamina and +6 defense on a neck at
   26, and +4/+4 on a ring at 23.

   1. Warrior and paladin. The Pendant of the Agate Shield is the only
      strength-and-defense neck a 29 can craft, and the ten-charge group
      stamina buff is worth more in a battleground than any single stat on
      it - especially for a companion character meant to buff a group.
   2. Druid and shaman. Bear and enhancement builds take the same neck; the
      rings fit anything.
   3. Rogue and hunter. Ring of Twilight Shadows is a fine stamina and
      intellect ring, but neither class wants intellect, and neither gets a
      strength neck. Blacksmithing or engineering serves them better.
   4. Priest, mage, warlock. Four intellect on a ring is real at 23, but
      tailoring's Spidersilk Boots and enchanting's enchants are better uses
      of a profession slot, and the neck is a strength item.
   5. The roster's miner. Prospecting at skill 20 is the cheapest way to
      feed this profession and the auction house, and it pairs with Mining
      the way skinning pairs with leatherworking.
   Last line: jewelcrafting is worth one item at 29 - the Pendant of the
   Agate Shield 20950 - and that item can be bought. Level to 120 only if
   the twink is the one supplying the account.
