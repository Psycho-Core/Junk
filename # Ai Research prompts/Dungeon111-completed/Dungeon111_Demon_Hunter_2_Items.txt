2. HOW ITEMS SCALE IN 110-120 DUNGEONS AT LEVEL 111
---------------------------------------------------
1) Zero Equipped Item Downscaling Inside Dungeons (TrinityCore 35662 Verified):
   - In 8.3.7, ContentTuning.db2 entries for all BfA Normal leveling dungeons (Freehold Map ID 1754, Atal'Dazar Map ID 1763, The Underrot Map ID 1841, Waycrest Manor Map ID 1862, Shrine of the Storm Map ID 1864, Temple of Sethraliss Map ID 1877, Tol Dagor Map ID 1771, The MOTHERLODE!! Map ID 1594) set MaxItemLevel = 0.
   - Unlike Battlegrounds/Arenas or Timewalking, dungeons never clamp or downscale item stats. Equipped ilvl 265-300 pieces retain 100% of their full Leather Armor, Agility, Stamina, Secondary stats, Azerite traits, Tier 21 bonuses, and Legion Legendary effects.

2) Per-Player Creature Level Scaling & Zero Equipped-iLvl Mob Scaling:
   - Creature::GetLevelForTarget resolves dungeon mob level independently per player from player->getLevel() (level 111 for the booster, level 110-119 for the boostee) and scales outgoing/incoming combat packets accordingly.
   - BfA open-world equipped-item-level mob scaling (where world mobs gain HP/damage as your average equipped ilvl rises) IS DISABLED inside dungeon instances. Increasing the 111 Demon Hunter's average ilvl from 180 to 270+ increases dungeon enemy health and damage by 0%.

3) Combat Rating Multipliers at Level 111 vs Level 120 (Patch 8.3.7):
   - Between levels 110 and 120, required combat rating per 1% increases by * 1.15115 per character level (Combat_rating_system 8.3.7):
     * Critical Strike (1% Crit): 17.62 at 110 | 20.28 at 111 | 72.00 at 120.
     * Haste (1% attack speed & Haste-reduced cooldowns on Immolation Aura 258920, Eye Beam 198013, Metamorphosis 191427, Throw Glaive 185123): 16.64 at 110 | 19.16 at 111 | 68.00 at 120.
     * Versatility (1% damage/healing done, 0.5% all-type damage reduction): 20.80 at 110 | 23.94 at 111 | 85.00 at 120.
     * Mastery point factor (Mastery: Demonic Presence Spell ID 185164; 1 Mastery point = +1.8% Chaos damage in Patch 8.0.1-8.3.7, plus the spec's movement-speed component): 17.62 at 110 | 20.28 at 111 | 72.00 at 120.
     * Leech (1% Leech, stacking additively with Soul Rending 204909): 9.79 at 110 | 11.27 at 111 | 40.00 at 120.
     * Avoidance (1% AoE damage reduction, capped at 20%): 6.85 at 110 | 7.89 at 111 | 28.00 at 120.
     * Speed (1% movement speed): 4.90 at 110 | 5.64 at 111 | 20.00 at 120.
   - Result: Every point of secondary/tertiary rating on gear, flat +50 epic gems, and enchants is ~3.55x stronger at level 111 than at level 120, while only 15.1% weaker than level 110 in exchange for unlocking ilvl 225/233 crafted BoE Leather/Fists/Rings and full BfA ilvl 200+ gems.

4) How Dropped Loot Scales Inside 110-120 Dungeons:
   - Normal Boss Non-Azerite Personal Loot (BonusListID 4777): Stamps RequiredLevel = looterLevel and scales item level strictly by the looter's character level in Patch 8.3.7:
     110 -> 210 | 111 -> 217 | 112 -> 224 | 113 -> 231 | 114 -> 238 | 115 -> 245 | 116 -> 252 | 117 -> 263 | 118 -> 272 | 119 -> 281 | 120 -> 400 (Heroic 415, Mythic 0 430, Req Lvl 120).
   - At level 111, Normal boss non-Azerite drops are always ilvl 217 (Required Level 111). Below level 120, BfA dungeon loot CANNOT warforge or titanforge extra item levels, but CAN proc a Prismatic Socket and/or Tertiary stat (Speed, Leech, Avoidance, Indestructible).
   - Normal Trash Green BoEs: Roll ~ilvl 208-214 (Required Level = looterLevel, 111 when looted by the booster).
   - Normal Boss Azerite Armor (Head, Shoulders, Chest; requires owning Heart of Azeroth Item ID 158075 to drop): Fixed bracket scaling rather than per-level scaling:
     * Levels 110-116: Base ilvl 280 (285 empowered), Required Level 110.
     * Levels 117-119: Base ilvl 295 (300 empowered), Required Level 110.
     * Level 120: Base ilvl 400, Required Level 120.
     * Azerite items never roll sockets or tertiary stats.
   - Loot Trading Rule: A level 111 Demon Hunter with higher-ilvl gear in a slot can trade personal-loot Leather, Ring, Cloak, Trinket, and 1H Agility Weapon drops to party members in the same kill; non-Azerite drops looted by the 111 have Required Level 111, so a 110 boostee can equip them upon reaching 111.
   - 4/4 Battle-Hardened Heirlooms (upgraded with Battle-Hardened Heirloom Armor Casing Item ID 167731 / Scabbard Item ID 167732): Scale identically to Normal boss drops at 110-111 (ilvl 210 at 110, ilvl 217 at 111), making them strictly inferior to ilvl 225-300 gear on the 111 booster.

4. PROFESSIONS THAT HELP AT LEVEL 111
-------------------------------------
Recommended Primary Pair on the Level 111 Booster: ENGINEERING + ALCHEMY (or ENGINEERING + ENCHANTING / TAILORING / SKINNING for passive dungeon materials).
Note: Patch 8.0.1 split professions into independent expansion tiers; Kul Tiran / Zandalari tiers can be learned at Level 110 with Skill 1.

1) Engineering (Best Overall Active Utility Profession — Keep Equipped):
   - Nitro Boosts (Spell ID 55016, Enchant ID 4223, requires Northrend Engineering 50): +150% belt sprint for 5 sec every 2 min indoors; bridges long corridors between Fel Rush (195072) charges.
   - Loot-A-Rang (Item ID 60854, requires Cataclysm Engineering 70): Ranged AoE corpse looting from 40 yd without stopping or turning back while dashing forward with Fel Rush (195072).
   - Jeeves (Item ID 49040, requires Northrend Engineering 75) & MOLL-E (Item ID 40768, requires Northrend Engineering 50): Instant indoor repair/vendor/bank bot and portable mailbox during 10-run/hour instance locks.
   - Goblin Glider Cloak Tinker (Spell ID 126392, Enchant ID 4897, Pandaria Engineering 1): Optional horizontal speed glider stacking with Binding of Agility (Item ID 128549).
   - Restriction Note: BfA Engineering Azerite goggles require Level 120 and cannot be equipped at level 111.

2) Alchemy (Best Sustain & Consumable Efficiency — Keep Equipped):
   - Mixology (Spell ID 53042, learned with Alchemy): Doubles Greater Flask of the Currents (168651) and Greater Flask of the Vast Horizon (168653) duration from 1 hr to 2 hr.
   - Siren's Alchemist Stone (Item ID 152637, Craft Spell ID 252372, Kul Tiran / Zandalari Alchemy 25): BoP ilvl 225 trinket usable at 111 granting Versatility, Agility proc, and +40% healing from Abyssal Healing Potion (169451). Also crafts BoE Endless Tincture of Renewed Combat (Item ID 152634, Craft Spell ID 252359, Skill 1).

3) Enchanting, Tailoring, or Skinning (Best Passive Material Yield During Dungeon Spam):
   - Kul Tiran / Zandalari Enchanting (Skill 1): Disenchants all ilvl 208-217 soulbound/BoE dungeon drops into Gloom Dust (Item ID 152875), Umbra Shard (Item ID 152876), and Veiled Crystal (Item ID 152877).
   - Kul Tiran / Zandalari Tailoring (Skill 1): Passively increases Tidespray Linen (Item ID 152576) and Deep Sea Satin (Item ID 152577) drops from Freehold humanoid pirates, and crafts BoE Tidespray Linen Cloak (Item ID 154697 / rare 161987, Craft Spell ID 257107, Skill 25).
   - Kul Tiran / Zandalari Skinning (Skill 1): Skins beast/saurid/crawg packs in Atal'Dazar and The Underrot for Coarse Leather (Item ID 152541), Shimmerscale (Item ID 153050), and Blood-Stained Bone (Item ID 154164), though stopping to skin slows boost clear times.

4) Leatherworking & Jewelcrafting (Use Temporarily or on an Alt Only — Do Not Keep):
   - Every 111-usable Leatherworking item — Coarse Leather armor set (Item IDs 154138-154145 / rare procs 161938-161945, Craft Spell IDs 256748-256756, Skills 1-50), Shimmerscale Striker 1H Agility Fist (Item ID 154160 / rare proc 161969, Craft Spell ID 256777, Skill 1), Drums of the Maelstrom (Item ID 154167, Skill 50), and Legion Legendary The Sentinel's Eternal Refuge (Item ID 146669) — plus every Jewelcrafting socketed ring (Item IDs 153687-153690, Skills 1-25) and ilvl 200+ gem is Bind on Equip (BoE), whereas all BoP raid-tier Leatherworking/Jewelcrafting crafts require Level 120. Craft or buy the BoEs once, then run Engineering + Alchemy/Enchanting/Tailoring on the 111.

5) Secondary Professions (Cooking & Archaeology):
   - Kul Tiran / Zandalari Cooking: Crafts Bil'Tong (168314), Baked Port Tato (168313), and F.E.A.S.T. (168315), though all meals are tradeable on the Auction House.
   - Archaeology: Best farmed on a flying level 120 alt rather than the 111, because Sanguinating Totem (160753) and Fetish of the Tormented Mind (160833) are Bind to Blizzard Account (ilvl 300, Req Lvl 110) and can be mailed directly to the 111 Demon Hunter.
