MOGU'SHAN VAULTS
================

1. THE STONE GUARD (3 guards in 10, 4 in 25;
weekly set varies)
PHASES: single; guardians share HP pool
unverified; bring all down together.
Energy/petrification: adjacent guardians gain
energy; leading guardian petrifies raid
(90% less damage from its school, slows to
stone at 100) - tanks keep the petrifying
guardian paired and rotate pairs so the
Overload (250000 school damage, interrupts
petrification) fires before 100 stacks.
Solid Stone: a lone guardian takes -90% and
gains no energy - never isolate one.
Rend Flesh tank bleed 15s (50000/66650).
Amethyst: pools (stand out); Cobalt Mine
(175000 + root 6s after 3s arm - trigger
deliberately or avoid); Jade Shards 50000
raid; Jasper Chains: chained pair stay
within 10 yd (35000 +15%/s apart, breaks
after 15s apart).
Heroic: Energized Tiles (+damage/healing
per tile), Power Down at 85/65/40%.
ADD ORDER: none - equalise guardians.

Lightning Fists phase: Epicenter 116018

3. Gara'jal the Spiritbinder
Hard enrage 6 min (Final Destination).
Voodoo Dolls: boss fixates current tank; tank
+ 2 others (3 in 25) copy 70% of damage taken
to each other until that tank is Banished -
heal dolls as one; tanks hold fixate, other
tank builds threat, taunts back after exit.
Spirit Totems: assigned 2 DPS + 1 healer
kill totem to enter Spirit World (30s): kill
Shadowy Minions (Spiritual Grasp 22500-27500
to real world, Shadow Bolt inside), be healed
to full (grants Spiritual Innervation buffs
on exit), leave via Return Soul at 3-5s left
- staying = death. Dolls cannot enter.
Banished tank must kill Severer of Souls
within 30 sec or dies (Soul Explosion).
Frenzy at 20%: no more totems, burn + heal
through rising minion damage.
ADD ORDER: Severer (inside) > Minions >
boss.

+100%/+600%). Mel'jarak: Whirling Blade
(120000 line out-and-back), Rain of Blades
(20000/0.5s x6s), Wind Bomb below 75%

(I - 5% group heal per page chunk; notes
25%), Quickening (D-Magic, stacks) - purge.
Blademasters: Kor'thik Strike - all focus
one player (80000 each per chunk; notes
150000) - cooldown/heal target.
ADD ORDER (typical): Trappers group >

single-target only.

ADD ORDER: Corrupted Waters > Kaolan >
Regail > Asani.
