WHAT WAS WRONG, AND WHAT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only one document had content defects, and all four were in its twink section.

  1. Armour proficiency. Sections 7.3, 7.6 and 7.11 of the battlegrounds
     document carried the pre-7.0.3 rule: "warrior and paladin wear mail until
     plate unlocks at 40", "hunter and shaman do not learn mail until 40", and
     "no plate heirloom can be worn in this bracket at all". All three are
     false in 8.3.7 and the client proves it:
       SkillRaceClassInfo 293 "Plate Mail", ClassMask 3 (warrior, paladin),
         MinLevel 1; SkillLineAbility sends it to spell 750, which has no
         SpellLevels row at all.
       SkillRaceClassInfo 413 "Mail", ClassMask 68 (hunter, shaman),
         MinLevel 1; ability spell 8737 "Mail" has BaseLevel 1.
       414 Leather (ClassMask 3695) and 415 Cloth (all classes) sit at 0.
     So at 19 a warrior and paladin wear plate, a hunter and shaman wear mail,
     and every plate heirloom is wearable by a warrior or paladin. The three
     passages were rewritten to the client rule, and the class table in 7.11
     now reads plate for warrior and paladin and mail for hunter and shaman.

  2. "The two cowls at item level 10 are the only 19-legal cloth helms in the
     item table" (7.4). False: 226 cloth helms pass the level test at 19 -
     168107, 156914, 4385 Green Tinted Goggles, 65914 Vishas' Hood and the
     rest. The sentence now says what is true, that the reachable ones are the
     statless item-level-10 cowls and the engineering goggles, and the row is
     labelled for what it is: the highest-item-level cloth helm that is not a
     quest reward, not the strongest helm in the table.

  3. The headings of 7.4, 7.5 and 7.6 said "the best item per slot". They are
     legality tables (one wearable item per slot with the stat types the client
     records), and 7.13 already said so. The headings now say it and point at
     the best-in-slot document for the ranked answer.

  4. 7.11's class table printed "mail" for warrior and paladin and "leather"
     for hunter and shaman; corrected with 1.

ONE COSMETIC ISSUE, LEFT ALONE ON PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The three best-in-slot tables in BfA_8.3.7_19_twink_bis.txt are 178-180 columns
wide, because all eight required fields (slot, item, id, item level, required
level, armour or weapon, stats, source) are printed on one row. Every other
document in the set stays inside 100 columns. Nothing in the content is wrong -
the tables are what the audit checks row by row - but they wrap in a narrow
viewer. Reformatting them to a four-line block per item would fix the wrapping
and would need the row parser to be rewritten with it; say the word and it is
done in the next pass.

================================================================================
 SECOND PASS, 2026-10-22: THREE NEW EVIDENCE CHANNELS
================================================================================
The first pass checked each document against the client and its own citations.
This pass added three checks that cross documents and reach outside the client
altogether, because a claim can agree with the client and still be wrong if it
was copied from the wrong id, and an id can be self-consistent inside a
document and still not exist.

1. Every number in the set, traced. work/audit_numbers_all.py builds one corpus
   from all 8.3.7 client exports, the TrinityCore tree, the cached wiki
   revisions and the MDT / DBM / Encounter Journal data, then looks up every
   number of three or more digits printed in the eight documents.
       10,982 distinct numeric tokens across the eight documents
       0 that occur nowhere in the corpus
   The only numbers exempted are wiki revision ids and bluetracker URL slugs,
   and both are cited with a date on the same line, so they are checked by eye
   rather than by table.

2. Every item id, checked outside the client. work/audit_wowhead_names.py opens
   the Wowhead tooltip for every item row printed in the battlegrounds document
   and in the twink best-in-slot document and compares the name with the client
   name.
       372 item rows checked
       372 identical to the client
       0 mismatched, 0 without a Wowhead page
   This is the first third-party confirmation of the item names in either
   document. It also proves no id was mistyped into a plausible but different
   item.

3. Every id in every document, resolved. work/resolve_ids.py takes every 5-7
   digit number in the eight documents that is not in any client table and asks
   Wowhead what it is. 112 such ids existed. All but 17 resolved as an NPC, an
   item, a spell, a quest or an object; the 17 are wiki revision ids printed in
   the source tables and one TrinityCore constant value (AV_MINE_RECLAIM_TIMER
   = 1200000 ms, printed in the battlegrounds audit file). None is unexplained.
   work/known_ids.json holds the answers.

4. Cross-document agreement. work/audit_crossdoc.py finds every item that is
   printed in more than one document and checks that the item level, required
   level and stat line are the same in each.
       1 item is shared (4818 Executioner's Sword, in the battlegrounds weapon
       table and in the best-in-slot main-hand row): 24 / 19 in both, 0
       disagreements.

5. The corrected proficiency rule is now machine-checked. Five new claims were
   added to the battlegrounds harness so section 7.3 can never drift back to
   the pre-7.0.3 rule unnoticed: the ClassMask 3 row of skill 293 at MinLevel
   1, the ClassMask 68 row of 413 at MinLevel 1, leather and cloth at 0, plate
   taught by spell 750 with no SpellLevels row, mail taught by 8737 at
   BaseLevel 1, and a count of the 19-legal cloth helms (226). That takes the
   battlegrounds harness from 870 claims to 877.
