Pages used: Master Poisoner, Ghostly Strike (Legion), Weaponmaster,
Dismantle and Smoke Bomb, all read for their patch-change lines rather
than their current tooltips. The same method as the priest, warlock and
paladin talent audits, and for the same reason: three of these five
talents changed row, spec or numbers after Battle for Azeroth.

1. THE GATING
   1  PASS  Seven normal rows at 15/30/45/60/75/90/100, three PvP slots at
      20/40/70, one of each at 29. The file answers the owner's question
      directly: the PvP slot is not shared with the normal talent point.
      That is worth stating plainly because the prompt asked it as an open
      question and the earlier files in this set assumed it silently.

2. THE LEVEL 15 TALENTS
   2  PASS  Master Poisoner: 7.0.3 "Redesigned as a level 15 talent...
      Increases the damage done by your poisons by 30% and their
      non-damaging effects by 20%", and a Legion hotfix the same week set
      the bonus damage to 30% (was 40%). So 30% and 20% are the 8.3.7 pair.
      10.0.0 "No longer increases poison damage" confirms the damage half
      existed until then.
   3  PASS  Ghostly Strike, spell 196937 from the page's external link:
      10.0.0 "was a level 15 Outlaw talent", 8.0.1 "Duration reduced from
      15 seconds to 10. Now has a 35-second cooldown." So level, duration
      and cooldown are all BfA-sourced.
   4  PASS  The 10% damage figure: 10.2.0 "Now increases damage to the
      target by 15% (was 10%)", which pins the pre-10.2.0 value at 10%
      and covers 8.3.7. The file says exactly this.
   5  PASS  Weaponmaster, spell 193537: 10.0.0 "was a level 15 Outlaw and
      Subtlety talent", 8.0.1 "Now also available for Outlaw and only
      affects Shadowstrike and Backstab (was all abilities). Proc chance
      increased to 15% (was 6%)." The 12.0.0 Shadow Clone redesign is
      correctly excluded.
   6  UNVERIFIED  Which Outlaw abilities Weaponmaster repeats. The 8.0.1
      line names Shadowstrike and Backstab, which are Subtlety abilities;
      the file names Weaponmaster as a legal Outlaw alternative without
      claiming an Outlaw spell list. Correct restraint, still open.

3. THE PVP SLOT
   7  PASS  Dismantle: the page's infobox gives "Level 20 Outlaw rogue PvP
      talent", and 9.1.0 "Now available to all specializations (was Outlaw
      only)" proves the 8.3.7 restriction. This is the audit's most
      valuable confirmation in the file, because a current guide offers
      Dismantle to every rogue.
   8  PASS  Duration 6 sec in 8.3.7: 7.1.0 "Duration reduced to 6 seconds
      (was 8 seconds)", 10.1.0 "Duration decreased to 5 seconds (was 6
      seconds)". 6 sits between them and 8.3.7 sits between the patches.
      The energy cost follows the same logic (25 before 10.1.0's 15).
   9  PASS  The 45 sec cooldown is the page's current figure and no patch
      line changes it; printing it without a flag is defensible, though
      this audit notes it rests on the infobox rather than a patch line.
   10 PASS  Smoke Bomb is left unverified on its BfA level: its only BfA
      patch line (8.0.1) converts it from a rank 40 honor talent and opens
      it to all specs without giving a level, and the current level 25
      cannot be projected backwards. Given that Psyfiend was 40 in BfA and
      20 on the modern page, refusing to guess is right.
   11 PASS  Assassination and Subtlety are left with no PvP pick. That is
      an unsatisfying answer and the correct one: of the rogue PvP
      talents, only Dismantle's BfA level and spec were read.
