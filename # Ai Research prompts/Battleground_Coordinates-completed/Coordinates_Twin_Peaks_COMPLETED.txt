=== 0. HOW TO READ / ORIENTATION ===
Facing values are reproduced exactly as stored (world_safe_locs rows:
same degrees-like convention as the other files; script floats: as
written). None of them was converted.
No readable source assigns north/south/east/west to map 726's axes; this
file therefore makes NO compass claims - layout is given by coordinates,
feature names and straight-line distances only. The two flag positions
sit at x 2117.637 (Alliance side) and x 1578.337 (Horde side) - a raw
east/west-style offset is visible in the numbers but is not named here.
Route legs are straight-line distances between sourced landmarks. They
are NOT walkable-path measurements: no pathing/navmesh source for map 726
could be read (unverified).

The graveyard rule (script GetClosestGraveYard, exact logic, using
  BG_TP_GraveyardIds = {1726, 1727, 1729, 1728, 1749, 1750}):
  - before the match: your team's ENTRANCE row (Alliance 1726, Horde
    1727) - the script calls these the FLAGROOM indices;
  - during the match: compare the corpse's distance to the ENEMY
    entrance row vs the enemy CENTER row; if the enemy entrance is
    closer, respawn at your team's BASE graveyard (Alliance 1729, Horde
    1728, called the START indices in the script); otherwise respawn at
    your team's CENTER graveyard (Alliance 1749, Horde 1750).
  Which graveyard belongs to which point: no points exist (CTF); the
  base and center rows are the two respawn options per team.
Spirit guides are spawned by the script at the four graveyard rows
  (entrances excluded - the setup loop covers the START and MIDDLE
  indices only), using the base-class spirit creatures 13116 (Alliance)
  / 13117 (Horde).
