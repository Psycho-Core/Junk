TWINK29 - PRIEST - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, PROFESSIONS, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
Sources for this file: the nine profession files in
Twink29_Professions-completed (Enchanting, Alchemy, Inscription, Cooking and
First Aid, Tailoring, Leatherworking, Engineering), the priest item and
talent steps, and item/spell tooltips read for this file. V = read.
Where a number was not read it says unverified rather than being filled in.

0. THE SHAPE OF THIS FILE
   A priest at 29 is the class whose consumables and enchants are the
   cheapest in the whole set and whose weapon enchant is the weakest,
   because the caster weapon enchants all sit above the Classic 225 cap.
   The two things that make the class work at this tier are Spirit, which
   the enchants and the food buff both give, and mana potions, which are
   the only answer a 29 healer has to a long fight.

1. ENCHANTS - ONE PER SLOT, ALL INSIDE SKILL 225
CLOAK|Enchant Cloak - Greater Defense|13746|+50 armour|skill 205|V
CLOAK|Enchant Cloak - Lesser Agility|13882|+3 Agility|skill 225|V
   A priest wears 13746. Armour is not a healer stat, but it is the only
   in-cap cloak enchant that is not Agility, and +50 armour is the largest
   single defensive number any in-cap enchant gives. 13882 is listed only
   so the file does not pretend there is a choice.
CHEST|Enchant Chest - Greater Health|13640|+35 health|skill 160|V
   3x Soul Dust 11083, Runed Golden Rod. The default chest enchant at this
   tier - there is no Intellect chest enchant inside 225 at all.
WRIST|Enchant Bracer - Spirit|13642|+5 Spirit|Runed Golden Rod|V
WRIST|Enchant Bracer - Lesser Intellect|13622|+3 Intellect|Silver Rod|V
   13642 is the priest's best in-cap enchant in this file: +5 Spirit, one
   Lesser Mystic Essence. Note for the record: the Enchanting profession
   file says the caster bracer enchant inside the cap "was not checked".
   It exists and it is this one. 13622 is the fallback at +3 Intellect.
HANDS|no caster glove enchant inside 225 - stated gap
   The only in-cap glove enchant is 13815 Enchant Gloves - Agility
   (+5 Agility, skill 210), which is the wrong stat on a priest. A priest
   wears nothing on the gloves or wears the Agility line for the armour
   value and admits it buys nothing.
LEGS|Heavy Armor Kit|4265|+24 armour|item level 30, req 20|V
   Not an enchant and not a caster item: 4265 permanently adds 24 armour to
   a chest, legs, hands or feet item and works on anything item level 15 or
   higher. There is no Intellect or Spirit leg enchant inside the cap, so
   this is what the slot takes.
FEET|Enchant Boots - Minor Speed|13890|movement speed|skill 225|V
   The single most valuable enchant in the bracket for any class, and worth
   more to a healer than to anyone: a priest that cannot reposition cannot
   heal. Its exact percentage is unverified in this build, per the
   Enchanting file. 13687 Enchant Boots - Lesser Spirit (+3 Spirit) exists
   and is strictly worse than movement.
WEAPON|no caster weapon enchant inside 225 - use an oil, section 2
   13693 Enchant Weapon - Striking (+3 weapon damage, skill 195) is the
   best in-cap weapon enchant and is melee-only; on a priest it is waste.
   Every spell-power weapon enchant is above 225 and needs an ALT.
OFF|nothing - the off-hand slot has no enchant in the Classic skill line
NECK|190894 - BfA neck enchant, ALT, not a 29 craft|unverified effect
RING|190871 and 191014 - BfA ring enchants, enchanter-only, ALT
   Ring enchantments are the enchanter's self-only perk from later
   expansions. A 29 with only the Classic skill line cannot apply one to
   his own rings, so both ring rows are ALT or nothing at all.
   ALT LIST, out of the 225 cap and needing a higher-level enchanter:
   13939 Bracer - Greater Strength (240), 13943 Weapon - Greater Striking
   (245), 13898 Weapon - Fiery (265), 20012 Gloves - Greater Agility (270),
   20031 Weapon - Superior Striking (300), 25080 Gloves - Superior Agility
   (300). All of them are melee enchants, which is the point: a caster's
   upgrades are not "an ALT enchant", they are oils.

2. CONSUMABLES
   BATTLE ELIXIR
     Elixir of Firepower 6373, +10 fire spell damage for 30 min, req 18 (V).
       The only spell-damage elixir a 29 can drink, and it is fire only, so
       a priest gets nothing from it except on Smite, which is holy. Honest
       reading: it is the best in-window battle elixir and it is a poor fit.
     No Intellect battle elixir in the window was found in the audited
       Alchemy file. Treat "there is none" as "none was found", not as a
       proof of absence.
   GUARDIAN ELIXIR
     Elixir of Greater Defense 8951, +250 armour for 1 hour, req 29 (V).
       Usable at exactly 29 and the single best guardian elixir in the
       bracket for a cloth class.
     Elixir of Fortitude 3825, req 25 (V). Cheaper, weaker, same slot.
   SCROLLS
     Scroll of Intellect II 2290, +5 Int for 30 min, req 15, spell 50598 (V).
     Scroll of Stamina II 1711, +5 Sta, req 15 (V).
     Both are made by Inscription at skill 75 and cost one ink each.
     A scroll does not stack with a class buff giving the same stat.
   WEAPON OILS - the priest's real weapon "enchant"
     Minor Wizard Oil 20744, +7 Intellect for 1 hour, req 5 (V).
     Minor Mana Oil 20745, +7 Spirit for 1 hour, req 20 (V).
     Lesser Wizard Oil 20746 is +15 Intellect but requires level 30, one
     level past the bracket, so it is out and not listed as a row (V).
     A priest picks one oil, not both, and 20745 is the one for a healer.
   POTIONS
     Lesser Mana Potion 3385, req 14 (V). The mana potion a 29 can drink;
       every better one is level 31 or higher (6149 Greater Mana is 31).
     Greater Healing Potion 1710, req 21 (V).
     Free Action Potion 5634, req 20 (V). No root or slow, and it is the
       potion that saves a flag carrier or a healer in a bad spot.
     Shadow Protection Potion 6048, req 17 (V). The anti-priest, anti-
       warlock potion, and the one a priest drinks before a mirror match.
     Frost Protection Potion 6050, req 28 (V).
     All potions share one cooldown, so a bag full of them is one potion.
   FOOD
     Roast Raptor 12210, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit for 15 min, req 25 (V).
       The best food buff a 29 can eat, and Spirit is the priest's stat:
       this is the only place in the file where the class is favoured.
     552-heal food at +6/+6 (V in the Cooking file) is the cheap version.
   BANDAGES
     8544 Mageweave Bandage, heals 1600 over 8 sec, Tailoring 240 (V).
       240 is above a 29 tailor's 225, so this one is bought, not made.
     6451 Heavy Silk Bandage, heals 1280, Tailoring 210 (V). The best a 29
       tailor can actually make.
     Bandages share one cooldown and apply a 60 second Recently Bandaged
     debuff, so they are an emergency heal, not a rotation.

3. PROFESSIONS
   ENCHANTING  The self-supply choice. A 29 enchanter disenchants his own
     quest greens, funds 13642 and 13640 himself, and can apply other
     people's enchants. The profession gives the character nothing to wear
     that a non-enchanter cannot buy, but at 29 the enchants are the point.
   TAILORING  Cloth is this class's armour, and 225 Tailoring makes the
     Heavy Silk Bandage 6451 and the cloth pieces in step 1's pool that are
     crafted rather than dropped. The bags matter too: a battleground bot
     with 16-slot bags carries the potions.
   ALCHEMY  The alternative to Tailoring. Elixir of Greater Defense 8951
     is the one elixir worth drinking and Alchemy makes it plus the mana
     potions; the catch is that elixirs are cheaper to buy than to level
     for, as the Alchemy file says.
   INSCRIPTION  Skill 75 buys Scroll of Intellect II and Scroll of
     Stamina II for one ink. It is the cheapest profession in the file and
     the least valuable, because the scroll is +5 and does not stack.
   RECOMMENDATION  Enchanting + Tailoring for a character that will be
     played, Enchanting + Alchemy if the bots are expected to fight long.
     Engineering and Blacksmithing give a priest nothing: no gun, no
     counterweight on a staff, no shield spike without a shield.

4. RACES
   A priest could be, in 8.3.7, any of the races that had the class before
   the Allied Race wave and the ones added with it. What follows lists the
   racial that matters and the id where one was read. Racial ids are
   Classic-era unless the spell was renumbered, and the whole race list is
   the one place in this file where the membership itself is unverified
   for 8.3.7 - the wiki page is a current-expansion page where every race
   can be a priest, so it cannot confirm what the class list was in BfA.
   ALLIANCE
     Human     59752 Will to Survive, a self CC break (V, from step 1 of
               the paladin set). The best racial a healer can have: the
               thing that kills a 29 priest is a fear or a stun.
     Dwarf     Stoneform, removes bleed, poison and disease and cuts damage
               taken. id unverified.
     Night elf 58984 Shadowmeld. Drops combat and hides; the best racial in
               the bracket for not being focused at all.
     Gnome     20589 Escape Artist, breaks a snare. Narrow but cheap.
     Draenei   Gift of the Naaru, a heal over time. id unverified.
     Void elf Spatial Rift, a teleport. id unverified.
     Lightforged  Light's Judgment, a damage/heal bolt. id unverified.
     Dark iron  Fireblood, removes debuffs and grants a stat. id unverified.
   HORDE
     Undead    7744 Will of the Forsaken, breaks fear, sleep and charm. The
               Horde answer to the Human racial and the single best racial
               for a priest in a bracket full of warlocks and priests.
     Troll     26297 Berserking, haste on demand. The damage choice.
     Blood elf 25046 Arcane Torrent, an area silence and mana return. The
               only interrupt a 29 priest has access to at all, which makes
               it worth more than its numbers.
     Tauren    20549 War Stomp, an area stun. The only hard CC a priest can
               carry.
     Pandaren  Quaking Palm, an incapacitate. id unverified.
     Nightborne  Arcane Pulse, a slow. id unverified.
     Zandalari  Regeneratin', a full heal channel. id unverified.
   PICKS  Human for Alliance and Undead for Horde, both because a priest's
     death is usually a crowd-control death. Troll if the bot is a Shadow
     priest that is expected to do damage, Blood elf if the bot needs the
     silence, Tauren if the group needs a stun.

5. WHAT TO BUY, IN ORDER
   First  the two oils: 20745 Minor Mana Oil for the staff and a stack of
          20744 Minor Wizard Oil for the swap. One hour each, five charges.
   Second the food: 12210 Roast Raptor, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit.
   Third  8951 Elixir of Greater Defense, then 6048 Shadow Protection.
   Fourth 3385 Lesser Mana Potions and 5634 Free Action Potions, a stack of
          each. This is the whole mana plan of a 29 priest.
   Fifth  2290 Scroll of Intellect II and 1711 Scroll of Stamina II.
   Last   the enchants: 13642 bracers, 13640 chest, 13746 cloak, 13890
          boots. The boots first among those four if gold is short.

6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT HAVE
   A caster glove enchant inside 225 (none exists that was read).
   A caster weapon enchant inside 225 (oils instead).
   A leg enchant (an armour kit instead).
   A neck or ring enchant a 29 can apply (enchanter-only, ALT).
   An Intellect battle elixir (none found in the window).
   A verified BfA priest race list (the wiki page is current-expansion).
   Those six gaps are the honest shape of the class at 29: everything that
   would make a caster strong is one expansion's worth of skill above him,
   and what he has instead is Spirit, armour and mana potions.
