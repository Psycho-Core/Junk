TWINK29 - PROFESSIONS - STEP 10 - HERBALISM, MINING AND SKINNING
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Scope: what the three gathering professions give a level 29 twink.

1. THE SHORT ANSWER
   Nothing. Of the ten steps in this set this is the only one where the
   honest answer to "what does it give a 29" is "no item, no buff, no
   stat, and no ability". Gathering in 8.3.7 is a money and materials
   profession: it feeds Alchemy, Inscription, Blacksmithing, Engineering,
   Jewelcrafting and Leatherworking, and it sells on the auction house.
   A twink takes one of these three only if he wants to be his own
   supplier, and he pays for it with one of his two primary slots.
   Everything that made gathering interesting in Wrath is gone, and the
   next section says when and why.

2. THE THREE PERKS THAT USED TO EXIST
   Wrath-era gathering gave a combat ability per profession. All three
   were deleted in patch 6.0.2 (2014-10-14). Verified on warcraft.wiki.gg,
   where each page carries a "removed in patch 6.0.2" banner.
   Herbalism - Lifeblood: instant, no global cooldown, 3 minute cooldown,
     haste plus a heal over 5 seconds, usable in arenas, undispellable,
     and it did not break Stealth or Prowl. Ranks at skill 1/75/150/225/
     300/375 gave 5/15/35/50/70/120 haste. In a 29 bracket this was the
     single best reason to take a gathering profession.
   Mining - Toughness: passive stamina, 3/5/7/10/30/60 at skill 75/150/
     225/300/375/450.
   Skinning - Master of Anatomy: passive critical strike rating, 3/6/9/
     12/20/40 at the same skill steps.
   None of the three exists in 8.3.7. There is no gathering ability to
   press and no passive line on the character sheet, so any guide that
   still recommends Herbalism to a twink for Lifeblood is describing
   Wrath or Mists, not this server.

3. THE SKILL BAR - AND A CORRECTION THAT AFFECTS STEPS 01 TO 09
   Since patch 8.0.1 each expansion has its own profession bar and the
   four old ranks were merged. warcraft.wiki.gg, Classic proficiency:
   "formerly known as Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Artisan ... the
   beginning stage of a profession learned at the appropriate profession
   trainer when a character has reached at least level 5 ... allows skill
   ups to a maximum of 300 points". The Profession page adds that
   "Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Artisan no longer appear in game
   and are rolled up under Classic".
   The old ladder, kept here only as reference, was:
     apprentice  level 5,   cap 75
     journeyman  level 10 and 50 skill,  cap 150
     expert      level 20 and 125 skill, cap 225
     artisan     level 35 and 200 skill, cap 300
   If 8.3.7 uses the current rule, a level 29 can carry Classic Herbalism,
   Mining or Skinning to 300, and the same is true of the crafting
   professions. That contradicts the 225 ceilings quoted in steps 01 to
   09, which were read off 3.x data (the wotlk pages still show the rank
   ladder). The correction is worth resolving set-wide, because it is the
   difference between "a 29 tailor stops at silk" and "a 29 tailor can
   reach 300 and make Runecloth".
   Two caveats before anyone rewrites the other files:
   - Check the live trainer window first. If the trainer offers one
     "Classic Tailoring" skill and the skill bar reads /300, the new rule
     is in force. If it offers Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, Artisan as
     separate purchases, the core kept the old ladder and 225 is right.
   - Even at 300 skill the item requirements do not move. A pattern that
     produces a level 40 item still produces a level 40 item, and the
     recipes above 225 often need materials from 40+ zones. Skill is a
     gate, not a licence.
   Gathering specifically is almost unaffected by this argument, which
   is why it is settled here and not in an audit: the zones a 29 can
   survive in are the binding limit, not the profession rank.

4. HERBALISM - SKILL LINE 182
   No tool is needed, ever. Since patch 6.0.2 "most classic herb nodes
   are accessible to anyone even with a skill of 1. However, low-skilled
   players will acquire petals, stems, leaves or brambles instead of the
   full item" (warcraft.wiki.gg, Herb). Ten fragments combine into one
   herb, so a low-skill pick is worth a tenth of a node, not nothing.
   Nodes stay orange/yellow/green/grey for skill-ups, so skill still has
   to be earned, just not gated.

   2453  Bruiseweed - level 10+ zones, near houses.

   3358  Khadgar's Whisker - level 25+ zones.
   3819  Wintersbite - Western Plaguelands and the like, 40+.
   4625  Firebloom - Badlands, Burning Steppes, Searing Gorge, Tanaris.

   8836  Arthas' Tears - Western and Eastern Plaguelands.

5. MINING - SKILL LINE 186
   Tools: Mining Pick 2901. The classic guide is explicit that it "does
   not have to be equipped, but you have to have one in your inventory".
   The current wiki says "a mining pick is not necessary but can help".
   The pick requirement was dropped somewhere between the two, so treat
   it as unverified for 8.3.7 and carry one anyway: it costs silver from
   the mining supply vendor next to the trainer.
   Ore nodes lost their hard skill gate the same way herbs did. A node
   page carries both readings: Mithril Deposit is Mining (1) in retail and
   Mining (175) in Classic, and it only guarantees a full Mithril Ore at
   Mining 150 or higher - below that the client hands out a nugget
   instead. So on 8.3.7 the gate is gone and the price is yield, not
   access. The classic ladder below is what the leveling guide and the
   Classic servers use, and it is still the best guide to what each node
   pays out:
   1-65     Copper Vein - Copper Ore 2770.
   65-125   Tin Vein - Tin Ore 2771; Silver Vein - Silver Ore 2775 at 75.
   125-175  Iron Deposit - Iron Ore 2772.
   155      Gold Vein - Gold Ore 2776.
   175-245  Mithril Deposit - Mithril Ore 3858.
   230      Truesilver Deposit - Truesilver Ore 7911; Dark Iron Ore 11370.
   245-300  Small and Rich Thorium Vein - Thorium Ore 10620.
   Two thresholds matter instead of one: 150 for full ore rather than
   nuggets, and the zone level for whether a 29 can stand there at all.
   Thorium means Burning Steppes, Winterspring or the Plaguelands, which
   a 29 cannot farm; Mithril means Badlands and Tanaris, which is already
   a corpse run.
   Smelting is a Mining ability and needs a forge. Verified example:
   Smelt Mithril, spell 10097, requires Mining 175, 1x Mithril Ore 3858
   gives 1x Mithril Bar 3860, difficulty 175/175/202/230, taught by every
   mining trainer. Copper starts at Copper Bar 2840. The higher smelts
   were not read individually and are unverified.
   Find Minerals is the tracking ability that comes with the profession;
   its spell id was not read. It has no combat value.
   What the ore is for: Engineering (step 01), Blacksmithing (step 03)
   and Jewelcrafting (step 07), plus Prospecting for Jewelcrafting.

6. SKINNING - SKILL LINE 393
   Tools: Skinning Knife 7005. Since patch 5.0.4 (2012-08-28) it is "no
   longer required to skin" and it "provides +10 Skinning while equipped
   or in inventory" (warcraft.wiki.gg, Skinning Knife). So a 29 skinner
   carries one for the +10, not because skinning fails without it.
   Skinning is the one gathering profession whose skill requirement is a
   formula rather than a node list, and this is the version players use:
   up to skill 100, the highest skinnable mob is (skill / 10) + 10; from
   skill 100 up it is skill / 5.
   225 skill skins level 45 mobs; 235 with the knife in the bag skins 47;
   300 skill skins level 60.
   The leathers, with ids and the mob levels the wiki gives:
   2934  Ruined Leather Scraps - level 5-17 mobs.
   2318  Light Leather - level 5-27 mobs.
   2319  Medium Leather - level 15-38 mobs.
   4234  Heavy Leather - level 26-46 mobs.
   4304  Thick Leather - level 35-58 mobs.
   8170  Rugged Leather - level 45-60 mobs.
   Hides drop alongside: 783 Light Hide, 4232 Medium Hide, 4235 Heavy
   Hide, and Thick and Rugged hides above those (ids 8169 and 8171,
   unverified here). Hides are the bottleneck for Leatherworking (step
   04), not the leather.
   What the leather is for: Leatherworking, plus a handful of Tailoring,
   Blacksmithing and Engineering recipes.

7. THE CHEAPEST ROUTE AT 29
   Gathering has no combines and no materials, so "cheapest" means the
   fewest zones and the least danger. All three use the same shape: a
   starter zone to about 70, a level 20-30 zone to about 150, and then
   the 40+ zones the class guides use, which a 29 cannot work in.
   Herbalism, from the classic route: 1-70 starter zone; 70-115 The
   Barrens, Silverpine, Loch Modan or Darkshore; 115-170 the same zones
   plus Bruiseweed and Wild Steelbloom country; 170-205 Arathi, Alterac,
   Stranglethorn; 205+ Tanaris, Feralas, the Plaguelands, Un'Goro.
   Mining: 1-65 starter zone; 65-125 Hillsbrad, Redridge, Ashenvale or
   The Barrens; 125-175 the same plus Arathi; 175-245 Badlands and
   Tanaris for Mithril; 245+ Burning Steppes, Un'Goro, Winterspring.
   Skinning: 1-75 Durotar or Dun Morogh; 75-155 The Barrens, Loch Modan,
   Wetlands; 155-205 Thousand Needles and Arathi; 205+ Un'Goro, Feralas.
   For a 29 the realistic stopping point is the third stage in each
   case: around 150 to 175 skill earned in 20-35 zones. Past that the
   character is farming mobs 10 levels above himself for a profession
   that gives him nothing, and the auction house sells the same herbs and
   ore for a few gold a stack.
   One route a 29 does own: skinning. He kills beasts anyway while
   leveling or in battleground corpse runs, and a dead beast is a free
   skill-up. If a companion character is going to carry any gathering
   profession, it should be this one.

9. ALT - WHAT A 29 CANNOT DO
   The old artisan rank, if the core still uses the ladder: level 35 and
   200 skill. That is the same level-35 wall as Cooking and every
     crafting file in this set.
   Full-yield thorium and rich thorium at a 225 cap: the nodes open to
     any skill, the ore does not. Same for everything in the Outland,
     Northrend, Cataclysm, Pandaria, Draenor and Battle for Azeroth
     tiers, and the BfA gathering tiers (Kul Tiran and Zandalari) are
     level 110+ content.
   Mobs above level 45 to skin at 225, or above 47 with the knife.
   Every herb from Wintersbite upward, in practice: the nodes are open
     since 6.0.2 but the zones are 40+.
   Any recipe that consumes these materials at a skill above the cap -
     that is a crafting-file problem, not a gathering one, and steps 01
     to 08 listed them.

10. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE IT
   1. Nobody, on combat grounds. There is no stat, no ability and no
      item here. In a 29 bracket two profession slots hold Engineering
      and one of Blacksmithing, Leatherworking, Tailoring or Alchemy.
      A gathering profession in one of those slots is a 25% loss of
      gear, every time.
   2. The self-supplier. Herbalism pairs with Alchemy (step 02) and
      Inscription (step 08), Mining with Engineering (step 01),
      Blacksmithing (step 03) and Jewelcrafting (step 07), Skinning with
      Leatherworking (step 04). If the plan is to level the crafting
      profession on the same character, the matching gathering
      profession saves gold and nothing else.
   3. The gold character. A 29 herbalist or miner in a 20-30 zone earns
      more per hour than a 29 crafter, because the crafting files all
      need materials a 29 cannot make cheaply. If one character in a set
      of companions exists to fund the others, this is its job.
   4. The skinner. Skinning is the only gathering profession a 29 can
      level without leaving the content he already fights in, and the
      only one that pays out for something he was going to kill anyway.
      If a companion must have a gathering profession, take Skinning.
   Last line: no twink takes Herbalism, Mining or Skinning for power in
   8.3.7 - the perks died in 6.0.2. Take one only as the supplier for a
   crafting file, take Skinning if the character kills beasts anyway, and
   spend the second slot on gear.
