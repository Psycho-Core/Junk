0. WHAT A WARLOCK WEARS AT 29, AND THE ONE THING THAT IS DIFFERENT
   A warlock is a cloth class, so the armour pool is the same nineteen rare
   rows the priest and mage files work from. Cloth is the best served
   armour type in the bracket: head, shoulder, chest, wrist, hands, waist,
   legs, feet and back all have at least one blue, and there are three
   cloth heirlooms that fill the two slots the blues leave thin.
   The difference is the weapon. A warlock can use one-hand swords, and
   the bracket has five blue ones - but only one of them is a caster
   weapon: 13033 Zealot Blade, 34/29, +5 Intellect +6 Spirit (V). The
   other four are melee:
     19557 Protector's Sword, 33/28, +7 Strength +3 Stamina (V)
     19553 Legionnaire's Sword, the Horde copy, 33/28
     8226 The Butcher, 31/26, Scarlet Monastery zone drop
     12974 The Black Knight, 31/26, world drop
   So a warlock's real choice is a staff, a dagger plus an orb, or the
   Zealot Blade plus an orb. Everything else in the sword list is a
   stat stick for a class that hits things.

4. THE THREE SPECS
   AFFLICTION  Section 1, the Spirit pieces included because they carry
     the Intellect, 7713 or the 13033 plus 55963, and 7708 Necrotic Wand
     for the Shadow flavour. Rings 19525 and 65966.
   DEMONOLOGY  Identical. A demonology warlock's pet does not need gear
     and nothing in the item pool changes with the pet, so the two
     damage specs wear one set.
   DESTRUCTION  Identical. Destruction wants crit, so 7712 Mantle of Doan
     and 7713 Illusionary Rod are the two pieces that carry it, and 55929
     Skull-Scorched Cloak is the crit cloak if the Warsong cape is not
     yet bought.

6. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   Every slot is covered by a blue except the second ring, which reuses
   the three rings of the first. Cloth being the best served armour type
   is the whole reason a warlock file looks comfortable; the class
   contributes nothing to the pool itself.
   The warlock-specific findings are two. The sword skill is almost
   worthless at this tier: five blue one-hand swords, one caster stat
   line, and that one is a world drop. And there is no neck in the window
   with both Intellect and a second caster stat - 55928 has Intellect and
   hit, 13087 has neither.
   What the class does have is choice of weapon: staff, dagger, sword and
   wand all exist at this tier with a caster line, which is more than the
   mage file could say and much more than the paladin had.
