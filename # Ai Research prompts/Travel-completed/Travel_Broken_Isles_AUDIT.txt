RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
30 claims: 28 PASS, 0 FIXED, 2 UNVERIFIED. Nothing in the file had to change.
The independent parser reproduced all 55 blocks, and only one line of the
document needed re-reading to see why: the 1848 -> 1849 leg appears twice by
design, once as an outbound row and once as an inbound-only row.

What was re-read rather than trusted:
  - all 55 positions, bit numbers, link sets and travel times were re-parsed
    from the section-1220 text and compared with the file;
  - the four web-resolved masters were re-opened on Wowhead, and the wiki pages
    for Krasus' Landing, Nesingwary's Retreat, Eye of Azshara, Shield's Rest,
    Hafr Fjall and Trueshot Lodge were re-checked for the claims the footer
    makes about them;
  - two document entries were spot-checked on Wowhead (Danise Stargazer,
    Duchess) and both hold;
  - the four gate ids, the ten-node Hunter group, the faction tally and the
    293-row link total were recounted.
Not re-read: the 8.3.7 TaxiNodes export itself (section 1220 of the research
document is the transcription used here, as in steps 01 to 06).
