HOW MUCH GEOMETRY THIS RAID HAS, COMPARED WITH THE LAST TWO FILES. It is
uneven, and that matters more than the totals. Four encounters are fully
furnished: Wrathion (24 placed points), Drest'agath (24 appendage spawns in

triggers disagree with the 2020 guides in three places. Where they disagree
the file gives both and says which one your server runs.

1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS
================================================================================
1.1 instance_nyalotha.cpp registers the map (2217) and nothing else; the
    encounter count is 12 (nyalotha.h, EncounterCount = 12).
1.2 The twelve boss scripts are real implementations of the fights, with
    timers, phase gates, adds and, for four bosses, hard coordinates. They are
    BfaCore-lineage code.
1.3 DBM 8.3.34 is the last BfA build of these modules. Its timers are the
    raiders' measurements, not client data, so a first cast that DBM never saw
    (because the script casts it earlier than retail did) will not appear here.
1.4 Some DBM entries are keyed to journal ("ej") ids rather than spell ids -
    those are marked as journal ids in the tables below.
1.5 Client tables (SpellRadius, SpellEffect, SpellDuration, creature spawns)
    were not readable in this step. So: no boss spawn row in this file is
    verified, and no ability radius is quoted unless it came from a script
    GetRandomPoint/GetRandomNearPosition call, which is arithmetic and marked
    [CALC].

4. BOSS 1 - WRATHION, THE BLACK EMPEROR
================================================================================
4.1 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION
  Boss spawn: unverified (world DB). The script's arena constant is
  (-1756.909, -43.431, -1477.872) o 6.2706 and its play rectangle is
  (-1792.640, -12.733) to (-1718.550, -73.557) at z -1478.160.
  TANK POSITION: hold him at the centre facing away. Searing Breath 305978 is
  a frontal cone and Tail Swipe 307974 hits behind him, so the tank faces him
  away and the raid stands at his flank - the two zones leave the flanks as
  the only safe side (tactics document, and the script's own Tail Swipe target
  selector picks units behind the boss).
  On death the script teleports the boss to (-1706.0, -44.0, -518.0) and the
  raid to (-1760.0, -43.0, -518.0): that is the exit pad, not a fight position.

4.2 PHASES [GUIDE]/[SRV] - energy, not health
  HEALTH PERCENTAGE: none.
  Stage One: The Black Emperor runs until his energy is gone. Each Burning
  Cataclysm spends 50 energy; when energy reaches 0 he casts Smoke and Mirrors
  and Stage Two begins (Wowhead journal text; the script does exactly this:
  ModifyPower(-50) then, at 0 or below, SchedulePhase2()).
  Stage Two: Smoke and Mirrors runs until he has finished transforming the
  Molten Pools; the script ends it on the third Crackling Shard summon
  (cracklingShards == 3 -> SPELL_BERSERK 301495) and returns to Stage One with
  the Smoke and Mirrors ending spell.
  So the cadence is P1 -> P2 -> P1 -> P2 and so on, with no percentage at all.
  Berserk: DBM has no berserk timer for this encounter (it is commented out);
  the script casts Berserk 301495 itself after the third shard summon.

  Incineration 306111        first 9.1 (28.6 later), cooldown 19.4
  Gale Blast 306289          first 45 (74.3 later), cooldown 90.9
  Burning Cataclysm 306735   first 59.7 (75.6 later), cooldown 90.9

4.4 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Searing Breath 305978 - assigned to the TANK. Trigger: the cast; keep the
     cone off the raid.
  b) Tail Swipe 307974 - assigned to EVERYBODY ELSE. Trigger: the script hits
     every unit behind the boss every 3 s; stand at the flank, not behind.
  c) Incineration 306111 - assigned to 3 MARKED PLAYERS. Trigger: the debuff;
     step out of the stack before it expires.
  d) Gale Blast 306289 - assigned to EVERYBODY. Trigger: the stomp; step out
     of the boss model.
  e) Burning Cataclysm 306735 - assigned to EVERYBODY. Trigger: the boss
     teleports to one of the four named sides; the raid moves to the opposite
     side of the room from the landing point.
  f) Scorching Blister 312490 - assigned to NOBODY, dodged. Twenty positions
     are pre-placed (3.1); each grows for 10 s and then explodes for 30 yards.
  g) Scales of Wrathion 308682 - assigned to the RAID as a catch rotation.
     Trigger: each scale falls on a random point inside the play rectangle;
     catching one applies Burning Madness 307013, missing one hits everyone.
  h) Crackling Shard 158327 with Hardened Core 313175 - assigned to the ADD
     GROUP. Trigger: Molten Pools become shards; run them over while carrying
     Burning Madness (that is what the madness is for).
  i) Smoke Shroud 312266 (Ashwalker Assassin 160291, Heroic and Mythic) -
     assigned to the RAID: kill the assassin.

4.5 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified: the play rectangle in 3.1 and the 20 blister points. Because every
  random position in the encounter is rolled inside the rectangle, standing
  outside it is safe from Blisters and Scales by construction - but the
  rectangle is 74 x 61 yards and the fight happens inside it, so the practical
  safe spot moves.
  Named sides: Cataclysm South/West/East/North, 49.6-72.0 yards from centre
  [CALC]. The safe side is the one diagonally opposite the chosen landing
  point.

5. BOSS 2 - THE PROPHET SKITRA
================================================================================
5.1 SPAWN AND TANK POSITION
  Boss spawn: unverified. Arena centre (-1487.0, -580.0, -518.4) o 4.71.
  TANK POSITION: hold him on that centre facing away; Shred Psyche 307937 /
  313276 is the tank hit and there is no positional mechanic on the tank.

5.2 PHASES [GUIDE] - two intermissions, with a script that predates them
  HEALTH PERCENTAGE: 66% and 33%. Blizzard's hotfix note is explicit:
  "Prophet Skitra now enters intermission phases at 33% and 66% health (was
  20%, 40%, 60% and 80%)". So in 8.3.7 there are exactly two intermissions
  (Illusionary Projections), at 66% and at 33%.
  DISCREPANCY: the script implements the pre-hotfix cadence - phase 2 at
  HealthBelowPct(81) and back to phase 1 at HealthBelowPct(75) - i.e. a single
  illusion window that opens just under 81% and closes at 75%. On your server
  that is what actually happens; the guides describe the hotfixed version.
  Berserk 480 s [DBM] (the module notes he only gains +300% damage, so the
  berserk is survivable).

5.3 MAIN ABILITY TIMERS, seconds from pull [DBM]
  Shred Psyche 307937        first 12.8 (15.2 later), cooldown 37.7
  Images of Absolution 313239 first 30.5 (33.9 later), cooldown 84.9
  Script schedule: Shadow Shock 3 s (repeating), Shred Psyche 15 s,
  Absolutions 30 s on Heroic and Mythic only.

5.4 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) Illusionary Projection 307445 - assigned to EVERY PLAYER SEPARATELY, and
     this is the one mechanic in the raid where two bots must disagree. Each
     unit sees its own set of images and must kill its own Image of Absolution
     313239 / Intangible Illusion 313208 / Surging Images 313210. The AI needs
     a per-unit target override; a shared target is wrong by definition.
  b) Dark Ritual 309657 - assigned to everybody in the illusion window; the
     script applies it to the whole raid every 5 s while phase 2 lasts.
  c) Psychic Outburst 309687 - assigned to the HEALERS (raid hit after each
     set of images).
  d) Mindquake 307864 / Psychic Reverberation 312741 - assigned to NOBODY,
     dodged.
  e) Shred Psyche 307937 / 313276 - assigned to the TANK.
  f) Clouded Mind 307784 / Twisted Mind 307785 - soak/split flags.

5.5 SAFE SPOTS
  Verified: the 60-yard disc around (-1487.0, -580.0, -518.4) - the script
  places both the projections and the boss's blink on a random point inside it.
  There is no fixed safe spot; the rule is "the image you can see is yours,
  everyone else's are not".
