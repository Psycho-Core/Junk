SERPENTSHRINE CAVERN (map 548)
1. HYDROSS THE UNSTABLE
PHASES: frost form <-> nature form each time he crosses
  the platform line; every switch adds +10% damage taken
  (mark stacks) and spawns 4 elementals of the NEW form.
TANK: two tanks, one per side of the line; pull him over
  the line only on the call - the group must be through
  the poison water before he switches.
GROUP: frost side: spread (frost bolts), nature side:
  move out of poison; kill switch elementals at once.
GROUND: poison water off the platform; frost patches.
ADD ORDER: P1 switch elementals, boss between switches.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Water Tomb, Mark of Hydross,
  Vile Sludge, summon elementals.
AI RULE: never fight him astride the line - a form you
  did not call for is a wipe in progress.

2. THE LURKER BELOW
PHASES: above water (tank/spout) then submerged (~60 sec)
  with island adds; emerges and repeats.
TANK: hold centre of the ring platform.
GROUP: when he readies Spout, JUMP IN THE WATER - the
  spout sweeps the platform and knocks everyone off;
  whirl hits melee. Submerged: kill island Naga, watch
  for his re-emerge under a platform section.
GROUND: the water is the safe zone, not a hazard.
ADD ORDER: P1 island adds while submerged, boss up top.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Spout, Whirl, Geyser, Submerge.
AI RULE: at the Spout cast, get wet - nobody stands on
  the platform through the sweep.

3. LEOTHERAS THE BLIND
PHASES: elf form (Whirlwind cycles) alternating demon
  form (Chaos Blast on the warlock-tank); at 15% both
  forms at once. Inner Demons spawn for 5 players in
  demon phase - kill YOUR OWN demon or be mind-controlled.
TANK: plate tank elf form; a fire-resist warlock tanks
  demon form at range.
GROUP: Whirlwind: run from him (he chases random players,
  untauntable in it). Silence/shadow debuff: decurse team.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: P1 your own Inner Demon, boss otherwise.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Whirlwind, Chaos Blast,
  Insidious Whisper (Inner Demons), Consuming Madness.
AI RULE: if a demon with your face appears, it is your
  only target until it is dead.

4. FATHOM-LORD KARATHRESS + 3 ADVISORS
PHASES: council: kill advisors then the boss (he gains
  each dead advisor's key ability - kill order chooses
  what he inherits last/best avoided).
TANK: one tank each; pull them apart to separate corners.
GROUP: kill order (page): Tidalvess (shaman) first -
  he deals the most raid damage; then Sharkkis (hunter);
  Caribdis (priest) LAST: she is the easiest to control,
  with a small assigned group interrupting every heal
  (page: rogue/warlock slows help). Spitfire Totems die
  on sight while advisors live. He inherits from each
  corpse (page): shaman -> he drops Spitfire Totems,
  hunter -> The Beast Within (permanent melee buff),
  priest -> Tidal Surge (10-yd freeze, 15-20 sec).
GROUND: Spitfire Totem (target, kill it, not a zone).
ADD ORDER: P1 Tidalvess, P2 Sharkkis + his pet, P3
  Caribdis (interrupt-controlled until her turn), P4 boss.
ABILITY (unverified ids): advisor abilities per class,
  Cataclysmic Bolt, The Beast Within pet (enrage pet).
AI RULE: a healing advisor alive means nothing else can
  die - she goes first, totems die on sight.

5. MOROGRIM TIDEWALKER
PHASES: single phase; Watery Graves teleport 4 players
  to the fountains (they take fall/drown pressure), murloc
  packs flood from the doors each grave cycle; below 25%
  the graves stop and the room floods harder (unverified).
TANK: hold him at his spawn; add tank collects murlocs.
GROUP: freed grave players run back fast; AoE murloc
  packs on the add tank; Earthquake stuns the room -
  healers pre-HoT.
GROUND: fountain graves (teleport destination).
ADD ORDER: P1 murloc packs, boss between.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Tidal Wave, Watery Grave,
  Earthquake, summon murlocs.
AI RULE: murlocs first, every time - they are the raid
  damage, he is only the timer.

6. LADY VASHJ
PHASES: P1 to 70%; P2 shielded/untargetable centre: adds
  stream in; Tainted Elementals drop Tainted Cores - a
  core carrier is rooted, so cores are PASSED player to
  player to the 4 Shield Generators; all 4 off ends P2.
  P3: P1 again plus Toxic Spore Bats above the platform.
TANK: P1/P3 hold her centre; P2 tanks hold Elites/Striders.
GROUP: spread 5+ yd (Static Charge ticks on neighbours);
  Static Charge carrier runs out too. Core relay: stand
  in a chain, throw, next - never let a core sit. Kill
  Striders (fear aura) at range, Elites in melee.
GROUND: P3 spore clouds dropped by bats (poison zones
  grow over the platform - DPS race).
ADD ORDER: P2 P1 Tainted Elementals (cores), Striders,
  Elites, Enchanted Elementals before they reach her
  (they buff her); P3 bats ignored, boss burn.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Shoot, Multi-Shot, Shock Blast,
  Entangle, Static Charge, Forked Lightning (P2 cone).
AI RULE: in P2 you are a relay team, not a DPS - move the
  core, everything else is second.

TEMPEST KEEP - THE EYE (map 550)
7. AL'AR
PHASES: P1 on the platforms (flies between them; quills
  if nobody is up top); at death P2: reborn on the floor,
  tanked centre, Flame Patches and meteor dives; dies for
  real at 0% (only the P2 kill counts).
TANK: P1 platform tank follows him up ramps; P2 centre,
  move him out of flame patches.
GROUP: P1 ranged on the floor kill ember adds when he
  leaves a platform; P2 spread for meteors, move patches.
GROUND: Flame Patch, Meteor impact zones (P2).
ADD ORDER: P1 embers when he moves, boss; P2 boss only.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Quill, Flame Buffet, Meteor,
  Dive Bomb, Rebirth.
AI RULE: if he leaves your platform, kill what he left
  behind and follow - do not chase him mid-air.

8. VOID REAVER
PHASES: single phase, 10-min enrage (era).
TANK: hold centre; knockback drops tank threat - second
  tank builds backup threat continuously.
GROUP: Arcane Orbs fall on ranged positions - the ranged
  ring keeps moving; melee stay (orbs target range).
  Pounding: 3-sec room shake, heal through.
GROUND: Arcane Orb impact circles at range.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Arcane Orb, Pounding,
  Knock Away.
AI RULE: ranged never stands still; melee never leaves
  his feet.

9. HIGH ASTROMANCER SOLARIAN
PHASES: P1 spread fight (Wrath of the Astromancer = bomb
  player runs out); at 20% P2: she vanishes, adds waves,
  then she returns as a voidwalker (shadow phase) - burn.
  Agents/priests in add cycles throughout (unverified splits).
TANK: P1 standard; burn phase tank swaps by threat.
GROUP: bomb = run from everyone before it pops; spread so
  Arcane Missiles do not splash; kill priest adds for heals
  interrupt, agents AoE.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: P1 priest adds, agents, boss; P2 boss burn.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Wrath of the Astromancer,
  Arcane Missiles, Blinding Light, Void form.
AI RULE: if the bomb is on you, you are the ground effect
  - leave the group.

10. KAEL'THAS SUNSTRIDER
PHASES (page): P1 four Advisors one at a time (half HP);
  P2 his seven legendary Weapons - kill and LOOT/equip
  them (they break P3/P4 mechanics); P3 all 4 Advisors
  resurrected at full HP together; P4 Kael'thas himself
  (Pyroblasts need the looted shield/ward; Phoenixes);
  P5 at 50%: Gravity Lapse cycles - swim in the air,
  avoid orbs/clouds; enrage ~15 min (unverified).
TANK: advisor tanks by role - Thaladred fixates (Gaze:
  the gazed player KITES, nobody tanks through it),
  Sanguinar fears, Capernian caster at range, Telonicus
  bombs. P4 tank centre, shield-bearer for Pyroblast.
GROUP: P2 kill order: shield/ward users first, equip;
  P3 standard order Thaladred > Telonicus > Sanguinar >
  Capernian (era, unverified); P4 interrupt Fireballs,
  burn Phoenix eggs before hatch, MC'd companions CC'd.
GROUND: P5 Nether Vapor clouds; Flamestrike circles.
ADD ORDER: as phased; eggs P1 in P4; NEVER waste the
  weapon phase - unlooted weapons mean unwinnable P4.
ABILITY (unverified ids): advisors' Rend/Gaze/Fear/
  Conflagration/bombs, Fireball (I), Pyroblast, Phoenix,
  Gravity Lapse, Nether Beam.
AI RULE: loot your weapon in P2 - the fight is won there.

HYJAL SUMMIT (map 534)
11. RAGE WINTERCHILL / 12. ANETHERON / 13. KAZ'ROGAL /
14. AZGALOR / 15. ARCHIMONDE - wave format, 8 trash
waves (allied NPC camps help) before each boss.
11 WINTERCHILL: Death and Decay under players (move);
  Icebolt freezes a player (heal them through/nuke ice);
  frost nova melee. AI: out of the green decay, fast.
12 ANETHERON: Carrion Swarm cone (face him away); Sleep
  on a player (dispel - D-Magic); Infernal drops on a
  player (offtank holds it away; do not kill priority -
  it despawns, unverified). AI: sleep dispel beats DPS.
13 KAZ'ROGAL: his Mark drains mana then explodes the
  mana-less - casters dump mana early/use abilities,
  stand spread; cleave. AI: never sit at full mana late.
14 AZGALOR: Rain of Fire zones; Silence cone; Doom on a
  player = they die and a Doomguard spawns (offtank it).
  AI: the doomed player runs to the add tank, then dies
  useful.
15 ARCHIMONDE: the set-piece: Air Burst knocks a player
  sky-high - USE the Tears of the Goddess (given at the
  pull) before landing or die; Doomfire trails chase
  players (run them out, never through the group); Fear
  cycles; Grip of the Legion (decursive - D-Curse);
  Soul Charge bursts after deaths. Wisps at 10% end it.
  AI RULE (whole raid): fire runs OUT, burst means click
  your tears - falling is the boss's main weapon.
ABILITY (all unverified ids): as named per boss above.

BLACK TEMPLE (map 564)
16. HIGH WARLORD NAJ'ENTUS
PHASES: single; Impaling Spine pins a player - another
  player clicks/pulls the spine out and KEEPS it: at his
  Tidal Shield (full heal building) the spine is thrown
  to break it. Needle Spines raid-wide.
TANK: centre, face away. GROUP: spread 6 yd (needles
  splash); spine duty assigned; shield = throw instantly.
GROUND: none. ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: the spine in your bag is the boss's off-switch;
  throw it the second his shield rises.

17. SUPREMUS
PHASES: tank phase alternating kite phase (fixates a
  player, slow chase - run him through his own fires is
  wrong: run him around, never through the raid).
TANK: threat phase centre; kite phase the fixated runs.
GROUP: move out of Molten Punch (tank-side cone) and
  volcano eruptions he summons under players.
GROUND: Volcano circles + his fire trail while kiting.
ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: if he is looking at you in kite phase, run a
  wide circle and let nobody cross his path.

18. SHADE OF AKAMA
PHASES: defend Akama: waves of Ashtongue (channelers
  first - they hold the Shade), then the Shade freed -
  burn phase with Akama helping.
TANK/GROUP: kill Channelers P1, defenders tanked at the
  stairs; when he breaks, all-in before adds overwhelm.
ADD ORDER: P1 Channelers, other adds, then Shade.
AI RULE: the channelers are the boss's shield - they die
  before anything else.

19. TERON GOREFIEND
PHASES: single; randomly a player becomes a GHOST (70-sec
  class change): kill the Shadowy Constructs your ghost
  spawns inside you or they walk to the raid and wipe it.
  Incinerate/crush on tank; death-and-decay style aura.
TANK: centre; GROUP: spread; ghost duty: use ghost bars
  (AoE frost nova chains) and clear all 4 constructs
  BEFORE returning.
ADD ORDER: constructs (ghost-only) P1, boss.
AI RULE: as a ghost, nothing in the living world matters
  until your constructs are dead.

20. GURTOGG BLOODBOIL
PHASES: single; Bloodboil hits the 5 FURTHEST players
  (stacking DoT) - two ranged groups rotate as soakers;
  Fel Rage cycle: a player balloons (huge threat/heals
  received buff) and becomes his target - heal them hard.
TANK: swap on Acidic Wound stacks; rage target is not
  tankable - kite/heal.
GROUP: soak groups swap when DoTs stack high; disarm
  him during rage windows (era tactic, unverified).
GROUND: none.
AI RULE: ranged groups trade the boil - nobody soaks
  twice in a row.

21. RELIQUARY OF SOULS
PHASES: three faces: Suffering (tank threat = pain:
  damage reflects on threat - DPS slow, tank aura),
  Desire (Spirit Shock interrupt chain; damage taken is
  shared back - stop on her casts), Anger (Seethe/rage
  race, Soul Scream). Between faces, add waves of Enraged
  Souls - never let them merge back (they heal her).
TANK/GROUP: face rules above; interrupt Desire's Spirit
  Shock (I) as top raid priority.
ADD ORDER: P1 Enraged Souls between faces, face.
AI RULE: each face punishes a different habit - on
  Desire, your interrupt is your DPS.

22. MOTHER SHAHRAZ
PHASES: single; Fatal Attraction teleports 3 linked
  players - they explode on each other if close: run
  APART in three directions instantly. Beams rotate
  debuffs (sinister strikes - unverified detail).
TANK: centre, shadow-resist framing era.
GROUP: spread attractions, never run through others;
  saber lash soakers share the frontal with the tank.
GROUND: attraction explosion (player-centred).
ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: linked to two others? Sprint away from both -
  the triangle must stretch until it snaps.

23. ILLIDARI COUNCIL (4)
PHASES: shared-health council: Malande (priest: heals -
  interrupt, Reflective Shield - stop DPS), Gathios
  (paladin tank), Zerevor (mage: Flamestrike circles,
  Blizzard - move), Veras (rogue: vanishes, poison).
GROUP: interrupt Malande's heals first forever; move out
  of Flamestrike/Blizzard; Veras's target gets heals.
ADD ORDER: shared pool - focus targets by call: Malande
  casts P1 interrupt, boss DPS split by assignment.
AI RULE: a council heal landing undoes a minute of work
  - interrupts outrank damage.

24. ILLIDAN STORMRAGE
PHASES (page): P1 glaives: Shear on the tank (active
  mitigation or -60% max health), Parasitic Shadowfiend
  (carrier runs out; fiends spawn if it expires on you);
  65% P2 flight: two Flames of Azzinoth from the glaives
  (fire-elemental tanks hold them apart on the grate
  lines; Eyebeam sweeps - run the room's lanes);
  P3 ground again + Agonizing Flames; from then demon
  form cycles (P4: Shadow Demons chase fixated players -
  kite them into Maiev's traps later; warlock-tank the
  demon with shadow kit, era); 30% P5 Maiev joins: traps
  cage him (tank drags him onto a trap when he enrages).
TANK: three roles over the fight - MT (Shear mitigation),
  2 flame tanks P2, demon-form tank by resistance.
GROUP: spread for Draw Soul/Flame Crash (move him out);
  fiend carriers out instantly; beam = move laterally.
GROUND: Flame Crash patches, Eyebeam lane, Maiev traps.
ADD ORDER: P1 Flames P2 (both, apart), Shadow Demons
  kited/CC, boss.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Shear, Parasitic Shadowfiend,
  Draw Soul, Flame Crash, Eyebeam, Agonizing Flames,
  Shadow Demon, Enrage (trap-cageable).
AI RULE: if it grows inside you (Shadowfiend), leave the
  group; if a demon has your name, run to a trap.

SUNWELL PLATEAU (map 580)
25. KALECGOS
PHASES: two realms at once: dragon outside (Kalecgos),
  demon Sathrovarr inside the portal realm. Portal cycle:
  assigned players click portals in rotation (Exhaustion
  stops repeat trips); if either half dies alone, enrage
  - kill both within moments. Corruption of Kalecgos:
  the dragon must be "won" while Sathrovarr dies inside.
TANK: one per realm; swap groups on portal calls.
GROUP: portal rotation strictly; inside healers keep the
  Kalec (human) NPC alive.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: the two halves together - call DPS % across
  realms.
AI RULE: your portal is a shift change - go on call,
  never twice in a row.

26. BRUTALLUS
PHASES: single, 6-min enrage (era gear check). Meteor
  Slash frontal stacking (tank swap on stacks); Burn on
  a player: spreads to neighbours on contact - the burn
  player stands ALONE behind the dragon until it ends;
  Stomp on tank.
TANK: two tanks swap Meteor Slash stacks at the sides,
  raid behind.
GROUP: burned player isolates; never cross in front.
GROUND: Burn (player-carried, spreads by touch).
ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: burning means leave - your damage output now is
  standing alone.

27. FELMYST
PHASES: ground ~60 sec: Gas Nova raid pulse (mass dispel
  instantly, D-Magic - page: it wipes even an 80 raid if
  not dispelled), Encapsulate on a player (page's level-80
  note: heal through it, do not run - earlier era guides
  said run out; unverified which this server wants), she
  is TAUNT-IMMUNE (page). Air phase: Demonic Vapor twice
  (a beam chases a marked player for ~10 sec leaving a
  lethal DoT trail that summons skeletons - kite it AWAY,
  never step in the trail, more skeletons spawn if you
  do), then Fog of Corruption three times, each covering
  a third of the ground: anyone caught is mind-controlled
  until killed - CC/kill them. Then she lands.
TANK: ground centre, frontal Cleave/Corrosion away from
  the raid (page); skeleton tank sweeps up vapor adds.
GROUP: air phase: spot her line like a deep breath and
  shift the whole raid north/south of it (page).
GROUND: Demonic Vapor trails + Fog of Corruption thirds.
ADD ORDER: P1 skeletons, boss on ground.
AI RULE: in the air phase the floor is a map, not a
  floor - be on the safe third before the fog lands.

28. EREDAR TWINS (SACROLASH + ALYTHESS)
PHASES: two bosses, shared room mechanics: kill order
  decides loot/abilities: Sacrolash (shadow) first is the
  era standard: Confounding Blow drops tank threat
  (swap), Alythess's Conflagration on a player (run out),
  Pyrogenics buff (spellsteal/dispel - D-Magic on her).
TANK: one per twin, held apart at the ledge/door.
GROUP: conflaged player out; shadow nova spread.
GROUND: flame patches (unverified).
ADD ORDER: P1 Sacrolash, P2 Alythess (era order).
AI RULE: fire on you = out; threat drop = swap - call
  both, every time.

29. M'URU
PHASES: P1: every 60 sec, 1 Shadowsword Fury Mage + 2
  Shadowsword Berserkers run in from BOTH sides (6 adds a
  minute, page); separately he summons Void Sentinels
  (tanked, they pulse AoE); Darkness every 45 sec covers
  the centre circle (~3000/sec, unhealable inside) and
  spawns 8 Dark Fiends. He himself beams Negative Energy
  at 4-5 random players all phase. P2 at his death:
  ENTROPIUS - burn while Void Spawns trickle.
TANK: side tanks catch each wave; Sentinel tank separate
  (they hit 8-10k and slow attacks - page).
GROUP: Fury Mages' Fel Fireball casts interrupted/CC'd
  (they are controllable, page); kill Void Spawns fast
  (each Sentinel death makes 8); move out for Darkness
  and kill Dark Fiends before they detonate.
GROUND: Darkness central void zone (45-sec cycle).
ADD ORDER: P1 door waves (casters first), boss between,
  P2 boss burn over adds.
AI RULE: doors first, centre second - waves that pile up
  end the fight regardless of boss DPS.

30. KIL'JAEDEN
PHASES (page): P1 three Hands of the Deceiver, tanked
  APART (volleys stack shadow damage); their portals
  spawn exploding fiends (kill at range, they explode on
  death too). P2 Kil'jaeden: Soul Flay on top threat;
  Fire Bloom on players (spread - it chains); P3 (85%-
  ish thresholds unverified): Darkness - huge cast, hide
  behind the Dragon Orb shield (a player clicks orb,
  becomes dragon, shields the raid); reflections (Sinister
  Reflections of players - kill/CC copies); P4/P5 more
  orbs, Armageddon meteors (soak/move), final burn with
  Anveena's sacrifice buff.
TANK: hands apart P1; boss tanked centre P2 on.
GROUP: orb duty assigned per Darkness; spread blooms;
  kill reflections fast (they are you, with your spells).
GROUND: Armageddon meteor circles, fire bloom.
ADD ORDER: P1 fiends at range, reflections P1 in P3+,
  boss.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Soul Flay, Fire Bloom, Darkness
  of a Thousand Souls, Sinister Reflection, Armageddon.
AI RULE: when the sky goes dark, be under the dragon's
  shield - nothing else survives Darkness.
