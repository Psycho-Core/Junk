SERPENTSHRINE CAVERN
====================================================
1. HYDROSS THE UNSTABLE
PHASES: One boss, two forms. Pure (frost) on pull;
crossing the boundary line switches him to Venom
(nature) form and back. Each switch resets threat
and spawns 4 elemental adds matching the new form.
TANK: Tank at the boundary; drag him across only
when the mark stack forces a switch (era: about 4
stacks / 60 sec; damage taken rises to +100%).
Stop DoTs near a switch so adds are clean.
GROUP: Stack for heals in each form phase; kill the
4 spawned elementals before returning to the boss.
GROUND: Water Tomb / form pools - id unverified.
ADD ORDER: P1 the 4 adds of the new form on every
switch (entries unverified). No do-not-kill adds.
AI RULE: never let the mark stack past the switch
call; adds first after every crossing.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Mark of Hydross /
Mark of Corruption (stacking, form-specific);
Vile Sludge; Water Tomb. I: none verified.
D: unverified.

2. THE LURKER BELOW
PHASES: P1 surfaced. At about 2 min he submerges
for 60 sec (adds only), then resurfaces; repeat.
TANK: Face him away; Whirl knocks back within 5 yd.
GROUP: Spout starts at the tank and rotates over
360 degrees - everyone jumps into the water to
dodge it. Geyser: 2500-3500 Frost + 10-yd knockback,
spread out. Submerged: kill/banish the 9 adds.
GROUND: none verified.
ADD ORDER: P1 Coilfang Ambushers (2 per platform,
3 platforms) + Coilfang Guardians (3, centre) -
entries unverified; CC is allowed, do not let an
Ambusher evade (despawn/respawn risk).
AI RULE: jump in the water on Spout, every time.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Spout; Whirl;
Geyser; Submerge. I: none verified. D: unverified.

3. LEOTHERAS THE BLIND
PHASES: P1 elf form (Whirlwind chases a fixated
target). P2 demon form (Inner Demons spawn).
Alternates until 15%, then both forms together.
TANK: Elf form is taunt-immune. Demon form: fire-
resistance tank (era: warlock tank) at range.
GROUP: Whirlwind victim kites; the bleed it leaves
is removed only by Divine Shield / Blessing of
Protection / Ice Block. Inner Demon (about 11k HP)
must be killed BY ITS OWNER before it finishes,
or that player is mind-controlled.
GROUND: Chaos Blast pools in demon form -
id unverified.
ADD ORDER: P1 Inner Demon of each targeted player
(entries unverified) - each owner kills their own.
AI RULE: your demon, your kill - before its cast
ends, or you turn on the raid.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Whirlwind; Insidious
Whisper (Inner Demons); Chaos Blast; Consuming
Madness. I: unverified. D: unverified.

4. FATHOM-LORD KARATHRESS
PHASES: One phase, boss + 3 adds. Each add that
dies gives Karathress that add's ability.
TANK: One tank per add and one on the boss; keep
them apart. Boss tank picks up gained abilities.
GROUP: Interrupt Caribdis' Healing Wave (1.5-sec
cast, heals about 100k+ - solo-heal reset risk).
Spitfire Totem (from Tidalvess) is an instant
ranged macro-kill.
GROUND: Spitfire Totem zone, Tidal Surge -
ids unverified.
ADD ORDER: P1 Fathom-Guard Tidalvess, P2 Fathom-
Guard Sharkkis, P3 Fathom-Guard Caribdis, P4
Karathress (all entries unverified). Order matters:
he inherits Spitfire Totem, The Beast Within and
Tidal Surge as each falls.
AI RULE: totem dies the second it appears; heals
are interrupted, always.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Cataclysmic Bolt;
Healing Wave (I); Spitfire Totem; The Beast Within;
Tidal Surge. D: unverified.

5. MOROGRIM TIDEWALKER
PHASES: One phase. At 25% he begins summoning
Watery Globules instead of Watery Graves.
TANK: Hold him at the room edge; Earthquake pulls
the side murloc packs - off-tanks gather them into
the AoE zone. Tidal Wave slows attack speed
(-400% debuff, unverified wording) - swap if needed.
GROUP: Watery Grave (never on top threat): 4 players
teleported to the water, burst them free / heal
through. At 25%: kite Watery Globules away and
burst the boss (burst damage about 6000 on reach).
GROUND: Watery Grave splash zone - id unverified.
ADD ORDER: P1 pulled murloc packs after each
Earthquake (entries unverified). P2 Watery
Globules: NEVER let them reach a player - kite,
do not tank them in the raid.
AI RULE: globules are kited, never soaked.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Tidal Wave;
Earthquake; Watery Grave; Summon Watery Globule.
I: none verified. D: unverified.

6. LADY VASHJ
PHASES: P1 (100-70%) tank and spank + Static Charge.
P2 (70-50%) she is shielded: 4 Shield Generators
must be disabled with Tainted Cores. P3 (50-0%)
P1 mechanics plus Toxic Spore Bats.
TANK: P1/P3 main tank centre. P2: Coilfang Elites
tanking in the centre; Striders kited by a ranged
tank (Panic Aura fears within 8 yd).
GROUP: P2 - kill Tainted Elementals fast (they
despawn in about 15-20 sec) and pass the Tainted
Core to a player who disables a generator. Do NOT
let Enchanted Elementals reach her (+5% damage
each, stacking). P3: move out of Toxic Spores
clouds (about 1500/sec).
GROUND: Toxic Spores (id unverified); Static Charge
(id unverified) - spread in P1/P3.
ADD ORDER P2: P1 Tainted Elemental (core carrier),
P2 Coilfang Strider (kite + DoT, never melee-tank),
P3 Coilfang Elite, P4 Enchanted Elemental (NEVER
let it reach Vashj - kill before contact; it is a
kill add, the ban is on contact, not damage).
AI RULE: cores disable generators; nothing touches
the boss in P2.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Static Charge;
Forked Lightning; Shock Blast; Entangle; Toxic
Spores. I: unverified. D: unverified.

4. KAEL'THAS SUNSTRIDER
PHASES: P1 four advisors, one at a time. P2 seven
legendary weapons. P3 all advisors return together
(3-min timer). P4 Kael'thas himself. P5 at 50%:
Gravity Lapse cycles. Weapons despawn at 15 min;
no hard enrage.
TANK: Thaladred fixates (gaze) - the target kites
him, staying over 10 yd. Sanguinar: fear (Bellowing
Roar) - fear-break rotation. Capernian is ranged-
tanked (Arcane Explosion if anyone is in melee).
Telonicus: do NOT kill him while the tank carries
his Remote Toy stun debuff - wait it out.
GROUP: P2 loot the weapons and EQUIP them (they are
the fight's buff). Kill order P2: Devastation
(longbow) first, Cosmic Infuser second (interrupt
its heals), then the rest. P4: interrupt Fireball;
move out of Flamestrike; Phoenix every 30-40 sec -
kill the Phoenix, then its egg (about 70k HP,
15 sec to hatch) before it re-hatches. Pyroblast
cycle: he shields (Shock Barrier, about 80k absorb)
- burst the shield and interrupt the 2nd Pyroblast.
Mind Control: CC the controlled, do not kill them.
P5: during Gravity Lapse (30 sec) swim in the air,
avoid Nether Vapor clouds (-10% max HP per stack),
and break Nether Beam chains by spreading.
GROUND: Flamestrike zone; Nether Vapor clouds -
ids unverified.
ADD ORDER: P3: P1 Thaladred, P2 Sanguinar, P3
Telonicus / Capernian before the timer. P4: P1
Phoenix, P2 Phoenix Egg. Advisors and weapons:
entries unverified.
AI RULE: weapons are looted and worn, eggs die
before they hatch, controlled allies are CC'd,
never killed.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Fireball (I);
Flamestrike; Pyroblast (I behind shield-break);
Shock Barrier; Mind Control; Gravity Lapse;
Nether Beam; Bellowing Roar; Conflagration;
Remote Toy. D: unverified.

2. ANETHERON
PHASES: One phase, 10-min berserk.
TANK: Two tanks - boss tank and Infernal tank
(fire-resist helps). Vampiric Aura heals him for
300% of his melee damage: keep Mortal Strike /
Wound Poison up.
GROUP: Carrion Swarm (every about 15 sec): cone,
3-6k Shadow + healing done -75% for 15 sec -
healers spread so one cone cannot catch them all.

the Forsaken / Tremor Totem do NOT work, per page).

in Immolation.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Carrion Swarm;
Sleep; Vampiric Aura; Inferno; Immolation (add).
I: none. D: unverified.

3. KAZ'ROGAL
PHASES: One phase; Mark of Kaz'rogal accelerates:

TANK: Single tank at the Thrall camp; NPCs add DPS.
GROUP: Mana users: when your mana is nearly gone,
move AWAY from the raid before the mark makes you

manage mana aggressively; non-mana classes stack

GROUND: none verified.

ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Mark of Kaz'rogal;

4. AZGALOR
PHASES: One phase, 10-min enrage. A raid member is
Doomed every 45 sec - deaths are scheduled, so the
fight is survival, not a pure burn.
TANK: Boss tank (melee 6-9k on plate, no crushes)
plus 1-2 Doomguard tanks. Face Cleave (5 targets,
melee +2000) away.
GROUP: Rain of Fire (15 yd, 1619-1881 per 2 sec,
10 sec) - move out; standing in it adds
Unquenchable Flames (1250/sec, 5 sec). Howl of
Azgalor: raid-wide 5-sec silence, about 15-sec CD -
pre-HoT the tank, stop melee swings during silence.
Ranged beyond 30 yd cannot be Rain-targeted.
Doom: the target dies after 20 sec, no dispel -
use the time to position, then a Lesser Doomguard
spawns there.
GROUND: Rain of Fire - id unverified.
ADD ORDER: P1 Lesser Doomguard (about 145k HP;
Thrash, Cripple -50% move/attack, Warstomp 1250 +
2-sec stun) - entries unverified.
AI RULE: fire first, healing second - nobody casts
through Rain of Fire.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Rain of Fire; Howl
of Azgalor; Cleave; Doom (D: none). I: none.

5. ARCHIMONDE
PHASES: One phase; Hand of Death (35354) kills the
raid at 9 min or if he reaches Nordrassil. Every
player MUST carry Tears of the Goddess (from
Tyrande in camp) for Air Burst.
TANK: Tank anywhere open; stance-dance / fear-break
his Fear - a feared tank drags him toward the tree.
GROUP: This is individual survival. Doomfire leaves
moving fire trails - keep 15-20 yd from every trail,
run in straight lines, never in circles. Grip of
the Legion (curse): decurse INSTANTLY - decursers
spread across the raid. Air Burst: at the top of
the launch, wait about 3 sec, then use Tears of
the Goddess (2-sec slow fall) - too late is death.
Any player death gives him a Soul Charge, which he
spends to kill another player - deaths chain.
Finger of Death (32111): only if nobody is in melee
range - never let the raid be fully out of melee.
GROUND: Doomfire trails - id unverified.
ADD ORDER: none.
AI RULE: nobody dies - one death starts the chain.
ABILITY LIST: 32111 Finger of Death; 35354 Hand
of Death; Air Burst, Doomfire, Grip of the Legion
(D-Curse), Fear - ids unverified. I: none.
