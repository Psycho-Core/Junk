---------------------------------------------------------------

- XP Lock Debuff / Flag Behavior:
  * Retail 8.2.0-8.3.7: Applies self-buff Experience Eliminated (Spell ID 306715) to the XP-locked player and applies Experience Eliminated (Spell ID 206662, 300-yard aura reducing XP gained from kills and quests by 95%) to all members of a manually formed party (does not affect matchmade LFG strangers).
  * Stock TrinityCore 8.3.7 (build 35662): Sets PLAYER_FLAGS_NO_XP_GAIN (0x02000000) on the booster without applying Spell ID 306715 or Spell ID 206662, so manually invited party members receive no -95% XP penalty unless custom-scripted.

Party Formation and Difficulty:
- Invite the level 111 booster and the low-level player into a 2-player party (do not convert to Raid; raid groups disable dungeon quests and penalize XP).
- Set Dungeon Difficulty to Normal 5-Player (DifficultyID 1). Heroic is DifficultyID 2; Mythic is DifficultyID 23; Timewalking is DifficultyID 24.
- Group Kill XP Distance: Keep the low-level player alive and within 74 yards of dying enemies on TrinityCore (MaxGroupXPDistance = 74) and within 100 yards on Retail 8.3.7.
- Summoning to Portals: Use the exterior Meeting Stone (requires 2 party members clicking; ignores dungeon max-level range) or Warlock Ritual of Summoning (Spell ID 698, Portal GameObject ID 194108).

Queueing and Entry by Level Range:
- Levels 10 to 14 (Walk-In Portal Only):
  * Dungeon Finder is locked below level 15.
  * Both players walk through the physical portal on Normal (DifficultyID 1). Only dungeons with Portal Minimum Level 10 (or 11 at level 11+) allow entry.
- Levels 15 to 109 (Two Entry Modes):
  * Standard Dungeon Finder Queue Check: Without Party Sync, a level 111 booster and a level 15-109 player have zero overlapping LFD dungeons because Classic (max 60), TBC/WotLK (max 80), Cata/MoP (max 90), WoD (max 100), and Legion (max 110) lock out level 111 as too high level.
  * Mode A - Party Sync Dungeon Finder (Patch 8.2.5+): Open Map & Quest Log and start Party Sync; both players accept. Booster effective level scales down to the low-level player's expansion cap:
    - Low-level player 15-60 (Classic): Booster scaled to 60. Queue Random Classic Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 258) or specific unlocked dungeons.
    - Low-level player 60-80 (TBC / WotLK): Booster scaled to 80. Queue Random Burning Crusade Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 259) or Random Lich King Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 261) (available from 58 if queuing TBC/WotLK).
    - Low-level player 80-90 (Cata / MoP): Booster scaled to 90. Queue Random Mists of Pandaria Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 463) or Random Cataclysm Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 300).
    - Low-level player 90-100 (WoD): Booster scaled to 100. Queue Random Warlords of Draenor Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 788).
    - Low-level player 100-110 (Legion): Booster scaled to 110. Queue Random Legion Dungeon (LfgDungeonID 1045) or specific Legion Normal at 98+.
    - Party Sync restrictions: Temporarily disables booster Azerite traits, Heart of Azeroth Essences, Legion Legendaries, socketed gems, enchants, trinket procs, and above-bracket talents/abilities while synced.
    - LFD Timers and Debuffs: Role Check 45 sec; Queue Proposal 45 sec; Random Dungeon Cooldown (Spell ID 71328, 15 min from queue start); Dungeon Deserter (Spell ID 71041, 30 min on early leave); Luck of the Draw (Spell ID 72221, +5% damage/healing/health per random player). LFD teleports directly inside and bypasses the hourly walk-in instance cap.
    - TrinityCore 35662 Note: Stock LFGMgr checks raw player level rather than Party Sync effective level; if Party Sync LFD is not implemented on the server, use Mode B (Walk-In Portal) until level 110.
  * Mode B - Walk-In Physical Portal (Levels 10-109, Unsynced):
    - Both players walk into the physical portal on Normal (DifficultyID 1). After clearing, both zone out and Party Leader uses Reset All Instances.
    - Instance Lockout Limit: 10 instances per hour per account/character on Retail 8.3.7 (stock TrinityCore config AccountInstancesPerHour = 5 unless raised to 10).
    - Gray-Mob XP Penalty Warning (Levels 10-97): Level 111 gray threshold is level 101 (111 - 10). In dungeons capped at 60, 80, 90, or 100, mobs are gray to an unsynced 111 booster. Retail 8.3.7 applies ~90% mob-XP carry reduction; TrinityCore 35662 sets _isFullXP = false, multiplying solo mob XP by (playerLevel / (playerLevel + 111)) / 2. In Legion Normal dungeons (98-110), mobs scale to 110 for the 111 booster (110 > 101, green/non-gray).
- Levels 110 to 120 (Battle for Azeroth Bracket - Peak 111 Booster Efficiency):
  * Disable Party Sync. Keep the group at exactly 2 players (111 booster + 110-119 player) on Normal (DifficultyID 1) and walk through the physical Freehold portal (Map ID 1754).
  * Horde Access to Freehold at 110-119: Horde characters below 120 cannot select Kul Tiras dungeons in Dungeon Finder, but CAN enter the physical Freehold portal starting at level 110 (reach Tiragarde Sound via the Horde War Campaign foothold at 110+ or summon to Castaway Point Meeting Stone).
  * Optional LFD Queue at 110-120: Both 111 and 110-119 share the 110-120 BfA Normal LFD bracket natively (Random Dungeon Battle for Azeroth Normal, LfgDungeonID 1670), but 5-player LFD splits trash XP 5 ways instead of 2 ways.
