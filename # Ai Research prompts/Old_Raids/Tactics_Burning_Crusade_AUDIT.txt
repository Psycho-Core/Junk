CORRECTED (applied with this audit):
 1. Curator phase structure was wrong: the "soft enrage
    when his mana is spent" conflated two events. His
    mana running out triggers Evocation (20 sec, +200%
    damage taken); the SOFT enrage is at 15% health (no
    more flares/Evocations, faster Hateful Bolts), plus
    a hard enrage at 10 min. Also the Hateful Bolt target
    rule is now known, not "unverified": it hits the
    non-tank with the most threat built since the last
    Evocation, which is exactly how a soaker is assigned.
 2. Shade of Aran: the file tied Polymorph/Pyroblast to
    "~40%". It has nothing to do with 40%: Water
    Elementals (4 of them, 90-sec despawn) trigger at 40%
    HEALTH; Polymorph-drink-Pyroblast triggers at 20%
    MANA. Shadows of Aran are only the 12-minute berserk,
    not a low-health phase. Flame Wreath numbers (3
    players, 3000-4000 room-wide if triggered) and the
    Arcane Explosion cast (10 sec, run to the wall) added.
 3. Prince Malchezaar ability list was missing 39095
    Amplify Damage (+100% damage taken - the P3 tank
    spike) from the page's list; added, and Flying Axes
    noted as ramping rather than left vague.
