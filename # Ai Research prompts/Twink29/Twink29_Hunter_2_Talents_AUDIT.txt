AUDIT - Twink29_Hunter_2_Talents.txt
File under audit: 95 lines, step 2 of 3. 14 claims checked: 8 pass,
2 corrected, 4 unverified.
Sources opened for this audit: the 9.0.2 hunter talent tree as reported by
Icy Veins (Beast Mastery, Marksmanship and Survival rows 15 and 25, plus
the "what changed" list), warcraft.wiki.gg PvP talent, and the druid
talents file for the two rules that are class-independent.

1. THE STRUCTURE
   1  PASS  One row open at 29: the level 15 row. Follows from the row
            gates, and the druid file used the same rule.
   2  PASS  Rows 2 to 7 are ALT. Nothing after the first row can be spent
            at 29 under any reading of the gates.
   3  PASS  Three specialisations, picked at level 10. Same rule the druid
            file states for its four, and hunters have three.
   4  UNVERIFIED  The row gates 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 100. No page with
            a BfA row table was reachable; the file says so and this audit
            could not change that. It is consistent with the druid file,
            which is the only cross-check available.
   5  UNVERIFIED  "The spec cannot be changed inside a battleground." The
            practical half is right - a spec change needs a class trainer -
            but no page was opened for it.

2. THE LEVEL 15 COLUMNS
   6  PASS  Beast Mastery: Killer Instinct, Animal Companion, Dire Beast.
            The 9.0.2 tree lists exactly these three in the level 15 row,
            and the only Beast Mastery note in that patch is that Dire
            Beast stopped costing focus - no row move. The row is very
            likely unchanged from 8.3.7.
   7  CORRECTED  Marksmanship: the file named Master Marksman, Serpent
            Sting and Careful Aim, on the reasoning that Careful Aim must
            have been in row 1 in 8.3.7 because it sits in row 2 in 9.0.2.
            That reasoning does not hold. The 9.0.2 tree puts A Murder of
            Crows in the level 15 row and Careful Aim, Barrage and
            Explosive Shot in the level 25 row, and the 9.0.2 change list
            mentions no row moves at all, so the arrangement in that patch
            is most likely the inherited BfA arrangement. The file now
            names the two secure columns and marks the third unverified
            with its candidates, including Lone Wolf, which was a
            Marksmanship talent in BfA and is the one a pet-less bot would
            want.
   8  PASS  Survival: Viper's Venom and Terms of Engagement named, third
            column hedged. That is the honest shape. The 9.0.2 tree has
            Alpha Predator in row 1 and Guerrilla Tactics in row 2, so the
            file's hedge - Guerrilla Tactics may have been the BfA
            occupant - is the right call.
   9  UNVERIFIED  "The Marksmanship pet is optional." That depends on Lone
            Wolf, which was a talent in BfA and a baseline passive from
            Shadowlands. The file should not assume it.

3. PVP
   10 PASS  One slot at 29, opened at level 20, holding a trinket-type
            talent. warcraft.wiki.gg, PvP talent: the slots sit "in the
            lower right corner of the Talents pane", and before
            Shadowlands "there were four slots in total. The first offered
            a choice of PvP trinket-type talents", with standard classes at
            20, 40, 70 and 110. Same page and same conclusion as the druid
            audit.
   11 PASS  Taking a level 15 talent does not spend or block a PvP slot.
            Two panels, stated on the same page.
   12 PASS  Hunter PvP talents are therefore ALT at 29: with one slot and
            that slot holding the crowd-control breaker, no hunter-specific
            PvP talent can be taken. The names in the file are labelled
            unverified and stay that way - no page with BfA hunter PvP
            talents was opened.
   13 PASS  "PvP talents are active in battlegrounds and arenas, inactive
            in dungeons and raids, and in the open world only with War
            Mode." Verbatim from the wiki page.

4. WHAT IS STILL MISSING FROM THE FILE
   14 UNVERIFIED  Nothing in the file covers what the bot does with a pet,
            which for Beast Mastery is most of the spec: taming a beast at
            or below level 29, and whether exotic families - core hounds,
            devilsaurs and the rest - are gated behind a level this bracket
            does not reach. The BfA answer is believed to be that exotic
            taming is a Beast Mastery perk with a level requirement above
            29, so a 29 BM bot tames ordinary families only. That belongs
            in step 3 or in a note here, and it is not in either yet.

5. WHAT TO CHANGE
   The Marksmanship row is the only substantive fix: two named columns and
   an unverified third, with Lone Wolf added as a candidate and the
   "optional pet" line softened. Everything else either matches a page or
   is already marked unverified. The file's real contribution is that it
   says plainly what it does not know, which is most of the talent tree.
