TWINK29 - PROFESSIONS - STEP 05 - TAILORING
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Scope: what Tailoring gives a level 29 battleground twink, Classic skill
line 197, capped at 225 for a character of that level.

1. WHAT TAILORING IS AT 29
   Tailoring is the weakest of the ten professions for this bracket. It
   makes cloth gear, bags and cloth itself, and at Classic tier it has no
   permanent item enhancement at all - no armour kit, no leg armour, no
   spellthread. What it does have is one genuinely good caster item and the
   cheapest bag line in the game.
   Ranks, from the Classic guide and the trainer pages: apprentice at level
   5, journeyman at skill 75 (level 10), expert at skill 150 (level 20),
   artisan at skill 225 (level 35). Note the shape of that last gate: the
   artisan trainer is only needed to pass 225, and he wants level 35, so
   225 is the hard ceiling for a 29 - the same ceiling as the other
   professions, by a different route.
   Tailoring needs no gathering profession. Cloth drops off humanoids, so
   the profession can be paired with anything, and enchanting is the usual
   partner because tailored greens can be disenchanting fodder.

2. THE ONE PIECE WORTH WEARING
   4320 Spidersilk Boots
        Feet, cloth, 29 armour, +4 Stamina, +4 Intellect, +7 Spirit.
        Item level 25, requires level 20, binds when equipped.
        Spell 3855, Tailoring 125, difficulty 125/150/167/185, 45 second
        cast, training cost 750.
        Reagents: 2x Bolt of Silk Cloth 4305 (= 8x Silk Cloth 4306),
        4x Medium Leather 2319, 4x Spider's Silk 3182, 2x Iridescent
        Pearl 5500.
        Taught by 15 trainers, so it is on every trainer's list from the
        expert rank up.
        Fifteen stat points on a foot slot at level 20 is the best single
        item tailoring produces for this bracket, and it is the only
        tailoring piece in the 1-225 band that a 29 can wear and would
        actually want. For a cloth caster it is a real boot. Everyone else
        cannot wear cloth.

   7048 Azure Silk Hood - the next thing people ask about, and it is not
        worth it. Head, cloth, 33 armour, no stats, item level 29, requires
        level 24. Spell 8760, Tailoring 145, difficulty 145/155/160/165,
        2x Bolt of Silk Cloth + 2x blue dye + 1x Fine Thread 2321. It is a
        levelling step, not a twink item.

   Higher mageweave and runecloth pieces: Black Mageweave Gloves 10003 at
   skill 220 and Black Mageweave Headband 10024 at skill 230, Runecloth
   Belt 13856 and Runecloth Bag 14046 at 260, Runecloth Gloves 13863 at
   290. The gloves are craftable at the 225 cap; their wear level was not
   checked and is above 29. The rest are past the cap or need the artisan
   rank. ALT.

3. BAGS - WHAT A 29 ACTUALLY BUYS FROM A TAILOR
   10050 Mageweave Bag - 12 slots, item level 35.
        Spell 12065, Tailoring 225, difficulty 225/240/255/270, one minute
        cast, training cost 45.
        4x Bolt of Mageweave 4339 (= 20x Mageweave Cloth 4338) + 2x Silken
        Thread 4291.
        This sits exactly on the cap, so a 29 tailor can make the largest
        bag he will ever make. Sells for 25 copper, which is why tailors
        sell bags rather than vendor them.
   4245 Small Silk Pack - 10 slots, item level 25.
        Spell 3813, Tailoring 150, difficulty 150/170/185/200.
        3x Bolt of Silk Cloth 4305, 2x Heavy Leather 4234, 3x Fine Thread
        2321.
   4238 Linen Bag - 6 slots, item level 5. Spell 3755, Tailoring 45,
        difficulty 45/70/87/105. 3x Bolt of Linen Cloth + 3x Coarse
        Thread 2320.
   4241 Green Woolen Bag - 8 slots, item level 15. Spell 3758, Tailoring
        95, difficulty 95/120/137/155.
   4240 Woolen Bag - 8 slots by its place in the guide, spell id
        unverified. None of the three small bags is worth a twink's gold.
   14046 Runecloth Bag - 14 slots at skill 260, past the cap. ALT.
   Practical note: if the point of the profession is storage for a twink
   roster, this is the only reason to level tailoring at all, and a 225
   tailor covering five alts pays for the cloth several times over.

4. THE CLOTH ECONOMY
   The bolts, which are also the leveling backbone:
        2963  Bolt of Linen Cloth 2996     skill 1    2x Linen Cloth 2589
        2964  Bolt of Woolen Cloth 2997    skill 75   3x Wool Cloth 2592
        3839  Bolt of Silk Cloth 4305      skill 125  4x Silk Cloth 4306
        3865  Bolt of Mageweave 4339       skill 175  5x Mageweave Cloth 4338
   Cloth comes off humanoid mobs, banded roughly by level: linen from the
   starting zones, wool from the teens, silk from the twenties and thirties,
   mageweave from the forties, runecloth from the fifties. All four are
   common auction house goods, which is the cheap way to level this
   profession; a twink who farms his own cloth is paying in time instead.
   Threads, dyes and bleach are tailoring supply vendor stock next to every
   trainer: Coarse Thread 2320, Fine Thread 2321, Silken Thread 4291,
   Heavy Silken Thread 8343 (item level 40, vendor trash thread), and the
   dyes. Gray Dye is 4340; the blue, red, orange and bleach item ids were
   not opened and are unverified.
   Two negatives worth writing down. Bolt-making is not a first aid step -
   bandages use raw cloth, so a tailor's bolts are useless to a medic.
   And tailoring has no permanent enhancement at this tier, so unlike
   leatherworking it cannot improve gear a 29 already owns.

7. ALT - WHAT A 29 CANNOT DO
   The artisan rank at skill 225 needs level 35, so 225 is the ceiling and
     nothing above it is reachable: Mooncloth, the Runecloth line, the
     14-slot Runecloth Bag 14046, Runecloth Gloves 13863, the Brightcloth
     patterns and everything the mageweave endgame builds on.
   Black Mageweave Headband 10024 at skill 230 is past the cap.
   Black Mageweave Gloves 10003 at skill 220 is craftable, but the wear
     level was not checked and is above 29 - treat the item as ALT.
   Outland and Northrend tailoring - spellthreads, the leg and shoulder
     enhancements, the flying-carpet mounts - needs higher skill and a
     level 60 or better character. ALT, and their pages were not opened
     here.
   The blue, red and orange dyes and the bleach are bought, not made; their
     item ids are unverified above.

8. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE IT
   1. Priest, mage, warlock. Only for Spidersilk Boots 4320 - +4 Stamina,
      +4 Intellect, +7 Spirit at level 20 - and only if the boots are not
      already on the auction house, which they usually are. After that the
      profession gives a caster nothing that enchanting, engineering or
      alchemy does not give better.
   2. The roster's bag maker and enchanter feeder. A 225 tailor producing
      12-slot Mageweave Bags for every alt, and green cloth items to
      disenchant, is the one place tailoring pays.
   3. Everyone else. No. Leatherworking kits work on cloth and are a better
      spend of the slot; engineering beats both.
   Last line: tailoring is a utility profession at 29, not a combat one.
   One pair of boots and a bag line, and no permanent upgrade anywhere in
   the tier.
