=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

The arena has four columns near the middle, so the whole opening
is a race for column control.

Melee DPS:
  Run straight at the nearest column and take the far side of it
  from the enemy team
  Using the column to break LoS on casters is the core of this
  map; open ground against casters loses the trade

Ranged DPS:
  Take a column early and hold it at max range from the melee
  Keep the column between yourself and the enemy melee
  Rotate around the column rather than standing still

Healers:
  Pick a column that stays within healing range of your partner
  Kite melee around the column; this is the standard Nagrand
  healer pattern and the wiki calls it out directly
  Do not take the centre of the map, it is open on all sides

General opener notes:
  Both teams spawn equidistant (65.2 units) from the centre and
  the columns, so there is no positional advantage
  Crowd control chains around a column are the usual opener
  A hunter can pre-place a trap at a column

  Y: 6627.999
  Z: 12.9556
  Orientation: 0.578 rad
  About 54.5 units from the arena centre

The two orbs are 108.9 units apart, on opposite sides of the
centre, so each team is roughly equidistant from one of them.

Spawn: 60 s after the gates open (core spawn call)
Respawn after pickup: 120 s (object spawn parameter)
Spell: 34709 Shadow Sight - 15 s, stealth and invisibility
  detection, +5% damage taken while active
Warcraft Wiki states the general arena rule as orbs appearing
after five minutes; the core uses 60 s and the exact retail
8.3.7 delay is unverified

No health, mana, haste or berserking pickups are spawned here.
The generic battleground pickup objects 179871 (speed),
179904 (regeneration) and 179905 (berserking) are not used on
arena maps.

=== 7. MATCH FLOW NOTES ===

Gates close before the start and open together, so both teams
leave at the same moment.
Dampening ("The Crowd Chose You") begins as on every arena.
Arena Preparation is applied on zone-in (health and mana refill).
The 2.1.0 cyclone hazard that used to spin into players was
removed, so there is no random map mechanic in 8.3.7.
The arena is also reachable in the open world as the Ring of
Trials in Nagrand; that world area has spectators and arena
vendors and is a separate map from 1505.
