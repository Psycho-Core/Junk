READ THIS FIRST - this arena works, and it has two floors.
Unlike Hook Point, this core implements it: BattlegroundMB exists
and CreateNewBattleground has a case for BATTLEGROUND_MUGAMBALA.
So the two gates and the two Shadow Sight orbs below are spawned
by the server at the coordinates given, not guessed from a wiki.
Everything else - the pillar, the upper floor, the stairs - is
terrain, so it has no object entry and no coordinates here.

The two doors are 14.66 apart, both at Y 1355, and their Z

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Read with caution: these positions are reasoned from the four
published descriptions, not from coordinates. The starts face
each other 43 deg off the straight line, so neither team opens
on a clean axis.

Melee DPS:
  Take the central pillar first; it is the only free-standing
  cover on the lower floor
  Do not go up unless the healer is coming with you: the two
  stairs are the only way up and the flanks are closed

Ranged DPS:
  The lower floor is open on both sides of the pillar, so max
  range works across the middle
  From the upper floor you can cast down on the whole lower
  floor; the counter is that the stairs are a choke and the
  flanks of that floor cannot be entered

Healers:
  Play the pillar, and keep one of the two stairs between you
  and the enemy melee
  Do not hold the upper floor alone: with the flanks closed the
  stairs are the only exit

General opener notes:
  Both teams are 68.01 from the midpoint, so first contact is
  close to simultaneous
  Game On sits 4.34 from the midpoint, so the countdown point is
  effectively the centre of the start line
  The two starts differ by 0.27 in Z, so neither team opens
  above the other

=== 6. BUFF PICKUPS ===

Two Shadow Sight orb objects, both spawned by the zone file, and
both at the same point:

Buff objects 184663 and 184664 (BG_MB_OBJECT_BUFF_1, _2):
  X: -1962.612305
  Y: 1245.579956
  Z: 34.431129
  Orientation: 1.652806 rad = 94.70 deg

That is not a misprint: both AddObject calls pass the same X, Y
and Z. The first passes 0, 0, 0 for the first three rotations
and the second passes 0, 0.70068, -0.0, so they differ in one
component only. On this server both orbs therefore spawn on top
of each other, 30.13 from the Team 1 start and 126.95 from the
Team 2 start. Where retail puts them is unverified.

Respawn: 60 s, set by StartingEventOpenDoors with
SpawnBGObject(i, 60) when the gates open. The 120 in both
AddObject calls is not a respawn time: AddObject takes four
rotations before the respawn time, so that 120 lands in the
rotation3 slot and the objects are created with respawn 0. The
60 s call is the one that takes effect.

Spell for reference: 34709 Shadow Sight - 30 yd, instant, 15 s,
stealth and invisibility detection, +5% damage taken while
active. That the spell exists is verified; that an orb on this
map carries it is the core's own object data, not readable here.

=== 7. MATCH FLOW NOTES ===

This arena is fully wired in this core:
  battleground_template row 903 with Weight 1 selects it
  CreateNewBattleground has a case for BATTLEGROUND_MUGAMBALA
  that constructs BattlegroundMB
  StartingEventCloseDoors spawns both doors; StartingEvent-
  OpenDoors opens them and spawns both orbs with a 60 s respawn
So, unlike Hook Point, a queue pop here produces a playable
instance.

Area triggers 4536 and 4537 are listed in HandleAreaTrigger and
deliberately do nothing (the switch breaks out). Every other
trigger falls through to the generic Battleground handler.
FillInitialWorldStates sets worldstate 0xE1A to 1, then defers
to Arena.

Dampening, Arena Preparation and the countdown come from Arena,
not from this file, and nothing here overrides them.
