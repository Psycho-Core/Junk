1. THE TWO CORRECTIONS
   1  CORRECTED  The Destruction note called 55929 Skull-Scorched Cloak "the
      crit cloak if the Warsong cape is not yet bought". The cloak does
      carry 5 critical strike, and it is also +6 Strength +4 Stamina (V) -
      a melee cloak. Telling a warlock to wear it for the crit was wrong
      and the note now says so: the Warsong cape stays, and 55929 is not a
      warlock item.
   2  CORRECTED  The trinket note said 4381 Minor Recombobulator "clears a
      polymorph" without qualification. The tooltip (V) is more specific and
      more useful: it restores 150 to 250 health and mana to a friendly
      target and attempts to dispel polymorph, at reduced effectiveness
      against casters of level 31 and higher, on a 5 minute cooldown, 10
      charges, and it needs Engineering 140 to use at all. In the 29
      bracket the level-31 clause never applies, which is worth knowing
      rather than trimming.
