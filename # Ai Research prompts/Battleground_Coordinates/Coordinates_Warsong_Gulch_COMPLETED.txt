6. GRAVEYARDS - WHICH ONE BELONGS TO WHICH POINT
   Four graveyard rows exist (section 2 ids 769-772), and the script's
   graveyard enum is literally those ids: WS_GRAVEYARD_FLAGROOM_
   ALLIANCE = 769, WS_GRAVEYARD_FLAGROOM_HORDE = 770,
   WS_GRAVEYARD_MAIN_ALLIANCE = 771, WS_GRAVEYARD_MAIN_HORDE = 772.
   Rule (GetClosestGraveYard, script):
  - Before the match starts (status not IN_PROGRESS): you respawn at
    your team's FLAGROOM row = the Enter Loc behind your base doors
    (Alliance 769, Horde 770).
  - During the match: you respawn at your team's MAIN row = the Rez
    Loc next to your own base flag room (Alliance 771, Horde 772).
  - No neutral/midfield graveyard exists (only these four ids in the
    enum; the map has no center GY row).
   Which graveyard belongs to which point: points are flags only, and
   dying anywhere in the match sends you to your own base's Rez row
   (771 Alliance, 772 Horde); only pre-match deaths use 769/770.
   The Anti-Cheat/Barrens rows (3698/3699/3784/3785) are teleport
   targets, not respawn points - their exact trigger rule (which fall
   or position teleports you there) is unverified: the LegionCore
   script does not reference them (checked).
   Map-2106 graveyard rows 7050-7053 (section 2): whether a 2106
   instance spawns spirits at them instead of 769-772 is unverified
   (script references only 769-772).
   Spirit guides: SetupBattleground spawns exactly two, at the MAIN
   rows' locations: AddSpiritGuide(WS_SPIRIT_MAIN_ALLIANCE, 771's loc)
   and AddSpiritGuide(WS_SPIRIT_MAIN_HORDE, 772's loc). The script
   passes no creature entry - the base Battleground::AddSpiritGuide
   default applies (same base-class convention as the Eye of the Storm
   source, which shows 13116 (Alliance) / 13117 (Horde) from
   Battleground.h). No spirit guide is spawned at the FLAGROOM rows.
