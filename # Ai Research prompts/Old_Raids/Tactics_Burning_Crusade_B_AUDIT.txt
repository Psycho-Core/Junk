CORRECTED (applied with this audit):
 1. Karathress kill order was INVERTED: the file put the
    healing priest first. Page order is Tidalvess
    (shaman, most raid damage), Sharkkis (hunter), then
    Caribdis (priest) LAST - controlled by a small
    interrupt group the whole time. The inheritance is
    now specific: shaman -> Spitfire Totems, hunter ->
    The Beast Within, priest -> Tidal Surge freeze.
 2. Felmyst air phase was garbled: it is not a generic
    "breath strafe". Demonic Vapor (x2) chases a marked
    player leaving a skeleton-summoning trail - stepping
    in it spawns MORE skeletons; then Fog of Corruption
    x3 mind-controls anyone caught. Also filed now: she
    is taunt-immune, and Encapsulate handling differs
    between era guides (level-80 note: heal through).
 3. M'uru wave details were vague ("Sentinels + casters
    from doors"): actually 1 Fury Mage + 2 Berserkers
    per side every 60 sec, Void Sentinels summoned
    separately, Darkness every 45 sec (~3000/sec,
    unhealable) spawning 8 Dark Fiends, Negative Energy
    beams on 4-5 random players throughout.
