================================================================================
FREEHOLD - POSITIONS AND NUMBERS, BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 BUILD 35662
Map id 1754. Four encounters: Skycap'n Kragg, Council o' Captains, Ring of
Booty (Ludwig Von Tortollan then Trothak), Harlan Sweete.
Companion file to the tactics document. Every number here is tagged with the
source it came from. Anything I could not read is written "unverified".

0. HOW TO READ THIS FILE

  Tags, used on every claim:
    [DB]    a row in the AshamaneCore world SQL for this instance:
            creature, gameobject, areatrigger, game_tele, instance_template.
    [SRV]   a line in the script: freehold.h, freehold.cpp,
            instance_freehold.cpp, or one of the four boss scripts.
    [MDT]   Method Dungeon Tools, BattleForAzeroth/Freehold.lua: the name,
            the per-kill force value, the health pool.
    [DBM]   DBM-Party-BfA/Freehold/*.lua at tag 8.3.34: a stopwatch value.
    [LW]    LittleWigs BfA/Freehold/*.lua: a second stopwatch value.
    [WOW]   a Wowhead NPC or spell page read directly.
    [CALC]  arithmetic I did on the numbers above. The inputs are always
            given so you can redo it.
    unverified   no readable source carries the value. It is never a guess.

  Coordinates are (x, y, z) in world yards on map 1754. Orientation is
  radians, 0 to 6.2832, and is only quoted where a row or a script
  constant carries one.

  Three things this file does that a normal guide does not:
  1. It gives every trash position from the database, not from a video.
     A position you can read out of a table cannot drift.
  2. It separates what the SERVER does from what RETAIL did. The script
     columns are what this core runs. The DBM and LittleWigs columns are
     retail stopwatches and they disagree with the script and with each
     other. Both are shown; neither is averaged.
  3. Where the fight has no safe spot in any source, it says so, and gives
     you the radius the script itself uses instead of a made-up coordinate.

1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS

1.1 instance_freehold.cpp registers map 1754 (RegisterInstanceScript at the
    end of the file) and owns the boss states, the captain-alliance counter
    and one NPC teleporter. It does not open or close a door: there is no
    GO state change anywhere in it, and the only game object the instance
    scripts touch is 288636 Council Tribute.
1.2 The four boss scripts are full implementations: phases, event timers,
    summon positions and arena constants. Kragg 519 lines, Council 874,
    Ring of Booty 1054, Harlan 429. freehold.cpp is the trash spell list and
    registers nothing (AddSC_freehold is empty).
1.3 The DB file is an AshamaneCore world update, 973 lines, dated
    2018-12-09. It carries 425 creature rows over 58 entries, 23 game
    object rows, 2 areatrigger rows, 1 game_tele row and the map row.
1.4 MDT supplies names, per-kill force values and health for 36 of the 58
    entries, and two addon files supply retail timers. All 36 MDT entries
    are present in the DB rows; 22 DB entries are not in MDT at all.
1.5 Entry accounting, the short version: 58 distinct creature entries.
    36 in MDT, 22 not. Of the 22, 9 are NPCs or props with no combat role
    (2.4.2), 6 are arena or ship dressing, and 7 are real but unlisted
    (129448 and 129359 the two sharks, 130850 the three cannons, 129732 a
    duplicate Kragg, 129743 Sharkbait, 130157 Shell Target, 136539 Booty
    Fanatic). Section 2.4.2 lists every one with its row count.
1.6 No client table was read. Spell radii, durations and cooldowns below
    are unverified unless the script, DBM or LittleWigs states them.

2.5.1 LINE OF SIGHT
  No source states a line-of-sight blocker anywhere in this instance. The
  DB has no door, no wall and no gate object; the script opens nothing.
  What the tables do give you is the geometry of each band change:
  - the ship to the quay is a 30.3 yd drop between P12 (z 86.95) and

  f) THROW CHUM 272534 / BLOODY MESS 272554 - HEROIC, EVERYBODY. Trigger:

10. DISCREPANCIES AND TRAPS

10.1 THE COUNCIL ENCOUNTER CANNOT END, AND THE CHEST IS SUMMONED BY A
     CREATURE THAT DOES NOT EXIST.
     Three things have to line up for the Council to finish, and one is
     missing:
     - boss_council_o_captains.cpp JustDied does not call _JustDied() and
       does not call SetBossState(DONE). It only counts a death on the
       controller.
     - the controller is npc_captains_controller, entry 160000 [SRV].
       160000 has NO row in the SQL. It is never spawned.
     - the controller is found with FindNearestCreature(160000, 500.0f),
       so with no row it returns null and the count never increments.
     Consequence: the boss state for the Council stays IN_PROGRESS and
     the chest the script wants to summon is never summoned. The DB
     places its own 288636 at the same coordinate (10.10), so a chest is
     there anyway, and a companion that reads the boss state will wait
     forever.
     Fix, if you want one: spawn 160000 at the captains' position in your
     world DB. Its AI is complete; it is only the spawn that is missing.

10.2 THE INSTANCE SCRIPT NAME DOES NOT MATCH THE DB.
     instance_template row for 1754 says script = 'instance_freehold'
     [DB]. The C++ struct is 'instance_free_hold' and the file registers
     RegisterInstanceScript(instance_free_hold, 1754) [SRV].
     'instance_freehold' and 'instance_free_hold' are different strings.
     Whether that leaves the instance script unloaded depends on how your
     core's RegisterInstanceScript builds its lookup name, so the
     consequence is unverified - but the two strings are not the same and
     one of them has to change.

10.3 VILE BOMBARDMENT HAS TWO IDS AND THREE TIMERS.
     Script: VileBombadment = 256005, cast by the mount, Repeat(5000).
     Wowhead's encounter journal: Vile Bombardment is spell 256035.
     DBM times 256005 at 5.9 s. LittleWigs times 256005 at 10.9 s in
     normal and 17.0 s in heroic, with the comment that normal is shorter
     because Dive Bomb is heroic-only.
     So the same mechanic is 5.0 s (script), 5.9 s (DBM) or 10.9/17.0 s
     (LW), and there are two spell ids in circulation. Use 256005 on this
     server; 256035 is what a Wowhead-based companion would look for.

10.4 KRAAG'S BREW IS THE BIGGEST SCRIPT / ADDON GAP IN THE FIGHT.
     Script: Revitalizing Brew 256060, first at 15.0 s after the dismount,
     then every 15.0 s, unconditional on health.
     DBM: 20.6 s cooldown. LittleWigs: 20.7 s, and its source comment says
     "this ability is only cast if the boss is < 50% health".
     Neither the 50% gate nor the 20.6 s cooldown exists in the script.
     A companion that only interrupts below 50% will never interrupt on
     this server.

10.5 THE HEROIC COUNCIL BREW MECHANIC IS UNREACHABLE.
     The three brew spells are 265086, 264715 and 265171 [SRV]. They are
     cast by npc_rummy_mancomb, entry 133219, and only when something
     sends ActionStartLaunchBrew. Nothing in the five Freehold script
     files sends it: the case exists in DoAction, and there is no caller.
     133219 also has no DB row, so the NPC is not even present.
     On this server the heroic brew mechanic does not happen.

10.6 TWO DBM TIMERS REFER TO SPELLS THIS CORE DOES NOT HAVE.
     257117 Lucky Sevens (29.1 s) and 264605 Tending Bar (8 s) appear in
     DBM-Party-BfA/Freehold/CounciloCaptains.lua and in no Freehold script.
     If your companion subscribes to those ids it will never fire.

10.7 BRUTAL BACKHAND HAS TWO IDS, ONE IN EACH SOURCE.
     freehold.cpp: 257246. LittleWigs Trash: 257426. Neither source has
     both. Unverified which one the core's creature_template carries.

10.8 THREE DB ROWS ARE STALE RELATIVES OF A SCRIPTED MOB.
     129732 Kragg is 108.18 yd from the scripted 126832 and 6.32 yd from
     129743 Sharkbait; the scripted mount is summoned on Kragg's own
     position and is 108.49 yd from that 129743 row.
     126969 Trothak's row is 57.3 yd from TrothakMovePos[2], where the

     In all three cases the script's position wins and the row is
