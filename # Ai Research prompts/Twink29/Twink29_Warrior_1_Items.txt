0. NO PLATE, AGAIN - AND THE WARRIOR'S OWN BATTLEGROUND SET
   The paladin file established the bracket's strangest fact and it
   applies unchanged: there is no rare plate at required 26-29 at all,
   because Classic plate starts at level 40. The only plate a 29 wears
   is the scaling bind-on-account pieces. A warrior wears mail below
   that, leather below that, exactly as a paladin does.
   What differs is the battleground set. The Lamellar pair (20108/20111
   Alliance, 20178/20182 Horde) is labelled Paladin only, and a warrior
   cannot buy it. The Mail pair is labelled Warrior, Paladin (V):
   20126 Highlander's Mail Girdle is +12 Strength +5 Stamina and 20129
   Highlander's Mail Greaves are +6 Strength +6 Agility +6 Stamina with
   run speed increased slightly, both 33/28 (V). The Horde copies are
   20207 and 20210. So the warrior's battleground armour is two slots,
   both Strength, both bought - the best waist and feet in this file
   cost honour, not luck.

3. ARMS
   Armour: section 1.
   Weapon: 7730 Cobalt Crusher, or 6687 Corpsemaker if its required
   level holds on the server, with 9449 as the swap-in for the on-use.
   Trinkets: 19024 and the Insignia. Arms is the simplest bot in the
   class: one weapon, no off-hand decision, no shield decision.

4. FURY
   Armour: section 1.
   Weapons: two one-handers - 7752 Dreamslayer and 19557 [19553], or
   7752 twice if the world drop is kind. Whether a 29 Fury warrior can
   dual wield two-hand weapons in 8.3.7 was not read for this file, so
   this loadout is built on one-handers and the two-hand option is
   stated as unverified rather than assumed either way.
   Trinkets: the same three. 4381 matters to Fury: it is the spec most
   likely to be standing in melee when a polymorph lands on the healer.

5. PROTECTION
   Armour: section 1, with 44099 in the shoulder slot.
   Weapon and shield: 7752 Dreamslayer + 65942 [66024] Shield of the
   Righteous Cause, or 7787 Resplendent Guardian where the quest shield
   is not yet done. A Protection warrior is the only spec in this set
   that prefers the Strength quest shield to the dropped one.
   Trinkets: 19024 and the Insignia; a tank without a crowd-control
   break is a tank that watches its healer die, and the Insignia is the
   only break here.

7. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   A 29 warrior wears plate in two slots, mail in four, leather in two
   and greens in three, and its two best armour pieces are bought from a
   battleground vendor rather than dropped. That is the paladin's
   situation with a better battleground set and a worse best weapon.
   This file also has the set's heaviest flag load: 6686, 1488, 9445,
   9455, 6690, 9446, 2816 and 6687 all read differently on the 8.3.7
   tooltip than in the search pool, and they are concentrated in exactly
   the slots a warrior cares about - head, chest, hands, legs and the
   main weapon. Eight disagreements is too many to settle on paper. One
   check on the server, against whichever of the two readings the core
   uses, would close all eight at once and would retroactively settle
   the same flags in the rogue, shaman, hunter and paladin files.
