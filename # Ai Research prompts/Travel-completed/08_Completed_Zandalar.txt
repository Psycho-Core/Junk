WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 1642 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: 37 of the 44 nodes print one and 7 print "unverified". 32 entries come from section 3.1 of the research
  document unchanged, and 5 were read or corrected this run - Zabar 121828, Du'ba 122191, Ripa the Wind Ripper 121252, Scroll of
  Flight 135387 and Paku'ai Chal 148157. The
  four Scroll of Flight nodes of Zuldazar are the ones section 3.1 gets wrong: it prints 137318 for all four, but 137318 is a
  Scroll of Flight standing in Stormsong Valley on Kul Tiras, and Wowhead lists three separate titled Scroll of Flight ids in
  Zuldazar (134008, 134011, 136331) with no page tying any of them to a single node, so 2066 Atal'Gral, 2075 Seeker's Outpost,
  2076 Atal'Gral and 2126 Scaletrader Post print "unverified" instead of a near match. Section 3.1 also prints 146507 for 1957
  Port of Zandalar, but that id has no location on Wowhead and was added in 8.1.0, while 121252 is the Ripa the Wind Ripper
  standing in Dazar'alor, so the block prints 121252 and says why. The Vol'dun scroll at 2120 Tortaka Refuge
  does print an entry, because 135387 is the only Scroll of Flight flight master Wowhead places in Vol'dun.
  Three nodes print no master at all: 2071 Dreadpearl, 2073 Throne Room and 2162 Devoted Sanctuary. All three start no TaxiPath
  row. 2071 sits on exactly the same coordinates as 2066 Atal'Gral and 2162 on exactly the same coordinates as 2119 Sanctuary of
  the Devoted, and 2073 is the one node in this map with neither faction bit set; none of the three has a master named in any
  source read here, so none was copied over from the twin roost.
  Gate evidence: one node of the 44 is gated and 43 are not. 1956 Forlorn Ruins carries ConditionID 64582, and the PlayerCondition
  row behind it sets no level, class, race or quest field, so there is no character-facing requirement to print beside it. No
  Zandalar taxi node is gated on level, class, race, reputation or an achievement.
  Faction: TaxiNodes.Flags gives 12 Alliance-only roosts, 22 Horde-only roosts, 9 with both bits set and 1 with neither, which
  sums to 44; each block prints the bits it found rather than inferring a side from the name. The single node with neither bit is
  the 2073 Throne Room.
  Four nodes hold a taxi bit but appear in no TaxiPath row at all, in either direction: 2066 Atal'Gral, 2071 Dreadpearl, 2073
  Throne Room and 2162 Devoted Sanctuary. Their blocks print "none" for links, and they are the only nodes in this map that can
  be neither flown to nor flown from.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Distances are per direction: 1953 -> 1954 reads 1345 yd while
  1954 -> 1953 reads 1350 yd, and the same holds for the other pairs. Section 1642 holds 190 directed rows over 40 nodes.
  Unlock: a node's bit is set by visiting its flight master, so the practical requirement is reaching the zone. Flying over
  Zandalar additionally needs Battle for Azeroth Pathfinder, Part One (achievement 12989) and Part Two (13250); a taxi flight
  needs neither, only the node's bit.
  Sources: TaxiNodes and TaxiPath exports for build 8.3.7.35662, the PlayerCondition decode of ConditionID 64582, the Warcraft
  Wiki pages for Zul'jan Ruins, Tortaka Refuge, the Mugambala and Scroll of Flight, and Wowhead pages for Du'ba, Zabar, Paula
  Waverunner, Paku'ai Chal and the Scroll of Flight ids of Zuldazar. Everything read from a source is printed; nothing was
  estimated.
