  Those first seven are not ordinary roosts. With 1847, 1848, 1849 and 1850 they are the ten nodes gated on the Hunter class
  (ConditionID 43356), which is the Trueshot Lodge order-hall network: their names are destinations - Dalaran, The Dreamgrove,
  Trueshot Lodge, Eastern Highmountain, and points across Stormheim, Suramar and Azsuna - rather than towns with a resident
  master.
  1855 Shield's Rest and 1863 Hafr Fjall are the opposite case: the wiki records a flightpath at each and no NPC that keeps it.
  Gate evidence: 13 of the 55 nodes are gated and 42 are not. Ten are the Hunter mask above. 1815 The Dreamgrove needs the Druid
  class and quest 40645, 1862 Acherus: The Ebon Hold needs the Death Knight class and quest 39017, and 1760 Trueshot Lodge needs
  the Hunter class alone (ConditionID 38053). No Broken Isles taxi node is gated on level, race, reputation or an achievement.
  Faction: TaxiNodes.Flags gives 3 Alliance-only roosts (1744, 1745, 1747 - all in Stormheim), 3 Horde-only roosts (1738, 1739,
  1741, also all Stormheim) and 49 with both bits set; each block prints the bits it found rather than inferring a side from the
  name.
  Six masters hold two nodes each, and all six are real rather than a copy error: Donovan at 1745 Lorna's Watch and 1747 Skyfire
  Triage Camp, Danise Stargazer at 1815 The Dreamgrove and 1847 its Hunter-network copy, Odan Battlebow at 1760 Trueshot Lodge and
  1848 its copy, Aludane Whitecloud at 1774 Dalaran and 1849 its copy, Izal Whitemoon at 1622 Illidari Stand and 1856 Vengeance
  Point, and Ancient Cloudwing at 1858 Meredil and 1879 Crimson Thicket.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Distances are per direction: 1613 -> 1615 reads 1487 yd while
  1615 -> 1613 reads 1464 yd, and the same holds for every other pair. Of the 293 directed rows in section 1220, 146 pairs carry a
  row each way and one carries a row in a single direction - 1848 Trueshot Lodge -> 1849 Dalaran for 6139 yd, with no row
  back - so the 1849 block prints that leg as a reverse row only. 1849 starts no TaxiPath row of its own, so it is an
  arrival-only node and nothing can be flown out of it. Every other node has at least one outbound row.
  Unlock: a node's bit is set by visiting its flight master, so the practical requirement is reaching the zone. Flying over the
  Broken Isles additionally needs Broken Isles Pathfinder, Part One (achievement 11190) and Part Two (11446); a taxi flight needs
  neither, only the node's bit.
