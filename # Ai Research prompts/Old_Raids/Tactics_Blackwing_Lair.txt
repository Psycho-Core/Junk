TACTICS - BLACKWING LAIR (OLD RAIDS STEP 2 OF 16)
Server: private BfA 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Context: level 60 raid entered by a level 120 character with
computer-controlled companions. Values read on warcraft.wiki.gg.
Zone: Blackwing Lair (Wowhead zone 2677). Instance map id and
all coordinates (map, x, y, z): unverified - wiki gives only
in-zone map percentages, which are not coordinates.
Boss entries verified on the Wowhead Blackwing Lair NPC list:
Razorgore 12435, Vaelastrasz 13020, Broodlord Lashlayer 12017,
Firemaw 11983, Ebonroc 14601, Flamegor 11981, Chromaggus 14020,
Nefarian 11583. Add entries unverified unless stated.
D = dispel type. I = interruptible (only where read).

1. RAZORGORE THE UNTAMED (12435)
PHASES: P1 kill Grethok the Controller (add, entry unverified)
  and his 2 Blackwing Guardsmen. P2 a companion uses the Orb
  of Domination to control Razorgore and destroy every egg
  while adds spawn in the corners (caps read: 40 orcs -
  Legionnaires and Mages - and 12 Death Talon Dragonspawn).
  P3 eggs gone: adds flee, Razorgore heals to full (23040),
  kill him. NEVER let Razorgore die before the eggs are gone:
  he instantly kills the whole room and resets (Wowhead).
TANK: P1 tank the Guardsmen; P3 tank Razorgore in the open,
  facing away from the group (Cleave 19632, War Stomp 24375).
GROUP: corner groups guard the spawn corners; protect the
  orb controller (adds run at the controller first); Mages
  are the priority kills, Dragonspawn are kited/CC'd, not
  killed (page: hard to kill, high damage).
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER (P2): P1 Blackwing Mages, P2 Legionnaires (kite),
  Dragonspawn: CC/kite only. Eggs are the real priority -
  Destroy Egg 19873 under control, Calm Dragonkin 19872.
ABILITY LIST:
  19873 Destroy Egg (while controlled) - the objective.
  19872 Calm Dragonkin (controlled) - sleep 30 sec.
  22425 Fireball Volley (controlled) - 2 sec cast.
  23023 Conflagration - 3000 Fire/10 sec + panic, splashes
    nearby allies for 150. Spread from the burning target.
  24375 War Stomp - AoE +500, knockback, stun 5 sec, 15 yd.
  19632 Cleave - frontal, up to 10 targets.
  23040 Warming Flames - heals him to full, starts P3.
AI RULE: in P2 use the orb and destroy eggs; never damage
  Razorgore to death before the last egg is gone.

2. VAELASTRASZ THE CORRUPT (13020)
PHASES: single phase from 30% health (he starts corrupted and
  low; exact starting percent read as "starts the fight
  already dying" - starting % unverified). A 3-minute race:
  Burning Adrenaline kills its targets one by one.
TANK: hold him facing the wall/alcove, tail away from the
  group (Tail Sweep 15847 knocks back 150 yd behind him);
  tanks rotate: every third Burning Adrenaline lands on the
  current tank, and that tank dies when it expires.
GROUP: ranged spread on his flanks, never front (Flame Breath
  23461 cone + Cleave 19983 chains 10 yd), never behind (tail).
  The Burning Adrenaline target keeps DPSing and dies clear
  of others (explodes 4376-5624 to surrounding allies).
GROUND: none placed; the death explosion point is the dying
  Burning Adrenaline target.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  23513 Essence of the Red - raid buff at pull: huge resource
    regen, 3 min. This is the enrage timer in effect.
  18173 Burning Adrenaline - +100% damage, instant casts,
    max health -5%/sec, explodes on death. On a mana user
    every 15 sec; every third cast on the tank instead.
  23461 Flame Breath - frontal cone 3500-4500 + DoT.
  23642 Fire Nova - 555-645 Fire, 100 yd radius, instant.
  19983 Cleave - chains to targets within 10 yd.
  15847 Tail Sweep - behind cone + huge knockback.
AI RULE: kill him before Essence of the Red ends; a companion
  with Burning Adrenaline moves away from the group before
  it kills them.

3. BROODLORD LASHLAYER (12017)
PHASES: single phase (the two Suppression Rooms before him
  are the real first phase - see TRASH).
TANK: tank him in his doorway/room with tanks rotating -
  Knock Away drops the target's threat by 50% each time, so
  the next tank takes over; never let him reach cloth (Mortal
  Strike crits 15000+ on cloth, read).
GROUP: ranged at max range; nobody but tanks in front of him
  (Cleave, Blast Wave knockback).
GROUND: none read. Suppression devices (before the boss)
  radiate an aura reducing move/attack/cast speed to 20%.
ADD ORDER: none in the boss fight.
ABILITY LIST (spell ids unverified - page lists no ids):
  Blast Wave - AoE fire knockback. Id unverified.
  Cleave - frontal high damage. Id unverified.
  Mortal Strike - tank hit + healing-reduction debuff.
    Id unverified.
  Knock Away - single target knockback, -50% threat.
    Id unverified.
AI RULE: when your tank is knocked away, the next tank taunts
  immediately; non-tanks never stand in front of him.

4. FIREMAW (11983)
PHASES: single phase.
TANK: tanked in the doorway of the room he guards, tank with
  his back to a wall; two tanks alternate - Wing Buffet
  23339 knocks back and cuts threat, and the Flame Buffet
  stack forces tank swaps so stacks can fall off (20 sec).
GROUP: DPS/healers hug the walls and break line of sight to
  drop Flame Buffet stacks; only expose to heal/DPS, then
  hide again. He is patrolling: clear the first trash packs
  of the Crimson Laboratories before pulling (page: ~3).
GROUND: none placed. Flame Buffet 23341 stacks on anyone in
  his line of sight - the "ground" here is his sightline.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  23341 Flame Buffet - 139-161 Fire +150 Fire damage taken
    per stack, 20 sec, NOT dispellable (page, explicit).
  22539 Shadow Flame - frontal cone 3938-5062 Shadow (the
    Onyxia Scale Cloak DoT protection note is Classic-era;
    cloak requirement in 8.3.7 unverified).
  23339 Wing Buffet - frontal knockback + threat reduction.
  3391 Thrash - 2 extra attacks on the tank.
  20279 Summon Player - summons a player to his front.
AI RULE: if Flame Buffet stacks climb, break line of sight
  behind the doorway wall until they drop; never face him
  toward the group.

5. EBONROC (14601)
PHASES: single phase.
TANK: tanks in a corner (knockback control), three-tank
  rotation read on the page: main tank, Wing Buffet eater,
  and Shadow cover. The instant a tank gets Shadow of
  Ebonroc 23340, another tank taunts - the debuffed tank's
  melee hits HEAL Ebonroc. Keep a Mortal Strike effect on
  him (halves that healing; from the page).
GROUP: DPS at his rear (6 o'clock), tanks at 3/9/12.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  23340 Shadow of Ebonroc - debuff: target's melee damage
    heals Ebonroc. D type unverified in source.
  22539 Shadow Flame - frontal cone (see Firemaw).
  23339 Wing Buffet - frontal knockback/threat cut.
  3391 Thrash. 20279 Summon Player.
AI RULE: if you have Shadow of Ebonroc, stop attacking and
  let another tank take him - your hits heal the boss.

6. FLAMEGOR (11981)
PHASES: single phase; Enrage 23342 every 10-15 sec is the
  fight (Fire Nova only while enraged, per page).
TANK: like Firemaw (doorway/wall), facing away from group.
GROUP: hunters run a Tranquilizing Shot rotation (book from
  Lucifron) - Enrage must be removed within seconds; a
  dedicated healer for offtanks/hunters (page).
GROUND: none. Fire Nova while enraged hits the whole raid
  and since patch 1.11 cannot be line-of-sighted (page) -
  damage value unverified in source.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  23342 Enrage - +150% attack speed 10 sec; dispel by
    Tranquilizing Shot (offensive, hunter only).
  22539 Shadow Flame, 23339 Wing Buffet, 3391 Thrash,
  20279 Summon Player - see Firemaw.
AI RULE: Tranquilizing Shot on every Enrage; if none is
  available, all companions stop and heal through 10 sec -
  never let a second Enrage stack onto an un-dispelled one.

7. CHROMAGGUS (14020)
PHASES: single phase; at 20% a hard Enrage joins the ongoing
  Frenzy cycle (frenzy 10-15 sec, Tranquilizing Shot). His
  two breaths are chosen per instance lockout from five.
TANK: tanked where the raid can break line of sight (door of
  his room / ramp cubbies, page list); everyone except the
  tank hides on each breath warning (breaths hit everything
  in line of sight, NOT just frontal). Time Lapse exception:
  an offtank stays out to take him while the MT is stunned.
GROUP: dispel teams by type (below); keep every companion at
  3 or fewer Brood Afflictions - at 5, the player mutates
  into an elite drakonid (undispellable; page: polymorph or
  kill the mutant, and its death heals Chromaggus).
GROUND: none placed (base version); breaths are LoS cones.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST (breath/affliction spell ids unverified - the
  page names them without ids):
  Breaths (2 of 5 per lockout, alternate every 30 sec):
   Incinerate - 3675-4275 Fire.
   Corrosive Acid - 875-1125 Nature/3 sec 15 sec, armour cut.
   Frost Burn - ~1400 Frost, attack speed -80%.
   Ignite Flesh - 657-843 Fire/3 sec 60 sec, stacks.
   Time Lapse - stun 6 sec, max health halved while stunned.
  Brood Afflictions (zone-wide auras, all five cycle):
   Red D-Disease: 50 Fire/3 sec; death heals Chromaggus.
   Green D-Poison: healing -50%, 250 Nature/5 sec.
   Blue D-Magic: move -70%, cast -50%, mana/health drain.
   Black D-Curse: +100% Fire damage taken.
   Bronze: random 4-sec stuns, 10 min; removed ONLY by
    Hourglass Sand (drops from the trash before him) or
    Ice Block. Dispel types read on the page.
  Frenzy - Tranquilizing Shot (id unverified here).
  Enrage (20%) - id unverified (page cites 28747 via an
    old external link; treat id as unverified).
AI RULE: dispel every Brood Affliction fast (disease, poison,
  magic, curse), and break line of sight on every breath
  unless you are the tank.

8. NEFARIAN (11583)
PHASES: P1 Lord Victor Nefarius (entry unverified) on his
  throne sends waves of drakonid adds from two doors (colours
  vary per lockout; counts unverified). At the phase's end he
  takes dragon form and lands: P2 class calls. P3 at 20% he
  resurrects every drakonid killed in P1 as Bone Constructs
  (add entries unverified).
TANK: P2 tank him near his throne facing away from the raid
  (Shadow Flame cone, Cleave 19983, Tail Lash 23364 behind);
  fear breaks ready (Bellowing Roar 22686, 35 yd).
GROUP: P1 split to both doors, AoE the waves; P2 spread on
  his flank; respond to class calls (below); P3 tanks pick
  up the Bone Constructs, raid stays on Nefarian.
GROUND: none placed read.
ADD ORDER: P1 drakonids as they spawn (both doors; the two
  colours' resists vary - unverified per lockout). P3 Bone
  Constructs P1 for tanks, but companions keep DPS on
  Nefarian unless constructs reach the healers.
ABILITY LIST:
  22686 Bellowing Roar - AoE fear 4 sec, 35 yd.
  19983 Cleave - frontal, up to 10 targets.
  22539 Shadow Flame - frontal cone.
  23364 Tail Lash - behind him: disarm + stun 2 sec.
  22687 Veil of Shadow - D-Curse type not printed (healing
    -75% 6 sec on target; decurse priority = tank. Type
    unverified in source, filed as curse-family by name).
  Class calls (spell ids unverified): force one class's
   signature harm - druids stuck in Cat Form; hunters'
   ranged weapons break (unequip before calls); mages cast
   Wild Polymorph on the raid (dispel it, D-Magic); paladins
   Blessing of Protection on Nefarian (stop DPS); priests'
   direct heals add a stacking DoT (priests stop direct
   heals); rogues teleported+rooted at his side (tank turns
   him); shaman spawn corrupted totems (kill the totems);
   warlocks summon 2 Infernals each (AoE them); warriors
   stuck in Berserker Stance +30% damage taken (heal tank).
AI RULE: obey your class call the second it is announced;
  otherwise never stand in front of or behind him.
