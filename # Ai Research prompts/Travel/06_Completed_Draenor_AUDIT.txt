AUDIT OF 06_Completed_Draenor.txt - ONE CLAIM PER LINE
==============================================================================

Run 2026-10-23. The file has 72 node blocks plus a closing evidence section.
Every block was re-derived from the source document with a second, independent
parser (not the one that wrote the file) and compared field by field. One entry
inherited from the source document turned out to be the wrong NPC and was
replaced; a second one could not be resolved at all and is now marked
unverified.

RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
29 claims: 25 PASS, 1 FIXED, 3 UNVERIFIED. One edit: the Breaker's Crown entry
changed from 81677 to 81674, and the Iron Docks block now prints its entry as
unverified instead of repeating 81677. No coordinate, link, travel time, faction
or gate value changed, and the block count is unchanged at 72.

What was re-read rather than trusted:
  - all 72 positions, bit numbers, link sets and travel times were re-parsed
    from the section-1116 text and compared with the file;
  - the two Nisha blocks, which is where the only edit came from: 81677 was
    opened on Wowhead and found to be her mount, not the flight master;
  - the five gated nodes and the quests behind the one quest gate, plus the two
    race-mask gates at the Iron Front pair;
  - the three faction-swap corrections inherited from step 05's method (1454,
    1512, 1644), each re-checked against its wiki NPC page.
Not re-read: the 8.3.7 TaxiNodes export itself (section 1116 of the research
document is the transcription used here, as in steps 01 to 05).
