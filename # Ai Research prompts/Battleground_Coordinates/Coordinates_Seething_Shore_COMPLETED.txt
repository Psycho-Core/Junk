=== 2. TEAM STARTS AND BASE POSITIONS ===
There are NO fixed start rows in the readable dump: teams start aboard
their faction gunship, and the script teleports players onto it:
  TeleportToStart (script): gunship->CalculatePassengerPosition(x, y, z),
  then TeleportTo(1803, x, y, z + 25.0 for Alliance / z + 40.0 for Horde,
  0) - i.e. starts are DYNAMIC transport positions, not world_safe rows;
  fixed start coordinates are unverified.
  Gunship transports (script SetupBattleground, CreateTransport):
    Alliance gunship gameobject 278407, Horde gunship gameobject 279254.
    Both spawn-position constants in the script are written identically:
    (1803, 1399.684, 2766.317, 3.485144) - reproduced as stored.
    Transport movement paths: unverified (no transport path rows for
    278407/279254 in the readable dump).
  Boarding/arrival: AddPlayer casts RocketParachute spell 262359 (script).
  Airship prep collision gameobjects 281224 (Alliance) / 281226 (Horde)
  are defined in the script enum but never referenced by it - spawn
  positions and use: unverified.
Supporting starts-adjacent dump rows (see section 5): the two "Graveyard
  Airship" rows 6337/6380 near the map origin.

=== 3. OBJECTIVES (AZERITE NODES; NO FLAGS/ORBS/CARTS) ===
No flags, no orbs and no mine cart depots exist in Seething Shore; the
  objectives are azerite nodes (warcraft.wiki: "Three nodes are active at
  any given time, and the first team to collect 1,500 Azerite claims
  victory").
Controller (script): creature 125269 at (1803, 1399.684, 2766.317,
  3.485144) - same coordinates as the transport constants.
Twelve azerite fissures (script Creatures::AzeriteFissurePositions,
  creature entry 125253, spawned by SetupBattleground at setup; node

  node 8  (1803, 1361.733, 2643.267, 4.608177)

  node 11 (1803, 1454.085, 2598.380, 15.35741)
  node 12 (1803, 1461.358, 2823.238, 31.80409)
Node activation (script): a 30-second timer activates a random non-active
  fissure, and activation stops at three concurrently active nodes
  (_activeAzerriteFissureCounter >= 3), matching the wiki's "three nodes
  active at any given time"; at match start three activations are queued
  500 ms apart.
Capture node objects (summoned at the fissure's position by the fissure
  creature casting the summon spell - two variants):
  gameobject 281307 via spell 262749, gameobject 272471 via spell 248674.
Capture interaction (script EventPlayerClickedOnFlag): click within 10 yd
  of the node with a fissure creature within 10 yd; each capture awards
  +100 azerite (teamScores += 100), 30 honor, anim kit 2560, cosmetic FX
  spells 262508 (Alliance) / 262512 (Horde), then the node object is
  deleted. Supporting spell ids: ActivateAzerite 248688, AzeriteGeyser
  248668, AzeriteKnockBack 262385, EarthquakeCameraShake 248719,
  VignetteDummy creature 129344.
Scoring (script + warcraft.wiki agree): first to 1500 azerite wins
  (Scores::MaxTeamScore = 1500); near-victory warning at more than 1200
  (Scores::NearVictory = 1200); world states 13844/13845 (team totals),
  13846 (max), 13855/13856 (earned display).

Which graveyard belongs to which point: Seething Shore has no point-owned
  graveyards. The script's GetClosestGraveYard returns a DYNAMIC safe
  location built every call from the team's own gunship passenger
  position plus the same +25 (Alliance) / +40 (Horde) z offset as
  TeleportToStart - every death respawns aboard the gunship, and
  RelocateDeadPlayers teleports ghosts there as well.
On resurrection the script casts UnchartedTerritory 262086,
  RocketParachute 262359 and RocketParachite2 250917 - players parachute
  back down to the island.
None of the six dump rows is referenced by the readable script: the
  helper GetTeamWorldSafeLocks (returning 6337/6380) is defined but never
  called, and rows 6167-6198 appear nowhere in the script - their retail
  use is unverified.

=== 6. BUFF / POWER-UP SPAWNS ===
BuffBox (script): creature entry 133532. One box per team, each on its
  own timer initialised to urand(180, 300) seconds (header default) and
  re-rolled to urand(180, 300) seconds after every spawn: the first boxes
  therefore fall somewhere between 3 and 5 minutes after the match
  starts. Spawn position (script): 40 yd below the team's own gunship,
  then displaced 65 yd toward the enemy gunship - i.e. the position moves
  with the transports; fixed coordinates are unverified.
  What buff the box grants: unverified (no buff spell for the box exists
  in the readable script). warcraft.wiki's official overview refers to
  "additional power-ups" watched for "in the sky" - parity with this
  creature: unverified.
No other buff or power-up spawns are defined in the readable script or
  described on the wiki page.
