Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
RULE FOR THIS FILE: cloth armour, rare (blue) or bind-on-account, item
level 35 or less, required level 26 to 29. Weapons, wands, jewellery and
trinkets follow the same quality and level window.

V = name, item level, required level and source read off a search or item
page. Rows without V carry an unverified number and say so.
Pool: an item search on cloth, rare quality, required level 26-29 and item
level 35 or less returns 15 rows; the same search on wands returns 1, on
staves 5 and on weapons 37. What follows is every row a mage can use.

0. THE THREE SLOTS THE POOL DOES NOT COVER
   LEGS   no rare cloth leg in the window, and the cloth heirloom list has
          only three items in it (two shoulders, one chest). A mage's legs
          are a green or nothing. That is the biggest hole in this file.
   OFF HAND  no off-hand frill and no held-in-off-hand item came back in
          the window. A one-hand mage has an empty off-hand unless a
          verified item is found elsewhere.
   BACK   no Back row appears in the rare cloth search at this tier, so
          there is no blue cloak in the window. Whether cloaks are filed
          under cloth in the database at all is unverified, so read this as
          "none found", not as "none exists". 10518 Parachute Cloak is item
          level 45: outside this file's item level rule and useless to a
          caster anyway (+8 Agility). Do not wear it for the stats; it is an
          engineering toy for a flag carrier, and the engineering file has
          it.
   Everything else has at least one blue item, and most slots have two or
   three. Cloth is by far the best-served armour type at 29.
   One exception to the item level rule is called out rather than listed:
   10518 Parachute Cloak sits at item level 45 and is not in the table.

3. ARCANE
   Gear: section 1 as written. Weapon: a staff, or a one-hand sword plus a
   wand if the off-hand question is ever answered. Wand: Starfaller 13063.
   Trinket: Arena Grand Master, Insignia, Minor Recombobulator.
   The Arcane stat line at 29 is Intellect, so the heirlooms matter more
   than the drops: 44107 or 42985 shoulders and 48691 chest carry the
   caster stat that the rare pool is short of.

4. FIRE
   Gear: section 1. Weapon: staff, same reasoning as Arcane. Wand:
   Starfaller 13063 - a mage's wand is a stat stick and a pull, not a
   damage source, so no spec-specific wand exists at this tier.
   Trinket: Arena Grand Master and the Insignia are the two that matter;
   Minor Recombobulator 4381 is the third because it clears a polymorph.
   The one Fire-specific item in the whole pool is a weapon oil, not gear:
   20744 Minor Wizard Oil, and it lives in step 3.

5. FROST
   Gear: section 1. Weapon: staff, or the one-hand and wand pair.
   Trinket: same three.
   Frost is the spec that gains least from the blue pool, because the
   useful Frost item at this tier is 20750 Wizard Oil (level 40, ALT) and
   a frost power ring or neck does not exist in the window.

7. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   Nine slots are properly served - head, shoulder, chest, wrist, hands,
   waist, feet, weapon and wand - and four are not: legs, back, off-hand
   and the rings. The heirlooms carry more weight for a mage than they do
   for the other classes, because the two best pieces (shoulders and
   chest) and the missing legs are all heirloom or nothing.
   Everything above is the WotLK-era item page. Where 8.3.7 re-itemised an
   item its item level can differ; no required level moved on any item
   checked.
