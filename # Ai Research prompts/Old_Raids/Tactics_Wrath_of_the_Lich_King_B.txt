TRIAL OF THE CRUSADER (map 649)
1. NORTHREND BEASTS (Gormok / Acidmaw+Dreadscale / Icehowl)
GORMOK: tank swap on Impale stacks; snobolds on backs
  (kill the snobold on a companion - it silences/pins);
  fire bombs = move. ACIDMAW+DREADSCALE: two worms, one
  rooted one mobile, swap: Burning Bile / Paralytic Toxin
  - a burned player frees a paralyzed one by TOUCHING
  them; jormungar tanks swap when one submerges.
  ICEHOWL: he fixates, charges a marked player (move at
  the last moment - he crashes and stuns himself), breath
  cone. AI RULE: bile cures toxin by touch; the charge is
  dodged, never soaked.
2. LORD JARAXXUS: Incinerate Flesh on a player = heal
  them TO FULL before it ends or they blow up the area
  (healing-absorb bomb); Mistress adds (kill/interrupt),
  Legion Flame on a player (run it out, leaves fire),
  portals/volcanoes spawn adds until destroyed.
  AI RULE: the burning companion is healed, not dispelled;
  flame carrier keeps moving.
3. FACTION CHAMPIONS: PvP fight, no threat tables: CC
  their healers, kill order healers > casters > melee,
  use interrupts/dispels like arena; defensive when
  focused. AI RULE: you are in an arena match - crowd
  control IS the tactic.
4. Val'kyr Twins (Fjola/Eydis): touch orbs to take the
  opposite colour essence; DPS only the twin OPPOSITE
  your colour; when one casts her Vortex/Twin's Pact
  (shield+heal), everyone swaps to the casting twin's
  colour-orbs and breaks the shield, interrupt the heal.
  AI RULE: your colour decides your target - wrong
  colour = you heal her.
5. ANUB'ARAK (ids read on page): P1 surface with
  Nerubian Burrowers (tank them ON permafrost); P2 he
  submerges (67322 Submerge): spikes PURSUE one player -
  kite them onto permafrost. Permafrost is made by
  killing Frost Spheres (npc 34564, page) -> 67856
  Permafrost patch blocks his burrow path; only ~6
  patches exist in heroic (plan them). P3 at 30%: no
  more submerge; Leeching Swarm - healers keep the raid
  LOW-but-alive (he heals from leech; healing high =
  healing him). 66012 Freezing Slash (tank freeze),
  68510 Penetrating Cold (heal the five).
  AI RULE: frost patches are a budget, spend them only
  on his pursuit line; in P3, minimal healing wins.

ONYXIA'S LAIR (map 249)
6. ONYXIA (level-80 version): P1 ground (cleave/tail -
  stand on her flanks, never front/back); P2 air: whelp
  waves from the side tunnels + deep breath (watch her
  position: breath covers a lane - move to safe side);
  P3 ground + fear (Bellowing Roar) + lava cracks erupt.
  AI RULE: sides only, always; deep breath is a lane,
  not an area - be out of the lane.

ICECROWN CITADEL (map 631)
7. LORD MARROWGAR: Bone Spike pins players (kill the
  spike, free them); Bonestorm (he spins, chases - run
  from him, healers spread); Coldflame lines (move out).
  Tanks stack together (Saber Lash splits).
  AI RULE: spiked companions are freed before boss DPS.
8. LADY DEATHWHISPER: P1 mana shield: casters burn the
  shield, melee kill adds (fanatics transform - kill
  fast/marked ones; adherents - interrupt, they are
  caster-immune by type). Death and Decay = move out.
  P2 shield down: tank swap (Touch of Insignificance
  threat drop), interrupt Frostbolt, ghosts run to
  players and explode (move away from ghosts).
  AI RULE: ghosts are walked AWAY from, never soaked.
9. GUNSHIP BATTLE: cannons + boarding: rocket-pack jump
  to the enemy ship, kill their mage (freezes your
  cannons) and crew, return; gunners keep firing, tank
  holds boarding party on your deck. AI RULE: frozen
  cannons mean the mage lives too long - board faster.
10. DEATHBRINGER SAURFANG: blood power: marks at 100
  power (marked players must NOT die - they heal him);
  blood beasts: slow/root/knockback, never let them hit
  anyone (they feed power); spread (Blood Nova).
  AI RULE: a beast touching anyone is boss health.
11. FESTERGUT: spores on 3 players: everyone stacks on
  them for inoculation before Pungent Blight; tank swaps
  on Gastric Bloat (10 stack = explosion risk);
  Vile Gas ranged spread. AI RULE: hug the spores.
12. ROTFACE: slimes: oozes merge - kite big ooze in a
  circle around the room's edge (offtank), little oozes
  are kited INTO the big one; Mutated Infection player
  runs to the ooze tank; slime pools move.
  AI RULE: everything slimy travels to the big ooze.
13. PROFESSOR PUTRICIDE: abomination vehicle player
  eats pools (slow him) and slows adds; gas/orange ooze
  fixates a player - kite until it reaches and pops;
  P3 tank swaps (Mutated Plague stacks), heroic: plague
  passed player-to-player (unverified here).
  AI RULE: fixated = run; pools are the abom's lunch.
14. BLOOD PRINCE COUNCIL: health shared; only one prince
  empowered (purple): DPS THE EMPOWERED one (others
  near-immune); Kinetic Bombs must be DPSed to keep them
  floating (they fall and explode); dark nuclei (hunter/
  lock pet or player babysits); empowered Valanar's
  vortex: spread/run out. Tank: Keleseth tanked by a
  caster at range. AI RULE: hit the purple prince;
  keep bombs in the air.
15. BLOOD-QUEEN LANA'THEL: bite chain: bitten players
  MUST bite another player before frenzy timer ends
  (plan bite order bite 1->2->4->8); air phase fear;
  Swarming Shadows = run fire out in a line; Pact of
  the Darkfallen: linked players run TOGETHER.
  AI RULE: bite on schedule or you kill us for her.
16. VALITHRIA DREAMWALKER: heal-the-boss fight: healers
  take portals into the dream (orbs = stacking heal
  buff, refresh before expiry), outside team kills adds:
  blazing skeletons (lay waste - move), suppressers on
  her (kill first), worms/mages interrupt.
  AI RULE: healers fly the rotation; the raid's job is
  keeping her sky open.
17. SINDRAGOSA: air phase: 5 players marked - they
  become ice blocks in a LINE; hide behind blocks for
  Frost Breath then DPS them free in order. Ground:
  Unchained Magic casters stop casting on stacks
  (backlash), Blistering Cold pulls everyone in - run
  OUT. P3 Mystic Buffet tank swaps.
  AI RULE: casters count stacks; pulled in = run out.
18. THE Lich King (15-min enrage, page): P1: Necrotic
  Plague on a player - they run TO the add pile (dispel
  there, plague jumps to adds and kills them); shamblers
  tanked apart (enrage). Intermission (70%/40%): edge of
  platform, Raging Spirits face who they copy - kill and
  look away rules (unverified), spheres shot down by
  ranged. P2: Val'kyr picks up a player and flies to the
  edge - ALL DPS on the Val'kyr or the player is dropped;
  Defile under a player GROWS if anyone stands in it -
  run out and never re-enter; Soul Reaper tank swaps.
  P3: Harvest Soul puts the raid inside Frostmourne
  (protect/heal Terenas, interrupt the Warden), Vile
  Spirits float down and explode (soak away or kill).
  AI RULE: Defile is a fire that grows - feet out, and
  it never gets a second tick on you.

RUBY SANCTUM (map 724)
19. MINIBOSSES (Saviana / Baltharus / Zarithrian):
  Saviana: flame breath cone + conflagration marks (run
  out). Baltharus: splits at health marks; blade tempest.
  Zarithrian: adds summoned; fear; tank swap cleave
  armour. AI RULE: copies die before the original (each).
20. HALION: two realms: outside (physical) and twilight
  realm (portal). Fiery Combustion (outside) / Soul
  Consumption (inside): debuff expires into a growing
  circle - run to the wall, drop it, cleansed at edge
  (dispel makes the circle - dispel placement matters).
  Corporeality balance: DPS him in the realm taking less
  damage (call switches: if one side lags he enrages
  that side); twilight cutter beams rotate around the
  room in P3 - stand between the beams, move with them.
  AI RULE: beams are clock hands - stand in the gap and
  walk with the clock.
