PART 1B - TWINK FACTS (paste under the header for every [TWINK] step)
============================================================
ALREADY ESTABLISHED - use it, do not research or repeat it:
 - The target is the LEVEL 29 twink bracket (the 20-29 battleground bracket). Death Knights and Demon Hunters cannot be twinks at this level and are not in this set. The Allied Races start at level 20 in 8.3.7, so they CAN be level 29 twinks.
 - A legal item has a required level of 29 or lower (or none) and can be obtained by a level 29 character without an auction house: vendor (including the battleground supply officers), quest reward, dungeon boss drop, battleground or dungeon crate, crafted, heirloom vendor, Darkmoon Faire, world rares with a real drop chance.
 - No armour type has a level gate in 8.3.7: Warrior and Paladin wear plate, Hunter and Shaman mail, Rogue, Monk and Druid leather, Priest, Mage and Warlock cloth, all from level 1.
 - Experience is turned off with Behsten (Alliance, entry 35365) or Slahtz (Horde, entry 35364).
 - Bind-on-account (heirloom) shoulders can take the Mists of Pandaria shoulder enchants, and legs can take leg armor.
 - A level 29 character has ONE PvP talent slot (slot 1 opens at level 20, slot 2 at level 40), separate from the normal talent rows. This comes from the 8.3.7 client data; do not research it.
 - Only the level 19 numbers are known: heirlooms were item level 23 at level 19 and scaling gear had item levels 19 to 31. Find out what they are at level 29.
