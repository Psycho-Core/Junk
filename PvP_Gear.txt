BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (build 35662) — SEASON 4 PvP GEAR

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: This season awarded gear through match rewards, 500-Conquest progress rewards, and rated weekly caches. It did NOT have the later expansion's purchasable, slot-priced Honor/Conquest combat sets. Calling Corrupted Aspirant an "honor vendor set" and Corrupted Gladiator a "conquest vendor set" with made-up per-slot currency costs would be incorrect. Marshal Gabriel/Xander Silberman handled conquest turn-ins and weekly reward pickup; later Marks of Honor ensemble sales are retrospective cosmetic unlocks, not 8.3.7 combat-gear purchase costs. Full 8.3.7 DB2 set IDs and every item/variant per slot: unverified; no arbitrary IDs filled in. Wowhead transmog-set IDs are website appearance identifiers, NOT in-game item-set IDs, and the looks do not imply a set bonus. Item pages today show level-squished item levels; do not use their modern ilvl 60 as season-four ilvl.

PvP SCALING / THE 'ITEM LEVEL TEMPLATE' QUESTION
Legion's normalized PvP stat templates were REMOVED for Battle for Azeroth. At level 120, entering a BG or arena does NOT automatically equip a 445/460/475 gear template and does NOT turn normal PvE gear into fixed-ilvl PvP gear. Normal equipped gear still supplies its real stats and Azerite traits; PvE gear can be used. BfA's separate PvP damage/healing scaling partially moderates the effect of item-level differences between players, including world PvP, arenas and BGs; it does not erase the advantage of good gear. The Warcraft Wiki PvP-scaling explanation says the calculation uses both players' character levels and equipped item levels, with heavier slot weighting for weapons and a minimum ilvl contribution around 286 for a level-120 empty/low slot; do NOT treat 286 as a free equipment upgrade. Precise coefficient/formula at build 35662: unverified. A level-120 character in normal gear keeps that gear and gets no fixed PvP gear set. PvP-specific item-ilvl increases in Shadowlands 9.1 and later must NOT be imported into BfA. PvP talent selection is separate from gear.

Use current equipped item effects and PvP tuning, not modern spell numbers.

BOT GEAR-SWITCH POLICY
Maintain two equipment loadouts: PvE for PvE encounters and PvP for arena/BG/PvP engagement. Select compatible armor type, spec primary stat, usable weapons, relevant Azerite traits, trinket effects, Versatility and survival; choose the actual higher-performing PvE piece if better than a season-four PvP drop. Preselect a PvP talent/CC response independently. Switch BEFORE queue entry / before combat when equipment changes are legal; do not attempt an illegal in-combat equipment-set swap, and retain a valid weapon. Switch back out of PvP, re-evaluate changed inventory and item bonus IDs. No BfA-only PvP item-level template should be scripted. Exact equipment-change restrictions in each arena/BG phase, spells and server-specific item-use conditions: unverified; test on build 35662.
