Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29). ALT = needs a higher-level crafter or content.
V = read off a page in this set. Unverified means unverified.

0. THREE THINGS THAT SHAPE A MONK'S SHOPPING LIST
   The classic enchant list was built for melee, which means it fits a monk
   better than any other class in this set. The mage file had to throw six
   of the seven enchants away; a monk keeps the agility cloak, the agility
   gloves, the boots, the chest and the weapon enchant, and only the bracer
   is the wrong stat.
   A monk's best weapon is a fist weapon, and fist weapons are blunt, so the
   weapon consumable is a weightstone, not a sharpening stone.
   Legs take an armour kit, not an enchant, and there is no belt enchant at
   this tier - a monk's belt is a green with nothing on it.
   Mistweaver is the only one of the three specs with a mana bar. For
   Brewmaster and Windwalker every mana potion and mana scroll in this file
   is dead weight.

2. WEAPON CONSUMABLES - THE ONE MONK-SPECIFIC DETAIL
   A weapon buff and a weapon enchant occupy the same slot, so a monk picks
   one. Both stones below are consumables, one use per 30 minutes, and
   neither needs a profession on the character using it.
   7965 Solid Weightstone|+6 weapon damage|item level 35, requires level 25,
     Blacksmithing 200|BLUNT - fist weapons, one-hand maces, staves
     This is the monk's stone, because 7683 Bloody Brass Knuckles is a fist
     weapon.
   7964 Solid Sharpening Stone|+6 weapon damage|item level 35, requires
     level 25, Blacksmithing 200|BLADED - swords, axes
     The one to buy if the monk is using 13033 Zealot Blade instead.
   3239 Rough, 3240 Coarse, 3241 Heavy are the cheaper weightstones;
   12643 Dense Weightstone and 12404 Dense Sharpening Stone need level 35.
   ALT.
   Oil is for the Mistweaver only: 20744 Minor Wizard Oil, requires level 5,
   +8 spell power for 1 hour, 5 charges, Enchanting 45, spell 25124, cannot
   be applied to an item above item level 165. A mistweaver heals off spell
   power, so the oil is worth more than +6 weapon damage to that one spec.

4. THE TWO PROFESSIONS
   LEATHERWORKING + ENGINEERING. Leatherworking because the leg kit is the
   only leg upgrade a 29 can have, and Engineering because its items are
   the only ones a melee twink can throw at someone.
   LEATHERWORKING
     4265 Heavy Armor Kit, skill 150, spell 3780 - the point of the
        profession at this level. Four of them cover legs on four characters.
     2304 Light Armor Kit, skill 1, +8 armor, no item level restriction.
     2313 Medium Armor Kit, skill 100, +16 armor.
     18948 Barbaric Bracers, item level 32, requires 27, Leatherworking
        spell 23399 - a blue bracer a monk can wear, and the only crafted
        one in the bracket. The spell id is unverified.
     Nothing else in Leatherworking matters at 29: every leg armour kit
     with a real number needs level 30 or 40.
   ENGINEERING
     4390 Iron Grenade|item level 35, skill 175, no level requirement|the
        stun, and the one Engineering item a 29 throws every fight
     4394 Big Iron Bomb|item level 43, skill 190|more damage, same idea
     4388 Discombobulator Ray|item level 32, 5 charges, skill 160|-40 melee
        and spell damage, -20% movement speed, and it dismounts
     4397 Gnomish Cloaking Device|item level 40, skill 200, no
        specialisation needed|a second vanish for a Windwalker
     4381 Minor Recombobulator|item level 28, trinket, 10 charges, no level
        requirement|breaks a polymorph on yourself - the only trinket in
        this file that does something every game
     10507 Solid Dynamite and 4384 Explosive Sheep, skill 175 and 150
     4385 Green Tinted Goggles|item level 30, cloth head, skill 150|legal,
        but a monk has 15339 Pathfinder Hat and does not need it
     10720 Gnomish Net-o-Matic is Gnomish Engineering 210, so a 29 cannot
        make one. ALT.
     109076 Goblin Glider Kit is auction house only. ALT.
   THE MISTWEAVER SWAP: Alchemy instead of Engineering, for 3385 Lesser Mana
   Potion and its own elixirs. It is a weaker argument than it looks, because
   every elixir and potion in section 3 can be bought - Alchemy only saves
   the markup. Engineering gives a mistweaver the recombobulator and a
   grenade, which is more than Alchemy gives back.
   ENCHANTING is the one profession a twink might want and cannot use: the
   neck and ring enchants in section 1 need the BfA enchanting skill, and a
   29 cannot train past the classic 225 cap. ALT by definition.

5. RACES
   Which races can be monks at all, before any ranking: the eight that have
   been monks since Mists of Pandaria - human, dwarf, night elf, draenei and
   worgen for the Alliance, blood elf, tauren and troll for the Horde - plus
   pandaren on both factions, plus the allied races.
   Gnome, orc and undead cannot be monks, so "races to avoid" is not
   advice for them; you cannot roll one.
   THE ALLIED-race line is from memory of the 8.3.7 character screen, not a
   page, and is UNVERIFIED: void elf, dark iron dwarf, kul tiran, mechagnome,
   nightborne, highmountain tauren, mag'har orc, vulpera. Allied races start
   at level 20 on 8.3.7, so all of them are legal 29 twinks.
   THE PICKS, best first, with the racial that earns the slot:
   TAUREN     20549 War Stomp - an area stun on no cast time, which is the
              single best racial a melee 29 can have. Also 20550 Endurance
              for base health.
   PANDAREN   107079 Quaking Palm - an incapacitate that ignores a lot of
              what breaks stuns, and 107072 Epicurean doubling the food
              buff. 107073 Gourmand, 107074 Inner Peace and 107076 Bouncy
              are the rest. The only race that adds both a control and a
              permanent stat gain.
   HUMAN      59752 Will to Survive - a crowd-control break on its own
              cooldown, separate from the PvP trinket, so a human monk
              effectively carries two. 20598 The Human Spirit.
   BLOOD ELF  50613 Arcane Torrent - area silence plus resource, the best
              answer to a caster in the bracket.
   NIGHT ELF  58984 Shadowmeld - a free stealth for a class that has none,
              and the escape that pairs with Roll.
   WORGEN     68992 Darkflight - a sprint on top of Roll and Tiger's Lust.
   TROLL      26297 Berserking - haste, which for a Windwalker is more
              presses of everything.
   DRAENEI    28880 Gift of the Naaru, a heal-over-time, plus 6562 Heroic
              Presence for hit rating.
   DWARF      20594 Stoneform - clears bleed, poison and disease and adds
              armor; the anti-rogue and anti-hunter pick.
   NO BAD PICK: a monk has no weapon skill, so no race's weapon bonus is
   wasted, and every monk-legal race has a racial a 29 can use. Pick by the
   control effect you want.

6. WHAT TO BUY, IN ORDER
   1. 13890 Minor Speed on the boots, then 13815 Agility on the gloves.
   2. 13882 Lesser Agility on the cloak, or 13746 on a second cloak.
   3. 13640 Greater Health on the chest, 4265 Heavy Armor Kit on the legs.
   4. 7965 Solid Weightstone for a fist weapon, plus 1477 Agility scrolls.
   5. 8949 Elixir of Agility and 3825 or 8951 as the guardian elixir.
   6. 1710 Greater Healing, 5634 Free Action, 2459 Swiftness, one school
      protection potion.
   7. 12210 Roast Raptor, doubled if the monk is a pandaren.
   8. 4390 Iron Grenades and 4381 Minor Recombobulator from Engineering.
   Everything in sections 1 to 3 except the neck, ring, shoulder and Outland
   entries is doable by a 29 with its own professions. The four ALT slots -
   neck, rings, shoulders and the bracer stat - are where a funded twink
   pulls ahead of a self-made one.
