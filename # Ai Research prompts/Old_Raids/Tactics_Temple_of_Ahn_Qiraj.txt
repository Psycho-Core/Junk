TACTICS - TEMPLE OF AHN'QIRAJ (OLD RAIDS STEP 4 OF 16)
Server: private BfA 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Context: level 60 raid entered by a level 120 character with
computer-controlled companions. Values read on warcraft.wiki.gg.
Zone: Temple of Ahn'Qiraj (Wowhead zone 3428). Instance map
id and all coordinates (map, x, y, z): unverified.
Boss entries verified on the Wowhead Temple NPC list:
Skeram 15263, Kri 15511, Yauj 15543, Vem 15544, Sartura 15516,
Fankriss 15510, Viscidus 15299, Huhuran 15509, Vek'lor 15276,
Vek'nilash 15275, Ouro 15517, C'Thun 15727 (Eye 15589 read on
its own page). Optional bosses: Bug Trio, Viscidus, Ouro.
D = dispel type. I = interruptible (only where read). Most
pages here name abilities without spell ids; ids are marked
unverified rather than invented (exceptions: Skeram, Sartura,
C'Thun P1, whose pages link ids).

1. THE PROPHET SKERAM (15263)
PHASES: splits at 75/50/25% (Summon Images 747: 2 copies of
  himself, and he teleports, swapping place with an image).
  Images must be identified (the real one keeps his health %;
  image health behaviour unverified) - burn the real one.
TANK: tank each Skeram (real + images) apart, facing away
  from casters (Earth Shock 26194 interrupts their casts).
GROUP: spread for Arcane Explosion 26192; the mind-controlled
  companion (True Fulfillment 785: +300% damage, instant
  casts, 20 sec) is crowd-controlled, NEVER killed - Wowhead
  warns MC'd players still hit very hard in small groups.
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: P1 images when they appear (tank/CC them), real
  Skeram always the kill target.
ABILITY LIST:
  26192 Arcane Explosion - 187-217 Arcane nearby.
  26194 Earth Shock - 149-174 Nature + 2-sec cast interrupt.
  785 True Fulfillment - mind control 20 sec (see GROUP).
  747 Summon Images - 2 copies at 75/50/25%.
  Teleport - swaps place with an image (id unverified).
AI RULE: CC the mind-controlled companion and keep killing
  the real Skeram; never DPS a companion to death.

2. SILITHID ROYALTY (OPTIONAL) - LORD KRI (15511),
   PRINCESS YAUJ (15543), VEM (15544)
PHASES: one shared fight; kill order sets loot (elite mode,
  page). Easiest order read: Kri, then Yauj, then Vem.
  15-minute hard enrage.
TANK: one tank per bug, held apart; Yauj tanked far from the
  raid (her AoE Fear wipes aggro). When Kri dies, Vem should
  be nearest his corpse (devours him) so Yauj cannot (which
  would give her a heal - page theory, filed as read).
GROUP: poison cleanse rotation (Kri's Toxic Volley stacks,
  D-Poison); on Kri's death EVERYONE runs from his corpse.
GROUND: Kri's death poison cloud - 2000/sec (page: almost
  guaranteed death stood in). The one ground zone here and
  the reason for the kill order spacing.
ADD ORDER: P1 Lord Kri, P2 Princess Yauj, P3 Vem; on Yauj's
  death AoE the small bugs she spawns (page strategy).
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified):
  Toxic Volley (Kri) - ~500 Nature + stacking poison DoT.
  Fear (Yauj) - AoE fear + aggro wipe.
  Heal (Yauj) - cast heal; INTERRUPT (kick/counterspell -
  she can resist and is silence-immune, page).
  Vem: heavy melee + devour-on-kill mechanic (see TANK).
AI RULE: kill Kri first, run from his corpse, then interrupt
  every Yauj heal cast.

3. BATTLEGUARD SARTURA (15516) + 3 ROYAL GUARDS (unv.)
PHASES: single phase; Enrage at 20% (+124 dmg, +60% speed -
  values from Frenzy 8269 family), hard enrage at 10 min.
TANK: Sartura needs 2 tanks ping-ponging taunts at the back
  of the room; one tank per guard, spread across the room.
  She and the guards randomly drop aggro for 5-sec chases -
  taunt back after. Never fight near the Fankriss tunnel
  entrance (guard punt + tunnel adds = wipe, page).
GROUP: everyone watches the Whirlwinds - a spinning boss/add
  moves fast and cannot be outrun in a straight line; run
  sideways and let it pass. Stun Sartura only when she is
  NOT spinning (immune to stun while Whirlwinding; guards
  can be stunned but keep spinning, page).
GROUND: 26083/26084 Whirlwind - 15-sec moving AoE (the mob
  itself is the zone).
ADD ORDER: P1-P3 Royal Guards, then Sartura (page: kill the
  guards away from her Whirlwind first).
ABILITY LIST:
  8269 Frenzy - +60% attack speed (Enrage family).
  25174 Sundering Cleave - +300% dmg, up to 3 targets,
    hits increase Physical damage taken by 25 (whether it
    stacks: unverified).
  26083 Whirlwind / 26084 Whirlwind (instant part).
AI RULE: if it is spinning, move; stun and kill only in the
  gaps between Whirlwinds.

4. FANKRISS THE UNYIELDING (15510)
PHASES: single phase with timed adds: from ~1 min after the
  pull, each of the 3 bug tunnels spawns 4 Vekniss
  Hatchlings (adds entry unverified) about every minute,
  and one companion is teleported and rooted in an alcove.
  Spawn of Fankriss worms (1-3, entry unverified) spawn
  randomly and ENRAGE after 20 sec (10-20k hits - page).
TANK: two tanks rotate on Fankriss (Mortal Wound cleave,
  healing -10%/stack - same family as Kurinnaxx); offtanks
  hold hatchlings (snare/fear them; they cannot be frost
  nova'd, page).
GROUP: the rooted companion is freed/healed in place; all
  DPS switches to a Spawn of Fankriss the instant it appears.
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: P1 Spawn of Fankriss (20-sec clock), P2 hatchling
  waves (kite/CC late in the fight, kill early), boss last.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Mortal Wound (cleave form),
  hatchling summons, teleport+root, worm spawn/enrage.
AI RULE: a yellow worm alive for 20 seconds wipes people -
  drop everything and kill it inside that timer.

5. VISCIDUS (15299, OPTIONAL)
PHASES: a shatter loop, not health phases: Frost hits slow
  him in 3 stages (read: 10 hits slowed 15%, 15 hits 30%,
  20 hits frozen solid). Once frozen, 30 melee hits shatter
  him into Globs (each glob = 5% of his health; he reforms
  at the centre from surviving globs). If shattered below
  5% total health he dies. Damage taken is halved.
TANK: one tank in his room only - he resets if pulled out
  of the room (page); run out of the room to reset a wipe.
GROUP: frost attackers use their FASTEST frost hits (count,
  not size - Frostbolt/Frost Shock/Icy Touch/read list);
  everyone melee-rushes on "frozen solid"; then focus-fire
  globs one at a time (splitting DPS wastes the shatter).
  Poison cleanse teams: Poison Volley DoT must never tick
  twice on a companion (500/2 sec, 10 sec, page).
GROUND: Toxin Clouds every 30-40 sec (~1500/2 sec inside,
  -40% move speed; radius bigger than the graphic; move on
  the single spit bolt that predicts one).
ADD ORDER: P1 Globs of Viscidus (assist train, one at a
  time), boss between shatters.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Poison Shock (~1200/10 sec,
  15 yd), Poison Volley (100-yd, ignores LoS, D-Poison DoT),
  Toxin Cloud. Frost/melee hit counters as PHASES.
AI RULE: count frost hits to frozen, melee hits to shatter,
  then kill globs one by one before they rejoin.

6. PRINCESS HUHURAN (15509)
PHASES: Frenzy phases (Tranquilizing Shot) until 30%, then
  Berserk: attack speed doubled, 2000-dmg Poison Bolts at
  the closest 15 players every ~3 sec for the rest.
TANK: taunt-immune - tanks rotate by threat management and
  aggro-drop tricks (page: invulnerability/BoP to drop);
  Acid Spit stacks only on the primary target (219-281/2s).
GROUP: 15 designated "soaks" stay closest to her and are
  kept alive at all costs (when one dies the volleys reach
  low-resist players); everyone else spreads out (Noxious
  Poison splashes: 2900/8 sec + silence, NOT cleansable).
  NEVER dispel Wyvern Sting (sleep): dispelling it deals
  3000 Nature to the woken player (page, explicit) - let
  sleepers sleep, except a tank who must take over.
GROUND: none placed; sleep area is melee range (10 targets).
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Frenzy (tranq-able), Berserk
  (30%, not tranq-able), Poison Bolt (closest 15), Noxious
  Poison, Wyvern Sting (sleep, see GROUP), Acid Spit.
AI RULE: never cleanse Wyvern Sting, and below 30% keep the
  15 closest companions alive - they are the shield.

7. TWIN EMPERORS - VEK'LOR (15276) + VEK'NILASH (15275)
PHASES: single fight, shared health percentage; Twin
  Teleport swaps their positions every 30-40 sec with a
  complete aggro wipe (they attack whoever is closest after
  a ~2-sec root). Berserk at 15 min. If they come within
  60 yd of each other they spam Heal Brother (30000/tick) -
  keep them at opposite walls.
TANK: Vek'nilash (physical, magic-immune except holy) is
  warrior-tanked against a wall. Vek'lor (caster, physical-
  immune) is warlock-tanked at range (Shadow Bolts 3000-
  4000): NOBODY enters his melee range - Arcane Burst
  (4000-4950 + knockback + 70% slow) counterattacks melee.
GROUP: DPS swaps sides at each teleport (physical DPS to
  Vek'nilash, casters to Vek'lor) and leaves 5 sec early so
  the right tank is closest at the swap; bug team kills
  Mutated bugs on the physical side (mutated bugs hit
  1000+, stay 4 min).
GROUND: Blizzard (Vek'lor) - moving/placed frost area,
  1500/tick + slow. Exploding bugs: 2813-3187 Fire after a
  3-sec spark warning (run) - Vek'lor detonates bugs within
  30 yd; Vek'nilash mutates bugs within 25 yd (grow 400%,
  hostile - kill them). Neutral scarabs/scorpions on the
  floor turn hostile if hit by AoE - do not AoE near them.
ADD ORDER: P1 Mutated Bugs (when they appear), P2 Emperors
  by DPS type. Neutral bugs: NEVER engage unless mutated.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Heal Brother, Twin Teleport,
  Uppercut + Unbalancing Strike (350% dmg, -100 defense
  6 sec - Vek'nilash), Shadow Bolt, Blizzard, Arcane Burst,
  Explode Bug, Mutate Bug, Virulent Poison (bug melee).
AI RULE: stay far from your emperor's opposite type - after
  every teleport, be on the correct side and never within
  melee range of Vek'lor.

8. OURO (15517, OPTIONAL)
PHASES: P1 surfaces/submerges: he submerges (untargetable)
  on a random ~90-sec chance and whenever nobody is in his
  melee range; scarabs spawn at each surfacing (despawn
  after 45 sec - CC them). P2 at 20%: no more submerging,
  enrage (+150% speed, +100% damage), Dirt Mound quakes
  continue; if nobody is in melee he throws ~6000 boulders.
TANK: multiple tanks rotate - Sweep knocks the tank back
  and Ouro turns to the next threat; the swept tank stays
  OUT until re-called (returning early re-flips aggro at
  low health, page). Sand Blast (every 20-25 sec, 180
  degrees frontal, 45 yd) hits the top-threat target and
  CLEARS their threat - tanks plan the swap around it.
GROUP: 3 groups spread 120 degrees apart (page); healers at
  the back beyond 45 yd where possible; never stand on the
  Dirt Mound (quake ticks until you die) or where he will
  surface (Ground Rupture 2000 on surfacing).
GROUND: Dirt Mound - his submerged position; surfacing
  point. Scarabs: CC, despawn 45 sec.
ADD ORDER: scarabs are CC-and-ignore (45-sec despawn);
  all DPS on Ouro. NEVER kill-order the scarabs.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Sweep, Sand Blast (3.5-
  4.5k Nature, binary/incapacitating), Submerge, Ground
  Rupture, Dirt Mound quake, boulder throw (P2, no melee).
AI RULE: someone must always be in his melee range (or he
  submerges / throws boulders), and nobody stands where
  the Dirt Mound is moving.

9. C'THUN (15727) - EYE (15589) THEN THE BODY
PHASES: P1 kill the Eye of C'Thun (15589). P2 the body
  rises with Carapace 26156 (99% damage reduction);
  players are devoured to his Stomach - kill both Flesh
  Tentacles inside to remove the carapace for 45 sec
  (burn window), repeat. Digestive Acid 26476 stacks
  inside - get out (exit platform behaviour unverified).
TANK: no conventional tank (fixed position). Melee stand
  spread around the Eye/body; tentacle duty tanks pick up
  Claw/Giant Claw Tentacles where they surface.
GROUP (P1): spread around the room so Eye Beam cannot
  chain (26134: jumps to nearby targets, +150% damage PER
  JUMP, 2-sec cast, cannot be interrupted - the spread is
  the whole defence). Dark Glare 26029: a rotating beam
  sweeps half the room over 35 sec (43750-56250) - track
  the Eye's facing and run with/behind the sweep, never
  through it. AI movement here is the raid's top priority
  (the beam is a fixed sweep - position, not health, is
  the defence; damage values are level-60-era).
GROUND: Dark Glare sweep zone; tentacle Ground Rupture
  points (26478 giant claw 2250-2750/15 yd; 26139 eye
  tentacle 1350-1650/10 yd, knockback).
ADD ORDER: P1 Eye Tentacles as they spawn (Mind Flay
  26143: 750/sec + snare - kill fast), P2 Claw Tentacles,
  P3 Giant tentacles; inside the Stomach: the 2 Flesh
  Tentacles above everything. C'Thun himself only during
  carapace-down windows (and P1's Eye is the target).
ABILITY LIST:
  26134 Eye Beam - chain (see GROUP). I: cannot (page).
  26029 Dark Glare - rotating sweep (see GROUP).
  26141 Hamstring (Claw) - 70% slow 10 sec.
  26478 / 26139 Ground Rupture - see GROUND.
  26143 Mind Flay (Eye Tentacle) - 750/sec, 10 sec.
  26156 Carapace of C'Thun - 99% reduction (P2 gate).
  26476 Digestive Acid - stomach stacking DoT 150/5 sec.
  Devour - sends a player to the Stomach (id unverified).
AI RULE: spread so the Eye Beam has nobody to jump to, and
  move with the Dark Glare - in P2, only hit C'Thun while
  the carapace is down.
