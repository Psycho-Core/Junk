  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 870 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: all 51 resolved, but none of them from creature_template - all 51 master names were run against the two
  creature_template exports in this repo (the 4.3.4 TDB update pack and the 8.x dump) and not one of them matches a row there, so
  those exports stop before Pandaria. 42 entries come from section 3.1 of the research document, which is a Wowhead lookup pass,
  and the other 9 were read
  this run from the wiki NPC page of each master, where the Elinks-NPC id is the Wowhead id: Studious Chu 59736, Tylen
  Moonfeather 59049, Sharinga Springrunner 61121, Tania Summerbreeze 61122, Infiltrator Ik'thal 65511, Tiper Windman 65865,
  "Dragonwing" Dan 58843, Kite Master Len 61512 and Morla Skyblade 65863.
  One of the 42 section-3.1 entries was wrong. That table gives node 1117 - Serpent's Spine, Kun-Lai Summit, Horde - the master
  Mai of the Wall (63497), but Mai of the Wall is the master of 1073, the Vale of Eternal Blossoms Serpent's Spine node, and
  section 3.1 gives 63497 to both. The Kun-Lai tower is the Serpent's Spine Horde Tower and its master is Morla Skyblade, whose
  three flight connections - Shrine of Two Moons (1058), Gao-Ran Battlefront (1054) and Longying Outpost (1053) - are exactly
  the three links this node has in the export. 65863 is printed and the master note says so.
  Gate evidence: two of the 51 nodes are gated and 49 are not. ConditionID 17197 sits on 894 Grookin Hill and decodes as after
  quest 29879, which Wowhead names "Swallowed Whole"; the wiki instead names "Orders are Orders" for that flight point, which is
  the next quest in the same chain. ConditionID 19486 sits on 1190 Lion's Landing and decodes as after quest 32108, 32109 or
  32477, the 5.1.0 Lion's Landing chain the wiki refers to. No Pandaria taxi node is gated on level, race, class, achievement or
  reputation, and no other node carries a ConditionID at all. Where the wiki adds a quest condition the export does not have -
  The Incursion, Eastwind Rest, Westwind Rest, Domination Point - that is printed as a master note and not as a gate.
  Faction: TaxiNodes.Flags gives 10 Alliance-only roosts, 10 Horde-only roosts and 31 with both bits set; each block prints the
  bits it actually found rather than inferring a side from the name.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Every one of the 258 directed rows in section 870 targets
  another node of this same set, so no leg in this file carries an off-map tag. Distances are per direction: the row 1073 -> 1025
  reads 3033 yd while the row 1025 -> 1073 reads 2977 yd, and each block prints its own direction rather than reusing the other.
  Sources: TaxiNodes and TaxiPath exports for build 8.3.7.35662, the PlayerCondition decode of ConditionIDs 17197 and 19486, the
  Warcraft Wiki flight-master tables by location and zone plus nine NPC pages read from it this run, Wowhead quest 29879, and the
  creature_template check that found no Pandaria rows. Everything read from a source is printed; nothing was estimated.
