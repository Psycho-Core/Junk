WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 1643 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: 49 of the 57 nodes print one and 8 print "unverified". All 49 are the entry section 3.1 of the research
  document prints for that node id, with two exceptions where that table names a master of the wrong faction: 2095 Shrine of the
  Storm is Horde-only but was given Galeheart, the gryphon the wiki places in the Alliance camp, and 2276 Tol Dagor is Horde-only
  but was given Jorum Hayes 130593, whom Wowhead marks Alliance react. Both print "unverified" rather than borrowing the Alliance
  master from 2133 and 2096. The other six open entries are 2052 Anglepoint Wharf, 2053 Old Drust Road, 2054 Firebreaker
  Expedition, 2055 Southwind Station and 2104 Eastpoint Station - five of the eight nodes the quest gate below covers, where no
  source read here names a master - and 2441 Prospectus Bay on Mechagon, where the wiki lists the Horde camp's goblins but no
  flight master.
  Two of the gated roosts do print an entry, because section 3.1 gives them the same master as their ungated twin at the same
  town: 2056 Fallhaven carries Clarence Page 124466 like 2033, and 2057 Fletcher's Hollow carries Bridget Bran 126724 like 2035.
  The third gated duplicate, 2105 Tradewinds Market, carries Joan Weber 124725 like 2083. All three pairs are Alliance on both
  sides, so the shared master is possible; no page pins either master to one roost rather than the other.
  Gate evidence: 8 of the 57 nodes are gated and 49 are not, and all eight share one gate, ConditionID 62086, decoded as a
  PrevQuest condition satisfied by quest 52128 or 55142. The eight are 2052, 2053, 2054, 2055, 2056, 2057, 2104 and 2105. No Kul
  Tiras taxi node is gated on level, class, race, reputation or an achievement.
  Faction: TaxiNodes.Flags gives 35 Alliance-only roosts, 17 Horde-only roosts and 5 with both bits set, which sums to 57; each
  block prints the bits it found rather than inferring a side from the name. Every one of the 57 nodes starts at least one
  TaxiPath row, so this map has no arrival-only node and no block prints a reverse-only leg.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Distances are per direction: 2023 -> 2077 reads 900 yd while
  2077 -> 2023 reads 1146 yd. The 234 directed rows form 117 pairs with a row each way, and one pair, 2037 Barbthorn Ridge and
  2084 Norwington Estate, happens to measure the same both ways.
  Unlock: a node's bit is set by visiting its flight master, so the practical requirement is reaching the zone. Flying over Kul
  Tiras additionally needs Battle for Azeroth Pathfinder, Part One (achievement 12989) and Part Two (13250); a taxi flight needs
  neither, only the node's bit.
  Fourteen names in this file are longer than the 33-character name column of the source table, which cuts them mid-word
