=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

There is no pillar set on this map. The LoS objects are a central
tomb and a scatter of tombstones.

Verified from the core: the server spawns no LoS object at all.
Only the two gates and the two Shadow Sight orbs are game objects,
so the tomb and the tombstones are world model geometry and carry
no coordinates in server data.

What the layout sources state:
  "In the middle of the arena, there is a tomb that can be used
   to block LoS and to get high ground advantage. There are also
   various tombstones that can be used to LoS as well."
  The arena is "basically flat" - no bridge, no stairs, both ends
  are symmetrical staging areas
  Patch 3.2.0 removed the alcoves from the starting chambers,
  smoothed the collision round the central tomb, and added two
  line-of-sight tombstones to the slime pool on the southern side
  The southern side of the map has a slime pool

Central tomb, verified height only:
  Graveyard row 1260 sits at 1285.81, 1667.9, Z 39.9576
  The twelve "Game On" teleport targets (WorldSafeLoc 3806-3816
  plus 3817) all sit at 1286.57, 1667.4, Z 39.602
  Those two Z values are the only evidence of the tomb's top
  height; the tomb footprint is unverified

Tomb and tombstone coordinates: unverified (no readable source
with x, y, z for any of them)
Tombstone count: unverified beyond "various", plus the two added
to the southern slime pool in 3.2.0

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Melee DPS:
  The standard opener documented for this map is to stay in the
  starting area and let the enemy team come to you; the staging
  chamber and the gate line give a free first hit to melee
  Otherwise close along the long axis and use a tombstone to
  break the first cast

Ranged DPS:
  Climb the central tomb and cast from the top
  The tomb both blocks LoS and gives height, so it is the
  strongest single spot on the map
  The tombstones are the fallback when the tomb is contested

Healers:
  Top of the central tomb is the documented healer perch, for
  the same two reasons
  Standing behind a tombstone at range is the alternative
  Do not stand in the open on the long axis; the map is flat, so
  there is nothing else to break LoS

General opener notes:
  Both teams start equidistant (79.7 units) from the centre, so
  the first contest is always the tomb
  The slime pool on the southern half is the only asymmetric
  feature on the map; the two LoS tombstones added there in
  3.2.0 make that side defensible

=== 7. MATCH FLOW AND RECOVERY NOTES ===

Gates close before the start and open together.
Worldstate 0xbba (3002) is set to 1 on this map.

area triggers 4941, 5041 and 5042, teleports the player to
1285.810547, 1667.896851, 39.957642 - the same point as the
graveyard row. That is the tomb top, not the floor.

four quadrant points listed in section 4.
