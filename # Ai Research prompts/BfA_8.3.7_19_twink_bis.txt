0. HOW THESE NUMBERS WERE PRODUCED, AND WHY THEY ARE NOT THE 2020 WIKI NUMBERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.1  Every armour value, weapon damage, weapon speed and stat value in this
     document was recomputed from the 8.3.7 client tables and from the exact
     formulas TrinityCore 8.3.7/35662 uses to turn those tables into item values:

     - ItemSparse (build 8.3.7.35662): ItemLevel, RequiredLevel, OverallQualityID,
       Bonding, InventoryType, ItemDelay (stored in milliseconds), DmgVariance,
       StatModifier_bonusStat_0..9, StatPercentEditor_0..9,
       ScalingStatDistributionID, Flags_0/1, RequiredSkill, RequiredSkillRank.
     - ItemArmorQuality.Qualitymod_q x ItemArmorTotal.<class> x ArmorLocation.<mod>
       = armour, rounded to the nearest integer (ItemTemplate::GetArmor).
     - ItemArmorShield.Quality_q = shield armour.
     - ItemDamageOneHand / ItemDamageTwoHand (and the *Caster variants for items
       flagged 0x200) .Quality_q = damage per second at that item level; average
       hit = dps x ItemDelay/1000; min = average x (1 - DmgVariance/2);
       max = floor(average x (1 + DmgVariance/2) + 0.5)
       (ItemTemplate::GetDPS and ItemTemplate::GetDamage).
     - stat = StatPercentEditor_i x RandPropPoints[ilvl] / 10000, rounded
       (Item::GetItemStatValue). Hybrid stat ids 71-74 (Agi/Str/Int, Agi/Str,
       Agi/Int, Str/Int) are applied to every component stat, which is what
       Player.cpp 7696-7719 does; that is why heirloom plate reads
       "+6 Strength, +6 Intellect".

0.2  Level scaling. An item whose ScalingStatDistributionID is not 0 takes its
     item level from the curve named in ScalingStatDistribution and not from
     ItemSparse (Item::GetItemLevel). Every heirloom used here is on
     ScalingStatDistribution 862/884/890/926/929, all of which point at
     Curve 956, which gives item level 23 at character level 19 (64 at 60, 284
     at 120). Heirlooms therefore appear in this document at item level 23, not
     at the 85/463/600 figures that heirloom wiki pages print; those figures are
     the old static upgrade tiers and are not level-19 data.

0.3  Why the numbers differ from old wiki tooltips. The item tooltips cached in
     Wowpedia's pages for old-world gear were never refreshed after the stat
     squishes. Shadowfang's page still shows "16 - 30 @ 2.70, +5 Agility", which
     is a pre-7.3.5 value; the 8.3.7 client says Shadowfang is item level 24,
     6 - 11 at 2.60, +9 Agility. Every number below comes from the client, so
     where an old guide quotes a bigger number for an old item, the guide is
     stale and not the client. Wowpedia and Wowhead were used only for names,
     ids, drop sources, quest levels and removal patches, never for values.

0.4  Availability rule used to decide what may be listed as best in slot:
     (A) heirlooms (account-bound, any level, item level 23 at character 19);
     (B) bind-on-equip items, because a 19 can buy them or loot them;
     (C) drops from the low-level dungeons a 19 can walk into: Ragefire Chasm,
         the Deadmines, Wailing Caverns and Shadowfang Keep (all four are
         level 15-26 instances in 8.3.7 and none has a level gate on entry);
     (D) quest rewards, but only where the quest itself is available at 19
         (quest level 19 or lower - each one is named with its level);
     (E) world events and PvP that a 19 can take part in: the Stranglethorn
         Fishing Tournament, the Gurubashi Arena, the Darkmoon Faire, and the
         battleground vendors;
     (F) crafted gear from a profession a 19 can reach - every craft caps at
         skill 150 at level 19 (section 1.3).
     Anything that fails (A)-(F) is named in section 7 with the reason it fails.
     An item being wearable is not the same as being obtainable, and the list
     below only contains items that are both.

     One class of item is excluded as a whole, not because it is bad but
     because its availability cannot be established: the 151xxx-156xxx item
     series (151063, 151070, 151427, 151430, 156955, 156990 ...). Several of
     those items would win a slot outright. Their pages either carry no source
     text or name the heroic mode of a level-85 instance, and no source states
     which of the two item series a level-19 run of a low-level dungeon
     produces. They are listed with their numbers in 7.6 instead of being
     guessed into a slot.

0.5  Ranking rule used inside a slot for a warrior, stated once so every table
     can be checked against it:
     - a usable point is Strength, Stamina, Critical Strike, Haste,
       Versatility or Hit; each counts 1.0;
     - Agility, Intellect and Spirit count 0: in 8.3.7 a warrior converts none
       of them into attack power, so a "+8 Agility or Intellect" item is worth
       only its Stamina and secondaries to this class;
     - Dodge and Parry count 0.4;
     - armour is not scored but breaks ties: at equal points the mail or plate
       item wins over leather, and leather over cloth;
     - weapons are compared on damage per second first, because Mortal Strike,
       Execute and Shield Slam all scale with weapon damage, and inside equal
       dps on Strength before Stamina. Section 5.2 explains the one place where
       a 4% dps difference is allowed to lose to six stat points.
     The weights are a ranking device only; the item values themselves are the
     client values.

1. WHAT A LEVEL 19 WARRIOR MAY ACTUALLY USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Armour proficiency. The client's SkillRaceClassInfo table gives Plate Mail
     (skill 293) to class mask 3 (warrior + paladin) at minimum level 1, so a 19
     warrior wears plate, not mail. This is the post-7.0.3 rule (Wowpedia,
     "Plate armor": "Paladins and warriors now begin with plate armor at level 1
     instead of mail armor", patch 7.0.3). Mail (413), Leather (414) and Cloth
     (415) are also open to a warrior, and Shields (433, class mask 67) are
     warrior/paladin/shaman.

     The staff row is the one surprise: mask 2005 = 1+4+16+64+128+256+512+1024,
     which includes the warrior bit, so the client does not lock staves away
     from a warrior. The only staff in the level-20-26 band that carries any
     Strength at all is Hardened Root Staff (1317, item level 25, +1 Strength),
     and it was removed from the game in 4.0.3a, so the skill changes nothing
     below.

1.3  Professions at 19. Every profession's first two ranks are open and the
     third is not: Apprentice at level 5 (skill 1-75), Journeyman at level 10
     (skill 75-150), Expert at level 20 (skill 150-225) - Wowpedia,
     "Engineering", revision of 2020-05-01, same table for the other crafts.
     A 19 therefore caps at skill 150, which is exactly why Engineering 150
     goggles are wearable and Engineering 175+ goggles are not, and why
     Blacksmithing 105/110 recipes are craftable while 120+ recipes for
     level-20 items are not.

5.2  Weapon. The heirloom one-hander is the pick because of its 18 usable
     stat points: Bloodsoaked Skullforge Reaver (122389), 6 - 11 at 2.60,
     +5 Str +7 Sta +6 Haste. The higher-dps alternatives are Black Metal Axe
     (885, 5 - 12 at 2.60, 3.47 dps, +6 Str +6 Sta) and Shadowfang (1482,
     6 - 11 at 2.60, 3.47 dps, +9 Agility); Bronze Mace (2848, blacksmithing
     110, 7 - 9 at 2.60, +4 Str +8 Crit +6 Sta) matches the heirloom's 18
     points at 3.18 dps and is the cheap substitute. A Protection warrior's
     weapon damage reaches Shield Slam and Devastate, but auto-attacks are a
     minority of its damage at 19, so 0.14 dps (4%) does not buy back the six
     stat points the heirloom has over Black Metal Axe.

5.3  Shield. Flamescarred Draconian Deflector (122391) is 49 armour with
     +4 Str +6 Sta +5 Haste +11 Int, 15 usable points. Redbeard Crest (12997),
     Grunt's Shield (15512) and Forest Buckler (6383) are 51 armour with
     +4 Str +6 Sta +11 Int, 10 usable points: two more armour against five
     points of Haste and one point of Stamina. The heirloom wins. Redbeard
     Crest is the best non-heirloom and it is bind on equip, so it can simply
     be bought.

9. AUDIT TRAIL FOR THIS PART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Every item row in sections 3-6 is generated by one script from ItemSparse,
     Item and the five formula tables (work/war/build_bis.py), and the same
     script prints the ilvl, the required level, the armour or damage line and
     every stat, so a row cannot drift from the client without the generator
     drifting with it. Every printed row was then re-derived independently by
     work/war/audit_bis.py, which re-reads the finished document, matches each
     row to its item id and compares name, ilvl, required level, armour or
     damage range and speed, and every stat against the client tables:
     111 rows scanned, 111 matched, 0 mismatched.
9.2  The non-item claims - profession and proficiency gates, spell levels,
     weapon skill masks, Titan's Grip, enchant item-level gates and enchant ids,
     armour kits, inscriptions, consumable levels, quest levels, removed-item
     levels and every "N usable points" count in the document - are checked one
     at a time by work/war/verify_bis.py: 212 checks, 212 pass, 0 fail.
9.3  This part was built in two passes, and the second pass changed the answer.
     The first pass ranked a hand-built candidate list of the classic level-19
     twink items. The audit then re-ran the ranking over the whole ItemSparse
     table - every wearable item with RequiredLevel <= 19 and item level <= 32,
     scored by the rule in 0.5 - and found better items in three slots:

       the second slot opposite the PvP Insignia.
