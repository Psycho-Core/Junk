1. Argaloth
PHASES: single; Fel Firestorm at 66% and 33%.
Berserk 47008 at 5 min (+150% speed, +900%).

2. Occu'thar
PHASES: single, alternates Focused Fire and
Eyes. TANK: Searing Shadows 101007 (105000

3. ALIZABAL

BASTION OF TWILIGHT
====================

10. Halfus Wyrmbreaker (+ Proto-Behemoth)
Weekly drake set (3 of 5 normal, all heroic):
free drakes to counter Halfus: each freed
drake debuffs him, is then mind-controlled,
must be tanked/killed; its death gives
Dragon's Vengeance (+100% damage taken on
Halfus, stacking). Proto-Behemoth: ignore
(never kill order), dodge Fireball barrage;
Scorching Breath if whelps dormant.
Halfus buffs by dormant drake: Frenzy (attack
speed), Malevolent Strikes (stacking -heal
on tank - swap), Shadow Nova (interrupt its
cast when Storm Rider freed slows it -500%).
Drake counters: Nether Scion -25% hit/speed/
damage; Slate Stone Grip (12s stuns on
Halfus); Time Warden slows fireballs; whelps
Atrophic Poison -750 drake damage x8.
Furious Roar below 50%: 3x 7600-8400 + 2s
stun - trinket/heal between roars.
Spell ids mostly unverified on page chunk.
ADD ORDER: freed drake (tanked) > Halfus >
NEVER Proto-Behemoth. Release order by
week: Scion first if Frenzy active.
