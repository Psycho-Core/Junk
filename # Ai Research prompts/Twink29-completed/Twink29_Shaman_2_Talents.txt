TWINK29 - SHAMAN - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

0. WHAT A LEVEL 29 HAS
   Normal talents: seven rows, unlocked at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and
   100. A level 29 character owns the first row only, one point.
   PvP talents: three slots at 20, 40 and 70. A 29 owns the first slot
   only, and even then a PvP talent is legal only if its own level is 20
   or lower. The PvP slot is separate from the normal talent point - the
   owner asked whether the two are shared, and they are not.
   All three specializations are open at 29. Elemental and Restoration
   are Intellect, Enhancement is Agility, so this is the one class in the
   set whose specs do not even share a primary stat.
   V = read off the talent's wiki page including the patch line that
   states its BfA level.

1. ELEMENTAL
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Earthen Rage, spell 170374. Passive. Your damaging spells incite the
     earth around you to attack your most recently attacked target for
     6 sec. It was a level 15 Elemental talent until 10.0.0 removed it
     (11.0.0 re-added it "was a level 15 Elemental talent") (V), and
     7.1.5 set its damage at 55% of spell power (was 50%), which is the
     last change before 8.3.7 and therefore the BfA figure.
   THE OTHER TALENTS of the row were not read for this file.
   PICK  Earthen Rage. Passive, no button, no setup, and it fires off the
     spells an Elemental bot is already casting. At 29, where the step 1
     file found this spec almost no Intellect gear, a talent that scales
     with spell power and asks for nothing is the only sane pick.
   PVP  no verified level 20 pick for this spec - see section 4.

2. ENHANCEMENT
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Hot Hand, spell 201900. Passive. Melee attacks with Flametongue
     active have a chance to make your next Lava Lash cost no Maelstrom
     and deal 100% increased damage. 9.0.1 moved it to level 25 ("was
     15") and rewrote it, so the text above is the pre-9.0.1 wording
     quoted in that patch line, which is the 8.3.7 form (V).
   TRAP  Forceful Winds is not available to a 29, and it is the talent a
     current Enhancement guide lists in this row. 9.0.1 says "Now
     available at level 15 (was 30)" (V), so in 8.3.7 it was a level 30
     talent, one level out of reach. Its BfA numbers were also different:
     8.1.0 reduced its damage per stack to 80% (was 100%).
   PICK  Hot Hand. It is the verified level 15 talent, it is passive in
     the sense that matters to a bot - the proc needs a Lava Lash after
     it, which a script can do - and it costs no Maelstrom when it fires.
   PVP  no verified level 20 pick for this spec - see section 4.

3. RESTORATION
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Undulation, spell 200071. Passive. Every third Healing Wave or
     Healing Surge heals for an additional 50%. 10.0.0 confirms it "was a
     level 15 Restoration talent" (V), and no patch between 7.0.3 and
     8.3.7 changes the 50% or the every-third count.
   THE OTHER TALENTS of the row were not read. Torrent is the name a
     current tree gives the row's first talent; no page under that name
     could be read for this file, so it is mentioned here and not filed
     as an option.
   PICK  Undulation. Passive and unconditional: it improves the two
     spells a Restoration bot casts most, on a fixed count it does not
     have to track. For a healing companion this is the easiest talent in
     the whole shaman file to get full value from.
   PVP  Grounding Totem - the verified pick for this class, section 4.

4. THE PVP SLOT AT LEVEL 20
   LEGAL AND VERIFIED
     Grounding Totem, Restoration only, level 20, 30 sec cooldown:
     summons an Air Totem at your feet that redirects all harmful spells
     cast on a nearby party or raid member to itself; it will not redirect
     area of effect spells. 8.0.1 converted it from a rank 43 honor talent
     (V), and no later patch line changes its level, which is the basis
     for reading 20 as the BfA value - the same argument from absence the
     warlock file used for Nether Ward, and stated as such.
     Two BfA details differ from the current page: the totem had 5 health
     (9.0.1 raised it to 50, "was 5") (V), and 7.3.0 stopped redirected
     spells damaging it (V). Its duration in 8.3.7 was not read, so no
     duration is claimed here beyond what is printed above.
   WHY IT IS THE PICK  A Restoration shaman's problem in a battleground
     is the interrupt: one Counterspell or Wind Shear landed and the heal
     does not happen. Grounding Totem eats that cast. It also has to be
     down before the cast, like Nether Ward, so for a companion bot it is
     a scripted drop on entering combat, not a reaction.
   NOT VERIFIED
     Elemental and Enhancement PvP talents. Grounding Totem is filed as a
     Restoration talent on its page, so it is not offered to the other
     two specs; whether either of them has a level 20 PvP talent in
     8.3.7 was not read. Names a current list would give - Thundercharge,
     Spectral Recovery, Swelling Maelstrom, Forked Lightning - are
     deliberately not filed with levels attached.
   THE PATTERN TO REMEMBER  Forceful Winds makes three talents in two
     classes (with the warlock's Writhe in Agony) that sat at level 30 in
     BfA and only reached the level 15 row in 9.0.1. It is the single most
     repeated error a current guide makes for this bracket.

5. THE THREE LOADOUTS
   ELEMENTAL    Earthen Rage. PvP unverified.
   ENHANCEMENT  Hot Hand. PvP unverified.
   RESTORATION  Undulation. PvP Grounding Totem.
   As with the rogue, only one spec has both slots filled from read
   pages, and it is the healer. A Restoration companion is therefore the
   shaman spec that can be scripted most completely at 29, which lines
   up with the step 1 finding that it is also the worst geared.

6. WHAT IS SOLID AND WHAT IS NOT
   Solid: the row and slot gating and that the two systems are separate;
   Earthen Rage 170374 at level 15 and 55% of spell power; Hot Hand
   201900 at level 15 in its pre-9.0.1 form; Undulation 200071 at level
   15 and 50% every third cast; Forceful Winds at level 30 in BfA as the
   trap; Grounding Totem for Restoration at 20 with 5 health.
   Not solid: the unread talents in every row, Torrent's BfA form,
   Grounding Totem's duration and whether its level ever moved, every
   Elemental and Enhancement PvP talent, and Hot Hand's proc chance -
   the 7.2.5 line says the proc rate was reduced by 29% without giving a
   number, so none is printed.
