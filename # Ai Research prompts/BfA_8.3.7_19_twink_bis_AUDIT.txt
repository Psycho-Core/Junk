A. WHAT WAS CHECKED, AND HOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1 Item rows. Sections 3, 4, 5 and 6 of the deliverable are tables of items.
   Every row was re-read out of the finished document, matched to its item id,
   and rebuilt from the 8.3.7 client tables by work/war/audit_bis.py: name,
   item level, required level, armour, or the damage range and speed, and every
   single stat. Heirloom rows are rebuilt at the item level a level-19
   character actually sees (Curve 956 = 23), not at the item level stored in
   ItemSparse. Result: 111 rows, 111 match.
A2 Non-item claims. The 212 checks below (work/war/verify_bis.py) each read one
   table or one page and compare it with one printed sentence or number:
   proficiency, weapon skills, spell levels, Titan's Grip, the absent
   Single-Minded Fury, enchant item-level gates, the enchant id behind each
   scroll, armour kits, inscriptions, consumable levels, removed-item required
   levels, the "N usable points" arithmetic on every ranked item, and the
   level of every quest named in section 7.
A3 Sources. Item sources come from the client Heirloom table (SourceText_lang)
   or from the item's Wowpedia page at a recorded revision (section D). Quest
   levels come from the quest page itself at a recorded revision (section D3).
A4 Nothing in this part is carried over from an earlier task unchecked. The
   enchant census was rebuilt in this task and every scroll named in the
   document was re-checked against SpellItemEnchantment (C10, C10b, C10c).

F. DEFECTS FOUND, AND WHAT WAS CHANGED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1 Waist. The first draft inherited 6468 Deviate Scale Belt (9 usable points,
   4 armour) and offered 14559 Prospector's Sash (10 points) as an
   alternative. A sweep of the whole ItemSparse table found 6460 Cobrahn's
   Grasp: plate, item level 21, 8 armour, +4 Str +4 Int +6 Sta +4 Crit +2
   Haste = 16 usable points, from Lord Cobrahn in the Wailing Caverns. It is
   now the pick; both older items are runners-up with their numbers.
F2 Hands. 12994 Thorbia's Gauntlets (7 armour, +8 Agi +8 Int +7 Sta = 7
   usable points) was the pick. 62885 Unmistakable Gloves are 9 armour and 10
   usable points and come from a level-18 Horde quest, so they are now the
   pick. Thorbia's stays as the faction-neutral, auction-house alternative;
   6577 Defender Gauntlets and 14747 Hulking Gauntlets are named as the same
   stat line in other art. 26035 Corin's Handguards, the Alliance equal, is
   named but not used because its quest page has no level (E5).
F3 Trinket. 19024 Arena Grand Master (+8 Sta) took the first slot. 126948
   Defending Champion (+6 Sta +4 Vers = 10 points) has the higher total and
   moves to the first slot; the Arena Grand Master stays in the document as
   the second-slot alternative, with the PvP Insignia for battlegrounds.
F4 Two-hander numbers. 6641 Haunting Blade was printed at 3.89 dps and 5187
   Foe Reaper at 3.89; the client says 4.02 and 3.83. Both corrected.
F5 4818 Executioner's Sword was described as the only 4.60 dps two-hander.
   False: 1455 Blackrock Champion's Axe is 4.60 dps at 3.60 s with +12 Str as
   well, and 15269 Massive Battle Axe is 4.60 dps with 15 points. The
   document now states the trade explicitly instead of claiming uniqueness.
F6 The 'world drop' label was asserted for 14743 Hulking Bands and 4818
   Executioner's Sword. Neither page states a source, so both rows now say
   'bind on equip, auction house', which is what the client's Bonding field
   supports and nothing more.
F7 Exclusion claims were re-checked against the quest page rather than the
   item page: 65724 Grasps of the Unyielding (quest 25), 60925 Festering
   Bracers (24), 60937 Restored Archeus (25), 65937 Serpentis' Gloves (21) and
   56688 Belt of Equilibrium (24) are confirmed out of reach; 2230 Gloves of
   Brawn and 3559 Night Watch Gauntlets were added as removed quest rewards;
   156953 'Liberated' Greataxe was moved from 'quest-gated' to unverified,
   because no page states its quest (E4).
F9 Exclusion list. Four items in 7.2 were excluded as "quest above level 19"
   when their pages actually say the quest that awarded them was removed in
   4.0.3a: 3743 Sentry Buckler, 3747 Meditative Sash, 3749 High Apothecary
   Cloak and 16978 Warsong Gauntlets. They are now in 7.1, where the reason
   they cannot be used is the one that applies.
F10 Gloves of the Jungle King (61127) was printed as 22 usable points; the
   client gives +6 Str +6 Int +9 Sta +6 Crit, which is 21 usable points
   (Intellect is worth nothing to a warrior). Corrected.
F11 The two Pauldrons of the Wayward Tribe ids were excluded without a named
   quest. The page gives Zul'Mamwe Mambo, level 29 on both faction pages, so
   the exclusion now names it. 57499 Infested Breastplate likewise now names

   Treasure, Guile of the Raptor and Wanted! Otto and Falconcrest, all 28.
F8 Earlier corrections that still stand, re-checked in this pass: the Mortal
   Strike / Execute / Shield Slam normalisation sentences (unsupported, cut);
   the 'Crusader is not unique' claim (unsupported, cut); the ring-enchant row
