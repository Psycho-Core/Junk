READ THIS FIRST - this arena and step 10's Hook Point are the
same map under different names. There is exactly one Kul Tiras
arena map in the client, and every readable source agrees on the
id even though they disagree on the name:
  - Map.dbc has a single entry, directory KulTirasArena,
    MapID 1825, and its MapName field says "Hook Point"
  - battleground_template has a single row for it, id 897,
    name 'Hook Point'; no row mentioning Tiras or Hook exists
    other than that one
  - the world safe locations on map 1825 are commented
    "Kul Tiras Arena - Purple Team - Start" and
    "Kul Tiras Arena - Gold Team - Start", so the server data
    itself uses the Kul Tiras Arena name
  - Wowhead lists two arena zones added in 8.0.1: 9278 "Kul
    Tiras Arena" and 9279 "Hook Point"
  - SharedDefines.h declares BATTLEGROUND_HOOK_POINT = 897 and
    no Kul Tiras arena enum; MAP_KUL_TIRAS = 1643 is the
    continent, not this instance
So this document and Arena_Hook_Point_COMPLETED.txt describe one
map with one queue entry and three names. Nothing below repeats a
different map: the coordinates are the same rows, verified again
against the source dump for this file.

Core status: identical to step 10, and just as fatal. This core
cannot create the arena at all:
  - BATTLEGROUND_HOOK_POINT = 897 is declared and the template

  - there is no zone file in src/server/game/Battlegrounds/Zones

=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

Sources disagree on how many pillars this map has.

Pillars and a wooden fence (Liquipedia, NamuWiki, Inven):

  "It is a squared off area with two pillars in the middle area.
   One side is covered by fences allowing for line-of-sight, with
   the middle and both ends open."
   (Liquipedia)

  "it has an asymmetrical shape, with pillars slanted and a tall
   and long wooden fence on one side."
   (NamuWiki arena page, from Korean)

  "There are two structures in the centre of the arena that act
   as pillars... The variable is the wooden wall located on one
   side of the arena."   (Inven preview, June 2018, from Korean)

Four plus two (one source, preview text):

  "It features four pillars for players to dance around - one
   with a shark hanging from it - and two smaller pillars."
   (Blizzard arena preview text, August 2018, quoted by xpoff)

Liquipedia and Inven say two pillars in the middle; NamuWiki
describes slanted pillars without giving a count. All three
describe a fence on one side. The Blizzard preview
claims four big pillars plus two small, which no later page
repeats. Pillar count is therefore conflicting between
sources; the majority reading is two LoS pillars in the
middle.

Pillar positions: unverified. Pillar size and slant: unverified.
Fence side: unverified beyond "one side". Nothing spawns here in
server data, and no readable source gives coordinates, so this
section is prose only. The starts being 25.06 and 21.88 degrees
off the line between them does back the asymmetry all sources
describe.

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Read with caution: with no zone file and no spawn data, the
following is reasoned from the published descriptions and the
start geometry, not from coordinates.

Melee DPS:
  Race for the middle pillars; two of the three map pages put
  the cover in the centre, and the fenced side is the only wall
  Approach along the diagonal: the starts face 25.06 and 21.88
  degrees off the line between them, so no straight run exists

Ranged DPS:
  The map is medium sized and open at both ends, so max-range
  casting across the middle works; do not stand in the open
  middle against a melee cleave
  Use the fenced side as a wall for LoS, the open ends for space

Healers:
  Play the edge of the fenced side, wall on one hand and a
  central pillar a short walk away
  Do not hold an open end: nothing to hide behind, and one
  enemy start sits near an end

General opener notes:
  Both teams are 56.86 from the midpoint, so first contact is
  close to simultaneous
  The start line is diagonal (86.35 by 73.99), so the approach
  is not along one clean axis
  Game On is 22.66 off the midpoint, consistent with an
  asymmetric layout rather than a mirrored one
