FIRELANDS (map 720)
1. BETH'TILAC: two levels: a team rides webs UP to her
  (tank/healer/dps); ground team: taunt Spinners down
  (their threads are the lifts), tank Drones facing away
  (99463 cone), kill Spiderlings BEFORE a Drone eats one
  (100634 Consume heals it 20% - page) or they reach her.
  She descends after her THIRD Smoldering Devastation
  (not at a health % - page): everyone down, burn race
  against stacking 99497 Frenzy; 99476 Widow's Kiss
  reduces healing on the tank and pulses on allies.
  (Exploding Engorged Broodlings are heroic-only.)
  AI RULE: count devastations, not her health - the
  third one brings her down; spiderlings are her food.
2. LORD RHYOLITH: he walks toward lava; steering him is
  the fight: hitting ONE leg turns him that way - DPS
  calls: left leg = turn left, right leg = right.
  Never let him drink lava (armor up/raid damage).
  Fragments/sparks (adds): offtank, fragments die fast,
  sparks kept 30+ yd from raid (they pulse). At 25%
  armor gone: tank and burn, stomp heal.
  AI RULE: legs are steering, not targets of opportunity
  - hit the CALLED leg only.
3. ALYSRAZOR: air team: 3 Molten Feathers grant 98619
  Wings of Flame (30 sec flight - page); her fire rings
  REFRESH the wings and stack 99461 Blazing Power
  (haste, to 25) - if wings expire mid-air you fall:
  rings are fuel, there is no fixed miss count.
  Ground: hatchlings imprint on the nearest player (that
  player tanks and gains +1000% damage vs it - page);
  drag them to Plump Lava Worms to keep them Satiated
  (99359) or they Tantrum (99362). Initiates: interrupt
  101223 Fieroblast (I; successes buff them). Burnout at
  0 energy: she crashes - BURN window; run out for her
  re-ignite blast, then the cycle restarts.
  AI RULE: rings are fuel, worms are sedatives; both
  run out on a timer nobody can extend.
4. SHANNOK: his dogs: Rageface (fixates a player - if it
  sticks, DPS dog or player dies; traps: crystal prison
  traps to catch dogs - drag them over traps), Rip
  (bleeds tank), Shannox throws his spear (dog fetches -
  kite time). Immolation traps on players (move).
  Kill order: Rageface first (era), then Rip, boss;
  killing dogs enrages him (stacking) - balanced burn.
  AI RULE: dogs are kited through traps, never tanked
  standing; traps are tools, not just hazards.
5. BALEROC: shard rotation (page): Shards of Torment
  (1 in 10, 2 in 25) - DPS soakers take Torment stacks
  (2 soak ~12 each or 3 soak ~8), then rotate out under
  Tormented (never re-soak until it falls). HEALERS gain
  Vital Spark by healing the Tormented soakers (1 per 3
  Torment stacks); a direct heal on the tank (Blaze of
  Glory target) converts it to 99263 Vital Flame (15
  sec boosted tank healing) - soakers never gain sparks.
  Two tanks: one builds Blaze of Glory health, one is
  the Decimation tank. 99353 Decimation Blade: strikes
  do 90% of max health (min 250000), unmitigable but
  dodgeable, slowed swing - heal him above 90% between
  swings. Countdown links are HEROIC-only: linked pair
  run together within 8 sec.
  AI RULE: shard soaks are a roster, the spark economy
  belongs to healers; decimate means top the tank.
6. MAJORDOMO STAGHELM: form follows the raid (page):
  7+ clustered (18+ in 25) = Scorpion 98379; spread =
  Cat. In each form he builds energy; at 100 he casts
  the special (Scorpion: 98474 Flame Scythe, frontal,
  split among everyone in front - soak stacked; Cat:
  Leaping Flames on a ranged player, leaves fire and a
  Spirit of Flame add - melee kill it at once). Each
  special gives Adrenaline (faster energy next time);
  swapping form resets Adrenaline but adds Fury (+8%
  special damage, permanent, stacking). Strategy is to
  ENDURE as many specials as healing allows (era starts
  5-7), THEN swap - swapping early does not dodge
  anything, it only stacks Fury faster. Every third
  shape-change: Searing Seeds (to Scorpion: run out
  before your seed timer pops) or Burning Orbs (to Cat:
  soak rotation - both normal mode, page).
  AI RULE: the raid's formation is his form; swaps are
  a healing budget, not a dodge - spend them late.
7. RAGNAROS (page ids, stage 1): P1: 98708 Sulfuras
  Smash (he faces a player, smash point kills within
  5 yd - page; lava waves 98928 roll out - sidestep),
  100115 Wrath of Ragnaros knockback on locations; magma
  traps on players (detonate carefully one at a time -
  each raises the next trap's damage; era).
  Intermissions (70%/40%): he submerges, Sons of Flame
  run to the hammer - kill/stun them BEFORE one reaches
  it (any arrival = raid blast), lava seeds (P2) on
  players: run them to the back together, meteors (P3)
  fixate players: kite, never through the group; living
  meteors knocked back by damage to delay. P2: seeds +
  elemental adds (molten elementals - AoE and slows);
  P3: meteors + burn race. Heroic P4: legs - he walks,
  dreadflame spread controlled by assigned soakers,
  roots/traps from NPCs (Cenarius etc.).
  AI RULE: intermission adds have a finish line - the
  hammer; a son arriving is everyone's failure.

DRAGON SOUL (map 967)
8. MORCHOK: Stomp splits damage among nearest players
  (stack close); crystal: 3 players soak its explosion
  (called soakers run to it); black blood phase: hide
  behind the rock spikes he leaves, never in the blood.
  Heroic: Kohlrohm twin mirrors him. AI RULE: blood =
  behind rocks; crystal = the called three go.
9. WARLORD ZON'OZZ: the void ball: it bounces between
  two called groups (stack points): each bounce = raid
  pulse; after called bounces (5-9 era) a player sends
  it INTO the boss (he takes +damage, tentacle phase:
  kill eyes/flails, interrupt). Failing to bounce =
  ball hits wall/boss early. AI RULE: the ball is a
  rally - be on your stack point before it arrives.
10. YOR'SAHJ: oozes run to him: raid kills ONE ooze
  (called colour), the others reach him and grant
  combos: purple = do NOT AoE heal (healing explodes
  stacks - single target only), green = spread, red =
  stack, yellow = fast phases, black = adds (kill),
  blue = mana void (kill void after drain, stand in
  return). AI RULE: one ooze dies by CALL; purple means
  small heals only - a big heal is a bomb.
11. HAGARA: ice phase: 4 crystals - kill while running
  the ice wall ring (stay ahead of the wall, gap at
  pillars); lightning phase: chain the lightning
  between players to the pillars (form lines); frozen
  orbs; ice tomb on players (kill tomb to free); watery
  entrenchment bubble safe spot in ice phase centre.
  AI RULE: phases are laps: keep moving ahead of the
  wall; lightning needs a chain, not a clump.
12. ULTRAXION: the button fight: Heroic Will (extra
  action button) - use it to leave reality before Hour
  of Twilight lands (except the 2-3 called soakers with
  cooldowns); Fading Light on tanks/current targets -
  also button (or swap); crystals from Aspects buff
  healers (assign colours by healer); Twilight
  Instability raid ticks ramp (heal race, 6-min enrage
  era). AI RULE: the button is your defensive cooldown -
  press it on Hour, press it on Fading, never otherwise.
13. WARMASTER BLACKHORN: ship fight: drakes drop
  twilight barrages (small circles: move; big Onslaught
  circle: STACK to split or ship dies); adds board:
  kill sappers first (they run to the engine - slow/
  kill before arrival); Goriona breath strafes; P2 he
  lands: shockwave cone, roar, vengeance stacks tank
  swap; ship health is a second boss bar.
  AI RULE: the ship can die before you do - soak the
  big circle, kill the sapper, in that order.
14. SPINE OF DEATHWING: ride his back: kill corruptions
  (tentacles) -> amalgamation adds: tank them, feed them
  9 blood residues (kill bloods ON the amalgamation,
  they leave residue the add absorbs walking through),
  at 9 stacks it pulses nuclear blast beside a plate:
  that blows the plate's armor loose; then burn the
  exposed tendon before it reseals (3 plates). Barrel
  roll: he rolls toward the side with more players -
  everyone STACKS in a corruption hole/middle on the
  roll call or is thrown off (death).
  AI RULE: bloods die on the add; roll call = stack
  instantly or fly off.
15. MADNESS OF DEATHWING: four platforms (aspects):
  fight a limb per platform: mutated corruption tentacle
  (tank, its impale on tank = cooldown), bolt: kill the
  elemental bolt before it lands (Nozdormu slows it if
  his platform lives), blistering tentacles (kill,
  Alexstrasza burns them), arm tentacle burn; order of
  platforms decides which aspect buffs remain (lose a
  platform's aspect help when you leave it - order is
  strategy; era standard saves Ysera last-ish,
  unverified). Cataclysm cast = platform must finish
  before it ends. P2 (20%): head down centre: fragments
  and terrors (tank/kill), shrapnel on players (Ysera
  dream button), burn with all aspect help gone except
  timed buffs. AI RULE: bolt and tentacle outrank the
  limb; the platform order is decided before the pull.
