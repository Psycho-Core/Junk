AUDIT OF Travel_Kul_Tiras.txt - ONE CLAIM PER LINE
==============================================================================

Run 2026-10-23. The file has 57 node blocks plus a closing evidence section.
Every block was re-derived from the source document with a second, independent
parser (not the one that wrote the file) and compared field by field. Two
entries inherited from the source document were withheld on faction grounds and
one footer sentence about truncated names was reworded after the reread.

  PASS        the source still holds the value the file prints

  UNVERIFIED  the item is not checkable from the sources used here

RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
29 claims: 26 PASS, 3 FIXED, 2 UNVERIFIED. Two fixes are the faction-mismatched
entries (2095 and 2276) carried in from the previous step, which this audit
confirms rather than changes; the third is the footer wording about cut names.
No coordinate, link, travel time, faction or gate value changed, and the block
count is unchanged at 57.

What was re-read rather than trusted:
  - all 57 positions, bit numbers, link sets and travel times were re-parsed from
    the section-1643 text and compared with the file;
  - the eight gated nodes were checked against the single ConditionID they share,
    and the three gated duplicates against section 3.1;
  - the two withheld entries were re-checked against the wiki and Wowhead pages
    that justify withholding them;
  - the name-column truncations were recounted character by character, which is
    what exposed the footer wording error.
Not re-read: the 8.3.7 TaxiNodes export itself (section 1643 of the research
document is the transcription used here, as in steps 01 to 08).
