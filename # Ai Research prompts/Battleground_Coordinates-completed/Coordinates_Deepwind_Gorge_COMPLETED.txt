=== DEEPWIND GORGE COORDINATES - BFA 8.3.7 (TRINITYCORE) - COMPLETED ===
Battleground_Coordinates - prompt 2 of 8 (Deepwind Gorge, maps 1105 + 2245).
Every coordinate is written as: map id, x, y, z. Values are reproduced exactly
from the named source; nothing is estimated. Where no readable source exists
the entry says "unverified".

=== 0. HOW TO READ / ORIENTATION ===
Facing/orientation values are reproduced exactly as stored in their source
(dump o column or script float); their unit is degrees where the source is a
world_safe_locs row (same convention as the Arathi Basin file) and radians for
script orientation floats.
North/south on map 1105 is PROVEN by the dump's own comments: for both teams
the cemetery labeled "(North)" has a smaller y than the one labeled "(South)"
(Alliance: 4546 y=195.135 vs 4488 y=242.712; Horde: 4489 y=758.708 vs 4545
y=802.188), and the wowhead MoP Classic guide's mine order "from north to
south: Pandaren, Central, and Goblin" matches y=431.1771 / 499.059 / 574.368.
So on map 1105: smaller y = north, larger y = south. Which way the x axis
points (east or west) could not be verified from any readable source; this
file therefore makes NO east/west claims about map 1105 - only north/south,
mine names and coordinates. For map 2245 the east/west words below are the
wowhead RBG guide's own map reading, quoted as such.
Route legs are straight-line distances between sourced landmarks. They are
NOT walkable-path measurements: no pathing/navmesh source for these maps could
be read (unverified).

own gate door -> objective; "direct" = straight-line start -> objective.
These are NOT walkable-path measurements; actual walkability is unverified.

  overview on warcraft.wiki): the quarry/gorge "filled with dislodged rock

    (1105, -222.743, 802.188, 137.451)  o 313.287
  Spirit guides are spawned at all four rows at setup; creature entries
  13116 (Alliance) and 13117 (Horde) (LegionCore Battleground.h
  BG_CREATURE_ENTRY_A/H_SPIRITGUIDE; wowhead MoP Classic also lists Horde
  Spirit Guide 13117 in Deepwind Gorge).
Deepwind graveyards do NOT belong to individual points in either readable
  rule - each team owns a north/south pair at its own base. Two conflicting
  rules were read; which one your server uses is unverified:
  a) Emulator rule (LegionCore GetClosestGraveYard): respawn at whichever
     of YOUR OWN two cemeteries is closer to the corpse. Applying it with
     the dump coordinates: deaths at Pandaren Mine -> Alliance 4546 /
     Horde 4489; at Central Mine -> Alliance 4488 (304 vs 309 yd) /
     Horde 4545 (308 vs 309 yd - the choice is marginal, 1-5 yd);
     at Goblin Mine -> Alliance 4488 / Horde 4545.
  b) Wiki rule (warcraft.wiki original page): "Dying on the northern half
     of the map results in re-spawning at your faction's southern graveyard
     (and vice versa)" - this gives the OPPOSITE answer for deaths at the
     northern (Pandaren) mine compared with rule (a).
  For reference: Alliance start to own cemeteries = 163 yd (North) / 88 yd
    (South); Horde start = 94 yd (North) / 165 yd (South); the North-South
    cemetery pair separation is 223 yd (Alliance) / 227 yd (Horde).
Map 2245 graveyards: unverified coordinates/entries. The wowhead RBG guide

=== 7. SCORE / RULES NOTES (VERIFIED) ===
Original ruleset: capture mines for gold + steal carts, cap 1600 in the
  MoP era (wowhead MoP Classic guide; MoP achievement 8333 "1600 to 0").
  The Legion-era emulator uses a 1500 cap, and the current wiki/8.3 text
  says 1,500 - the cap is 1500 for map 2245 (wiki patch 8.3.0 note and RBG
  guide agree).
Match sizes: 15v15 (original wiki page); RBG converts to 10v10 (guide).
No orbs, no bullet-carts or other objectives beyond the above (both
  readable rulesets).
