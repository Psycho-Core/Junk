MOGU'SHAN VAULTS (map 1008)
1. THE STONE GUARD (3-4 guardians by size): guardians
  only gain energy within 12 yd of another guardian; a
  lone one turns Solid Stone (90% less damage, NO energy
  - page). THE RULE: the guardian currently casting
  Petrification must be the one paired (so IT reaches
  100 energy first): its Overload damage is cut 90% by
  its own petrification aura. All other guardians are
  kept ISOLATED so they cannot gain energy - if a
  non-petrifying guardian overloads, the blast is
  unreduced. Tanks swap pairings every petrification.
  Jasper Chains pair: stay within 10 yd or the chains
  burn (they break after 15 sec apart - page); Cobalt
  Mines move out (3-sec arm, roots on trigger).
  AI RULE: make the petrifying guardian overload, keep
  every other guardian alone and energy-starved.
2. Feng the Accursed: boss steals a player ability set
  per phase (fists phases by statue): tank swap on his
  boss debuffs; use the stolen-ability counters the raid
  gains: Shroud of Reversal (copy his cast onto him),
  Nullification Barrier (stack in bubble for the epicentre
  quake), Wildfire Spark on a player (run out); draw
  flame phases spread; lightning fists interrupt chain.
  AI RULE: the bubble and the shroud are raid buttons -
  stack for quake, shroud his big cast back at him.
3. GARA'JAL THE SPIRITBINDER: tank banished to spirit
  realm on swap (the banished tank + a healer + dps
  cross via totems: kill the spirit adds inside, heal
  the severed souls, exit before timer); voodoo dolls:
  damage copies to linked players - heal all linked;
  totems spawn continuously - a fresh crosser rotation.
  AI RULE: crossing is a rotation with a clock - kill
  inside, get out before the realm kills you.
4. THE SPIRIT KINGS (4 in sequence): Qiang (tank swap,
  massive attacks frontal), Subetai (volley - spread,
  pillage (unverified); rain of arrows circles), Zian (shadow
  shields - stop DPS on shielded, kill undying shadows
  and pin them away - they fixate and explode),
  Meng (cowardice reflect - STOP dps when he reflects;
  maddening shout = MC players, break by damage).
  AI RULE: Meng reflecting means hands off - reflected
  damage has killed more raids than the bosses.
5. ELEGON: Stage 1: tank him centre; Celestial
  Protector adds are dragged OFF the platform edge
  (they explode at death - 129711 Total Annihilation;
  page: kill them over the edge or soak it called).
  Stage 2 (85%): he constructs 6 Energy Charges that
  run for the Empyreal Focuses; killing a whole wave
  forces another Draw Power wave (+10% damage taken on
  him, stacking, charges 20% faster each wave - page);
  the stage ends when one charge escapes - so one wave
  is nearly always let through late. Stage 3: destroy
  all six Focuses (stand out of the conduit beams);
  stages then repeat, and each Focus death also buffs
  his output (116989). At 50% Radiating Energies makes
  inside/outside the vortex different damage - position
  by call. AI RULE: charges are a wave budget - kill
  waves for his debuff, never let wave one through.
6. WILL OF THE EMPEROR: two bosses (Qin-xi/Jan-xi) +
  add waves: rage adds (fixate players - kite/kill),
  courage adds (they block their path to bosses -
  they shield the bosses), strength adds (smash circles
  dodge). Boss combo: devastating combo = 5 strikes:
  dodge the arcs (watch his facing pattern) to gain
  Opportunistic Strike (big free hit). Gas phases: adds
  priority rises. Tank swap on boss debuff.
  AI RULE: dance the combo - five dodges buy the
  biggest hit in the fight.

HEART OF FEAR (map 1009)
7. IMPERIAL VIZIER ZOR'LOK: platforms rotate: Force and
  Verve (stack in the shield bubbles on his platform),
  Attenuation: dodge sonic rings spiralling out (run
  the spiral gaps), Convert = MC player broken by
  damage; final phase all abilities + echoes of him
  copying casts (heroic). Tank swap on exhale stacks.
  AI RULE: rings are a dance floor, bubbles are the
  only stillness allowed.
8. BLADE LORD TA'YAK: tank swap on Overwhelming Assault
  stacks; Unseen Strike: marked player - everyone STACKS
  behind them to split it; wind step teleport bleeds;
  at 20%: tornado gauntlet - run the corridor dodging
  tornadoes to reach him, then burn both sides.
  AI RULE: unseen strike is soaked as a group standing
  still, never run from the mark.
9. GARALON: leg fight: kill legs standing IN the leg's
  blue circle (Weak Points damage buff - page strategy);
  each broken leg slows him, legs mend over time. Two
  tanks stay in front soaking Furious Swipe - if nobody
  is in front he gains Fury (page). The Pheromones
  carrier kites him around the room's edge leaving a
  trail; swap carrier at 20 stacks (4+ kiters cycle,
  stacks last 2 min - page). CRUSH happens on every
  pheromone SWAP (raid topped before each) and if anyone
  stands in the purple circle under his body - never
  under him; it is not triggered by leg deaths.
  AI RULE: the carrier draws a circle the raid never
  crosses; swaps are announced and pre-healed.
10. WIND LORD MEL'JARAK: adds (3x3 classes): CC a set
  with the amber prisons (click spears - imprison one
  of each type set), kill one TYPE at a time (killing
  buffs the others); wind bombs on players (run out),
  rain of blades heal phase; quickening dispel (D-Magic
  on adds - steal/dispel or they out-heal).
  AI RULE: one type dies at a time; prisons hold the
  rest - break a prison early and you fight everything.
11. AMBER-SHAPER UN'SOK: amber monstrosity tank swap;
  players transformed (vehicle): mutated construct
  players: interrupt his explosion cast (button), stack
  amber pools... reshape life on tank (they become the
  construct - keep boss+construct apart); amber scalpel
  beam phases dodge; burn monstrosity in P2 while
  constructs manage pools; P3 burn boss, transformed
  players drink pools to stay alive (heroic details
  unverified).
  AI RULE: if you are the construct, your interrupt
  button is the raid's life.
12. GRAND EMPRESS SHEK'ZEER: dissonance fields: two
  fields cry - players stand in fields to stop the
  cries (standing in one drains it; never let both
  fields cry unanswered or detonate together - era);
  adds: reavers/earthshakers
  (tank apart, interrupt); eyes of the empress: gaze
  stacks on tanks swap; P3 fear/visions: sha energy -
  spread, hearts of fear on players (run out, they
  persist as zones).
  AI RULE: fields are silenced by bodies, staggered -
  never empty, never both at once.

TERRACE OF ENDLESS SPRING (map 996)
13. PROTECTORS OF THE ENDLESS (3): kill order decides
  powers: standard Kaolan -> Regail -> Asani (each death
  empowers rest): lightning prison on players (free by
  killing prison? dispel - unverified), corrupted waters
  orb (kill orb before it heals - Asani), defiled ground;
  Asani last = fastest bolts. AI RULE: order is the
  strategy; the water orb is a heal, kill it.
14. TSULONG: night/day: night - DPS boss, shadow breath,
  sunbeam safe circle shrinks (stand in beam to clear
  debuff stacks); day - HEAL him: healers burn healing
  into him (golden breath buff from his breath - healers
  stand in front), adds (large sha tanked, small sha
  run to him - kill/slow or they drain him), bathe in
  light spots. Race: his corruption (night damage) vs
  raid healing (day).
  AI RULE: by day you heal the boss; small sha reaching
  him undo your healing - they outrank everything.
15. LEI SHI: tank swap-less: she hides (AoE to find her),
  Get Away: she pushes the raid back - keep moving and
  damaging her to end it (details unverified here);
  protectors adds phases
  (kill one, CC rest); spray stacks swap tanks.
  AI RULE: when she hides, splash damage finds her.
16. SHA OF FEAR: the tank is teleported to an outside
  platform (kill the guardian archer there; the
  survivor returns as champion of the light - stand in
  the light or be feared); breath of fear: stand behind
  the light wall; adds (terrors) run to the light wall -
  kill before arrival; heroic P2: submerge adds and
  huddle movement (unverified).
  AI RULE: light is the only safe ground - fight from
  it, kill what runs at it.
