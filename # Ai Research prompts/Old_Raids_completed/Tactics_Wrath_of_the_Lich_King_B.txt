3. FACTION CHAMPIONS
No tanking (aggro resets ~9s); PvP rules,
DR after 3 CC, champions entry unverified.
Kill healers first: Resto Druid (Rejuv/
Lifebloom/Nourish/Regrowth - dispel HoTs,
mostly instant), then Resto Shaman (Healing
Wave/Lesser/Chain Heal I, Earth Shield D,
Hex CC), Holy Paladin (Flash/Holy Light I,
Divine Shield at ~25% - Shattering Throw/
Mass Dispel; Hand of Protection - Purge),
Disc Priest (Greater/Flash Heal I, Renew/
PW:Shield D). Then Rogue (Shadowstep burst,
Fan of Knives) > Warrior (Bladestorm) > Ret
> DK (grip) > Warlock (interrupt Hellfire)
> Enh Shaman (kill totems) > Hunter/Mage/
SPriest/Boomkin. PvP trinkets; pets draw
heals - pets attack enemy pets, do not kill.

Power of the Twins: other twin +20% damage
+attack-speed stacks while Pact casts.
Twin Spike: +20% damage taken 15s/10 hits
- tanks swap twins. Spell ids unverified.
