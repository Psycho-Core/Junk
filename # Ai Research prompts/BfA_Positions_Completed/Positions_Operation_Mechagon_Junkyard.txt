=== 2. HEALTH PERCENTAGES / PHASE TRIGGERS ===

KING GOBBAMAK (DATA 1):
No health-based phases. Energy-based mechanic: gains energy via
PERIODIC_ENERGY_GAIN (295065).
  At 100 Energy -> Charged Smash (297254), energy resets to 0.
  No scripted phase transitions.

GUNKER (DATA 2):
  No health-based phases. Stationary (rooted), energy-based.
  Gains energy via PERIODIC_ENERGY_GAIN (295065).
  At 100 Energy -> Toxic Wave (297834), energy resets to 0.
Becomes attackable after King Gobbamak is defeated (MoveInLineOfSight check).

TRIXIE & NAENO (DATA 3):
  Two-boss encounter sharing Mechacycle vehicle (NPC 153756).
  Phase 1 (Normal): Trixie active, Naeno in vehicle.
    Naeno enters vehicle at start -> PHASE_VEHICLE (60s).
    Trixie casts abilities independently.
  Phase 2 (Vehicle): Naeno pilots Mechacycle.
    Pedal to the Metal (298651) every 20s, Burnout (298571) every 25s.
    Exit Vehicle at 60s -> returns to Phase 1.
Phase 3 (Super Boost): When Naeno defeated, Trixie gains Super Boost (303525,
100% haste).
Phase 4 (Turbo Boost): When Trixie defeated, Naeno gains Turbo Boost (299241).
No health % phase triggers in script - phases time-based (60s vehicle cycles).

=== 4. ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS & EXACT TRIGGERS ===

KING GOBBAMAK:
  - Electrical Charge (297257): Random player, spread 8yd.
  - Rumble (297261): Boss stops moving, casts AoE. Move away (>10yd).
- Charged Smash (297254): At 100 Energy, on current victim. Tank swap not
required.
- GetEm (summon adds): Every 25s, summons 4 Scrapbone Grunters (158871) at
cave_pos (1188,-75,21.84).
Assigned: DPS kill adds quickly. Grunters cast Thrash (297382) and Zap
(297543).

GUNKER:
  - Sludge Bolt (298212): On top aggro if >5yd. Stay in melee or be targeted.
  - Splatter (297985): AoE goo. Dodge / move out.
  - Gooped (298124): Incapacitates players in goo. Assigned: dispel/avoid.
  - Toxic Wave (297834): At 100 Energy, frontal cone. Move behind boss.
  - Toxic Flames (298228): Ground effect. Move out.
- Squirt Bots (154746): 3 stationary bots cast Sanitizing Spray (297901) -
cleanses goo.
    Assigned: Stand in spray to remove Gooped.

TRIXIE & NAENO:
  - Taze (298669): Random player. Spread.
- Mega Taze (302682/302687/298718): Random target, 8s channel -> explosion.
Assigned: run out (>10yd) before detonation.
  - Electric Slide (298849): Trixie jumps to stalker. Dodge landing spot.
  - Vehicle phase (Naeno pilots Mechacycle 60s):
Pedal to the Metal (298651): Mechacycle charges farthest player. Dodge line.
    Burnout (298571): Mechacycle leaves fire trail. Avoid.
Smoke Cloud (298573): Random positions, apply Smoke Cloud aura (298602).
Avoid.
  - Foot phase (Naeno exits vehicle):
    Bolt Buster (298940): On tank. Tank swap at 2 stacks?
    Roadkill (298946): Charges farthest player. Assigned: kite.
  - Super Boost (303525): When Naeno dies, Trixie 100% haste. Burn phase.
- Turbo Boost (299241): When Trixie dies, Naeno melee adds Nature damage. Heal
through.
