BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - JEWELCRAFTING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
=============================================================================================

Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client) levels Jewelcrafting on a
TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Every id below was read from a source this
week; anything without a readable source says "unverified" instead of a guess.

1. THE SKILL LINES AND HOW A COMPANION LEARNS THEM
---------------------------------------------------

Learn Skill (Jewelcrafting, skill=755, value 1); wowhead infobox "Training
cost: 10"; Difficulty 111; "Taught by (33)" row does not render on the page;
"Other ranks (9)": 158750 (requires level 1), 195116 (requires level 1),
25230 (50), 28894 (125), 28895 (200), 28897 (275), 51311 (350), 73318

"Added in patch 8.1.0" except wrapper 264533, whose quick facts read 8.0.1
(section 9 item 12). Mains print "Requires Jewelcrafting (1)" and "Training
cost: 10" except Classic 264532 and the Draenor/Legion mains 264544/264546,
whose pages print neither line:

the guide's stale link).
Prospecting: spell 31252, 2-second cast, Effect "Prospect" value 5, GCD 1.5
seconds, flag "Target must be own item", text "Search 5 ore of a base metal
for precious gems. This will destroy the ore in the process." Learned
automatically on learning Jewelcrafting (vanilla guide links 31252
directly). Since patch 2.1.0 prospecting always yields at least 1 gem;
powders dropped except Adamantite Powder in 3.0.2; Titanium Ore added in
3.2.0 (warcraft.wiki Prospecting patch rows). Per-expansion prospecting
spells (Outland Prospecting 382980, Kul Tiras and Zandalar Prospecting
382973, etc.) were all added in patch 10.0.0 ("Each expansion proficiency
now has its own spell for Prospecting") - after 8.3.7 (section 9 item 7).
Mass prospecting: consumes 20 ore per attempt, queueable, "does not appear
to reduce the gem yield per ore" (warcraft.wiki Prospecting). The guide's
route spell is Mass Prospect Osmenite 300619 (section 9 item 15).

7. WHAT THE CORE MUST DO FOR A CRAFT TO SUCCEED
----------------------------------------------
Verified against TrinityCore master fetched this week (blobless sparse
clone, HEAD 209d6d51d1; paths under src/server/game/). Line numbers are
from this fetch; upstream moves - section 9 item 18 records the comparison
with the earlier files.
a) Learn press: NPCHandler.cpp 97 HandleTrainerListOpcode -> 112
   SendTrainerList (entry guards at 102-109, missing trainer data at 121)
   -> 127 trainer->SendSpells; 130 HandleTrainerBuySpellOpcode -> 155
   trainer->TeachSpell. Trainer.cpp 41 SendSpells (list money with
   reputation discount at 43/65), 76 TeachSpell -> 98-99
   reputationDiscount x MoneyCost -> HasEnoughMoney / NotEnoughMoney at
   102 -> ModifyMoney(-moneyCost) -> IsCastable ? CastSpell : LearnSpell;
   149 CanTeachSpell, 172 GetSpellState, 212 SendTeachFailure. This is how
   25229, the eight expansion mains (264532, 264534, 264537, 264539,
   264542, 264544, 264546, 264548/265811) and their wrappers enter the
   character. Profession bars themselves are applied by SPELL_EFFECT_SKILL
   (effect 118, SpellEffects.cpp 210 dispatch) -> EffectSkill at
   SpellEffects.cpp 4668: reads skillStep from the effect value, skillid from
   MiscValue, checks GetSkillStep, SkillRaceClassInfo + SkillTiers (4689),
   and SetSkill(skillid, skillStep, skillval, maxSkillVal) at 4699 - this
   is the server path behind both the mains' "Learn Skill (skill=NNNN)"

   Spell" lands in EffectLearnSpell SpellEffects.cpp 2129 (TriggerSpell

   264545; the Legion wrapper 264547 arrives through the quest-reward
   learn path (quest 40523).

   Spell.cpp 7805-7835 case SPELL_EFFECT_PROSPECTING: target item exists,
   ITEM_FLAG_IS_PROSPECTABLE (7811), item owned by caster/no trade slot
   (7814-7815), item RequiredSkillRank <= GetSkillValue(SKILL_JEWELCRAFTING)
   else SPELL_FAILED_LOW_CASTLEVEL (7817-7819), stack >= 5 else
   NEED_MORE_ITEMS (7823-7828), LootTemplates_Prospecting.HaveLootFor else
   CANT_BE_PROSPECTED (7831-7832). EffectProspecting SpellEffects.cpp
   4614: re-checks flag and count >= 5; if SkillChance.Prospecting is
   enabled it calls UpdateGatherSkill(SKILL_JEWELCRAFTING, ...) at 4633;
   then LOOT_PROSPECTING (Loot.h 115, type 23) FillLoot from
   LootTemplates_Prospecting (LootMgr.cpp 55, load 1308, refs check 1450)
   and SendLoot. LootHandler.cpp 347-348 destroys only 5 items from the
   stack "in case prospecting and milling".
c) Crafting a product (the common case): recipes are Effect Create Item
   (sampled pages print Effect: Create Item on the routed cuts, e.g. the
   BfA and Legion gem cuts; the exact effect rows were not re-probed for
   every spell - the TC path below is the one that matters). TC path:
   Spell::cast takes power at Spell.cpp 3869 TakePower() and reagents at
   3872 TakeReagents() (definition 5600; skips reagents for
   ITEM_FLAG_NO_REAGENT_COST items); EffectCreateItem SpellEffects.cpp
   1473 / EffectCreateItem2 1482 with SPELL_ATTR0_IS_TRADESKILL selecting
   ItemContext::Trade_Skill (1478/1492/1522/2484); DoCreateItem

   1469 (`if (!m_CastItem) player->UpdateCraftSkill(m_spellInfo);`), with
   the same call at 1499 in EffectCreateItem2 and at 2492/3533 in other
   item-granting effects; item-cast crafts instead grant skill through
   Spell.cpp 3863-3865 (SPELL_ATTR9_ITEM_CAST_GRANTS_SKILL_GAIN).

   SPELL_ATTR1_NO_SKILL_INCREASE (5412), walks SkillLineAbility rows for
   the spell (SkillupSkillLineID at 5421-5422), multiplies NumSkillUps by
   SkillGain.Crafting (5433, default 1 at World.cpp 790), rolls
   SkillGainChance against TrivialSkillLineRankHigh/Low (5435).
   Roll odds: SkillChance.Orange 100 / Yellow 75 / Green 25 / Grey 0
   (World.cpp 784-787). SkillLineAbility rows are database content - not
   readable from the source clone (unverified per recipe).

8. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------
1) Click profession-window buttons: Prospecting/Mass Prospect (and every
   recipe) is a cast the client sends with an item target
   (Spell.cpp CheckCast requires m_targets.GetItemTarget() - a server-side
   actor has no UI to pick the ore stack; wowhead's own-item flag matches).
2) Talk to trainers or vendors: every "ask a guard", trainer-list and
   buy-spell interaction is a client opcode (NPCHandler.cpp 97 and 130
   chains, section 7a); a no-session character cannot open the list, click
   a design, or pay a vendor (all section 5 purchases).
3) Turn in quests: the Legion chain (40523 -> 40562 and the 24-quest
   profession storyline), the WoD scroll chain (34019/33410), the 4
   Cataclysm daily-quest days, Uniting the Isles 43341 and the Nazjatar
   intro all need client quest opcodes.
4) Socket gems: shift-right-click socketing UI (warcraft.wiki Socketing:
   select socket, "Socket Gems") is pure client interaction.
5) Choose a race at creation for Gemcutting +5 or Jack of All Trades +2 -
   the server can only apply a racial it was created with.
6) Click the Shattrath Jewelcrafting bookcase (Outland learn object
   interaction, section 2 guide note) or a garrison Gem Boutique vendor
   prompt (WoD scroll source 2).
7) Buy from the Auction House - every shopping list in section 3 assumes
   one.
8) Roll World Quests or see the guide's "wait even a few days" rank-3
   rotation (Legion) on a timer it cannot observe without a session.
