Firelands
==========

1. Beth'tilac
P1: boss on upper web. Ground team taunts
Cinderweb Spinners down (their threads let
players climb); top team (tank+healer+melee)
damages boss. Venom Rain on ground (id unv).
Smoldering Devastation x3 (cast when her
energy fills - everyone below leaves web top
or dies; id unverified), then P2 descent.
Ground adds: Cinderweb Drone - face away
(Boiling Splatter 99463 cone 63750), Burning
Acid 99934, Consume 100634 eats Spiderlings
(heals 20% - kill spiderlings first, snare).
Cinderweb Spiderling: Seeping Venom 97079 if
no drone up. Spinner heroic Fiery Web Spin
stun 25s (interrupt - id in chunk cut, unv).
Heroic Engorged Broodling: Volatile Burst
99990 fixate - kite/kill at range.
P2: Frenzy 99497 (+5% damage stacking, soft
enrage); Widow's Kiss 99476 on tank (-heal
growing + fire splash to allies 10 yd - tank
isolates / swap); Ember Flare 98934 on her
level only; Consume 99332 heals her 10% per
spiderling - no spiderlings may live at P2.
ADD ORDER: Spiderlings > Drone > Spinners >
boss (P2: boss only).

Fire taken x20). Crater 97234 lava streams

99845 (8000/s aura), Concussive Stomp 97282

damage grows the longer P1 lasted.
ADD ORDER: Fragments > Spark > feet/boss.

4. Shannox (+ Riplimb, Rageface)

>60: separation buff unverified). Both apply
stacking bleeds (Jagged Tear 99937 via Limb
Rip 99832) that must expire: Hurl Spear -

at Shannox's side/back.
Traps: Immolation (fire DoT) for dogs,
Crystal Prison for players/dogs - free
trapped players by killing the prism.
Rageface: Face Rage 100129 mauls a player
(8000/0.5s rising) - one 30000+ hit breaks

At Shannox 30% both dogs Frenzied Devotion
100064 (+400%) - kill order Rageface first,
Riplimb with Shannox low together; heroic
Riplimb revives after 30s at 0 HP; if Rip-
limb dies first, Shannox Magma Rupture.
ADD ORDER: Rageface > Riplimb > Shannox.

5. Baleroc
Single phase; enrage timer unverified.
Blaze of Glory on current tank (stacking
+HP, boss gains Incendiary Soul - ids unv):
two roles: Blaze tank (grows huge HP) and
Decimation tank (~250k HP). Blades 15s:
Inferno Blade 99351 (100000 Fire per hit) on
Blaze tank; Decimation Blade 99353 (90% max
HP Shadow, min 250000, unmitigable but
dodgeable, slower swings) on Decimation
tank - swap by blade, first blade Inferno.
Shards of Torment (1 in 10 / 2 in 25) spawn
near closest non-tank: DPS rotate soaking
Torment stacks (rising Shadow damage; ~12
each x2 or 8 each x3), then Tormented 40s
(+Shadow taken, healing halved if tank/
healer ever gets it - NEVER tanks/healers).
Empty shard = Wave of Torment raidwide.
Healers: direct heals on Torment targets
build Vital Spark -> Vital Flame 99263 when
healing Blaze target (+5%/stack 15s).
Heroic Countdown: linked pair meet in 8s.
AI RULE: shard is always soaked by a fresh
DPS; Decimation tank topped in 5 sec gaps.

8. Morchok (heroic: + Kohcrom at 90%)
TANK: Crush Armor stacking (id unverified) -
swap on first application then as it falls.
Stomp: damage split, two closest take double
- tanks always the two closest, raid hug.
Resonating Crystal 110041: explodes 12s,
damage split by 3 (7 in 25) closest, worse
with distance - assigned soakers stand on it
(link turns blue). Pattern: Stomp-Crystal-
Stomp-Crystal-Black Blood.
Black Blood phase: drawn in (5% HP/s 5s),
Earth's Vengeance spikes erupt - hide BEHIND
spikes, out of Black Blood (5000/s +100%
Nature taken stacking); wait for his next
Stomp before running back in.
Furious at 20% (+30% speed, +20% damage) -
cooldowns. Heroic: split raid, one team per
giant, crystals belong to Morchok team.
AI RULE: tanks closest, crystal soakers on
the mark, nobody in black blood.
