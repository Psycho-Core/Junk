TWINK29 - HUNTER - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29). ALT = needs a higher-level crafter or content.

1. WHAT A HUNTER RUNS ON AT 29 - THREE THINGS TO KNOW FIRST
   No mana. Hunters spend focus in BfA, so every mana potion, mana oil
   and Spirit stat on this page is dead weight. That removes half the
   caster consumable list at a stroke.
   No ammo. Hunters do not carry arrows or bullets in BfA, and a quiver or
   ammo pouch is cosmetic. Nothing in the gear list needs a supply line.
   Mail, not leather. The items file follows that rule; the enchant list
   below does not care, because an enchant goes on whatever item is worn.

2. ENCHANTS - THE CLASSIC SLOTS, ONE PER PIECE
   Skill numbers are the enchanter's, all 225 or below, so no higher-level
   crafter is needed. The wearer needs no profession.
   CLOAK    Enchant Cloak - Lesser Agility, 13882, +3 Agility. Skill 225.
            Formula 11206, drops. 2x Lesser Nether Essence 11174, Runed
            Truesilver Rod 11145. The best single enchant for a hunter.
   CLOAK    Enchant Cloak - Greater Defense, 13746, +50 armor. Skill 205.
            3x Vision Dust 11137. The alternative if the bot is being
            trained by melee; +3 Agility is better in most fights.
   CHEST    Enchant Chest - Greater Health, 13640, +35 health. Skill 160.
            3x Soul Dust 11083, Runed Golden Rod 11130. The only chest
            enchant inside the cap.
   BRACERS  Nothing worth taking. The one verified in-cap bracer enchant
            is 13661 Enchant Bracer - Strength, +5 Strength, skill 180,
            and Strength gives a hunter nothing - it does not convert to
            attack power. A minor agility bracer enchant exists in the
            classic line, but its id and skill were not verified, so the
            slot is either that or empty. Do not buy 13661.
   GLOVES   Enchant Gloves - Agility, 13815, +5 Agility. Skill 210.
            1x Lesser Nether Essence 11174 + 1x Vision Dust 11137.
   BOOTS    Enchant Boots - Minor Speed, 13890, "a slight movement speed
            increase" - the tooltip's own words, the percentage is
            unverified. Skill 225. 1x Small Radiant Shard 11177,
            1x Aquamarine 7909, 1x Lesser Nether Essence 11174. For a
            ranged class, kiting speed beats any stat here.
   WEAPON   Enchant Weapon - Striking, 13693, +3 weapon damage. Skill 195.
            2x Greater Mystic Essence 11135 + 1x Large Glowing Shard
            11139. Survival only. A bow or gun cannot take a classic
            weapon enchant - unverified, but no classic enchant is written
            for ranged weapons.
   HEAD     Nothing inside the 225 cap. Treat the slot as empty.
   SHOULDERS  Nothing wearable. The Mists inscriptions 83006, 87580,
            44133 and 50335 need level 80 or 85 on the character who
            applies them, so an heirloom shoulder carries no inscription.

3. NECK AND RING - BOTH ALT, BOTH HIGHER-LEVEL LEGION RECIPES
   Each carries an item level cap and a level 29 item at item level 33 or
   less passes it.
   NECK   190894 Mark of the Hidden Satyr, enchant id 5439. Sometimes
          summons a satyr that fires a nightmare bolt. Cap: "cannot be
          applied to items above level 120". 15x Chaos Crystal 124442,
          12x Leylight Shard 124441, Runed Enchanting Rod. The damage
          pick for Beast Mastery and Marksmanship.
         190892 Mark of the Claw. Sometimes +critical strike and +haste
          for 6 sec. The pick for a Survival hunter standing in melee, and
          the safer pick in general because it cannot be wasted on an
          overkill.
         228405 Mark of the Trained Soldier. Mastery, a stat a level 29
          does not have. Do not buy it.
   RING   190871 Binding of Haste rank 1, 191014 rank 3, item form
          128542. Flat Haste on a ring, capped at "items above level 60".
          Two rings, two enchants - the largest stat gain on this page.
          The Critical Strike and Agility variants of the same family are
          unverified by id; the Agility one is the right choice if it can
          be found.

4. ARMOR KITS - AND THE SLOT THEY COMPETE WITH
   4265 Heavy Armor Kit, +24 armor, item level 15 and up, no character
   level gate. Crafted at Leatherworking 150, spell 3780, 5x Heavy
   Leather 4234. Skill 8173 Thick (+32, level 30 required) and 15564
   Rugged (+40, level 40) are ALT.
   Caveat that matters: a kit and an enchant occupy the same enhancement
   slot on the same item, so one piece takes one or the other. The clean
   split for a hunter is kits on the LEGS, where there is no enchant at
   all, and enchants everywhere else. Whether this server really enforces
   the conflict is unverified; test it on one piece before buying four.

5. CONSUMABLES A 29 HUNTER CAN USE
   POTIONS - one shared cooldown, so one per fight.
     2459  Swiftness Potion        req 5   +50% speed 15 sec, 2 min cd
     5634  Free Action Potion      req 20  stun and root immunity 30 sec
     1710  Greater Healing Potion  req 21  455-585 health
     Nothing else in the potion list: a hunter does not drink mana.
   ELIXIRS - one hour, lost on death, one battle and one guardian elixir.
     8949  Elixir of Agility          req 27  +15 Agility
     8951  Elixir of Greater Defense  req 29  +250 armor
     3825  Elixir of Fortitude        req 25  +120 health
     3389  Elixir of Defense          req 16
   PROTECTION POTIONS - one hour, one school each.
     6051 Holy req 10, 6048 Shadow req 17, 6049 Fire req 23,
     6050 Frost req 28, 6052 Nature req 28.
   SCROLLS - 30 minutes, usable on another character.
     1477 Scroll of Agility II, req 15, +5 Agility. The hunter scroll.
     2289 Scroll of Strength II is the other one sold at that tier and a
     hunter has no use for it.
   WEAPON ENHANCEMENTS
     20744 Minor Wizard Oil is spell power - irrelevant here.
     Survival's two-hander takes a stone instead: 7964 Solid Sharpening
     Stone, req 25, on the axe, or 7965 Solid Weightstone, req 25, on the
     mace. 2862, 2863 and 2871 are the cheaper tiers. A stone is consumed
     and lasts 30 minutes; the +6 damage figure is the one to check in
     game.
     Whether a scope can still be fitted to a bow or gun at 29 is
     unverified.
   FOOD - well fed, 15 minutes, ten seconds seated.
     12210 Roast Raptor, req 25, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit. The Spirit half
     is wasted on a hunter, so this is +8 Stamina and nothing else.
     12209 Lean Wolf Steak req 15 and 5479 Crispy Lizard Tail req 12 are
     the same shape, smaller.
     No agility food exists at 29 - every primary-stat food starts at 35.
   BANDAGES - no profession and no level to use, 60 second cooldown,
   usable in arenas. 8544 Mageweave Bandage heals 1600 over 8 sec and is
   the best a 29 can buy; 6451 Heavy Silk Bandage heals 1280.
   PETS need no food in BfA - pet happiness was removed years before this
   build - so a Beast Mastery bot carries no pet consumable at all.

6. THE TWO PROFESSIONS
   Engineering first, for the grenades, a debuff, an escape and one item
   that roots a target. Everything in this first list a 29 can make and
   use himself.
     4390  Iron Grenade, skill 175, spell 3962. 132-218 Fire and a 3 sec
           stun in 3 yards, 1 minute cooldown.
     4394  Big Iron Bomb, skill 190, spell 3967. 149-201 Fire and a 3 sec
           stun in 5 yards.
     4388  Discombobulator Ray, skill 160, spell 3959. Turns the target
           into a leper gnome for 12 sec: -40 melee and spell damage, -20%
           movement, and it forces a dismount. The strongest debuff a 29
           can apply with an item.
     10518 Parachute Cloak, skill 225, spell 12616. Back slot, +8 Agility,
           10 sec of slow fall on a 30 minute cooldown. Takes a cloak
           enchant as well, so it is a real upgrade to the slot.
     4397  Gnomish Cloaking Device, skill 200, spell 3971. 10 sec of
           invisibility on a 1 hour cooldown, and it needs no
           specialisation to craft or to use. The reset button for a bot
           that has been caught out of position.
     10507 Solid Dynamite, skill 175, spell 12586. 213-287 Fire in 5
           yards, no stun, shared cooldown with the grenades.
     4381  Minor Recombobulator, skill 140, 10 charges. Dispels a
           polymorph on a friendly target and heals 150-250.
     4385  Green Tinted Goggles are cloth head, +8 Stamina and +7 Spirit.
           On a mail build that trades armor for Spirit a hunter cannot
           use, so goggles are the engineering item to skip.
   Bought, not made - a 29 cannot craft these because both are
   specialisation recipes, and specialisation needs level 30 and 200 skill.
   The items still work for a 29: they carry no profession requirement to
   wear, so a higher-level engineer makes them and mails them over. ALT.
     10720 Gnomish Net-o-Matic Projector, spell 12902, Gnomish
           Engineering, skill 210. Nets the target in place - a root on a
           class whose whole job is keeping distance - on a 10 minute
           cooldown, and it can backfire and net the user instead.
           1x Mithril Tube 10559, 2x Shadow Silk 10285, 4x Thick Spider's
           Silk 4337, 2x Solid Blasting Powder 10505, 4x Mithril Bar 3860.
     10716 Gnomish Shrink Ray, spell 12899, Gnomish Engineering, skill
           205. -250 attack power to the target for a window on a 5 minute
           cooldown.
     10726 Gnomish Mind Control Cap, skill 215, and 10721 Gnomish Harm
           Prevention Belt, skill 215 - both specialised, both ALT.
     109076 Goblin Glider Kit is auction house only: the recipes behind it
           are Draenor and Legion engineering, which a 29 cannot learn.
   Second slot:
     Leatherworking - the Heavy Armor Kits 4265 above and mail pieces to
       fill the shoulder, wrist and leg slots the item pool leaves empty.
       The best pairing for a hunter, because the armor is the weak half
       of the gear.
     Alchemy - self-supply of 2459 and 5634 for a whole bot roster.
     Skinning - feeds the Leatherworking; supplier characters only.
     Not worth it: Enchanting has no self-only perk at classic tier,
     Jewelcrafting has nothing to socket at 29, Inscription has nothing
     wearable inside the cap, Blacksmithing is stones only, and Herbalism
     and Mining are gathering.

7. RACES THAT CAN BE A HUNTER
   The wiki's hunter-race page lists, for the two factions: human, dwarf,
   gnome, night elf, draenei, worgen, void elf and lightforged draenei on
   the Alliance; orc, jungle troll, blood elf, tauren, goblin, forsaken,
   zandalari troll and nightborne on the Horde; pandaren in a separate
   "other races" section.
   Its lore text also records hunters among the dark iron dwarves, the
   kul tirans and the mechagnomes, and gnome hunters were opened up in
   Legion.
   Highmountain tauren, mag'har orc and vulpera can be hunters in game but
   are not on that page, so treat their hunter status here as unverified.
   Every allied race starts at level 20, which makes all of them legal 29
   twinks.
   ORC - the Beast Mastery pick, and the best Horde hunter race.
     21563 Command, passive: pet damage increased. With Animal Companion
           from the level 15 row, that is the largest free damage a 29 can
           buy.
     20572 Blood Fury, the attack power version; 33697 and 33702 are the
           spell power and other variants. Ids verified, the 8.3.7 one to
           read in the spellbook.
     20573 Hardiness, passive: stun duration reduced. The anti-rogue and
           anti-warrior racial, and at 29 that is most of what kills you.
   UNDEAD - the pick for a bot that has to keep shooting through fear.
     7744 Will of the Forsaken, breaks charm, fear and sleep.
     5227 Touch of the Grave, passive shadow damage on hit.
     20577 Cannibalize, 20579 Shadow Resistance.
   DWARF - the Alliance pick against melee.
     20594 Stoneform, removes bleed, poison and disease and reduces
           damage taken for its duration.
     59224 Might of the Mountain, 20596 Frost Resistance, 92682 Explorer.
     There is no gun specialization any more: the dwarf rifleman fantasy
     does not come with a weapon bonus in this build.
   BLOOD ELF - Arcane Torrent is an area interrupt, which a hunter
     otherwise lacks.
     50613, 25046, 28730, 69179, 80483, 129597, 155145, 202719 and 232633
           are all Arcane Torrent ids, one per class version. Which one the
           8.3.7 hunter gets is unverified.
     154742 Arcane Acuity, 822 Arcane Resistance, 28877 Arcane Affinity.
   NIGHT ELF - 58984 Shadowmeld drops you out of combat standing still,
     20582 Quickness, 21009 Elusiveness, 20583 Nature Resistance,
     154748 Touch of Elune. A hunter already has Feign Death, so
     Shadowmeld is the weaker of the two escapes.
   WORGEN - 68992 Darkflight (+40% movement 10 sec), 68975 Viciousness
     (+1% crit), 68976 Aberration. Good for a flag runner.
   TAUREN - has the best racial in the bracket and cannot use it. 20549
     War Stomp stuns within 8 yards, which is melee range for a ranged
     class. 20550 Endurance, 154743 Brawn, 20552 Cultivation, 20551
     Nature Resistance are a fine stat package, but the stun is the reason
     anyone picks tauren and a hunter will rarely be standing in it.
   Everything else - human, gnome, draenei, troll, goblin, pandaren, void
   elf, lightforged draenei, dark iron dwarf, kul tiran, mechagnome,
   nightborne, zandalari troll - is a stat package whose racial ids were
   not opened for this file. Unverified, and none of them is likely to
   beat Command, Will of the Forsaken or Stoneform at 29.

8. SPECS AT LEVEL 29
   All three are open and one is picked at level 10:
     Beast Mastery  ranged. The pet is most of the spec, and the level 15
                    row's Animal Companion doubles it. The default for a
                    bot that has to work unattended.
     Marksmanship   ranged. Strongest raw shot damage; whether it wants a
                    pet depends on Lone Wolf, which is unverified.
     Survival       melee, two-hand weapon, no ranged slot, so the whole
                    bow list in the items file does not apply.
   Pets: a 29 can tame any beast at or below its own level. Exotic
   families are a Beast Mastery perk gated above this bracket, so a 29 BM
   bot tames ordinary families - unverified, check the spellbook.
   There are no forms, stances or shapeshifts for a hunter; the only
   comparable choice is the pet family, which decides the pet's own
   ability, and that is a taming decision rather than a talent one.

9. THE SHORT VERSION
   Orc for Beast Mastery, undead if the bracket is full of priests and
   warlocks, dwarf on the Alliance side. Enchant cloak, gloves and boots
   with agility and speed, put armor kits on the legs, take the satyr on
   the neck and haste on both rings. Carry Swiftness and Free Action
   potions, an Elixir of Agility and Mageweave Bandages, eat Roast Raptor,
   and take Engineering for the grenades and the Discombobulator Ray. Ask
   an engineer above 29 for a Net-o-Matic Projector if the roster can
   supply one - it is the only root a hunter can carry.
