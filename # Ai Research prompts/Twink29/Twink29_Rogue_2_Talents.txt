TWINK29 - ROGUE - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

0. WHAT A LEVEL 29 HAS
   Normal talents: seven rows, unlocked at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and
   100. A level 29 character owns the first row only, one point.
   PvP talents: three slots at 20, 40 and 70. A 29 owns the first slot
   only, and even then a PvP talent is legal only if its own level is 20
   or lower. The PvP slot is separate from the normal talent point - the
   owner asked whether it is shared, and it is not: a 29 has one point in
   each system.
   All three specializations are open at 29, and all three are Agility
   melee, so this file's differences are in the talents, not the gear.
   V = read off the talent's wiki page including the patch line that
   states its BfA level. A current tooltip is not evidence: several rogue
   talents changed row or spec after 8.3.7.

1. ASSASSINATION
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Master Poisoner, spell id unverified. Passive. Your poisons deal
     30% more damage and their non-damaging effects are 20% stronger.
     Redesigned as a level 15 Assassination talent in 7.0.3, and a 2016
     hotfix set the damage bonus at 30% (was 40%), so 30% and 20% are the
     8.3.7 numbers (V). The current page says it no longer increases
     poison damage - that change is 10.0.0, not this build.
   THE OTHER TALENTS of the row were not read for this file.
   PICK  Master Poisoner. It is passive, it is always on, and an
     Assassination bot's damage is poison damage. A bot does not have to
     do anything to get the full value, which is the test every pick in
     this file has to pass.
   PVP  no verified level 20 pick for this spec - see section 4.

2. OUTLAW
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Ghostly Strike, spell 196937. 30 Energy, awards 1 combo point: a
     strike that makes the target take 10% more damage from your
     abilities for 10 sec, on a 35 sec cooldown. Added in 7.0.3, was a
     level 15 Outlaw talent until 10.0.0 made it a tree row, and 8.0.1
     set the duration to 10 sec and the cooldown to 35 sec (V). The 15%
     figure on the current page is a 10.2.0 change; 8.3.7 is 10%.
     Weaponmaster, spell 193537, is also a verified level 15 Outlaw and
     Subtlety talent in BfA (V): 8.0.1 raised its proc chance to 15%.
     Which Outlaw abilities it repeats was not read, so it is named as a
     legal alternative and not recommended over the read one.
   PICK  Ghostly Strike. It is one extra button on a 35 second cooldown
     that a script can press on the pull, and 10% more damage from every
     ability for 10 seconds is the largest single gain in this row.
   PVP  Dismantle - the one verified rogue pick in this file, section 4.

3. SUBTLETY
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Weaponmaster, spell 193537. Passive. Shadowstrike and Backstab have
     a 15% chance to hit the target twice. 8.0.1 narrowed it to those two
     abilities (was all abilities) and raised the chance to 15% (was 6%),
     so 15% on two abilities is the 8.3.7 form (V). The current page's
     Shadow Clone wording is a 12.0.0 redesign.
   THE OTHER TALENTS of the row were not read.
   PICK  Weaponmaster. Passive again, and it multiplies the two abilities
     a Subtlety bot presses most. No timing, no setup, no energy cost.
   PVP  no verified level 20 pick for this spec - see section 4.

4. THE PVP SLOT AT LEVEL 20
   LEGAL AND VERIFIED
     Dismantle, Outlaw only in this build, level 20, 45 sec cooldown:
     disarms the target, preventing the use of weapons or a shield. The
     8.3.7 duration is 6 sec - 7.1.0 reduced it to 6 (was 8) and 10.1.0
     reduced it to 5 (was 6), so 6 is the value between them (V). Its
     energy cost was 25 before the 10.1.0 change set it to 15.
     9.1.0 made Dismantle available to all specs; in 8.3.7 it is Outlaw
     only, which is the single most useful thing this file can tell an
     Assassination or Subtlety player.
   WHY IT IS THE PICK  A disarm is a defensive cooldown pointed the other
     way: five to six seconds in which the warrior or rogue training you
     cannot use a weapon ability. For a companion bot it is also easy to
     script - press it when a melee target is in range and your health is
     falling.
   NOT VERIFIED
     Smoke Bomb is a rogue PvP talent, available to all specs since
     8.0.1, but its page gives a current level of 25 with no BfA patch
     line for the level, so whether a 29 could take it in 8.3.7 is
     unverified. Maneuverability, Creeping Venom, Shiv, Blade Rush,
     Honor Among Thieves and the rest of the rogue PvP list were not read
     at all. Assassination and Subtlety are therefore left without a PvP
     pick rather than given a guessed one.
   THE PATTERN TO REMEMBER  Dismantle is the fourth talent in this set
     whose spec availability changed after BfA (Psyfiend and Dome of
     Light changed level, Writhe in Agony and Eradication changed row).
     A current rogue guide will offer Dismantle to all three specs, and
     in 8.3.7 that is wrong for two of them.

5. THE THREE LOADOUTS
   ASSASSINATION  Master Poisoner. PvP unverified.
   OUTLAW         Ghostly Strike. PvP Dismantle.
   SUBTLETY       Weaponmaster. PvP unverified.
   Outlaw is the only spec with both slots filled from read pages, which
   is a reason to prefer it for the companion bots that have to be
   scripted rather than played: it is the only rogue here whose PvP
   talent is known to be legal.

6. WHAT IS SOLID AND WHAT IS NOT
   Solid: the row and slot gating (and that the two systems are separate),
   Master Poisoner at 30%/20%, Ghostly Strike 196937 at 10% for 10 sec on
   a 35 sec cooldown, Weaponmaster 193537 at 15% on Shadowstrike and
   Backstab, and Dismantle at level 20, Outlaw only, 6 sec.
   Not solid: Master Poisoner's spell id, the unread talents in every
   row, which Outlaw abilities Weaponmaster repeats, Smoke Bomb's BfA
   level, and every other rogue PvP talent's level. Where an id was read
   off an 8.x-era external link it is printed (196937, 193537); where it
   was not, no number appears.
