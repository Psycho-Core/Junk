HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Boss block fields:
  PHASES      journal stage names in order; "->" is the transition. "single phase" means the
              client journal data for the encounter carries no stage split.
  TANK        tank positioning and turning requirement.
  GROUP       where every other unit stands, and how it moves.
  GROUND      ground effects that must be left or dropped away, with spell id.
  ADD ORDER   kill order with P1..Pn priority; NEVER = must not be killed.
  AI RULE     the state-machine rule a companion needs for this fight.
  ABILITY LIST  every ability the client journal lists: id | name | flags.
              flags: I = interruptible cast (SpellCategories PreventionType 1 or 3);
              D:<type> = dispellable, with the client DispelType
              (magic / curse / disease / poison / enrage).
              "-" = neither. "I*" / "D*" = the client spell table does not flag the spell but the
              journal role text tells the player to interrupt / dispel it, so treat it as one.
  JOURNAL ADD / NPC GROUPS  the journal add sub-groups, with creature entries where a
              verified source resolves them and their top abilities.

  Trash sections give the prose pack breakdown first, then the instance trash table built from
  Method Dungeon Tools v5.0.2 dungeonEnemies (creature entry, name, enemy forces, spell ids).
  Raid trash is spell-driven: the ids DBM-BfA and BigWigs_BattleForAzeroth actually register.

ORDER  Boss blocks are printed in the client DungeonEncounter OrderIndex order for the instance.
  Where an instance lets the raid choose its own order, that order is not a forced route.

ID CONVENTION  A number written directly after a spell name is that spell id. Add and NPC names in
  the prose carry no id. In ADD ORDER a number after an add name is a spell that add casts; the
  add creature entry, where verified, is in the JOURNAL ADD table of the same block. Creature
  entries are also printed in the header line of each boss block.

VERIFIED (client tables, build 35662)
  Map.dbc map ids and names; DungeonEncounter ids and order; JournalEncounter ids; every boss
  ability spell id, name, interrupt flag and dispel type; journal stage names; journal add groups;
  MDT v5.0.2 dungeon trash creature entries, names, forces counts and spell ids.
NOT VERIFIED / NOT AVAILABLE
  Pull coordinates (MDT map space only, no z), pull timers, per-boss health percentages (not in
  the 8.3.7 client journal data used here), and raid trash creature entries where neither BigWigs
  nor DBM names them. Anything in this state is written unverified, never filled with a guess.

Global rules for companion AI
  - A ground effect is a spell that leaves a persistent object at the victim position. The victim
    walks out of the stack, then drops it at the wall; no unit paths through it afterwards.
  - Turning matters only where the journal lists a cone, breath or frontal. There the tank faces
    the boss away and every other unit stays behind or beside it.
  - Interrupt priority everywhere: heal > raid-wide damage > single-target nuke > everything else.
  - Dispel priority everywhere: anything that spreads > tank debuffs > damage DoTs.
  - Seasonal content is excluded: Reaping, Beguiling, the Awakened obelisks and their four elites
    (Samh'rek 161243, Urg'roth 161124, Voidweaver Mal'thir 161241, Blood of the Corruptor 161244),
    the N'Zoth assault Emissaries 155432/155433/155434, and the MDT-only ids 209859 / 277564 /
    277242 / 132951 / 205276. None of it is part of a plain 8.3.7 instance.

18.2  WHAT THIS EDITION CHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS DRAFT
  - Boss ability lists are now the client Encounter Journal sets, not hand-collected ids. This
    added whole ability families the old draft missed: Cave In 297283 on Gobbamak, Splatter
    297985 on Gunker, Protocol: Ninety-Nine 292290 on King Mechagon, the three carapaces of
    Shad'har, both phases of N'Zoth.
  - Interrupt and dispel flags come from SpellCategories, so every I and D:<type> is a client
    fact. Where the journal role text tells the player to interrupt or dispel but the spell table
    has no flag, the row is marked I* or D* and both facts are stated.
  - Freehold encounter ids corrected: Skycap'n Kragg 2093, Council o' Captains 2094, Ring of
    Booty 2095, Harlan Sweete 2096. The previous draft had Kragg on 2095 and Trothak on 2094.
  - Operation: Mechagon DungeonEncounter ids corrected to the client rows: Junkyard 2290 (King
    Gobbamak), 2292 (Gunker), 2312 (Trixie & Naeno), 2291 (HK-8); Workshop 2257 (Tussle Tonks),
    2258 (K.U.-J.0.), 2259 (Machinist's Garden), 2260 (King Mechagon).
  - Uldir printed in client order, 2122 G'huun last; Zul is 2145 (sixth), Mythrax 2135 (seventh).
  - Journal add groups are printed per encounter, with creature entries resolved from the live NPC
    database and zone-checked against the instance the group belongs to.
  - 21 creature names in the previous draft were Method Dungeon Tools short names, not live NPC
    names (the Tol Dagor spectral set, 134174 Risen Hexer, 134157 Umbral Warrior, 135204 Phantom
    Hex Priest, 135239 Spectral Shaman, 134990 Storm Adept, 68819 Arcane Eye and others). The live
    name is printed, with the MDT name in brackets where they differ.
  - Seasonal Awakened elites (161243, 161124, 161241, 161244) are excluded from every trash list:
    they are obelisk content added in 8.3 and their NPC zones are not the instance zones.

18.3  KNOWN GAPS (explicit, not hidden)
  - No pull coordinates and no route draws. MDT stores map-space x/y only; its z is not the world
    z, so an x/y/z triple here would be false precision. Pack order and pack names are given.
  - No cast timers, no health percentages, no damage numbers: the 8.3.7 client journal does not
    carry them and no TrinityCore 8.3.7 spell_dbc numbers were used.
  - Raid trash creature entries are only printed where DBM-BfA or BigWigs names the mob. For
    Ny'alotha, the Eternal Palace and Crucible the raid trash is spell-driven only.
  - Champion of the Light and Jadefire Masters exist twice in the journal (Horde and Alliance).
    The creature entries printed are the ones verified with a matching instance zone; the other
    faction's equivalents are unverified.
  - The Irontide Grenadier on Harlan Sweete has no verified creature entry.
  - 28 journal add groups still print "unverified" in the creature column: no Wowhead NPC row
    matched both the name and the instance zone. They are left unverified rather than filled with
    a plausible id.

18.4  USING THIS WITH COMPANION AI
  One block = one profile object; fields in order of authority over a companion:
    1. AI RULE is the transition that is easy to get wrong; treat it as the block's manual override.
    2. PHASES gives the state list; switch on the first cast that only exists in the new state.
    3. TANK and GROUP give the two anchor positions. Every other unit derives its position from the
       group anchor, not from the tank.
    4. GROUND ids become avoid-volumes with top priority over both anchors.
    5. Interruptible rows (flag I or I*) become a per-unit interrupt queue; the highest-priority id
       on the list holds one interrupt at all times.
    6. Dispellable rows (flag D:<type> or D*) are filtered by each companion's class dispel type.
    7. ADDS priority P1..Pn (and never-kill markers) is a target-priority key, so a P1 spawn pulls
       every switch automatically.
    8. Any single-target mechanic (mark, bomb, fixate, grab, tether) is a personal state that
       overrides 2-7 until it expires or is removed.
