================================================================================
KINGS' REST - POSITIONS AND NUMBERS, BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 BUILD 35662
Map id 1762. Four encounters: The Golden Serpent, Mchimba the Embalmer, The
Council of Tribes (Kula the Butcher, Aka'ali the Conqueror, Zanazal the Wise),
King Dazar.
Companion file to the tactics document. Every number here is tagged with the
source it came from. Anything I could not read is written "unverified".

0. HOW TO READ THIS FILE

  Tags, used on every claim:
    [DB]    a row in the 8.3.7 world SQL for this instance: creature,
            gameobject, areatrigger, instance_template, and the 25 rows of
            smart_scripts that drive every trash mob.
    [SRV]   a line in the script: kings_rest.h, instance_kings_rest.cpp, or
            one of the four boss scripts.
    [MDT]   Method Dungeon Tools, BattleForAzeroth/KingsRest.lua: the name,
            the per-kill force value, the health pool, the level.
    [DBM]   DBM-Party-BfA/KingsRest/*.lua: a stopwatch value.
    [LW]    LittleWigs BfA/KingsRest/*.lua: a second stopwatch value.
    [CALC]  arithmetic I did on the numbers above. The inputs are always
            given so you can redo it.
    unverified   no readable source carries the value. It is never a guess.

  Coordinates are (x, y, z) in world yards on map 1762. Orientation is
  radians. It is quoted only where the row carries one.

  The important structural fact about this dungeon on this core: ALL TRASH
  BUT ONE IS SMART SCRIPTED. instance_kings_rest.cpp registers the instance
  script and exactly one creature AI, npc_shadow_of_zul (8.3). Every other
  trash mob is SmartAI, and its
  rotation is the smart_scripts table in the world DB - 25 rows for 18
  entries. That is why section 8 can give exact trash timers and why a
  different world DB will run different trash.

1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS

  1.1 The scripts are Psycho-Core-8.3.7_Scripts, scripts/Zandalar/King's
      Rest: four boss files, instance_kings_rest.cpp and kings_rest.h. They
      are short - 17.9 KB in total - and the four bosses schedule 15
      events between them. Where a script is thin, this file says so
      instead of filling the gap from a guide.
  1.2 The spawn rows are NOT from the AshamaneCore update that steps 01-07
      used. AshamaneCore has no King's Rest SQL. The 69 creature rows, 5
      game-object rows over 4 entries, 1 areatrigger and 25 smart_scripts
      rows here come from the LatincoreBfa 2020_07_20_00_kings_rest.sql. It
      is internally consistent - its rows add up to 240 of MDT's 246 total
      forces - but if your world DB is a different one, every coordinate
      here can move and you should re-read your own creature table.
  1.3 No client table was read. Radii, durations and damage below are
      unverified unless the script, DBM or LittleWigs states them.
  1.4 MDT clone positions were not used. They are stored in MDT's own map
      space, not in world coordinates, so only MDT's names, forces, health
      and levels are cited here.

4.4 ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS AND EXACT TRIGGERS
  a) SPIRIT OF GOLD 265773 - ONE RANDOM PLAYER, and it is a move-out.
     Trigger: the 5.0 s event, DoCastRandom(265773, 100.0f). The aura is a
     periodic damage aura; when it is removed (AURA_EFFECT_HANDLE_REAL) the
     caster casts 255583 Molten Gold on the same player, which is the gold
     pool. So the assigned player is whoever has the debuff, and the
     assignment is "walk it away from the group before it expires".
     The pool's radius is unverified; 265914 exists in the script as
     "Molten Gold AT damage" and nothing casts it.
  b) TAIL THRASH 265910 - THE TANK. Trigger: DoCastVictim on the 10.0 s
     event. It is aimed at the current victim and is the only
     victim-targeted cast in the encounter.
  c) SERPENTINE GUST 265781 - NOBODY. Self-cast AOE, no target. It is also
     the fall-through of Tail Thrash (4.3).
  d) SOAK LISTS, KICK ORDER AND TANK SWAPS: none. There is no interruptible
     cast and no stacking debuff in this fight.

4.5 SAFE SPOTS, WITH z
  There is no safe-spot coordinate in any source, and the script never
  positions anything. What the data does fix:
  - the arena is at z 827.161 [DB]. Anything on the corridor floor (z 809.9
    to 810.8) is 16.4 to 17.3 yd below it - out of line of sight in
    practice, but no line-of-sight data exists to prove it.
  - the Molten Gold pool spawns on the player who had 265773, at that
    player's own position, at whatever z they stood on [SRV].
  - Serpentine Gust has no radius in any source. Do not put one in a bot.
  Everything else in this encounter is unverified.

     actors inside a boss room in this instance.

10. DISCREPANCIES AND TRAPS
================================================================================
10.1 KING DAZAR CASTS NOTHING. boss_dazar.cpp EnterCombat calls
     _EnterCombat() and returns. There is no ScheduleEvent in the file, so
     Blade Combo 268586, Quaking Leap 268932 and Gale Slash 268403 are never
     fired, never repeated and never seen. DBM and LittleWigs both time
     them. On this server the encounter is a melee boss, one Reban and one
     Tzala (and see 10.2 for the second one).
10.2 THE 60% BRANCH SETS THE WRONG FLAG. DamageTaken's second block tests
     !hp60 and then assigns hp80 = true. hp60 is never set, so every damage
     event with the boss below 60% summons another Tzala. Fix it before you
     write a bot that counts adds.
10.3 MCHIMBA'S ENTOMB EVENT CASTS NOTHING. The case calls Talk(SAY_ENTOMB)
     and re-arms for 30.0 s. Spell 267702 is never cast, and 267763 (the
     dispel-driven part DBM and LittleWigs watch) never appears either.
     Both addons still put an Entomb bar on the screen.
10.4 THE GOLDEN SERPENT'S TAIL THRASH FALLS THROUGH. case EVENT_TAIL_THRASH
     has no break, so it runs the Serpentine Gust case as well: every Tail
     Thrash also casts 265781 and re-arms the Gust at 35.0 s. The Gust's own
     15.0 s first cast therefore never happens on its own schedule.
10.5 SHADOW OF ZUL FALLS THROUGH THE SAME WAY. Every Shadow Barrage also
     runs Dark Revelation, so the 3 s spell also summons a Minion of Zul.
10.6 LUcre'S CALL AND LUSTER ARE DEAD ENUMS. 265923 and 265991 are declared
     in Spells and Events and scheduled by nothing. LittleWigs still bars
     265923 at 40.0 s.
10.7 HUNTING LEAP HAS THREE IDS. The core casts 270502. DBM times 269231 and
     LittleWigs logs 269230. A companion keyed to the addon ids sees nothing.
10.8 TWO OF THE THREE COUNCIL BOSSES HAVE NO DB ROW. Aka'ali 135470 and
     Zanazal 135472 exist only as script constants (3.2). Creature-table
     queries for them return nothing, and MDT lists both.
10.9 THIS IS NOT THE ASHAMANE DB. The spawn rows are LatincoreBfa
     2020_07_20_00_kings_rest.sql. Its 69 rows total 240 force against MDT's
     246, so it is close to complete, but it is not the world DB that steps
     01-07 used and it is not necessarily yours.
10.10 EMBALMING FLUID IS A CREATURE. 137989 has 10 rows and force 1 each, so
     10 of the instance's 240 force in this DB come from killing puddles.
     Their SmartAI casts 271565 Splash Fluid at a random player every 10-20 s.
10.11 BOTH CHESTS ARE LOCKED AT CREATE. instance_kings_rest.cpp sets
     GO_FLAG_LOCKED on 288637 and 288638 in OnGameObjectCreate. Zanazal's
     JustDied removes it from 288637 within 300 yd, and Dazar's from 288638
     within 300 yd. If either chest is further than 300 yd from the corpse
     it stays locked forever - and 288638 is 27.9 yd from Dazar, so that one
     works, while 288637 is 46.9 yd from Kula and 55.4 yd from Zanazal's
     spawn, so that one works too. The failure mode is real but not triggered
     by these coordinates.
10.12 THE INSTANCE SCRIPT NAME MATCHES HERE. instance_template says
     'instance_kings_rest' and the C++ registers instance_kings_rest on 1762.
     Unlike Freehold there is no mismatch - noted because steps 01-07 hit
     one.
