================================================================================
TWINK29 PROFESSIONS - 03 BLACKSMITHING
Patch 8.3.7 (build 35662), level 29 battleground twink
================================================================================

Ids checked against 8.3.7 client tables (ItemSparse, SpellName, SpellReagents,
SkillLine, SkillLineAbility) and Wowhead Classic item and spell pages. Where a
number could not be checked it says "unverified".


================================================================================
1. WHAT A LEVEL 29 CAN ACTUALLY REACH
================================================================================

Blacksmithing is skill line 164 in 8.3.7 (SpellBookSpellID 2018). The classic
recipe tier is still 2485, so the whole Classic 1-300 list is present.

Rank gates (Classic table, restated by the 1-300 guides; costs undiscounted):
  Apprentice  1-75    level 5   10 copper
  Journeyman  1-150   level 10  5 silver      (needs 50 skill)
  Expert      1-225   level 20  50 silver     (needs 125 skill)
  Artisan     1-300   level 35  5 gold        (needs 200 skill)

=> At level 29 the ceiling is 225. Artisan is 6 levels out of reach.

Caveat: the level gate on the ranks is trainer and world data, not something
the 8.3.7 DB2 tables expose, so it could not be re-verified inside the client.
225 is the working cap.

Specialisation is out of reach twice over: Armorsmithing and Weaponsmithing
both need skill 200 AND character level 40. A 29 cannot take either, so every
specialisation-only plan is ALT.

Three things decide what blacksmithing is worth at 29:

  1. Plate is not wearable. Warriors and paladins get the plate skill at level
     40, so every blacksmithing plate piece in this file is ALT regardless of
     its item level. The wearable output is mail: the Green Iron set.
  2. The armour recipes need a Blacksmith Hammer 5956 in the bag and an anvil
     (confirmed on spell 3502, which lists both). The sharpening stones and
     weightstones need neither - spell 9921 lists no tools at all. Anvils are
     world objects in every city and most starting towns; no anvil could be
     listed with an entry number here, so treat anvil placement as unverified.
  3. Blacksmithing's real product for a twink is not armour. It is the
     consumable weapon buffs - sharpening stones, weightstones and the iron
     counterweight - plus shield spikes. Those work at any level the item
     allows, and they are made in bulk and carried.


================================================================================
2. USABLE AT 29 - WEAPON BUFFS
================================================================================

Sharpening stones (sharp weapons: swords, daggers, axes with an edge)
  Rough Sharpening Stone 2862. Item level 5, no required level.
    Use: +2 weapon damage for 30 min, spell 2828.
    Recipe: spell 2660, skill 1. Materials: 1x Rough Stone 2835.
  Coarse Sharpening Stone 2863. Item level 15, requires level 5.
    Use: +3 weapon damage for 30 min, spell 2829.
    Recipe: spell 2665, skill 65 (trivial 72-80).
    Materials: 1x Coarse Stone 2836.
  Heavy Sharpening Stone 2871. Item level 25, requires level 15.
    Use: +4 weapon damage for 30 min, spell 2830.
    Recipe: spell 2674, skill 125 (trivial 132-140).
    Materials: 1x Heavy Stone 2838.
  Solid Sharpening Stone 7964. Item level 35, requires level 25.
    Use: +6 weapon damage for 30 min, spell 9900.
    Recipe: spell 9918, skill 200 (trivial 200-210).
    Materials: 1x Solid Stone 7912.
  Dense Sharpening Stone 12404 requires level 35 and skill 250: ALT.

Weightstones (blunt weapons: maces, staves, fists)
  Rough Weightstone 3239. Item level 5, no required level.
    Use: +2 blunt damage for 30 min, spell 3112. Recipe spell 3115, skill 1.
  Coarse Weightstone 3240. Item level 15, requires level 5.
    Use: +3, spell 3113. Recipe spell 3116, skill 25 (unverified).
  Heavy Weightstone 3241. Item level 25, requires level 15.
    Use: +4 blunt damage for 30 min, spell 3114. Recipe spell 3117,
    skill 125 (trivial 125-140).
  Solid Weightstone 7965. Item level 35, requires level 25.
    Use: +6 blunt damage for 30 min, spell 9903.
    Recipe: spell 9921, skill 200 (trivial 200-210).
    Materials: 1x Solid Stone 7912, 1x Silk Cloth 4306.
  Dense Weightstone 12643 requires level 35 and skill 250: ALT.

Neither stone stacks with the other, and each is consumed when applied. A 29
carries Solid Sharpening Stone 7964 or Solid Weightstone 7965 - +6 for 30
minutes, at skill 200, which is inside the cap.

Iron Counterweight 6043. Item level 33. Requires Blacksmithing 165 to APPLY.
  Use: makes a two-handed sword, mace, axe or polearm 3% faster, spell 7218.
  Recipe: spell 7222, skill 165 (trivial 190-215).
  Materials: 4x Iron Bar 3575, 2x Coarse Grinding Stone 3478,
  1x Lesser Moonstone 1705. Requires an anvil.
  Learned from Plans: Iron Counterweight 6045, a world drop, not a trainer.
  This is the one blacksmithing item a 29 should not skip. 3% haste on a
  two-hander is a permanent weapon enhancement, not a 30-minute buff, and 165
  is well inside the cap. Note there is no weapon chain in the Classic recipe
  list that could be found: the "chain" of later expansions has no Classic
  equivalent, and item 6040 is Golden Scale Bracers, not a chain.

Shield spikes - shield users only
  Iron Shield Spike 6042. Item level 30. Requires Blacksmithing 150 to APPLY.
    Use: damages anyone who blocks, spell 7216. Stack 5.
    Recipe: spell 7221, skill 150 (trivial 180-210), anvil.
    Materials: 6x Iron Bar 3575, 4x Coarse Grinding Stone 3478.
    Learned from Plans: Iron Shield Spike 6044, a world drop.
  Mithril Shield Spike 7967. Item level 43. Requires Blacksmithing 215.
    Use: same, bigger, spell 9781.
    Recipe: spell 9939, skill 215 (trivial 235-255), anvil.
    Materials: 4x Mithril Bar 3860, 2x Truesilver Bar 6037,
    4x Solid Grinding Stone 7966.
    Learned from Plans: Mithril Shield Spike 7976, a world drop.
  Both are inside the 225 cap and both are permanent until overwritten. The
  Iron spike needs only 150, so a 29 can make and fit it from the start.


================================================================================
3. USABLE AT 29 - THE MAIL ARMOUR
================================================================================

These are the only wearable pieces blacksmithing makes that a 29 can both
craft and equip. They are leveling greens, not twink gear: an instance drop of
the same level beats every one of them. They are listed because the prompt
asks, not because they are worth wearing.

Green Iron Boots 3484. Item level 29, feet, mail, binds when equipped.
  134 armour, +3 Strength, +7 Stamina, requires level 24.
  Recipe: spell 3334, skill 145 (trivial 175-205), anvil.
  Materials: 4x Iron Bar 3575, 2x Lesser Moonstone 1705,
  2x Coarse Grinding Stone 3478, 1x Green Dye 2605.
  NOT a trainer spell: learned from Plans: Green Iron Boots 3611, a world drop.

Green Iron Leggings 3842. Item level 31, legs, mail, binds when equipped.
  176 armour, +8 Strength, +8 Stamina, requires level 26.
  Recipe: spell 3506, skill 155 (trivial 180-205).
  Materials: 8x Iron Bar 3575, 1x Heavy Grinding Stone 3486, 1x Green Dye 2605.

Green Iron Bracers 3835. Item level 33, wrist, mail, requires level 28.
  86 armour, no stats.
  Recipe: spell 3501, skill 165 (trivial 190-215). Binding unverified.
  Materials: 6x Iron Bar 3575, 1x Green Dye 2605.

Green Iron Helm 3836. Item level 34, head, mail, binds when equipped.
  171 armour, +5 Strength, +11 Stamina, requires level 29.
  Recipe: spell 3502, skill 170 (trivial 195-220).
  Materials: 12x Iron Bar 3575, 1x Citrine 3864, 1x Green Dye 2605.
  The best of the four and the only one that lands exactly on 29.

Green Iron Hauberk 3844. Item level 36, chest, mail, binds when equipped.
  358 armour, +7 Strength, +11 Stamina, requires level 31: ALT by two levels.
  Recipe: spell 3508, skill 180.

Tools and reagents: Blacksmith Hammer 5956 is required for all of the above
and is itself a blacksmithing item (skill 1). Green Dye 2605 comes from
tailoring and leatherworking supply vendors, not from a gatherer.

                                 blocked twice over

By profession

  are white and green items a few levels below the bracket's instance drops.
  Do not level the profession for a weapon.

5. CHEAPEST ROUTE TO 225
================================================================================

Only two stopping points matter:
  170 - Green Iron Helm 3836, the last wearable piece.
  200 - Solid Sharpening Stone 7964 and Solid Weightstone 7965, the +6
        weapon buffs, which is what the profession is actually for.
If the goal is the stones, stop at 200 and do not pay for the last 25 points.

Route, cheapest classic path (step, recipe, craft spell, skill band):
   1-30    40x Rough Sharpening Stone     2660   skill 1
   30-65   55x Rough Grinding Stone       3320   min skill 25
   65-75   25x Coarse Sharpening Stone    2665   min skill 65
   75-90   35x Coarse Grinding Stone      3326   min skill 75
   90-100  10x Runed Copper Belt          2666   min skill 70
   100-105 5x  Silver Rod                 7818   min skill 100
   105-110 5x  Runed Copper Belt          2666
   110-125 15x Rough Bronze Leggings      2668   min skill 105
   125-140 35x Heavy Grinding Stone       3337   min skill 125
   140-150 10x Patterned Bronze Bracers   2672   min skill 120
   150-155 5x  Golden Rod                 14379  min skill 150
   155-165 10x Green Iron Leggings        3506   min skill 155
   165-190 25x Green Iron Bracers         3501   min skill 165
   190-200 10x Golden Scale Bracers       7223   min skill 185
   200-210 30x Solid Grinding Stone       9920   min skill 200
   210-225 15x Heavy Mithril Gauntlet     9928   min skill 205

Materials for 1-225, tallied from the steps above (the guides add 10-20% on
the yellow steps; this is the raw count):
  150x Rough Stone 2835
   95x Coarse Stone 2836
  150x Copper Bar 2840
    5x Silver Bar 2842
  140x Bronze Bar 2841        (smelted 2 at a time from 1 Copper Bar 2840
                              plus 1 Tin Bar 3576: 70 + 70 to make 140)
  105x Heavy Stone 2838
    5x Gold Bar 3577
  230x Iron Bar 3575
   35x Green Dye 2605

Where the materials come from
  Rough Stone 2835, Coarse Stone 2836   mining Copper and Tin deposits,
    every starting zone: Elwynn, Dun Morogh, Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal
  Heavy Stone 2838, Solid Stone 7912   mining Iron and Mithril deposits,
    25-50 zones: Arathi Highlands, Badlands, Stranglethorn Vale, Tanaris
  Copper Bar 2840   smelted from Copper Ore 2770
  Bronze Bar 2841   smelted from 1 Copper Bar 2840 plus 1 Tin Bar 3576,
                    which yields 2 Bronze Bar
  Silver Bar 2842, Gold Bar 3577   rare veins inside copper and iron nodes
  Iron Bar 3575     smelted from Iron Ore 2772
  Steel Bar 3859    Iron Bar plus Coal 3857; Coal is sold by trade supply
                    vendors in every capital, needs Mining to smelt
  Mithril Bar 3860  smelted from Mithril Ore 3858
  Mageweave Cloth 4338   level 40-50 humanoids
  Green Dye 2605    tailoring and leatherworking supply vendors

Practical note: Mining is skill line 186. Every bar above except the bought
Green Dye and Coal comes out of Mining, and the stone counts are the real
bottleneck - a 1-225 run eats 150 Rough Stone and 120 Solid Stone. An alt
miner is cheaper than the auction house for everything except Silver and Gold,
which are rare-vein drops.

7. WHAT A 29 ACTUALLY CARRIES
================================================================================

In the bag
  20x Solid Sharpening Stone 7964     or 20x Solid Weightstone 7965
  20x Iron Shield Spike 6042          if the character uses a shield
  1x  Iron Counterweight 6043         applied once to a two-hander

Fitted
  Iron Shield Spike 6042 on the shield (permanent until replaced)
  Iron Counterweight 6043 on the weapon (permanent)

The stones are the whole point: +6 weapon damage for 30 minutes, stackable in
bags of 20, made at skill 200 for one Solid Stone each. One stack per
battleground is enough, and a 29 blacksmith can supply the whole account.


================================================================================
8. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE BLACKSMITHING
================================================================================

Be blunt: blacksmithing is the weakest of the four gear professions for a 29.
It makes no item a twink would want to wear, it cannot touch plate, and its
one good permanent item (Iron Counterweight 6043) is a single two-handed
weapon enhancement. Take it for the stones and the spikes, not for gear.

1. Warrior. The counterweight on a two-hander, Solid Sharpening Stones 7964,
   and the Iron Shield Spike 6042 if the build uses a shield. The Green Iron
   Helm 3836 is the only wearable piece that lands on 29 and it is a leveling
   green - useful only if nothing better dropped.

2. Paladin. Shield spike plus stones. The Green Iron set fits, but a paladin
   at 29 is a healer or a mail-wearing retribution build, and tailoring or
   engineering serve both better.

3. Rogue. Sharpening stones only. No shield, no two-hander, no mail. Alchemy
   or engineering is a better use of the profession slot.

4. Hunter. Weightstones on a two-handed axe or a staff, if the build uses one.
   Otherwise nothing here.

5. Everyone else. None. Casters get nothing at all from blacksmithing - no
   sharpening stone, no weightstone, no spike, no wearable mail.

Ranking inside this file, best first: the Solid stones, the Iron Counterweight,
the shield spikes, then the Green Iron set. If a 29 must pick one profession
for damage, engineering wins on every count; blacksmithing is a supplier
profession for a warrior or a paladin and a waste for everyone else.
