0. THE POOL IS THE HUNTER'S POOL, AND IT IS THIN
   Rare mail at required 26-29 returns 15 rows. Eight are class-locked to
   Paladin or Warrior. What is left is seven pieces, three heads among
   them, and no rare mail shoulder, wrist or legs at all. A shaman shares
   that pool with the hunter, and shares the battleground exception too:
   the Arathi Basin belt and boots tagged Hunter and Shaman are filed
   under leather in the database, not mail, but they are this class's own
   set and a shaman can buy them.
   The difference from the hunter is in the specs. The hunter is one
   Agility class; the shaman is two Intellect specs and one Agility spec
   wearing the same seven pieces, so the mail pool serves Enhancement
   and starves the casters, whose gear comes from weapons and heirlooms.

3. ELEMENTAL
   Armour: section 1 with 42951, the Intellect heirloom shoulders. The
   battleground belt and boots are Agility pieces; an Elemental shaman
   wears them for the Stamina if the greens are worse, and should know
   that is what it is doing.
   Weapons: 7714 Hypnotic Blade + 7787 Resplendent Guardian, or 19573
   [19569] staff. The staff is the simpler bot loadout: one item, no
   off-hand decision, and the Warsong staff is sold, not dropped.
   Trinkets: 19024 and the Insignia.

4. ENHANCEMENT
   Armour: section 1 with 44101, 6686 if its required level holds, 20090
   [20152] and 20093 [20157]. Enhancement is the only shaman spec the
   mail pool actually feeds.
   Weapons: 7682 Torturing Poker main hand, 7683 Bloody Brass Knuckles
   or a second 7682 off hand. There is no axe, so the classic Enhancement
   picture of two axes does not exist at this tier.
   Trinkets: the same three. A melee spec gets more from 4381 than a
   caster does, as the monk file noted.

5. RESTORATION
   Armour: as Elemental. A healer in mail with no Intellect mail is
   wearing heirloom shoulders and whatever the weapon gives it, which is
   the thinnest gearing position of any healer in this set - the priest
   and the Restoration druid both have a full armour type to shop in.
   Weapons: 7714 + 7787, or the staff. Take the shield build where the
   bot is expected to be hit; armour is the one defensive stat a
   Restoration shaman can actually buy at 29.
   Trinkets: the same three.

7. HONEST SUMMARY OF THE POOL
   A 29 shaman is two different gearing problems in one class. Enhancement
   is served about as well as the hunter: battleground belt and boots, an
   Agility helm if its level holds, and two good one-hand weapons from a
   single Scarlet Monastery boss. Elemental and Restoration are the worst
   off of any caster in this set, because their armour type has no
   Intellect piece in it at all at this tier - not one - and their entire
   spell power comes from a staff or a dagger, two heirloom shoulders and
   a Warsong ring.
   Four tooltip-versus-search conflicts are flagged in place (6686, 1488,
   9445, 2816), all on Razorfen, Blackfathom or Gnomeregan items, and all
   following the pattern the rogue audit found: the 8.3.7 tooltip reads
   several of these at required 30 where the search reads 27 or 28. The
   search values are printed, per the convention of the other files, and
   the disagreements are stated so they can be settled on the server.
