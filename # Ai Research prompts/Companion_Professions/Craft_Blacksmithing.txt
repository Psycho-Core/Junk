BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - BLACKSMITHING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
============================================================================================

Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client) levels Blacksmithing on a
TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Every id below was read from a source this
week; anything without a readable source says "unverified" instead of a guess.

Classic rank note: warcraft.wiki Profession patch note for 8.0.1 (2018-07-17)
says "the profession proficiencies were merge and renamed. For example,
Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, and Artisan are now just "Classic". Wowhead's
2018 page still lists "Other ranks (9)" - 3100 (requires Blacksmithing 50,
Training cost 5), 3538 (125), 9785 (200), 29844 (275), 51300 (350), 76666
(425), 110396 (500), 158737, 195097 - these are the pre-8.0.1 unified-bar rank
spells. On 8.3.7 the classic bar is one Classic line (2477, cap 300) and the
leveling guide treats it as an unbroken 1-300 bar; the sampled trainer list
(Alard Schmied, 102 rows) contains spell 2018 but no Journeyman/Expert/Artisan
rank spell. The ranks are not part of the route here (whether any trainer
still sells them in 8.3.7: unverified).

7. WHAT THE CORE MUST DO FOR A CRAFT TO SUCCEED
-----------------------------------------------
From TrinityCore master source (Spell.cpp, SpellEffects.cpp, Player.cpp,
NPCHandler.cpp, Trainer.cpp - re-read this week; line numbers refer to
master, your 8.3.7 fork may differ slightly):
1) The character must know the recipe spell (Player.HasSpell), and the spell
   must pass CheckCast: for tradeskill spells the server checks the required
   skill (failure packet SPELL_FAILED_MIN_SKILL carries the skill line id and
   the required value - Spell.cpp line 4558).
2) Reagents must be in the bags: Spell::TakeReagents (Spell.cpp line 5599)
   destroys SpellInfo Reagent[x] / ReagentCount[x] with DestroyItemCount over
   the MAX_SPELL_REAGENTS loop, and it runs BEFORE the effects - the code
   comment above the function says the reagents must be removed before
   HandleEffects so the crafted item can land in the same slot. Reagents are
   gone even if the create fails later.
3) Inventory space: DoCreateItem (SpellEffects.cpp lines 1438-1445) handles
   EQUIP_ERR_INV_FULL / EQUIP_ERR_ITEM_MAX_COUNT by trimming the amount
   actually created; when bags are completely full no product is stored and
   UpdateCraftSkill is never called (its call sites are SpellEffects.cpp
   lines 1469, 1499, 2492) - reagents already lost. A bot must guarantee a
   free slot for the product count before casting.
4) The product is created by SPELL_EFFECT_CREATE_ITEM (DoCreateItem with
   ItemContext::Trade_Skill for spells with SPELL_ATTR0_IS_TRADESKILL);
   sampled blacksmithing spells are 2-second casts with "Tradeskill recipe"
   flags (2660, 55835, 253183 all "Cast time: 2 seconds").
5) Skill-up: only after at least one item was created, Player::
   UpdateCraftSkill(SpellInfo const*) runs (Player.cpp line 5410): it finds
   the SkillLineAbility entry whose SkillupSkillLineID is the skill being
   raised; gain per craft = NumSkillUps x CONFIG_SKILL_GAIN_CRAFTING (line
   5433), rolled through UpdateSkillPro with SkillGainChance(skill,
   TrivialSkillLineRankHigh, midpoint, TrivialSkillLineRankLow) - the
   orange/yellow/green/grey numbers the client shows as recipe colours.
   warcraft.wiki colour prose: orange always grants a point, yellow about
   60% ("yellow recipes increase it about 60% of the time"), green rarely,
   grey never (wowpedia's table: yellow 50-75%, green 50% or less, grey 0%).
6) DISCOVERY: inside UpdateCraftSkill, if the spell's Mechanic is
   MECHANIC_DISCOVERY the server rolls GetSkillDiscoverySpell(line, spellId,
   player) and LearnSpell(result) - the comment in the code is literally
   "Alchemy Discoveries here" (Player.cpp lines 5426-5431). None of the
   sampled blacksmithing recipes showed "Mechanic: Discovery" (2660, 55835,
   253183 spell details print Mechanic n/a) - for blacksmithing this branch
   is likely unused; whether ANY blacksmithing spell carries it is
   unverified.
7) ANVIL / SPELL FOCUS: sampled recipes print "Requires Anvil" in their wowhead
   quick facts (55835 Northrend, 253183 BfA) while the starter sharpening
   stone 2660 has no such line - matching wowpedia: "Most recipes require the
   use of a blacksmith hammer and anvil. However, some products (such as
   Rough Sharpening Stone, for example) do not", and the official overview
   "The majority of blacksmithing work must be done with a hammer and anvil.
   Smithing anvils (and forges to smelt minerals) can be found in most towns
   and cities." The core enforces this data-driven: Spell::
   FindGameObjectFocus uses Trinity::GameObjectFocusCheck(caster,
   m_spellInfo->RequiresSpellFocus) searching grid GameObjects within
   GetVisibilityRange (Spell.cpp line 2321); no hard-coded anvil strings
   exist in Spell.cpp/Player.cpp (grep this week - only
   ITEM_SUBCLASS_PROFESSION_BLACKSMITHING bag-sorting in Player.cpp line
   9313). The focus id per spell was not read (unverified). Legion's
   Firmament Stone (section 3.7) is the same proximity pattern - a station
   named in the guide; the core has the generic focus machinery, per-spell
   data unverified.
8) Tool: recipe pages list Tools: Blacksmith Hammer (2660 has no tool line;
   55835 and 253183 do). How your fork checks the tool (carried item vs
   reagent vs cosmetic) was not read in the core (unverified).
9) Learning a recipe from a trainer: the client sends CMSG_TRAINER_BUY_SPELL
   (NPCHandler.cpp HandleTrainerBuySpellOpcode line 130). The server
   requires: NPC in interaction range, an OPEN trainer interaction (line 125
   StartInteraction(..., PlayerInteractionType::Trainer); line 145
   IsInteractingWith(Trainer) guard), then Trainer::TeachSpell (Trainer.cpp
   line 76) runs the gates of section 6, takes the money with reputation
   discount (line 98), and either CastSpell(self, spell, triggered) or
   LearnSpell directly (CanTeachSpell line 149, GetSpellState line 172).
   Buying without an open interaction is rejected (log line 137: "not found
   or you can not interact with him").
10) Cooldowns and daily crafts: Secrets of Draenor Blacksmithing 176090 has
    "Cooldown: 1 day" (wowhead) - the server runs it from spell data. No
    shared profession cooldown pattern (like alchemy transmutes) was found
    for blacksmithing (unverified-negative). Passive perks that change
    numbers, not success: racial skill bonuses Lightforged Draenei +5, Dark
    Iron Dwarf +5, Kul Tiran +2 (all eight wow-professions guides; the
    warcraft.wiki racial table lists Dark Iron "Mass Production" for
    Blacksmithing but no value printed - value unverified).

8. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------
- Cannot see the profession window: recipe lists, spell ids, reagent counts
  and the orange/yellow/green/grey colours live in client UI fed by server
  data (SkillLineAbility ranks). The bot must read recipes/ids from the DB
  or this file and compute colours itself, or ignore them.
- Cannot craft "anywhere": unlike the alchemy file's wiki line ("created
  anywhere"), blacksmithing recipes sampled here declare Requires Anvil -
  the bot must path to an anvil GameObject (and, on the Legion line, to the
  Firmament Stone at Ironhorn Enclave for Demonsteel armor) and fail
  otherwise. Positioning per anvil/station object ids: unverified.
- Cannot learn a spell "by opening the trainer": it must script the trainer
  interaction sequence (open trainer list, then CMSG_TRAINER_BUY_SPELL with
  NPC guid + trainer id + spell id). Buying without an open interaction is
  rejected (NPCHandler interaction guard).
- Cannot run quests the normal way: Legion's path needs quest 38499 plus
  34 Blacksmithing quests (later ones level-gated, last is Tribal Knowledge
  38533), Draenor needs the Haephest's Satchel/Fractured Forge Hammer chain
  (quests 34019/33410) or the garrison work-order quest, BfA Hoofplates R3
  needs humanoids killed for item 166260 and the Nazjatar recipes need the
  Nazjatar intro - all require quest-system scripting (or directly LearnSpell
  server-side, which changes what "earned" means but works).
- Cannot reliably farm reputation- or dungeon-gated recipes: Outland plans
  from quartermasters Enuril 19331 / Endarin 19321 (Scryers/Aldor rep) and
  Fedryen 17904 (Cenarion Honored); Stormsteel R3 from Ransa Greyfeather /
  Vindicator Jaelaana at Revered; Felsteel Gloves/Earthpeace plans drop from
  Auchenai Monk 18497 / High Botanist Freywinn 17975 (dungeon content a
  headless character cannot enter without dungeon automation).
- Cannot predict randomness: yellow/green craft counts (every guide warns
  about extras - "usually 17-19", "you will probably have to craft a lot
  more"), and the Hoofplates R3 drop roll (10-20 kills).
- Cannot buy out of the shopping list without AH/mail flows: the guides'
  cheapest routes assume auction house purchases; limited-supply vendor
  plans need vendor-purchase packets plus wait timers (Mithril Scale
  Bracers 30-60 min; Adamantite Cleaver 5-10 min; Lesser Ward of Shielding
  15-60 min).
- Cannot bypass the daily Secrets cooldown (176090, 1 day) or the Draenor
  Ore-trader daily garrison spawn (rotation every 5 days, guide mechanics).
- Cannot get Mark of Honor without PvP: the BfA counts-cheapest route
  (section 3.8 alternative) needs Mark of Honor 137642 from battleground/
  arena wins (the guide's Oribos vendor shortcut post-dates 8.3.7).
- Can fail with a full bag and silently lose reagents (section 7.3) - the
  client player would see an error; a bot must pre-check bag space.
- Easy trap: TWO Alard Schmieds - Northrend Dalaran creature 28694 (teaches
  Northrend line and the 102-recipe list, position [45.5, 28.0]) vs Legion
  Dalaran creature 92183 (the Legion quest chain). The guide warns the
  Broken Isles copy does not teach Northrend recipes. Wrong twin = wrong
  quest flag.
- Reputation discounts (Trainer.cpp line 98) only apply once the faction
  reputation is in the server data - a fresh bot pays full price.
- Tools of the Trade (BfA, at 150) is a quest chain needing Expulsom 152668
  x10, Platinum Ore 152513 x20, Storm Silver 152579 x30 plus quest items
  168136/168137/168135 - quest scripting or direct spell learning required.
