AUDIT - Twink29_Monk_2_Talents.txt
File under audit: 147 lines, step 2 of 3. 18 claims checked: 13 pass,
2 corrected, 3 unverified.
Pages opened for this audit: Tiger's Lust, Chi Wave, Chi Burst, Zen Pulse,
Eye of the Tiger, Ride the Wind, Turbo Fists, Double Barrel, Grapple Weapon,
Counteract Magic and the Monk PvP talent category on warcraft.wiki.gg, plus
the item 20102 page. One web search for BfA-era Mistweaver tier lists.

1. THE SHAPE OF THE ANSWER
   1  PASS  "A level 29 monk has one normal talent." Tiger's Lust's patch
      history carries the proof: 9.0.1 made it "available at level 25 (was
      30)", and 10.0.0 then made it a row 1 talent "was level 25". So in
      BfA that row sat at 30 and a 29 misses it by one level. This is the
      same conclusion the mage file reached by inference; here it is
      verified, which upgrades the mage file by association.
   2  CORRECTED  "one point per row, and you must finish the row above
      before the next one opens" - the second half is wrong. In BfA no
      point had to be spent to unlock the next row; rows opened on level
      alone. Fixed in the file: rows open at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and
      100 and that is the only gate.
   3  PASS  All three specs open at 29 and are chosen at 10. Windwalker,
      Brewmaster and Mistweaver all wear the same armour, so nothing locks
      out a spec at this level.

2. THE LEVEL 15 ROW
   4  PASS  Eye of the Tiger is spell 196607 and it was "a level 15
      Brewmaster and Windwalker talent" in this era - the wiki's own patch
      note for 10.0.0 says so while moving it to row 7. That also corrects
      the id: 196725 was my first guess, the page links 196607.
   5  PASS  Chi Wave is spell 115098. WowDB's entry gives "requires level
      15", and the wiki patch note for 10.0.0 records the move off level 15.
      It was added to Mistweaver in 7.2.0, replacing Mistwalk, so it is a
      legal option for all three specs in BfA.
   6  PASS  Chi Burst is spell 123986, "was a level 15 talent" per the same
      10.0.0 patch note, and WowDB's spell page puts it on Windwalker and
      Mistweaver.
   7  PASS  Mistweaver really has only two options in that row. Zen Pulse
      was removed on 8.0.1 - the spell's patch notes say so in one line -
      and the 8.2 Mistweaver tiers I found list tier 1 as Chi Burst and Chi
      Wave only. The file's "and that is not a typo" survives the audit.
   8  UNVERIFIED  "Chi Burst generated 1 Chi per enemy hit in BfA, which
      made it the best talent in the row." The Chi generation comes from a
      search snippet of a 2018 Windwalker write-up, not from a page I
      opened, and "best talent in the row" is a guide's opinion, not a
      fact. The file attributes it to the guide by name, which is the right
      handling; it stays unverified.

3. PVP TALENTS
   9  PASS  The PvP pane is separate from the normal tree and gives four
      slots at 20, 40, 70 and 110 - carried over from the PvP talent page
      read earlier in this set. One slot at 20, so one PvP talent at 29.
   10 PASS  Turbo Fists, spell 287681, level 20, Windwalker, added in
      8.1.0 so it exists on 8.3.7: Fists of Fury slows by 90% and the monk
      parries while channelling. Read off its page.
   11 PASS  Ride the Wind, spell 201372, "Level 21 Windwalker monk PvP
      talent", converted from a rank 43 honour talent in 8.0.1.
   12 PASS  Double Barrel, spell 202335, "Level 21 Brewmaster", 45 sec
      cooldown, next Keg Smash +50% damage and a 3 sec stun, converted from
      a rank 28 honour talent in 8.0.1.
   13 PASS  Zen Focus Tea: level 20, Mistweaver, silence and interrupt
      immunity for 5 sec off Thunder Focus Tea. Left with no id, correctly -
      the page links 468430, a modern spell id, and the BfA id was not
      found.
   14 PASS, AND THIS IS THE BEST FINDING IN THE FILE  Grapple Weapon, spell
      233759, is a level 40 PvP talent on 8.3.7. Its own page says the
      header "level 20" only because 9.0.1 lowered it "for Windwalker (was
      40)". A reader who trusts the header takes a talent a 29 cannot have.
   15 PASS  Counteract Magic sits at level 30 on the page, so a 29 misses
      it by one level. Its BfA number is unverified, which the file says.
