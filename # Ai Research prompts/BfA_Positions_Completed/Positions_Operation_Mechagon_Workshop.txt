=== 2. HEALTH PERCENTAGES / PHASE TRIGGERS ===

HK-8 (DATA 4):
Phase 1: Tank Buster MK1 active. HK-8 flies overhead, casts Cannon Blast,
Reinforcements.
Phase 2 trigger: MK1 at <2% HP -> MK1 despawns, HK-8 lands at Annihilation
pos,
    channels Annihilation Ray (295939, 3min), Force Shield (300011) active.
    Overcharge Station (152138) becomes clickable - click to Hack (296260).
Phase 3: Hacked -> HK-8 loses Force Shield, gains Haywire (296080, 30s),
falls,
    becomes attackable. After 31s -> Lift Off (301177), returns to Phase 1.
  Hard Mode: Tank Buster MK2 (155760) spawns instead of MK1.

Tussle Tonks (DATA 5):
  Two bosses share HP: Platinum Pummeler (144244) + Gnomercy 4.U. (145185).
No health-based phases. Platinum Plating (282801) at 100 Energy (35s gain), 3
stacks.
  When one dies, other continues. Electrical Storm (1216443) when second dies.

K.U.-J.0. / Kujo (DATA 6):
  No health-based phases. Single phase throughout.
  Wendy Cogsworth (150694) provides voice lines.

Head Machinist Sparkflux (DATA 7):
  Energy-based: gains energy via PERIODIC_ENERGY_GAIN (295065).
  At 100 Energy -> Hidden Flame Cannon (285440), energy resets to 0.
Script has placeholder events for Plants, Discom, Hedge but no initial
ScheduleEvent.
DBM/LW show: Self-Trimming Hedge 3.4s, Plant 5.9s, Discom Bomb 8.3s, Flame
Cannon 11.9s.

King Mechagon (DATA 8):
  Phase 1 (Aerial Unit R-21/X, NPC 150396): King in vehicle.
    Recalibrate, Mega-Zap, Take Off, Cutting Beam.
Phase 2 trigger: Aerial Unit dies -> King exits, enters Omega Buster (144249).
Phase 2 (Omega Buster): Recalibrate (8s repeater), Mega-Zap (3-cast pattern),
    Magneto Arm (30s), Protocol: Ninety-Nine (292290) if no melee in range.
Hard Mode: H.A.R.D.M.O.D.E. (292750) starts ~32s, repeats ~42.5s (mythic
only).
Phase 3 trigger: Omega Buster dies -> King exits, jumps to (699.518, 623.309,
-238.285).
Phase 3: King on foot (script incomplete - no Phase 3 abilities implemented).

=== 4. ASSIGNED-PLAYER MECHANICS & EXACT TRIGGERS ===

HK-8 (DATA 4):
  Stage 1 (MK1 active):
    - Wreck (302279): On current tank every 3s. Tank: use defensive.
    - Fulminating Zap (302274): Random player, spreads. Healer: spot heal.
- Reinforcement Relay (301351): Every 30s, summons Walkie Shockie X1 (163746).
Assigned: DPS kill adds. Shockies cast Area Denial Bot (296517) ->
Self-Destruct (296522, 10s).
- Cannon Blast (295536): HK-8 targets all players with missile. Dodge line.
  Stage 2 (Annihilation):
- Annihilation Ray (295939): HK-8 channels 3min at center stalker. Entire
raid: stack in Anti-Trespass Field (303252) when available.
- Force Shield (300011): HK-8 immune. Click Overcharge Station to Hack
(296260).
      Assigned: 1 player clicks station -> HK-8 falls.
  Stage 3 (Haywire):
    - Haywire (296080): HK-8 takes 100% increased damage for 30s. Burn phase.
    - After 31s -> Lift Off, returns to Stage 1 (repeat).
Hard Mode (MK2): Fulminating Burst (303885) - targeted player must stack with
allies to share damage.

Tussle Tonks (DATA 5):
  Platinum Pummeler:
- Platinum Plating (282801): At 100 Energy (35s gain), 3 stacks, 1s cast.
Interruptible.
      If stunned during cast, recasts after 5s. Assigned: Interrupt / stun.
- Platinum Pummel (1215065): Frontal cone on tank. Tank: dodge or use
defensive.
    - Ground Pound (1215102): AoE on tank/melee. Move out or heal through.
  Gnomercy 4.U.:
- Maximum Thrust (283422): Charges farthest player. Assigned: targeted player
run out.
- B.4.T.T.L.3. Mine (1216431): Places mines. Dodge. Timer varies: 17s, 27.5s,
then 35.2s.
- Foe Flipper (285152): Throws targeted player. Assigned: targeted player move
away.
    - Vent Jets (285388): Chases nearest player. Kite.
Shared: When one boss dies, other continues. When both dead -> Electrical
Storm.

K.U.-J.0. / Kujo (DATA 6):
- Air Drop (291930): Summons Flying Claw (150442) at random pos in
middle_of_room.
      Claw falls, deals damage/knockback on impact. Dodge circles.
- Venting Flames (291946): Cast at Kujo's position, creates shelter. Assigned:
all players move to Kujo (findshelter).
- Explosive Leap (291973): Leaps to farthest player, leaves fire. Assigned:
farthest player run out (runout).
- Blazing Chomp (294929): Tank debuff, stacks. Tank swap at ~2-3 stacks.
Dispellable? (magic).
    - Junk Bomb (291953): From Flying Claw. Avoid.

Head Machinist Sparkflux (DATA 7):
- Hidden Flame Cannon (285440): At 100 Energy, 14.5s cast, targets random
area. Dodge.
- Discom-BOMB-ulator (285454): Random player, magic debuff. Assigned: dispel
(RemoveMagic).
- Activate Plant (294853): Summons Inconspicuous Plant (152033). Plant casts
Blossom Blast (294855).
    - Self-Trimming Hedge (294954): AoE damage. Spread.
    - Electrified Floor (1213154): Ground damage. Move out.

King Mechagon (DATA 8):
  Phase 1 (Aerial Unit):
- Recalibrate (291865): Plasma Orbs (150485) charge farthest player. Dodge.
    - Mega-Zap (291928): Random player, spreads. Assigned: run out from group.
- Take Off (291613): Aerial Unit flies up, then Cutting Beam (291626) sweeps
arena. Run from beam.
- Cutting Beam (291626): 6s channel, line AoE. Assigned: targeted player run,
others avoid.
  Phase 2 (Omega Buster):
    - Recalibrate: Now 8s repeater (was 20s). More frequent orb charges.
- Mega-Zap: 3-cast sequence (14.8s gap, then 3.5s, 3.5s, then 28.6s gap...).
- Magneto Arm (283143): Pulls all Plasma Orbs to Omega Buster, then pulls
players. Run away.
- Protocol: Ninety-Nine (292290): If no player in melee range, casts deadly
AoE. Tank stay in melee.
- Hard Mode (mythic): H.A.R.D.M.O.D.E. (292750) - button sequence. Assigned:
click buttons in order.
  Phase 3 (King on foot): Script incomplete - no abilities.

=== 5. SAFE SPOTS / POSITIONING (z = map units from script) ===

HK-8:
  Stage 1: Tank on MK1. Melee behind MK1. Ranged spread for Fulminating Zap.
  Cannon Blast: Move perpendicular to missile line (farfromline).
  Reinforcements: Kill Walkie Shockies before Self-Destruct (10s).
  Stage 2: Stack at Overcharge Station for Hack -> HK-8 falls.
  Annihilation Ray: Stack in Anti-Trespass Field (303252) when available.
  Stage 3 (Haywire): Stack on HK-8 for burn. Spread for Lift Off.

Tussle Tonks:
  Platinum Pummeler: Tank front, melee behind. Dodge Pummel frontal.
  Ground Pound: Move >8yd from Pummeler.
  Platinum Plating: Interrupt 1s cast. Stun delays recast by 5s.
  Gnomercy: Maximum Thrust targeted player runs to edge.
  Foe Flipper: Targeted player runs away from group.
  B.4.T.T.L.3. Mine: Watch ground, avoid mines.
  Vent Jets: Kite Gnomercy around arena.

K.U.-J.0. / Kujo:
  Tank on Kujo. Melee behind.
  Air Drop: Dodge Flying Claw impact circles (random in middle_of_room).
  Venting Flames: Stack ON Kujo (findshelter) - creates safe zone.
  Explosive Leap: Farthest player runs out (runout), Kujo leaps to them.
  Blazing Chomp: Tank swap at 2-3 stacks. Dispel magic if possible.

Head Machinist Sparkflux:
  Spread for Discom-BOMB-ulator (magic dispel).
  Hidden Flame Cannon: Dodge targeted area (14.5s cast bar).
  Plants: Kill Inconspicuous Plant quickly (Blossom Blast targets random).
  Self-Trimming Hedge: Spread 8yd.
  Electrified Floor: Move off electrified tiles.

King Mechagon:
  Phase 1: Spread for Mega-Zap. Dodge Recalibrate orb charges (farthest).
  Take Off -> Cutting Beam: Run perpendicular to beam sweep.
  Phase 2: Same but faster Recalibrate (8s). Mega-Zap 3-cast pattern.
  Magneto Arm: Run away from Omega Buster (pulls players + orbs).
  Protocol: Ninety-Nine: Tank MUST stay in melee range.
  Hard Mode: Click buttons in sequence shown on Annihilo-tron 5000.
