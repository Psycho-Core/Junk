BATTLE FOR AZEROTH 8.3.7 (BUILD 35662) - ENCHANTING CRAFTING FOR A SERVER-SIDE COMPANION
===========================================================================================

Scope: how a computer-controlled character (no client) levels Enchanting on a
TrinityCore-based 8.3.7 server. Every id below was read from a source this
week; anything without a readable source says "unverified" instead of a guess.

weapons; Essence - mostly uncommon weapons, less often uncommon armor

(weapons skew essences), shards from rare, crystals from epic; since level
scaling hit old zones "you can no longer sort enchanting results just based
on item level" (wiki patch 7.3.5: materials "now based on the item's
expansion level instead of item level").

7. WHAT THE CORE MUST DO FOR A CRAFT TO SUCCEED
-----------------------------------------------
Verified against TrinityCore master fetched this week (file/line numbers
below are from that fetch; paths under src/server/game/):
a) Learn press: NPCHandler.cpp 130 HandleTrainerBuySpellOpcode -> 145
   interaction guard -> 155 trainer->TeachSpell; Trainer.cpp 76 TeachSpell
   -> 79 CanTeachSpell -> 149 CanTeachSpell body: spell state must be
   Available (172 GetSpellState: known check, race/class 179, skill
   181-183, prerequisite spells 185-187, level 189-191, rank-chain logic
   193+), free primary profession point (156/165), money at 100-106 with
   reputation discount 98; then IsCastable -> CastSpell or LearnSpell.
   For Enchanting this is how 7411, the 8.0.1 wrappers (264457, 264461,
   264463, 264466, 264468, 264470, 264472), BfA 264473/265805 and every
   trainer-taught formula enter the character.
b) Casting an enchant recipe: SpellEffects.cpp 2463
   EffectEnchantItemPerm (registered for effect 53 SPELL_EFFECT_ENCHANT_ITEM
   at table line 145): needs an item target; if the target IsVellum (2476)
   it destroys one vellum, creates the scroll via DoCreateItem
   (effectInfo->ItemType, Trade_Skill context) and clears the item target;
   otherwise it reads enchant_id = effectInfo->MiscValue, looks up
   SpellItemEnchantmentEntry (2494-2498) and applies it. Reagents are taken
   in Spell::cast before effects: Spell.cpp 3871/3876 TakeReagents()
   (definition 5599). DoCreateItem must report inventory full (1440
   EQUIP_ERR_INV_FULL || EQUIP_ERR_ITEM_MAX_COUNT -> SendEquipError).
   Recipes print 2-second cast (7418, 255103) and tools "Runed Enchanting
   Rod" (wowhead Tools field on both; wiki patch 5.0.4: "All enchantments
   now only require Runed Copper Rod").
c) Cast-time target checks in Spell::CheckCast (Spell.cpp):
   - Vellum branch of SPELL_EFFECT_ENCHANT_ITEM (7664-7678): target must
     exist with an effect ItemType; vellum must be owned by the caster
     (7668-7670 "cannot enchant vellum for other player" ->
     SPELL_FAILED_NOT_TRADEABLE); casting a scroll-produced item onto a
     vellum is blocked (7671-7674, ITEM_FLAG_NO_REAGENT_COST ->
     SPELL_FAILED_TOTEM_CATEGORY); space must exist for the scroll
     (CanStoreNewItem).
   - Item target required (7687 SPELL_FAILED_ITEM_NOT_FOUND).
   - Level floor: unless SPELL_ATTR2_ALLOW_LOW_LEVEL_BUFF, the target's
     required level (or item level if none) must reach the spell's
     BaseLevel (7691-7697 -> SPELL_FAILED_LOWLEVEL).
   - Level cap: when casting from an item (scroll, m_CastItem) or prismatic
     effect, target item level above the spell's MaxLevel ->
     SPELL_FAILED_HIGHLEVEL (7699-7701). This is what backs the printed
     "Cannot be applied to items higher than level 120" (7418, cata
     scrolls 52750-52753, BfA 255103 all print a level-120 cap; classic
     example "items higher than level 136" on wowpedia's Spellpower page).
   - On-use conflict: enchant type USE_SPELL onto an item that already has
     an on-use -> SPELL_FAILED_ON_USE_ENCHANT (7716-7723); socket-full ->
     SPELL_FAILED_MAX_SOCKETS (7728-7736); foreign trade-slot item with
     soulbound enchant flag -> SPELL_FAILED_NOT_TRADEABLE (7741-7746).
   - Temporary enchants repeat the level floor/cap (7754-7777).
d) Skill-ups on a successful gear enchant: SpellEffects.cpp 2490-2492
   ("// do not increase skill if vellum used" comment sits above
   `if (!m_CastItem) player->UpdateCraftSkill(m_spellInfo);` - i.e. skill
   is granted for direct gear casts only when not cast from an item;
   the vellum branch at 2476-2486 makes no UpdateCraftSkill call at all).
   UpdateCraftSkill: Player.cpp 5410, early-out SPELL_ATTR1_NO_SKILL_
   INCREASE 5412, SkillLineAbility lookup 5419-5424, discovery branch
   MECHANIC_DISCOVERY 5427-5431, gain = NumSkillUps x
   CONFIG_SKILL_GAIN_CRAFTING 5433, chance via SkillGainChance over the
   row's TrivialSkillLineRankHigh/Low then UpdateSkillPro 5435-5437.
   Other call sites: DoCreateItem success 1469, EffectCreateItem2 loot
   crafting 1499, disenchant 3533, and Spell.cpp 3864 for item-cast
   skills (SPELL_ATTR9_ITEM_CAST_GRANTS_SKILL_GAIN). Colour probabilities
   (orange/yellow/green/grey) are the SkillLineAbility row plus
   SkillGainChance; wiki definitions: orange always, yellow probably,
   green probably not, grey never.
e) Disenchant craft: spell 13262 (learned with the profession - DE guide);
   handler table SpellEffects.cpp 191 = EffectDisEnchant for effect 99
   SPELL_EFFECT_DISENCHANT; body 3525-3538: skill via UpdateCraftSkill
   3533 unless cast from an item, then FillLoot on the item's
   GetDisenchantLootId() with LootTemplates_Disenchant (LOOT_DISENCHANTING)
   and SendLoot. CheckCast SPELL_EFFECT_DISENCHANT (Spell.cpp 7784-7804):
   item target exists, belongs to the caster (no trade-slot DE,

8. WHAT A SERVER-SIDE CHARACTER (NO CLIENT) CANNOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------
- Enchant the Blood Elf bonus into its stats: Arcane Affinity 28877 is a
  racial Apply Aura: Mod Skill (Enchanting) value 5 (wowhead; "Enchanting
  skill increased by 5"). A non-Blood-Elf server-side character never gets
  the +5 orange-extension; the wiki's "+10" text (section 9 item 3) must
  not be implemented as-is. Wowhead also lists Dracthyr Visage (races 75/76)
  as having the spell - on a companion you still have to grant the aura
  yourself, races considered.
- Cross the reputation/time gates without code support: Tortollan
  Revered (Kojo formulas), Nightfallen/Dreamweavers Exalted (R3 Bindings),
  Dreamweavers Honored (128599), Argussian Reach Friendly (Chaos Shatter),
  Revered Voldunai/Order of Embers/Talanji's/Proudmoore (weapon formulas),
  limited-supply vendor respawns (Madame Ruby 5-10 minutes), World Quest
  rotation (BfA R3 work orders; Legion WQs every 2-3 days; Uniting Kul
  Tiras/Zandalar war-campaign prequests; Uniting the Isles 43341 at 45),
  Void Sphere's 2-day cooldown route, and daily/lockout content - all are
  external schedules a plain server-side script cannot pass by clicking.
- Enter the places the routes name without teleport/instance support:
  Annora is inside Uldaman and "only appears when room is cleared"
  (wiki); the Legion chain needs Darkheart Thicket, Halls of Valor,
  Neltharion's Lair, The Stonecore, Scholomance, Black Rook Hold mobs and
  Suramar patrol loot; the Draenor scroll chain needs zone drops/quest
  mobs; Raid Finder farming needs the queue NPC path (Archmage Timear).
  None of these are craft actions - they are world actions.
- Trade and auction: the guides' material lists assume Auction House
  buying; wiki Trading says vellums enable AH sale/mail of enchants. A
  server-side character has no AH/mail UI - it must DE its own stock or
  hit vendor lists (section 4) - and the TC vellum path grants no skill
  point (section 7d), so AH-style skill grinding by vellum spam does not
  work on this core even if a UI existed.
- Enchant other people's gear: soulbound-flag enchants on foreign items
  fail (Spell.cpp 7746), vellums owned by others fail (7670), DE of
  trade-slot items fails (7790-7794), scroll-to-vellum exploit fails
  (7671). Also cannot enchant past the scroll item-level cap (7701) or
  under the spell's BaseLevel floor (7696), and cannot add on-use enchants
  to on-use items (7723).
- Hold more than two primary professions: CanTeachSpell's free primary
  profession point gate (Trainer.cpp 156) - a companion that already has
  two professions is Unavailable for the Enchanting first-rank spells.
- Read the client's profession UI: rank-star hover sources, red/yellow/
  green recipe colours in the window, guard map-markers, and the
  "disenchant loot box" picking UI (TC SendLoot the packet still needs a
  scripted claim) are UI features; the data behind them (colours, ranks,
  sources) must be carried server-side from the dbc/guides instead.
- Rely on wiki "Any trainer teaches any skill level" (4.0.3a) without
  checking: the wiki table still marks many trainers "Teaches only
  Apprentice" (stale rows) while the patch note says ranks were removed -
  use the patch note, and treat the Apprentice-only notes as historic.
