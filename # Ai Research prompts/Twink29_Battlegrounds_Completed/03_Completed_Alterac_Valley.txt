OPEN. Alterac Valley is part of the 20-29 bracket in 8.3.7 (build 35662): 40 vs 40 (BattlemasterList 1 "Alterac Valley": MaxPlayers 40 PER TEAM, MinLevel 20, MaxLevel 120, InstanceType 3, GroupsAllowed 1, MaxGroupSize 5), the 20-29 range is PVPDifficulty 173 (map 30, RangeIndex 0), it is a normal (unrated) queue; rated play is a separate list (BattlemasterList 102, MinLevel 100), so a level 29 can never be rated. Minimum 10 per team to start (MinPlayers 10), RatedPlayers 25, HolidayWorldState 1941.

EXTRA BRACKET FACTS: experience-off characters queue only against other experience-off characters (rule from patch 3.2.0), so a 29 twink meets twinks. Per the 5.2.0 rule a character is scaled up to the top of the bracket (a 20 scaled toward 29); whether your core implements that is unverified. Note the practical consequence for bots: 40v40 with only a 10-per-team minimum still needs 20 players queueing, so a private server will rarely fire a 20-29 Alterac Valley at all, and the NPC levels (guards, marshals, captains, bosses) come from your world database and are not scaled by the battleground code - whether a 61-63 boss is killable by level 29s on your server is unverified and is the first thing to test before scripting behaviour for this map.


1. OBJECTIVE AND WIN CONDITION, WITH THE NUMBERS
- Both sides start with 600 reinforcements (BG_AV_SCORE_INITIAL_POINTS = 600). A team that reaches 0 loses; UpdateScore ends the battleground and awards the win to the other team.
- Player deaths cost reinforcements: every player death subtracts 1 from that player's own team (HandleKillPlayer calls UpdateScore(player->GetTeam(), -1)).
- Towers and bunkers: destroying one costs its owner 75 reinforcements (BG_AV_RES_TOWER = 75), gives the destroyer 3 bonus-kill honour and 12 reputation (BG_AV_KILL_TOWER = 3, BG_AV_REP_TOWER = 12).
- Captain: killing the enemy captain costs that team 100 reinforcements (BG_AV_RES_CAPTAIN = 100), gives 3 bonus-kill honour and 125 reputation (BG_AV_REP_CAPTAIN = 125).
- Boss: killing the enemy boss ends the battleground immediately in the killer's favour (HandleKillUnit calls EndBattleground for Vanndar 11948 or Drek'Thar 11946), with 4 bonus-kill honour and 350 reputation (BG_AV_KILL_BOSS = 4, BG_AV_REP_BOSS = 350).
- Near-lose warning at 120 reinforcements (SEND_MSG_NEAR_LOSE = 120, broadcasts 23210 Alliance and 23211 Horde).
- End-of-match bonuses for what survived: each surviving tower gives its owner 2 bonus-kill honour and 12 reputation (BG_AV_KILL_SURVIVING_TOWER / BG_AV_REP_SURVIVING_TOWER), a surviving captain gives 2 bonus-kill honour and 125 reputation (BG_AV_KILL_SURVIVING_CAPTAIN / BG_AV_REP_SURVIVING_CAPTAIN).
- The mines are the only source of reinforcements: an owned mine adds 1 reinforcement to its owner every 45 s (AV_MINE_TICK_TIMER = 45000, UpdateScore(owner, 1)). Mines can be recaptured by the neutral team after 20 minutes (AV_MINE_RECLAIM_TIMER = 1200000, marked @todo in the core, so treat it as a placeholder value).
- Captain buffs: while the captain is alive the core casts 23693 (Alliance, Balinda) or 22751 (Horde, Galvangar) on the whole team every 120 s plus a random 0-4 minutes (m_CaptainBuffTimer = 120000 + urand(0,4)*60000). Both the interval and the random part are marked @todo in the core, so treat the exact period as unverified.
- Graveyard capture times: 4 minutes (BG_AV_CAPTIME = 240000) for a graveyard that has had an owner, 5 minutes for Snowfall's first capture (BG_AV_SNOWFALL_FIRSTCAP = 300000). The timer runs on the node and is not tied to the player who clicked, exactly like Arathi Basin.
- There is no time limit in the core for this battleground: the match ends on reinforcements or on a boss kill. (The 47-minute check in Battleground.cpp is arena-only.)


2. THE MAP
Map id 30. The battleground is a long north-south valley: the Alliance hold Dun Baldar in the south-east, the Horde hold Frostwolf Keep in the north-west. The corridor between them is the whole map, and the fighting moves along it from one end to the other. All coordinates are map 30, game coordinates (x, y, z).

GRAVEYARD RULES
- Graveyards are captured by clicking their flag; the node becomes contested for the capture time above and then flips. A graveyard is the respawn point for whoever owns it, so the front line follows the graveyards.
- A player who dies respawns at the closest graveyard his team owns, using the same resurrection batching as every battleground: 30 s (RESURRECTION_INTERVAL = 30000 in Battleground.h).
- Spirit guide and resurrection spells are the shared ones: 22011, 22012, 44535, 2584, 24171.
- Snowfall starts neutral, so its first capture takes 5 minutes; every later capture of any graveyard takes 4.


3. ROLES, SPLIT, OPENING 30 SECONDS, HOLDING THE MIDDLE, WHEN TO PUSH
Team size 40 (20 minimum). Everything in this section is estimate except where a number is quoted from section 1.
- 8-12 tower group ("O"): the players who go for Icewing/Stoneheart bunkers or Iceblood Tower/Tower Point. At 29 these are the objectives that actually decide games, because 4 towers is 300 reinforcements.
- 6-10 graveyard fighters: the group that takes and holds Stormpike or Iceblood, and then Stoneheart.
- 4-6 defence: one or two per owned graveyard plus the tower/bunker guards.
- 2-4 miners/roamers: players who flip the north or south mine for the +1 per 45 s and who call incoming.
- 3-5 healers total; at 40 players that is thin, but 29 twink groups rarely bring more.
- 1-2 boss runners late: a small group that goes for Vanndar or Drek'Thar only after the enemy team has collapsed.

Opening 30 seconds (estimate):
- 0-3 s: gates open, everyone mounts (riding 33388; mount availability at 29 unverified).
- 3-30 s: the main body runs the road toward Snowfall/Stoneheart (about 785-800 yd from the base, about 70-72 s mounted), the tower group splits off to the nearest enemy tower, and 2-4 players go to the nearest mine. The first contact is at Snowfall at about 70-90 s.
- The first graveyard capture lands at roughly 5-7 minutes (4-5 minute capture timer plus the fight), which is the first real score swing of the match.

Holding the middle:
- In Alterac Valley "the middle" is Snowfall plus Stoneheart: Snowfall is the neutral graveyard in the valley floor, Stoneheart is the graveyard between Snowfall and the Alliance half. Holding both means the enemy has to fight 300-500 yd from its own respawn.
- Holding the middle is done with the graveyard, not with a kill count: as long as your graveyard is yours, your dead come back 30 s later and 100 yd away, while the enemy's come back at their base.

When a team pushes:
- Push a tower/bunker when its marshal pack is dead or the enemy is not defending it; a tower is 75 reinforcements and cannot be recaptured, so it is worth more than any number of kills.
- Push the enemy base when the enemy has lost its forward graveyards: with no forward graveyard the defenders respawn at their relief hut and the attackers keep the field.
- Do not push the boss while the enemy still has a captain and towers up unless the group is 10+ and the enemy is elsewhere; the boss ends the game, so it is the last push, not the first.
- When behind on reinforcements, stack the mine and towers, never the boss: 75 per tower plus 1 per 45 s per mine is the only comeback path.


4. WHAT DECIDES A TWINK FIGHT IN ALTERAC VALLEY
- There is no flag carrier in Alterac Valley; the "handoff" equivalent is graveyard control and the tower race. Bots should treat a graveyard flag as the objective, not a player.
- Burst on the graveyard clicker: killing the player does not stop the 4-minute timer, so the fight is about clearing the flag area and clicking it back, exactly like Arathi Basin.
- Crowd control at the flag and on the ramp: a root or fear that keeps the defender off the flag radius decides the node.
- Interrupts and healing: a 29 healer has one long heal; one interrupt decides most small fights in a tower or on a flag.
- NPC packs: every tower and graveyard has guards and, at the towers, a marshal; at 29 the guards' level comes from your world database (unverified). If your NPCs are not scaled, bots must pull them in small groups rather than charge in.
- Kiting and line of sight: the valley is open ground with ridges; hunters and frost mages dominate the road, while melee needs the towers and the base interiors to fight in.
- Stealth: rogues and druids decide tower races - a stealthed player can take a tower's flag while the enemy group is on the road.
- Numbers: 40v40 means a 3-player advantage at a tower is decisive, and a 10-player group that stays together beats 20 players arriving one at a time.
- Gear and level in the bracket: heirlooms, one PvP talent slot at 29, enchants and consumables matter as much as class.


5. HOW A HUMAN BEHAVES INSTEAD OF A SCRIPT (all frequencies and times in this section are estimate)
- Time stopped to fight near an objective: a graveyard fight lasts 20-60 s; tower fights 30-90 s because of the NPC packs. After a win players stand around the flag for 5-20 s before moving.
- Chasing off the route: about 1 in 3 players chases 40-100 yd off the road; in Alterac Valley the road makes this expensive, so the chaser usually dies to the next group coming down it.
- Going the wrong way: 2-4 players per team per match take a 30-90 s detour, most often into the wrong tower or down the wrong side of the valley after a respawn.
- Leaving an objective undefended: at 40 players this is constant - estimate: in most matches at least one owned graveyard has no defender for 30 s or more, and towers are usually left with only their NPC guards.
- Arriving late: the first wave reaches Snowfall 70-90 s after the gates open; stragglers arrive 20-60 s behind. A death costs the 30 s resurrection wait plus a 60-132 s run (419-927 yd) at 29, so 90-160 s of absence - the single biggest reason a 29 AV feels empty.
- Running into a group: solo players walk into 5+ enemies at least 2-4 times per match, most often on the road between Snowfall and Stoneheart.
- Reaction to a tower being attacked: 5-10 s to notice the yell and the map icon, then 20-40 s to decide to go; usually only 2-5 players rotate because the rest are on the road.
- Reaction to a graveyard capture in progress: a defender within 30 yd almost always clicks back within 3-8 s; a defender who is dead does not return in time against a 4-minute timer.
- Grouping: 40-player teams break into 4-8 loose packs of 3-8 players; only the tower groups stay together for more than a minute.
- Boss attempts: humans start asking for a boss push only after the enemy has lost at least two towers or its captain (estimate).
- Mine behaviour: 1-3 players per team care about the mine; the rest ignore it.


6. COMMON MISTAKES OF AVERAGE PLAYERS HERE
- Fighting on the road instead of taking towers: kills cost the enemy 1 reinforcement each, a tower costs 75.
- Leaving towers with only NPC guards and no player, so a single stealth class takes 75 reinforcements for free.
- Not clicking a contested graveyard flag back, or clicking and leaving before the 4-minute timer finishes.
- Pushing the boss too early and wiping at 29 against NPCs that were never pulled or scaled.
- Ignoring the mine for the whole match: 1 reinforcement per 45 s is small, but two mines for 20 minutes is hundreds.
- Dying in the enemy half: the 419-927 yd run back means the team is 2 minutes short for every death far from home.
- Splitting into four groups of ten that each lose to the enemy's one group of twenty.
- Pulling a marshal's pack with the whole tower group and dying to the NPCs before the enemy even arrives.
- Chasing a runner off the road into the next enemy graveyard.
- Letting Snowfall stay neutral for the first 5 minutes instead of taking the free middle graveyard.
- No call-outs, so the tower group and the road group never combine.
- Fighting for a graveyard that is already lost instead of falling back to the next one while the timer runs.
