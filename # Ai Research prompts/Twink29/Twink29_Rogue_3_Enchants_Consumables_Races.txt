TWINK29 - ROGUE - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, PROFESSIONS, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
Sources: the profession files in Twink29_Professions-completed (Enchanting,
Alchemy, Inscription, Cooking and First Aid, Engineering, Leatherworking),
the rogue item and talent steps, and the wiki pages for Deadly Poison,
Wound Poison and Crippling Poison read for this file.
V = read. Where a number was not read it says unverified.

0. THE SHAPE OF THIS FILE
   A rogue is the melee mirror of the warlock: every enchant in the cap
   that matters to it exists, because the Classic enchant list was built
   for melee first. Gloves, cloak and weapon all take an Agility or damage
   enchant inside 225, which no caster in this set can say. What the rogue
   does not have is its poisons, unless it is Assassination - see section
   4, which overturns the assumption this step was written under.

1. ENCHANTS - ONE PER SLOT, ALL INSIDE SKILL 225
CLOAK|Enchant Cloak - Lesser Agility|13882|+3 Agility|skill 225|V
   2x Lesser Nether Essence 11174, Runed Truesilver Rod. The formula is
   item 11206 and it drops, so the enchanter has to own it. Cheapest
   Agility point per slot in the bracket.
CLOAK|Enchant Cloak - Greater Defense|13746|+50 armour|skill 205|V
   The alternative: a rogue that expects to be trained takes the armour.
   Two cloaks, one of each, is the practical answer.
CHEST|Enchant Chest - Greater Health|13640|+35 health|skill 160|V
   No Agility or Stamina chest enchant inside 225.
WRIST|Enchant Bracer - Strength|13661|+5 Strength|skill 180|V
   The only in-cap bracer enchant a melee can use, and it is the wrong
   stat: a rogue's attack power comes from Agility. +5 Strength is not
   nothing, but this is the weakest slot on the list and the one to skip
   if gold is short. No Agility bracer enchant inside 225 was found.
HANDS|Enchant Gloves - Agility|13815|+5 Agility|skill 210|V
   1x Lesser Nether Essence 11174, 1x Vision Dust 11137.
LEGS|Heavy Armor Kit|4265|+24 armour|item level 30, req 20|V
   Not an enchant: usable on chest, legs, hands or feet at item level 15
   or higher. No Agility leg enchant inside the cap.
FEET|Enchant Boots - Minor Speed|13890|movement speed|skill 225|V
   The most valuable enchant here, and doubly so for a rogue: it stacks
   with the run speed on the Arathi boots 20114/20188 in effect if not in
   wording - that stacking is unverified, and the percentage is unverified
   in this build, as in every file in this set.
WEAPON|Enchant Weapon - Striking|13693|+3 weapon damage|skill 195|V
   Flat damage on every swing, on both weapons. For a class whose off
   hand swings constantly to apply poison, +3 on the off hand is worth
   more than it looks on paper.
OFF|Enchant Weapon - Striking|13693|same enchant, second weapon|V
   A rogue enchants both weapons; no other class in this set pays for
   two weapon enchants, and it is the rogue's largest single gold cost.
NECK|190894 and RING|190871 / 191014 - current-expansion enchanter-only
   enchants, ALT, effects unverified, unusable with only Classic skill.
   ALT LIST, above the cap and needing a higher-level enchanter: 13939
   Bracer - Greater Strength (240), 13943 Weapon - Greater Striking
   (245), 13898 Weapon - Fiery Weapon (265), 20012 Gloves - Greater
   Agility (270), 20031 Weapon - Superior Striking (300), 25080 Gloves -
   Superior Agility (300). The two glove enchants and Fiery Weapon are
   the rogue-relevant ones; Fiery is the first proc enchant a rogue can
   reach, and it is 40 skill above the cap.

2. CONSUMABLES
   BATTLE ELIXIR
     Elixir of Agility 8949, +15 Agility for 1 hour, requires 27,
     spell 11328 (V). The primary-stat elixir the class wants, and the
     Alchemy file's first pick for a rogue. Its item level is 37, above
     the gear cap in step 1, but that cap was a gear rule, not a
     consumable rule, and the required level is what gates drinking it.
     Elixir of the Mongoose 13452 (+25 Agility, +2% crit) requires 46
     (V): out.
   GUARDIAN ELIXIR
     Elixir of Greater Defense 8951, +250 armour for 1 hour, requires
     exactly 29 (V). Elixir of Fortitude 3825 (+120 health, requires 25)
     is the cheap alternative; a rogue's survival problem is usually
     being caught out of stealth, and armour helps less than health
     there, so Fortitude is the better buy for this class.
   POTIONS
     Swiftness Potion 2459, requires 5 (V): the flag-run and escape
       button, and the Alchemy file pairs it with this class directly.
     Free Action Potion 5634, requires 20 (V): no root or slow for 30
       sec. For a rogue this is the anti-kite potion.
     Greater Healing Potion 1710, requires 21 (V).
     Shadow Protection Potion 6048, Frost Protection 6050 (V).
     All potions share one cooldown, so a bag of them is one potion.
   NO WEAPON OIL  The wizard and mana oils are Intellect and Spirit; no
     Agility or damage oil exists in the audited Alchemy file, and a
     rogue's weapon coating is a poison, not an oil - section 4. A rogue
     that is not Assassination has nothing to put on its blades at all.
   SCROLLS
     Scroll of Agility II, item id unverified, +5 Agility for 30 min.
     Scroll of Stamina II 1711, +5 Sta, requires 15 (V, Inscription file
     rank II rows at skill 75). A scroll does not stack with a class buff
     giving the same stat.
   FOOD
     Roast Raptor 12210, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit for 15 min, requires
     25 (V). The Spirit is dead weight; the food is taken for Stamina.
     A food with Agility at this level was not found in the Cooking file,
     so this is the honest best rather than the ideal one.
   BANDAGES
     8544 Mageweave Bandage, heals 1600 over 8 sec, Tailoring 240 (V):
       bought, not made. A bandage matters more to a rogue than to most:
       Vanish plus a bandage is the class's only self-heal at 29, and
       that combination was not read anywhere, so it is stated as the
       reason to carry them, not as a verified mechanic.
     6451 Heavy Silk Bandage, heals 1280, Tailoring 210 (V).

3. PROFESSIONS
   ENGINEERING  The rogue pick, and the Engineering file says why in its
     own class table: a grenade stun into an opener, and Discombobulator
     Ray 4388, which reduces a target's damage. Iron Grenade 4390 and Big
     Iron Bomb 4394 give a 3 sec stun, Minor Recombobulator 4381 (needs
     Engineering 140, 10 charges) undoes a polymorph, and the bombs share
     a cooldown so they cannot be chained (V, Engineering file). For a
     class whose whole fight is the first five seconds, a stun that does
     not cost energy is the best profession item in the bracket.
   LEATHERWORKING  The self-supply choice: 18948 Barbaric Bracers are a
     Leatherworking item (spell 23399), the step 1 wrist pick, and the
     armour kits come from the same profession. Skinning funds it.
   ENCHANTING  Supplies 13815, 13882 and two lots of 13693, which is the
     most expensive enchant loadout in this set; a rogue enchanter pays
     for himself faster than any caster does.
   ALCHEMY  Elixir of Agility 8949 and the Swiftness potions. The
     Alchemy file calls it the cheapest profession to level in the set.
   RECOMMENDATION  Engineering + Leatherworking for a played character;
     Engineering + Enchanting if the bots must be self-sufficient.
     Blacksmithing gives a rogue nothing it can wear.

4. POISONS - THE SECTION THIS STEP ASKED FOR, AND THE ANSWER CHANGED
   The prompt asks which poisons to carry and where to buy the
   ingredients. In 8.3.7 there are no ingredients and nothing to buy:
   poisons stopped being crafted items in 5.0.4 and became spellbook
   abilities that coat both weapons at once (V). And they are not a class
   feature any more - they are an Assassination feature.
     Deadly Poison, Assassination, learned at level 10 (V): Lethal, lasts
       1 hour, each strike has a 30% chance to apply Nature damage over
       12 sec. An 8.0.1-era hotfix (2018-08-10) makes it deal 20% less
       damage against players (V), which is the number that matters here.
       Spell id unverified.
     Wound Poison, Assassination only, learned at level 25 (V): Lethal,
       so it replaces Deadly rather than stacking with it. Reduces
       healing received by 25%, and by 20% in PvP (24% with Master
       Poisoner), per a 2017 hotfix (V); it does not stack in this build -
       the stacking version is 9.0.1. This is the battleground poison:
       against a team with a healer it is worth more than Deadly's damage.
     Crippling Poison, Assassination only, and NOT available at 29: it
       was moved to level 38 in 7.1.5 (V). Its slow is 50%, and 30% in
       PvP. Every guide that tells a 29 rogue to run Crippling is reading
       a build where it came back to all rogues at low level (9.0.1 made
       it a level 33 all-rogue ability; 10.0.0 lowered it to 10).
     Outlaw and Subtlety have no poisons at all in 8.3.7. Their weapons
       carry nothing - no poison, no oil - which is a real disadvantage
       the item files cannot fix and the talent file's spec choice should
       know about.
   PRACTICAL ANSWER  An Assassination bot carries Wound Poison for
     battlegrounds and Deadly Poison for anything without a healer, and
     re-coats after every death, because the coating lasts an hour but
     not through a resurrection - that last clause is unverified.

5. RACES
   Which races could be rogues in 8.3.7 is not verified (the wiki race
   pages are current-expansion pages), so the membership of this list is
   unverified and the racial effects are the verified part.
   VERIFIED OR READ EARLIER IN THIS SET
     Undead    Will of the Forsaken, 2 min cooldown, removes charm, fear
       and sleep (V, warlock file). The strongest rogue racial here for
       the same reason it is the strongest warlock one: a feared rogue
       loses its opener and its target.
     Human     59752 Will to Survive, a self crowd-control break (V,
       paladin set).
     Orc       Blood Fury, 2 min cooldown: for a rogue it raises attack
       power, not Intellect (V - the Blood Fury page lists rogues among
       the attack power classes). The burst pick.
     Blood elf Arcane Torrent, 2 min cooldown: removes 1 beneficial magic
       effect from all enemies within 8 yards and restores energy for a
       rogue - the energy figure was not read for this class, so only the
       purge is claimed (V for the purge, warlock file).
   NAMED, IDS AND FIGURES UNVERIFIED
     Gnome     Escape Artist, a snare break - the Alliance rogue classic.
     Night elf Shadowmeld, a stealth reset.
     Troll     Berserking, haste on demand.
     Goblin    Rocket Jump, a leap.
     Void elf  Spatial Rift, a teleport.
     Mag'har   Ancestral Call, a stat gain.
     Zandalari Regeneratin', a heal channel - the one that has to survive
       being hit, which a rogue in stealth can arrange.
     Dark iron Fireblood, a debuff purge and stat gain.
   PICKS  Undead for Horde, Gnome or Human for Alliance, Orc if the bot
     is scripted to burst on a cooldown. Avoid nothing outright; the
     weakest rogue racials are the passive ones, and no passive racial
     was read closely enough to name one as a trap.

7. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT HAVE
   An Agility bracer enchant inside 225 (Strength only).
   An Agility leg enchant (an armour kit instead).
   A weapon oil (poisons instead, and only for one spec).
   Poisons for Outlaw or Subtlety, or Crippling Poison at 29.
   A ring or neck enchant a 29 can apply (enchanter-only, ALT).
   A verified rogue race list for 8.3.7.
   The gaps are different from the casters': a rogue can enchant almost
   everything it wears, and what it is missing is not an enchant at all
   but a class feature that two of its three specs do not have in this
   build.
