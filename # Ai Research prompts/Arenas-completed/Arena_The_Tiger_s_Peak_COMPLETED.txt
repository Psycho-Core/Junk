=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

Two tiger statues and two raised platforms. Both wiki and press
agree on the count and on where they sit:

  "The map features two statues that function as pillars, as
   well as two raised platforms that can be reached by a set of
   stairs on the outer side of each platform."   (Liquipedia)

  "an open circle, with two smaller half circles that will allow
   some LoS on each side ... two raised platforms on the left and
   right side of the arena that can only be climbed via a set of
   stairs on the side facing the wall ... a fence around the side
   of the platform facing the center ... too high to jump from
   the arena to the platform ... the fence does not block line of
   sight"   (MMO-Champion preview, 2013-05-11)

  "two tiger statues, facing each other ... the run from the
   door to the tiger statue is very short ... at the other two
   corners of the map, are two raised platforms"   (Engadget
   patch 5.3 PTR preview)

Reading that against the server coordinates:
  The two gates sit at opposite ends of the X axis, so the two
  statues stand near those gates, close to each start.
  The two platforms therefore sit on the Y axis, at the other
  two corners, and that is where the two Shadow Sight orbs are
  placed (section 6).

  Statues: narrow but deep front to back, so they block LoS
  along their depth. They are good cover on the opening run and
  they are close enough to the spawn doors that both teams reach
  their own statue at the same time.
  Platforms: circular, fenced on the arena side, stairs on the
  outer side against the wall. The fence stops knockbacks toward
  the arena but does not block casting, so the platform is a
  one-way LoS advantage: you can cast down into the centre and
  cannot be walked up to except by the outer stairs.

Statue positions: unverified (no readable source with x, y, z)
Platform centres: unverified (the orbs sit on them; see below)
Statue count: 2. Platform count: 2. Both verified by two sources
No other pillar, crate or wall object is spawned in server data:
the map spawns exactly four objects, two gates and two orbs.

=== 4. HEIGHT LEVELS AND RAMPS ===

Two levels.

  Floor (gates and both starts): 380.704 to 380.708
  Platforms (orb Z):            383.6809 to 383.6813

Orb Z sits 2.97 units above the floor: 2.9733 above gate 1's
380.708 and 2.9769 above gate 2's 380.704. The raised platforms
are the only higher ground on the map, so this is consistent with
both orbs standing on the two platforms. Treat it as the step
height that falls out of the orb data, not as a measured platform
height; the platform floor height itself is unverified.

Access:
  Up: stairs on the outer side of each platform, against the
    wall. There is no way up from the arena side.
  Down: jump the arena-side fence. The fence is too high to jump
    from the floor onto the platform but easy to drop over.
  A Demon Hunter can double jump up over the fence.

The map has no bridge and no second floor: the two platforms are
the only elevation. One recent guide describes an open pit, but
no source with coordinates shows one and the server spawns
nothing there, so treat that description as unsupported.

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

The opening run is 75.45 units to the centre for both teams, but
each team's own tiger statue is only a short run from its gate,
so the statue is the first contact point, not the centre.

Melee DPS:
  Take your own statue, not the centre. The run from the door to
  the statue is very short and the statue's depth gives real LoS
  cover, which the centre does not
  Expect to be pulled toward the enemy platform all game: the
  stairs are on the wall side, so a caster up top forces you to
  run the long way around

Ranged DPS:
  Climb the platform early. The fence does not block LoS, so you
  can cast on the centre and on the far statue from up there,
  and nothing can walk up to you except by the outer stairs
  Rotate between the platform edge and the statue rather than
  standing in the open centre

Healers:
  The platform is the safest ground on the map: the fence blocks
  knockbacks toward the arena, and melee must take the stairs
  to reach you
  Keep your partner in range of the platform edge, because the
  centre is a long cast from up there on a 150.89-unit map

General opener notes:
  Both teams spawn 75.45 from the centre, so the centre is a
  coin flip; the statues near each gate are the reliable choice
  Both Shadow Sight orbs sit on the two platforms, so a stealth
  comp that wants orbs has to climb the stairs first

=== 7. MATCH FLOW NOTES ===

Gates close before the start and open together.
Worldstate 0xE1A is set to 1 on this map.
Dampening applies like every other arena.
Arena Preparation is applied on zone-in.
No out-of-bounds or under-map return points are wired in the BfA
core for this map: the core handles only triggers 4536 and 4537
and sends everything else to the default handler.
Known retail defects behind the 2019 removal, as reported by
players: blinking into the stair geometry and getting under the
platform, being feared through the statue pillars, and LoS
casting through the platform walls. Not verified against the
server source; listed because they explain the removal.
