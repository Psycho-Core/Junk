================================================================================
TWINK29 PROFESSIONS - 02 ALCHEMY
Patch 8.3.7 (build 35662), level 29 battleground twink
================================================================================

Ids checked against 8.3.7 client tables (SpellName, SpellReagents,
SkillLineAbility) and Wowhead Classic item pages for the tooltips, required
levels and cooldowns. Where a number could not be checked it says "unverified".


================================================================================
1. WHAT A LEVEL 29 CAN ACTUALLY REACH
================================================================================

Classic Alchemy is skill line 171 in 8.3.7, and every classic recipe still
carries the classic tier id 2485 in SkillLineAbility.SkillupSkillLineID. Both
are live in 8.3.7, so the whole Classic 1-300 recipe list is present.

Rank gates (Classic table, restated by the 1-300 guides; costs undiscounted):
  Apprentice  1-75    level 5   10 copper
  Journeyman  1-150   level 10  5 silver      (needs 50 skill)
  Expert      1-225   level 20  50 silver     (needs 125 skill)
  Artisan     1-300   level 35  5 gold        (needs 200 skill)

=> At level 29 the ceiling is 225. Artisan is 6 levels out of reach, so every
recipe with a 226+ requirement is ALT.

The level gate on the ranks is trainer/world data and is not in the 8.3.7 DB2
tables, so it could not be re-verified inside the client. 225 is the working
cap; confirm on the trainer before spending gold.

Alchemy has no specialisation. Unlike Engineering there is no quest chain, no
Goblin/Gnomish split and no membership card, so nothing here is gated the way
the engineering specialisation recipes are gated.

BfA-tier Alchemy (the Kul Tiran/Zandalari recipes) is out of reach at 29; exact
character-level requirement for those trainers is unverified, but every BfA
recipe needs materials from level 110+ zones regardless.

Consequences that shape the whole list:
  - A 29 can craft anything with skill 225 or less, and can use anything whose
    "requires level" is 29 or less. Those are two different filters and the
    second one is the harsher of the two: most of the famous elixirs are
    craftable in principle but unuseable at 29.
  - Alchemy makes no armour, no weapon and no trinket with stats in Classic.
    There is nothing to wear, so nothing to buy from a level 60 alchemist the
    way a twink buys engineering boots and goggles.
  - Everything alchemy gives a 29 is a consumable: one-hour elixirs, one-hour
    absorb potions, instant potions. Elixir buffs are removed on death.
  - Potion use is limited by a shared cooldown. Wowhead Classic files every
    potion in this file under shared cooldown group 65, and every elixir under
    group 81. The 8.3.7 SpellCooldowns table was not queried, so treat both
    groups as unverified in-client.

================================================================================
4. ALT - WHAT NEEDS A HIGHER-LEVEL CHARACTER
================================================================================

ALT here means: a level 60 alchemist, the auction house, or nothing at all.
Nothing in this section can be made or used by the 29.

Flasks - the long buffs that survive death
  Flask of the Titans 13510, Flask of Distilled Wisdom 13511, Flask of
  Supreme Power 13512, Flask of Chromatic Resistance 13513. All four require
  level 50 (individually unverified) and 300 skill. A flask is the only
  alchemy buff that persists through death, which is exactly what a twink
  wants and exactly what a level 29 cannot have.

Skill above 225
  Transmute: Arcanite, spell 17187, skill 275: 1x Thorium Bar 12359 and
  1x Arcane Crystal 12363 into 1x Arcanite Bar 12360. ALT, and worthless to a
  twink anyway - it is a gold maker for a level 60.
  Major Healing Potion 13446 and Major Mana Potion 13444, skill 275-300.
  Recipe: Major Healing Potion is sold by Evie Whirlbrew, Everlook,
  Winterspring (limited supply).
  Superior Mana Potion 13443, skill 260: recipe 13477 from Ulthir (Darnassus)
  or Algernon (Undercity), limited supply.

Buying instead of crafting
  A 29 can use every item in section 2 bought from the auction house. If the
  only goal is the buff, buying the elixirs is cheaper than levelling alchemy
  - see section 6 for what levelling actually costs.


================================================================================
5. THE ONE PERMANENT CRAFT - AND WHY IT IS NOT A TWINK ITEM
================================================================================

Philosopher's Stone 9149. Item level 45, binds when picked up, no stats,
"Required for Alchemical Transmutation". No required level.
  Recipe: spell 11459, Alchemy 225 (trivial 240-280).
  Materials: 4x Iron Bar 3575, 1x Black Vitriol 9262, 4x Purple Lotus 8831,
  4x Firebloom 4625.
  A 29 with 225 alchemy can make and hold it, and it is the only alchemy item
  that is not consumed. It does nothing in combat: it is the tool that unlocks
  the transmute recipes, and every transmute worth doing needs skill 250+.
  Verdict: not a twink item. It is the last 30 points of the profession.

Alchemy therefore gives a level 29 twink no gear at all. Compare Engineering,
where a 29 can wear Goblin Rocket Boots 7189 and a Gnomish Harm Prevention
Belt 10721 bought from a level 60.

  195 - the last genuinely useful recipe for a 29 (Elixir of Greater Defense).

================================================================================
9. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE ALCHEMY
================================================================================

1. Warrior. Elixir of Fortitude 3825 plus Elixir of Greater Defense 8951 is
   the largest survivability swing any profession gives a 29 - 120 health and
   250 armour, both one-hour, both cheap. Free Action 5634 covers the class's
   worst matchup. The only thing missing is a rage potion, and rage potions
   are level-gated.

2. Rogue. Elixir of Agility 8949 is the primary-stat buff the class wants, and
   Swiftness 2459 plus Free Action 5634 make the opener and the escape
   reliable. Cheapest profession to level of any in this set.

3. Hunter. Same agility elixir, plus Swiftness for kiting and Free Action for
   the melee that closes on you. Fire Protection 6049 is the anti-mage buy.

4. Mage. Elixir of Firepower 6373 is small (+10 fire damage for 30 min) but it
   is the only spell-power buff available at 29 from any profession. Mana
   potions and Frost Protection 6050 are the rest of the reason.

5. Warlock and Priest. Shadow Protection 6048 for the mirror match, Fortitude
   3825 for the health pool, mana potions for everything else. Alchemy is a
   defensive pick for both.

6. Paladin and Shaman. Greater Defense 8951, Fortitude 3825, and the school
   protection potion that matches the bracket's dominant caster.

7. Druid. Agility for cat, Nature Protection 6052 for the mirror, Fortitude
   for bear. Fine, not exciting.

Last: if the character is a pure damage twink with Engineering already
levelled, alchemy is the second profession only as a supplier. Two
professions at 29 means one of them is a crafting profession and the other is
Herbalism, and Engineering's bombs are worth more per slot than alchemy's
elixirs are - unless the twink is a warrior or a rogue, where the armour and
agility elixirs are the stronger pick.
