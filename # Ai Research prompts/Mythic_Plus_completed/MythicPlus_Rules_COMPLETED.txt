1. HOW THE KEYSTONE LEVEL SCALES HEALTH AND DAMAGE
=====================================================================

- Per keystone level: enemies gain +10% health and +10% damage, compounding.
  Verified two ways:
  * MethodDungeonTools (MDT) formula, commit 4d36744c (2020-07-19),
    MythicDungeonTools.lua function MDT:CalculateEnemyHealth:
    multiplier = round(1.10 ^ max(level - 2, 0), 2); health = multiplier * baseHealth.
    So +2 = x1.00, +3 = x1.10, +4 = x1.21, +5 = x1.33, ... +15 = x3.45 of the base.
  * warcraft.wiki patch 7.2.5 note (quoted on the Mythic+ page, Feb-2020 revision
    oldid 4869763): "Mythic Keystone enemy health and damage now increase by 10%
    per level (was 8% per level)." No later scaling change is listed on that page.
- Base of the scale = the dungeon's Mythic 0 (+0) enemies.
- Affix multipliers on top (MDT CalculateEnemyHealth, BfA data):
  * Fortified: non-boss enemies x1.20 health.
  * Tyrannical: boss enemies x1.40 health.
  * Damage percentages of Fortified/Tyrannical: unverified (wiki text says both
    increase health and damage; no numeric damage value in any source read).
- Season 4 level rebase (warcraft.wiki Patch 8.3.0 notes, 2020-01-14):
  * Non-keystone Mythic (+0) enemy health/damage +45%; Heroic +25%; Normal unchanged.
  * "Mythic Keystones have been reduced by 4 levels, to account for this
    difficulty increase."
  * M+ rewards raised by 30 item levels for season 4.
- On this fork (HavenCore): GetHealthMultiplier/GetDamageMultiplier return
  100 * (GameTable Scalar - 1), loading "ChallengeModeHealth.txt" and
  "ChallengeModeDamage.txt" (GameTables.cpp lines 126-127). The per-level scalar
  VALUES in those tables for build 35662: unverified (wago.tools csv endpoint for
  build 8.3.7.35662 returned "Table not found" / HTTP 500). The +10%-per-level
  formula above is the retail rule; the fork formula is equivalent to it.

2. WHICH AFFIXES APPLY AT WHICH LEVEL (BFA SEASON 4)
=====================================================================

Slots (warcraft.wiki "Mythic+ affix" page, Feb-2020 revision of Mythic+, and
mythicpl.us snapshot 2020-04-02 - both agree):
- Level 2: first affix - always Fortified OR Tyrannical, alternating weekly.
- Level 4: second affix - exactly one of 5:
  Bolstering (7), Bursting (11), Raging (6), Sanguine (8), Teeming (5).
- Level 7: third affix - exactly one of 6:
  Explosive (13), Grievous (12), Necrotic (4), Quaking (14), Skittish (2), Volcanic (3).
- Level 10: fourth (seasonal) affix - does NOT rotate weekly; one per whole season.
  Season 1: Infested. Season 2: Reaping. Season 3: Beguiling.
  Season 4: Awakened (patch 8.3.0, removed in 9.0.1). Wowhead affix id: 120.
- Active affixes are the same for all players each week (weekly reset).
- Total: +2 has 1 affix, +4 has 2, +7 has 3, +10 and above have 4.

Affix id numbers (fork comments in InstanceScript CastChallengeCreatureSpell /
CastChallengePlayerSpell list 2 Skittish, 3 Volcanic, 4 Necrotic, 6 Raging,
7 Bolstering, 8 Sanguine, 9 Tyrannical, 10 Fortified, 11 Bursting, 12 Grievous,
13 Explosive, 14 Quaking; 1 Overflowing and 5 Teeming from the affix links on the
archived mythicpl.us page; Overflowing was removed in patch 7.2.0 and is not BfA).

Affix effects, BfA-era text (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020 revision, Mythic+ page):
- Fortified: increases health and damage of non-boss enemies (health x1.20 per MDT).
- Tyrannical: increases health and damage of boss enemies (health x1.40 per MDT).
- Bolstering: on death, bolsters allies within 30 yards, permanently +20% max
  health and damage; stacks.
- Bursting: slain non-boss enemies deal 5% of max health to all players over
  4 sec; stacks.
- Raging: non-boss enemies enrage at 30% health, +100% damage until defeated.
- Sanguine: pools heal enemies 5% of max health per sec and damage players 15%
  of max health per sec.
- Teeming: additional non-boss enemies throughout the dungeon; the enemy-forces
  requirement is raised (MDT totals below give both variants).
- Explosive: enemies summon Explosive Orbs in combat that detonate if not
  destroyed (detonation damage: unverified).
- Grievous: below 90% health players take stacking damage over time until healed
  above 90%.
- Necrotic: melee attacks deal physical damage every 2 sec and reduce healing
  and absorption received by 2% per stack for 9 sec; stacks.
- Quaking: periodic shockwave, damage + interrupts casting.
- Skittish: enemies pay far less attention to tank threat.
- Volcanic: eruptions under distant players' feet.

Season 4 affix: Awakened (warcraft.wiki "Awakened (affix)", Patch 8.3.0 notes):
- At +10 and above, four obelisks (visible on the dungeon map; no use in combat)
  pull players into Ny'alotha ("Ny'alotha Incursion") where a lieutenant waits.
  Kill it or it joins the final boss fight. Return portal spawns where the
  lieutenant died (used for skips). Voice of the Corruptor offers a
  damage/healing buff that resets Heroism/Bloodlust debuffs.
- The four lieutenants (appear in all 12 dungeons in the forces data below):
  Urg'roth, Breaker of Heroes (Brutal Spire) 161124;
  Voidweaver Mal'thir (Cursed Spire) 161241;

  Samh'rek, Beckoner of Chaos (Entropic Spire) 161243.
  Patch 8.3.0 note detail: Blood of the Corruptor rot deals 60,000 damage/sec.

3. HOW ENEMY FORCES ARE COUNTED AND WHAT EACH CREATURE ADDS
=====================================================================

Rule: a run completes when all bosses are dead AND the enemy-forces counter
reaches the dungeon's required total (100% of the map's count budget). The
counter shows killed points / required total as a percentage (MDT
FormatEnemyForces, default format "forces/forcesmax (percent%)"). Bosses are on
the list with count 0 (they add nothing; they are required separately).
With the Teeming affix active, the higher "teeming" total is used instead of the
normal total (MDT: IsCurrentPresetTeeming -> dungeonTotalCount[].teeming).

Fork status: HavenCore has NO enemy-forces tracking and no forces packet at all

4. THE TIMER AND CHEST THRESHOLDS
=====================================================================

Start: the keystone is placed in the Font of Power (GO 246779, item 158923),
Activate forces a UI reload, puts a green wall at the entrance and starts a
10-second countdown; when it reaches 0 the wall drops and the timer starts
(warcraft.wiki both era revisions; fork sends SMSG_START_TIMER 0x25BC, 10 s,
TIMER_TYPE_CHALLENGE).

Timer: counts down from the dungeon's par time. Deaths remove time (section
below). If the objectives (all bosses + 100% forces) are not done when it hits
0, the run fails.

Keystone outcome (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020 revision):
- Finished within the timer: +1 keystone level; more than 20% time left: +2;
  more than 40% left: +3 (max +3). The keystone moves to a random dungeon.
- Finished after the timer: keystone -1 level, same dungeon.
- Resetting the dungeon: -1 level, same dungeon (downgrade-by-reset exists
  since 7.2.5; party cannot be changed after start without a reset).

Death cost: each player death subtracts 5 seconds from the timer
("Challenger's Burden"; warcraft.wiki Feb-2020 revision and mythicpl.us
snapshot both state 5 s). Fork formula = total duration is elapsed + 5 x death
count (ChallengeModeMgr, step-1 audit), buff SPELL_CHALLENGER_BURDEN = 206151.
Fork's per-bracket chest thresholds compare against MapChallengeMode
CriteriaCount[3]; the retail percentage thresholds are the table above.

Fork note: _chestTimers[3] thresholds are never initialized in this repo
(step-1 audit), so bracket comparisons are unverified server-side.

5. WHAT THE END OF THE RUN GIVES
=====================================================================

Keystone: placed in the initiating player's bag, upgraded per section 4
(new random dungeon on upgrade; same dungeon downgraded on failure/reset).
A toast shows the result. Achievements granted when the run is timed
(fork CompleteChallengeMode): 11183 (key >= 2), 11184 (>= 5), 11185 (>= 10),
11162 and 11224 (>= 15).

End-of-run chest (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020 revision + patch 7.2.5 notes + mythicpl.us):
- A chest (Challenger's Cache style, "dungeon chest") spawns after the final
  boss whether or not the timer was beaten; all players may loot it.
- Guaranteed: 2 pieces for the group from the dungeon's loot table; +1 piece
  (3 total) if the timer was beaten. Personal loot, class/spec appropriate,
  tradeable if not an ilvl upgrade.
- Extra-piece chances above +15 (sources conflict - listed as read):
  warcraft.wiki Patch 8.3.0 notes: M+ has a chance to drop more than 2 items
  at +16 or higher (was +11). mythicpl.us snapshot: +11 = 40% for one extra,
  +13 = 100% for one extra (+20% for another), +15 = two extra guaranteed
  (5 total). The official +16 line is authoritative for season 4; treat the
  community extra-loot ladder as unverified.
- Ilvl of end-of-run piece by keystone level (mythicpl.us snapshot; anchors
  match Patch 8.3.0 notes: "up to item level 465 at +15"):
  +2:435  +3:435  +4:440  +5:445  +6:445  +7:450  +8:455
  +9:455  +10:455  +11:460  +12:460  +13:460  +14:465  +15:465

Weekly chest (faction hub - Boralus/Zuldazar city; "chest from your faction's
hub the week after" per warcraft.wiki Feb-2020 revision; mythicpl.us detail):
- Appears after the weekly reset; contains one gear piece, Azerite Power,
  Titan Residuum, and a keystone.
- Gear ilvl from the highest keystone level COMPLETED last week, timed or not
  (mythicpl.us snapshot; Patch 8.3.0: weekly ilvl scaling now extends to +15,

  +9:460  +10:465  +11:465  +12:470  +13:470  +14:470  +15:475
- Keystone inside the weekly chest: based on the highest level COMPLETED in the
  season, -1 for every week you do not complete a higher one (example on source:
  best +11 this week -> chest next week gives +10; if you never top it, the
  week after gives +9). Minimum floor: unverified (source only shows the -1/week
  decay; M0 completions grant a +2 keystone - warcraft.wiki Feb-2020).
- Titan Residuum amounts in the weekly chest (mythicpl.us snapshot):
  +10:1700, +11:1790, +12:1880, +13:1970, +14:2060, +15:2150, +16:2240,

  +24:2860, +25:2915. (The weekly cache no longer awards a random Azerite
  armor piece as of 8.3 - it gives Titan Residuum instead, buyable Azerite
  from Thaumaturge Vashreen.)
- Corrupted items can drop from season-4 M+ rewards (Patch 8.3.0 notes);
  cloaks do not drop from season-4 weekly caches.
