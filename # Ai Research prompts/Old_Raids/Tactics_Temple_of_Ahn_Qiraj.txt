Raid: Temple of Ahn'Qiraj (AQ40), 40-player, 9 bosses

Source rule: mechanics and spell ids from

====================================================
1. THE PROPHET SKERAM (15263)
====================================================
PHASES: One phase; splits at 75%, 50% and 25% into
  himself plus 2 images (747 Summon Images). Images
  share his abilities, spawn at his current health
  percentage, and have far less total health.
TANK: 2 tanks at each of the 3 blink spots (centre
  platform, left stairs, right stairs). The instant a
  form appears at your spot, engage it - an untanked
  form spams Earth Shock at range.
GROUP: Max 4 players in melee per spot (Arcane
  Explosion only fires with more than 4 in melee).
  Ranged on the steps; mages watch for mind controls.
GROUND: 13810 Ice Trap (hunter class spell id as
  listed on the page - treat as unverified for this
  creature); blink spots are the movement hazard.
ADD ORDER: P1 images (kill both fast, then resume the
  real Skeram). Mind-controlled players are NEVER a
  kill target - polymorph/fear/stun them instead.
AI RULE: the second a split happens, stop, let tanks
  claim all three forms, kill images first; crowd-
  control - never kill - any mind-controlled player.
ABILITY LIST:
  785 True Fulfillment - mind control 20 sec, +300%
    damage, instant casts, not dispellable. D: none
    (polymorph/fear/stun/sleep the player instead)
  26192 Arcane Explosion - 187-217 Arcane, melee
    range, only with >4 in melee. I (interruptible)
  26194 Earth Shock - 149-174 Nature, interrupts the
    victim's cast 2 sec; spammed if his target is
    out of melee
  747 Summon Images - at 75/50/25%
  Teleport - swaps place with an image, clears aggro
    (spell id unverified)
ADD GROUPS: 2 images per split. Creature entry of
  the images: unverified.

2. SILITHID ROYALTY (OPTIONAL) - LORD KRI,
   PRINCESS YAUJ, VEM (entries unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: All three fought together. Kill order sets
  loot and difficulty: easiest Kri > Yauj > Vem;
  hardest Kri last. 15-min hard enrage.
  Death specials: Kri leaves a poison cloud (about
  2000/sec - never stand in it); Yauj spawns small
  bugs (CC-able); Vem enrages the other two, and the
  survivor devours the corpse (if Yauj devours Kri
  she gains her heal - keep Vem nearer Kri's corpse
  than Yauj is).
TANK: Kri on the main raid with poison cleansing;
  Yauj tanked far from the raid by 2 tanks swapping
  on her roughly 20-sec AoE fear (fear also wipes
  aggro); Vem offtanked (negligible on a tank, 2-3k
  on cloth).
GROUP: Spread for fear and poison; move out of Kri's
  cloud the instant he dies; AoE Yauj's bugs down.
GROUND: Kri death poison cloud (above).
ADD ORDER: P1 Kri > P2 Yauj (interrupt every heal) >
  P3 Vem. Yauj adds P0 when they spawn.
AI RULE: kill in the set order, never stand where
  Kri died, and never let a Yauj heal finish.
ABILITY LIST (spell ids unverified - pages link
  almost none): Toxic Volley (Kri, raid-wide poison
  DoT, dispellable D-Poison, stacks); AoE Fear
  (Yauj, aggro wipe); Heal (Yauj, I - top interrupt
  priority in the whole encounter); Charge and
  Knockback (Vem).

3. BATTLEGURD SARTURA (entry unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: One phase with 3 Royal Guard adds (entries
  unverified). Enrage at 20% (+124 physical damage,
  +60% attack speed); hard enrage at 10 min (10-15k
  hits, +150% attack speed - a wipe).
TANK: 2 tanks on Sartura, 1-2 per guard, all spread
  across the room; ping-pong Taunt. Boss and adds
  periodically drop aggro for about 5 sec and chase
  random players, then return to the top threat.
GROUP: Never stand still: when a whirlwinding mob
  moves at you, run - you cannot outrun it, but its
  path is random. Stay away from the Fankriss tunnel
  entrance (guard knockbacks can punt you into the
  patrols). Sartura stunnable only when not spinning;
  guards stunnable while spinning (spin continues).
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: P1 Royal Guards, then Sartura (page
  strategy: kill guards clear of her Whirlwind).
AI RULE: if it is spinning, move away and do not
  melee it; if it stops spinning, stun and burn.
ABILITY LIST:
  26083 Whirlwind - 15 sec spin, normal damage +110,
    immune to stun while spinning (boss and adds)
  26084 Whirlwind - Physical damage within 10 yd
  25174 Sundering Cleave - normal +300% to 3 targets,
    +25 Physical damage taken per hit
  8269 Frenzy - +60% attack speed, 2 min (enrage at
    20%; dispel type unverified)

4. FANKRISS THE UNYIELDING (entry unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: One phase. From shortly after pull, roughly
  every minute each of the 3 wall tunnels spawns 4
  Vekniss Hatchlings and one player is teleported
  and rooted in an alcove. 8 Vekniss Drones stand in
  the room and join on pull.
TANK: 2 tanks rotating on Mortal Wound (10% healing
  reduction per stack, cleaves - face him away).
  Offtanks hold Hatchlings (300 on tanks, 800-900 on
  cloth).
GROUP: Kill Spawn of Fankriss the instant they
  appear (they enrage after 20 sec and then hit for
  10-20k). Hatchlings can be snared/feared but NOT
  frost-nova'd or frost-bolt snared; late in the
  fight kite them instead of killing.
GROUND: none (alcove root/teleport is the hazard).
ADD ORDER: P1 Spawn of Fankriss (entries unverified,
  20-sec enrage fuse) > P2 Vekniss Drones (on pull) >
  P3 Vekniss Hatchlings (tank/kite, do not chase).
AI RULE: a fresh Spawn of Fankriss outranks the boss
  for every damage dealer, every time - it has a
  20-second fuse.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified, page names mechanics):
  Mortal Wound - healing -10%/stack, cleave (shares
    the name of Kurinnaxx 25646; same id unverified)
  Hatchling spawns; player teleport+root; Spawn of
    Fankriss timed enrage (20 sec, stunnable)
TRASH NOTE (the Bug Tunnel before him): packs of
  Vekniss Soldier + many Vekniss Drones respawn
  almost as fast as killed. Do not stop to clear:
  drag them, AoE in bulk, summon the dead. Ends at
  the left alcove "Safe Spot" (entries unverified).

5. VISCIDUS (OPTIONAL, entry unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: Cycle, not health-based. Frost hits slow
  him: 10 hits "begins to slow" (-15%), 15 "freezing
  up" (-30%), 20 "frozen solid". Then melee hits:
  30 hits shatter him into Globs (one glob per 5% of
  his health, 10-15k health each). Globs run to the
  centre to reform him; each glob killed removes
  that 5% permanently. Shatter him below 5% to kill.
  He takes 50% reduced damage throughout and resets
  if pulled out of his room.
TANK: One nature-geared tank; he hits about 1000 and
  starts fast, slowing as frost builds.
GROUP: Frost users spam fastest frost (rank does not
  matter, hit count does); on "frozen solid" EVERYONE
  melees; then assist-train globs one at a time (DPS
  on a glob that survives is wasted; globs cannot be
  slowed or stunned). Cure the Poison Volley DoT so
  nobody takes more than one tick.
GROUND: Toxin Clouds every 30-40 sec (about 1500/2
  sec, 40% slow, damage radius larger than the
  graphic; a single spit bolt lands where one will
  form - move then).
ADD ORDER: P1 Globs of Viscidus (focus-fire one at a
  time; never split). NEVER: none - globs must die.
AI RULE: frost to 20 hits, then melee to 30, then
  kill globs single-target in assist order - repeat.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified, page gives numbers):
  Poison Shock - about 1200 Nature, 15 yd, ~10 sec
  Poison Volley - about 1500 Nature raid-wide (100
    yd, ignores line of sight) plus dispellable DoT
    about 500/2 sec for 10 sec. D-Poison (mass cure)
  Toxin Cloud - above

6. PRINCESS HUHURAN (entry unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: Above 30%: frenzies (tranquilizable - hunters
  have about 5 sec), tank rotation on Acid Spit.
  Below 30%: Berserk - attack speed doubled, Poison
  Bolt volleys every 3 sec to the closest 15 players;
  this frenzy cannot be tranquilized.
TANK: Rotate 2-3 tanks: Acid Spit stacks 219-281/2
  sec on the current tank, unresistible. She is
  immune to taunt - rotate with aggro drops
  (invulnerability/BoP) and threat races.
GROUP: 15 nature-resistant "soaks" stand closest and
  must be kept alive through Berserk. Spread for
  Noxious Poison. Do NOT dispel Wyvern Sting (dispel
  detonates it for 3000 Nature) - let sleepers sleep,
  except the incoming tank.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: none (no adds).
AI RULE: below 30%, keep all 15 closest players
  alive and burn - never dispel a sleeping player.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified, page gives numbers):
  Frenzy - tranquilizable (D: Tranquilizing Shot)
  Poison Bolt - 2000 to closest 15, only in Frenzy/
    Berserk; pets do not count toward the 15
  Noxious Poison - about 2900 over 8 sec + silence,
    splashes around the target, not dispellable
  Wyvern Sting - AoE sleep, up to 10 closest;
    dispelling deals 3000. D: DO NOT DISPEL
  Acid Spit - stacking DoT, tank only
  Berserk - at 30%, untranquilizable soft enrage

7. TWIN EMPERORS - VEK'NILASH (melee) AND VEK'LOR
   (caster) (entries unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: Shared percentage health (damage to one
  lowers both). Every 30-40 sec Twin Teleport swaps
  their positions with a full aggro wipe; each then
  attacks the closest player (about 2 sec rooted
  first). Within 60 yd of each other they spam Heal
  Brother (30000/tick) - keep them at opposite walls.
  Berserk at 15 min.
TANK: Each side: a warrior (must be the closest
  player to whichever emperor arrives) and a warlock
  who tanks Vek'lor at range with Searing Pain/
  Curse of Doom (Vek'lor is immune to physical,
  Vek'nilash immune to magic except Holy). Warrior's
  back to the wall (Uppercut knockback).
GROUP: Melee DPS follow Vek'nilash, casters follow
  Vek'lor; everyone leaves their emperor about 5 sec
  before a teleport so the warrior is closest. NEVER
  enter Vek'lor's melee range (Arcane Burst).
  A bug team handles mutated bugs on the physical
  side; never AoE the neutral bugs.
GROUND: Blizzard (Vek'lor) 1500/tick + slow - move
  out. Exploding bugs: 2813-3187 Fire after 3 sec.
ADD ORDER: P1 Mutated bug (grows 400%, 300% health,
  hits 1800% - tank and kill) > P2 burning/exploding
  bug is NEVER a kill target - run from it, it dies
  on its own in 3 sec.
AI RULE: at the teleport, stop, let the warrior be
  closest, then DPS only your own emperor type -
  physical on Vek'nilash, magic on Vek'lor.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified, page gives numbers):
  Heal Brother - 30000/tick within 60 yd
  Twin Teleport - 30-40 sec, aggro wipe
  Uppercut (Vek'nilash) - knockback, melee range
  Unbalancing Strike - 350% weapon, -100 Defense 6 s
  Mutate Bug - every 10-15 sec
  Shadow Bolt (Vek'lor) - 3000-4000 spam
  Blizzard - 1500/tick ground effect
  Arcane Burst - 4000-4950 Arcane if anyone is in
    his melee range, knockback + 70% slow
  Explode Bug - every 7-10 sec
  Virulent Poison (bugs) - stacking DoT on melee hit

8. Ouro (OPTIONAL, entry unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: P1 above ground until he submerges (chance
  roughly every 90 sec, sooner if nobody is in melee:
  untargetable; Dirt Mounds quake and Scarabs spawn
  each surfacing - scarabs fixate their first target
  and despawn after 45 sec, CC them, do not chase).
  P2 at 20%: enrage (+150% attack speed, +100%
  damage), never submerges again, still summons
  mounds and scarabs; if nobody is in melee he
  throws boulders for about 6000.
TANK: Chain of tanks: Sand Blast (every 20-25 sec,
  3500-4500 Nature, 180-deg frontal, 45 yd) hits the
  highest-threat player and CLEARS their threat -
  next tank takes over. After a Sweep (1-2.5k +
  knockback) the swept tank stays out until healed.
GROUP: 3 groups at 120 degrees; healers beyond 45 yd
  of his face. Never stand on a Dirt Mound. Move a
  tank into melee within about 10 sec of a surfacing
  or he dives again.
GROUND: Dirt Mound quake (about 1000 Nature) and
  Ground Rupture (about 2000) where he surfaces.
ADD ORDER: Scarabs: NEVER a priority kill - CC and
  ignore for their 45-sec life.
AI RULE: always have a tank in melee, never be the
  highest threat standing in front of him, and never
  stand where he is about to surface.
ABILITY LIST: 26616 Boulder (id from the page's
  external spell link, behaviour as above). Others
  id unverified: Sweep, Sand Blast, Submerge,
  Ground Rupture, Dirt Mound quake.

9. C'THUN (15727) - EYE OF C'THUN (entry unverified)
====================================================
PHASES: P1 kill the Eye of C'Thun. P2 the body
  rises: 26156 Carapace of C'Thun (-99% damage)
  until both stomach Flesh Tentacles die - then
  Vulnerable for 45 sec. Repeat until dead.
TANK: No conventional tank: the Eye faces its beam
  target; tentacles are killed where they spawn.
GROUP P1: Spread 10+ yd apart at all times. One
  nature-resistant volunteer enters first to eat the
  opening beams while the raid spreads. Eye Beam
  chains (+150% per jump - a stacked raid dies to
  one cast). Dark Glare every 85 sec: a 43750-56250
  Shadow beam sweeping 180 degrees over 35 sec -
  run ahead of the rotation, never through it, it
  cannot be line-of-sighted.
GROUP P2: Devoured players land in the stomach:
  26476 Digestive Acid (150 Nature/5 sec, stacking)
  - kill both Flesh Tentacles fast, then exit. On
  Vulnerable, all cooldowns into C'Thun for 45 sec.
GROUND: Dark Glare sector (above); tentacle spawn
  knockbacks.
ADD ORDER P1: Eye Tentacles (8 every 45 sec, 2289
  HP, Mind Flay MUST be interrupted/stunned) and
  Claw Tentacles (1526 HP) > Eye of C'Thun.
ADD ORDER P2: stomach Flesh Tentacles > Giant Eye/
  Giant Claw Tentacles > C'Thun (only when
  Vulnerable). NEVER DPS C'Thun through Carapace.
AI RULE: spread always; tentacles before the boss;
  inside the stomach, kill both Flesh Tentacles
  immediately; hit C'Thun only in the 45-sec window.
ABILITY LIST:
  Eye of C'Thun:
  26134 Eye Beam - 2625-3375 Nature, chains +150%
    per jump, 2 sec cast. I: NO (not interruptible)
  26029 Dark Glare - 43750-56250 Shadow sweep
  Claw Tentacle: 26478 Ground Rupture (2250-2750,
    15 yd), 26141 Hamstring (-70% move, 10 sec)
  Eye Tentacle: 26139 Ground Rupture (1350-1650,
    10 yd), 26143 Mind Flay - 750/sec for 10 sec,
    30% snare, unresistable. I (interrupt/stun it)
  C'Thun body: 26156 Carapace (-99% damage);
    Devour (stomach teleport, id unverified)
  Stomach: 26476 Digestive Acid - stacking aura
  Giant Claw: 26478 Ground Rupture, 6524 Ground
    Tremor (2-sec stun), 26141 Hamstring, 3391
    Thrash (2 extra attacks)
  Giant Eye: 26134 Eye Beam, 26478 Ground Rupture
