TWINK29 - SHAMAN - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, PROFESSIONS, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
Sources: the profession files in Twink29_Professions-completed (Enchanting,
Alchemy, Inscription, Cooking and First Aid, Engineering, Leatherworking),
the shaman item and talent steps, and the wiki pages for Flametongue,
Flametongue Weapon, Windfury Weapon, Earthbind Totem and Tremor Totem

V = read. Where a number was not read it says unverified.

0. THE SHAPE OF THIS FILE
   A shaman is two classes sharing one file: Enhancement enchants and
   drinks like the rogue, Elemental and Restoration like the priest and
   warlock. The step 1 file found the casters have almost no Intellect
   gear; this file finds they at least have the casters' full enchant and
   oil list, which the melee half cannot use. And the class feature this
   step asks about - weapon imbues - turns out to be mostly absent in
   this build. Section 4 is the one to read.

3. PROFESSIONS
   ENGINEERING  The pick for all three specs. Iron Grenade 4390 and Big
     Iron Bomb 4394 give a 3 sec stun, Minor Recombobulator 4381 (item
     level 28, Engineering 140, 10 charges) restores 150 to 250 health
     and mana to a friendly target and attempts to dispel polymorph (V),
     and Discombobulator Ray 4388 cuts a target's damage for 12 sec
     (V, Engineering file). Bombs share a cooldown and cannot be chained.
     A class with an interrupt already (Wind Shear, whose level was not
     read for this file) gets less from the stun than a rogue does, and
     more from 4388, which no other profession offers.
   LEATHERWORKING  Makes mail and leather armour and the armour kits;
     the step 1 wrist and chest answers are heirlooms, so this is a
     money profession here rather than a gearing one. Skinning funds it.
   ENCHANTING  Self-supply for the most split enchant list in the set:
     an Enhancement bot needs the melee column, a caster bot the other.
   ALCHEMY  Elixir of Agility 8949, the mana potions, 8951.
   RECOMMENDATION  Engineering + Enchanting for self-sufficient bots;
     Engineering + Leatherworking for a played character that will skin
     its way to its own kits.Blacksmithing was not checked for this class.

4. WEAPON IMBUES - THE SECTION THIS STEP ASKED FOR
   The classic picture of a shaman choosing between Flametongue,
   Windfury, Frostbrand and Rockbiter on each weapon does not exist in
   8.3.7. Two of the four imbues were removed in 6.0.2 and only came back
   in 9.0.1, after this build.
     Flametongue, spell 193796, Enhancement, learned at level 12 (V).
       Not an imbue in BfA: an instant attack on a 12 sec cooldown that
       scorches the target and enhances your weapons for 16 sec, each
       attack dealing up to 2.6% of attack power as Fire damage. Removed
       in 9.0.1 and replaced by Flametongue Weapon. This is what Hot
       Hand, the step 2 Enhancement talent, procs from, so a 29
       Enhancement bot presses it on cooldown and that is the whole
       imbue routine.
     Flametongue Weapon - NOT in this build. Removed in 6.0.2 ("Replaced
       with Flametongue"), re-added in 9.0.1 at level 7 (V). Any guide
       telling a 29 to imbue Flametongue Weapon for an hour is reading
       Shadowlands or later.
     Windfury Weapon - NOT in this build either. Removed in 6.0.2 and
       replaced by a passive; reintroduced in 9.0.1 "replacing the
       passive ability Windfury" (V). So an 8.3.7 Enhancement shaman does
       get Windfury extra attacks, but from a passive it never casts,
       and - this is the part that matters for the talent file -
       Forceful Winds, the level 30 trap, was written against that
       passive, which is why its text talks about Windfury attacks
       stacking rather than about an imbue. The passive's proc chance at
       29 was not read, so no number is given.
     Elemental and Restoration have no imbue at all in this build. Their
       weapons carry an oil (section 2) or nothing, which is one more
       reason the staff loadout in step 1 is the simpler bot build.
   PRACTICAL ANSWER  Enhancement: keep Flametongue up, enchant both
     weapons with 13693, and carry no imbue item, because there is none
     to carry. Casters: wizard or mana oil, refreshed hourly.

5. TOTEMS WORTH CARRYING
   Totems are abilities in 8.3.7, not items - nothing goes in a bag. What
   a 29 can actually drop is shorter than the classic list:
     Earthbind Totem, learned at level 8 in BfA (9.0.1 lowered it to 5)
     (V): slows enemies within 10 yards by 50% for up to 20 sec, 30 sec
     cooldown. Available to all three specs and the one totem a bot
     should drop on being trained or on a flag carrier.
     Grounding Totem, Restoration, PvP talent, level 20 (V, step 2):
     redirects harmful spells to itself, 30 sec cooldown, 5 health in
     this build.
     Tremor Totem - NOT available at 29. Removed in 7.0.3 and re-added
     in 8.0.1 at level 58 (V). This is the totem every classic shaman
     guide builds around for fear fights, and a 29 in this build simply
     does not have it; the fear break has to come from the Insignia or a
     racial, section 6.
     Healing Stream Totem, Capacitor Totem and Wind Rush Totem: their
     BfA learn levels were not read for this file, so they are named as
     existing and not filed as available or unavailable. Capacitor is
     the one worth checking first on the server, since a stun totem
     would change the Enhancement script.

6. RACES
   Which races could be shamans in 8.3.7 is not verified (the wiki race
   pages are current-expansion pages), so this list's membership is
   unverified and its racial effects are the read part. Shaman has the
   widest race list of any class in this set, which makes the pick
   matter less, not more.
   READ EARLIER IN THIS SET
     Orc       Blood Fury, 2 min cooldown: the Blood Fury page lists
       shaman among the classes that receive both the attack power and
       the spell power (Intellect since 8.0.1) buff (V, warlock file).
       The only racial here that helps all three specs.
     Undead    shamans cannot be undead - included to say so plainly,
       because Will of the Forsaken has been the first pick in four
       files running and it is not available to this class. That is a
       real cost in a bracket where Tremor Totem is also missing:
       a 29 shaman has no fear break of its own at all.
     Blood elf shamans cannot be blood elves either, so no Arcane
       Torrent purge.
   NAMED, IDS AND FIGURES UNVERIFIED
     Troll     Berserking, haste on demand.
     Tauren    War Stomp, a short area stun - the closest thing this
       class gets to a second interrupt.
     Goblin    Rocket Jump, a leap; a shaman's only gap closer or escape.
     Dwarf     Stoneform, a debuff purge and damage reduction.
     Draenei   Gift of the Naaru, a heal over time.
     Pandaren  Quaking Palm, an incapacitate.
     Mag'har   Ancestral Call, a stat gain.
     Zandalari Regeneratin', a heal channel; Embrace of the Loa, a stat.
     Kul Tiran Haymaker, a knockback stun.
     Dark iron Fireblood, a debuff purge and stat gain.
     Vulpera   Bag of Tricks, damage or healing on demand - Vulpera are
       an 8.3 addition, so this is the one allied race that a build
       35662 server definitely postdates; whether it is enabled is
       unverified.
   PICKS  Orc first for any spec. Tauren if the bot needs the stun,
     Goblin if it needs to move. Avoid nothing outright, but note the
     class-level fact: with no undead or blood elf option and no Tremor
     Totem, a 29 shaman is the only healer in this set with neither a
     fear break nor a purge racial, and the Insignia trinket is not
     optional for it.

8. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT HAVE
   A caster glove enchant inside 225 (none exists that was read).
   An Intellect battle elixir (none found; Firepower is unverified for
   this class rather than recommended).
   Flametongue Weapon or Windfury Weapon as castable imbues.
   Tremor Totem, or any fear break native to the class at 29.
   A ring or neck enchant a 29 can apply (enchanter-only, ALT).
   A verified shaman race list for 8.3.7.
   The gaps here are different in kind from the other classes': a rogue
   was missing poisons for two specs, and a shaman is missing the imbues
   and the fear totem that define the class in every other era. What it
   has instead is the widest race list in the set and an Orc racial that
   is the only one anywhere in these files to buff all three specs.
