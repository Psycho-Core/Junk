=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS (map 1672) ===

The arena is the bridge map: a wide central bridge with a pit
under it, two pillars that reach bridge height, and ramps up.

What is verified:
  - The only server-spawned LoS-relevant objects are the two
    Shadow Sight power-ups in section 6. Pillars, crates and
    bridge geometry are world model geometry, not game objects,
    so no coordinates for them exist in server data.
  - The world model directory is bladesedgearena2b (map 1672).

What is not verified for map 1672:
  - Pillar centre coordinates
  - Ramp start and end coordinates
  - Bridge width and deck height
  - Pit depth

Layout as described for this arena (applies to the map design;
exact numbers belong to the model files):
  Central bridge spans the arena, with the pit underneath
  Two pillars stand under the bridge and reach deck height
  Ramps connect the ground floor to the bridge deck
  Dropping off the bridge does not kill a player, it only costs
  the movement time to climb back

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE (map 1672) ===

Melee DPS:
  Open through the gate and take the nearest ramp to the bridge
  deck, or hold the ground floor and force the enemy down
  Ground-floor play uses the pillars and the pit for LoS

Ranged DPS:
  Take the bridge deck for free casting and range
  The bridge centre is the strongest free-cast spot but has no
  hard cover, so a pillar edge is the usual compromise

Healers:
  Bridge deck behind a pillar, so the pillar blocks enemy casts
  without cutting the healer off from their own team
  If pushed off the bridge, the pit is the fallback LoS spot

General opener notes:
  Controlling the bridge is the standard win condition for
  caster comps; melee comps prefer the pit and pillar play
  Gates open simultaneously, so both teams reach the ramps at
  the same time; there is no first-move advantage

=== 6. BUFF PICKUPS (map 1672) ===

Only Shadow Sight exists on this map.

Shadow Sight A (GO 184663):
  X: 2815.306
  Y: 5972.267
  Z: -4.452
  Orientation: 2.24 rad
  About 42.1 units from the arena centre

Shadow Sight B (GO 184664):
  X: 2761.437
  Y: 6038.106
  Z: -3.49
  Orientation: 5.57 rad
  About 43.1 units from the arena centre

The two orbs sit on opposite sides of the centre, so each team is
roughly equidistant from one of them.

Spawn: 60 s after the gates open (core constant
  ARENA_COUNTDOWN_MAX = 60)
Respawn after pickup: not set in the core file for this map, so
  the game object template default applies - unverified
Spell: 34709 Shadow Sight - 15 s, stealth and invisibility
  detection, +5% damage taken while active
  Warcraft Wiki states the general arena rule as orbs appearing
  after 90 s; the exact retail 8.3.7 delay is unverified

No health, mana, haste or berserking pickups are spawned here.
The generic battleground pickup objects 179871 (speed),
179904 (regeneration) and 179905 (berserking) are not used on
arena maps.
