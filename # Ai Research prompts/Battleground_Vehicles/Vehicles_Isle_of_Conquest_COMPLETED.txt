1. MATCH FRAME (context for sections 4-5, source C; win rules from C,
   reinforcement count from C)
   40 vs 40. Win: kill the enemy general (Alliance: High Commander
   Halford Wyrmbane, creature 34924; Horde: Overlord Agmar, creature
   34922 - both spawn rows in source A) OR reduce enemy reinforcements
   to 0. Each team starts with 300 reinforcements; each player death
   costs 1. At least 1 of the enemy keep's 3 gates must be destroyed
   before attackers can enter the general's command room or capture
   the keep graveyard (C). Node capture = a channeled, interruptible
   effect (C). Refinery and Quarry each give a stacking +15% siege
   weapon damage to their owner (C).

4. SEQUENCE A GOOD TEAM FOLLOWS (each step's mechanics are sourced;
   WHICH order a team should run is strategy: no readable source
   gives a canonical team order - order below is capability-gated
   fact, "the usual opening" itself unverified)
   Opening (both sides symmetric - C):
   1) Capture nodes: Docks, Workshop, Hangar are the vehicle nodes;
   Quarry/Refinery are passive +15% siege damage (stacking) node
   buffs (C). Capture = channeled, interruptible (C).
   2) Workshop owner immediately gets 4 demolishers + 2 seaforium
   bombs (A spawn + C confirm); siege engine becomes usable at
   3 minutes (A timer + C). Drive demolishers toward the enemy
   front gate (Alliance front target: 628, 1150.9, -762.606,
   47.5077; Horde front target: 628, 413.479, -833.95, 48.5238 -
   gate rows section 2a) and fire from siege range; Hurl Boulder
   works to about 50 yd (C).
   3) Docks owner gets 2 glaives + 4 catapults (A+C). Glaives fire
   at gates from the docks line or from the peninsula spots across
   from the keeps (C's stated gate-harass positions; peninsula
   coordinates unverified). Catapults: one Launch per driver over
   the walls for the Back Door Job path (C) to reach the enemy
   courtyard and the huge bombs.
   4) Hangar owner boards the gunship (5 cannons, AoE on gates and
   players, C), or jumps with the automatic parachute into the
   enemy courtyard (C). When the ENEMY holds the hangar your ship
   is stopped and its cannons disabled (A).
   5) Gate down (siege damage from section 2a weapons; seaforium
   plants need 10 s undisturbed, C): inner portcullis opens, keep
   graveyard becomes capturable (A + C); kill the general (34922 /
   34924) to win, or bleed their 300 reinforcements (C).
   6) Defenders' tools (C): keep cannons on the tower rows (3f) with
   Rocket Blast/Incendiary from 10-70 yd; gate portals (2b) to
   cross between gates; their own huge bombs to disarm; siege engine
   repaired at the workshop timer.
   Drive-to / fire-from points that ARE verified coordinates: the six
   gate rows (2a), the bomb rows (3h), the vehicle spawn rows
   (3a-3f). Roads/walking lines between them: unverified (no pathing
   source).

5. ONE PLAYER ALONE vs NEEDING TWO OR MORE (mechanics from A/B/C;
   tactics opinions are unverified)
   Solo-capable (mechanic needs only 1 player): driving any
   single-seat vehicle - demolisher (pilot alone fights; passengers
   optional, C), glaive (driver's seat only, C), catapult, keep
   cannon, gunship cannon; planting and defending a seaforium bomb
   (all steps are single-player channels/interactions, C); repairing
   a destroyed tower gun (short channel, C); capturing a node
   (single channel, interruptible, C); killing the general or any
   1-reinforcement pick-off (C rule).
   Needs 2+ players (by the mechanics): crewing the Siege Engine's
   full loadout - driver + main turret + 2 flame turrets are 4
   simultaneous seats (C + accessory seats 1/2/7 in B); holding a
   planted bomb against an enemy disarmer while others keep fighting
   is a 2+ situation (mechanic allows the enemy to disarm by
   channel - C; whether 1 can always hold is tactics, unverified);
   carrying extra fighters to the courtyard: demolisher passengers
