POSITIONS AND NUMBERS - ATAL'DAZAR
================================================================================
Instance : Atal'Dazar (dungeon, 4 encounters)
Map      : 1763   Client: 8.3.7 build 35662
Written  : 2026-10-23
Purpose  : the boss spawns, the tank anchors, the pull positions with their z,
           the route between them, the safe spots, the phase and timer numbers
           and the assignment triggers. Companion bots read this to path, to
           circle and to spread.

COORDINATE ORDER. (x, y, z) in yards on map 1763, as written in the source.
Orientation: server orientation in radians (0 = +x, pi/2 = +y). The database
rows carry one per spawn; the scripts carry almost none, because this dungeon's
scripts spawn everything at run time.

WHAT MAKES THIS FILE DIFFERENT FROM THE LAST FOUR. The raid files were built on
script constants. Here the scripts hold 45 static positions in total - three
Vol'kaal totems and Rezan's 42 bone piles - and nothing else. Every other
coordinate in this file comes from the world database rows, which is better for
a bot, because those are the exact places the server will put the creatures;
but it means the four boss spawns are DB spawns, not script constants, and
they are marked [DB] throughout.

THE SECOND DIFFERENCE: this is a dungeon, so half the file is trash. Forty-three
spawn clusters, their z, the route between them, and a per-ability interrupt and
dispel list. The pull table is generated from the DB rows by 15-yard clustering
and is stated as such; it is not a raider's route.

================================================================================
1. SOURCES AND THEIR LIMITS
================================================================================
1.1 instance_atal_dazar.cpp registers map 1763 and is a pure door script. It
    holds 4 encounter ids (DATA_PRIESTESS_ALUNZA, DATA_VOLKAAL, DATA_REZAN,
    DATA_YAZMA), 16 gameobject entries, and one piece of logic: on the death of
    the third of Alun'za / Vol'kaal / Rezan it sets GO_GATE_009, GO_GATE_010,
    GO_COLLISION_WALL and GO_WATERFALL_STAIRS to GO_STATE_ACTIVE.
1.2 The four boss scripts are full implementations with timers and phase
    triggers, but no arena constants except Vol'kaal's three totem spawns and
    Rezan's forty-two bone-pile areatrigger points.
1.3 The DB file is an AshamaneCore world update from 2018-12-09. Its creature
    rows carry spawnDifficulties "1,2,23,8", i.e. one row serves every
    difficulty, so no row here is Mythic-only or Heroic-only. The core this
    file was built for is the one in your scripts directory, not a retail
    snapshot, so a row count is what your server spawns, not necessarily what
    retail had.
1.4 MDT supplies the names for 20 of the 40 entries in the DB rows, the
    per-kill enemy forces, and the health pools. Its clone coordinates are in
    map space, not world space, and could not be converted - so MDT gives this
    file names and forces only, never positions.
1.5 Twenty of the 40 entries are in MDT. Three more have DB rows and are
    named only in the scripts (125828 Soulrend, 125977 Vol'kaal's totem,
    128956 the cauldron), and three are script entries the DB never places
    at all (129517 Reanimated Raptor, 130738 Corrupted Gold, 131009 Spirit
    of Gold). Of the remaining seventeen, eleven now carry a name (2.4.2)
    and six are unverified. One of the 40, 127352 Risen Honor Guard, is real
    trash - 28 spawn rows - with no force value, no spell list and no module
    anywhere; that is the biggest gap in this file and it is marked every
    time it appears.
1.6 No client table (SpellRadius, SpellDuration, creature_template) was
    readable in this step. Every radius and every duration in this file either
    comes from a script comment, from a DBM/LW timer, or is marked unverified.

     Trigger: SpellHitTarget on 249923 summons 125828 at the victim's
     position. The spawn is passive, follows Yazma, and when it gets within
     1 yd of her it casts 256577 and despawns. Damage ids 259145 (normal)
     and 259191 (heroic).
     MYTHIC REFLECT, and it is broken: DamageTaken does
     `float percent = damage * (15 / 100);` and 15/100 is integer division,
     so the multiplier is 0 and the reflect deals nothing. Do not plan
     around it on this core.
  b) ECHOES OF SHADOW 250050 -> 257291 / 263092 - EVERYBODY.
     Trigger: the boss casts 250050 on up to four random players; each one
     spawns an echo at their feet (SpellHitTarget -> 257291 big, 263092
     small). Five seconds later the echo gains 258612 / 256036 and follows
     the nearest player at 100 yd for 3.2 s; if it touches a player it
     casts AT 250022 and kills itself.
  c) WRACKING PAIN 250096 - INTERRUPT rotation. DBM: interrupt warning.
  d) SKEWER 249919 - TANK. DBM: tank defensive warning.

7.5 SAFE SPOTS, WITH z
  Platform z: 730.338 (the six echo/soul rows and the floor they stand on)
  to 734.506. She stands 3.97 yd above the soul rows [CALC]; everything
  else in the fight is generated at a player's feet, so there is no static
  geometry to use except the platform edge.
  - Against Soulrend: the spawn appears exactly where its victim stood.
    The safe position is therefore relative to your own spawn, and the safe
    tactic is to be far enough from Yazma that the spawn cannot reach her
    before the group kills it - the platform's 38.9 yd y-axis is the short
    axis to cross, so fight on the far side of her.
  - Against Echoes: AT 250022 only fires within 1 yd of the echo, and the
    echo follows the nearest player. Moving away hands it to someone else;
    killing it at range is what the tactics document asks for [TAC].
  - The six pre-placed 125828 rows at z 730.338 are 26.1 to 41.4 yd from her
    (3.4). They are not part of the script's summon path and their purpose
    is unverified; do not path around them.

10. DISCREPANCIES AND TRAPS
================================================================================
10.1 TRANSFUSION 255577 IS NOT A COOLDOWN AND NOT AN INTERRUPT PROBLEM.
  Script: mana at 100 -> cast in 5.0 s. DBM: a 34 s cooldown starting at
  25.0 with a MoveTo warning keyed to the Tainted Blood debuff. LW: 25.1
  and a 34 s repeat, with a 4.0 s cast bar. Tactics document: "interrupt
  Transfusion on cooldown".
  All four describe the same ability differently. The server runs the mana
  gate, so an interrupt rotation will find nothing to interrupt until her
  mana fills - and then a 4.0 s cast that can be interrupted, on the
  script's clock rather than DBM's.

10.2 THE TOXIC POOL HAS FIVE IDS.
  DBM's GTFO warning is keyed to 250585. The script creates areatrigger
  263922, applies aura 263927 while you stand in it, and fires missiles
  250697 and 250694. The journal id in the tactics document is 250585.
  A bot watching one of these will miss the other four. Watch 250241 (the
  boss aura) if you only get one.

10.3 YAZMA'S SKEWER AND ECHOES COOLDOWNS ARE SWAPPED IN THE SCRIPT.
  Script repeats: Skewer every 32.8 s, Echoes every 12.0 s.
  DBM repeats: Skewer every 12.0 s, Echoes every 32.8 s.
  LW repeats: Skewer every 12.1 s, Echoes every 31.5 s.
  Two independent addons agree, so the script has them the wrong way round.
  First casts agree to within 1.5 s (Skewer 5.0 / 5.0 / 5.1, Echoes
  16.9 / 16.9 / 15.4).

10.4 THE OTHER TIMER SPREADS, for reference and not for action.
  Alun'za Gilded Claws first cast: 10.5 script vs 12.6 LW (2.1 s apart).
  Rezan Terrifying Visage repeat: 40.9 script and DBM vs 34.0 LW.
  Rezan Serrated Teeth repeat: 38.0 script and DBM vs 35.2 LW.
  Rezan Pursuit repeat: 38-44 script vs 41.2 DBM vs 35.2 LW.
  Vol'kaal Noxious Stench repeat: 18.2/20.6 script vs 19.4 LW.
  In every one of these the script and DBM agree and LW is the outlier, so
  the script is the operative number on this core.

10.5 127352 RISEN HONOR GUARD IS UNDESCRIBED.
  28 spawn rows from z 662.24 to z 727.71, i.e. on the entrance hall, the
  underpass, the first-hall floor, the cliff ledge and the south terrace.
  Not in the MDT enemy list (no forces, no health, no spells), not in the
  tactics document, not in any DBM or LW module.
  Wowhead gives only "Elite, Undead, patch 8.0.1". Treat it as an unknown
  pull of unknown weight.

10.6 THE MYTHIC SOULREND REFLECT DEALS ZERO.
  npc_ataldazar_soulspawn::DamageTaken computes
  `float percent = damage * (15 / 100);` and 15/100 is integer division, so
  percent is 0.0 and the reflect hits the attacker for nothing. Retail's
  Mythic rule is a 15% reflect; this core does not implement it.

10.7 ONE OF THE TWO COLLISION WALLS NEVER OPENS.
  Entry 278820 is spawned at (-911.58, 2147.01, 704.99) and at (-847.70,
  2389.78, 678.36). instance_atal_dazar.cpp keeps a single GUID, so only
  the row it saw last is set to GO_STATE_ACTIVE when the third boss dies.
  If the shortcut past the waterfall stays shut, that is why.

10.8 GATE 1-4 LOGIC READS A SWITCH THAT MAY NOT EXIST YET.
  Gates 1-4 copy their state from GO_ANCIENT_SWITCH01 inside
  OnGameObjectCreate, and AddFlag(GO_FLAG_NOT_SELECTABLE) is applied after
  the copy. If the switch loads after the gates, GetGameObject returns null
  and the gate keeps its DB state. The second switch, GO_ANCIENT_SWITCH02
  at (-729.31, 2279.52, 681.55), is stored and never read by any case.

10.9 WHAT THE TWO ADDONS DISAGREE WITH THE SCRIPT ABOUT, IN ONE LINE.
  Where all three differ, this file quotes the script and says so in the
  table; where the script and DBM agree, the script wins by default because
  it is the server.

10.10 THE YAZMA APPROACH IS MINED AND NO ADDON SAYS SO.
  Sixteen 126734 Echo of Shadra rows at z 705.46 to 730.97 (2.4.3) are live
  scripted hazards - an aura roll and a 1 yd detonation - yet they have no
  MDT entry, no force value and no DBM or LW module. A route planner that
  trusts MDT alone will walk the group straight through them.

11. WHAT IS NOT IN THIS FILE, AND WHY
================================================================================
11.1 Every boss spawn here is a [DB] row, not a script constant, because the
     scripts contain no arena positions except Vol'kaal's three totems and
     Rezan's 42 bone piles. If your world database differs from the
     AshamaneCore update of 2018-12-09, the four boss spawns move and
     nothing else in this file does.
11.2 No radius, no duration and no cooldown is quoted from a client table.
     The only radii in the file are 7 yd (Rezan's capture), 1 yd (Soulrend
     and echo contact), 50 yd (target search ranges), 100 yd (Wild Thrash
     and the target scans), and 8 yd (Toxic Leap, marked [TAC]).
11.3 The 43 pull clusters are DB rows clustered at 15 yd. They are not
     recorded pulls and they are not MDT's groups: MDT's clone coordinates
     are in map space and could not be converted to world space with any
     transform that fitted three boss points, so MDT contributed names and
     forces only.
11.4 Walkability is unverified everywhere. The route legs in 2.5 are
     straight lines with a z delta; whether a leg crosses a wall, a stair or
     a drop is not in any source I could read. The one thing that is known
     is where the gates are (2.3).
11.5 The PortLoc rows 6123 and 6124 that the two areatriggers teleport to
     are not in this SQL, so the exit and entrance landings are unverified.
11.6 127352 Risen Honor Guard has no abilities, no forces and no health in
     any source. 19 of the 43 clusters contain at least one, and those carry
     a '+' or a '?' in the force column because of it.
11.7 The purpose of the six pre-placed 125828 Soulrend rows at z 730.338 is
     unverified: the script summons its own at each victim's feet and never
     reads those six. The 16 pre-placed 126734 rows are different - they do
     have a registered script (2.6) - but whether retail treated them as
     worth clearing is unverified.
11.8 The 8.3 Awakened lieutenants are in the MDT data (Samh'rek 161243,
     Urg'roth 161124, Voidweaver Mal'thir 161241, Blood of the Corruptor
     161244, 4 forces each, 2,151,786 hp) but there is not a single spawn
     row for any of them in this database, so their obelisk positions are
     unverified. If your core does not run the Awakened affix they do not
     exist at all.
11.9 No damage, health or armour number is quoted for any boss. The MDT
     health values in 8.1 are dungeon-mob pools from the 8.3 client, not
     your server's creature_template.
