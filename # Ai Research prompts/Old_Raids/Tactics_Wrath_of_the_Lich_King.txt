14. SAPPHIRON: hide behind ice blocks for Frost Breath

15. KEL'THUZAD: P1 timed waves; P2 spread (Frost Blast

OBSIDIAN SANCTUM (map 615)
16. SARTHARION (+3 twilight drakes if left alive)
PHASES: single; lava waves sweep the platform from one
  side (called by emote); drake portals open: a player
  team enters the twilight portal to kill the disciple/
  acolyte inside or the dragon outside stays immune.
TANK: hold him centre-side, never in a wave path; drake
  tanks pick up Tenebron (whelps hatch - AoE), Shadron,
  Vesperon as they land (if up).
GROUP: MOVE for every lava wave (cross the gap in the
  wave); adds: fire elementals on enrage of lava, kill;
  portal team kills the inner add fast then returns.
GROUND: Lava Strike circles, lava waves, flame walls.
ADD ORDER: P1 portal inner add, drake adds, boss.
ABILITY (unverified ids): Lava Wave, Lava Strike,
  Twilight Portal, drake abilities (portal immunity).
AI RULE: wave call beats everything - be on the far
  side before it arrives.

EYE OF ETERNITY (map 616)
17. MALYGOS
PHASES (page): P1 ground: Arcane Breath cone leaves a
  5-sec delayed explosion on its targets (move apart
  after breath); Power Sparks drift to him (+50% his
  damage if they arrive) - kill them and STAND in the
  spark patch (+50% your damage, stacks). Vortex tosses
  the raid (heal in the air). At 50% P2: he flies;
  Nexus Lords land on discs (tank them, loot discs,
  melee ride discs up to Scions); Deep Breath: stand in
  a purple anti-magic bubble (page: discs exempt).
  P3: platform shatters - everyone on red drakes
  (vehicle): combo builders/spenders, stack engines on
  him, healers use drake heals; Static Field zones move.
TANK: P1 face him from the group; breath targets spread
  after.
GROUP: sparks die mid-path, buff patches stacked; P3
  drake rotation by role (DPS combo, heal combo).
GROUND: spark patches (stand IN), anti-magic bubbles
  (stand IN, P2), Static Field (OUT, P3).
ADD ORDER: P1 sparks, P2 Nexus Lords then Scions, boss.
ABILITY (ids unverified): as named above; 10-min enrage
  across all phases (page).
AI RULE: sparks are a race you win by standing in the
  puddle they leave - take the buff, starve the boss.

VAULT OF ARCHAVON (map 624)
18. ARCHAVON: tank swap on Stomp (he picks the tank up);

  AI RULE: if he holds your tank, the OT talks to him.
19. EMALON: adds (Tempest Warders) heal/buff him - one
  add becomes Overcharged (grows, explodes): ALL DPS
  switch to the charged add until dead, then back.
  Chain Lightning: spread. AI RULE: charged add = boss.
20. KORALON: Burning Breath cone (rotate him slowly);
  Meteor Punch splits between tanks - tanks STACK

  share the punch, nobody else shares the front.
21. TORAVON: frost orbs (kill them), Frozen Ground,
  tank swap on frost stacks (Whiteout raid pulse).
  AI RULE: swap before stacks, kill orbs on spawn.

ULDUAR (map 603)
22. FLAME LEVIATHAN (vehicle fight): siege vehicles:
  demolishers launch passengers/pyrite (reload at blue
  pyrite barrels - more damage), choppers drop oil and
  rescue, siege engines interrupt his casts (Overload =
  burn window). Kill his turret passengers via launch.
  GROUND: tar (light it), pursuit fixate - kite.
  AI RULE: your vehicle IS your character; pyrite is
  your mana - never run dry mid-burn.
23. IGNIS: golems: heat them in his furnace, drag to
  water = brittle, shatter them (they buff him while
  alive); Flame Jets knock casters (stop casting);
  Slag Pot: a player is grabbed - heal them hard, they
  come out buffed. AI RULE: a golem alive is boss damage
  up - brittle and break them on schedule.
24. RAZORSCALE: ground teams kill dwarf waves and use
  harpoon turrets (4 repaired) to chain her down; when
  grounded: burn; at 50% permanent ground: breath cone,
  Flame Buffet tanks swap, Devouring Flame patches.
  AI RULE: harpoons first - nobody DPSes air she cannot
  be reached in.
25. XT-002 DECONSTRUCTOR: heart exposed at 75/50/25%
  (kill heart = hard mode trigger - do NOT unless called;
  heart damage carries to boss). Light Bomb (run out),
  Gravity Bomb (run out, drops void zone), Tympanic
  Tantrum (heal through). Adds from scrap piles in heart
  phases (bots: bomb bots explode - kill at range).
  AI RULE: bombs carry YOU out of the group; heart is a
  button, not a target, unless the raid calls hard mode.
26. ASSEMBLY OF IRON (3): kill order sets the survivor's
  new powers: standard Steelbreaker last (his tank melts
  - tank swaps + dispel Fusion Punch D-Magic), Runemaster
  mid (runes: stand in Rune of Power OFF the boss's -
  pull him out; death rune = move), Stormcaller first
  (interrupt Chain Lightning/static). AI RULE: kill order
  is the difficulty dial; interrupts on Stormcaller.
27. KOLOGARN: arm fight: right arm dies = rubble adds +
  he takes burst; left arm grips 3 players (kill arm to
  free); Eyebeam chases a player (kite it away); body
  killed = fight ends (arms alone do not end it).
  AI RULE: gripped companions are freed by killing the
  arm, not by healing through.
28. AURIAYA: cat adds (Sanctum Sentries: they pounce and
  buff each other - tank together, kill together or CC);
  her fear (Terrifying Screech - tremor/berserker),
  Sentinel Blast interrupt; Feral Defender (9 lives,
  leaves void zone per death - tank it away each time).
  AI RULE: the little cat gets more dangerous dead -
  move its corpse zone each of its nine deaths.
29. HODIR: singed/flash freeze: snowdrifts after Flash
  Freeze - jump ON a drift or be frozen in ice (frozen
  players freed by DPS); stand in singed circles (haste
  buff) and moonbeam light; keep MOVING (Biting Cold
  stacks on still players). Free the frozen NPC mages.
  AI RULE: stillness freezes you - keep moving, stand in
  the fire on purpose.
30. THORIM: two teams: arena team survives waves; the
  gauntlet team races the hallway (traps, mini-bosses)
  to reach him before his buff maxes - then he jumps
  down: tank centre; Lightning Charge buffs his side of
  the room in thirds (rotate out of the charged third);
  Chain Lightning spread. AI RULE: gauntlet speed is the
  boss's damage - race, do not farm.
31. FREYA (+3 elders optional): add waves rotate: snap-
  lasher pack (AoE at low), storm lasher/storm water
  (interrupt), big tree (tank); mushrooms = stand under
  for her Nature's Fury; she heals unless adds die fast
  (150-stack attunement falls per add death - unverified).
  AI RULE: adds are her health bar - boss DPS happens
  only when nothing is alive.
32. MIMIRON: four vehicles in sequence: Leviathan MkII
  (mines, Napalm on a player - run out; shock blast),
  VX-001 (spin-up laser barrage - run behind; rockets
  hit marked spots - move), Aerial Unit (plasma ball
  tank at range, magnetic core drops it - ground team
  burns when down), then ALL parts together (kill within
  moments of each other). Fire patches everywhere (bots
  put them out in hard mode only). AI RULE: each body
  part has one movement answer - learn the four, in order.
33. GENERAL VEZAX: no mana regen in the room (his aura);
  healers use Saronite Vapors (green clouds -> crystal:
  standing in the puddle gives mana but stacks a DoT);
  Shadow Crash on ranged (move, leave crash puddle -
  stand in it for +damage as caster); Mark of the
  Faceless on a player (run from allies - it leeches);
  Surge of Darkness: interrupt/stop tank damage (tank
  cooldown). AI RULE: mana comes from standing in poison
  - take turns, watch your stack.
34. YOGG-SARON (+keepers optional): P1 Sara above a
  cloud-spawned guardian fight (guardians explode near
  her - tank blows them up at her); P2 brain portals:
  a team descends, kills illusion mobs, hits the brain
  (30-sec stun burn on him upstairs), exits before
  sanity 0; sanity is the real health bar (stand near
  keepers' wells as filed, unverified); P3 burn outside,
  face AWAY when he casts Lunatic Gaze (sanity).
  AI RULE: your sanity is your life - portals are a job
  with a clock, gaze means look away.
35. ALGALON (optional, timed): tank swap on Phase Punch
  (punch 5 = tank removed to another realm briefly);
  Collapsing Stars die -> black holes (never let star
  die beside the raid; feed stars on schedule); Cosmic
  Smash circles move; Big Bang (90-sec cycle): everyone
  but one tank hides IN a black hole, return after.
  AI RULE: Big Bang = get in the hole; the one who stays
  is chosen, not brave.
All Ulduar ids unverified.
