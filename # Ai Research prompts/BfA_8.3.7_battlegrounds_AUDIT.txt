WHAT THE SECTION 7 REBUILD CHANGED
==================================

The twink section was rebuilt from the client tables rather than from the
earlier curated list, and three things it had wrong are worth recording:

  1. The old filter treated the ItemSparse ItemLevel of the enchant scroll as
     the gate. That value is the scroll's own item level (1 for a classic
     scroll, 110 for a Legion one) and using it removed every neck enchant and
     every weapon enchant from Mists of Pandaria onward. The real gate is the
     ItemLevel column of SpellItemEnchantment, which is the item level the
     target piece must reach: 1 for classic and Burning Crusade, 30 for Wrath,
     55 for Naxxramas and Ulduar, 162 for Ulduar hard modes, 290 for Icecrown.
     With the correct gate the neck slot has twenty legal scrolls, including
     Mark of the Hidden Satyr (128553), which is the only damage-proc neck
     enchant in the table.

  2. The old section listed heirlooms only as prose. Heirloom.db2 has 132
     rows, 131 wearable, and 98 of those are legal at 19; all 98 are printed
     with their 8.3.7 id and their pre-8.0 id.

  3. The old section said hunters and shamans could not wear the mail table
     and that warrior and paladin wore mail until 40. Both halves were the
     pre-7.0.3 rule. In 8.3.7 the armour type is a class property with no
     level gate: SkillRaceClassInfo 293 (Plate Mail) carries ClassMask 3 at
     MinLevel 1 with ability spell 750 and no SpellLevels row, and 413 (Mail)
     carries ClassMask 68 at MinLevel 1 with ability spell 8737 at BaseLevel
     1. Sections 7.3, 7.6 and 7.11 were rewritten to the client rule, and the
     class table in 7.11 now reads plate for warrior and paladin and mail for
     hunter and shaman.

Every enchant row printed in section 7.9 is re-resolved during the audit
through ItemEffect -> SpellEffect(Effect 53) -> SpellItemEnchantment and
checked against the scroll's required level, the enchant's character range and
its item-level figure.
