=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

This map is the Nagrand layout in Uldum art. The wiki is explicit:

  "The map's design is based on the simplistic Nagrand Arena.
   The only difference between the two arenas is the art style
   and the direction of the pillars."

So: four pillars, same spacing and same open-field shape as
Nagrand Arena, rotated relative to the Nagrand arrangement.

Verified: the server spawns no LoS object on this map. Only the
two gates and the two Shadow Sight orbs are game objects, so the
pillars are world model geometry with no coordinates in server
data.

Pillar count: 4 (inherited from the Nagrand layout)
Pillar positions: unverified (no readable source with x, y, z)
Pillar rotation versus Nagrand: unverified as an angle; the wiki
  says only that the direction differs

Useful cross-check from the orb axis: on this map the two orbs
sit 91.5 units apart along Y, perpendicular to the 128.3-unit
start axis along X. On Nagrand the orbs sit 108.9 apart on a
diagonal. That is consistent with "pillars rotated" being a real
difference between the two maps.

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Because the map is Nagrand with rotated pillars, the opening is a
race for the pillars, but the approach angle is different from
Nagrand - the enemy comes along the X axis here, so the pillars
do not line up the same way against your healer.

Melee DPS:
  Cross toward the nearest pillar and take the far side of it in
  one move; the short 64-unit run from spawn to centre means both
  teams arrive together
  Use the pillar to break the first caster casts, then fight on
  the pillar's shadowed side

Ranged DPS:
  Take a pillar early and hold max range. The map is flat, so the
  pillars are the only LoS on the field
  Rotate around the pillar rather than standing still

Healers:
  Choose a pillar that keeps your partner inside healing range;
  on this map the pillars sit at a different angle to Nagrand,
  so re-check the geometry at the start rather than copying a
  Nagrand habit
  Do not take the centre; it is open on every side

General opener notes:
  Both teams are 64.2 units from the centre, so the first contact
  is simultaneous
  The orbs sit off the start axis, so picking one costs a detour
  away from the fight - worth it only against a stealth comp

Only Shadow Sight exists on this map.

  X: -10717.63

  Orientation: 1.555 rad
  About 45.8 units from the arena centre

Shadow Sight B (GO 184664):
  X: -10716.6
  Y: 475.364
  Z: 24.4131
  Orientation: 0
  About 45.9 units from the arena centre

The two orbs are 91.5 units apart and sit on the Y axis, i.e.
perpendicular to the 128.3-unit start axis. Both are level with
the arena floor to within 0.0003 units.

Spawn: 60 s after the gates open (core spawn call)
Respawn after pickup: 120 s (object spawn parameter)
Spell: 34709 Shadow Sight - 15 s, stealth and invisibility
  detection, +5% damage taken while active
The wiki quotes 90 s as the arena appearance time, the BfA core
spawns them at 60 s; the exact retail 8.3.7 delay is unverified
wiki: two orbs "appear on opposite sides of the Arena", which
  matches the Y-axis placement measured above

No health, mana, haste or berserking pickups are spawned here.
The core spawns exactly four objects on this map - two gates and
two orbs - so any other pickup object would have to come from the
map itself, and none is placed there.

=== 7. MATCH FLOW NOTES ===

Gates close before the start and open together.
Worldstate 0xE1A is set to 1 on this map.
Dampening: the arena did not have Dampening until the hotfix of
  2014-10-30, which "should now correctly" apply it. In 8.3.7 it
  applies like every other arena.
Arena Preparation is applied on zone-in.
No out-of-bounds or under-map return points are wired in the BfA
core for this map: the core handles only triggers 8451 and 8452
and sends everything else to the default handler.
