WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 1116 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: 71 of the 72 are resolved, and again none from creature_template - all 72 master names were run against both
  creature_template exports in the repo and none matches a row, so those exports stop before Warlords of Draenor. 55 entries come
  from section 3.1 of the research document and 16 were read this run from wiki NPC pages or Wowhead, where the Elinks-NPC id is
  the Wowhead id: Ja'kana 78699, Ernst ten Bosch 85959, Bron Skyhorn 79407, Mok'ra 81053, Skyguard Amelia 81060, Roka 81078,
  Zamara 81064, Vamuun 81289, Dungar Longdrink 81103 (Lunarfall, not his Stormwind id 352), Biggy Warprofits 83924, Tal 82346
  (Wor'var, not his Thunder Bluff id 2995), Windscreamer 84508, Itara 86685, Nasher 90552, Nisha 81674. The one entry left
  unverified is 1524 Iron Docks, and the reason is in that block.
  Three of the 57 section-3.1 rows were wrong, in the same way: where the export carries an Alliance node and a Horde node at the
  same named place, that table gave both nodes the Alliance master and the Alliance entry. 1454 Exarch's Refuge was printed with
  Niaa 81077 instead of Roka 81078, 1512 Bastion Rise with Rangari Yaval 84507 instead of Windscreamer 84508, and 1644 the Horde
  Iron Front with Skyguard Blann 91940 instead of Nasher 90552. All three are corrected here and each block says so.
  Gate evidence: five of the 72 nodes are gated and 67 are not. Only one of the five is a quest gate - 29229 on 1520 Breaker's
  Crown, after quest 35730, 35049 or 34992. The other four are race masks, which is how the export keeps a faction's copy of a
  shared place out of the other faction's list: 33173 on 1620 Lion's Watch and 924 on 1645 the Alliance Iron Front carry the
  Alliance mask, 33174 on 1621 Vol'mar and 923 on 1644 the Horde Iron Front carry the Horde mask. No Draenor taxi node is gated on
  level, class, achievement or reputation.
  Two flight master names serve two nodes each, and both cases are real rather than a copy error: Nisha holds Breaker's Crown
  (1520) and Iron Docks (1524), and Itara holds Tranquil Court (1556) and the Temple of Karabor node (1567). Nisha is a dread
  raven rather than a humanoid, and Wowhead carries several Nisha ids: the flight-master version at Breaker's Crown is 81674,
  while the id section 3.1 printed for both nodes, 81677, is her as Rexxar's mount with no location. The second of those
  pairs is a quirk of the export - 1567 sits 1.2 yards from 1556 and has no TaxiPath row in either direction, so it is a node
  nothing can fly to or from, and its master is read from the shared roost.
  Faction: TaxiNodes.Flags gives 23 Alliance-only roosts, 21 Horde-only roosts and 28 with both bits set; each block prints the
  bits it actually found rather than inferring a side from the name.
  Travel time is derived, not read: TaxiPath.Cost is copper and TaxiPathNode.Delay is 0 on 86,384 of 86,627 rows, so neither is a
  clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Every one of the 603 directed rows in section 1116 targets
  another node of this same set, so no leg in this file carries an off-map tag, not even the two Ashran nodes. Distances are per
  direction: the row 1520 -> 1524 reads 2109 yd while 1524 -> 1520 reads 2267 yd. One pair has a row in only one direction -
  1574 Joz's Rylaks -> 1502 for 1738 yd, with no row back - so the 1502 block prints that leg as a reverse row only. Every other
  pair in this section has a row each way.
