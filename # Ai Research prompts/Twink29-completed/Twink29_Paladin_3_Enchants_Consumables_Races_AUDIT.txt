AUDIT - Twink29_Paladin_3_Enchants_Consumables_Races.txt
File under audit: 222 lines, step 3 of 3. 23 claims checked: 16 pass,
3 corrected, 4 unverified.
Sources used: the audited profession files already in this repository
(Enchanting, Blacksmithing, Alchemy, Cooking and First Aid, Inscription,
Engineering, Leatherworking), and the two earlier paladin files for
internal consistency. Nothing new was fetched for this audit - every number
this file quotes comes from a source the set has already audited once, which
is the cheapest kind of audit and also the most likely to inherit an error.

1. THE OPENING FRAME
   1  PASS  "A paladin is the second class in this set that can use nearly
      all of the classic enchant list." Counted: it keeps the armour cloak,
      the chest, the Strength bracers, the boots and the weapon enchant, and
      wastes only the gloves. That is six of seven, one more than the monk
      and four more than the mage. The claim is right and the file could
      have said so more strongly.
   2  CORRECTED, AND IT IS THE FILE'S HEADLINE CLAIM  "There is no Strength
      elixir at 29." What the audited Alchemy survey actually supports is
      narrower: the only Strength elixir that survey found is Elixir of
      Giants 9206, and it requires level 38. The file now says "no Strength
      elixir was found" and invites a correction if a low-level one exists.
      Claiming an absence is exactly what this set's rule against invention
      is for, and a survey is evidence, not proof.
   3  UNVERIFIED  "A paladin has mana in all three specs and spends it on
      Flash of Light." True as I know the class, not read on a page, and it
      decides whether 3385 Lesser Mana Potion belongs in a Protection
      paladin's bag.

      read for a paladin, +5 Strength on a bracer, and it is the mirror
      image of the mage and monk files where the same enchant is worthless.
   7  PASS  The gloves row is honest: 13815 agility is the only gloves

      270 marked ALT.
   8  PASS  4265 Heavy Armor Kit with its item level 15 floor, and 8173 and
      15564 marked ALT with the right reasons - the Thick kit fits items of

   9  PASS  The neck and ring block is unchanged from the mage file after

5. PROFESSIONS AND RACES
   20 PASS  Blacksmithing plus Leatherworking with the Engineering and
      Alchemy swaps, and every item named carries an id from an audited
      file: 4265, 18948, 4390, 4388, 4397, 4381. The Enchanting paragraph is
      right for the same reason it was right in the mage and monk files - a
      29 cannot train past classic 225, so the neck and ring enchants are
      ALT by definition.
   21  CORRECTED  "Three breaks is the best defensive racial in the
      bracket." A human paladin has two - the racial and the PvP trinket -
      and only a Retribution one that also spends its single PvP slot on
      Blessing of Sanctuary gets a third. Reworded to say two, or three for
      that build. The file was counting a talent it had itself established
      costs the only PvP slot the character has.
   22  CORRECTED  "A paladin has no ranged interrupt." It has one interrupt,
      Rebuke, and it is melee range. Reworded so the grenade argument rests
      on the range rather than on a missing ability.
   23  UNVERIFIED, AND LABELLED IN THE FILE  The allied-race half of the
      paladin roster; the Dark Iron Fireblood, Lightforged Light's Reckoning
      and Zandalari Regeneratin' effects and ids; and the claim that thirteen
      other races cannot be paladins at all. The classic five races and their
      racial ids are the part of this section the set can stand behind.

6. WHAT TO CHANGE
   Done: the Strength elixir claim is now an absence in the sources rather
   than an absence in the game, the human break count no longer double-counts
   a PvP talent, and Rebuke is acknowledged. The rest of the file is a
   straight carry-forward of audited numbers and reads cleanly.
   The set's shape for paladins is now complete and consistent: an item file
   that has to explain why there is no plate, a talent file with one pick and
   a Holy PvP shrug, and this file, whose best paragraph is the one telling a
   paladin that its four crafted Strength pieces are the only plate-shaped
   things it can make and that they are all out of the rule.
