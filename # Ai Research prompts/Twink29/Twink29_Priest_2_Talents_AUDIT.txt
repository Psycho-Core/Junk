AUDIT - Twink29_Priest_2_Talents.txt
File under audit: step 2 of 3, 121 lines. 18 claims checked: 13 pass,
1 corrected, 4 unverified.
Pages used: the priest talent pages for Schism, Castigation, Twist of Fate,
Trail of Light, Enlightenment, Fortress of the Mind and Shadowy Insight, and
the PvP talent pages for Trinity, Psyfiend, Dome of Light, Driven to Madness
and Spirit of the Redeemer. Every one of them was read for its patch-change
line, not for its current tooltip - the wiki serves 12.x pages and three of
these talents were renumbered or redesigned after 8.3.7.

1. THE GATING CLAIMS
   1  PASS  Seven normal talent rows at 15/30/45/60/75/90/100, so a level 29
      has one row, and three PvP slots at 20/40/70, so a level 29 has one
      slot. This is the BfA shape and it is what the file opens with.
   2  PASS  "Some PvP talents carry their own level above 20." Proven by the
      two traps below, which is why this is a pass and not a general claim:
      the file does not say every talent is gated, only that some are.

2. DISCIPLINE
   3  PASS  Schism was a level 15 Discipline talent in BfA: the 10.0.0 line
      reads "Now a row 3 Discipline talent (was a level 15 Discipline
      talent)". The 40% figure is right for 8.3.7 - 9.0.1 cut it to 25% and
      8.3.0 is the patch that limited it to Priest spells and abilities.
   4  PASS  Castigation, spell 193134, "was a level 15 Discipline talent"
      per its own 10.0.0 line, and its effect is Penance firing one extra
      bolt. Both the level and the effect are read.
   5  PASS  Twist of Fate, spell 109142, level 15 for Discipline from 7.2.5,
      and 45 for Shadow from 8.0.1. The 20% damage and healing is the 7.0.3
      value. This one talent is the source of two lines in the file, the
      Discipline pick and the Shadow trap, and both are right.
   6  CORRECTED  The Discipline Schism row prints "214621?" with the id
      marked unverified. That is the right instinct and the wrong place: a
      guess with a question mark next to it still reads as a number to
      anybody skimming. The row now reads "id unverified" with no digits,
      and section 6 keeps the statement that six ids in the file are 7.x-era
      ids and the rest are not proof.

3. HOLY
   7  PASS  Trail of Light, spell 200128, was a level 15 Holy talent per its
      10.0.0 line, and 8.1.0 set the replicated share to 35% and added Heal
      to the effect. The file's "35%" and "Flash Heal and Heal" both follow.
   8  PASS  Enlightenment, spell 193155, level 15 Holy, "regenerate mana
      10% faster" from the 7.0.3 redesign. The file is explicit that the
      25% and the 10%-in-PvP figures on the page are 2026 hotfixes, which is
      the distinction that keeps this honest.
   9  UNVERIFIED  The third Holy level-15 talent. It is named nowhere in the
      file and that is deliberate: the wiki lists current rows, and the
      priest abilities page cannot enumerate a BfA row. Two of three is what
      the sources support.

4. SHADOW
   10 PASS  Fortress of the Mind, spell 193195, level 15 Shadow, removed in
      10.0.0, +10% Mind Flay and Mind Blast damage and +20% Insanity. Every
      number in the row matches the page.
   11 PASS  Shadowy Insight: 8.0.1 "Moved to the level 15 talent row (was
      level 75)", removed in 9.0.1. The file's claim that it is a BfA talent
      and legal at 29 is right, and so is the note that its BfA spell id is
      unverified - the page's current id is a post-10.0.0 spell.
   12 PASS  Death and Madness is the 9.0.1 replacement and is absent from

   13 UNVERIFIED  The third Shadow level-15 talent, same reasoning as claim 9.

5. THE PVP SLOT
   14 PASS  Trinity, spell 214205, level 20. There is no 9.0.1 line on the
      page, and 9.0.1 is the patch that renumbered Psyfiend (40 to 20) and
      Dome of Light (70 to 44), so a talent with no such line stayed where
      it was. The effect lines are right for 8.3.7 as well: +20% Atonement
      transfer from 8.2.0 and +25% crit from 8.1.0, with the 9.1.0 removal
      of the transfer not yet in force.
   15 PASS  Psyfiend was a level 40 PvP talent in BfA - "8.0.1: Now a level
      40 PvP talent (was a rank 10 honor talent)" - and 9.0.1 lowered it to
      20. The trap is stated correctly, including the warning that current
      guides list it at 20.
   16 PASS  Dome of Light was a level 70 PvP talent in BfA per its 8.0.1
      line, lowered to 44 in 9.0.1 and removed in 10.1.0. Nothing a 29 can
      use, exactly as the file says.
   17 UNVERIFIED  The BfA levels of Ultimate Radiance, Spirit of the Redeemer
      and Driven to Madness. None of the three pages prints a level in its
      8.0.1 line - all three were "converted from a rank N honor talent" -
      and the levels shown now (23, 29, 23) are post-squish numbers that
      cannot be mapped back without a table this file does not have. Holy
      and Shadow are therefore left with an empty PvP slot rather than a
      guessed one.
   18 UNVERIFIED  Whether any priest PvP talent outside the three checked is
      legal at 20 in 8.3.7. The file claims only what it checked, and an
      empty slot on two specs is the cost of that honesty.
