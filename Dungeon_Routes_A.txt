ATAL'DAZAR — instance map 1763; Wowhead zone 9028 (different namespace)
Bosses: Priestess Alun'za (creature 129614), Vol'kaal (129399), Rezan (dungeon boss creature 122963 in a BfaCore implementation; Wowhead also lists dungeon Rezan as 143577; which is spawned by this specific core/difficulty is unverified), then Yazma (129412). The first THREE can be fought in any order. Safe landmark route: from entrance climb the outer terrace toward Alun'za's golden sanctum; clear the mobs guarding the approach, kill her. Return toward the central platform, take the opposite/upper balcony toward Vol'kaal, avoiding the tiki-mask flame jets and hidden Shadowblade Stalkers; kill the mobs that body-block your approach, then kill Vol'kaal. Return to central stairs and descend into the lower bone-strewn pit to kill Rezan. When all three die, the waterfall/barrier at the top of the central approach disappears and the stairway to Yazma becomes passable. Climb to Yazma's upper-west platform, dodge shadow spiders, kill her. Alternative branch: descend to Rezan first, or approach Vol'kaal before Alun'za; the upper routes and lower pit connect by central stairs, and a bridge drop can shortcut to the pit if safe. No fixed mandatory order between the first three. At Alun'za the red Tainted Blood pools are boss mechanics, NOT clickable levers; at Vol'kaal kill all THREE Reanimation Totems nearly together (otherwise Reanimate restores them on Heroic/Mythic) to remove Bad Voodoo; do not mistake totems for route switches. The Ancient Switch at the bottom of the stairs by the central platform raises BOTH gates (contemporary route guide; exact GO entry and coordinates unverified). Do not set a fictitious interaction ID; if the gate is shut, pathfind to the platform and locate/test the switch. Waterfall/stair transition after three bosses is AUTOMATIC, not a key. Mandatory trash: enemies engaged on each chosen approach; specific packs that script-open the waterfall: unverified (boss kills are the documented gate). Skips: side packs on balconies and central courtyard can be avoided with proximity/line-of-sight planning, but hidden Stalkers can aggro; never script walking through their spawn as an unconditional safe skip. World boss xyz: unverified.

FREEHOLD — instance map 1754; Wowhead zone 9164
Bosses: Skycap'n Kragg (creature 129732), Council o' Captains (Raoul 126847, Eudora 129431, Jolly 126845), Ring of Booty/Trothak (126969), Harlan Sweete (129440). From entrance cross Smuggler's Run toward Kragg's roost; clear one Enforcer/Mastiff pack adjacent to the boss so the pull does not add them, defeat Kragg. Cross the long rope bridge into The Rum Quarter; choose a safe street (right-hand town edge is a documented option) to reach the council yard. BEFORE engaging council, locate the week's marked crew invitation event: (1) Bilge Rats: speak to the highlighted vulpera/grog giver and have the party drink to recruit Eudora; (2) Blacktooth Brawlers: start the knuckleduster brawl at the training dummy and defeat its responding wave to recruit Raoul; (3) Cutwater Corsairs: follow Murphy the pug (creature 130467) until he sleeps, right-click him for the jail key, then open the captive crew member's cage to recruit Jolly. Exact dog/cage/keys GO and item entries, exact number of drinks and exact positions: unverified. Only ONE crew event is selected by the instance's rotation; check the currently active event rather than attempting all three. An allied captain aids the party and some corresponding trash becomes friendly/disappears; skipping the event leaves all three captains hostile/Under One Banner and is not the safe route. Kill the remaining hostile council members. Go from the Rum Quarter toward the open Ring of Booty (the streets split around the town: use a safe side, not an assumed hard gate). At the arena initiate the ring event by interacting with/speaking to its event NPC (exact NPC entry and gossip option unverified), chase/CATCH Lightning the pig, defeat Ludwig Von Tortollan, then fight Trothak. Ring event is a SEQUENCE of scripted stages, not just a Trothak pull; no confirmed lever or door key. From ring head back toward the rope-bridge approach and onward to Sweete's Landing, kill the Irontide Enforcer/guard packs controlling the narrow last approach (these are reported unavoidable), then Harlan. Other town patrols and non-route packs can be bypassed if no aggro and M+ enemy forces already planned. At Harlan, Cannon Barrage and his grenadiers are combat mechanics, not a route-wide cannon click. Opening/clicking treasure chests for an achievement is optional, not an access requirement. World boss xyz: unverified.

KING'S REST — instance map 1762; Wowhead zone 9526 (apostrophe/spelling may vary)
Fixed bosses: The Golden Serpent (135322), Mchimba the Embalmer (134993), Council of Tribes (Kula the Butcher 135475; Aka'ali/Zanazal creature entries unverified), King Dazar (136160). Enter the tomb, descend to Hall of the Honored Dead; THREE doorways: the Approach of the Worthy is initially open, while Tomb of the Unworthy and Resting Halls of the Conquered are initially CLOSED. Take open approach to Lustrous Apse, clear the mobs around the balcony and group up at the Serpentine Seal on the pillar. CLICK the Serpentine Seal to initiate the judgement: several enemy groups appear before the Golden Serpent can be fought. Seal GO entry and use-spell: unverified; do not call it a lootable key. Defeat spawned groups then serpent; kill Animated Gold before it reaches boss. Backtrack/advance through the now-accessible doorway (exact door unlock trigger/GO entry unverified) into the gauntlet of former Zandalari kings and queens: defeat the FOUR sequential packs; their order can vary. They are scripted progress waves, not optional trash. Proceed to Chamber of Eternal Preservation and defeat Mchimba; when Entomb puts an ally into one crypt, allies must CLICK the correct occupied coffin (the imprisoned player can signal via Struggle). Opening the wrong crypt releases an extra mummy; coffin GO entries unverified. Continue through the tomb's next passage into Halls of Ash, clear the necessary spectral packs/totems on the chosen corridor and defeat the Council of Tribes (Kula, Aka'ali and Zanazal appear one at a time in a BfA rotation that changes by run/week; identify the active councilor rather than hard-code their order; these are encounter phases, not separate route branches). After council a bridge forms and a waterfall stops, exposing the route into the Crypt of the First King; these transitions are scripted, not player-operated levers. Defeat Shadow of Zul if it blocks the final approach (exact 8.3.7 requirement and NPC entry unverified), then Dazar. Optional adjacent spectral groups/side alcoves can be avoided when path is clear; the seal's initial packs and post-serpent gauntlet cannot be skipped for ordinary progression. Encounter's Explosive Totem and Mchimba's occupied coffin require combat action, not an inventory key. World boss xyz: unverified.

SHRINE OF THE STORM — instance map 1864; Wowhead zone 9525
Linear bosses: Aqu'sirr (134056), Tidesage Council (Brother Ironhull 134063 and Galecaller Faye 134058), Lord Stormsong (139737), Vol'zith the Whisperer (134069). From entrance enter Tidewater Sanctum, through the first Tidesage mural and past entrance Shrine Templars, Temple Attendants and Tidesage Spiritualists toward Aqu'sirr's flooded platform; approaching Lord Stormsong's apparition triggers Aqu'sirr's summon automatically, with no documented player click. Kill Aqu'sirr and his 50%-health Aqualing split to reform him. Cross onward to the Fonts of Cleansing; fight or carefully avoid patrolling Windspeaker Heldis and Runecarver Sorn, and deal with the Guardian Elemental on the selected path. Defeat the Council in the fonts (wards are boss-created ground effects to reposition around, not doors/gongs). Continue toward the long Blessed Path bridge into Storm's End. The three bridge packs BEFORE Lord Stormsong can be skipped only with actual stealth/Shroud/invisibility and coordinated party movement (or fought); an unsupported walking path through them is not a valid skip. Interrupt Deepsea Ritualists/clear necessary chokepoint enemies on the chosen route. Kill Lord Stormsong, then follow the passage deeper into the Shrine of Shadows to Vol'zith; kill the obstructing void/temple trash and trigger final boss by approach/attack. During Vol'zith's Grasp of the Sunken City intermission, split-party adds must be killed to return to the boss; this is a REQUIRED combat wave, not a lever. No ordinary-route key, gong, door control, or inventory item is verified for these boss transitions; quest-only Staff of Storms/Ritual of Safe Passage is NOT a required dungeon-completion key. Other side trash can be skipped if path is safe; exact mobs required by a progression door: unverified. Boss locations by UI map percent (NOT x/y/z): Aqu'sirr approx 46.5,40.7; council 24.7,39.8; Stormsong 40.2,53.7; Vol'zith 69.5,35.4 on their respective two UI floors. Their UI-map IDs/instance-world xyz: unverified. The two floors' map percents cannot be compared across floors to infer direction.
