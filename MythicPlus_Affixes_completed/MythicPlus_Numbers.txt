MYTHIC+ NUMBERS — retail BfA season 4, patch 8.3.7 / build 35662 reference

SCOPING: Retail season-4 numbers below are not measurements of this TrinityCore-based private server. Affix and spell data in current online databases may have changed after 8.3.7. Modifier tables, item bonus lists, exact reward context and server-side spell scripts for build 35662 were not obtained: those are unverified, not silently reconstructed from current retail. No creature map/x/y/z or item ID applies to a universal scaling coefficient.

Exact stored client coefficients, server rounding at each level, damage exceptions, specific health or melee values: unverified.

AFFIX TIERS (BfA): +2 Fortified or Tyrannical, +4 second affix, +7 third affix, +10 Awakened fourth affix. Affix IDs below are keystone-affix records, not necessarily spell IDs. A spell that merely ADDS an affix to a keystone is not the creature effect. Numbers described as historical come from BfA/contemporary sources; where a spell page now differs, its current tooltip is not proof of the 8.3.7 coefficient.

Fortified (affix 10): non-boss enemies +20% maximum health, up to +30% damage; exact exceptions by mob unverified. Underlying 8.3.7 creature buff spell ID: unverified (268037 is the add-to-keystone spell, NOT its creature buff).
Tyrannical (affix 9): boss health +40%, damage up to +15% (pre-2021 nerf); BfA tooltip concerned bosses, not today’s 25% health wording. Underlying 8.3.7 creature buff spell ID: unverified.
Bolstering (affix 7): nearby surviving non-boss enemies in combat gain stacking maximum health/damage on a non-boss death. BfA-era affix describes +20% health and +20% damage; Bolster spell 209859 (current database tooltip has changed to +10% health for 20 seconds, and a later PTR says +15% health/+20% damage): actual 8.3.7 spell values and stacking/linger duration unverified; do NOT substitute today’s tooltip. Range shown on 209859: 30 yards.
Raging (affix 6): non-boss enemy enrages at 30% HP and increases damage; BfA-era guides disagree on +75% versus +100%, and the precise 8.3.7 value is unverified. A later 2021 nerf reduced a then-current +75% to +50%: that does not prove the earlier BfA coefficient. WoWDB associates Enrage 178658 (current listing +100% damage) with this affix, but the exact BfA applied aura ID and coefficient remain unverified. Current spell 228318 now gives CC immunity, so it must NOT be copied as a BfA damage-enrage spell merely because 2019 player UI filters referenced its ID. Soothe/removal of Enrage is possible. Do not use the 2023 replacement CC-immunity behavior.
Bursting (affix 11): a non-boss death applies stacking Burst to players for 4 seconds, ticking Shadow damage; effect spell 240443. Exact season-4 percentage PER stack unverified (2019 hotfix changed earlier percentages; modern spell data is not a build-35662 combat log). Do not implement later keystone-scaled fixed-damage Shadowlands version as BfA.
Sanguine (affix 8): slain non-boss enemies leave a pool: allied enemies heal for 5% of THEIR max HP per second (226510), players take 15% of THEIR max HP as damage each second (226512). Current spell listing says one-minute aura; actual pool lifetime/position and server triggering spell: unverified. Get enemies out of pools.
Necrotic (affix 4): enemy melee hits apply stacking Necrotic Wound 209858, dealing periodic damage and reducing healing/absorption per stack; contemporary affix guide reports 2% per stack and a 9-second refreshed duration. Current 209858 tooltip lists a 5-second duration; final BfA 8.3.7 duration and damage per tick on this core: unverified. Kiting/stopping melee allows stacks to expire.
Grievous (affix 12): injured players below 90% HP accumulate Grievous Wound 240559; stacking periodic Physical damage, removed by healing above threshold. Current listing has 3-second tick and scripted 90% threshold; EXACT BfA per-stack percent, maximum stacks and out-of-combat reset for this core: unverified.
Quaking (affix 14): periodic player Quake 240447, 2.5-second warning, spreads a 5-yard shockwave, damages nearby party members and interrupts casts; spell database has 40% target max HP for nearby ally hit. Timing of random triggering and exact on-self handling in build 35662: unverified. Avoid stacking and stop casts.
Explosive (affix 13): combat enemies periodically spawn an Explosives unit (2019/2020 player-observed NPC 120651), which casts Explosion 240446 if not killed. Current listing: 8-second cast, 60-yard radius, damage 40% of player maximum HP; 2020 affix guides differ (50% and 6 seconds): exact 8.3.7 live server values unverified. Low-health orbs should be directly targeted; exact Enemy Forces credit on this private core: unverified (do not award count without checking its criteria script). Avoid using unrelated Legion invasion orb NPC 110395.
Volcanic (affix 3): distant players can get a flame eruption during combat; Volcanic Plume 209862: 2-second cast, 2-yard radius, 20% target max HP Fire hit and knock-up in current listing. Proc frequency, player distance condition and exact season-4 script: unverified.
Skittish (affix 2): tanks generate reduced effective threat against enemies; precise threat coefficient and server aura spell ID unverified; no evidence supports an invented fixed 50% coefficient.
Teeming (affix 5): predetermined extra non-boss enemies/changed packs and a correspondingly higher Enemy Forces requirement; no universal spawn count, universal percent increase or spell ID for spawns verified. MDT supports teeming placements separately from normal placements.
Overflowing (affix 1): NOT ACTIVE in BfA 8.3.7 (removed in Legion patch 7.2.0); historical effect: excess healing converts into a heal-absorption shield, Overflow spell 221772. Historical conversion coefficient: unverified for this build; do not schedule the affix.
Relentless (affix ID 15, listed by WoWDB): proposed/test affix, NOT established as an active 8.3.7 seasonal rotation; historical description says non-bosses gain temporary loss-of-control immunity. Executable spell ID, immunity duration and activation conditions: unverified. Do not activate it by default.

TIMER AND KEY REWARDS: Let T be the dungeon-specific allowed duration and t the elapsed time after completion of required bosses AND Enemy Forces. On time t <= T: key upgrades by +1; with more than 20% of T spare (t < 0.8T): +2; with more than 40% of T spare (t < 0.6T): +3. Exact equality/rounding at 0.8T and 0.6T in the core: unverified. These are KEYSTONE levels, not a promise of two/three separate end chests. Late completion: no upgrade; BfA key degradation on late finish is described by contemporary guides, but precise server implementation unverified.

WEEKLY CHEST: BfA weekly Challenger’s Chest / Grand Challenger’s Bounty becomes claimable after the next REGIONAL weekly reset following a completed keystone run (not immediately on finishing it). Its item level follows the highest completed key during the preceding week, capped at +15; it is one random season-4 piece from eligible dungeon/spec loot, not the later multi-choice Great Vault. It additionally grants Titan Residuum (currency; at +10 = 1,700, +11 = 1,790, +12 = 1,880, +13 = 1,970, +14 = 2,060, +15 = 2,150, with +90 per further level per Blizzard’s launch post), Artifact Power and a new Mythic Keystone; amounts for lower keys, AP amount, gold and cap of Residuum beyond +15: unverified. Since BfA 8.1, Azerite armor is not the normal random weekly dungeon gear piece: Titan Residuum purchases it separately. Season-4 weekly reward items were always Corrupted per Blizzard’s 8.3 launch explanation; no Warforge/Titanforge. Season-transition chests opened before January 28 2020 (NA) still contained season-3 rewards and no new Residuum; regional reset dates differ.

ITEM CONTEXT / BONUS IDS: An item link can carry an itemContext, a count and ordered bonus IDs, and modifier fields. The precise build-35662 itemContext plus bonus-ID combination that awards each season-4 dungeon or weekly row above, and the eligible base item IDs for each dungeon, are UNVERIFIED: no build-35662 ItemBonus/ItemBonusTree/ItemContext/loot-table mapping was inspected. Do not assume one generic bonus ID per item level or that adding +30 to a season-3 bonus ID produces correct 8.3.7 gear; Corruption, sockets and item variants can have separate bonuses. A client presentation alone does not implement server loot.
