1. THE HEART OF AZEROTH
================================================================================================

1.1  THE ITEM AND WHERE IT COMES FROM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Item id 158075, "Heart of Azeroth", neck slot, artifact quality [T][H]. TrinityCore names
  the id as a constant: ITEM_ID_HEART_OF_AZEROTH = 158075 [T].
  It is granted by the quest "The Heart of Azeroth" (quest id 51211) [H]; the chain it
  belongs to runs on into "Infusing the Heart" (quest id 52428), where the Heart absorbs
  the azerite in the Chamber of Heart [H]. Without it azerite armor does not drop: the
  same item drops as its ordinary non-azerite version, and world quests whose reward is
  azerite pay nothing [W].
  The Heart starts at level 1 and item level 280, and every level adds exactly 2 item
  levels [C][W]. The client table below carries that progression to level 129.

1.2  THE FORGE: WHERE YOU SPEND AND UNLOCK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Heart Forge is inside the Chamber of Heart, the titan vault under Silithus [W].
  Access is a Titan Translocator next to Magni's Encampment in the BfA phase of Silithus,
  "Silithus: The Wound" [W]. The client map ids are:

    map id | map name (Map.dbc)              | type | notes
    -------+---------------------------------+------+------------------------------
    1817   | Silithus: The Wound             | open | the outdoor approach [C]
    1929   | Chamber of Heart                | open | as shipped in 8.0.1 [C]
    2215   | Chamber of Heart - Repaired     | open | the 8.2 rebuild [C]
    2257   | Chamber of Heart - Scenario     | scen | the intro scenario [C]

  Area ids: 9667 (8.0.1), 10504 (8.2 rebuild), 12905 (scenario) [W]. Coordinates of the
  translocator, of Magni, of MOTHER and of the forge are UNVERIFIED: no client table and no
  page I could read prints them, and I will not invent them. They are in your world
  database: the creature positions on maps 1929 and 2215 are the forge, MOTHER and Magni.

  The forge and the essence interface are the same object: you can also right-click the
  Heart itself to open the essence slots once the forge is unlocked [W]. The forge is
  unlocked by the quest line that begins with "Essential Empowerment" from Magni in
  Mezzamere or Newhome and ends with the quest "The Heart Forge" [W]. Completing it in
  8.3 sets the Heart to level 50 if it is lower (8.2 set it to 35) [W][B].
  Creature entries for Magni Bronzebeard, MOTHER and Wrathion as they stand in the Chamber
  in 8.3.7: UNVERIFIED. The wiki infoboxes carry current-era ids (Magni 214890, Wrathion
  210077), not the BfA copies, and no BfA-era id I could read is confirmed.

2. HOW THE NEXT LEVEL IS COMPUTED
================================================================================================
  TrinityCore's implementation, in words [T]: the azerite required for a level is the
  larger of two numbers - the level's BaseExperienceToNextLevel multiplied by the
  knowledge multiplier, or the level's MinimumExperienceToNextLevel floor.
  A character's azerite is applied in a loop that may cross several levels in one grant
  [T], and the constants in that source are [T]:

    constant                            | value
    ------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------
    ITEM_ID_HEART_OF_AZEROTH            | 158075
    MAX_AZERITE_ITEM_LEVEL              | 129
    MAX_AZERITE_ITEM_KNOWLEDGE_LEVEL    | 30
    knowledge epoch                     | 2020-01-14, one step per week

  The floor matters. MinimumExperienceToNextLevel equals the base for levels 1 to 8
  (350 to 700) and is exactly 1,000 for every level from 9 to 129 [C]. That is why the cost
  of levels 9 to 69 never falls below 1,000 however high knowledge climbs, and why a
  level-70 character in 8.3.7 needs 1,000 azerite for 71, not 341 [C][T].
  The second client column, MinimumExperienceToNextLevel, is NOT a running total: it is
  flat 1,000 from level 9 on [C]. A server that sums it will produce nonsense.

3. AZERITE KNOWLEDGE: THE WEEKLY MULTIPLIER
================================================================================================
  What the system is, from the sources [W][H][B]:
    - Azerite Knowledge is the BfA catch-up system. Each rank reduces the azerite REQUIRED
      for the next level by 30%; it does not increase what content pays out [H][B].
    - It is applied automatically at the weekly reset; there is no research and no item to
      collect [H].
    - Blizzard added 10 ranks in 8.1 between January 22 and March 2019, then extended the
      last increment to the reset of the week of April 23, 2019 [B].
    - For 8.3 Blizzard stated that the January 14, 2020 increase would bring a level-70
      Heart to 17,500 azerite needed for level 71 [B]. That number is exactly the client's
      base for level 70 [C], which is what fixes the epoch: knowledge counts from
      2020-01-14 and the first week runs at multiplier 1.
  TrinityCore implements the clock literally: a character's knowledge level is the number
  of whole weeks between 2020-01-14 and today, capped at 30 [T]. Build 35662 is the 8.3.7
  client: 8.3.7 released on 2020-07-21 and its last version date is 2020-08-26 [W]. Under
  TrinityCore's clock the table below is at week 28 on the release date, reaches the cap
  of 30 on 2020-08-04 and stays there [computed from T and W]. 8.3.7 therefore ran at the
  cap for all but its first few weeks.

  Multipliers are the client's own floats [C], printed to six places. From knowledge 16
  onward the value is constant at 0.0195366, so 0.0195366 is the effective floor: a
  base cost is divided by about 51.2 at best, and never below the 1,000 floor [C][T].
  Two practical consequences for a server:
    - If you want 8.3.7 as it stood in August 2020, start the counter at the cap (30).
    - If you want the 8.0/8.1 progression, the same table gives you the whole ladder; the
      multiplier is a pure function of weeks since 2020-01-14, so a fresh server can set
      any date it likes as the epoch and get a correct curve [T].
  Applying the multiplier AND the already-known 17,500 figure is a double reduction: the
  17,500 in Blizzard's note is the knowledge-1 value, and the table above is how it fell
  from there [B][C].

4. EVERY SOURCE OF AZERITE, WITH AMOUNTS
================================================================================================
  The client carries the currency (CurrencyTypes 1553 "Azerite", artifact quality, no cap)
  [C] but it does not carry reward amounts: those live in quest and world data. The
  amounts below are the ones Wowhead publishes, with the wiki and Blizzard patch notes
  where they disagree. Where an 8.3-specific number exists on a quest page, it is quoted
  with the quest id. Azerite is a currency: it is consumed automatically when gained, so
  there is no item to click [W].

5. THE FASTEST ROUTE FOR A CHARACTER WITH NO GROUP
================================================================================================
  Order matters more than speed: the big payouts are weekly and daily, so the route below
  takes every reset-gated amount first and fills the rest with repeatable quests. Every
  amount is from section 4; the ordering is the procedure this document recommends, not a
  measured rate.

  STEP 1, on the first login after reset (weekly, about 40 minutes, est.):
    1. Three assaults: the major (750) and both minors (500 each) - 1,750 [H].
    2. Island weekly "Set Sail" - 350 [H] - plus one Mythic island win - 450 [H].
    3. Island treasure mission from the table - 2,000, or 3,500 at 200% [H].
    4. Warfront contribution quests if the cycle is up - 500 each, 5,500 total [H].
    5. Every follower mission with an azerite reward - 65 to 400 each [H].
    Weekly solo total: 4,550 plus contributions and missions [computed from H].

  STEP 2, every day:
    1. Read the three emissaries. Take any whose reward is azerite (1,500) or a faction
       cache (2,500) first; they expire after three days [W][H].
    2. Clear the assault dailies in Uldum or the Vale while the assault is up - roughly
       300 to 450 each, and they are the best per-completion repeatable in the patch [H].
    3. Fill the rest with world quests that show the azerite icon - 100 to 400 each [H].
    4. Kill rare spawns on the way - 35 each [H].
    Daily solo total: one emissary (1,500 to 2,500) plus five dailies (1,500 to 2,250)
    plus filler quests, in the 3,000 to 5,000 band [computed from H].

  STEP 3, only if you still have time:
    - Heroic dungeon, first of the day: 350 to 380 including the 150 bonus [computed from H].
    - Random battleground first win: 151 [H].
    - Arena skirmish: 16 per match, not worth the queue [H].
  What NOT to do solo: Warfronts and raid boss kills are slow and the azerite per minute is
  low or unverified [H]; Mythic+ bosses pay 35 each [H]: a key is for gear, not for azerite.
