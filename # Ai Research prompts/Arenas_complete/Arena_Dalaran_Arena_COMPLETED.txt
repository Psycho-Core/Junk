=== 3. PILLARS AND LINE OF SIGHT OBJECTS ===

The map has no free-standing pillars. Line of sight is broken by
the raised centre platform edge, two crate piles on the platform,
and the central waterfall while it is running.

Central waterfall (LoS blocker plus knockback):
  Collision object GO 194395: 1291.56, 790.837, 7.1
  Visual object GO 191877:    1291.56, 790.837, 7.1
  Orientation: 3.14238
  This is the exact centre of the playfield
  Knockback spell 58873, cast by creature 28567 at
  1291.76, 791.02, 7.115 every 1.5 s while the water runs
  Cycle: short warning, then about 30 s of flowing water
  (timer values are server constants marked "NOT blizzlike"
  in the core - treat exact retail timings as unverified)

Crate piles (persistent LoS blocks, described as "huts"):
  Count: 2, at two corners of the raised platform

Platform edge:

=== 5. USUAL FIRST POSITIONS BY ROLE ===

Everyone starts inside a pipe and is flushed onto the floor
within a few seconds, so the first real position is post-flush.

Melee DPS:
  Land at the pipe exit, then take the nearest stairs to the
  raised platform. The platform is the normal first contact.
  Alternative: stay on the lower floor and force the enemy
  ranged players down, or fight under the platform edge

Ranged DPS:
  Climb to the raised platform and use a crate corner
  The middle of the platform is the best free-cast spot but
  the waterfall cuts LoS there for ~30 s at a time

Healers:
  Take a crate corner on the platform, not an open corner
  A crate pile is the standard healer anchor because it
  blocks LoS permanently
  If forced off, kite around the crates the same way you
  would kite around the Nagrand Arena pillars

General opener notes:
  Knocking a target off the platform is a standard opener;
  the ~4-unit drop costs the target movement time to climb
  back, and this map allows mounts since patch 3.2.0

=== 6. BUFF / HEALTH PICKUPS ===

Only one pickup type exists on this map: Shadow Sight.

Shadow Sight objects (GO 184663 and GO 184664):
  North object: 1291.7, 813.424, 7.11472 (orient 4.64562)
  South object: 1291.7, 768.911, 7.11472 (orient 1.55194)
  Both are on the raised platform, about 22 units either side
  of the centre line, on opposite sides of the waterfall
  First spawn: 90 s after the gates open
  Respawn: 120 s after being taken
  Spell 34709 Shadow Sight - 15 s of stealth and invisibility
  detection, and +5% damage taken while it lasts

There is no health, mana, haste or berserking pickup here.
The classic battleground pickup objects (179871 speed,
179904 regeneration, 179905 berserking) are not spawned on
map 617, and neither are modern health or haste orbs.
