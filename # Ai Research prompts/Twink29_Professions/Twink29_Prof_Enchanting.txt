TWINK29 - PROFESSIONS - STEP 06 - ENCHANTING
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Scope: what Enchanting gives a level 29 battleground twink, Classic skill
line 333, capped at 225 for a character of that level.

1. WHAT ENCHANTING IS AT 29
   Enchanting is the only profession in this set whose product the twink can
   wear without having the profession. An enchant is a service: hand the
   item, the materials and a tip to any enchanter and the stats are yours.
   So this file has two halves - what a 29's gear can take at this tier
   (section 2), and what levelling the profession buys the character
   (disenchanting, self-supply, and selling the service).
   Ranks: apprentice at level 5, journeyman at skill 75 (level 10), expert
   at skill 125 (level 20), artisan at skill 200 (level 35), and the only
   artisan trainer in the game is Annora 11073 inside Uldaman. A 29 reaches
   225 and stops there; 226 to 300 is closed to him.
   There is no self-only perk at Classic tier. Unlike the current expansion's
   enchanting, a Classic-skill enchanter has no exclusive ring or glove
   enchant to wear; what he gets is the ability to disenchant and the rods.
   The one thing worth saying plainly: the enchants below are the same for
   every class and every player, enchanter or not.

2. THE ENCHANTS A 29 SHOULD ACTUALLY HAVE
   Six enchants, one per slot, all of them inside the 225 cap. Materials in
   brackets are the item ids. Every one of them needs a rod, listed in
   section 4, and an enchanter who owns that rod tier.

   Cloak - Enchant Cloak - Lesser Agility, spell 13882
        +3 Agility. Enchanting 225, difficulty 225/245/265/285.
        2x Lesser Nether Essence (11174). Tool: Runed Truesilver Rod.
        Not trainer-taught: the formula is item 11206 and it drops, so the
        enchanter has to own it. Cheapest agility point-per-slot on the
        whole list.

   Chest - Enchant Chest - Greater Health, spell 13640
        +35 health. Enchanting 160, difficulty 160/180/200/220.
        3x Soul Dust (11083). Tool: Runed Golden Rod. Trainer-taught.
        The default chest enchant at this tier; there is no stat version
        inside the cap.

   Bracers - Enchant Bracer - Strength, spell 13661
        +5 Strength. Enchanting 180, difficulty 180/200/220/240.
        1x Vision Dust (11137). Tool: Runed Golden Rod. Trainer-taught.
        Melee only; the caster bracer enchant inside the cap was not
        checked, so treat that as unverified.

   Gloves - Enchant Gloves - Agility, spell 13815
        +5 Agility. Enchanting 210, difficulty 210/230/250/270.
        1x Lesser Nether Essence (11174) + 1x Vision Dust (11137).
        Tool: Runed Truesilver Rod. Trainer-taught.

   Boots - Enchant Boots - Minor Speed, spell 13890
        "a slight movement speed increase" - the tooltip's own words; the
        exact percentage is unverified in this build.
        Enchanting 225, difficulty 225/245/265/285.
        1x Small Radiant Shard (11177), 1x Aquamarine (7909), 1x Lesser
        Nether Essence (11174). Tool: Runed Truesilver Rod.
        Trainer-taught. This is the single most valuable enchant in the
        bracket: movement speed is worth more in a battleground than any
        stat on the list.

   Weapon - Enchant Weapon - Striking, spell 13693
        +3 weapon damage. Enchanting 195, difficulty 195/215/235/255.
        2x Greater Mystic Essence (11135) + 1x Large Glowing Shard (11139).
        Tool: Runed Golden Rod. Trainer-taught.
        Flat damage on every swing, which is why it beats the stat
        enchants for a fast-hitting melee class at this level.

   The full set - cloak, chest, bracers, gloves, boots, weapon - is what a
   29 should be aiming at. Six enchants, all applicable at 225 or below,
   and none of them needs a higher-level crafter.

3. WHAT IS OUT OF REACH - ALT
   Every enchant with a proc or a big number sits above the cap. Skill
   values below are from the Classic spell lists.
     Enchant Bracer - Greater Strength, spell 13939, skill 240. 2 reagents.
     Enchant Weapon - Greater Striking, spell 13943, skill 245. 2+2.
     Enchant Weapon - Fiery Weapon, spell 13898, skill 265. 4+1.
     Enchant Gloves - Greater Agility, spell 20012, skill 270. 3+3.
     Enchant Weapon - Superior Striking, spell 20031, skill 300. 2+10.
     Enchant Gloves - Superior Agility, spell 25080, skill 300. 3+8+4.
   Anything from skill 226 up needs the artisan rank, which needs level 35,
   which for a 29 means a higher-level crafter applying it - ALT by
   definition. The same goes for every Outland, Northrend and later
   enchant, and for the current-expansion ring enchants, which a character
   with only the Classic skill line cannot use at all.
   Formulas are a separate gate from skill. The cloak formula above drops;
   many others are vendor stock or world drops. A formula the enchanter
   does not own is as good as a skill he does not have.

4. RODS - THE TOOLS THAT ACTUALLY GATE THE LIST
   An enchanter cannot apply anything without the rod tier for it. All four
   are crafts; the plain rod is the base item.
     Runed Copper Rod 6218 - skill 1.
       1x Copper Rod (6217), 1x Strange Dust (10940), 1x Lesser Magic
       Essence (10938). Copper Rod is trade supply vendor stock.
     Runed Silver Rod 6339 - skill 100.
       1x Silver Rod (6338), 6x Strange Dust (10940), 3x Greater Magic
       Essence (10939), 1x Shadowgem (1210).
     Runed Golden Rod 11130 - skill 155.
       1x Golden Rod (11128), 1x Iridescent Pearl (5500), 2x Greater Astral
       Essence (11082), 2x Soul Dust (11083).
     Runed Truesilver Rod 11145 - skill 200.
       1x Truesilver Rod (11144), 1x Black Pearl (7971), 2x Greater Mystic
       Essence (11135), 2x Vision Dust (11137).
   Practical note: the Truesilver rod is the last one a 29 needs, and it
   unlocks every enchant in section 2. Where the plain Silver, Golden and
   Truesilver rods come from - vendor or another profession - was not
   checked, so treat their sources as unverified.

5. DISENCHANTING - THE REAL RETURN ON THE PROFESSION
   Only an enchanter can disenchant, and disenchanting is the only way to
   make the materials in this file. The Classic ladder, by item level:
     Strange Dust (10940), Lesser Magic Essence (10938), Greater Magic
       Essence (10939), Small Glimmering Shard - low level greens.
     Soul Dust (11083), Lesser Astral Essence (10998), Greater Astral
       Essence (11082) - the twenties.
     Vision Dust (11137), Lesser Mystic Essence (11134), Greater Mystic
       Essence (11135), Large Glowing Shard (11139) - the thirties.
     Dream Dust (11176), Lesser Nether Essence (11174), Small Radiant Shard
       (11177), Large Radiant Shard (11175) - the forties.
     Illusion Dust (16204), Greater Eternal Essence (16203), Small and
       Large Brilliant Shard (14343, 14344) - the fifties.
   Which item level an enchanter of a given skill can disenchant was not
   checked in this build, so the exact thresholds are unverified; the tier
   ladder above is from the Classic material list, not from a table.
   For a 29 this is the actual business case. He disenchants his own quest
   greens, sells the dust and essences, and funds his own enchants. It is
   also the reason enchanting is expensive to level: there is no gathering
   profession that feeds it, every material comes from destroying gear.

8. WHICH CLASSES SHOULD TAKE IT
   Enchanting is the one profession in the set that is equally useful to
   all nine classes and required by none - because the enchants are sold as
   a service. Take it for the disenchanting, not for the enchants.

   1. Rogue and warrior. The enchants they want are the expensive ones -
      gloves, bracers, weapon - and an enchanter who owns those formulas and
      the Truesilver rod can supply his own roster. Between them, weapon
      Striking and the two agility enchants are the best damage per gold in
      the bracket.
   2. Hunter. Minor Speed and the agility pieces, and a class that benefits
      least from the caster-side enchants this tier does not really have.
   3. Druid, shaman, paladin. All three use the strength, agility and health
      enchants depending on spec, so the same six apply.
   4. Priest, mage, warlock. The +35 health chest and Minor Speed are worth
      having, but the intellect and spell-power enchants at this tier were
      not found inside the cap, so casters get the least out of an enchanter
      at 29.
   5. Everyone, as a second profession. Enchanting plus tailoring is the
      classic pair - tailor the greens, disenchant them, sell the dust - and
      both need no gathering.
   Last line: enchanting is the only profession here that a 29 should
   consider for the economy rather than the character. Buy the six enchants
   from an enchanter; level the profession only if the twink is meant to
   supply his own account.
