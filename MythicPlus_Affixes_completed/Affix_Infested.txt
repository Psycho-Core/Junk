INFESTED — Battle for Azeroth Mythic+ Season 1 (historical behavior on an 8.3.7/build 35662 client)

SCOPE: Affix ID 16 was the season-1 fourth affix on keystones +10 and above. It was retired when the next season replaced it; retail BfA 8.3.7 season 4 did NOT have Infested as its active seasonal affix. Re-enabling it on an 8.3.7 private core requires a matching server-side implementation. This document describes documented season-1 behavior, not a measured trace of that private core. Unknown core-specific numbers are explicitly unverified; do not import the different Uldir raid Spawn of G'huun (NPC 136461) or a later custom-server rework into the dungeon affix.

HOW MANY ARE INITIALLY INFESTED / SELECTION
Only SOME eligible NON-BOSS enemies start with the Symbiote of G'huun (277242), indicated by its buff and visible gray/decaying aura. Contemporary guides report that placements changed between weekly resets and remained consistent for runs of the same dungeon during a given week. Whether hosts were chosen from a fixed designer-authored spawn list, selected by a script, or assigned by another mechanism: unverified. It is NOT 'every enemy', a fixed infected percentage, or a guaranteed one infested per pull. Exact number initially infested in any one dungeon/week, the list of eligible spawn GUIDs, the method/seed that chooses the weekly pattern and whether the particular 8.3.7 core reproduces it: unverified. A live worm can re-infest another eligible enemy, so the number currently infested may change during a run and is not necessarily the starting count. Identify the actual buff in-game instead of hard-coding a stale weekly pattern. Bosses are not eligible hosts; do not place the buff on a boss. The creature's species alone does not determine whether its specific spawn is selected.

VERIFIED SPELLS / NUMBERS
277242 Symbiote of G'huun: aura on the host; periodically triggers Regenerative Blood every 3 seconds. Nearby allied enemies within EIGHT YARDS regain 10% of EACH HEALED TARGET's own maximum health every 3 seconds (Blizzard's 2018 MDI guide). Do not convert this to 10% of the symbiote host's HP, or to 10% every second. The host may also receive healing in the spell listing; exact self-heal behavior on this core: unverified. The aura's extra creature size is +30% on the spell listing; this is a visual size change, not a verified health/damage multiplier. Host base HP and damage modifications solely from being Infested: unverified.
277564 Regenerative Blood: named periodic healing effect associated with the Symbiote; tooltip says 'heals for 10% of max health every 3 sec'. Its spell listing shows a scripted dummy effect rather than a separately verified direct-heal coefficient. Implement the observed healing behavior via the affix script; do not assert that casting 277564 once on its own reproduces the entire periodic aura or invent a second additive heal.
277567 Infest: when a worm reaches a suitable enemy, it attempts a ONE-SECOND cast on its nearest eligible allied NON-BOSS NPC, applying the Symbiote to that new host when successful. The spell listing shows a 20-yard range and a script effect; physical target acquisition/search radius of the creature AI and whether the 20-yard spell range is also the chase radius: unverified. Do not equate its AI target selection with the Uldir raid's Dark Purpose (which fixates a random PLAYER).

WORM ENTRY / SPAWN / TARGET / STOPPING SPREAD
NPC 141851 is the dungeon-affix Spawn of G'huun (documented in the BfA dungeon zone pages). When ONE infested host dies, TWO worms spawn NEAR ITS DEATH POSITION, even though that dead host itself grants ordinary Enemy Forces as appropriate. The worms seek the nearest available non-boss host; if one survives and infests a new enemy, killing THAT new host can repeat the two-worm cycle. They can be crowd-controlled, slowed and KILLED: keep them from reaching another enemy or completing Infest. Practical safe sequence: if the original infected host is susceptible to control, crowd-control it; otherwise keep it separated by tank positioning. Move other enemies OUTSIDE the eight-yard healing radius, kill the ordinary trash, then kill the isolated host; immediately turn to the TWO worms, use slows/stuns and focused damage, and prevent either from reaching a fresh host. Alternatively focus host first only if the team can control and kill both worms before they reach other eligible mobs. Exact HP of 141851 at each keystone level, its melee damage, base run speed, acceleration and HP/damage compared with its dead host: unverified; no trustworthy 8.3.7 numeric table or combat-log measurement was supplied. The only confirmed movement behavior is that it runs toward a new host; do not assign a numeric speed, health percentage or damage multiplier. A 2018 guide reports that if no eligible enemy is within about 40 yards the worm may attack the nearest player; that precise AI boundary and any melee damage are unverified for this core. A player is NOT the Infest host in this dungeon mechanic. Once both worms are killed with no new infection, that chain ends. Do not simply interrupt/ignore the 1-second cast and assume interruption alone finishes the worm; secure a kill or control it until killed. Exact interrupt immunity in this core: unverified.

ENEMY FORCES
The original non-boss enemies count for their usual Enemy Forces on death, whether they happened to be infested or not. Infection does NOT replace or defer their ordinary enemy-forces credit. The TWO summoned Spawn of G'huun are an affix complication, not a way to farm extra enemy-forces progress; independent packet/spawn-table proof of their exact per-kill forces value on THIS private core: unverified. Do not grant them extra forces without inspecting that core's scenario/criteria implementation; likewise do not give the original host's forces to each worm. A chain reaction can raise the number of infested hosts/worms but cannot be used as a replacement for killing required dungeon trash. Exact thresholds/required count are dungeon-specific, not fixed by Infested.
