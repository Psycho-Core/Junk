Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29). ALT = needs a higher-level crafter or content.
V = read off a page or an audited file in this set.

0. THREE THINGS THAT SHAPE A PALADIN'S SHOPPING LIST
   The classic enchant list was built for melee, and a paladin is the second
   class in this set that can use nearly all of it - the Strength bracers,
   the weapon enchant, the boots, the chest and the armour cloak. The one
   slot it wastes is the gloves.
   THERE IS NO STRENGTH ELIXIR AT 29. Elixir of Giants 9206 is +25 Strength
   and requires level 38 (V, from the audited Alchemy file), so the battle
   elixir slot is the one slot a paladin cannot fill with its own stat.
   Elixir of Agility 8949 is legal and wrong. This is the opposite of the
   monk, who had the agility elixir and no plate.
   A paladin has mana in all three specs, not just Holy - Protection and
   Retribution spend it on Flash of Light - so a mana potion is not a
   Holy-only item for this class, unlike the monk.

2. BLACKSMITHING ENHANCEMENTS - WHERE A PALADIN BEATS EVERY OTHER CLASS
   These are permanent and they occupy the same slot as a stone or a weapon
   enchant, so a character picks one per weapon.
   6043 Iron Counterweight|item level 33, Blacksmithing 165, spell 7222,
     4x Iron Bar 3575, 2x Coarse Grinding Stone 3478, 1x Lesser Moonstone
     1705, anvil|plans 6045, a world drop|TWO-HANDED weapons only
     Makes a two-handed sword, mace or axe swing 3% faster, permanently. This
     is the best single item in the whole file for a Retribution paladin,
     and it is the reason Blacksmithing is a paladin's first profession.
   6042 Iron Shield Spike|item level 30, Blacksmithing 150, spell 7221,
     6x Iron Bar 3575, 4x Coarse Grinding Stone 3478, anvil|plans 6044, a
     world drop|SHIELD users only
     Damages anyone who blocks, permanently. Protection and Holy only.
   7965 Solid Weightstone|+6 weapon damage for 30 minutes|item level 35,
     requires level 25, Blacksmithing 200|BLUNT: maces, fists, staves
   7964 Solid Sharpening Stone|+6 weapon damage for 30 minutes|item level
     35, requires level 25, Blacksmithing 200|BLADED: swords, axes
     These are the cheap option for a one-hander with no counterweight
     available, and they are consumed on use. 12643 Dense Weightstone and
     12404 Dense Sharpening Stone need level 35, ALT.
   A PALADIN'S WEAPON PLAN: two-hander build takes 6043 and never buys a
   stone again; one-hander build takes 13693 on the weapon and a 7965 or
   7964 stone in the bag; shield build adds 6042 to the shield.

4. THE TWO PROFESSIONS
   BLACKSMITHING + LEATHERWORKING, and the first one is not optional.
   BLACKSMITHING is the only profession that gives a 29 a permanent weapon
   upgrade: 6043 Iron Counterweight at 165 for a two-hander, 6042 Iron Shield
   Spike at 150 for a shield, and the 7965/7964 stones at 200 for everything
   else. It also makes the Green Iron mail set, which is the only wearable
   armour it produces at this level:
     3836 Green Iron Helm, item level 34, requires 29, +5 Str +11 Stam, 171
       armour, skill 170, spell 3502 - the one piece that lands exactly on
       29, and it is a leveling green, not twink gear
     3842 Green Iron Leggings, 31, requires 26, +8 Str +8 Stam, skill 155
     3484 Green Iron Boots, 29, requires 24, +3 Str +7 Stam, skill 145
     3835 Green Iron Bracers, 33, requires 28, no stats, skill 165
     All four are uncommon, so the blue-only rule throws them out. They are
     named here because a paladin has no plate in the bracket and these are
     the only crafted Strength pieces that exist - the honest answer is that
     an instance drop beats every one of them.
   LEATHERWORKING for 4265 Heavy Armor Kit, four of them for four characters,
     and 18948 Barbaric Bracers as the only crafted blue bracer a paladin
     can wear. Its leather-and-mail focus is why it is a support profession
     for this class rather than a gear one.
   SWAPS
     ENGINEERING instead of Leatherworking, for a bot that fights players:
       4390 Iron Grenade (stun), 4388 Discombobulator Ray, 4397 Cloaking
       Device, 4381 Minor Recombobulator to break a polymorph on yourself.
       A paladin has no ranged interrupt, so a grenade is worth more to it
       than to a monk.
     ALCHEMY for a Holy paladin that wants to make its own mana potions and
       elixirs. Every consumable in section 3 can be bought, so this only
       saves money.
     ENCHANTING cannot be a twink's main profession: the neck and ring
       enchants in section 1 need the BfA enchanting skill and a 29 cannot
       train past the classic 225 cap.

5. RACES
   The paladin class list is short - five classic races and three allied
   ones on 8.3.7:
     ALLIANCE  human, dwarf, draenei, lightforged draenei, dark iron dwarf
     HORDE     blood elf, tauren, zandalari troll
     Night elf, worgen, gnome, orc, undead, troll, goblin, pandaren, void
     elf, highmountain tauren, mag'har orc, kul tiran and mechagnome cannot
     be paladins at all, so there is no "avoid" list for this class - the
     list of choices is simply short.
     THE ALLIED-race half of that roster is from memory of the character
     screen and is UNVERIFIED.
   THE PICKS, best first, with the racial that earns the slot:
   HUMAN      59752 Will to Survive - a crowd-control break on its own
              cooldown, separate from the PvP trinket and from Blessing of
              Sanctuary. Three breaks is the best defensive racial in the
              bracket. 20598 The Human Spirit.
   TAUREN     20549 War Stomp - an area stun on no cast time, which a
              Protection paladin uses to hold a group and a Retribution one
              uses to land a two-hander. 20550 Endurance for base health.
   DARK IRON  Fireblood - removes bleed, poison, disease and curse and
              grants primary stat. Spell id unverified. The best anti-dot
              racial for a tank.
   BLOOD ELF  50613 Arcane Torrent - area silence plus resource, which is
              the answer to every caster in the bracket.
   DWARF      20594 Stoneform - clears bleed, poison and disease and adds
              armor; 59224 and 20596 are the rest.
   LIGHTFORGED Light's Reckoning, a damage-and-heal blast. Spell id
              unverified.
   ZANDALARI  Regeneratin', a full heal over the channel, broken by damage.
              Spell id unverified, and useless on a bot that keeps getting
              hit.
   DRAENEI    28880 Gift of the Naaru, a heal-over-time, plus 6562 Heroic
              Presence for hit rating.
   NO BAD PICK: a paladin has weapon skills for every weapon it uses, so no
   race's weapon bonus is wasted, and all eight races carry a racial a 29
   can use. Pick the control effect you want.

6. WHAT TO BUY, IN ORDER
   1. 6043 Iron Counterweight for a two-hander, or 13693 plus 7965/7964 for
      a one-hander, and 6042 for a shield build.
   2. 13890 Minor Speed on the boots, 13661 Strength on the bracers.
   3. 13746 armour on the cloak, 13640 health on the chest, 4265 kit on the
      legs.
   4. 2289 Strength scrolls, 3825 or 8951 as the guardian elixir.
   5. 1710 Greater Healing, 5634 Free Action, 3385 mana, one school
      protection potion.
   6. 12210 Roast Raptor, and 2459 Swiftness for flag runs.
   7. 4390 Iron Grenades and 4381 Minor Recombobulator if Engineering.
   Everything except the neck, ring, shoulder and shield enchant slots is
   doable by a 29 with its own two professions. Those four slots are where a
   funded twink pulls ahead.
