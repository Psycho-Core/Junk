=== 0. HOW TO READ THIS FILE ===

Every coordinate below was read from a listed source. Nothing is estimated; anything no source could answer says "unverified". Distances are straight-line arithmetic between source coordinates - they are NOT walkable-path measurements. No pathing/navmesh/spline source for these maps could be read, so every actual walking path between the points below is unverified for getting stuck (see section 4).

Direction convention: on the in-game Arathi Basin map, +y runs toward the top (north) edge and +x runs toward the left (west) edge. This was derived by anchoring source coordinates to the in-game map image (the Alliance start sits under the "Trollbane Hall" label in the top-left corner, the Horde start under "Defiler's Den" in the bottom-right) and cross-checked against warcraft.wiki "Arathi Basin strategy": "Alliance players will appear ... located in the northwest of the Basin. Horde players will appear in the southeast".

Facing values from world_safe_locs are reproduced exactly as stored in the dump; the column's unit/format is not verified (values behave like degrees - battleground_template stores 3.91571 rad / 0.813671 rad for the two starts, i.e. 224.4 / 46.6 degrees against the 220.05 / 46.1249 facing values - but no readable source documents the column format).

Respawn rule (script GetClosestGraveYard - what a companion must implement):
1. If your team occupies at least one of the five nodes: you resurrect at the graveyard of the OWNED node closest to your corpse (2D distance to the world_safe_locs row of each owned node).
2. If your team owns no node: you resurrect at your team's base graveyard - the script hardcodes ids 898 (Alliance) / 899 (Horde), i.e. the map 529 rows. If your server runs AB on 2107 and its DB still has 898/899 pointing at map 529, this would teleport across maps; whether your DB repointed them is unverified (7251/7255 are the 2107 base rows).
3. When a node your team controlled is lost while you are waiting to resurrect there, the script moves those ghosts to their closest own graveyard by rule 1/2 (RelocateDeadPlayers -> GetClosestGraveYard).
