Baradin Hold (map 757; faction-controlled zone boss set)
1. ARGALOTH: tank swap on Meteor Slash stack share -
  tanks stack in front together; Fel Firestorm: run
  continuously, fire falls on positions. Consuming
  Darkness: mass dispel team (D-Magic).
  AI RULE: share the slash, run the storm.
2. OCCU'THAR: eyes beam adds (kill eyes before they
  finish casting); focussed fire cone - tanks keep him
  turned, group behind; searing shadows pools move.
  AI RULE: eyes die first - they out-damage the boss.
3. ALIZABAL: Skewer on tank (heal through, swap);
  Blade Dance: she spins to a player and whirls (spread,
  move from her); Seething Hate on a player (soak split
  by stacking on them). AI RULE: dance = scatter.

BLACKWING DESCENT (map 669)
4. MAGMAW: tank eaten (Mangle - heal hard, tanks swap);
  exposed head phase: two players click chains to pin
  the head (burn window); parasites: ranged kite/kill,
  never let them reach anyone (they infect/spawn more);
  lava spew/pools move. AI RULE: parasites are kept at
  range forever; chained head = all cooldowns.
5. OMNOTRON (4 golems, 2 active): kill the ACTIVE pair
  in order called; never hit a shielded golem (shield
  breaks = raid damage/punish); Electron: Lightning
  Conductor player runs out; Toxitron: poison adds,
  slime pools (stand in green for buff only when called
  - unverified), Arcanotron: stand in his power circle
  (buff) but pull him out of it first; Magmatron:
  flamethrower chases (kite), Acquisition Target laser.
  AI RULE: shields decide targets, not health bars.
6. MALORIAK: vial phases: red (adds burn, AoE them
  grouped), blue (spread, frost), green (all adds take
  +damage - burn adds), dark (heroic). Interrupt his
  casts (Arcane Storm, Release Aberrations is allowed/
  stopped by call - unverified); below 25%: frost orbs
  kite. Adds must die in green or tank is overrun.
  AI RULE: colour calls the job - green means adds die.
7. ATRAMEDES: SOUND is the second health bar: hitting
  100 sound = killed by Sonar. Discs on the floor chase
  players (run out, they spawn sonar bombs); air phase:
  run the beam (he fixates and breathes a lane, speed
  matters), gongs interrupt his Searing Flame cast and
  reset via stun (limited gongs - spend on searing only);
  ground: modulation circles move out, tracking flame
  kited. AI RULE: watch your sound, not your health;
  gongs are a budget, never waste one.
8. CHIMAERON: bile-o-tron ON = raid can live at 10k
  (heals only need to top past 10000 - do not overheal);
  Caustic Slime hits if above... split spit between
  stacked groups; Feud: stack tight, heal to 10k;
  tank swap on Double Strike/Break; below 20%: bot
  off, mortality - pure burn, no healing works as
  before. AI RULE: 10,000 is full health until 20%.
9. NEFARIAN (page ids): P1 Nefarian flies, resurrects
  Onyxia; dragons tanked 50+ yd apart (78619 Children of
  Deathwing: +100% attack speed if close - page); bone
  warrior adds (94121 Hail of Bones) kited, NOT tanked
  (they decay - unverified); kill Onyxia first while
  Nefarian at high health: every 10% of Nefarian = 94090
  Electrocute raid blast (page) - push Onyxia, control
  his %. P2 lava: swim to platforms, interrupt blast
  novas on each platform add. P3 ground: crackle timing
  (heal up before each 10%), shadowflame adds kited,
  tank swaps on breath. 10:30 enrage (page 26662).
  AI RULE: his health bar is a raid-wide metronome -
  never push a crackle unhealed.

BASTION OF Twilight (map 671)
10. HALFUS WYRMBREAKER: proto-behemoth + drake choice:
  free drakes by the raid's pick - each freed drake
  removes an ability and debuffs him; the whelps/storm
  rider/timestopper combos vary weekly (this core's set
  unverified). Furious Roar: third roar stuns (trinket/
  break). Tank swap on mortal strike stacks (healing
  reduced). AI RULE: freed drakes are the kill order's
  first half - mechanics removed beat DPS.
11. VALIONA AND THERALION: ground dragon swaps with the
  flying one: Valiona down: Blackout on a player - STACK
  on them to split it; Devouring Flames breath (face
  away cone, run sideways). Theralion down: Engulfing
  Magic - the marked caster moves OUT and stops casting
  near others (their casts explode around them); twilight
  meteors on players (spread). Twilight realm portals
  (heroic). AI RULE: Blackout = hug; Engulfing = exile.
12. ASCENDANT COUNCIL: P1 Ignacious (fire: interrupt
  casts, tank swap on burning wound; flame torrent run)
  + Feludius (water: glaciate if near him when he
  freezes - players spread out for frost; waterlogged
  dispelled D-Magic); P2 Arion (wind: stay GROUNDED -
  get the grounded buff near wells or his lightning rod
  chain kills; disperse when he vanishes) + Terrastra
  (earth: gravity well pulls, eruption spikes move);
  each pair to 25% then P3 Monstrosity: lava seeds on
  players (run out), gravity crush lifts a player (heal),
  chain lightning spread, burn race. AI RULE: 25% on both
  pairs evenly - one left high becomes P3 health.
13. CHO'GALL (page ids): CORRUPTION is the real health
  bar: 93189 Corrupted Blood stacks; at 25% 81836
  Corruption: Accelerated (D-Magic - dispel it, page);
  50% sickness - the player vomits in front: face AWAY
  from the group; 75% malformation (shadowbolts at
  allies - spread); 100% = faceless (unhealable but
  +damage). Avoid: blood pools (+5/sec standing - page),
  crash (+10), adherent casts. Adherents: interrupt
  Depravity (page list); fester blood slimes killed.
  P2 at 25%: tentacles (kill - they channel beams),
  worshipping players broken by damage/interrupt.
  10-min enrage (47008, page).
  AI RULE: face your vomit at a wall and keep corruption
  under 25 - dispellers own the first threshold.
14. SINESTRA (heroic only): eggs shielded: burn a shield
  off when she channels... Calen (red dragon) helps;
  whelps: spit pools - the spit's Twilight Essence orbs
  picked up by assigned players (they buff then kill -
  trade off); slicers: two beams fixate - kite apart so
  beams never cross the raid; twilight orbs chase.
  AI RULE: beams and orbs are kited, never held.
  (Heroic script on this core: unverified.)

THRONE OF THE FOUR WINDS (map 754)
15. CONCLAVE OF WIND (3 platforms, jump by wind):
  Nezir (ice: tank swap, wind chill stacks; his ultimate
  at full energy = stack for heals), Anshal (nature:
  adds - kill flowers, stand in his green circle hurts -
  pull out; ultimate: spread), Rohash (wind: tornadoes
  dodge, wind blast rotates around him - stay behind the
  turn; ultimate: group up). All three must die within
  ~60 sec of each other or they revive. Ultimate energy
  calls: everyone crosses to Nezir for his (healing),
  spreads for others (era calls, unverified locally).
  AI RULE: three bosses, one death - synchronise the
  last 10%.
16. AL'AKIR: P1: squall line tornado wall has ONE gap -
  stand in it; wind burst knocks back (stand near him,
  run in after); ice patches on ranged. P2: acid rain
  stacks (heal race), stormlings killed near him (they
  debuff him when dying close - feedback). P3 (25%):
  flight: group stacks above/below as called (lightning
  rods on players - spread vertically), clouds close in
  from top/bottom - move to the open band.
  AI RULE: P1 stand in the gap; P3 fly the band.
