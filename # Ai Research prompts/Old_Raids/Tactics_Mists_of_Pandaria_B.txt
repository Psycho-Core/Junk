THRONE OF THUNDER (map 1098)
1. JIN'ROKH: throw tank swap (Static Wound); Focused
  Lightning orbs fixate players - carry them to a grate
  OUT of water; conductive water pools: fight him in a
  pool for +damage, but Lightning Storm = everyone OUT
  of water and spread. AI RULE: water is damage you
  borrow - storm means leave it dry.
2. HORRIDON: gates: adds pour per tribe gate; interrupt
  dinomancer casts (heals boss - I), kill adds by gate
  call; Jalak joins at end (kill fast, then boss takes
  +damage). Direhorn spirit fixates a player (kite,
  knockback slows it). Tank swap on Triple Puncture.
  AI RULE: the add gate is the boss - boss DPS happens
  between waves.
3. COUNCIL OF ELDERS: kill order by empowerment: the
  empowered elder (dark power) must die... rotation:
  damage switches to whoever is empowered (Gara'jal
  possesses them): Kazra'jin (reflect damage during
  overload - stop), Sul (sand traps - stack out of
  quicksand, adds), Malakk (tank swap frost stacks),
  Mar'li (adds interrupt, spirits add waves).
  AI RULE: hit the possessed one; reflected casts mean
  hands off Kazra'jin.
4. TORTOs: shell spin: whirl turtles - kick a turtle
  (after it dies, aim it) INTO him to interrupt Fury
  and knock him; bats: tank kites them (they heal him
  if they hit players... they drain - kill at range);
  crystal shell shields (player shield mechanic).
  Rocks fall circles. AI RULE: turtles are ammunition
  after death - save a corpse for the Fury cast.
5. MEGAERA: heads: kill one head at a time (never the
  same colour twice in a row if avoidable - rampage
  every 2 heads: stack/heal); the living heads NOT in
  melee range breathe/use from behind: green poison
  bombs (spread), red flame beam (run out of line),
  blue frost beam kited. Poison pools grow.
  AI RULE: two heads die, everyone stacks - the cycle
  is arithmetic, plan it.
6. JI-KUN: nests: flyers use feathers to reach nests
  (kill eggs/juveniles before hatch), feed young: the
  flying group intercepts feed (pool buff); down teams
  tank adds; Quills raid-wide (heal), talon rake swaps.
  AI RULE: nests on a clock outrank boss DPS - the sky
  team is a rotation, not volunteers.
7. DURUMU: colourblind beams: red/yellow/blue beams on
  3 players reveal matching adds (beam players sweep
  their cones over the room to find crimson/amber/azure
  adds - kill revealed adds before beams end); light
  phase then maze: Disintegration Beam carves a path;
  maze phase: run the opening path in the eye sore
  maze (follow the safe lane as it opens, never ahead).
  AI RULE: the maze opens in front of you only - walk
  the gap, never guess a shortcut.
8. PRIMORDIUS: living fluid blobs run to him (kill/
  slow); dead blobs leave pools: stand in pools to gain
  mutation stacks (good mutations buff; at 10 stacks
  transformed), avoid bad mutation pools by call; he
  evolves every X blobs absorbed (hardens).
  AI RULE: his food is your buff - blobs die away from
  him, pools are chosen, not random.
9. DARK ANIMUS: anima golems: small golems killed beside
  bigger ones to transfer anima (feed the plan: wake the
  LARGE golems in called order; if Dark Animus himself
  fills first he wipes (Interrupting Jolt + full power);
  anima orbs (unverified); crimson wake paths move. Tank swaps on
  explosive slam. AI RULE: anima flows uphill by kills -
  never kill a golem where its anima feeds the wrong
  body.
10. IRON QON + 3 quilen: mounts sequential: Ro'shak
  (fire: spread, molten stacks tank swap), Quet'zal
  (wind: tornadoes, wind storm - stack in the eye... his
  storm throws players - stand in centre ring), Dam'ren
  (frost: frozen spikes lines, reflect shield stops).
  Final: all dead mounts' abilities cycle; fist smash
  phases spread. AI RULE: each dog is a weather - dress
  for the current one only.
11. TWIN CONSORTS: day/night: Lu'lin (night: star chart
  drawing - a player traces constellations (vehicle
  drawing) to buff; cosmic barrages stars), Suen (day:
  tears of the sun, fan of flames; she melts ice comet
  (stand behind comet for Lu'lin's nuclear... unverified
  cross-detail). Tank swap per phase; crane/rush adds.
  AI RULE: one twin is fought, the other is weather -
  switch targets on the phase call only.
12. LEI SHEN (page-read): 4 conduits, one per quadrant;
  he charges the conduit he stands near; if near none,
  Discharge ramps on the raid (page) - tanks keep him
  AT a conduit. Choose WHICH conduit levels highest:
  in intermissions (65%/30%) he supercharges all 45
  sec; the highest-level conduit OVERLOADS and disables
  (normal) - so the raid stands in the quadrant whose
  ability they want GONE (Static Shock chain / Diffusion
  Chain spawns adds per hit / Overcharge ring stun -
  run out through its gap / Bouncing Bolt: soak every
  bolt circle or Unharnessed Power adds spawn - page).
  Decapitate: the thrown axe hits the tank by distance
  (farther = less - page) - tank cooldown + swap.
  Stage 3: Lightning Whip marks, violent winds push
  (run against, stay off edges), summon ball lightning.
  AI RULE: you vote with your feet which ability he
  loses - stand in the quadrant you want deleted.

SIEGE OF ORGRIMMAR (map 1136)
13. IMMERSEUS: split adds: sha puddles (kill, move) and
  contaminated puddles (HEAL them full - they run to
  him healed) - split phase repeats: each returned
  puddle removes corruption; swirl circles.
  AI RULE: black puddles die, blue puddles are healed -
  wrong verb on a puddle wastes the phase.
14. FALLEN PROTECTORS (3): shared arena: each at 66/33%
  summons its desperate measures: Rook (adds tombstones
  - tank spin), He (mark of anguish passed player to
  player - pass before stacks, dark meditation bubble),
  Sun (calamity, adds - interrupt, sha sear spread).
  Kill synced at the end (they heal each other if one
  dies... they must die near-together - unverified).
  AI RULE: measures happen by %, not by call - know
  whose 66 is next.
15. NORUSHEN: personal corruption: players enter solo
  trials (kill your sha self; healers/tanks their role
  trials) to purify (then +damage to boss); outside:
  adds (big adds tank-swap aura), cutter beams spin.
  AI RULE: your trial is private and timed - nobody
  can help you pass it.
16. SHA OF PRIDE: pride bar: gaining pride from hits;
  at 100 you become proud... manifestations spawn
  behind players (face them / kill), rifts: a player
  closes rifts (click) or adds stream; banishment
  maze realm (run to your light); swollen pride burst:
  spread by pride level (higher pride = bigger ring).
  AI RULE: rifts closed by hand beat adds killed.
17. GALAKRAS: tower fight: ground team kills waves and
  protects NPCs (they open towers), tower teams climb
  and kill mini-bosses, shoot Galakras down with the
  turrets when towers cleared; drakes strafe (move);
  final: tank swap flames, fire pools.
  AI RULE: protect the demolition crew - towers are
  the timer.
18. IRON JUGGERNAUT: assault/siege modes: siege = spread
  (mortars, shock pulse knockback - stand near wall),
  mines crawl to random players (stomp them - soakers),
  cutter laser fixates (kite out), oil pools (never
  ignite near raid); bored drill circles.
  AI RULE: mines are stomped by the healthy, laser is
  walked out alone.
19. DARK SHAMAN (2): tank apart (they share health):
  toxic mist players run out (leave pools), foul slimes,
  ashen wall blocks the street (reposition), falling
  ash circles; bloodlust timer (their enrage): swap
  tanks on froststorm strikes (elementalist) / iron
  tomb on player (kill the tomb to free them).
  AI RULE: two bosses, two streets - your tank group
  stays on its own side.
20. GENERAL NAZGRIM: stances: battle (normal), berserker
  (+damage taken by him... he takes more - burn window
  but generates rage), defensive (STOP attacking - rage
  gen: hitting him builds his rage; at 100 he ravages).
  Adds by rage spending: kill banners first, shamans
  (interrupt heals), assassins on healers (peel).
  AI RULE: defensive stance = hands completely off -
  your DPS is his rage bar.
21. MALKOROK: shield phase: players' shields absorb
  (healing fills shields - keep shields topped, green/
  yellow/red callouts); orbs soak (called players eat
  orbs; never let orbs expire on the ground), breath of
  Y'Shaarj marks zones (move, they persist); imploding
  energy... P2 blood rage solo soak (one tank cooldowns,
  raid spreads far).
  AI RULE: your shield colour is your health bar -
  call red, soak orbs on roster.
22. SPOILS OF PANDARIA: two rooms, box opening: open
  small boxes steady, big boxes spawn mini-bosses; kill
  quota by timer (energy); mogu side (statues come alive
  - tank, amber pools) / mantid side (bombs - carried
  out, pheromones). Both rooms finish before enrage.
  AI RULE: pace boxes to the timer - greedy opening is
  the wipe.
23. THOK: cage phases: he fixates a player and chases
  (kite room-to-room; each pass through a jail door -
  freed prisoner NPC buffs); blood frenzy when his
  chase targets drop... at phase end he eats a prisoner
  (chosen buff: fire/frost/poison breath added);
  tank swap swallow (he eats tanks at stacks - cooldown).
  AI RULE: fixated means run the long corridor - he
  must never catch anyone before the door.
24. SIEGECRAFTER BLACKFUSE: conveyor belt team (vehicle):
  ride the belt, kill the weapon being built (one of
  shredder/mines/magnet/laser/crawler by round), jump
  pipes back; floor: sawblades fixate (kite), mines
  (kill/CC), magnet pulls (run against), superheated
  laser lines; shredder add: tank, kill with sawblade
  positioning (sawblade positioning - belt
  choice is counter-pick). AI RULE: the belt decides
  what the floor suffers - kill the called weapon.
25. PARAGONS OF THE KLAXXI (9, 3 active): each death
  empowers survivors and a new one lands: kill target
  gains a power from the Corpse... each arrival: choose
  who gains whose ability. Notable: amber on a player
  (run out), parasite (do NOT kill hungry parasite adds
  - they... unverified: avoid killing parasites or they
  heal him), fiery lines between players (spread/move),
  aim: player marked - line up others OUT of the arrow.
  AI RULE: the kill order is a build order - the raid
  leader's sequence is the strategy.
26. GARROSH (page-read): P1: adds stream (warbringers,
  farseer wolf riders - interrupt Greater Healing Wave
  and Chain Lightning, page), siege engineers: kill at
  least one per wave or its Iron Star rolls (it kills
  adds for you too - stand clear of its lane; exploding
  star damage fades with distance - page). Desecrated
  Weapon: kill the weapon to shrink its ground.
  Intermissions at 10% heals: Realm of Y'Shaarj (temple
  scenes): kill sha adds, then interrupt Consume
  break his shield, survive Annihilate behind remaining
  sha bubbles (unverified), stop him gaining energy.
  P2: Touch of Y'Shaarj MC - DPS the player free and
  interrupt them (page); Whirling Corruption: run far
  (distance reduces it); at 25 energy empowered: minions
  spawn - kill them SPREAD OUT (they empower each
  other if close - page). P3 (second 10%): all
  empowered, bombardment, desperate burn.
  AI RULE: engineers first, stars are lanes, and every
  empowered minion dies alone.
