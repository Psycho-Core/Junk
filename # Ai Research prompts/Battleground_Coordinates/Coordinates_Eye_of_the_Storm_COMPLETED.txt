=== 0. HOW TO READ / ORIENTATION ===
Facing values are reproduced exactly as stored (world_safe_locs rows: same
degrees-like convention as the other files; AzerothCore StartO: radians;
script floats: as written). None of them was converted.
No readable source assigns north/south/east/west to map 566's axes: the
dump carries no direction labels and the wiki map images could not be read
at available resolution. This file therefore
makes NO compass claims about Eye of the Storm - layout is given by
tower names, coordinates, bands and straight-line distances only.
Vertical relief (recomputed from sourced z values): the two start plateaus
(z 1247.7 / 1249.19) sit about 51-59 yd above the tower platforms
(z 1189.96 - 1197.11), and the central flag plaza (z 1160.361938) sits
about 30-37 yd below the towers.
Route legs are straight-line distances between sourced landmarks. They are
NOT walkable-path measurements: no pathing/navmesh source for map 566 could
be read (unverified).
Layout bands (sourced coordinates): Blood Elf and Draenei tower spots sit
at y 1393.65 / 1402.36; Fel Reaver and Mage tower spots at y 1729.68 /
1731.23; Fel Reaver and Blood Elf at x 2044.28 / 2048.83; Draenei and
Mage at x 2286.56 / 2284.48. By straight-line distance the Alliance
start's two nearest towers are Mage Tower (275 yd) and Draenei Ruins
(306 yd); the Horde start's two nearest are Blood Elf Tower (286 yd) and
Fel Reaver Ruins (307 yd).

The graveyard rule (BattlegroundEY::GetClosestGraveYard, exact logic):
  eligible graveyards are YOUR team's main graveyard (always) plus each
  tower graveyard only while your team OWNS that tower and it is
  UNDER_CONTROL; respawn goes to the nearest eligible graveyard in 3D.
  So a point's graveyard serves your corpse at that point only while your
  team holds it - otherwise your main graveyard is used, whichever of the
  eligible ones is closest.

=== 6. BUFF / POWER-UP SPAWNS ===
Scripted buff objects (HavenWoW BattlegroundEY.cpp + shared
  Battleground.h Buff_Entries): three gameobjects per tower -
  speed 179871, regeneration 179904, berserking 179905 - all three
  placed at that tower's buff area trigger position:
  Fel Reaver buff trigger 4569, Blood Elf 4568, Draenei 4571,
  Mage 4570 (TR_..._BUFF enum; spawn orientation o 0.907571 each).
  The trigger positions come from the client DB2 AreaTrigger table, which
  is not readable within the working limits: exact buff coordinates are
  unverified (one shared position per tower, identified only by its
  trigger id).
  Spawn behaviour: at door open ONE RANDOM type per tower is shown
  (urand(0,2)); the other two stay hidden (RESPAWN_ONE_DAY). After a buff
  is picked up it respawns via the shared base-class constant
  BUFF_RESPAWN_TIME = 180 s (same object, same type).
Retail parity: warcraft.wiki has no buff section for Eye of the Storm,
  but the wiki achievement list includes "Take a Chill Pill" - "kill a
  player who is under the effects of the Berserker power-up" - which
  confirms at least the Berserking power-up exists in retail. Speed and
  regeneration retail parity: unverified. Which spell each gameobject
  casts in your own gameobject_template: unverified.
No other buff or power-up spawns are defined in the readable script or
  described in the wiki page.
