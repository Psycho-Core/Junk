TWINK29 - WARLOCK - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, PROFESSIONS, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
Sources: the profession files in Twink29_Professions-completed (Enchanting,
Alchemy, Inscription, Cooking and First Aid, Engineering, Tailoring), the
warlock item and talent steps, and item/spell tooltips read for this file.
V = read. Where a number was not read it says unverified.

0. THE SHAPE OF THIS FILE
   A warlock is the one caster in this set whose consumables and enchants
   line up with what the class actually does. The bracer enchant, the
   weapon oil, the battle elixir and the food buff all give Intellect or
   fire damage, and the class's own Healthstones are the only consumable in
   the bracket that another character can use. What it does not have is any
   caster weapon enchant inside the 225 skill cap, exactly like the priest.

1. ENCHANTS - ONE PER SLOT, ALL INSIDE SKILL 225
CLOAK|Enchant Cloak - Greater Defense|13746|+50 armour|skill 205|V
   The only in-cap cloak enchant that is not Agility. +50 armour is the
   biggest defensive number available and a warlock is the softest target
   on the field, so this is the cloak choice without argument.
CHEST|Enchant Chest - Greater Health|13640|+35 health|skill 160|V
   3x Soul Dust 11083, Runed Golden Rod. There is no Intellect chest
   enchant inside 225 at all.
WRIST|Enchant Bracer - Lesser Intellect|13622|+3 Intellect|Silver Rod|V
WRIST|Enchant Bracer - Spirit|13642|+5 Spirit|Golden Rod|V
   THIS IS THE ONE PLACE THE WARLOCK DIFFERS FROM THE PRIEST. The priest
   file takes 13642 (+5 Spirit) because a healer lives on Spirit. A
   warlock does not: Spirit gives a warlock no damage and, in BfA, no
   meaningful regen in combat, so 13622 at +3 Intellect is the better of
   the two, and it costs two Greater Astral Essence 11082 and the Runed
   Silver Rod. 13642 stays listed for a warlock that is levelling the
   profession and already owns the Golden Rod.
HANDS|no caster glove enchant inside 225 - stated gap
   13815 Enchant Gloves - Agility (+5 Agility, skill 210) is the only
   in-cap glove enchant and it is the wrong stat. The slot stays empty.
LEGS|Heavy Armor Kit|4265|+24 armour|item level 30, req 20|V
   Not an enchant: 4265 adds 24 armour to a chest, legs, hands or feet
   item of item level 15 or higher. No Intellect or Spirit leg enchant
   exists inside the cap, so this is what the slot takes.
FEET|Enchant Boots - Minor Speed|13890|movement speed|skill 225|V
   Worth more to a warlock than any stat: a caster that cannot reposition
   is a caster that dies to the first melee that reaches it. Exact
   percentage still unverified in this build.
WEAPON|no caster weapon enchant inside 225 - use an oil, section 2
   13693 Enchant Weapon - Striking (+3 weapon damage, skill 195) is the
   best in-cap weapon enchant and is melee-only.
OFF|nothing - no off-hand enchant in the Classic skill line
NECK|190894 and RING|190871 / 191014 - current-expansion enchanter-only
   enchants, ALT, effects unverified, and a 29 with only the Classic skill
   line cannot apply a ring enchant to his own rings.
   ALT LIST, above the cap and needing a higher-level enchanter: 13939
   Bracer - Greater Strength (240), 13943 Weapon - Greater Striking (245),
   13898 Weapon - Fiery (265), 20012 Gloves - Greater Agility (270), 20031
   Weapon - Superior Striking (300), 25080 Gloves - Superior Agility (300).
   All six are melee enchants. A warlock's real weapon upgrade is an oil.

2. CONSUMABLES
   WEAPON OIL - the warlock's weapon enchant
     Minor Wizard Oil 20744, +7 Intellect for 1 hour, requires 5 (V).
       This is the warlock pick: Intellect is the class's whole stat line.
     Minor Mana Oil 20745, +7 Spirit for 1 hour, requires 20 (V). The
       priest's pick, not this class's.
     Lesser Wizard Oil 20746 is +15 Intellect but requires level 30 (V),
       one level past the bracket. A 29 cannot use it.
   BATTLE ELIXIR
     Elixir of Firepower 6373, +10 fire spell damage for 30 min, requires
     18 (V). The only spell-damage elixir in the window, and uniquely
     useful to this class: a Destruction warlock's Immolate, Conflagrate
     and Chaos Bolt are fire, so the +10 is not wasted the way it is on a
     priest. Affliction and Demonology get almost nothing from it.
     No Intellect battle elixir in the window was found in the audited
     Alchemy file, so this is the best a warlock can do.
   GUARDIAN ELIXIR
     Elixir of Greater Defense 8951, +250 armour for 1 hour, requires 29
     (V). Usable at exactly 29, and the best guardian elixir for a cloth
     class. Elixir of Fortitude 3825 (requires 25) is the cheap version.
   SCROLLS
     Scroll of Intellect II 2290, +5 Int for 30 min, requires 15 (V).
     Scroll of Stamina II 1711, +5 Sta, requires 15 (V).
     Inscription skill 75, one ink each. A scroll does not stack with a
     class buff giving the same stat.
   POTIONS
     Lesser Mana Potion 3385, requires 14 (V): the mana potion a 29 can
       drink, and a warlock burns mana as fast as any class here because
       Life Tap is not free at this level.
     Greater Healing Potion 1710, requires 21 (V).
     Free Action Potion 5634, requires 20 (V). No root or slow - the
       answer to being trained by a melee.
     Shadow Protection Potion 6048, requires 17 (V). The mirror-match
       potion: it blunts the other warlock's dots and the priest's shadow.
     Frost Protection Potion 6050, requires 28 (V).
     All potions share one cooldown, so a bag of them is one potion.
   FOOD
     Roast Raptor 12210, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit for 15 min, requires 25
     (V). The best food a 29 can eat. The Spirit half is dead weight to a
     warlock, so the food is taken for the Stamina, honestly.
   BANDAGES
     8544 Mageweave Bandage, heals 1600 over 8 sec, Tailoring 240 (V):
       bought, not made, because 240 is above a 29 tailor's 225.
     6451 Heavy Silk Bandage, heals 1280, Tailoring 210 (V): the best a
       29 tailor can make.
   WHAT THE CLASS MAKES FOR OTHERS
     A warlock conjures Healthstones, which is the only consumable in the
     bracket that another character can pick up and use without a
     profession. The 8.3.7 heal amount and the spell id were not read for
     this file, so treat both as unverified. A warlock bot in a group is
     worth more than its damage for this alone.

3. PROFESSIONS
   ENGINEERING  The warlock pick, and the strongest single note in this
     file. Minor Recombobulator 4381 is a trinket that restores 150 to 250
     health and mana to a friendly target and attempts to dispel polymorph
     at reduced effect against casters of level 31 and above - which in a
     29 bracket never applies (V). The same profession's Iron Grenade 4390
     and Big Iron Bomb 4394 give a stun, and the Engineering file notes
     bombs cannot be chained because they share a cooldown.
   TAILORING  Cloth is this class's armour and bags carry the potions.
     Skill 225 makes the Heavy Silk Bandage 6451 and the crafted cloth in
     the step 1 pool.
   ENCHANTING  The self-supply choice: a 29 enchanter supplies his own
     13622 and 13640 and can apply other people's enchants.
   INSCRIPTION  Skill 75 buys Scroll of Intellect II for one ink. Cheap,
     and the least valuable of the four.
   ALCHEMY  Elixir of Greater Defense 8951 and the mana potions. As with
     the priest file, elixirs are usually cheaper to buy than to level for.
   RECOMMENDATION  Engineering + Tailoring for a character that will be
     played; Engineering + Enchanting if the bots are to be self-sufficient.
     Alchemy is the fallback and Blacksmithing gives a warlock nothing.

4. RACES
   Racials are the only place a race choice matters at 29, and the ids
   below are the BfA ones where a page was read for this file. Which races
   could be warlocks in 8.3.7 is NOT verified: the wiki race pages are
   current-expansion pages, so treat the membership of this list as
   unverified and the racial descriptions as the verified part.
   VERIFIED FOR THIS FILE
     Undead    7744 Will of the Forsaken. 2 min cooldown, removes charm,
       fear and sleep (V). It does NOT remove the horror from Death Coil,
       and it shares a 30 second cooldown with the Insignia trinket (V) -
       so a warlock either carries one self-break or the other, not two.
     Orc       Blood Fury, 2 min cooldown. 8.0.1 changed it from Spell
       Power to Intellect (V), which makes it a straight caster cooldown
       in this build. Spell id unverified.
     Blood elf Arcane Torrent, 2 min cooldown: removes 1 beneficial magic
       effect from all enemies within 8 yards and restores 3% of a
       warlock's mana (V). 8.0.1 turned the old silence into this purge (V)
       - note that the priest file in this set still describes Arcane
       Torrent as an area silence, which is pre-8.0.1 text.
     Human     59752 Will to Survive, a self crowd-control break, read in
       the paladin set.
   NAMED, IDS UNVERIFIED
     Gnome     Escape Artist, a snare break.
     Troll     Berserking, haste on demand - the damage pick.
     Goblin    Rocket Jump, a leap.
     Worgen    Darkflight, a sprint.
     Void elf  Spatial Rift, a teleport.
     Nightborne  Arcane Pulse, a slow.
     Zandalari Regeneratin', a heal channel.
     Dark iron  Fireblood, a debuff purge and stat gain.
   PICKS  Undead first for both factions' sake: a feared warlock loses the
     pet's damage too, and the fear break is the largest single quality
     change a bot can get. Orc if the bot is expected to do damage and the
     script already handles trinket breaks, Blood elf if the group needs a
     purge and the warlock is running dry, Human on the Alliance side.

5. WHAT TO BUY, IN ORDER
   First  a stack of 20744 Minor Wizard Oil - the whole weapon plan.
   Second 12210 Roast Raptor for the Stamina.
   Third  8951 Elixir of Greater Defense, then 6048 Shadow Protection.
   Fourth 3385 Lesser Mana Potions and 5634 Free Action Potions.
   Fifth  2290 Scroll of Intellect II, 1711 Scroll of Stamina II, and a
          Healthstone from any warlock in the group.
   Last   the enchants: 13890 boots first, then 13622 bracers, 13640
          chest, 13746 cloak.

6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT HAVE
   A caster glove enchant inside 225 (none exists that was read).
   A caster weapon enchant inside 225 (oils instead).
   A leg enchant (an armour kit instead).
   A ring or neck enchant a 29 can apply (enchanter-only, ALT).
   An Intellect battle elixir (none found; Firepower is fire damage).
   A verified 8.3.7 warlock race list.
   The gaps are the same six the priest file ends with, and for the same
   reason: everything a caster wants is one expansion's worth of skill
   above this character. What a warlock has instead is the only class
   consumable in the bracket, a fire-damage elixir that one of its three
   specs can actually use, and a trinket from Engineering that undoes a
   polymorph with no level penalty at 29.
