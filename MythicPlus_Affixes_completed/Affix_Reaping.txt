REAPING — Battle for Azeroth Mythic+ Season 2; 8.3.7 (build 35662) client compatibility

Scope: historical season-2 behavior, not an active season-4 affix. Reaping (affix ID 117) was the seasonal fourth affix on keystones +10 and above in BfA season 2, beginning January 22, 2019. By BfA 8.3.7/season 4, it had rotated out. An 8.3.7 private server can recreate it, but the original retail 8.3.7 season-4 schedule does not naturally activate Reaping. Exact custom-core script behavior, spawn mappings and damage tables require testing there; unverified. No world (map ID,x,y,z) coordinates or inventory items are required by the historical mechanic, and no universal world position should be invented.

WHEN / HOW MANY / WHERE
At the instant Enemy Forces reaches or crosses each of 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% and 100%, the pending spirits associated with non-boss enemies killed since the PREVIOUS threshold become hostile and move toward the party. This may occur DURING an ordinary trash pull, not after combat ends. Each qualifying killed creature contributes at most ONE spirit, anchored at that creature's death/corpse location. The spirits wait there, initially not attackable; at their threshold they activate and seek the group even from far away. A spirit killed in one wave is not raised a second time. If a wave is reset by wiping, stealth/Shadowmeld or dropping combat, surviving spirits return toward their original death locations and remain hostile with a very large aggro radius; living holdovers can join a later wave. Spirits walking past unrelated packs do not themselves aggro those packs. Kill their original living creatures close together, or deliberately time the threshold in a safe area so incoming spirits can be grouped. Do not blindly set an exact fixed number of spirits per wave: count and mix depend on WHICH eligible creatures died in that 20%-of-Enemy-Forces interval; one mob's forces value is not the same thing as one spirit. Number of Risen Souls, Risen Critters, Tormented Souls and Lost Souls for any specific dungeon/pull: unverified without its qualifying-kill list and core mapping. A 100% wave can be avoided in a route that completes the final boss before crossing 100% and finishes forces afterward, but do not presume every dungeon or core will permit that order.

NPC ENTRIES / CLASSIFICATION
148716 Risen Soul: verified dungeon-present creature entry; common melee spirit from ordinary trash. 148439 is ALSO named Risen Soul in Wowhead, but its page does not verify a dungeon location or its precise relation to 148716: version/spawn usage unverified. Do not assume both are interchangeable or spawn two souls for one death. 148892 Risen Critter: separate verified dungeon creature entry from critters; its actual combat abilities, exact health formula and whether a particular killed critter creates one in this core are unverified. Do not treat it as a fourth dangerous lieutenant type or assume it yields Enemy Forces. 148893 Tormented Soul: generally comes from ranged/caster trash; interrupts and line-of-sight are important. 148894 Lost Soul: generally comes from larger/lieutenant or crowd-control-immune trash; avoid its frontal and death effect. 148714 Bwonsamdi: the Loa associated with Reaping and the post-timeout bargain, NOT one of the attacking ghosts. All these NPC-name-to-entry mappings are verified; which live trash entry chooses which spirit type is a SERVER-SIDE mapping and is unverified for this private core. The three primary HOSTILE combat archetypes are Risen, Tormented and Lost; Risen Critter is listed separately because the prompt explicitly asks for it.

HEALTH / DAMAGE RELATIVE TO ORIGINAL TRASH
A Reaping spirit has approximately 50% of its own source creature's health as observed in 2019 season-2 play. A contemporary guide, updated for a hotfix, describes this more precisely as HALF the source's base health AFTER keystone-level scaling but BEFORE Fortified/Bolstering modifications: spirits have the same health regardless of those other affixes. This is NOT a fixed hit-point number and is not 50% of the entire dungeon's trash HP. Exact 8.3.7 server-core calculation and critter exception: unverified. They use simplified spirit abilities, NOT the original mob's complete damage kit. There is no verified universal 'X% of source trash damage' multiplier; ordinary melee-hit damage and whether Fortified changes it in this particular core are unverified. Known attacks instead have player-health-percent effects below. Do not scale their entire damage output by an invented constant. Contemporary reports indicate ordinary other-affix interactions such as Sanguine healing/Bolstering/Raging/Explosives do not apply to these spirits; verify on custom core.

SPELLS / RESPONSE (verified spell IDs; do not mistake a debuff ID for the entire death-event script)
Risen Soul: passive Risen Soul 288844 procs Reap Soul 288388 on successful melee attacks. Reap Soul is a STACKING, magic-dispellable DoT: 2% of the VICTIM's maximum health per tick every 3 seconds, listed aura duration 9 seconds. Gather, AoE and kite the common spirits; dispel dangerous stacks on tank. Their basic melee damage itself: unverified.
Tormented Soul: Grave Bolt 288693 has a 5-second cast and hits its target for 25% of that player's maximum health if allowed to finish. INTERRUPT, stun/control, break line of sight or kill these casters; they may remain at range while melee spirits close. Exact extra direct damage beyond that percent: unverified.
Lost Soul: Shadow Smash 288694 is a dodgeable frontal (listed 3-second cast). A hit reduces maximum health by 20%; contemporary players report the max-health penalty lasting about 1 minute, but the exact 8.3.7 aura/secondary spell ID is unverified. On Lost Soul DEATH, Expel Soul creates delayed ground/swirling hazards under players; move out, avoid knockback/damage and the 30-second -10% damage-and-healing effect. Expel Soul 290085 is specifically the verified PLAYER DEBUFF (-10% damage and healing for 30 seconds); the separate spawning/death trigger's spell ID and exact impact damage are unverified. Kill this spirit away from a narrow unavoidable crossing; dodge the death hazards even after it has been defeated.
Risen Critter: entry 148892 is verified but no critter-specific combat spell ID or damage number is independently confirmed; unverified. Do not assign the Risen Soul debuff automatically without a tested spell/AI mapping.
Bwonsamdi's Bargain 289422 is the post-expiry +20% damage and +20% healing aura; it also modifies pet damage/absorb done according to the spell listing. Death's Burden 289423 is a separate 2-hour dummy-aura debuff associated with the bargain, NOT verified as a numerical damage, healing or health penalty. The direct price, if any, beyond having this debuff and the already-failed timer: unverified; do not invent a sacrifice, extra death, currency charge or permanent stat loss.

BARGAIN / ENEMY FORCES
When the keystone timer EXPIRES without completion, speak to Bwonsamdi near the dungeon START to accept the optional bargain and finish an over-time key: +20% damage and healing for the remaining run, with Death's Burden (2-hour aura). Contemporary player reports say talking to him again may remove Sated/Exhaustion; exact repeat dialogue and effect on this core: unverified. The bargain does NOT retroactively beat the timer. Bwonsamdi NPC 148714 is not an attacking wave member, and accepting is not required to defeat the spirits. The living trash's original death provides its normal Enemy Forces credit; killing its Reaping ghost provides NO additional Enemy Forces. Never increment the forces counter twice, and never wait for a soul kill to obtain the original trash's forces credit. The percent thresholds are keyed to Enemy Forces, not raw corpse count.
