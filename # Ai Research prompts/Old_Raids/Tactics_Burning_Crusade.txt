KARAZHAN (map 532; 10-player)
1. ATTUMEN THE HUNTSMAN + MIDNIGHT
PHASES: P1 Midnight alone to 95%; P2 Attumen spawns;
  at 25% of either, P3 he mounts (merged health = the
  higher of the two) and wipes threat (page).
TANK: two tanks until P3, then one; face him away
  (Shadow Cleave frontal); threat stops at each join.
GROUP: P3 everyone stacks directly behind him - Berserker
  Charge has a MINIMUM range (~8 yd, page): inside that
  nobody can be charged. Decurse Intangible Presence.
  Attumen can be Disarmed (weakens him - page).
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: Midnight P1 to 95%, both in P2, merged P3.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Shadow Cleave, Intangible
  Presence (D-Curse), Berserker Charge (P3 only).
AI RULE: stand closer than 8 yards behind him when he is
  mounted - the charge cannot hit you there.

2. MOROES (+4 random dinner guests from a pool of 6)
PHASES: single phase; Vanish then Garrote on a random
  player; Enrage at 30% (page). He is taunt-immune.
TANK: MT holds him; when Gouge stuns the MT he takes the
  2nd-threat target - the OT must be #2 on threat (page).
GROUP: guests are the fight: CC/kill by assignment
  (priest guest's heals interrupted, others CC'd -
  Shackle/trap/sheep by guest type; guest pool and spells
  unverified here). Blind hits his closest non-tank -
  keep squishies off his flank. Garrote (1000/3 sec, 5
  min, page) cannot be out-healed forever - kill fast.
GROUND: none.
ADD ORDER: P1 the healing guest, others CC'd, Moroes
  after guests are controlled/dead (exact order by the
  week's guest draw - unverified).
ABILITY (ids unverified): Vanish, Garrote, Blind
  (D-Poison), Gouge, Enrage 30%.
AI RULE: never let Moroes' guests run free, and live
  with the Garrote clock - speed is the dispel.

3. MAIDEN OF VIRTUE
PHASES: none; Berserk at 10 min (page).
TANK: hold her centre where she stands.
GROUP: spread one player per pillar gap around the room
  (page layout): outside Holy Ground (silence aura at
  her feet), in LoS of dispellers, spread so Holy Wrath
  does not chain. Holy Fire (D-Magic) is dispelled
  INSTANTLY - cancel anything to dispel it. Repentance
  incapacitates the whole raid except her target for
  12 sec - pre-HoT the tank, one healer breaks early by
  damage/immunity (page's list of tricks).
GROUND: Holy Ground silence/damage zone around her.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Holy Fire (D-Magic), Holy
  Wrath (chain), Holy Ground, Repentance, Berserk 10:00.
AI RULE: dispel Holy Fire before any heal, and stay at
  your pillar - never chain Holy Wrath.

4. OPERA EVENT (one of three plays, random weekly)
PHASES: WIZARD OF Oz: Dorothee, Tito, Roar, Strawman,
  Tinhead, then Crone; kill order matters (below).
  BIG BAD WOLF: he turns a player into Red Riding Hood
  and chases them - that player RUNS the room's edge.
  ROMULO/JULIANNE: kill Julianne, Romulo, then both
  TOGETHER (each resurrects the other - kill them within
  moments of each other, unverified window).
TANK/GROUP: Oz: burn Strawman (fire-vulnerable), control
  Roar (fearable), Tinhead stacks rust (slows - kite).
  Wolf: only the chased player moves; everyone else DPS.
GROUND: Crone cyclone (unverified detail). Otherwise none.
ADD ORDER: Oz as listed; wolf none; Romulo both last.
ABILITY (ids unverified): play-specific, page-not-fetched
  for this step - treat all Opera spell data unverified.
AI RULE: in Oz kill in the printed order; as the little
  red girl, run and never stop.

5. THE CURATOR
PHASES: 90-sec Flare phase (one Astral Flare every 10 sec,
  10 per phase, each costing him 10% mana - page) then a
  20-sec Evocation (inactive, refills mana, damage taken
  +200% - burn window). At 15% health: soft enrage (no
  more flares or Evocations, faster bolts). Hard enrage
  at 10 min (page).
TANK: hold him (he is taunt-immune, page); a designated
  soaker (warlock/shadow priest) keeps the highest non-
  tank threat on him - Hateful Bolt hits the non-tank
  with the most threat built since the last Evocation
  (page), so the soaker eats the bolts by design.
GROUP: kill every Astral Flare within 10 seconds, before
  the next spawns - flares are the DPS race; all cooldowns
  into the boss ONLY during Evocation.
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: P1 Astral Flares (each before the next), boss
  during Evocation only.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Summon Astral Flare (10 sec),
  Hateful Bolt, Evocation, Soft Enrage 15%, Hard Enrage
  10:00; flare's Arcing Sear chains 10 yd (page).
AI RULE: a living flare always outranks the boss.

6. TERESTIAN ILLHOOF (optional)
PHASES: single phase; chains sacrifice a player in a
  pentagram; imp adds stream from portals.
TANK: hold him centre; add tank gathers imps.
GROUP: BREAK THE CHAINS on the sacrificed player
  immediately (they take escalating damage); kill the
  imp adds in waves; his shadow bolts hit the tank.
GROUND: pentagram chains zone (targeted, not floor).
ADD ORDER: P1 chains (object), imps, boss.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Sacrifice (chains), summon
  imps, Shadow Bolt.
AI RULE: the chained companion dies before the boss
  matters - free them first.

7. SHADE OF ARAN
PHASES: single phase in a locked round room. Three
  separate triggers, do not conflate them (page): at 40%
  HEALTH he summons 4 Conjured Water Elementals (despawn
  after 90 sec); at 20% MANA he polymorphs the raid,
  drinks ~10 sec, then Pyroblasts the raid; Shadows of
  Aran come only with the 12-minute berserk. Interrupt
  choices control how fast his mana burns, so the two
  triggers can be kept apart (page strategy).
TANK: no real tanking - he is a caster; interrupters are
  the front line (Frostbolt/Fireball/Arcane Missiles are
  interruptible, page; immune to Silence/Tongues, page).
GROUP: FLAME WREATH: three players get rings of fire -
  IF YOU HAVE THE WREATH, DO NOT MOVE AT ALL until it
  fades (moving detonates it on the room). Run OUT of
  the ringed Blizzard as it moves; when he pulls everyone
  to the centre (Magnetic Pull + Slow + Arcane Explosion,
  page) run back out immediately. Water Elementals (~40%):
  CC/kill them, they are timed.
GROUND: Flame Wreath rings (on players), moving Blizzard
  ring, Arcane Explosion centre.
ADD ORDER: the 4 Water Elementals when up, then boss.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Frostbolt (I), Fireball (I),
  Arcane Missiles (I), Flame Wreath (3 players, 20 sec -
  moving in/out detonates 3000-4000 on the room, page),
  Circular Blizzard, Magnetic Pull/Slow/Arcane Explosion
  (10-sec cast, 9000-11000 in 20 yd - run to the wall),
  Polymorph + Pyroblast, AoE Counterspell (10 yd).
AI RULE: with Flame Wreath on you, freeze completely -
  your stillness is the whole raid's survival.

8. NETHERSPITE
PHASES: Portal phase 60 sec / Banish phase 30 sec
  alternating (page); enrage at 9 min.
TANK: Portal phase: the RED beam blocker is the tank
  (beam forces his aggro and gives +31,000 health first
  tick, page). Banish phase: normal tanking at range.
GROUP: three beams must ALL be blocked by assigned
  players: RED by the tank, GREEN (healer beam - if it
  reaches him he heals 4000+/tick stacking, page) by a
  mana-less companion if possible, BLUE (DPS beam) by a
  damage dealer, swapping before stacks get lethal;
  after leaving a beam you are Exhausted for that colour
  90 sec (page) - plan two blockers per beam. Banish
  phase: run out of Void Zones and keep DPS, nobody
  tanks (he casts Nether Breath at range, page chunk).
GROUND: Void Zones (Banish phase).
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY (ids unverified): beams (Perseverance/Serenity/
  Dominance), Nether Exhaustion, Nether Burn aura, Void
  Zone, Nether Breath, Empowerment (+200% dmg, Portal).
AI RULE: your beam is your job - never leave it unblocked
  and never take a colour you are Exhausted for.

9. CHESS EVENT
PHASES: control chess pieces against Medivh's side;
  he cheats (fire on squares, piece buffs) as the fight
  runs. Win by killing the enemy king.
TANK/GROUP/GROUND/ADD ORDER: not a normal encounter -
  companions must each pilot one piece and follow the
  event's own rules (unverified here; no page fetched).
AI RULE: protect your king; take the piece you are told.

10. PRINCE MALCHEZAAR
PHASES: P1 100-60%, P2 60-30% (axes adds), P3 30-0%.
  Infernals fall all fight and grow in number.
TANK: hold him in the open centre; move him as Infernals
  land so melee keep space. Enfeeble (30843) targets
  must not be healed (their health is cut - page family;
  rule unverified in chunk).
GROUP: spread 30 yd for Shadow Nova 30852 (knockback);
  never stand in an Infernal's Hellfire (they are rooted,
  permanent ground denial - reposition as a group).
  P2: kill/avoid the two flying axes (unverified detail).
GROUND: Infernal Hellfire patches - the whole mechanic.
ADD ORDER: NEVER the Infernals (do not kill - move), axes
  P1 in P2, boss.
ABILITY: 30843 Enfeeble, 30852 Shadow Nova, 30898 Shadow
  Word: Pain (D-Magic), 3391 Thrash, 30901 Sunder Armor,
  39095 Amplify Damage (+100% damage taken - P3 spike,
  from the page's ability list), Summon Infernal and
  Flying Axes (ids unverified; axes ramp up over time).
AI RULE: when an Infernal lands near you, the whole
  group's position changes - never DPS through Hellfire.

11. NIGHTBANE (summoned, optional)
PHASES: ground/air cycles (air at 75/50/25%, era rule -
  unverified for this server).
TANK: ground: face him away (Cleave + Tail Sweep);
  fear rotation on the tank (Bellowing Roar - bring fear
  breaks); Charred Earth under the tank = move him.
GROUP: air phase: kill Rain of Bones skeletons, stay
  spread from Smoking Blast targets (unverified detail).
GROUND: Charred Earth patches.
ADD ORDER: P1 skeletons in air phase, boss on ground.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Bellowing Roar (fear), Charred
  Earth, Cleave, Tail Sweep, Distracting Ash, Smoking
  Blast, Rain of Bones.
AI RULE: on Charred Earth, move - it is under the tank,
  so the whole melee group moves with him.

Kara trash (entries unverified): stable undead (25-min
respawn until Attumen dies, page - clear fast, Stable
Hands first, they heal); banquet guests before Moroes;
library spell shades/protecters - kill order unverified.

GRUUL'S LAIR (map 565)
12. HIGH KING MAULGAR + 4-COUNCIL
PHASES: council fight, then Maulgar alone (charge/fear/
  whirlwind in his last phase, unverified); all five are
  fought at once from the pull.
TANK: Maulgar tank centre; Krosh Firehand is MAGE-TANKED:
  a mage Spellsteals his Fire Shield and tanks him at
  range (Blast Wave punishes melee); Kiggler is ranged/
  moonkin-tanked (Polymorphs his target - shapeshift or
  two tanks, page); Olm warlock/OT tanked, felhounds
  banished/feared; Blindeye tanked in a corner.
GROUP: kill the healer first: Blindeye shields then casts
  a full-heal (page) - burst the shield and interrupt
  fast (silence effects work through the shield - page's
  list). Interrupt Olm's casts; keep Felhounds CC'd.
GROUND: Krosh Blast Wave (around Krosh only).
ADD ORDER: P1 Blindeye, P2 Olm, P3 Kiggler, P4 Krosh,
  P5 Maulgar (standard order; verify on server).
ABILITY (ids unverified): Blindeye shield+heals (I after
  shield break), Krosh Fire Shield (stealable)/Blast
  Wave/Greater Fireball, Kiggler Hex (Polymorph)+
  Lightning Bolt/Arcane Shock, Olm summons Felhounds/
  Death Coil.
AI RULE: the priest ogre dies first - every heal he lands
  restarts the fight.

13. GRUUL THE DRAGONKILLER
PHASES: single phase DPS race: Growth stacks +15% damage
  every 30 sec to 30 (page) - ~10 min practical limit.
TANK: MT front; ONE OT stays second on threat IN MELEE
  RANGE - Hurtful Strike always hits the #2 threat in
  melee (page); melee DPS must stay BELOW the OT.
GROUP: Ground Slam throws everyone randomly, then Gronn
  Lord's Grasp slows you to a stop and Stoned freezes
  you; Shatter then hits everyone, damage by CLUMPS -
  after every slam, run APART from everyone (15+ yd is
  near-minimal damage, page). Move out of Cave Ins.
  Reverberation silences the raid 4 sec (undispellable).
GROUND: Cave In circles (falling rock zones).
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY (ids unverified): Growth, Hurtful Strike,
  Ground Slam, Gronn Lord's Grasp, Stoned, Shatter,
  Reverberation, Cave In.
AI RULE: after Ground Slam, your only job is distance
  from every other companion before the Shatter.

Trash: Lair Brutes + Gronn-Priests (page trash list);
priests' heals interrupt priority - unverified detail.

MAGTHERIDON'S LAIR (map 544)
14. MAGTHERIDON (+5 Hellfire Channelers, P1)
PHASES: P1 (2 min): kill channelers (at least 3 dead) or
  he frees himself early at full rage; P2 boss active:
  Blast Nova every ~60 sec must be stopped by ALL FIVE
  Manticron Cubes clicked at once (he then takes +300%
  damage briefly, page); one clicker cannot re-click
  for 30 sec (Mind Exhaustion) - two cube teams rotate.
  P3 at 30%: ceiling collapse (raid damage + debris).
TANK: boss tank centre; one tank per channeler in P1.
GROUP: P1 interrupts: Dark Mending first (heals a
  channeler up to 32k), then Shadow Volley; each dead
  channeler buffs the survivors (+30% dmg/cast speed,
  stacking - kill order is a race, page). Burning
  Abyssals (max 5, page): banish/fear/trap, do not tank
  them near the raid. Quake knocks back and interrupts
  casts - stop casting. Cubes: click on call, all five.
GROUND: Conflagration patches; P3 falling debris zones.
ADD ORDER: P1 channelers in a fixed rotation, Abyssals
  CC-only (NEVER priority), boss from P2.
ABILITY: channeler/boss ids unverified (not linked);
  Cube channel and Mind Exhaustion (object, page).
AI RULE: when Blast Nova starts, five companions click
  five cubes - one missing cube means the raid burns.
