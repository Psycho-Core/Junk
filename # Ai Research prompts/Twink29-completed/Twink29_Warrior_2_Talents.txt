TWINK29 - WARRIOR - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

0. WHAT A LEVEL 29 HAS
   Normal talents: seven rows, unlocked at 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and
   100. A level 29 character owns the first row only, one point.
   PvP talents: three slots at 20, 40 and 70. A 29 owns the first slot
   only, and even then a PvP talent is legal only if its own level is 20
   or lower. The PvP slot is separate from the normal talent point - the
   owner asked whether it is shared, and it is not: one point in each.
   All three specializations are open at 29, and all three are Strength
   specs - the only class in this set where that is true of every spec.
   V = read off the talent's wiki page including the patch line that
   states its BfA level. A current tooltip is not evidence.

1. ARMS
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Sudden Death, spell 29725 for Arms. Passive. Your attacks have a
     chance to make your next Execute cost no Rage, be usable on any
     target regardless of health, and deal damage as if you spent 40
     Rage. 8.0.1 re-added it and extended it to all melee abilities (V),
     and 10.0.0 confirms it "was a level 15 Arms/Fury talent", so level
     15 covers 8.3.7. No BfA patch gives its proc rate, so none is
     printed here.
   THE OTHER TALENTS of the row were not read for this file.
   PICK  Sudden Death. It is the verified level 15 talent, and it turns
     a finisher a 29 Arms warrior can normally only use below 20% health
     into an occasional opener. For a bot the value is that it needs a
     script rule - press Execute when the proc lights up - and nothing
     else.
   PVP  War Banner - the verified pick for this class, section 4.

2. FURY
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Fresh Meat, spell 215568. Passive. Bloodthirst has a 60% increased
     critical strike chance against targets above 80% health. 10.0.0
     confirms it "was a level 15 Fury talent" (V), 7.1.5 set the bonus at
     60% (was 40%), and 9.0.1 redesigned it entirely, so the sentence
     above is the 8.3.7 form and the current page's Enrage text is not.
     Sudden Death is also a level 15 Fury talent here (the Fury version
     is spell 280721) (V), with the same 8.0.1 wording as Arms.
   PICK  Fresh Meat for a bot, Sudden Death for a played character. A
     companion script opens on a full-health target by definition, so
     the 60% crit window is the part of the fight it will always get;
     Sudden Death is worth more across a whole fight but only if
     something is watching for the proc, which is the same condition as
     in the Arms row.
   PVP  no verified level 20 pick for this spec - see section 4.

3. PROTECTION
   LEVEL 15 ROW
     Punish, spell 275334. Passive. Shield Slam deals increased damage
     and reduces the damage the target deals to you. Added in 8.0.1, and
     10.0.0 confirms it "was a level 15 Protection talent" with its
     damage bonus reduced there to 10% "(was 20%)", so 20% is the 8.3.7
     figure (V). The reduction percentage in 8.3.7 was not read, so only
     the 20% is claimed.
   TRAP  Devastator is not available to a 29, and it is the talent every
     Protection guide puts in this row. 9.0.1 says "Now available at
     level 15 (was 75)" (V) - in Battle for Azeroth it was a level 75
     talent, forty-six levels out of reach. This is the largest level
     gap of any trap in the set; the others were one level out.
   PICK  Punish. Passive, always on, and it improves the one ability a
     Protection bot presses most while making the bot harder to kill.
     Nothing to time and nothing to proc-watch.
   PVP  no verified level 20 pick for this spec - see section 4.

4. THE PVP SLOT AT LEVEL 20
   LEGAL AND VERIFIED
     War Banner, spell 236320, Arms only, level 20, 1.5 min cooldown:
     throw down a banner at your feet for 15 sec; allies within 30 yards
     gain 30% movement speed and have the duration of incoming crowd
     control reduced by 50%. 8.0.1 converted it from a rank 43 honor
     talent (V), and no later patch line changes its level or spec,
     which is the basis for reading both as the BfA values - an argument
     from absence, stated as such, as in the warlock and shaman files.
   WHY IT IS THE PICK  It answers the two things that kill a 29 melee
     bot: being kited, and being crowd-controlled while its healer is
     controlled too. Halving CC duration on the whole nearby group is
     the closest thing to a fear break this class gets at 29, and it is
     a drop-and-forget button a script can press on engagement.
   NOT VERIFIED
     Fury and Protection PvP talents. War Banner is filed as an Arms
     talent, so it is not offered to the other two, on the same logic
     that kept Dismantle Outlaw-only in the rogue file. Names a current
     list would give - Death Wish, Barbarian, Bodyguard, Sharpen Blade -
     are deliberately not filed with levels attached. Sharpen Blade in
     particular is widely listed for Arms in later expansions; whether
     it existed at level 20 in 8.3.7 was not read.
   THE PATTERN TO REMEMBER  Devastator at level 75 makes four traps in
     this set that a current tree places at 15 (Writhe in Agony,
     Eradication, Forceful Winds, Devastator), and it is the only one
     that was not merely one row too high but in the last row of the
     old tree. A 9.0.1 patch note, not a guide, is what settles each.

5. THE THREE LOADOUTS
   ARMS        Sudden Death. PvP War Banner.
   FURY        Fresh Meat (Sudden Death if procs are scripted). PvP
               unverified.
   PROTECTION  Punish. PvP unverified.
   Arms is the only spec with both slots filled from read pages, which
   matches step 1: it was also the spec with the simplest gearing - one
   weapon, no off-hand decision. For companion bots that have to be
   scripted rather than played, Arms is the warrior this set can
   specify most completely.

6. WHAT IS SOLID AND WHAT IS NOT
   Solid: the row and slot gating and that the two systems are separate;
   Sudden Death at level 15 for Arms (29725) and Fury (280721) in its
   8.0.1 form; Fresh Meat 215568 at 60% against targets above 80%
   health; Punish 275334 at +20% Shield Slam damage; Devastator at
   level 75 in BfA as the trap; War Banner 236320 for Arms at 20.
   Not solid: Sudden Death's proc rate, Punish's damage-reduction
   percentage in 8.3.7, the unread talents in every row, War Banner's
   spec restriction resting on the absence of a widening patch, and
   every Fury and Protection PvP talent.
