TWINK29 - WARRIOR - STEP 3 OF 3 - ENCHANTS, CONSUMABLES, PROFESSIONS, RACES
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).
Sources: the profession files in Twink29_Professions-completed (Enchanting,
Blacksmithing, Alchemy, Inscription, Cooking and First Aid, Engineering),
the warrior item and talent steps, and the racial notes carried from the
earlier files in this set.
V = read. Where a number was not read it says unverified.

0. THE SHAPE OF THIS FILE
   A warrior is the class the Classic profession files were written for.
   It is the only class in this set that can use every melee enchant in
   the cap on the right stat, the only one whose bracer enchant is its
   primary stat, and the only one with a profession - Blacksmithing -
   that gives it a permanent weapon enhancement and a shield enhancement
   no enchanter can sell it. Nothing in this file is a substitute.

2. CONSUMABLES
   BATTLE ELIXIR - there is no Strength battle elixir at 29
     Elixir of Giants 9206, +25 Strength for 1 hour, requires level 38
     (V). Nine levels out of reach, so a warrior's battle elixir slot is
     empty where a rogue drinks +15 Agility. What it drinks instead is
     the guardian elixir below and a scroll.
   GUARDIAN ELIXIR
     Elixir of Greater Defense 8951, +250 armour for 1 hour, requires
     exactly 29 (V), plus Elixir of Fortitude 3825, +120 health for 1
     hour, requires 25 (V). The Alchemy file's own warrior line calls
     that pair "the largest survivability swing any profession gives a
     29", and it is right: 120 health and 250 armour, both one hour.
   WEAPON STONES - the warrior's second weapon buff
     Solid Sharpening Stone 7964, +6 weapon damage for 30 min, requires
     25, Blacksmithing skill 200 (V): for swords, daggers and edged axes.
     Solid Weightstone 7965, +6 weapon damage for 30 min, requires 25,
     skill 200 (V): for maces, staves and fists.
     A warrior carries whichever matches its weapon - 7730 Cobalt
     Crusher takes a weightstone, 6687 Corpsemaker a sharpening stone -
     and an Arms bot needs one stack of one kind, while a Fury bot with
     a mace and a sword needs both. Dense stones (12404, 12643) require
     35 and skill 250: ALT. V, Blacksmithing file.
   POTIONS
     Free Action Potion 5634, requires 20 (V): the Alchemy file gives it
       to this class by name. No root or slow - for a melee with no
       ranged slot, this is the gap closer.
     Greater Healing Potion 1710, requires 21 (V). Swiftness Potion
       2459, requires 5 (V). Shadow Protection 6048, requires 17 (V),
       Frost Protection 6050, requires 28 (V).
     Rage potions exist in the wider game and are level-gated above 29
       according to the Alchemy file's warrior note; no rage potion item
       was read for this file, so none is listed.
     All potions share one cooldown, so a bag of them is one potion.
   SCROLLS
     Scroll of Strength II 2289, +5 Strength for 30 min, requires 15,
     Inscription skill 80 (V, Inscription file). Scroll of Stamina II
     1711, +5 Sta, requires 15 (V). Scroll of Agility II 1477 (V).
     A scroll does not stack with a class buff giving the same stat, and
     a warrior has no Strength buff of its own at 29 that was read, so
     the scroll is real value here rather than a duplicate.
   FOOD
     Roast Raptor 12210, +8 Stamina and +8 Spirit for 15 min, requires
     25 (V). The Spirit is dead weight, as it is for four classes before
     this one; the Stamina is the food.
   BANDAGES
     8544 Mageweave Bandage, heals 1600 over 8 sec, Tailoring 240 (V),
     bought not made; 6451 Heavy Silk Bandage, heals 1280, skill 210 (V).
     A warrior has no heal of its own that was read at 29, so a bandage
     after a disengage is its only recovery - which is why this slot
     matters more here than it did for the shaman.

3. PROFESSIONS
   BLACKSMITHING  The warrior's own profession, and the only one in this
     set that gives a permanent weapon enhancement:
     Iron Counterweight 6043, item level 33: makes a two-handed sword,
       mace, axe or polearm 3% faster, spell 7218. Applying it needs
       Blacksmithing 165, well inside the cap; Plans: Iron Counterweight
       6045 is a world drop, not trainer-taught (V). Permanent, not a
       30-minute buff, and an Arms bot's single best profession item.
     Iron Shield Spike 6042, item level 30, Blacksmithing 150 to apply:
       damage to anyone who blocks you, spell 7216 (V). Protection only,
       and it is the only shield enhancement in the bracket - there is
       no shield enchant in the Classic Enchanting list at all.
     Mithril Shield Spike 7967, item level 43, skill 215 (V): the bigger
       spike, still inside 225.
     The stones above, at skill 200 (V).
     What Blacksmithing does not give a 29: armour. Every plate recipe
       produces plate a 29 cannot wear (the Blacksmithing file confirms
       plate is not wearable before 40 on spell 3502), so the profession
       is taken for enhancements, not gear - the same conclusion the
       shaman file reached by the other road.
   ENGINEERING  The second profession. Iron Grenade 4390 and Big Iron
     Bomb 4394 give a 3 sec stun the Engineering file assigns to this
     class by name, Discombobulator Ray 4388 cuts a target's damage for
     12 sec, and Minor Recombobulator 4381 (Engineering 140, 10 charges)
     restores health and mana to a friendly target and attempts to
     dispel polymorph (V). Bombs share a cooldown. A warrior already has
     gap closers, so the stun is a second interrupt rather than an
     opener, which makes Engineering worth slightly less here than it
     is to a rogue and still worth taking.
   ENCHANTING  Self-supply for 13661, 13815, 13693 and 13746. A warrior
     enchanter covers every slot it owns except legs.
   ALCHEMY  The two guardian elixirs and Free Action. Usually bought.
   RECOMMENDATION  Blacksmithing + Engineering for a played warrior -
     the only pairing in the set where both professions hand the
     character something permanent. Blacksmithing + Enchanting for
     self-sufficient bots. Mining funds Blacksmithing.

4. RACES
   A warrior can be any race, which makes this the widest list in the
   set and the least decisive: there is no race a warrior bot must be.
   Which specific races were enabled on this server in 8.3.7 is not
   verified, so the membership below is unverified and the racial
   effects are the read part. Warriors also have the widest racial
   coverage in the set because two of the four crowd-control racials
   that other classes were denied are open to them: undead warriors
   exist, so Will of the Forsaken is available to this class, unlike
   the shaman.
   READ EARLIER IN THIS SET
     Undead    Will of the Forsaken, 2 min cooldown, removes charm, fear
       and sleep (V, warlock file). For a class with no fear break of
       its own at 29 that was read, this is the strongest racial here.
     Orc       Blood Fury, 2 min cooldown, attack power for a warrior
       (V, warlock file - the page lists warrior among the attack power
       classes). The burst pick.
     Human     59752 Will to Survive, a self crowd-control break (V,
       paladin set). The Alliance answer to the undead pick.
     Blood elf Arcane Torrent, 2 min cooldown, removes 1 beneficial
       magic effect from enemies within 8 yards (V, warlock file). A
       purge on a class with no other purge, though it costs the fear
       break the undead gets.
   NAMED, IDS AND FIGURES UNVERIFIED
     Tauren    War Stomp, a short area stun - a second interrupt.
     Night elf Shadowmeld, a stealth reset and a threat drop.
     Troll     Berserking, haste on demand.
     Dwarf     Stoneform, a debuff purge and damage reduction.
     Gnome     Escape Artist, a snare break.
     Goblin    Rocket Jump, a leap - another gap closer.
     Draenei   Gift of the Naaru, a heal over time: the only self-heal
       racial available to a class with none.
     Pandaren  Quaking Palm, an incapacitate.
     Dark iron Fireblood, a debuff purge and stat gain.
     Mag'har   Ancestral Call, a stat gain.
     Zandalari Regeneratin', a channelled heal.
     Kul Tiran Haymaker, a knockback stun.
     Void elf  Spatial Rift, a teleport.
     Lightforged Light's Judgment, area damage.
     Nightborne Arcane Pulse, an area slow.
     Mechagnome and Vulpera - 8.3-era allied races; whether they are
       enabled on build 35662 is unverified, as in the shaman file.
   PICKS  Undead for Horde, Human for Alliance, Orc if the script
     already handles trinket breaks and wants the burst. Avoid nothing
     outright - a warrior is the one class where the passive-stat races
     are merely uninteresting rather than wrong, because every active
     racial on the list does something a warrior wants.

5. WHAT TO BUY, IN ORDER
   First  6043 Iron Counterweight on the two-hander (Arms) or 6042 Iron
          Shield Spike on the shield (Protection) - permanent, once.
   Second 7964 or 7965 stones to match the weapons carried.
   Third  8951 Elixir of Greater Defense and 3825 Elixir of Fortitude.
   Fourth 5634 Free Action Potions, 1710 Greater Healing Potions.
   Fifth  12210 Roast Raptor, the Stamina scroll, 8544 bandages.
   Last   the enchants: 13890 boots, 13661 bracers, 13693 weapons,
          13815 gloves, 13746 cloak, 13640 chest.

6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT HAVE
   A Strength battle elixir at 29 (Elixir of Giants needs 38).
   A Strength leg enchant (an armour kit instead).
   A shield enchant (a shield spike instead, from Blacksmithing).
   A rage potion inside the bracket (level-gated, per the Alchemy file).
   A ring or neck enchant a 29 can apply (enchanter-only, ALT).
   A verified warrior race list for this server.
   This is the shortest gap list of any class in the set, and it is
   short because the Classic profession files were written around this
   class: its bracer enchant is its primary stat, its profession gives
   it a permanent weapon and shield enhancement, and its two guardian
   elixirs are the largest survivability swing in the bracket. What a
   warrior cannot fix with a profession is the thing step 1 found -
   it still has no plate to wear - and no profession in the game makes
   armour a 29 plate wearer can put on.
