1. ANUB'REKHAN
PHASES: One phase; Locust Swarm every 70-120 sec
(20 sec, 25-man); berserk at 10 min.
TANK: Tank him at his spawn end; during Locust
Swarm the tank KITES him down the room - the swarm
is a 30-yd aura: 54021 (25), 1313-1687 Nature per
2 sec stacking + silence (even auto-attack stops).
GROUP: Spread in a semicircle (Impale: 56090 (25),
line to a random player, 5688-7312 + launch + 50%
fall damage). Crypt Guards at pull (25) and one per
Locust Swarm: kill near the boss, then AoE the 10
Corpse Scarabs from each guard corpse (5 per player
corpse) before they scatter.
GROUND: none (Locust Swarm moves with the boss).
ADD ORDER: P1 Crypt Guard, P2 Corpse Scarabs.
AI RULE: swarm means run - nobody stands in 30 yd.
ABILITY LIST: 56090 Impale (25) / 28783 (10);
54021 Locust Swarm (25) / 28785 (10); Summon
Corpse Scarabs id unverified; Crypt Guard: Cleave,
Acid Spit (stacking, 500/2 sec), Frenzy at low HP.
D: unverified.

2. GRAND WIDOW FAERLINA

has only 4 Worshippers to spend - a long fight
cannot be controlled past the last sacrifice.
TANK: Drag her to the centre. Frenzy (28798):
+150% damage, +50% haste - tank cooldowns until
Widow's Embrace lands.
GROUP: Poison Bolt Volley (54098): 10 closest
players (25), DoT 1900-2100/2 sec - D-Poison.
Rain of Fire (58936): 8-yd zones, 3700-4300/2 sec,
move out. On Frenzy: 25-man a priest Mind Controls
a Worshipper and casts Widow's Embrace beside her
(removes Frenzy, silences nature 30-60 sec, kills
the Worshipper); 10-man the pre-damaged (about 5%)
Worshipper is killed beside her AFTER she frenzies.
GROUND: 58936 Rain of Fire.
ADD ORDER: P1 Naxxramas Follower x2 (kill fast or
off-tank; AoE silence, immune to silence). NEVER
kill Worshippers early - they are the Frenzy
answer, spent one per Frenzy.
AI RULE: a Worshipper dies only on a Frenzy call.
ABILITY LIST: 54098 Poison Bolt Volley (D-Poison);
58936 Rain of Fire; 28798 Frenzy. I: none. Entries
unverified.

====================================================
PHASES: Kill the 3 twilight drakes first (easy) or
leave any alive for hard mode: each living drake
adds its mechanic and, if its disciple lives,
raid-wide effects. Each drake killed WHILE
Sartharion is engaged gives him Twilight Revenge
(+25% damage/haste per drake, stacks 3) - so the
standard is drakes first, boss last. 15-min enrage.
TANK: Tank Sartharion side-on (Cleave 35% to 10
targets; Tail Lash behind; Fire Breath frontal
8750-11250). Lava walls sweep from either side
every about 30 sec on his emote: the whole raid
(including the tank) shifts through the gap.
GROUP: Shadow Fissure (57579 (10) / 59127 (25)):
5-sec void zone - move out (one-shots non-tanks
with drake buffs up). Drake portals (after 15-20
sec): a portal team (off-tank + healer + DPS) goes
in: Vesperon's disciple applies Twilight Torment
(58835: +75% fire/shadow taken, self-damage on hit);
Shadron's disciple keeps Gift of Twilight on its
drake (58766 on Sartharion in multi-drake: +50%
fire, immune) - kill the disciple to drop it.
Tenebron's portal holds eggs: kill eggs inside or
accept hatched whelps outside (stacking armor
reduction) and AoE them on the off-tank.
GROUND: Shadow Fissure; lava wall lanes.
ADD ORDER: P1 Tenebron / Shadron / Vesperon before
Sartharion (any order for 0-drake; escalating for
hard mode), P2 disciples inside portals, P3
Twilight Whelps. NEVER pull Sartharion with a
portal team split. Entries unverified.
AI RULE: fissure out, wave gap, disciples die fast.
ABILITY LIST: 57579/59127 Shadow Fissure; 58835
Twilight Torment; 57835/58766 Gift of Twilight;
Cleave / Tail Lash / Fire Breath / Pyrobuffet
(out-of-area punishment) ids unverified. I: none.
