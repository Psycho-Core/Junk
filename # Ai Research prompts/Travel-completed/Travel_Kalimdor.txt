WHAT IS VERIFIED AND WHAT IS NOT
  Positions are TaxiNodes.Pos from Map 1 of the 8.3.7.35662 export: the roost the taxi mount sits on, which is also where the
  flight master has to be spawned for the node to open. They are not a player walk-up spot, and no file in this repo carries one,
  so every "walk-to position" line says unverified rather than a guessed offset.
  Creature entries: 66 come from the research pass whose Wowhead lookup returned the exact master name for 8.3.7, 2 more were
  looked up on Wowhead while writing this file (Tal 2995, Doras 3310), and 12 were matched on the exact NPC name against
  creature_template exports (the 4.3.4 TDB update pack and the 8.x creature_template dump from the same server repository).
  6 masters have more than one creature_template row with that exact name and nothing here decides between them, so those entries
  are printed unverified with all candidate ids: Tak (402), Friz Groundspin (613), Kurzel (652), Jock Lindsey (653), Kroum (683)
  and Ranela Featherglen (781). One master is named by the research pass but has no row in any export read here: Nah'te at
  Westreach Summit (node 30), printed unverified. Nodes 48 and 64 are closed and have no master at all.
  Five of the 66 Wowhead-verified entries are NPC versions that the 4.3.4 dump does not contain (Daelyshia 134649, Bera
  Stonehammer 111450, Kroum 111381, Delanea 144375, Dinorae Swiftfeather 119843); they come from the 8.3.7 lookup and are printed
  as returned, which is why a dump search for those names only turns up the older row.
  The three pairs of faction twins carry a different master per faction: Gadgetzan (39 Alliance Bera Stonehammer, 40 Horde
  Bulkrek Ragefist), Everlook (52 Alliance Maethrya, 53 Horde Yugrek) and Cenarion Hold (73 Alliance Cloud Skydancer, 72 Horde
  Runk Windtamer). A by-name lookup that ignores the faction letter returns the Alliance master for both nodes of a pair, which is
  what the research document does for 40, 53 and 72; this file gives the Horde master for the Horde node.
  The Speedbarge (node 513) is one node with two masters, one per faction, which is why only Tilly Topspin is printed there and
  the wiki note names Zazzix Boomride as well.
  Travel time is derived, not read: the tables carry TaxiPath.Cost in copper and a TaxiPathNode.Delay that is 0 on 86,384 of
  86,627 rows, so neither is a clock. Minutes here are yards / 1920, i.e. 32 yards per second. Every pair of Kalimdor nodes in the
  export exists in both directions, so no leg in this file is one-way; no Kalimdor link leaves map 1.
  Gate evidence: the 89 Kalimdor nodes use exactly these ConditionIDs - none (81 nodes), 62814 (27, 456, 457 - the Teldrassil
  nodes), 19444 (32, Theramore), 53948 (72 and 73, Cenarion Hold) and 5793 (48 and 64, the two closed nodes). Every one of those
  rows decodes to a MinLevel/MaxLevel, a class mask, a race mask or a PrevQuest, or to no field at all; there is no achievement or
  reputation field in that decode, and no level requirement other than the deliberate 999 on the two closed nodes.
  Sources: TaxiNodes and TaxiPath exports for build 8.3.7.35662, the PlayerCondition decode of every ConditionID listed above, the
  Warcraft Wiki flight-master tables by location and zone, Wowhead NPC pages for the two manual entries, and the creature_template
  exports named above. Everything read from a source is printed; nothing was estimated.
