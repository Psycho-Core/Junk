CONSUMABLES — Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 (build 35662)

Scope/verification: Item numbers below identify actual items, not recipes. Historical BfA values are quoted only where a contemporary guide records them. Modern Wowhead/WoWDB tooltips are level-squished and MUST NOT be used for 8.3.7 amounts or required levels. Unless a historical threshold is explicitly given below, the exact 8.3.7 minimum usable level is unverified: check the server's item_template.RequiredLevel and spell conditions before issuing an item. An expansion label is NOT a verified usable-level threshold. Values on a TrinityCore fork may differ. Spell IDs below refer only to identified spells, not guessed item-use effects. There are no creature entries, map IDs or x/y/z coordinates needed for these portable consumables; vendor coordinates are unverified.

Prefer primary-stat greater flask at endgame; Stamina for survival tanking if needed.

FOOD (one Well Fed stat buff at a time; re-eat after death or expiry; while seated/out of combat)
BfA original options: 154882 Honey-Glazed Haunches — Crit; 154884 Swamp Fish 'n Chips — Haste; 154887 Loa Loaf — Mastery; 154885 Mon'Dazi — Versatility. Historical stat amounts and minimum levels unverified.
BfA 8.2 stronger single-stat options: 168310 Mech-Dowel's "Big Mech" — +93 Crit; 168313 Baked Port Tato — +93 Haste; 168311 Abyssal-Fried Rissole — +93 Mastery; 168314 Bil'Tong — +93 Versatility. Eat at least 10 seconds for one-hour Well Fed buff (contemporary cooking guide; verify on custom server). 168315 Famine Evaluator And Snack Table — feast, +131 primary stat for one hour after eating (contemporary patch guide); compare per-serving cost to individual food. Exact 8.3.7 minimum levels for these foods unverified.
Food stat selection: physical or caster DPS: use the spec's best secondary (not universally the same; confirm stat weights on this character), then appropriate primary-stat feast when it is better/cheaper per serving. Healers: Haste/Mastery/Versatility according to spec and mana needs; tanks: Versatility or preferred defensive secondary. Do not claim one secondary is BiS for every spec. A food buff does NOT replace flask, rune or scroll.

WAR-SCROLLS (only where the corresponding stronger class buff is absent; refresh after death/expiry as appropriate)
158202 War-Scroll of Battle Shout — +7% attack power for 30 minutes (historical BfA); replace with Warrior Battle Shout (+10%) when available.
158201 War-Scroll of Intellect — +7% Intellect for 30 minutes; replace with Mage Arcane Intellect (+10%).

Current post-BfA 3% tooltips do not apply to historical BfA. Historical minimum usable level and scroll spell IDs unverified.

HEALTHSTONES AND BANDAGES
5512 Healthstone — Warlock-created emergency self-heal, supplied by Warlock/ritual rather than purchased as a general potion. Historical heal amount, use spell and exact 8.3.7 cooldown interaction with healing potions unverified: do not assume they share a cooldown or always can be used together; test. Use defensively when low and obtain another stone when consumed.
158381 Tidespray Linen Bandage and 158382 Deep Sea Bandage — BfA Tailoring self/target heals over a channel; damage interrupts the channel. Deep Sea Bandage historically heals 36,577 over 8 seconds (BfA tailoring guide; 8.3.7 coefficient unverified), with historical required level 101 reported by an older item page; Tidespray threshold/amount unverified. Crafting spell 267202 creates Deep Sea Bandage; this is NOT its use spell. Use out of combat or when protected; avoid as an emergency heal under damage. First Aid was removed in BfA and bandage crafting moved to Tailoring.

LEVEL-BAND SHOPPING POLICY (8.3.7 character levels; prices and cheapest ranking are unverified without a particular realm's AH/vendor prices)
60–80: Prefer already owned/vendor 22829 Super Healing Potion and 22832 Super Mana Potion; inexpensive food appropriate to the character's level (specific food ID/requirement unverified). If eligible, a legacy flask/elixir only after verifying minimum level and cost; do not feed level 60 a BfA flask merely because the modern squished tooltip allows it.
80–100: Check 109145/109147/109148 Draenic flasks at level 90+ (other exact minima unverified); 109223 or 109222 when usable. Below their valid level, stick to verified in-game lower-tier inventory. Do not spend expensive BfA materials on routine leveling.
100–110: Compare owned Draenic flasks against 127848/127847 Legion flasks once actually usable; 127834 for emergency health. If BfA greater flask 168651's reported pre-squish level-100 minimum applies on this core, compare its price and effect; availability of other BfA items at 100–109 unverified. Avoid blanket claims that all BfA items start at 110 or 120.
110–120: Compare standard BfA 152638/152639/152641/152640 with greater BfA 168651/168652/168654/168653, choosing role's primary stat; during routine leveling use the cheapest sufficient flask or no flask, reserving greater for hard encounters. Use cheap 154882/154884/154887/154885 or stronger 168310/168313/168311/168314 food if eligible, and 152494/152495 before upgrading to 169451. At 120 hard content use a superior battle potion, applicable missing-buff war-scroll categories (up to three), and 160053 rune if the measured benefit justifies cost. Exact thresholds for each item and actual cheapest choice remain unverified until the server item table and current market prices are checked.

BOT DECISION/STACKING SUMMARY
Strength: Warrior, Paladin (Retribution/Protection), Death Knight; Agility: Rogue, Hunter, Demon Hunter, Monk (Windwalker/Brewmaster), Druid (Feral/Guardian), Shaman (Enhancement); Intellect: casters and healers including Paladin Holy, Monk Mistweaver, Druid Balance/Restoration, Shaman Elemental/Restoration. Verify spec primary stat against actual 8.3.7 server implementation. Tanks may prefer Stamina flask or defensive potion if surviving a specific hit outweighs damage; healers prioritize mana potion only if they use mana. Default combination: ONE flask + ONE Well Fed food + ONE augmentation rune + missing war-scroll categories + temporary encounter potion when needed. Class buffs outrank matching scrolls. Refresh flask on expiry; food/rune on death and expiry; scroll on expiry/death if lost; weapon enhancement only if validated. Healthstones/bandages/potions are tactical healing, not continuous maintenance. Check inventory, required level, cooldown, death persistence, spec and actual server spell auras before using anything.
