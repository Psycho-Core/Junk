TWINK29 - MAGE - STEP 2 OF 3 - TALENTS, NORMAL AND PVP
Server: private Battle for Azeroth 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Bracket: level 29 (20-29).

1. HOW MUCH OF THE TREE IS OPEN AT 29
   Battle for Azeroth talent rows unlock at levels 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90
   and 100, one row at a time. A level 29 mage has exactly one row open -
   the level 15 row - and one choice to make.
   Everything from row 2 up is ALT: it needs level 30 or higher.
   The row levels are the usual BfA rule and were not read off the 8.3.7
   client, so treat the numbers as unverified. The pattern matches the
   druid and hunter files.
   Talent ids were not read for any column. No reachable page carries BfA
   talent ids, so every id in this file says unverified rather than a
   guess.
   Source for the row itself: the 9.0.2 talent tree as published by Icy
   Veins, which lists a level 15 row for each of the three specs, and the
   same article's change list, which moves no level 15 talent. Shadowlands
   spaced the rows at 15, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50, so the BfA second row
   at 30 corresponds to their level 25 row - the first row is the one that
   did not move.

2. THE THREE SPECIALISATIONS ARE ALL OPEN
   A mage picks a specialisation at level 10, so Arcane, Fire and Frost are
   all legal at 29. The choice decides the level 15 row and the whole spell
   rotation; it does not change the gear, because the cloth pool at 29 is
   one list for all three.
   The spec cannot be changed inside a battleground, so it is a build
   decision made once per bot.

3. ROW 1 - LEVEL 15 - ONE CHOICE PER SPEC
   All three columns of every spec's level 15 row, with the usual twink
   pick and why.
   ARCANE
     Amplification     - Arcane Missiles fires 2 extra missiles while
                         Clearcasting is up. Id unverified.
                         The pick. Clearcasting is a passive proc, so the
                         talent pays out without the bot doing anything.
     Rule of Threes    - the third Arcane Charge makes the next Arcane
                         Blast or Missiles free. Mana sustain. Id
                         unverified.
                         The alternative if a build is mana-starved, which
                         at 29 it usually is not.
     Arcane Familiar   - a familiar that adds sustained damage. Id
                         unverified.
                         The slow-and-steady choice for a bot that fights
                         longer than a burst window.
   FIRE
     Firestarter       - Fireball and Pyroblast always crit against a
                         target above 90% health. Id unverified.
                         The pick, and it is a battleground talent: every
                         engagement starts on a full-health target, which
                         is exactly the window it covers.
     Pyromaniac        - casting Pyroblast or Flamestrike during Hot Streak
                         has a chance to re-grant it. Id unverified.
                         Needs crits first, so it is the weaker opener at
                         this level.
     Searing Touch     - Scorch deals 150% more damage and always crits
                         below 30% health. Id unverified.
                         The finisher alternative. Take it only if the bot
                         is expected to be chasing a fleeing target.
   FROST
     Bone Chilling     - every chill grants a stacking frost damage bonus,
                         up to 5% at ten stacks. Id unverified.
                         The pick if the bot just casts Frostbolt: the
                         chill is automatic, so the bonus is automatic.
     Lonely Winter     - no Water Elemental, but Frostbolt, Ice Lance and
                         Flurry deal 25% more. Id unverified.
                         If a level 29 cannot summon the elemental yet -
                         unverified - this is the strongest column in the
                         row, because the cost never applies.
     Ice Nova          - an area root. Id unverified.
                         The PvP pick. A mage that is being run down by
                         melee wants the root more than the damage, and
                         this is the only column in the row that stops a
                         charge.

4. ROWS THAT ARE NOT OPEN AT 29 - ALL ALT
   Arcane  rows 2 to 7: level 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 100. Includes Shimmer -
           the double Blink every mage wants, which is a level 30 talent
           and therefore out of reach by one level.
   Fire    rows 2 to 7: the same levels. Includes Blazing Soul, Blast Wave
           and Shimmer.
   Frost   rows 2 to 7: the same levels. Includes Glacial Insulation, Ice
           Floes and Shimmer.
   No column of any of these rows can be taken by a 29, so the file does
   not list them.

5. PVP TALENTS AT 29 - ONE SLOT, AND IT IS THE TRINKET
   The brief's assumption needs correcting on one point and confirming on
   another. It is not shared: the PvP panel is a separate set of slots with
   its own unlocks, and taking a level 15 talent neither spends nor blocks
   a PvP slot. It is one slot, and that part is right.
   How many: one. warcraft.wiki.gg, PvP talent, on the system this build
   uses - the slots sat "in the lower right corner of the Talents pane",
   and before Shadowlands "there were four slots in total. The first
   offered a choice of PvP trinket-type talents". Standard classes
   unlocked them at levels 20, 40, 70 and 110.
   So a 29 has the slot that opened at level 20, and it is a choice of
   crowd-control breakers rather than a mage ability. The three the wiki's
   patch notes name are:
     Gladiator's Medallion  - the manual break. Id unverified.
                              The pick for a bot: it fires exactly when
                              the bot decides it is in trouble, and a mage
                              that can act on its own timer beats one that
                              waits for a script.
     Adaptation             - breaks the next crowd control automatically,
                              on a cooldown. Id unverified.
                              The alternative for a bot with no reaction
                              logic: it never mistimes, but it also never
                              saves it.
     Relentless             - all crowd control lasts less. Id unverified.
                              The passive choice, best against a bracket
                              full of roots and fears rather than stuns.
   Every mage-specific PvP talent is ALT at 29, because there is no second
   slot to spend on one. Their names and ids were not read and are not
   quoted here.
   Where it applies: always on in battlegrounds and arenas, always off in
   dungeons and raids, and in the open world only with War Mode on. It can
   be changed at the start of a match or in a rested area.

6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT COVER
   Talent ids: unverified, all of them.
   The row gates 30 to 100: the standard BfA rule, not read off a client.
   Whether a 29 has Clearcasting, Arcane Charges, Hot Streak, Shatter or
   Summon Water Elemental - the abilities four of these talents depend on.
   That is the single most useful thing to check in game, because it
   decides between Amplification and Arcane Familiar, and between Lonely
   Winter and Bone Chilling.
   Anyone building the bots should open the talent pane once at 29 and
   correct this file from what the client shows. The two facts to carry
   into the code are that there is one row and one PvP slot, and that the
   PvP slot holds a trinket rather than a mage talent.
