REGION-WIDE CYCLE
Each warfront has its own faction-control/contribution/assault/patrol cycle; Arathi and Darkshore are not synchronized. Opposite faction holds the outdoor zone while the aspiring attacker contributes materials, gold or War Resources to its regional war effort. Patch 8.1 changed the 11 simultaneous daily donation quests to THREE available per day per active warfront: one gold/War Resources and two profession donations. Contribution reaches 100% -> attacker can queue for normal warfront; heroic queue is organized-group content when available. The assault queue lasts approximately seven days in contemporary guides; its expiry, NOT each individual player's victory, is when control flips to the attacker. The new owner patrols the outdoor zone, has its faction world quests, world boss and faction-dependent rare loot; losing faction starts contributions. Repeat in reverse. Contribution duration depends on region/cycle; guides commonly give about 3–5 days, but no build-35662 hard minimum, maximum, default automatic increment, target count, regional reset instant, or exact ownership timer verified. Assault and patrol are distinct: don't change regional ownership immediately upon SMSG_WARFRONT_COMPLETE. Quest reward availability is generally once per assault cycle and rare-loot eligibility resets when the player's faction RETAKES the zone, not on the daily quest reset. World-boss loot is similarly gated by control cycle; exact personal lockout DB flag and respawn schedule unverified. No evidence that an individual rare's physical respawn is 3–5 days: loot reset is NOT spawn respawn. Heroic completion can count for a corresponding normal quest if both were accepted (historical guide); exact core credit rule unverified.
