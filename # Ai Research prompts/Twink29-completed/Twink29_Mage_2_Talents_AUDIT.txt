AUDIT - Twink29_Mage_2_Talents.txt
File under audit: 133 lines, step 2 of 3. 18 claims checked: 12 pass,
2 corrected, 4 unverified.
Sources opened for this audit: the 9.0.2 mage talent tree as published by
Icy Veins (all three specs, rows 15 and 25, plus that patch's change list),
four independent Shadowlands mage guides that print the same level 15 rows
with the numbers, the wowwiki-archive mage talent table, and
warcraft.wiki.gg PvP talent.

1. THE LEVEL 15 ROWS
   1  PASS  Arcane: Amplification, Rule of Threes, Arcane Familiar. The
      9.0.2 tree and three separate guides all print that row.
   2  PASS  Fire: Firestarter, Pyromaniac, Searing Touch. Same sources.
   3  PASS  Frost: Bone Chilling, Lonely Winter, Ice Nova. Same sources.
   4  PASS  The values the file quotes are the published ones: Firestarter
      crits Fireball and Pyroblast above 90% health; Searing Touch is
      +150% Scorch and a guaranteed crit below 30%; Pyromaniac is an 8%
      chance; Lonely Winter is +25% to Frostbolt, Ice Lance and Flurry;
      Bone Chilling stacks to 10 at 0.5% each, which is the 5% the file
      says; Amplification is 2 extra Arcane Missiles; Rule of Threes makes
      the next Arcane Blast or Missiles free at three charges.
   5  PASS  The inheritance argument is sound. Shadowlands rows run 15, 25,
      30, 35, 40, 45, 50 against BfA's 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 100, so the
      first row is the one that did not move, and the 9.0.2 change list
      moves no level 15 talent. The file states the reasoning instead of
      asserting the result, which is the right way to handle it.

2. THE ONE CLAIM THAT NEEDED QUALIFYING
   6  CORRECTED  "Shimmer ... is a level 30 talent and therefore out of
      reach by one level." Right conclusion, wrong certainty. Shimmer sits
      in the second row in 9.0.2, which maps to level 30 in BfA, so the
      mapping is an inference from the same row-shift argument as the
      level 15 rows - not a reading. The file now says so. It is still the
      most useful line in section 4, because a mage bot that cannot double
      Blink is a different bot.
   7  CORRECTED  "Frost: Lonely Winter ... if a level 29 cannot summon the
      elemental yet - unverified - this is the strongest column." The
      sentence is honest but tangled: Lonely Winter's 25% is paid for by
      losing the elemental, so if the elemental is not available the
      talent is simply free. Reworded to say that in one line.

3. THE PICKS
   8  PASS  Amplification over Rule of Threes and Arcane Familiar: it pays
      out on a passive proc, which suits a bot that cannot time a burst
      window. Defensible and stated as a reason rather than a fact.
   9  PASS  Firestarter for Fire: the above-90% window is exactly the
      opening of a battleground engagement. Defensible.
   10 PASS  Ice Nova for Frost as the PvP pick, Bone Chilling as the PvE
      pick. Ice Nova is an area root and the only column in the row that
      stops a melee charge. Correct shape of argument.

4. PVP
   11 PASS  One slot at 29. warcraft.wiki.gg, PvP talent: the slots sit in
      "the lower right corner of the Talents pane", and before Shadowlands
      "there were four slots in total. The first offered a choice of PvP
      trinket-type talents", with standard classes at 20, 40, 70 and 110.
   12 PASS  The three trinket-type choices named - Gladiator's Medallion,
      Adaptation, Relentless - are the right three for this era. The same
      page's patch notes record all three being touched in 9.0.1, which is
      the patch that removed the medallion slot, so they were live in
      8.3.7. The file does not quote ids for them, which is correct,
      because none was read.
   13 PASS  "Not shared with the normal talent row", correcting the
      brief's assumption. Two panels, stated on the same page.
   14 PASS  "Every mage-specific PvP talent is ALT at 29." Follows: one
      slot, and the slot is the trinket choice.
   15 UNVERIFIED  The one-line descriptions of the three trinket talents.
      Medallion is the manual break and Relentless is the passive
      duration reduction - both safe. Adaptation's exact behaviour at this
      level was not read and the file's version is a paraphrase.

5. WHAT THE FILE DOES NOT SAY, AND SHOULD
   16 UNVERIFIED, AND IMPORTANT  Whether a level 29 mage has Clearcasting,
      Arcane Charges, Hot Streak, Shatter or Summon Water Elemental. Four
      of the nine level 15 columns are conditional on those passives, and
      no page was opened for any of them. The file flags this in section 6
      as the thing to check in game, which is the right place for it, but
      it means the picks in section 3 are picks between talents whose
      value cannot be confirmed at this bracket.
   17 UNVERIFIED  The row gates 30 to 100 themselves. Standard BfA rule,
      consistent with the druid and hunter files, still not read off a
      client.
   18 PASS  The structure claim - three specs, chosen at level 10, one row
      open - matches the other two class files and no page contradicts it.

6. WHAT TO CHANGE
   Two sentences: Shimmer marked as an inference rather than a fact, and
   the Lonely Winter line rewritten so it says what it means. The rest of
   the file is either printed in four independent guides or already
   labelled unverified. The audit's own conclusion is that the file is
   right about the rows and honest about the ids, and that its weakest
   point is the dependency on abilities nobody has confirmed a 29 has.
