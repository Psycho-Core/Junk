=====================================================================
1. AFFIXES THAT NEED AN ACTION (rules, spell ids, creature entries)
=====================================================================

General: all enemies inherit the key's affixes; a party member's job is to
prevent the affix damage/healing loss and keep the timer. On this fork the
server sends NO affix state (REQUEST_AFFIXES -> Handle_NULL; Group::Affixes
never filled; Challenger's Might/Burden effect points hardcoded 0 in
InstanceScript::CastChallengeCreatureSpell / CastChallengePlayerSpell) - the
companion must track the active affixes from the keystone itself.

--- Fortified (affix index 10; +2) ---
Effect: non-boss enemies x1.20 health and x1.30 damage (multipliers from the
fork's Challenger's Might script; health cross-checked against MDT's BfA
CalculateEnemyHealth: non-boss x1.2, boss x1.4). Action: rotate group
cooldowns on trash packs; bosses are normal - save damage for them.
Spells: Challenger's Might 206150 (creature aura, applied by
CastChallengeCreatureSpell with the health/damage multipliers in BASE_POINT0/1);
Challenger's Burden 206151 (player aura).

--- Tyrannical (affix index 9; +2) ---
Effect: bosses x1.40 health and x1.15 damage (fork Challenger's Might script;
health x1.4 cross-checked against MDT). Action: plan cooldowns/bloodlust on
bosses; trash is normal.
Spell: 206150 (as above).

--- Bolstering (affix index 7; +4) ---
Effect: when a non-boss enemy dies it bolsters allies within 30 yards,
permanently +20% max health and damage per stack; stacks (warcraft.wiki
Feb-2020 revision). Action: kill a pull together - do not let single mobs
survive long alone; stop DPS on lone survivors and regroup pulls.
Spell: ChallengerBolstering 209859 ("Bolster" - fork script header) - cast on
death; the fork's spell_challengers_bolster filters targets to in-combat
non-player, non-boss creatures within the aura.

--- Bursting (affix index 11; +4) ---
Effect: when slain, non-boss enemies deal 5% of max health to all players over
4 sec; stacks (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: stagger or stop AoE so stacks
do not snowball; heal through waves; dispel not possible - heal above the DoT.
Spells: ChallengerBursting 240443 (cast on death - wowhead spell=240443 title
"Burst", added 7.2.0, "Upon death the creature Bursts ... stacks",4 sec,
Magic) -> stacking player DoT 243237 (fork spell_challengers_burst builds
stacks from 240443 ticks; wowhead shows the buff as spell243237). Wowhead's
displayed hit is 19396 Shadow damage/sec at its shown scaling - use the wiki's
5% rule for BfA, not the flat number (unverified for arbitrary key levels).

--- Raging (affix index 6; +4) ---
Effect: non-boss enemies enrage at 30% health, +100% damage until defeated
(warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: focus-fire or kite raged mobs; tank should
not take raged melee without a slow/CC rotation.
Spell: ChallengerRaging 228318 (fork enum; live cast slot BASE_POINT2 in
CastChallengeCreatureSpell, currently 0).

--- Sanguine (affix index 8; +4) ---
Effect: slain enemies leave pools that heal enemies standing in them 5% of
max health per sec and damage players 15% of max health per sec
(warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: never tank or kite mobs through pools;
move the pack when a pool spawns under the melee stack.
Spell: ChallengerSanguine 226489 (fork enum, slot BASE_POINT10).
Creature entry for the pool itself: unverified (no summon entry for it in the
fork's scripts; only the cast spell id exists).

--- Teeming (affix index 5; +4) ---
Effect: additional non-boss enemies are present; the enemy-forces requirement
rises (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020; MDT totals normal vs teeming, e.g. Atal'Dazar
198 -> 237). Action: plan pulls for extra bodies; the extra spawns are marked
in MDT as clones with ["teeming"] = true - same creature entries as the
dungeon's normal mobs, no separate id.
Creature entries: dungeon trash entries (the per-dungeon lists in
MythicPlus_Rules_COMPLETED section 3); spell: none (spawn-based, no spell id
found - fork has no Teeming handling anywhere except DBCEnums).

--- Necrotic (affix index 4; +7) ---
Effect: enemy melee attacks deal physical damage every 2 sec and reduce
healing and absorption received by 2% per stack for 9 sec; stacks
(warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: tank must kite when stacks get high
(threshold unverified); keep the stack rolling off between packs.
Spell: ChallengerNecrotic 209858 (fork enum, slot BASE_POINT8; the Might
script gates its proc to once per 1000 ms).

--- Quaking (affix index 14; +7) ---
Effect: periodically all players emit a shockwave, damaging and interrupting
casting (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: stop hard casts when the ring
appears; melee stack normally (no spread requirement stated in BfA sources).
Spell: ChallengerQuake 240447 (fork enum, slot BASE_POINT11). Interval between
quakes: unverified.

--- Explosive (affix index 13; +7; fork name "Fel Explosives") ---
Effect: enemies periodically summon Explosive Orbs that detonate if not
destroyed (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: hard-switch and kill every orb
immediately; assign ranged/orb kick priority before pulls.
Creature entry: 120651 (fork script header "// 120651 - Fel Explosives";
class npc_challenger_fel_explosives - passive, totem mask).
Spells: summon 240444 (short distance) / 243110 (long distance), visual
240445, detonation damage 240446 (fork enum ChallengeSpells). Fork spawn
cadence while in combat (commented Might logic): first orb after 2-10 s, then
every 8-16 s if a player is within 60 y; orb casts detonation 500 ms after
summon in the fork script. Retail detonation fuse time: unverified (only the
"detonate if not destroyed" rule is sourced).

--- Grievous (affix index 12; +7) ---
Effect: below 90% health players suffer an increasing stacking damage-over-
time until healed above 90% (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: top players
past 90% promptly; tanks/healers prioritise direct heals over efficiency.
Spell: 240559 (fork script header "//240559" above spell_challengers_
grievous_wound; cast below 90%, stack +1 per tick, removed at >=90% in the
script; retail spell name for 240559: unverified - the constant
ChallengerGrievousWound is referenced but never defined in-repo).

--- Skittish (affix index 2; +7) ---
Effect: enemies pay far less attention to threat on tanks
(warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: tank uses defensive openers; DPS must not
over-threat early; misdirect/tricks-style redirect helps (class utility, not
sourced per-class - unverified).
Mechanics: carried by Challenger's Burden 206151 - the fork's
CastChallengePlayerSpell sets BASE_POINT2 (comment "2 Skittish"); the Burden
script applies SPELL_AURA_MOD_THREAT only while the player is in tank spec.
Threat reduction percentage: unverified (spell data not read).

--- Volcanic (affix index 3; +7) ---
Effect: enemies periodically cause gouts of flame under the feet of distant
players (warcraft.wiki Feb-2020). Action: ranged/healer move when the plume
appears under them; stay in the melee-friendly band when possible.
Mechanics (fork Challenger's Might script): every 8th tick, players in combat
between 15 and 60 yards from the caster get ChallengerSummonVolcanicPlume
209861 -> summons creature 105877 (script header "// 105877 - Volcanic
Plume"), which casts ChallengerVolcanicPlume 209862 after 250 ms. Retail
trigger distance/interval beyond the wiki text: as scripted (fork parity
unverified).

2. WHICH PACKS TO SKIP
=====================================================================

Count budget: a run only needs ALL BOSSES plus the required enemy-forces
total (section 3 of MythicPlus_Rules_COMPLETED: e.g. Atal'Dazar 198,
Freehold 261, Shrine of the Storm 695; Teeming weeks use the higher teeming
total). Killing a clone adds its ["count"] value (MDT FormatEnemyForces sums
counts toward forcesmax), so any subset of packs summing to the total works -
skipping is always allowed as long as the bar fills.

Skip tools with ids:
- Rogue Shroud of Concealment: spell 114018,30 yd,15 sec,6 min cooldown
  (warcraft.wiki Shroud of Concealment; active throughout BfA - patch history
  shows no radius/duration change between Legion and Shadowlands). Patch
  8.3.0 notes explicitly discuss skips "that don't involve Rogues' Shroud of
  Concealment or invisibility".
- Invisibility Potion: item 9172, invisible18 sec (warcraft.wiki; the 5-min
  cooldown and level cap were only added in patch 9.0.1, so in BfA it had no
  cooldown and no level cap). Wiki Mythic+ text uses Invisibility potions as
  the standard trash-skip.
- Death run: streak past trash, die, resurrect behind it - costs5 sec per
  death; decide with the timer (warcraft.wiki Challenger's Burden text, both
  era versions). Example in source: Black Rook Hold opening hallway.
- Season-4 example skips: Patch 8.3.0 notes credit Awakened obelisks with
  "interesting skips"; raider.io BFA SEASON 4 route articles (e.g. news/88,
  news/91) describe skipping everything in Siege of Boralus that cannot be
  killed with Ashvane Spotters (then back-pull), avoiding Atal'Dazar's middle
  pack before obelisk access, and taking the Underrot last boss with one
  awakened enemy because all four obelisks are slow to access.

Companion rule: prefer packs whose count gap still fills the bar (use the
required totals with the per-creature counts in MythicPlus_Rules_COMPLETED
section 3); never skip a required boss; on Teeming weeks remember the extra
["teeming"] clones and the higher teeming total.

=====================================================================
3. WHAT TO DO ON DEATH
=====================================================================

- Every death costs the timer 5 seconds (BfA value: warcraft.wiki Feb-2020
  "Challenger's Burden subtracts ... whenever any player dies" and the
  mythicpl.us 2020-04-02 snapshot "Five seconds are subtracted ... any time a
  player dies"; the current wiki's15-sec rule is the later Shadowlands-era
  change, not BfA). The fork computes run duration as elapsed + 5 x death
  count (ChallengeModeMgr) and pushes the death count to clients
  (SMSG_MYTHIC_PLUS_UPDATE_DEATH_COUNT 0x2620).
- Deliberate death runs are a legal tactic (wiki): run past an unwanted
  stretch, die, resurrect behind it - but only when the saved time exceeds
  5 sec per death (and the regroup cost).
- Combat resurrections work raid-style in keystones (patch 7.3.5 note, still
  the standing rule in the BfA-era page): start with1 charge, gain1 every
  10 minutes, shared pool capped at5 charges. Use a charge before releasing
  when the fight is still winnable; save charges when a death run is planned.
- On release: regroup at the resurrection point; do not chain-die while
  behind - the wiki's warning is explicit: frequent accidental deaths run the
  clock out faster than any pull. The fork also teleports the party at start
  to the instance entrance checkpoint (Atal'Dazar checkpoint position is the
  only coordinate set in fork BfA scripts: map 1763, -839.0337, -2092.268,
  725.8119).
- Death handling this server actually gives the AI: death count packet
  0x2620, elapsed timer 0x261C with CurrentDuration (elapsed + 5 x deaths),
  completion packet 0x2622 - no affix or forces data (see section 1).

Map ids for all season-4 dungeons (fork RegisterInstanceScript): Atal'Dazar
1763, Freehold 1754, King's Rest 1762, Operation: Mechagon 2097 (other half
unverified), Shrine of the Storm 1864, Siege of Boralus 1822, Temple of
Sethraliss 1877, The MOTHERLODE!! 1594, The Underrot 1841, Tol Dagor 1771,
Waycrest Manor 1862.
