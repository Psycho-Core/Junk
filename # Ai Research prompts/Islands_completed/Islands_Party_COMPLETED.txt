1. THE ORDER IN WHICH TO COLLECT NODES

   Per-time efficiency order (icy-veins):
   1) Rare enemies - a rare equals a large treasure chest: 250 azerite
      on Heroic. Rares drop a blue Azerite Globe (see buffs): if a
      dead ally is near a rare being killed, do not release - the
      globe revives them.
   2) The announced "a large amount of Azerite has appeared" event:
      kill the large azerite creature it points to - lots of azerite
      plus 2 large Azerite bags on the ground (icy-veins).
   3) Mining nodes - never skip them; even the small veins are very
      valuable per time spent (icy-veins).
   4) Every treasure chest - larger chests pay more than smaller;
      interactables such as seashells pay azerite equal to a rare
      (icy-veins).
   5) Stage flow: stage 1 loose nodes and mobs -> stage 2 azerite
      clusters rising in an announced area, guarded by elementals
      (moudi; triggers at total N ~3-4k/33%, H ~6k/33%, M ~12k/50%)
      -> stage 3 event at an announced spot: boss + 2 huge chests,
      several rares with normal chests and satchels, mini-bosses with
      chests (N 6k/50%, H 12k/50%, M 20k/80%) (moudi).
   Conflicting community order for reward farming (reddit): kill
   rares, skip mining and small/old chests and azerite sacks, wait
   for stage 3, kill the new rares, loot the themed chests. Which
   order is better for THIS server's bot: unverified.
   Node-to-node route (nearest first, pathing between nodes):
   unverified - no source ranks individual node positions.

2. WHEN TO FIGHT THE ENEMY TEAM

   - Each side's kill value grows the longer it survives, resets on
     death, and is capped at 250 (icy-veins).
   - If you are behind: kill the enemy team ONCE when their value has
     reached the 250 maximum - that kill is worth 750 azerite and
     sends them to the graveyard, halting their gathering (icy-veins).
   - Then do NOT re-engage: their value rebuilds slowly and fighting
     them below maximum is heavily discouraged - they are usually the
     island's hardest fight (icy-veins). Rule: fight once, avoid,
     fight again only at 250.
   - While fighting: kill their healer first and crowd-control one of
     the other two, as in real PvP (icy-veins). Enemy AI uses saps,
     grips, and swaps to low-health targets (icy-veins; step 2 - our
     server has no enemy AI in code, only 3/11 islands scripted).
   - The AI grows more aggressive when players fight instead of
     gather, and scales with player count and current azerite score
     (superlativelyrude) - keep gathering to keep it calm.
   - Stage 3 on Mythic (20k target is tight): split the party - one
     player keeps the enemy team busy so it cannot gather while two
     players kill and loot the event; keep 1-2k azerite headroom
     before winning because the event itself pays about that much
     (moudi).
   - PvP difficulty: both teams usually avoid each other and gather;
     the losing team may attack the winners (icy-veins).
   - Our server's own numbers (+3 azerite per kill flat, 250 stack
     cap, stack payout on kill, win at 9000): step 2 document.
   - Exact aggro radius and trigger weights: unverified.

5. ISLAND BUFFS - USE THESE

   Azerite Globe - drops from elites/rares, lies on the ground 30 s,
   anyone can take it (wowhead guide): spell 265703 "Azerite Energy" -
   on pickup heals 50% health and grants +30% damage and +20% health
   for 30 s, refreshes the timer, and revives nearby dead party
   members who have not released.
