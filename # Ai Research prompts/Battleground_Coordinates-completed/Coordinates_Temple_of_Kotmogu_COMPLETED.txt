=== TEMPLE OF KOTMOGU COORDINATES - BFA 8.3.7 (TRINITYCORE) - COMPLETED ===
Battleground_Coordinates - prompt 6 of 8 (Temple of Kotmogu, map 998).
Every coordinate is written as: map id, x, y, z. Values are reproduced
exactly from the named source; nothing is estimated. Where no readable
source exists the entry says "unverified".

=== 0. HOW TO READ / ORIENTATION ===
Facing values are reproduced exactly as stored (world_safe_locs rows:
same degrees-like convention as the other files; script floats: as
written). None of them was converted.
No readable source assigns north/south/east/west to map 998's axes; this
file therefore makes NO compass claims. The four orbs form a rectangle
(recomputed from the scripted positions): x 1716.78 - 1850.29,
y 1249.93 - 1416.77 - the script names the corners only by orb colour.
Route legs are straight-line distances between sourced landmarks. They
are NOT walkable-path measurements: no pathing/navmesh source for map 998
could be read (unverified).

=== 1. MAP ID ===

  below are tagged "Valley of Power - BG" - the internal name; warcraft.
  wiki: introduced in Mists of Pandaria, 10v10). No second map id appears
  in any readable source (the dump's row 4288 tagged "Scenario - Kotmogu"
  is map 1035, the separate scenario, not the battleground); which

=== 3. OBJECTIVES (FOUR ORBS; NO FLAGS/CARTS/NODES) ===
Four mogu orbs of power (warcraft.wiki: green, blue, purple, orange, one
  in each corner of the temple; hold it for victory points; one orb per
  player; death returns it to its origin). Script standard-mode spawns
  (SetupBattleground; gameobject entries, script spawn positions):
  Green orb   gameobject 212091 (998, 1716.78, 1416.64, 13.5709)
    o 1.57239 - pickup spell 121176, holder aura 121220
  Purple orb  gameobject 212092 (998, 1850.26, 1416.77, 13.5709)
    o 1.56061 - pickup spell 121175, holder aura 121219
  Orange orb  gameobject 212093 (998, 1850.29, 1250.31, 13.5708)
    o 4.70848 - pickup spell 121177, holder aura 121221
  Blue orb    gameobject 212094 (998, 1716.83, 1249.93, 13.5706)
    o 4.71397 - pickup spell 121164, holder aura 121217
  Each orb also gets a WORLD_TRIGGER aura creature at its exact position
  (script BG_KT_CREATURE_ORB_AURA_1..4). A Brawl variant of the script
  spawns only two orbs at (998, 1716.747, 1333.411, 13.18124) and
  (998, 1849.882, 1333.389, 13.32879) instead - brawl mode on your
  server: unverified.
Scoring (script + warcraft.wiki): while an orb is held its zone decides
  the points per tick - script BG_KT_TickPoints = {3, 4, 5} for
  outside / plaza / middle (KT_ZONE_OUT, KT_ZONE_IN, KT_ZONE_MIDDLE;
  "the farther you are from the heart of the temple, the fewer points"
  per warcraft.wiki); tick cadence: unverified. Win cap:
  script BG_KT_MAX_TEAM_SCORE = 1500. warcraft.wiki also credits a
  significant bonus for killing blows - that mechanism was not traced in
  the script (unverified). Holder effects: enlarged target, deals and
  receives increased damage, reduced healing, stacks over time
  (warcraft.wiki); the stacking spell in the script is HOTMOGU 237738
  (usage detail unverified).
Related: insignia spells 131527 (Alliance) / 131528 (Horde) are defined
  in the script enum; use unverified. No flags, carts or capture nodes
  exist here.
