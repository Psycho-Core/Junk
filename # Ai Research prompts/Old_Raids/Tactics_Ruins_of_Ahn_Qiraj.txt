TACTICS - RUINS OF AHN'QIRAJ (OLD RAIDS STEP 3 OF 16)
Server: private BfA 8.3.7 build 35662, TrinityCore base.
Context: level 60 raid (20-player in its era) entered by a
level 120 character with computer-controlled companions.
Values read on warcraft.wiki.gg.
Zone: Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj (Wowhead zone 3429). Instance map
id and all coordinates (map, x, y, z): unverified.
Boss entries verified on the Wowhead Ruins NPC list:
Kurinnaxx 15348, General Rajaxx 15341, Moam 15340, Buru the
Gorger 15370, Ayamiss the Hunter 15369, Ossirian the
Unscarred 15339. Add entries unverified unless stated.
D = dispel type. I = interruptible (only where read).
Most boss pages here name abilities WITHOUT spell ids; ids

1. KURINNAXX (15348)
PHASES: single phase; Frenzy at 30% health (page strategy:
   enrage, more physical damage + attack speed).
TANK: tank him facing away from the raid (Wide Slash 25814
   frontal, Mortal Wound stacks on the frontal target);
   offtank stays behind him and taunts when Mortal Wound
   stacks climb (10% healing reduction per stack, read).
GROUP: melee stack on one flank; ranged spread. A player
   caught in a Sand Trap 25656 (silence + 75% miss) waits
   it out - bandage/potion, do not run through the group.
GROUND: 25656 Sand Trap - placed under a player; silence
   zone. Move out of the trap area.
ADD ORDER: none.
ABILITY LIST:
  26527 Frenzy - +75% attack speed (at 30%, see PHASES).
  25646 Mortal Wound - healing -10% per stack on target.
  25656 Sand Trap - see GROUND.
  26446 Summon Player - summons an enemy player to him.
  3391 Thrash - 2 extra attacks.
  25814 Wide Slash - frontal cone +314.
AI RULE: only the current tank stands in front of him;
  swap tanks on Mortal Wound stacks, never heal through
  a full stack.

2. GENERAL RAJAXX (15341) - SEVEN WAVES, THEN THE BOSS
PHASES: waves 1-7 (officer + Swarmguard Needlers + Qiraji
  Warriors each), then Rajaxx. Allied NPCs Lieutenant
  General Andorov + 4 Kaldorei Elite help (entries
  unverified) - keep Andorov alive, he heals the raid
  (Aura of Command, read). Talking to Andorov starts it.
TANK: hold each wave at its spawn side; wave officers tanked
  facing away (Pakkon's Sweeping Slam is frontal). Rajaxx
  tanked with the raid's backs to a wall (Thundercrash
  knockback); re-taunt instantly after Thundercrash and
  after his aggro-drop.
GROUP: CC adds (they can be frozen/feared/rooted/stunned -
  page Weaknesses), kill the officer first each wave; on
  wave 2 kill Captain Tuubid fast (Attack Order marks one
  companion and all adds swarm them - protect the marked).
  Keep Drenn's Hurricane away from Andorov (move him).
GROUND: Drenn's Hurricane - channelled AoE around him
  (id unverified). Move out / move him away from NPCs.
ADD ORDER (per wave): P1 the officer, P2 Swarmguard
  Needlers (fragile, deadly - page), P3 Qiraji Warriors.
  Wave officers (entries unverified): W1 Captain Qeez
  (Intimidating Shout, AoE fear) 2N+4W; W2 Captain Tuubid
  (Attack Order) 3N+3W; W3 Captain Drenn (Hurricane)
  5N+1W; W4 Captain Xurrem (Shockwave) 3N+3W; W5 Major
  Yeggeth (Blessing of Protection - physical immunity;
  switch to casters or wait it out) 4N+2W; W6 Major Pakkon
  (Sweeping Slam) 2N+4W; W7 Colonel Zerran (Enlarge -
  dispellable, offensive dispel) 3N+3W (counts read).
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified unless stated):
  Thundercrash (Rajaxx) - halves everyone's current health
    (minimum 100), knockback, aggro reset. Id unverified.
  Aggro drop (Rajaxx) - drops current target. Id unv.
  Officer abilities as above; Enlarge is D-Magic offensive
  (dispellable per page).
AI RULE: officer first every wave, and after Thundercrash
  the tank re-taunts before anyone else acts.

3. MOAM (15340)
PHASES: P1 drain race: he drains mana (6 targets, +1000
  to himself per target) toward a full bar = Arcane
  Explosion (3000 raid-wide + knock-up). At 90 sec he
  turns to stone (immune, inactive) and summons 3 Mana
  Fiends; resume when they die or after 90 sec (page).
TANK: hold him centre; no positional ability was read.
GROUP: mana-drain classes (priest/warlock/hunter drains
  per page) drain HIM constantly - the fight is won by
  keeping his mana bar from filling; mana users enter
  a few seconds late (page: avoids the first drain).
GROUND: none read.
ADD ORDER: P1 Mana Fiends (x3, entry unverified) the
  moment they spawn - killing them ends stone form.
  Mana Fiend: Arcane Explosion (no knockback), Counterspell
  12-sec school lock - spread casters by school.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified - page names, no ids):
  Drain Mana, Arcane Explosion (at full mana), Trample
  (physical AoE), Turn to Stone (90-sec form change).
AI RULE: drain his mana non-stop; if his bar fills, the
  explosion is the group's fault, not the healers'.

4. BURU THE GORGER (15370)
PHASES: P1 kite-and-eggs to 20%: he cannot be tanked and
  is immune to taunt; he fixates a player and speeds up
  until an egg bursts on him. P2 at 20%: shell breaks,
  normal tank fight + Creeping Plague stacking raid DoT
  (80/tick, stacks every 3 ticks, undispellable even by
  Ice Block - page). Kill fast in P2.
TANK: P1 NO tank - the fixated companion kites him over
  the eggs. P2 tank normally.
GROUP: pre-damage the eggs to ~5%, burst one only when
  Buru stands on it (an egg does 45000 to Buru, 100-500
  to nearby players by proximity - keep distance from
  the burst). Kill each Hive'Zara Hatchling that hatches
  (entry unverified). Six eggs in the arena (read: "the
  six eggs").
GROUND: the eggs themselves are the weapon/zones; the
  pool water slows the kiter - kite on land (page warns
  swimming gets the target caught: Dismember stacking
  bleed 1248/2 sec if he reaches you).
ADD ORDER: P1 Hive'Zara Hatchlings as they hatch; P2
  none. NEVER DPS Buru directly in P1 (immune progress
  is by eggs only).
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Dismember (on catch),
  Thorns 200 aura, Creeping Plague (P2), egg burst.
AI RULE: if he is following you, run him over an egg -
  never stop, never swim; everyone else bursts the egg
  only when he is on it.

5. AYAMISS THE HUNTER (15369)
PHASES: P1 airborne to 70%: ranged only, melee cannot
  reach her; she is taunt-immune. P2 at 70% she lands -
  complete aggro wipe (page), re-establish threat before
  DPS; offtank ready (main tank may be sacrificed).
TANK: P1 a ranged companion holds her (Poison Stinger
  stacks climb on the top-aggro target, 25 Nature/sec,
  stacks to 100 - swap/let stacks be healed; page tactic:
  never let a target pass ~20 stacks without help).
  P2 tank at the altar, back to the mountain edge.
GROUP: melee wait at the altar base, NOT under her: their
  job is the Hive'Zara Larva. When a companion is Paralyzed
  on the altar, a larva runs for them - kill it before it
  arrives or the player dies and an elite Hive'Zara wasp
  spawns (page; entries unverified). AoE the swarmer waves
  (~20 low-health wasps).
GROUND: none read; Stinger Spray is raid-wide (1000
  Nature, ~30 sec).
ADD ORDER: P1 Hive'Zara Larva (sacrifice target), P2
  swarmers (AoE), P3 the elite wasp ONLY if a larva got
  through. Boss priority returns after each add call.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Stinger Spray, Poison
  Stinger (flying only), Paralyze (altar sacrifice),
  swarm summon, Fly (to 70%).
AI RULE: kill the larva before it reaches the altar -
  that one add is worth more than any boss damage.

6. OSSIRIAN THE UNSCARRED (15339)
PHASES: single phase, but fought as a kite loop: he starts
  in Supreme Mode (huge melee, fast). An Ossirian Crystal
  activated near him debuffs him 45 sec (weak, slow, one
  school vulnerable; crystals rotate around the room).
  If the debuff lapses in Supreme Mode he one-shots the
  tank - the loop never stops. (Holy damage is exempt
  from his resistances/vulnerabilities - page note.)
TANK: tanks wait behind the first crystal; the puller
  (mounted, marked) body-pulls; tank picks him up only
  after the first crystal fires, then kites crystal to
  crystal; no raid DPS until after the second crystal
  (threat is thin - page).
GROUP: move with the tank between crystals; designated
  clicker fires the next crystal at 10-15 sec remaining
  on the debuff (it has a cast time); stay out of the
  moving Sand Vortex tornadoes (very high damage inside;
  hunters can Track Elementals to see them); decurse
  Curse of Tongues off healers first.
GROUND: Sand Vortex tornadoes - moving zones, id
  unverified; crystals' positions are the safe rally
  points (tank him just short of the crystal so his
  Warstomp knockback cannot throw the clicker).
ADD ORDER: none. Crystals are objects, not adds.
ABILITY LIST (ids unverified): Supreme Mode, Enveloping
  Winds (10-sec stun on the aggro target; he ignores the
  stunned target, threat preserved; cannot be dispelled),
  Curse of Tongues (AoE around him, D-Curse), Warstomp
  (1500 + knockback), Sand Vortex summon.
AI RULE: when his weakness debuff is about to expire, be
  moving to the next crystal - never let him reach anyone
  in Supreme Mode.
